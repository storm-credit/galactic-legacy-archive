# AXIOM Clean-Surface Generation Prompt v1

Status: VISUAL PRODUCTION PROMPT
Canon Promotion: NONE
Publication: NOT AUTHORIZED
Reference Priority: USER-SELECTED AXIOM VISUAL MASTER

## Goal

Generate a production/reference sheet of LFX-01 AXIOM while preserving the exact visual identity of the user-selected AXIOM baseline. This is a cleanup/reproduction pass, not a redesign pass.

## Identity lock — do not change

- preserve the original AXIOM silhouette and body proportions;
- preserve the original head topology, low aggressive sensor face, neck volume and face-to-torso ratio;
- preserve the original chest, shoulder, waist, hip, thigh, shin and foot geometry as closely as possible;
- preserve the original rear wing / propulsion structure and its overall span;
- preserve the original weapon-carrying logic and overall heroic presence;
- preserve the dominant large-area color blocking: ceramic white, scarlet crimson, graphite/black internal frame, restrained gunmetal/titanium, small cyan sensors;
- do not turn AXIOM into a generic humanoid robot, a new prototype, or a different franchise-like design.

## CLEAN ARMOR / NO SPECKLE — mandatory

Remove or avoid all decorative surface noise that reads like spots or freckles on the machine.

DO NOT ADD:
- tiny isolated red dots on white armor;
- scattered red/orange squares, triangles, dashes or micro-stripes;
- random warning-color marks with no functional reason;
- dense micro-decals across shoulders, forearms, hips, thighs, knees or shins;
- random greebling, random vents, tiny repeated panel cuts or confetti-like markings;
- all-over scratches or weathering that become visual noise.

USE INSTEAD:
- broad, clean armor plates;
- large intentional scarlet armor masses;
- black/dark mechanical frame primarily at joints and recesses;
- clear structural seams only where armor removal, load transfer, cooling, articulation or service access makes sense;
- minimal unit-number or warning marking only where compositionally necessary.

Thumbnail rule: at small size, AXIOM must read as clean large white/red/black masses, not as a spotted or mottled machine.

## Sheet requirements

Create a premium animation/mechanical design sheet with:
- large hero 3/4 view;
- strict front / side / rear views with the same machine identity;
- head detail and sensor states;
- action poses;
- wing / propulsion detail;
- weapon and shield-related detail only if supported by the supplied reference;
- core/frame and articulation detail;
- scale reference;
- color/material swatches;
- legacy/related-item panel only when supplied by project canon or explicitly labeled proposed;
- clean editorial grid and Korean-first labels with English in parentheses where useful.

## Hard negatives

no redesign, no generic Gundam-like V-fin, no demon horns, no random spike forest, no human mouth mask, no angel/feather wings, no ornamental gold filigree, no all-over panel noise, no speckled red markings, no dotted armor, no random micro-decals, no dirty mottled surfaces, no dark monochrome palette, no photorealistic live-action robot.

## Canon guardrail

Image-generation models must not invent canon facts. Pilot, abilities, exact dimensions, output, acquisition, ownership, lineage status, legacy item function and story placement must come from project sources or remain visibly PROPOSED/HOLD. Generated typography is decorative until separately verified against the repository.
