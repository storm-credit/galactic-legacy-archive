## 1. Purpose

Secondary mysteries provide local investigation, reversals and rewards without delaying the twenty core series answers.

Rules:

- every secondary question has a retirement episode/window;
- false answers must arise from evidence, incentives or compressed records—not arbitrary lying;
- the truth must change a current decision or asset state;
- no secondary mystery may become a new core cosmological answer;
- no new `CORE` mystery is introduced after GA6;
- a decoy retires when its narrative function is complete, even if characters retain disagreement;
- no more than 2–4 unresolved secondary mysteries remain active in one 25-episode window.

## 14. Retirement Audit

At the end of every 25-episode block:

- list active secondary mysteries;
- close or renew each with an explicit reason;
- remove retired proper nouns from the active glossary;
- confirm no false answer has become accidentally true through later retcon;
- confirm core payoff timing is not delayed;
- confirm the reveal changes a material, relationship or institutional state.

## 15. Completion Ruling

> Secondary mysteries, decoys and false relics are no longer an undefined future layer.

Forty-one controlled secondary questions and ten false relics now have open, answer and retirement logic. Exact local scene staging remains part of detailed episode production.
