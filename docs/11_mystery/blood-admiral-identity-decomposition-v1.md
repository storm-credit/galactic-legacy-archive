## 1. Core Answer

`피의 제독 / Blood Admiral` is not:
- a fabricated person with no real acts;
- one completely innocent H-001 framed by history;
- a title worn by dozens with no personal responsibility;
- a secret mastermind unrelated to H-001.

Recommended truth:

> **The future record compressed a command title, H-001’s logistics/denial doctrine, orders issued by at least three commanders, propaganda attributions, inherited command keys and several regionally distinct incidents into one singular historical identity anchored to H-001.**

Why H-001 became the anchor:
- his route network and name connected otherwise separate incidents.
- some decisive orders were genuinely his.
- later successors used his doctrine/title/keys.
- enemies and supporters both benefited from a singular hero/monster.
- Archive compression favored one causally central identity over distributed responsibility.

## 2. Identity Layers

### Layer 1 — H-001 the Person

Owns:
- creation/evolution of route-federation doctrine.
- one or more real denial/closure decisions.
- political choices, omissions and public statements.
- failure to control doctrine/title/keys after institutional spread where foreseeable.

Does not own:
- every later order by successors.
- false enemy propaganda incidents.
- actions taken before/without his knowledge.
- all deaths statistically attributed to route war.

### Layer 2 — Office/Title

Working office:
- `Federation Route Admiral`, `Corridor Admiral`, `Emergency Route Coordinator` or similar.

Function:
- coordinates fleet/route/denial across multiple nodes.

Title inheritance:
- used by acting deputies, successors or regional commanders during communication splits.
- field reports shorten the office name to “Admiral.”

Compression:
- later archives treat office-continuity as person-continuity.

### Layer 3 — Doctrine

Named by supporters/enemies:
- `Blood Line`, `Red Corridor`, `Scorched Route Doctrine`, etc.; final term open.

Components:
- evacuation before denial.
- distributed charges/keys.
- local/federation approval thresholds.
- deny capture of strategic route.
- support/compensation for dependent regions.

Historical distortion:
- complex thresholds reduced to “destroy route before enemy use.”

### Layer 4 — Command Keys and Signatures

- inherited/replicated emergency route signatures carry H-001’s authority chain.
- subordinates may issue valid orders under delegated scope.
- compromised/replayed signatures create disputed incidents.

Archive issue:
- systems cluster orders by authority signature and doctrine, then resolve to one identity.

### Layer 5 — Propaganda and Testimony

Enemy narrative:
- singular butcher who destroys worlds/routes.

Supporter narrative:
- savior who denied occupiers and freed frontier.

Victim/survivor narratives:
- differ by node, timing, evacuation and later shortages.

State/Continuity narrative:
- proof that decentralized route authority becomes mass destruction.

### Layer 6 — Successor Commanders

At least three distinct command actors:

1. H-001 — original doctrine/real D4 decision.
2. a radical/scorched commander — expands doctrine or acts without threshold.
3. a local/federal/acting route commander — issues delegated or forged/ambiguous orders.

Optional fourth:
- Continuity/State false-flag, replay or administrative attribution, but do not make conspiracy explain everything.

## 6. H-001’s Responsibility Framework

H-001 should accept:

1. direct orders he issued.
2. foreseeable consequences of doctrine and delegation.
3. failures to disclose uncertainty or protect dissent.
4. failure to revoke/respond to misuse where capable.
5. political benefits gained from myth/title.

H-001 should contest:

1. acts he did not order/control.
2. fabricated or replayed signatures.
3. downstream totals assigned without causal distinction.
4. the idea that all actors were powerless before his doctrine.
5. erasure of victims’ and other commanders’ agency.

No “I take responsibility for everything” heroic simplification.
No “it wasn’t me” exoneration.

## 8. Record and Trial/Review Design

Do not use one courtroom verdict to settle everything.

Possible process:
- local inquiries for direct incidents.
- federation public audit and office records.
- victim/community claims.
- military/Compact proceedings.
- independent plural archive.
- restorative/compensation measures.
- office removal/election/limits.

Possible H-001 outcomes:
- remains route leader under reduced/bounded office.
- resigns title but continues political/logistics role.
- is convicted/held responsible for specific act while contesting broader myth.
- loses factions/relationships but not agency.

Recommended:
- accepts specific responsibility and institutional limits; does not disappear into martyrdom or become P-001 subordinate.
