## 1. Purpose

The macro architecture assigns every CORE mystery a final payoff, but the exact episode and decisive scene are not locked.
This ledger prevents:

- late exposition clusters;
- a reveal without material consequence;
- duplicate payoffs competing in the same chapter;
- a mystery being “resolved” only through Archive UI;
- deliberate ambiguity being confused with unfinished planning.

Every thread must receive:

1. exact final episode or 2–5 episode range;
2. physical/operational scene;
3. character decision;
4. institutional or relationship change;
5. remaining deliberate ambiguity, if any;
6. evidence of earlier plants and partial payoffs;
7. confirmation that the payoff cannot be removed without changing the ending.

---

## 2. Status Codes

- `PLANTED`: early clue exists.
- `ADVANCED`: at least one mid-series reinterpretation exists.
- `MACRO LOCKED`: final GA and material outcome assigned.
- `SCENE OPEN`: exact episode/scene not fixed.
- `SCENE LOCKED`: exact scene dependency approved.
- `PAYOFF VERIFIED`: scene drafted/reviewed and earlier clues still fair.
- `DELIBERATELY OPEN`: remaining uncertainty is intentional and supported.

Current default for M-001–M-020:
- `PLANTED / ADVANCED / MACRO LOCKED / SCENE OPEN`.

Rules:
- no final payoff is only a conversation, document reading or Archive display;
- a reveal must alter an action, right, relationship, resource or institution;
- no more than two CORE final payoffs share one climactic scene unless they are causally inseparable;
- an epilogue may show aftermath, but the decisive choice must occur before the epilogue;
- all earlier clues must remain factually true after reinterpretation.
