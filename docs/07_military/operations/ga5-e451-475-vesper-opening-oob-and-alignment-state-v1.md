## 6. Readiness and Command Rules

Readiness must be stated in two dimensions:
- physical/technical;
- legal/cohesion/authentication.

No ship may be counted as mission-ready solely because engines and weapons work.

Command acceptance:
- each ship lends mission authority for named objective, geography and duration;
- title, pay, family, maintenance, weapons and route keys do not silently transfer;
- captain safety/surrender authority remains;
- defined technical/medical/physical stops remain;
- crew representation does not become tactical plebiscite.
