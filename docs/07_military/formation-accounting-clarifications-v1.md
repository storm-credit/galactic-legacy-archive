## 1. Non-Additive Rule

Rows in the front-stage formation registry are not all simultaneous, independent inventories.

There are two classes.

### Source formations

Examples:
- Imperial fleets;
- Helix security groups;
- Independence/local fleets;
- Neutral service pools.

These draw from the global holdings.

### Derived/mission formations

Examples:
- Ardis Joint Defense;
- Mutual Route Federation Wing;
- Common Fleet Mission Trust;
- Orpheus Mixed Evacuation Command;
- Plural Record Protection Network;
- Staged Transition Compact.

These temporarily combine, detach, reflag or coordinate assets from source formations and local/background pools.

> Source and derived rows must never be summed as separate galaxy-wide hulls.

## 2. Snapshot Rule

Each registry count is an envelope at its first major activation or most useful planning state.

It is not:
- an R0 simultaneous total;
- a permanent maximum;
- a guarantee all hulls share one location;
- a replacement for episode/operation OOB.

## 3. Required Operation Accounting

Every derived formation operation sheet must list:

- source formation/pool ID;
- hulls and support detached;
- activation date;
- travel and assembly time;
- source duty left uncovered;
- command/charter basis;
- return, transfer, loss or internment destination.

Example:

`JNT-FLT-05` may draw from `IMP-MD-06`, `IMP-LM-03`, regional pools, Helix service vessels and independent route forces. Those assets remain deducted from the sources while assigned.

## 4. Personnel and Support Accounting

A combat hull transferred without its original crew/support may have a lower readiness than the source row.

Track separately:
- hull;
- crew;
- command team;
- ammunition;
- repair and tender allocation;
- insurance/legal flag;
- frame/craft complement.

A captured or reflagged hull is not combat-ready merely because the hull exists.

## 5. Global Ceiling

At no time may active source plus unassigned pools exceed the locked galaxy-scale holdings after deducting:

- destroyed/scrapped hulls;
- long refits;
- interned/impounded assets;
- unavailable crews;
- local defense commitments.

Joint operations redistribute readiness; they do not create it.

## 6. Completion Ruling

> The formation registry is a front-stage identity and persistence system, not an additive master inventory.

Detailed E101–1100 operation sheets must use source-to-derived accounting.
