## 2. Historical Origin

AUXILIA program purpose:
- 황실 초기 격자망의 검사·구조·외벽수리·통제키 운반.
- 여러 민간 조선소와 지역규격을 다뤄야 했기에 광범위한 물리·데이터 인터페이스를 탑재.
- 전투기는 아니었으나 노드 반란과 구조작전에서 군용 개조.

Why lineage was erased:
- 헬릭스 표준협약 이전의 개방형 인터페이스가 기업 독점과 보안정책에 위협.
- 황실 후계 인증과 연결된 오래된 통제키가 일부 잔존.
- 여러 사고와 불법개조 책임을 기체 계보에 전가.
- 정식 원형은 해체됐고 07호는 교육용 프레임으로 재등록.

Archive error:
- 유산록은 07호를 황실 전용 전투기로 오인하거나, 7대 자매기 중 하나로 분류할 수 있음.
- 실제로는 전투기 계보가 아니라 여러 기체의 인터페이스 조상이었을 가능성.

## 3. Physical Envelope

Height:
- 10.8 m provisional.

Operational mass:
- 41 t in opening configuration.

Crew:
- 1 pilot.
- maintenance access supports external technician but no combat co-pilot.

Peak combat window:
- 11–14 minutes in opening configuration.

Low-output work endurance:
- 3–5 hours with external supply and conservative operation.

Transport:
- standard medium frame cradle.
- can be carried by school utility shuttle after removing external radiator boom.

Visual silhouette:
- narrow torso built around old inspection core.
- oversized industrial forearms and hands.
- asymmetrical shoulder radiator package.
- one leg from a later military trainer, producing visibly different armor geometry.
- sensor head is a boxy multi-spectrum work unit rather than heroic face.
- anchor cables and tool hardpoints remain exposed.

Design rule:
- ugly because of history and function, not random scrap decoration.

## 4. Opening Configuration

### Core — Legacy inspection kernel

Strength:
- broad hardware handshake and diagnostic access.
- can read old node-maintenance protocols.

Weakness:
- outdated encryption.
- potential backdoors and identity conflicts.
- generates contradictory registration data.

### Right arm — industrial manipulator

Strength:
- high grip precision, cutting·welding·rescue tools.
- can physically anchor or redirect objects.

Weakness:
- slow combat traverse.
- exposed hydraulic/electroactive bundles.

### Left arm — trainer shield / anchor plate

Strength:
- absorbs debris and docking impact.
- magnetic·mechanical anchors.

Weakness:
- poor against repeated focused fire.
- heavy and unbalanced.

### Torso — school-limited reactor governor

Strength:
- stable and safe at work output.

Weakness:
- severe peak-output restriction.
- governor logs and transmits unauthorized use.

### Radiator — salvaged asymmetric wing

Strength:
- better heat rejection than current school record indicates.

Weakness:
- vulnerable when deployed.
- uneven torque and silhouette.

### Legs — mixed industrial/military pair

Strength:
- one leg has strong anchoring; the other has better vector thrust.

Weakness:
- gait·thrust asymmetry.
- P-001’s former-life muscle memory initially worsens control.

### Sensor head — utility multispectral box

Strength:
- close-range material, heat, stress and life-sign detection.

Weakness:
- poor long-range fire control.
- not designed for fast target tracking.

## 5. Opening Weapons and Tools

### Thermal cutter lance

Original role:
- hull cutting, rescue, weld separation.

Combat use:
- close-range armor joint attack.
- door·anchor·weapon mount removal.

Limit:
- requires contact time and exposes frame.
- heat feeds back into arm and core.

### Cable launcher / rescue tether

Use:
- anchor, retrieve debris or people, alter relative movement, save drifting ally.

Combat use:
- not magical grappling; cable length, tension and anchor strength matter.

### Low-velocity training projector

Use:
- paint·sensor marker or low-energy projectile for drills.

Potential upgrade:
- electronic decoy, sensor-tag, breaching marker.

### Shield plate

Use:
- debris, docking, temporary cover.

Combat principle:
- opening frame wins by controlling position and preserving targets, not by heavy firepower.

## 6. Unique System — Open Service Bus

Working name:
- `Open Service Spine / 개방형 정비척추`

Function:
- physical mounts, power conversion, cooling and data translation designed to connect tools from many early lattice contractors.

What it enables:
- unusual part combinations.
- rapid field diagnosis.
- old node and ship interfaces.
- collection of modules with meaningful compatibility puzzles.

Costs:
- weight and wiring complexity.
- cybersecurity exposure.
- conversion loss and heat.
- no manufacturer warranty or standard certification.
- each new module needs H-002’s calibration and documentation.

Anti-overpower rule:
- “anything can be equipped” does not mean “anything works well.”
- incompatible voltage, control latency, cooling, ammunition and structural load can damage the frame.
- only 2–3 nonstandard modules can be safely integrated in the opening arc.

## 7. Why P-001 Is Not Immediately Perfect With It

- his former-life ace body used later military control standards.
- 07호의 asymmetric thrust and industrial response are unfamiliar.
- current young nervous system cannot sustain former S2/S3 habits.
- he remembers the frame as disposable training equipment, not its hidden lineage.
- H-002 understands the machine better than he does.

First competence proof:
- P-001 notices tactical uses of its defects.
- H-002 makes the machine survive those uses.
- neither can succeed alone.

## 9. Long Upgrade Philosophy

The frame should not remain the unquestioned final machine forever.

Possible paths:

### Path A — Ship of Theseus

- components replaced over time; identity becomes crew·records·open bus rather than original metal.

### Path B — Training / legacy platform

- P-001 eventually gives it to another pilot or academy.
- remains symbol and testbed while P-001 moves to command frame.

### Path C — Distributed lineage

- its open-standard architecture spreads across many frames, making the ‘collection’ a production ecosystem.

Recommended blend:
- P-001 pilots it through academy and early frontier arcs.
- later, the frame becomes team inheritance and open-standard prototype rather than endlessly scaling personal super-robot.

## 10. Counter-Doctrine

Enemies can exploit:
- exposed interfaces with malware.
- weak long-range fire control.
- asymmetric mobility patterns.
- radiator vulnerability.
- limited peak output.
- legal/registration anomalies that deny docking and resupply.

Effective counters:
- stand-off drones.
- cable-cutting munitions.
- heat saturation.
- fake legacy interfaces.
- standardized repair denial and parts embargo.

Rule:
- fame makes its unusual flexibility less surprising and forces new tactics.

## 13. Battle-Writing Checklist

Every 07호 battle must track:
- asymmetric thrust orientation.
- heat state and radiator exposure.
- cable anchors and relative movement.
- low long-range lethality.
- current tool/module configuration.
- structural target that should survive.
- H-002’s maintenance warning and whether P-001 obeys.

Do not:
- suddenly reveal hidden beam cannon.
- let universal interface bypass all security.
- repair severe damage between scenes without time and parts.
- use industrial hand precision at full combat speed without tradeoff.
