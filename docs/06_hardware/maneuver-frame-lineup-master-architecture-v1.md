## 1. Executive Ruling

The setting already contains military doctrine, fleet-scale frame holdings, service limits and the full GA1-GA10 story architecture. It does **not** yet contain a reader-facing catalog comparable to a mature mecha franchise's mass-production, ace, support, rival, regional and successor lineups.

The current 415 collection registry rows are not 415 machines. They mix entities, parts, rights, state changes, promises and set records. The only stable named protagonist machine is `AUX-07 / 07호`; `LFX-01 AXIOM` remains a separate noncanon legacy-machine candidate.

## 2. Frozen Intent and Success Conditions

### 2.1 User intent carried forward

- The lineup must have the immediate appeal of a major hero/rival/mass-production mecha range without copying a Gundam design or naming grammar.
- AXIOM should retain the preferred modern white/crimson/graphite/titanium/cyan direction, dual eyes, armored visible neck and sword-led hero presence.
- No long central horn, demon-like horns, V crown, bald round head, antique finish, all-dark palette or two-color-only body.
- The collection system must include mass units, ace machines, rivals, support machines, regional forks, rebuilds, successors and losses.
- A machine is not collectible merely because its color, weapon or software changed.

### 2.2 Success conditions

1. E1-E20 still exposes only 07 as the principal machine and one principal weapon.
2. At 64 px grayscale, the manufacturing lineage remains identifiable without color, logo, weapon or pose.
3. Every placed chassis slot proposes a distinct load-frame reason, acquisition barrier, maintenance burden and loss consequence; only a completed Section 5 gate may verify it as an independent chassis.
4. Every front-stage machine has a narrative role beyond winning a duel.
5. GA7 and GA9 reuse earlier lineages instead of flooding the story with new machines.
6. 07 remains a service/open-standard lineage; AXIOM cannot absorb its public-service ending.
7. Candidate names use a code plus one everyday name; upgrades do not masquerade as new machines.
8. All new rows remain `PROPOSED — NONCANON`, with `canon_promotion=NONE`.

## 5. What Counts as a New Machine

An entry counts `+1 independent chassis` only when all are true:

1. the primary load-bearing frame is new;
2. at least two of power, heat rejection, propulsion, control, cockpit interface, armor support or weapon support are redesigned;
3. it has an independent parts and certification path;
4. it can be lost, retired or inherited independently of its parent;
5. it can operate as an entity rather than only as an equipment package.

Before a demand is classified as reuse, its proposed host must already exist by the demand window and must carry usable role, cradle/certification, cooling/recovery and support fields. These checks prove that reuse is operationally representable at planning depth; they do not promote the host or profile to canon.

| Change | Entity count | Index treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| color, weapon, shield, sensor calibration | +0 | state/loadout |
| reversible mission package | +0 | configuration; optional FACET author tag |
| permanent two-system refit on same frame | +0 | refit state; optional TEMPER author tag |
| rebuild after major damage on same legal/mechanical entity | +0 | rebuild state |
| one-operation high-risk fit | +0 | operation fit; optional RESOLVE author tag |
| integrated redesign still using same independent chassis | normally +0 | integrated state; review case by case |
| new frame plus independent logistics and loss path | +1 | new code, new proper name, INHERIT relation only in the ledger |

`FIRSTMARK / FACET / TEMPER / INTEGRAL / RESOLVE / INHERIT` remain useful author-side taxonomy. Reader-facing Korean uses functional phrases such as `구조 구성`, `1차 개수형`, `재구축형` and `작전 구성` unless a form earns a historical nickname.

## 9. Collection Desire Architecture

### Discovery

- Recognize lineage by load rails, service voids, knee/shoulder rhythm and maintenance motion before color or logo.
- Introduce a machine through a failed repair, disputed license, unusual recovery action or enemy countermeasure, not a static stat block.
- Let a damaged donor frame reveal an ancestor or regional fork.

### Acquisition

Operational acquisition requires more than possession of the body:

1. legal custody or explicit mission authority;
2. compatible cradle and cooling;
3. certified control software and interface;
4. trained pilot and maintenance crew;
5. consumables, spares and recovery route;
6. acceptance by the community or institution affected by its use.

### Synergy

- Recon/EW opens an access window.
- Command/relay shares a bounded picture without centralizing every decision.
- Breach/capture secures the facility or weapon node.
- Rescue/recovery preserves people, evidence and repairable hardware.
- Open-service frames translate between incompatible lineages at a cost.

No machine receives a flat collection bonus because the set is complete.

### Completion and legacy

Completion means the production lineage, service records, losses, ownership chain and public consequences are understood. It does not mean the protagonist owns every machine. A finished lineage may end as a public training standard, a closed corporate lock-in, a dismantled military program or a regional fork with refusal rights.

## 11. Visual and Weapon Boundaries

### Shared mechanical rules

- standard frames stay within the setting's existing 9-14 m and 25-70 t envelope unless a canon source says otherwise;
- 14-20 m heavy/ace frames are exceptional and require larger cradle, cooling and direct service burdens;
- central long-weapon storage and a central heat-rejection pack compete for the same back volume;
- hips prioritize magazines/tools, shoulders prioritize sensors/decoys, lower legs prioritize propellant/anchors;
- a two-handed sword requires a stowed view, draw envelope, two-hand grip, guard clearance, maintenance seam and dock clearance.

### AXIOM hold baseline

Retain only as visual-development constraints:

- A/B-style dual-eye face with low armored brow;
- compact but visibly articulated armored neck;
- modern hard-surface body, not a smooth android torso;
- white/crimson/graphite/titanium/cyan balance;
- physical sword as decisive-contact weapon, carbine as ordinary-combat weapon;
- broken shear line and asymmetric notch instead of horns or a V crown.

Do not assign abilities, pilot, transformation, acquisition, exact dimensions or GA placement from an image.
