# 1. Outcome classes

### O0 — authorized / not completed
- paper authorization, test rig or partial procurement only;
- no operational full article required.

### O1 — whole-article/test failure
- useful lesson possible;
- article fails intended operational purpose, safety, legality or viability.

### O2 — partial technical success
- subsystem/process succeeds;
- whole article does not become stable production root.

### O3 — lineage success
- architecture proves reproducible enough to seed one or more later production families/processes.

### O4 — exceptional / non-scalable success
- impressive performance or unique result;
- too costly, unsafe, fragile, operator-specific or politically unacceptable for lineage adoption.

Outcome is independent of present provenance.

# 5. Why P-13 is O4 rather than “best prototype”

P-13 should not become the hidden final supermachine by default.

Its O4 role is more useful if:
- test performance is exceptional;
- acceleration/sensor response looks decades ahead;
- only a tiny operator population can survive/use it;
- maintenance/heat/service burden is extreme;
- one or more injuries/long-term damage make adoption unacceptable;
- modern designers copy narrow subsystems but reject the whole architecture.

Possible myths:
- “the only true successful combat prototype”;
- “machine no modern frame could match.”

Reality:
- it solved one test envelope while failing civilization-scale reproducibility.

This provides a powerful rival/relic temptation without making old hardware universally superior.

Present survival/provenance remains HOLD.

# 6. Why P-20 can be O3 without becoming the ultimate frame

P-20’s success is **manufacturability/repeatability**.

It may be less impressive in a duel than P-13 or later specialist machines.

Success means:
- common subassemblies;
- stable production tolerances;
- predictable training/service;
- bounded mission variation;
- economically repeatable load frame.

This makes it a plausible root for later mass/general-purpose ecology.

It is important because many machines could descend from its lessons, not because the original article is strongest.

Scalable structural/mass-production architecture.

- Helix can use P-20 manufacturing + P-12 sensor confidence + P-03/P-13 medical interface limits;

No modern lineage needs exclusive ownership of one O3 root.
