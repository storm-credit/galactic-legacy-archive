## 1. Core principle

A multi-species galaxy is believable only if ordinary infrastructure carries the cost of difference.

The setting must answer not only “what do they look like?” but:

- where do they breathe?
- where do they sleep?
- what do they eat?
- what happens in a fire/decompression?
- how are they restrained during acceleration?
- how does a hospital stabilize them?
- how does a prison hold them lawfully?
- how does a school teach them?
- how does a rescue ship triage incompatible environments?

Infrastructure is therefore a political and economic layer, not background decoration.

---

## 2. Universal-service versus specialized-service model

Do not build every facility for every species at full capability.

Use four service bands.

### MS0 — Baseline public access

- physically enter/communicate;
- basic identity and emergency support;
- safe waiting zone;
- temporary environmental assistance;
- no guarantee of full medical/residential compatibility.

### MS1 — Common mixed-species service

- supports the most common regional atmospheric/gravity/body-plan differences;
- standardized adapters, food labeling, sanitation and restraint options;
- trained staff and translation support.

### MS2 — Specialist support

- dedicated medical chemistry;
- high/low pressure compartments;
- aquatic/fluid habitat;
- unusual gravity/rest cycles;
- species-specific neonatal/care systems;
- specialized military or industrial gear.

### MS3 — Rare/extreme environment

- custom facility, ship module or home-system service;
- long preparation and high cost;
- emergency stabilization may be possible without normal life support.

Rule:
- legal equality does not imply every small ship carries MS3 support.
- service-capacity limits must be visible and appealable rather than used as invisible exclusion.

## 3. Standard mixed-species habitat architecture

A large public habitat/ship may contain:

1. **Common pressure spine** — majority-safe shared environment.
2. **Environment transition locks** — pressure/gas/temperature conversion.
3. **Adaptive residential modules** — local atmosphere, humidity and gravity support.
4. **Shared visual/audio/data signage** — multimodal alerts.
5. **Food segregation and contamination controls**.
6. **Species-variable sanitation modules**.
7. **Medical refuge compartments**.
8. **Emergency transport capsules**.
9. **Variable restraint/seat systems**.
10. **Remote participation interfaces** for persons who cannot safely enter a shared environment.

Blind spot:
- “everyone wears a suit” is not a universal solution. Long-term suits create health, dignity, cost and class problems.

---

## 4. Gravity and acceleration

Gravity difference affects:

- bone/muscle health;
- circulation;
- work shift length;
- pregnancy/development;
- sport;
- furniture;
- combat;
- evacuation;
- ship maneuver limits.

Mixed ship rule:
- the captain cannot choose acceleration purely from tactical convenience if passengers/crew have incompatible physiological limits.

Possible solutions:
- rotating local-gravity habitats;
- low-g common transit zones;
- acceleration couches with species modules;
- temporary pharmacological/medical support;
- mission-specific crew selection.

Military consequence:
- a high-g capable species/person does not automatically make the whole ship high-g capable; structure, cargo, patients and other crew remain constraints.

---

## 5. Atmosphere, pressure and temperature

Every common facility records:

- breathable gas range;
- contamination/toxicity risk;
- safe pressure;
- humidity;
- thermal range;
- emergency cross-exposure duration.

Shared-space options:

- one common atmosphere with personal supplements;
- separated atmosphere zones;
- pressure-compatible common suit areas;
- remote/robotic participation.

Political conflict:
- which atmosphere becomes “normal” can reflect demographic power and building cost.

Rule:
- this must appear through ordinary inconvenience before becoming a discrimination plot.

---

## 6. Food and biochemistry

A food system tracks:

- macronutrient compatibility;
- toxic compounds;
- microbial contamination;
- allergens/chemical incompatibilities;
- preparation temperature/pressure;
- cultural restrictions;
- waste-processing consequences.

Do not assume:
- all carbon-based food is mutually edible;
- alien food is automatically disgusting;
- one nutrient paste solves culture and health.

Mixed crew logistics:
- food diversity increases cargo complexity but can create trade niches and cultural exchange.

---

## 7. Medicine and rescue

### Emergency medicine baseline

All MS1 medical facilities need:

- species/lineage identification with consent and emergency exception rules;
- current physiology profile;
- incompatible-drug warnings;
- atmosphere/temperature/pressure stabilization;
- species-variable blood/fluid/organ protocols;
- cybernetic and implant emergency isolation;
- reproductive/developmental status handling;
- quarantine capability.

### Rescue doctrine

Rescue craft cannot always carry every environment.

Possible capacity:
- universal sealed casualty pods;
- interchangeable atmosphere cartridges;
- fluid-support pod;
- low/high-temperature pod;
- cybernetic life-support adapter.

Narrative cost:
- limited pod counts force capacity choices without treating one species as lesser.

Legal principle:
- triage uses current medical survivability and mission capacity, not political/species value.

---

## 8. Ships and public transport

Passenger/crew manifests should track **service needs**, not just species labels.

Fields:
- atmosphere;
- pressure;
- gravity/acceleration;
- food;
- medical risks;
- mobility/body-plan space;
- cybernetic power/service;
- communication/translation;
- dependent/caregiver requirements.

This avoids stereotyping when individuals within one species differ.

Commercial consequence:
- route operators advertise supported service bands;
- insurers price unsupported environments;
- Neutral Assembly can gain strategic value by operating broader MS1/MS2 rescue and transit infrastructure.

---

## 9. Schools and academies

A mixed-species school must address:

- developmental age equivalence;
- physical education;
- sleep cycles;
- food;
- environmental housing;
- medical consent;
- communication;
- uniform/equipment fit;
- discipline methods;
- guardian law;
- test fairness.

Important continuity rule:
- the existing K-13 Academy opening can remain locally human/posthuman-heavy. If nonhuman students are later added retroactively, exact scene and facility effects require author-approved revision.

No silent crowd replacement.

---

## 10. Detention, prison and military custody

This is a major rights blind spot.

A lawful facility needs:

- safe environment;
- species-appropriate food and medical care;
- restraint that does not become torture because of body plan;
- communication/translation;
- religious/cultural practice within security limits;
- caregiver/dependent arrangements;
- implant/cybernetic policy;
- emergency evacuation.

Prohibitions:
- atmosphere deprivation as routine compliance tool;
- confiscating life-support prosthetics;
- using incompatible gravity as punishment;
- denying translation to create consent;
- treating species-specific medicine as privilege rather than necessary care.

Military POW rules must account for these same needs.

---

## 11. Employment and industrial safety

A job cannot assume one body by default when diversity is common.

Workplace design fields:

- reach/load geometry;
- pressure suit/interface;
- gravity;
- sensory alarms;
- shift/sleep cycle;
- toxic exposure;
- heat;
- prosthetic/cybernetic service;
- emergency extraction.

Conflict:
- employers may argue full accommodation is impossible in small/remote sites.

Required analysis:
- essential job function;
- reasonable adapter;
- shared cost;
- current danger;
- alternative assignment;
- anti-discrimination appeal.

---

## 12. Identity, law and citizenship

Identity systems must separate:

- species/lineage;
- legal personhood;
- citizenship;
- health/service needs;
- current embodiment;
- political faction.

Rules:

1. species does not prove nationality;
2. citizenship does not prove environment compatibility;
3. species data is sensitive where it exposes health/reproduction;
4. mixed heritage/engineered lineage cannot be forced into false binary categories where current medicine/law can represent nuance;
5. emergency service eligibility must rely on current need and standing rather than species rank.

This integrates naturally with GA9’s existing linked-profile exclusion theme.

---

## 13. Language and translation

Do not solve everything with invisible perfect translation.

Translation layers:

- common trade/administrative languages;
- machine translation;
- certified legal/medical interpretation;
- local dialect/cultural context;
- non-auditory modalities;
- untranslatable or uncertain historical records.

Failure modes:

- literal meaning correct, legal scope wrong;
- biological signal interpreted as emotion;
- name/title compressed into one identity;
- machine model lacks rare species data;
- emergency warnings use inaccessible modality.

GA8 Archive/record war can use translation provenance without inventing a magic language barrier.

---

## 14. Military integration

Mixed-species forces require:

- compatible barracks/berths;
- food and medicine;
- suits/armor;
- cockpit/vehicle access;
- evacuation;
- acceleration planning;
- communication;
- rank/legal equivalence;
- funeral/missing-person handling.

Doctrinal consequences:

- some units specialize around a shared environment for efficiency;
- other units are deliberately mixed for legitimacy/skills;
- neither arrangement implies segregation is inherently good or bad; context matters.

No species receives a universal combat role.

---

## 15. Maneuver-frame and cockpit infrastructure

Possible shared solutions:

- adjustable pilot cradle;
- removable species-specific seat/restraint;
- alternate limb-control mapping;
- neural translation layer;
- remote/telepresence cockpit;
- local atmosphere capsule;
- temperature/fluid support;
- multi-sensory display packs.

New chassis only when adaptation repeatedly fails within shared frame architecture.

07 implication, proposal only:
- broad service interfaces can make 07-derived standards attractive for **maintenance interoperability** across heterogeneous fleets;
- this does not mean 07 cockpit itself fits every species or grants automatic cross-species neural compatibility.

---

## 16. Economy and inequality

Multi-species service creates real capital costs.

Potential inequalities:

- majority species receives cheap MS1 everywhere;
- rare species pays MS2 travel premium;
- employers avoid hiring people needing specialist environments;
- rural/frontier clinics cannot stock rare medicine;
- insurers classify environment support as optional;
- rich hubs advertise diversity while exporting service costs elsewhere.

Counterinstitutions:

- public accessibility funds;
- Neutral rescue guarantees;
- portable service rights;
- open adapter standards;
- mutual-aid clinics;
- species/community-run specialist centers;
- cross-subsidy in large transit hubs.

---

## 17. Disaster and environmental crisis

This architecture also covers non-war crises:

- water shortage;
- food crop collapse;
- atmosphere processor failure;
- temperature control failure;
- habitat contamination;
- drought/desertification on planetary settlements;
- gravity-system failure;
- epidemic affecting only some species/lineages;
- incompatible evacuation destinations.

Blind spot:
- a disaster can be “minor” for one species and lethal for another.

Therefore civil-defense plans need multi-species vulnerability maps.

---

## 18. Red-team stop conditions

Reject a multi-species scene/system if:

- infrastructure adapts instantly with no cost;
- rare species survives in an incompatible atmosphere for convenience;
- all mixed ships are universally compatible without modular volume/energy cost;
- perfect translation erases cultural/legal ambiguity;
- species-specific medicine appears only when plot needs a death;
- prison/military systems ignore physiology;
- one species is always the refugee/victim class;
- biological difference becomes a moral hierarchy;
- accessibility is portrayed as effortless or as impossible by default.

## 20. Current ruling

> **Multi-species diversity is not complete until its infrastructure burden is visible. This proposal makes coexistence a material part of the world rather than a character-design layer.**
