## 5. Provisional Personhood Presumption

A current agent receives provisional personhood protection when the evidence supports:
- a continuity claim or current self-model;
- current preferences and refusal capacity;
- ability to communicate, participate or appoint representation;
- interests not reducible to a temporary service process.

The presumption protects:
- bodily/substrate integrity;
- memory and privacy;
- representation;
- refusal and consent;
- claims and succession;
- death and loss recognition.

Final metaphysical or population-count doctrine remains open.
