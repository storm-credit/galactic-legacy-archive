# Episode 33 — 판결보다 빠른 경매

Date:
- CY 742-04-17.

Episode function:
- show Juno’s independent exit and its emotional incompleteness.
- turn 07’s scattered parts into separate acquisition problems.
- set a forty-eight-hour preservation/auction deadline.

## Scene 33.1 — Juno’s departure

POV:
- Rian.

Action:
- Juno leaves active Academy-cell status for the Neutral-supervised technical/education placement.
- Juno’s cellmates retain limited guarantees under the mutual-fund package.

Relationship:
- Rian offers future cooperation, not recruitment.
- Juno says survival does not erase that Rian’s cell received chances paid for by others.
- no reconciliation speech.

Reward:
- Juno remains alive, visible and autonomous.

Cost:
- Rian loses a capable rival/supporting leader from the Academy front.

## Scene 33.2 — Forty-eight hours

Nera receives:
- Helix notice that the detached radiator must be dismantled, recertified or sold because its current damaged state cannot remain in temporary evidence storage.
- Academy notice that the industrial arm/hull may be separated as salvage.
- Neutral notice that sensor evidence hardware can be released only with mirrored data safeguards.

Rian asks:
- why the hearing cannot freeze everything.

Answer:
- storage, safety, insurance and ownership processes continue unless a party posts cost/claim and accepts liability.

## Scene 33.3 — Component board

Haren creates a real acquisition board:

1. radiator — heat/cooling, Helix claim, debt path.
2. industrial arm — rescue/tool function, Academy/worker labor claim.
3. sensor/telemetry — evidence and navigation, Neutral conditions.
4. core — officially Imperial security custody.
5. secondary certified cooling/control manifold — small but essential for safe sustained use.

Conflict:
- Rian prioritizes core because without it the frame is not operable.
- Nera says a core without cooling, tools and maintainable controls is only an expensive hostage.
- Haren ranks by near-term ability to preserve people/mission rather than symbolic importance.

## Scene 33.4 — The auction moves

Action:
- assets are scheduled for viewing/transfer through Ring A, Dock D and Old Yard claim routes.
- several bidders/claimants enter: Helix, Academy, independent repair/salvage groups and contract security.

Hook:
- one auction transport route passes through an old external truss with weak jurisdiction/sensor coverage.
- Rian identifies it as a theft opportunity.

Reward:
- exact component targets and deadline.

Cost:
- no money/title sufficient for all parts.

# Episode 34 — 기체의 가격

Date:
- CY 742-04-18.

Episode function:
- separate technical value, legal title, labor contribution and future operational cost.
- keep Helix certification materially credible.
- recognize Nera/Bram worker contributions without granting whole-frame ownership.

## Scene 34.1 — Public inspection platform

POV:
- Rian at Ring A sponsor/claims inspection.

Physical display:
- detached radiator wing with burn/structural damage.
- industrial arm/hull records.
- damaged sensor package.
- certified manifold/control part.

Helix evaluator:
- demonstrates why the radiator cannot simply be bolted back on.
- identifies microfracture/channel risk that could kill pilot/workers.
- offers certified repair, inspection and warranty under debt/data terms.

Nera:
- agrees with the failure diagnosis but challenges exclusive ownership and labor erasure.

## Scene 34.2 — Who made it usable

Bram Ido and Academy workers:
- provide maintenance/incident logs showing years of local labor kept 07 components operational after formal value was written down.

Nera:
- identifies a family/workshop design mark and prior undocumented modification on the industrial arm or interface.

Legal effect:
- not enough to transfer title of the whole component.
- enough to establish labor/contributor and shared salvage-use claims.

Rian:
- initially treats logs as supporting evidence for “his frame.”
- corrects language when workers object.

## Scene 34.3 — A fair price under unequal choices

Helix offer:
- radiator repair/use now.
- future mission revenue/debt and inspection access.
- technical data and compliance obligations.

Academy offer:
- retain hull/arm, but student use only under school mission board.

Independent bidder:
- offers cash/salvage relief but intends to separate/resell components.

Neutral:
- sensor hardware can be refurbished if all evidence mirrors remain and privacy boundaries are respected.

Question:
- which constraint is survivable, not which actor is pure.

## Scene 34.4 — Core confidence

Imperial paperwork:
- lists core as received or expected at a security vault.
- prevents immediate auction but also prevents student inspection.

Rian:
- accepts temporary delay, assuming the core is safe.

Serin:
- notes the document states legal receipt/authority, not a physical scan.
- lacks case scope to challenge it yet.

End hook:
- auction schedule combines several parts into a single external transfer window, increasing sabotage/theft risk.

Reward:
- labor claims and technical truth.

Cost:
- credible Helix debt choice; core remains inaccessible.

# Episode 35 — 훔치면 누구 것이 되는가

Date:
- CY 742-04-19 to CY 742-04-20.

Episode function:
- compare theft with legal/technical dependency.
- build three acquisition paths and a constrained action plan.
- reveal the external convoy route is being manipulated.

## Scene 35.1 — Rian’s theft proposal

POV:
- Rian in Ring C workshop/mission planning.

Plan:
- use old service route and a utility frame to seize radiator/manifold during Old Yard transfer.
- hide parts long enough to reconstruct 07 and create bargaining leverage.

Why tempting:
- fast.
- avoids long debt.
- future knowledge suggests authorities may later seize everything anyway.

Nera objection:
- stolen radiator remains unsafe and unmaintainable without tools, certification and people.
- physical possession would erase worker/evidence claims and invite destructive pursuit.

Serin objection:
- breaks chain of custody and can invalidate White Dock evidence.

Haren objection:
- no sustainable place, supply or legal route for the resulting frame.

Rian withdraws the proposal as primary plan, not because theft is morally unthinkable but because it fails the actual goal.

## Scene 35.2 — Three acquisition paths

Path A — Radiator:
- assume limited repair debt/inspection with Helix.
- preserve right to challenge data/ownership terms later.

Path B — Industrial arm:
- prove Academy/worker salvage labor and essential education/rescue use.
- accept shared title/use rather than student ownership.

Path C — Sensor:
- refurbish under Neutral evidence mirroring and privacy/audit conditions.

Core:
- use Imperial security interest to delay permanent transfer; no current access.

Manifold:
- no affordable separate path if all other claims are pursued.

## Scene 35.3 — What can be abandoned

Haren:
- requires the cell to name one useful component they will not obtain.

Nera:
- selects the certified secondary cooling/control manifold as the loss.
- designs a lower-capacity documented bypass using existing Academy parts.

Consequence:
- 07 can be reconstructed later but retains lower heat endurance.
- the abandoned part may serve another buyer/system rather than being destroyed.

Rian:
- accepts a permanent performance limit created by resource ethics, not random damage.

## Scene 35.4 — False route clearance

Serin/White Dock worker evidence:
- finds auction convoy route changed through Old Yard under a routine “safety” authorization.
- the change benefits an opportunist bidder/insurer by moving assets into ambiguous recovery jurisdiction.

Kara:
- authorizes observation/asset-safety support but not seizure.

Mia:
- clears Rian only for short low-interface utility-frame operation, not 07/high-sync use.

End hook:
- the transfer tug leaves early with workers still attached to one maintenance pallet.

Reward:
- acquisition plan and chosen sacrifice.

Cost:
- debt/inspection accepted in principle; manifold abandoned.

# Episode 36 — 무중력 경매장

Date:
- CY 742-04-21.

Episode function:
- deliver action through salvage, towing and chain-of-custody rather than combat destruction.
- give Rian ace movement payoff in an unarmed utility frame.
- make Haren choose people/parts and Nera/Serin preserve claims.

## Scene 36.1 — Old Yard transfer

POV:
- Rian in a low-interface Academy utility frame.

Physical setup:
- radiator pallet, manifold/control pallet, worker/tool tug and related cargo move along old truss.
- Academy, Helix, salvage-broker and worker observers occupy separate craft/suits.
- destruction or unauthorized removal can void competing claims.

State:
- Rian has 5–7 minutes approved powered work and no combat weapons.
- Nera/worker teams are on external maintenance lines.
- Haren monitors mass, people and reachable trajectories.
- Serin records custody tags/commands.

## Scene 36.2 — The “accident”

Sabotage:
- a tug/anchor command releases or misaligns multiple pallets under the appearance of structural failure.
- opportunist actors intend assets to cross into recoverable salvage space or be damaged enough to trigger insurance transfer.

Immediate trajectories:
- radiator pallet threatens a truss/worker line.
- manifold drifts toward a bidder tug.
- worker/tool tug loses attitude control.

Rian cannot reach all three.

Haren choice:
- prioritize worker tug and radiator because the radiator’s mass/impact also threatens people/structure.
- let the manifold leave the team’s reachable plan.

## Scene 36.3 — Ace without a weapon

Action:
- Rian uses precise relative-motion timing, tether angles and minimal thrust to redirect the worker tug and catch/soften the radiator pallet.
- he does not perform future-machine acceleration beyond current utility limits.
- Nera/ordinary workers secure physical lines and complete attachment.

Independent action:
- one worker or Juno-associated external crew prevents the arm/hull documentation pallet from being detached elsewhere.

Cost:
- manifold enters another bidder’s custody/trajectory.
- Rian reaches medical/physical stop and cannot pursue.

## Scene 36.4 — Chain of custody under fire

Serin:
- preserves command/time/tag evidence showing the release was not a normal failure.
- refuses to label a final saboteur without physical/user proof.

Helix evaluator:
- helps stabilize the radiator because uncontrolled damage would hurt people and destroy their own claim.

Security:
- wants to seize all assets as crime evidence.

Kara/Neutral:
- use the public multi-party record to keep claims separated.

End hook:
- the manifold is legally captured by another bidder, while the saved radiator now carries a larger repair/liability bill.

Reward:
- worker tug and radiator preserved; Rian ace skill expressed through rescue mechanics.

Cost:
- vital component lost and debt rises.

# Episode 37 — 이긴 부품, 진 빚

Date:
- CY 742-04-22.

Episode function:
- convert action into legal/technical acquisition states.
- identify the limits of the sabotage proof.
- make every recovered part carry a different owner/condition.

## Scene 37.1 — Incident review, not victory parade

POV:
- Rian recovering from utility operation.

Facts:
- no clean proof of one mastermind.
- route authorization, tug command and bidder incentives point to several possible actors.
- workers and assets survived because multiple claimants cooperated for different reasons.

Rian:
- wants to use the incident to invalidate the whole auction.

Serin:
- says evidence supports route negligence/manipulation and specific custody corrections, not universal cancellation.

## Scene 37.2 — Radiator terms

Helix disposition:
- repairs and recertifies damaged radiator under a limited-use contract.
- team/Academy assume debt and inspection/data obligations.
- Helix cannot remotely deploy or own the whole frame.

Nera conditions:
- repair data and known limits visible to student/Academy maintainer.
- no silent update/control beyond stated safety scope.
- technical stop remains independent.

Helix gain:
- audit access, repayment and valuable performance data.

## Scene 37.3 — Arm and sensor terms

Industrial arm:
- Academy physical title remains.
- worker/Nera contributor history recognized.
- mission/rescue use cannot be reassigned or modified without contributor/maintenance record.

Sensor package:
- damaged unit is replaced/refurbished through Neutral/evidence release.
- telemetry mirrors remain with Neutral/White Dock case.
- private record access remains limited.

Result:
- the team gains use, not clean ownership.

## Scene 37.4 — The part they lost

Manifold/control component:
- sold/transferred to another bidder under valid claim after the Old Yard incident.
- team cannot reclaim it without breaking the new rules they demanded.

Nera:
- begins lower-capacity bypass plan.

Rian:
- accepts 07 will be weaker than the version he remembers.

End hook:
- final reconstruction schedule awaits only the core, which officials say is already in the Imperial vault.

Reward:
- radiator, arm and sensor paths secured.

Cost:
- debt, inspection, shared title and permanent heat limit.
