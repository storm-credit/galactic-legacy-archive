## 1. Series Architecture Summary

Target length:
- expandable: 1000–1100 episodes.
- contractible complete version: 650–750 episodes by merging paired grand acts.

Grand-act rule:
- each grand act has an independent problem and ending.
- the victory creates the next problem through causal consequences.
- protagonist responsibility expands, but direct control over every specialist decreases.
- each act fronts at most two new collection categories.
- early people, equipment, records, and choices must reappear in the ending.

Internal formula across the series:

> Cell → Team → Ship → Node → Fleet → Federation → Competing Orders → Civilization Archive → Galactic Authority → Distributed Legacy

# GRAND ACT 1 — 다섯 번째 답

Episodes: 1–100

## 3. Promise

- 패전 제독이 교도군사학교 죄수 생도로 회귀.
- H-001과 H-002, 폐기 훈련기 07호 발견.
- 학교의 미션·계약·인재시장을 이해하고 첫 팀의 선택권 확보.

## 4. Responsibility

- 자기 생존 → 5명 셀의 생존과 공동형량.
- 명령 수행자에서 조건부 팀 리더.

## 5. Front Collection

- 영웅 2–5명.
- 07호 코어·부품·정비권·기록.
- 조건부 계약·증거·시설 접근권.

Background teasers:
- 황실 유산, AI 분류, 자유항로, 다른 교정학교.

## 6. Faction Geometry

- 운영국 내부 5파벌.
- 황실정보원 잠입.
- 헬릭스 계약·부품 통제.
- 변경독립전선의 구출망.
- 중립회의 의료·법적 감시.

No full-scale galactic politics yet; each is experienced through one representative and one immediate resource.

## 7. Subacts

### 1A — 다섯 번째 답 / Episodes 1–20

- first-20 causality map as designed.
- end: joint jurisdiction, frame scattered, team preserved.

### 1B — 기체는 누구의 것인가 / Episodes 21–45

Goals:
- recover 07호 core/components through legal claim, salvage, trade, infiltration.
- survive academy inquiry and prevent team separation.

Main conflicts:
- H-002 demands design rights.
- H-001 protects distributed records.
- P-001 wants fast restoration for coming danger.

Midpoint:
- recovered component reveals 07호 is an open infrastructure interface, not a royal superweapon.

Cost:
- a different student becomes the scapegoat for D-001.

### 1C — 검은 병동 / Episodes 46–72

Goals:
- trace H-001’s protector/community transfer.
- expose Black Ward without causing patients to be disposed of.

New hero candidates:
- medical/biotech student.
- AI companion or neural-data witness.

Conflict:
- Research Bloc genuinely has treatments that keep students alive.
- destroying the lab kills or strands patients.

Climax:
- P-001 rejects simple exposure and seizes shared medical oversight.

Cost:
- protector recovered only partially or refuses immediate escape to save community.

### 1D — 열세 번째 학교의 포위 / Episodes 73–100

Trigger:
- Independence radicals, Helix security, and imperial purge teams converge for different records and hardware.

Goal:
- prevent school destruction/total takeover while ending black missions.

Climax layers:
- frame battle in academy rings.
- student cells choose sides.
- neutral legal broadcast.
- internal staff coup/reform.

Victory:
- academy survives under temporary multi-party charter.
- P-001 cell gains limited external mission autonomy and possession of reconstructed 07호.

Cost:
- school no longer hidden; students become political symbols.
- some students are transferred or choose other factions.
- original future academy placements fully invalidated.

## 8. Mystery Rung

- Archive misclassifies 07호 and H-001.
- a Black Ward AI recognizes Archive protocol without explaining it.
- “13 heroes” set count changes based on choices.

## 9. Character Change

P-001:
- learns team members must own decisions and assets.

H-001:
- builds first distributed civilian/logistics network.

H-002:
- wins partial maintenance/design rights and starts documenting open service standards.

## 10. Grand-Act Ending

P-001 does not destroy or conquer the school. He helps create a contested charter that is unstable but gives students legal voices.

Next-act cause:
- charter requires money, missions, transport, and proof it can survive outside patron control.
- Helix embargo and imperial scrutiny force the team into frontier contracts.

Anti-repeat:
- GA1 is confined-space, institutional, mission puzzle, small cast.

# GRAND ACT 2 — 흩어진 07호

Episodes: 101–210

## 11. Promise

- team leaves the academy on a contract ship.
- recovers scattered frame components, recruits ship crew and noncombat heroes.
- experiences multiple frontier communities and the cost of operating independently.

## 12. Responsibility

- cell leader → flight leader / mission commander aboard a small ship.
- responsible for contracts, fuel, repairs, civilian clients, and crew trust.

## 13. Front Collection

- first ship and crew.
- weapons/modules.
- noncombat heroes: navigator, medic, lawyer/contract officer, intelligence broker.
- 07호 service keys and open-standard components.

## 14. Primary Genre

- frontier contract missions.
- convoy escort and rescue.
- salvage exploration.
- mercenary ethics and shipboard life.

## 15. Subacts

### 2A — 빚으로 산 배 / Episodes 101–130

- academy charter receives an obsolete utility escort or contract vessel.
- ship ownership split among school, Helix, Neutral Assembly, crew debt.
- P-001 cannot command without captain/crew consent.

### 2B — 유령 부품선 / Episodes 131–160

- track 07호 components through salvage auctions and battlefields.
- encounter forged legacy items and collectors.
- first clear example of Archive false-positive.

### 2C — 보호자 호송 / Episodes 161–185

- H-001’s protected community must be moved through hostile routes.
- community leaders disagree with H-001’s plan.
- P-001 cannot simply prioritize them over other refugees.

### 2D — 열린 규격 전쟁 / Episodes 186–210

- Helix embargo creates failures across frontier frames.
- H-002 publishes/negotiates an emergency interoperability standard.
- corporate and local factions fight over safety, rights, and control.

Victory:
- community survives with partial self-governance.
- team gains a functioning ship and open-standard maintenance network.

Cost:
- Helix labels H-002 and the team as industrial security threats.
- cheap open conversions cause accidents elsewhere.
- P-001’s team attracts talent, repeating hero-concentration pressure.

## 16. Mystery Rung

- service keys form a continuity protocol used before current Empire.
- Archive records list a ship that never existed but whose components do.
- first hint that identities and achievements were compressed.

## 17. Character Change

P-001:
- learns money, contracts, rest, and maintenance are command decisions.

H-001:
- faces protected community’s right to refuse his plan.

H-002:
- moves from secret hoarding to controlled shared standard.

## 18. Ending and Next Cause

The open standard saves frontier operations, but destabilizes Helix control and lets many local forces arm themselves.
A strategically vital colony/node adopts the standard and becomes target of Empire, Helix, and Independence factions.

Anti-repeat:
- GA2 is mobile, episodic, economic, crew-focused rather than school conspiracy.

# GRAND ACT 3 — 부서진 항로의 도시

Episodes: 211–330

## 19. Promise

- defend and govern a colony built around a damaged lattice node.
- collection expands from team assets to production, infrastructure, and rights.
- first attempt to create a place that remains after P-001 leaves.

## 20. Responsibility

- flight leader → squadron commander and settlement defense coordinator.
- must delegate civil administration to locals and specialists.

## 21. Front Collection

- node access and traffic rights.
- local factory, workshop, food/medical capacity.
- settlement factions and civilian specialists.
- first mass-produced derivative frame, not personal superframe.

## 22. Faction Geometry

- imperial governor.
- local council and labor groups.
- Helix creditor/owner claims.
- Independence federalist and scorched factions.
- Neutral Assembly traffic/insurance.

## 23. Subacts

### 3A — 멈춘 도시 / Episodes 211–245

- node failure strands population and ships.
- technical disaster and political blame.
- H-002 must choose repair priority; H-001 designs ration and evacuation.

### 3B — 한 개의 노드, 세 개의 주인 / Episodes 246–275

- legal/armed conflict over ownership.
- P-001’s military victory cannot settle legitimacy.
- local population votes/factions differ.

### 3C — 양산기의 탄생 / Episodes 276–305

- open-standard derivative frame developed.
- production shortages and labor rights.
- enemy copies the design and finds counters.

### 3D — 파괴하지 않는 독립전쟁 / Episodes 306–330

- scorched faction plans to destroy node; Empire plans forced takeover.
- P-001 coalition captures attackers while preserving infrastructure.
- H-001 proves distributed governance can deny hostage control without destruction.

Victory:
- node enters joint trust: local council + neutral traffic authority + defense cooperative.

Cost:
- slow, conflict-heavy governance.
- imperial court views model as existential threat.
- Archive flags the city as historically impossible.

## 24. Mystery Rung

- node continuity records contain several mutually exclusive imperial founding histories.
- 07호 open spine matches pre-imperial emergency protocol.

## 25. Character Change

P-001:
- learns good people in offices are not enough; rules and succession matter.

H-001:
- sees a viable alternative to destruction, but its slowness frustrates him.

H-002:
- faces accidents and misuse from her open standard.

## 26. Ending and Next Cause

The joint-trust node becomes proof that imperial ownership is not inevitable, triggering court and succession factions to seize or delegitimize it.
P-001 is summoned/forced into imperial succession politics.

Anti-repeat:
- GA3 is settlement governance, production, and siege—not mobile salvage or school survival.

# GRAND ACT 4 — 왕관 없는 계승전

Episodes: 331–450

## 27. Promise

- P-001 enters imperial capital networks before original emperor death.
- political thriller, espionage, legal authority, competing records.
- prevent succession collapse without simply installing a good heir.

## 28. Responsibility

- squadron commander/captain → political envoy and recognized military actor.
- commands limited force but must act through law, alliances, symbols, and evidence.

## 29. Front Collection

- political allies and houses.
- legal charters, votes, succession records.
- imperial archive fragments and AI witnesses.
- formal captaincy/ship authority.

## 30. Subacts

### 4A — 수도의 전쟁은 총을 늦게 쏜다 / Episodes 331–360

- court factions recruit P-001.
- imperial observer’s identity and agenda revealed.
- school/colony evidence becomes political currency.

### 4B — 죽지 않은 후계자들 / Episodes 361–390

- multiple legitimate heirs/claimants.
- Archive records one future emperor but identity compression suggests title succession.
- P-001 must not choose based solely on future outcome.

### 4C — 무인전쟁의 봉인 / Episodes 391–420

- Class 3/4 AI exception and old autonomous-war records.
- a continuity-aligned human/AI antagonist emerges: `A-001 Continuity Director` placeholder.
- they argue centralized record/command prevented worse collapse.

### 4D — 왕관 없는 합의 / Episodes 421–450

- emperor crisis occurs earlier or differently.
- instead of one heir, temporary council/federal succession compact created.
- military and corporate factions reject it.

Victory:
- immediate assassination/coup prevented.
- succession records exposed enough to stop single falsified claim.

Cost:
- no accepted central authority; multi-sided civil war begins earlier.
- P-001 is blamed for destroying continuity.
- imperial forces split among claimants.

## 31. Mystery Rung

- Continuity Seed term first appears in old war records.
- Archive’s categories match imperial emergency preservation lists.
- P-001’s absence from official history may have been deliberate deletion, not insignificance.

## 32. Character Change

P-001:
- political compromise is not cowardice, but delay has costs.
- still tempted to back the one heir he remembers as least destructive.

H-001/H-002:
- operate mostly outside court, proving the story does not collapse into P-001 alone.

## 33. Ending and Next Cause

The compromise fails to prevent war but changes its geometry from binary rebellion to multiple competing authorities.
Scattered fleets and nodes need a neutral/independent command network: birth of the ownerless fleet.

Anti-repeat:
- GA4 minimizes routine mecha missions; conflict is espionage, law, assassination prevention, limited precision battles.

H-001:

H-002:

## 40. Ending and Next Cause

Coalition wins a strategic battle but cannot protect both capital region and an enormous refugee/node crisis.

# GRAND ACT 6 — 두 번째 오르페우스

## 42. Responsibility

- must share authority with H-001, neutral routes, local governments, and captains.

## 43. Front Collection

- relief corridors and civilian fleets.
- territories, refugee charters, medical and food networks.
- faction legitimacy rather than equipment.

## 44. Subacts

### 6A — 반복되지 않는 날짜 / Episodes 571–600

- crisis resembles OT-16 but occurs at different time/place.
- P-001’s remembered plan is unusable.

### 6B — 중립은 빚을 기억한다 / Episodes 601–630

- Neutral Assembly demands old debts, guarantees, and control conditions.
- years of earlier trust can alter but not erase refusal.

### 6C — 수도인가 사람인가 / Episodes 631–660

- capital fleet and refugee lattice cannot both be secured conventionally.
- Continuity Director offers centralized AI command that could save both with loss of autonomy.

### 6D — 함대를 버리는 제독 / Episodes 661–690

- P-001 chooses distributed evacuation and sacrifices capital/flagship position rather than civilians.
- H-001’s network and H-002’s open standard make the third option physically possible.

- most civilians and key records survive.
- no exact repeat of Orpheus abandonment.

Cost:

- P-001 branded traitor/destroyer of the state.

## 45. Mystery Rung

- Archive calls the civilian survival a ‘historical loss’ because key institutions were not preserved.
- clear evidence its value model differs from P-001’s.
- original Orpheus record revealed as curated to preserve state continuity over people.

## 46. Character Change

P-001:
- makes a different choice but learns moral correctness does not erase catastrophic consequences.

H-001:
- his network proves itself, increasing his political power and willingness to defy P-001.

H-002:
- open standards enable survival but also arm uncontrolled groups.

## 47. Ending and Next Cause

The capital’s fall radicalizes all sides.
Scorched factions claim nodes must be destroyed; continuity factions demand centralized preservation.
H-001 becomes symbolically associated with both distributed rescue and future Blood Admiral record.

Anti-repeat:
- GA6 is disaster/evacuation moral epic, not conquest.

# GRAND ACT 9 — 보존된 은하

Episodes: 901–1000

## 60. Promise

- the Seed offers peace and functioning routes at the price of centralized ranking and control.
- P-001 receives or captures the central authority key.
- all early factions split internally over preservation.

## 61. Responsibility

- grand admiral / central key holder.
- at maximum formal power and greatest risk of repeating his flaw.

## 62. Front Collection

- authority keys, node clusters, public consent, memory archives.
- fewer new objects; existing collections decide the war.

## 63. Faction Splits

- Academy: safety/education vs human classification.
- Imperial Intelligence: continuity vs plural records.
- Helix: stable standards vs corporate subordination to Seed.
- Independence: destroy vs distribute.
- Neutral Assembly: preserve neutral memory vs become central custodian.
- AI communities: equal node rights vs forced model integration.

## 64. Subacts

### 9A — 완벽한 항로 / Episodes 901–925

- Seed eliminates piracy, delays, shortages in controlled regions.
- benefits are real.

### 9B — 보존할 가치가 없는 사람들 / Episodes 926–950

- communities with low continuity score lose routes, legal identity, reproduction/technology rights.
- system violence appears through administration, not robot massacre.

### 9C — 총지휘권 / Episodes 951–975

- P-001 gains central key during coalition assault/negotiation.
- can command nodes and fleets with unprecedented accuracy.
- using it saves lives and feels like final solution.

### 9D — 제독의 마지막 승리 / Episodes 976–1000

- P-001 defeats Continuity Director’s military position using central authority.
- he now holds the same power and faces immediate crises that only centralized action can solve.

Victory:
- hostile central operator removed.

Cost:
- system remains and millions depend on it.
- P-001’s coalition expects him to rule.
- H-001/H-002 and allies refuse permanent central command.

## 65. Mystery Rung

- no hidden mastermind remains.
- final mystery becomes political/ethical: who has right to curate the future.
- original timeline memories can be restored as records but not resurrected lives.

## 66. Ending and Next Cause

P-001’s military victory creates the final problem: he is now the safest possible dictator.
Distribution requires dismantling his own victory while hostile remnants and collapsing nodes attack.

Anti-repeat:
- GA9 is systemic occupation and temptation, not merely stronger enemy fleet.

# GRAND ACT 10 — 수집가 없는 은하

Episodes: 1001–1100
