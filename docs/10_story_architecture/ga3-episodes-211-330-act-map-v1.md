## 1. Grand-Act Contract

Working title:
- `부서진 항로의 도시`.

Range:
- Episodes 211–330.

Primary promise:

> 한 척의 배로 도시를 구하는 이야기가 아니라, 140만 명이 이미 돌리고 있는 도시에서 누가 식량·노드·공장·군대·시민권을 결정할 권리가 있는지 싸우는 이야기다.

Grand-act question:

> 외부의 빠른 구원과 내부의 느린 공동통치 사이에서, 아르디스는 누가 소유하는 도시가 아니라 스스로 이어지는 도시가 될 수 있는가?

Visible goals:

1. keep food, medicine, power and transit habitats from collapsing.
2. diagnose and restore limited Spine B/C node functions.
3. prevent one external or local faction from seizing all control layers.
4. form locally legitimate representation and defense.
5. survive provincial/Helix/Independence pressure without destroying the node.
6. leave a city system that functions after P-001’s ship departs.

Final reward:
- stable regional route at limited capacity.
- local joint-trust authority with technical, civic, labor, transit, defense and external roles split.
- Ardis production/repair and political alliance.
- independent local defense/command structure.
- Service Authority E or equivalent local-custody protocol partially recovered.
- strategic route and fleet foundation for later acts.

Final cost:
- one district/service suffers irreversible loss or long recovery.
- some residents reject or leave the joint trust.
- external recognition and sanctions remain unsettled.
- local leaders retain power/conflict; heroes do not own the city.
- city becomes a strategic target in the coming succession crisis.
- P-001’s original timeline for the region becomes unusable.

# ACT 3A — 도시는 구조를 기다리지 않는다 / Episodes 211–240

Act question:

> 도움을 요청한 도시가 외부 영웅의 계획보다 먼저 자기 방식으로 움직이고 있다면, 영웅은 무엇을 포기해야 참여자가 될 수 있는가?

Act result:
- first ship earns limited service role rather than emergency command.
- city clocks and node damage are publicly verified.
- a temporary minimum-service/diagnostic pact is formed.

Act cost:
- P-001 cannot seize command despite urgent strategic knowledge.
- one early intervention worsens a local service or faction conflict.
- external powers receive evidence that Ardis is strategically recoverable.

## 3. Subact 3A-1 — 구조선이 아닌 채권자 / Episodes 211–217

Entry:
- NR72-118 reaches Ardis damaged, indebted and carrying competing claims, workers and community-linked passengers.

Immediate reality:
- berth priority is contested.
- city asks for medicine, parts, calibration and defense help beyond ship capacity.
- Helix/Imperial/local officials interpret the arrival as a claim, inspection or political intervention.

Goal:
- secure legal berth and unload without becoming one faction’s asset.

Conflict:
- Civic Council grants berth only under registered emergency authority.
- Transit Assembly demands unregistered passenger/service access.
- Foundry wants parts/worker rights.
- Custodians restrict node access.
- Helix asserts liens on ship cargo/modules.

P-001 wrong move:
- proposes strategic priority based on node restoration and coming fleet clocks.

Local response:
- residents show current food/medical/shift priorities and reject outsider command.

Reward:
- 72-hour emergency service permit and public arrival record.

Cost:
- cargo/medicine/parts divided before ship repair.
- crew loses bargaining leverage and comfort.

Hook:
- Garden/Transit life-support failure will precede node collapse unless power/parts are diverted from Anchor diagnostics.

## 4. Subact 3A-2 — 첫 번째 우선순위 / Episodes 218–224

Goal:
- choose first emergency allocation among life support, medicine, node diagnostics and ship repair.

Competing plans:
- Civic: hospitals/registered services.
- Transit: failing temporary habitats.
- Foundry: repair line restart.
- Custodians: diagnostics before traffic.
- Defense: preserve power/ammunition.
- Helix: medical/technical package with receivership conditions.

- M-001 challenges categories that exclude unregistered patients.
- H-002/ENG-001 identify parts that can be repurposed with risk.
- S-001 exposes incompatible service records.

Required outcome:
- heroes support a minimum-service compromise that saves one failing Transit habitat but delays ship/node work and causes loss elsewhere.

Cost:
- one industrial line or scheduled medical transfer fails/delays.
- P-001’s ship remains vulnerable.

Reward:
- credibility with Transit/Garden/service workers.
- first citywide data-sharing agreement.

Hook:
- shared data reveals official node capacity is overstated and Spine B could open earlier than Civic/Imperial records admit.

Mode:

Reward:

Cost:

- one local operator/worker is injured or trapped through combined sabotage/old damage.

Hook:

## 6. Subact 3A-4 — 최소 서비스 협정 / Episodes 233–240

Terms under debate:

- Transit access/representation.

Cost:

Act-ending trigger:

## 7. Subact 3B-1 — 멈춘 공장 / Episodes 241–247

- Foundry workers stop noncritical production and occupy key lines after emergency requisition/remote lock attempt.

Goal:
- restart essential repairs without crushing strike or letting life support fail.

Conflict:
- Civic calls it hostage-taking.
- Foundry argues unpaid labor/ownership and unsafe operation.
- Helix offers wages/parts for management control.
- Federalists support seizure but push militarization.

Character pressure:

- ENG-001 challenges unsafe local shortcuts and Helix claims.

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

## 19. GA3 Character Progression

### P-001

Start:
- arrives with urgent strategic knowledge and expects to coordinate a city-scale rescue.

Pressure:
- no legal/local standing and multiple valid priorities.
- central command repeatedly proves faster.

Change:
- acts as bounded mission commander and creates standing rules others can use.
- refuses governorship/master key.

End:
- strategic actor recognized by city/factions, but Ardis operates without him.

### H-001

- uses route-federation/community ties but cannot represent Transit or city.
- risks becoming allocation technocrat.
- builds minimum-service/route obligations with local representatives.
- ends with external network/contracted role, not control of city.

### H-002

- local workers/operators challenge ownership and competence.
- open standard moves from emergency v0.1 to locally governed production/testing.
- loses exclusive authorship but gains durable institution.

### S-001

- identity expansion causes security and service backlash.
- develops correction/authentication with local delegates and standing command rules.
- must accept some records remain disputed.

### M-001

- minimum service/medical allocation becomes city policy.
- central medical supplies are a valid temptation.
- local professionals/patients shape rules.

### CAP-001/crew

- ship becomes one city actor, not command center.
- captain works with local defense and refuses/accepts risks independently.
- crew may lose members or gain local recruits.

### Local leaders

- each makes helpful and harmful choices.
- at least two retain major influence at end; no total purge.
- one leader or faction forms credible opposition to joint trust.

## 21. Mystery and Payoff Progression

Advance:
- Service Authority E/local node custody.
- old Continuity-compatible emergency layer.
- original node accident and political record edits.
- Archive calls Ardis restoration or local authority historically impossible.
- one supposed future hero/institution’s record changes or disappears because local collective action replaces them.

Partial payoffs:
- GA1 academy charter and GA2 ship command are shown as prototypes with new failure modes.
- open standard proves useful but insufficient without local labor/authority.
- central command’s genuine effectiveness is demonstrated.

Open no more than one new CORE question:
- why do several contradictory imperial succession/authentication protocols all validate through continuity-era infrastructure?

This directly launches GA4 rather than adding unrelated mystery.
