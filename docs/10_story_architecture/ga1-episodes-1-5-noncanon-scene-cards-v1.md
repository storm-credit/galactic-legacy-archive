# Episode 1 — 역사에 없는 생도

## Episode promise

A future admiral wakes in his correctional-cadet body and recognizes the discarded frame 07, but the first person he saves is someone his perfect future memory does not remember.

## Opening image

Academy intake cadets cross the frame hangar while workers mark machines for reuse, sponsor transfer or stripping.
07 carries a red disposal band.

## POV state

Rian knows the original future emotionally and strategically, but present sensory/body information comes first.
He is weak, young and not licensed.

## Scene 1 — Intake line / Hangar threshold

Goal:
- pass intake without drawing attention and determine the date.

Obstacle:
- seeing 07 triggers future memory and physical sync response.

Action:
- Rian compares present 07 to the burned future machine.
- Archive warning appears: `당신은 역사에 기록되지 않았습니다.`

Explanation allowed:
- correctional cadets are passing through equipment allocation; 07 is scheduled for strip.

Value change:
- from “I returned with an advantage” to “the system does not recognize me, but 07 does something familiar.”

## Scene 2 — Actuator/coolant failure

Goal:
- prevent immediate injury without exposing impossible knowledge.

Obstacle:
- a fixed maintenance arm/07 shoulder assembly begins collapsing toward a worker/cadet.
- Rian has no authority and a weaker body.

Action:
- he shouts a precise physical instruction and moves before staff react.
- an ordinary dock worker changes the lock sequence and completes the save.
- Rian takes a minor impact/sync shock.

Key detail:
- he remembers that “someone” lost a hand here, but not the name.

Value change:
- future knowledge saves a person but reveals its dehumanizing gaps.

## Scene 3 — Immediate institutional consequence

Goal:
- avoid medical isolation/security detention.

Obstacle:
- Rian touched/approached restricted equipment and predicted a failure.
- Nera notices his instruction used a service interface term cadets should not know.

Action:
- Rian claims he read the maintenance label/geometry.
- staff flag him for review and tie the event to the intake cell’s evaluation.

Reward:
- 07 stripping is paused for incident inspection, not saved permanently.

Cost:
- Rian becomes a monitored anomaly and may cause collective penalty.

End hook:
- in the powered-down cockpit, a service light answers his presence with an old authority handshake rather than a pilot-recognition salute.

Reader questions:
- why does a discarded training frame know him?
- why is he absent from history?
- who bears the collective penalty?

# Episode 2 — 한 사람의 벌이 서른여섯 명에게 간다

## Episode promise

Rian’s heroic-looking intervention threatens Haren’s protected community because Academy punishment and external guarantees are bundled.

## Scene 1 — Intake review

Goal:
- explain the accident without revealing regression.

Obstacle:
- Security/Operations wants a simple violation category.
- Education/Field staff argue the save matters.

Action:
- Serin, still a cadet records assistant, notices the incident log attributes the dock worker’s final action to Rian alone.
- Rian corrects it, losing some individual credit but preserving evidence.

Institution change:
- Serin becomes interested in how records create liability/heroism.

## Scene 2 — Haren confrontation

Goal:
- learn why his action affects strangers.

Obstacle:
- Haren is angry because cell penalties can lower contract value and freeze part of the Doran community guarantee.

Action:
- Haren lists concrete effects: medicine renewal, ration date, identity review and one transfer deadline.
- Rian initially treats it as leverage the future enemy used; Haren forces him to name current people.

Voice contrast:
- Rian asks probability/cost.
- Haren asks who misses treatment first.

Value change:
- Haren moves from “future Blood Admiral target” to present person with obligations and independent anger.

## Scene 3 — The transfer field

Goal:
- find a temporary way to prevent immediate account freeze.

Obstacle:
- only an authorized cell/instructor can accept collective evaluation risk.

Action:
- Rian proposes that the accident be classified as a joint safety intervention requiring a practical re-test, not individual sabotage.
- Haren refuses to become Rian’s grateful dependent but agrees because it buys time.

Cost:
- Rian, Haren and others are provisionally grouped for a re-test; one failure can now harm all of them.

End hook:
- the re-test equipment assignment includes 07, because its incident state must be reproduced before stripping.

# Episode 3 — 조종사는 기체를 고칠 수 없다

## Episode promise

Rian may know how 07 fights in the future, but Nera is the person who decides whether it can move today.

## Scene 1 — 07 inspection

Goal:
- make 07 pass a basic movement/safety test.

Obstacle:
- missing parts, mixed standards and an inaccurate disposal report.

Action:
- Rian gives a future-derived fix order.
- Nera rejects it because present 07 lacks the later replacement assembly and the proposed load will break a current mount.

Autonomous correction:
- Nera proves it with a physical measurement/test and is right.

Value change:
- Rian’s future expertise is contextual, not absolute.

## Scene 2 — Rights before repair

Goal:
- get Nera to help.

Obstacle:
- Helix/Academy will absorb undocumented student repair work and blame Nera for failure.

Action:
- Nera demands her work be logged under her name and refuses to sign whole-frame safety.
- Serin creates a provisional contributor/change log.
- Haren calculates the time/parts cost.

Institution seed:
- technical authorship and plural maintenance responsibility.

## Scene 3 — Service coupler

Action:
- while replacing a common line, Nera finds a civilian rescue/service coupler beneath the military training interface.
- it responds more deeply than the pilot controls.

Reward:
- 07 can stand/move under strict limitations.

Cost:
- weapons/power remain restricted; the re-test will emphasize rescue/manipulation rather than combat.

End hook:
- the service coupler carries the same unfamiliar protocol family that appeared in Rian’s Archive warning.

# Episode 4 — 오르페우스의 빈칸

## Episode promise

Rian tries to use the future to prevent the next failure, but the memory that matters most is missing and the attempt physically harms him.

## Scene 1 — Re-test briefing

Goal:
- predict the test hazard and secure a safe route.

Obstacle:
- future memory supplies ship/fleet consequences but not the current trainee/worker details.

Action:
- Rian sees a brief original-Orpheus memory: official State convoy, missing civilian fields and a route-window countdown.
- he experiences sync symptoms.

No exposition:
- the reader sees fragments and body response, not full Orpheus history.

## Scene 2 — Mia medical check

Goal:
- avoid being removed from the test while remaining safe.

Obstacle:
- Mia identifies neurological/sensory abnormalities and refuses his “I’m fine.”

Action:
- Rian provides enough truth about involuntary memory/sensory episodes without revealing full regression.
- Mia establishes a stop condition and keeps the medical record bounded.

Relationship change:
- medical authority is not subordinate to his tactical knowledge.

## Scene 3 — Wrong prediction, useful principle

Action:
- Rian predicts the exact failure location incorrectly because the present maintenance state changed.
- his broader warning about load transfer is right.
- Nera/Haren/Mia adapt it into a current plan.

Reward:
- team avoids one failure mode.

Cost:
- another risk appears; Rian’s reputation becomes “useful but unreliable,” not prophet.

End hook:
- Archive count briefly shows thirteen hero slots, then twelve, after one ordinary worker is added to the active safety chain.

# Episode 5 — 다섯 번째 답의 시작

## Episode promise

The Academy tries to classify the cell through obedience, betrayal or sacrificial failure; the group creates a fifth process that saves the test but binds them together under harsher public responsibility.

## Scene 1 — Closed choice

Test setup:
- 07 must move a damaged training module/person through a simulated White Dock corridor.
- rules allow:
  1. obey route and abandon the module/person;
  2. violate route and accept sabotage classification;
  3. one cadet assumes individual blame/failure.

Goal:
- complete safety test and preserve Haren’s guarantee/07 inspection.

## Scene 2 — Fifth process

Action:
- Rian shares the real risk instead of choosing alone.
- Haren splits manifest/obligation.
- Nera changes the tool configuration under logged limits.
- Serin records the rule conflict and source.
- Mia sets a body/time stop.
- the dock worker/other ordinary actor owns one physical step.

Outcome:
- the group completes a slower partial rescue and fails the official route/performance metric.

No perfect victory:
- one equipment asset is damaged/lost.
- another team or schedule bears a cost.
- institutional conflict becomes public.

## Scene 3 — Consequence

Academy response:
- cannot call it simple success or betrayal.
- forms a provisional joint-evaluation cell and assigns continuing observation.
- 07 stripping remains paused only while the cell proves value and accepts liability.

Reward:
- core opening team and practical access to 07.

Cost:
- their failures are now linked; sponsors/institutions begin competing for data/control.

End hook:
- White Dock’s real external test notice arrives earlier than Rian remembers.
