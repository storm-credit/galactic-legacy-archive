## 1. Grand-Act Contract

Working title:
- `보존된 은하`.

Range:
- Episodes 901–1000.

Primary promise:

> Seed 체제가 실제로 병원·급여·신분·항로·군령을 회복해 환영받는 가운데, 그 안정이 누구의 이동·기억·기술·정치적 거부를 조건부로 만드는지 보여준다.

Grand-act question:

> 수십억 명이 의존하는 안정된 체제가 권리를 침해할 때, P-001은 그것을 파괴하거나 빼앗는 대신 어떤 권한을 어디까지 행사하고 넘겨야 하는가?

Visible goals:

1. show the regime’s benefits and voluntary expansion through named lives/regions.
2. identify how review, classification and cascade powers become permanent exclusion.
3. preserve appeals, plural mirrors, local/AI/technical rights and opt-out routes.
4. prevent sabotage/local abuse from validating unrestricted forced synchronization.
5. split regime supporters and institutions rather than treat them as one enemy.
6. defeat or remove the coercive military/operational monopoly.
7. gain enough central Seed access to stop immediate catastrophe without keeping it as personal rule.

Final reward:
- coercive hardliner/operator and single-command military control defeated or bounded.
- plural mirrors, local nodes, reformers, partner institutions and beneficiary communities survive.
- P-001 obtains real central corrective/command capacity capable of stabilizing transition.
- evidence/classification maps and distributed-key paths needed for GA10 are secured.

Final cost:
- central system remains active because billions depend on it.
- P-001 becomes the safest and most trusted central operator.
- some synchronized regions demand he retain control.
- resistance/opt-out failures cause named deaths and renewed fear.
- low-confidence/excluded people and destroyed technologies cannot all be restored.
- A-001/Continuity institution remains useful and politically legitimate even if leadership/force is defeated.

# ACT 9A — 체제가 살린 사람들 / Episodes 901–925

Act question:

> 체제가 실제로 사람을 살리고 일상을 되돌렸다면, 그 규칙을 거부할 권리는 누가 어떤 비용으로 주장할 수 있는가?

Act result:
- regime benefits and support base established without irony.
- classification domains/status become visible through current lives.
- P-001 begins formal/conditional corrective role.

Act cost:
- review/deferred populations and routes suffer out of public view.
- successful exceptions increase P-001/Seed dependence.
- voluntary synchronization begins conditioning neighbors and partners.
