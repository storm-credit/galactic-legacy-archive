# Episode 27 — 서로 다른 증언

Date:
- CY 742-04-09.

Episode function:
- make contradictory testimony a strength and legal weakness.
- expose Kara’s limited protection and the Academy’s live-evaluation overlay.
- keep Juno’s confession formally effective despite obvious doubt.

## Scene 27.1 — The hearing room

POV:
- Rian.

Physical/social setup:
- Ring A visitor court/hearing room.
- Academy officials, Neutral auditor, White Dock witness, Helix evaluator and Security observer occupy separate authority positions.
- students remain under guard and medical restrictions.

Goal:
- prevent the inquiry from collapsing into a single accusation of collusion.

Problem:
- the chair asks for one unified sequence and one responsible commander.

Rian:
- begins to answer as if delivering an after-action report.
- Haren interrupts to preserve separate testimony promised on the Neutral deck.

## Scene 27.2 — Contradictions on record

Testimonies:
- Rian admits blocking a Security firing line and abandoning Ern pursuit.
- Haren states his route plan did not prioritize the same target as Academy command.
- Nera states releasing the radiator was not the only imaginable option, only the one preserving people within the remaining time/heat.
- Serin states her external hashes prove sequence but not every person’s intent.
- Mia states Rian should have been stopped earlier by a stricter medical rule.
- Juno states their own confession remains unchanged.

Effect:
- no clean heroic story and no clean conspiracy story.
- officials use contradictions to challenge credibility.

Reward:
- individual agency remains visible.

Cost:
- collective legal defense weakens.

## Scene 27.3 — Kara’s allocation

Kara Ven:
- confirms the mission included a live betrayal/priority evaluation layered over real White Dock security work.
- admits she reassigned Juno to WD-F/WD-A and therefore knows Juno’s physical position.

Harmful truth:
- Kara previously protected Rian’s cell’s chance to deploy by accepting lower support/resources for other cells.
- Juno’s cell entered White Dock with less equipment/credit partly because of that allocation.

Juno response:
- refuses to let Kara’s testimony turn Juno into another student the instructor “saves” selectively.

Outcome:
- hearing expands into mission-allocation and credential review.
- Kara’s authority is restricted pending review.

## Scene 27.4 — Effective confession

Security position:
- Juno’s confession, signed under valid internal procedure, remains enough for transfer/detention unless contrary evidence is authenticated.

Neutral limitation:
- suspicion is not proof; Neutral cannot cancel Academy sentence/transfer based on moral doubt.

Rian:
- wants to prove physical impossibility immediately.

Juno:
- says, “하지 마. 내가 빠지면 나머진 남아.”

End hook:
- Juno’s transfer order includes protection/placement for the rest of Juno’s cell if the confession is not challenged.

Reward:
- inquiry scope broadens.

Cost:
- saving Juno may punish others.

# Episode 28 — 기록은 알리바이가 아니다

Date:
- CY 742-04-10.

Episode function:
- investigate through lawful access and physical records, not magical hacking.
- reveal Serin’s delegated credentials and reporting complicity.
- establish why an impossible confession can still govern real outcomes.

## Scene 28.1 — Bounded audit access

POV:
- Rian accompanying/observing Serin under hearing authorization.

Location:
- Ring A audit deck and secured terminal.

Access conditions:
- Serin receives read-only access to specified Academy/White Dock authentication events.
- Neutral supplies time hashes.
- White Dock supplies physical lock logs.
- Academy Security withholds personnel-routing details.

Goal:
- compare Juno’s physical route with the credential event.

Constraint:
- Serin cannot query unrelated Doran/Black Ward/student files without exposing or violating the case scope.

## Scene 28.2 — A credential can move without a person

Findings:
- Juno’s credential appears in a route/data event after Juno’s cell was recorded and witnessed at WD-F.
- the event used a delegated/replayed token from an earlier shared training/equipment session.
- a Security service account validated the replay as routine rather than anomalous.

Serin distinction:
- this proves Juno did not personally perform the recorded access at that time.
- it does not prove who ordered/replayed it or that Juno never shared information earlier.

Rian:
- presses for the simplest exoneration statement.

Serin:
- refuses to turn a limited finding into a complete innocence narrative.

## Scene 28.3 — Serin’s old reports

Discovery:
- Serin’s delegated student-audit role previously supplied routine behavior/technical reports.
- one of those reports made a student/cell easier to classify or transfer.
- she did not know the full consequence but benefited from access/protection.

Conflict:
- Security threatens to revoke access and frame Serin as the true data source.
- Serin can protect herself by treating the replay as Juno’s negligence.

Choice:
- she preserves her own complicity in the audit record.

Cost:
- suspension/discipline risk and trust damage.

## Scene 28.4 — The protected remainder

Haren obtains the practical confession terms:
- Juno accepts sole responsibility.
- Juno’s cellmates retain placement, treatment and family-account review.
- challenging the confession reopens collective penalties.

Mia:
- identifies at least one medically vulnerable student in the remainder.

Rian realization:
- factual exoneration without replacement support can harm the people Juno chose to protect.

End hook:
- Juno’s transfer date is moved forward before the expanded audit can conclude.

Reward:
- physical impossibility and credential replay proven.

Cost:
- Serin exposed; collective-coercion structure revealed.

# Episode 29 — 구하지 말라는 사람

Date:
- CY 742-04-11.

Episode function:
- give Juno independent motives and grievance.
- prevent Rian from solving the case by unilateral rescue or moral argument.
- connect student scarcity to Rian’s protected opportunity.

## Scene 29.1 — Detention visit

POV:
- Rian.

Access:
- short monitored meeting granted through inquiry/medical request.

Rian goal:
- obtain Juno’s permission to challenge the confession.

Juno goal:
- keep the remaining cell’s guarantees intact.

Conflict:
- Rian argues the confession creates a reusable method for punishing future students.
- Juno argues Rian’s cell repeatedly receives extra review, equipment time and adult advocates while other cells are asked to absorb the cost.

Juno line of attack:
- Rian calls collective sacrifice unjust only when he can see the named person.

## Scene 29.2 — What Juno protected

Facts:
- Juno’s cell includes students with a medical placement, family/account guarantee and one external technical contract.
- Security/Operations offered to preserve those outcomes if one leader accepts the data/route charge.
- Juno does not believe a public inquiry can protect all of them in time.

Agency:
- Juno did not confess because of mind control or simple fear.
- it is a coercive but strategic choice under real constraints.

Mia/Haren perspective:
- consent is compromised by linked hostages but not meaningless.
- replacing the decision requires replacing the guarantees.

## Scene 29.3 — Rian’s bad rescue plan

Rian plan:
- use White Dock route knowledge and a service path to intercept the transfer physically.

Nera:
- points out they have no operable 07, no safe transport and no place to keep Juno.

Serin:
- says forced extraction would validate Security’s collusion case and remove existing legal witnesses.

Haren:
- asks for destination, food, treatment and status for Juno and cellmates.

Rian:
- admits he has only an escape action, not a survival arrangement.

## Scene 29.4 — Replacement obligation

Decision:
- team will challenge the confession only after building an alternative guarantee for the affected cellmates.

Juno condition:
- Juno will not join Rian’s cell or owe personal loyalty if the plan works.

Rian accepts:
- the goal is preserving choice/procedure, not recruitment.

End hook:
- the guarantee requires credits, adult sponsorship, medical continuity and a destination before the next transfer slot.

Reward:
- Juno’s agency and grievance recognized.

Cost:
- time and resources; no simple exoneration.

# Episode 30 — 일흔두 명의 작은 보증

Date:
- CY 742-04-12 to CY 742-04-13.

Episode function:
- build the first student mutual institution through actual contributions and disagreement.
- show support for Rian is not universal.
- turn noncombat resources into a collectible/progression reward.

## Scene 30.1 — The actual guarantee list

POV:
- Rian, with Haren leading.

Required package:
- temporary legal bond/appeal cost.
- short family/account ration bridge.
- medical placement continuation.
- adult education/placement sponsor.
- receiving cell/institution.

Haren:
- breaks the problem into obligations rather than asking for sympathy.

Theo-like function is not yet present:
- Academy clerks and Neutral claims staff provide numbers/terms.

## Scene 30.2 — Student assembly without unity

Location:
- Ring B dining/recreation or memorial/community room.

Proposal:
- students contribute a small portion of discretionary credits to a mutual guarantee.
- target working model: approximately 72 students at four units each, plus matching/conditional external guarantee.

Opposition:
- some students say Rian’s team already consumes scarce support.
- some fear Security retaliation.
- some prefer using funds for immediate medicine/food.
- Juno’s own cell is divided over challenging the confession.

Rian:
- does not give a heroic speech.
- states exact purpose, loss risk and that the fund cannot buy freedom.

## Scene 30.3 — First contribution and first refusal

Action:
- ordinary students/workers contribute, refuse or attach conditions.
- one student asks whether the fund would protect someone accused against Rian too.

Rule adopted:
- guarantee applies through stated criteria/procedure, not personal loyalty.
- contributors cannot control the protected person’s future faction.

Reward:
- first noncombat student institutional resource.

Cost:
- contributors lose credits and become visible to authorities.

## Scene 30.4 — The missing adult signature

Problem:
- student money is insufficient without an adult/institution accepting placement/liability.

Kara:
- offers education sponsorship but her authority is under review.
- signing may end her career and expose past selective protection.

Neutral:
- will match/guarantee only if evidence shows the confession is unreliable and a real receiving placement exists.

End hook:
- the necessary proof requires Serin to submit the audit trail that also documents her own reporting complicity and restricted D-001/Doran links.

Reward:
- student mutual fund formed.

Cost:
- debt, visibility and unresolved sponsorship.

# Episode 31 — 보고한 사람

Date:
- CY 742-04-13 to CY 742-04-14.

Episode function:
- force Serin to choose between self-protection and usable partial truth.
- prove trial data was commercial/political property before the test ended.
- avoid one-villain explanation.

## Scene 31.1 — Three subscribers

POV:
- Rian with Serin leading the audit presentation.

Findings:
- the training/behavior data package was prepared and provisionally sold/shared before White Dock outcome.
- Helix received safety/performance valuation fields.
- Imperial cultivation/intelligence received anomaly/candidate indicators.
- Academy Research/Operations received behavioral and mission-compliance predictions.

Meaning:
- several institutions expected to profit/use the result regardless of the official test conclusion.
- this does not prove all actors knew about the forged route credential.

## Scene 31.2 — Security replay chain

Evidence:
- Juno’s delegated token was replayed through a Security service account.
- the account can be used by multiple authorized staff/processes.
- the physical user/order remains unknown or classified.

Serin statement:
- Juno cannot be the sole actor described by the confession.
- the record does not identify the complete culprit.

Security counter:
- claims token negligence and earlier informal sharing still justify transfer.

## Scene 31.3 — Serin enters herself

Serin:
- includes her prior routine reports, access benefits and one harmful downstream classification.
- refuses to delete or minimize them.

Rian reaction:
- feels betrayed that Serin previously fed the system information.
- recognizes he also withheld future information and attempted to centralize the story.

No instant forgiveness:
- the team keeps operational trust conditions, not emotional absolution.
- Serin’s credentials are partially suspended but the evidence is admitted because external hashes corroborate it.

## Scene 31.4 — Transfer accelerated

Security action:
- uses a separate placement/safety authority to move Juno before final hearing.
- transfer is framed as protection from student retaliation and evidence contamination.

Kara/Neutral:
- can appeal but not stop departure without completed guarantee and receiving placement.

End hook:
- transfer transport is scheduled from Dock D at the next shift.

Reward:
- Juno’s sole guilt undermined and data-sale system exposed.

Cost:
- Serin’s complicity public, credentials restricted, transfer accelerated.

# Episode 32 — 출발하지 못한 수송선

Date:
- CY 742-04-15.

Episode function:
- resolve the immediate scapegoat crisis through layered physical/legal action, not a courtroom speech.
- grant partial victory and an autonomous Juno outcome.
- launch the component auction.

## Scene 32.1 — Dock D transfer state

POV:
- Rian.

Physical setup:
- Juno and several transfer cases are loaded toward a contracted shuttle.
- the student mutual guarantee is nearly complete but one signature/placement confirmation is late.
- Security has lawful internal transfer papers.

Rian impulse:
- physically block or seize the shuttle using a utility frame/service path.

Team response:
- Nera refuses unsafe/illegal equipment use.
- Haren says physical escape voids guarantees.
- Serin tracks the exact manifest and authority window.
- Mia inspects whether transfer medical/life-support conditions match the listed passengers.

## Scene 32.2 — A manifest that cannot depart

Mia/Serin finding:
- shuttle medical/life-support plan does not cover one listed vulnerable student/placement condition.
- transfer destination and actual manifest differ because Security bundled cases to save cost/time.

White Dock/Neutral witness:
- confirms the mismatch violates the written return/inquiry and medical evidence conditions.

Effect:
- departure must pause for correction; not a permanent cancellation.

Physical tension:
- shuttle crew and Security pressure for immediate release before the slot closes.

## Scene 32.3 — The alternative guarantee arrives

Completed package:
- student mutual fund posts limited bond/bridge.
- Neutral/education guarantee matches only the stated case period.
- Kara signs the educator/placement responsibility and accepts review/removal risk.
- an external Neutral-supervised technical/education placement accepts Juno under audit.
- Juno’s cellmates retain specific medical/placement guarantees without requiring Juno’s false sole guilt.

Conditions:
- Juno is not acquitted of every possible unauthorized act.
- Juno leaves Rian’s active school-cell orbit and owes no allegiance.
- the fund incurs debt and requires future contributions.

## Scene 32.4 — Partial victory and moving parts

Disposition:
- disappearance/secret transfer prevented.
- confession is suspended/limited pending wider inquiry.
- Juno chooses the external placement rather than Rian’s team.
- Juno tells Rian the result does not erase the unequal resource allocation that caused the crisis.

Simultaneous consequence:
- while attention is on the transfer, 07 components are approved for auction/claim transport before the ownership hearing is complete.
- Helix radiator convoy and Academy salvage assets begin moving.

End hook:
- Nera receives a notice that the radiator will be dismantled/recertified within forty-eight hours.

Reward:
- Juno survives with choice; student mutual fund becomes real.

Cost:
- Juno leaves, Kara endangered, collective debt and components moving.
