# Episode 85 — 여섯 개의 전선

Date:
- CY 742-06-09, initial hours.

Episode function:
- establish the siege as six linked problems rather than one duel.
- force Rian to choose one front and delegate the rest.
- begin departures/casualties without making all external actors enemies.

## Scene 85.1 — Conflicting command map

POV:
- Rian at Dock D/command feed.

Inputs:
- Imperial transfer order.
- Academy Operations/Security order.
- Helix remote asset locks.
- medical stop/Neutral corridor.
- student/worker local calls.

Problem:
- feeds contradict and some are delayed/forged.
- Rian cannot verify or command all fronts.

Haren produces a front/current-person board and assigns local owners with their consent/standing.

## Scene 85.2 — Front allocation

- Haren/Yori: Ring B then life-support routing.
- Nera/Bram/Sera: Ring C.
- Mia/Amel/Serin-linked medical team: Black Ward/Spine.
- Serin/Neutral reps: Ring A/broadcast before moving as needed.
- Kara: staff/student independent field force and Dock D sectors.
- Rian/07: Dock D/exterior because frames/shuttles can remove people/assets.

Rian dislikes losing direct control and sets intent/limits rather than detailed orders.

## Scene 85.3 — First 07 sortie

Action:
- Imperial shuttle/frames move toward Dock D under valid transfer.
- radical/Independence interference and Academy locks create collision/boarding risk.
- Rian launches under narrow emergency defense/rescue scope.

07 role:
- tag/discriminate shuttles.
- block disputed transfer route without attacking willing departure shuttle.
- tether/redirect one craft or frame.

Constraint:
- volunteers are physically mixed with disputed cases.

## Scene 85.4 — First departures and first blood

Outcome:
- one shuttle departs with volunteers and some unresolved cases before full halt.
- one Dock D/security/transfer person is killed in a combat/accident chain.
- the death hardens both security and resistance narratives.

End hook:
- Ring B bulkheads close on a current-person count that Yori says is wrong.

Reward:
- siege fronts/authority clarified; one forced transfer route slowed.

Cost:
- people leave, first death and Rian cannot reverse all fronts.

# Episode 86 — 닫힌 기숙사

Date:
- CY 742-06-09 to CY 742-06-10.

Episode function:
- center Ring B current-person, crowd and voluntary/forced separation.
- give Darin/Yori/Aven autonomous choices.
- cause a casualty without making Rian present.

## Scene 86.1 — Wrong count behind the door

POV:
- Rian receives partial reports, but the scene’s decisive information comes from Yori/Darin/local actors through communications.

Ring B:
- Security seals sectors to organize transfer and prevent breakout.
- manifest excludes workers, visitors or moved students.
- crowd pressure and conflicting instructions rise.

Yori:
- maintains actual current-person board and demands local opening.

## Scene 86.2 — Darin’s side of the door

Darin’s Imperial-aligned squad is ordered:
- secure/seal a corridor until transfer force arrives.

Problem:
- unlisted students/workers and a medical route remain inside.

Darin choice:
- refuses full seal/abandonment and opens/holds a controlled route.
- cites current safety/contract protection, not loyalty to Rian.

Imperial commander:
- treats it as insubordination or local discretion depending response.

## Scene 86.3 — Aven and the breakout

Aven/Independence contacts:
- can open a route but lack capacity for everyone and risk stampede/armed entry.

Aven:
- stops a mass uncontrolled breakout and prioritizes consented groups/actual transport.
- accepts anger from students who expected liberation.

## Scene 86.4 — Ring B casualty

A door/rotation/crowd incident caused by conflicting commands and panic kills one student/worker and injures others.

Responsibility:
- Security closure, inaccurate list, resistance/interference and delayed local authority all contribute.

Outcome:
- Darin’s route prevents a larger casualty and protects people Rian cannot reach.
- Darin still intends Imperial departure.

End hook:
- Ring C’s remote locks begin shutting life-critical workshop/power equipment while workers are inside.

Reward:
- current-person route and independent Darin/Aven agency.

Cost:
- death/injuries and deeper factional anger.

# Episode 87 — 소유권이 전원을 끈다

Date:
- CY 742-06-10.

Episode function:
- center Ring C technical conflict and Helix internal split.
- show remote ownership can protect and endanger.
- cause a lasting workshop/power loss.

## Scene 87.1 — Helix remote safety lock

POV:
- Rian remains largely off-site through limited communications; Nera/Bram/Sera own decisions.

Helix Finance/Security activates:
- locks on proprietary tools, medical/frame components and software.

Justification:
- prevent state/radical/unauthorized seizure and unsafe operation.

Effect:
- one shared power/cooling/recycler path is degraded because systems overlap.

## Scene 87.2 — Nera’s bypass versus Sera’s stop

Nera proposes:
- rapid local bypass to keep power/workshop access.

Sera:
- says the bypass can cascade into certified medical/power equipment and refuses a blind override.

Bram:
- demands physical inspection/current workers before either remote or student decision.

Combined path:
- local manual separation and bounded bypass.
- slower than Nera wants but safer than full lock/override.

## Scene 87.3 — Helix protects against Helix

Manufacturing/Frontier technical staff:
- defy or limit Finance/Security repossession to protect patients/workers and their own equipment.
- provide necessary certificate/key or physical aid.

They do not join Rian’s faction and expect future contracts/claims.

## Scene 87.4 — Industrial loss

Radical sabotage, remote lock conflict or fire destroys/cripples one Ring C workshop/power segment.
- one worker/technician dies.
- production/repair capacity falls.

Nera cannot save every tool/part.
- chooses life-support/worker route over a valuable 07/frame component or workshop asset.

End hook:
- Black Ward/Spine receives a blanket protected-patient/ORA transfer command while its power reserve is reduced.

Reward:
- Ring C remains partly operational; Helix technical allies prove autonomy.

Cost:
- death, workshop loss and technical capacity reduced.

# Episode 88 — 환자를 옮기는 군대

Date:
- CY 742-06-10.

Episode function:
- center Black Ward/Spine as a patient-specific rather than territorial front.
- give Mia/Amel/Serin/ORA decisions beyond Rian.
- show centralized clinical action can be necessary.

## Scene 88.1 — Blanket transfer order

POV:
- Rian receives reports; decisive close-third may remain Rian via comm/brief arrival but clinical choices are not his.

Order:
- protected patients/ORA-compatible equipment move under Imperial/Academy continuity transfer.
- Helix claims selected treatment systems.
- Neutral protects only consented/medical route.

Patients:
- some consent to Imperial/Helix move.
- others refuse/dispute.
- current conditions differ.

Amel insists one list cannot define one decision.

## Scene 88.2 — Clinical emergency

Power/cooling instability from Ring C/Spine causes one patient/system crisis.

Plural process is too slow for immediate stabilization.

Mia/licensed clinician invokes centralized clinical emergency authority for a bounded time.
- overrides a patient/representative or administrative delay within emergency care rules.
- action stabilizes the patient but creates later consent/accountability conflict.

This proves central action can save lives.

## Scene 88.3 — Serin and ORA-3

Security/Research attempts bulk export or erasure.

Serin/ORA:
- preserve current clinical/consent state and limited process proof.
- some research/record fragments are lost/corrupted because infrastructure is failing and bulk copy is refused.

Cost:
- not all data survives.

## Scene 88.4 — Partial movement

Consenting/medically approved patients move through Neutral/Imperial/Helix routes.
- disputed blanket movement is delayed.
- one patient/staff/crew death or serious deterioration can occur from delay/attack/system failure, subject to final casualty lock.

End hook:
- Dock D external forces converge, including Ern and a radical denial craft targeting core/command routes.

Reward:
- patient-specific choices and clinical survival.

Cost:
- centralized emergency override, lost records and possible casualty.

Date:

Episode function:
- deliver the major 07/Ern/external multi-sided action.
- prevent radical denial without making Ern an ally or Imperial/Helix pure enemies.
- exhaust 07’s second significant sortie.

## Scene 89.1 — Dock D geometry

POV:
- Rian in 07 after cooldown/repair.

Forces:
- Imperial frames/shuttles protecting transfer.
- Helix frames/service craft protecting assets/contract students.
- Ern/Independence craft opening voluntary route.
- radical frames/denial craft targeting core/command/route.
- Academy/security/student/worker craft.

07 state:
- second 10–12-minute high-demand window with accumulated wear.

Objective:
- keep at least one controlled exit and stop destructive denial.

## Scene 89.2 — Voluntary shuttle under fire

A shuttle contains willing Imperial/Helix/Neutral/Independence departures or mixed protected people.

Radical action or crossfire threatens it because it also blocks/contains strategic assets.

Rian:
- protects the shuttle despite losing people to rival factions.

Imperial/Helix pilot:
- cooperates tactically for its own passengers/assets.

## Scene 89.3 — Rian and Ern against the same object

Radical denial craft/charge route threatens Dock D/core/life support.

Rian and Ern:
- independently attack/disable the same physical objective.
- no shared command or loyalty.
- Ern prioritizes breakout/records; Rian prioritizes station/people/charter possibility.

Ern may deliberately prevent Rian from capturing a consenting Independence group or evidence after the denial is stopped.

## Scene 89.4 — 07 cannot chase

Outcome:
- denial attempt fails.
- one radical operative/frame is destroyed or abandoned; one death in casualty envelope.
- 07 heat/attitude/sensor wear worsens.
- Ern escapes or completes a limited voluntary extraction.
- Rian cannot pursue without abandoning Dock D/corridor and medical/pilot limits.

End hook:
- Ring A broadcast/legal record is being cut or replaced by a declaration that Imperial emergency command now governs the whole school.

Reward:
- exit preserved, radical destruction stopped, ace battle payoff.

Cost:
- rival departures/evidence escape and 07 degraded.

# Episode 90 — 전쟁의 공식 기록

Date:
- CY 742-06-11.

Episode function:
- center Ring A legal/broadcast front and competing public truths.
- preserve the preference/transfer/claim process enough to bind factions.
- force Serin/representatives to choose what to transmit under threat.

## Scene 90.1 — Competing declarations

POV:
- Rian mostly receiving/approaching, but Serin/representatives control the broadcast content.

Imperial/Operations declaration:
- emergency command necessary to stop civil war and protect students.

Helix declaration:
- technical/medical assets endangered by unlawful seizure and sabotage.

Independence/radical narratives:
- school is enslaving students or collaborators are surrendering them.

Charter/preference reps:
- need to prove conditional choices and disputed transfers without exposing vulnerable individuals.

## Scene 90.2 — What to publish

Serin/Amel/Yori/Bram/Kara choose:
- aggregate preference results.
- documented transfer conditions and disputed count.
- casualty/current service status.
- five-package/hybrid funding facts.

They withhold:
- person-level medical/identity/preferences beyond consent.
- some tactical/security information.

Result:
- less dramatic but defensible record.

## Scene 90.3 — Broadcast under physical pressure

Security/claim forces attempt to shut or seize Ring A communications.

Local staff/students/Neutral witness protect transmission.
- no single hacker solution.
- physical power, antennas, people and external mirrors required.

One record/archive section is lost while key proof survives in fragments.

## Scene 90.4 — Recognition without resolution

External actors cannot claim uncontested consent/authority after the transmission.
- Imperial/Helix still retain valid contracts/forces.
- Neutral gains legal basis for temporary ceasefire/trusteeship talks.
- Independence gains proof of disputed transfers.

End hook:
- station central safety system reports rotation/life-support/door command conflicts will exceed safe tolerance within hours.

Reward:
- public legal/operational record survives.

Cost:
- private data remains unavailable and systems approach failure.

# Episode 91 — 모든 전선을 지킬 수 없다

Date:
- CY 742-06-11.

Episode function:
- converge losses and reveal central-command tradeoff.
- show Rian’s current inability to solve all fronts and 07’s degraded state.
- make local actors choose what to abandon.

## Scene 91.1 — Front status board

POV:
- Rian/command room or mobile route after E89.

Status:
- Ring B route partly held, people departed/stranded.
- Ring C one workshop lost, power unstable.
- Black Ward operating but reduced.
- Dock D damaged/contested.
- Ring A broadcast partial.
- central safety commands conflict.

Casualties and missing people accumulate.

Rian requests another 07 sortie/central push.
- Mia/Nera/Kara restrict based on pilot/frame state.

## Scene 91.2 — Abandonment choices

Haren/local operators:
- cannot maintain every habitat, workshop, dock and training zone.
- propose shutting/isolating one low-occupancy section to preserve life support elsewhere.

People/assets in that section must be moved or lost.

Representatives disagree which zone bears cost.

Decision:
- current-person evacuation first, then abandon equipment/space.
- one valuable archive/workshop/frame asset is lost.

## Scene 91.3 — Central command option

Imperial/Academy/Helix systems offer or demand one emergency authority to coordinate:
- rotation.
- doors.
- life support.
- security.
- Dock D.

Benefits:
- fastest stabilization and lower immediate casualty.

Risk:
- the same authority can complete transfers/seizure and persist.

07/Authority A–B may bridge access.

## Scene 91.4 — The missing hours

Engineering estimate:
- without central or segmented emergency command, safety/authority failure in 3–5 hours.

Segmented alternative:
- possible but slower, requiring multiple local leaders and physical links.
- likely additional damage/loss.

End hook:
- one claimant begins taking central command before the group has an alternative ready.

Reward:
- true climax problem defined.

Cost:
- another zone/asset abandoned and time nearly gone.

# Episode 92 — 한 명이 명령해야 한다

Date:
- CY 742-06-11 to CY 742-06-12.

Episode function:
- end D3 with the strongest case for central control.
- position Rian to seize a key without making it an obvious villain trap.
- establish charter as a wartime operating solution, not meeting-room ideal.

## Scene 92.1 — Central authority demonstration

POV:
- Rian.

A central claimant briefly coordinates a subsystem successfully:
- stops a collision/crowd/power failure.
- proves centralized command saves time/lives.

Then uses or prepares to use the same control for:
- transfer lockdown.
- asset/core/record seizure.
- wider detention.

No clean separation exists in current architecture.

## Scene 92.2 — Segmented plan requirements

Haren:
- life support/logistics/current-person routes.

Nera/Bram/operators:
- power/technical/manual fallbacks.

Mia/clinicians:
- medical priority.

Serin/Neutral reps:
- communications/records/legal audit.

Kara/professional Security:
- doors/security/field restraint.

Rian/07:
- reach central access and hold it long enough to distribute control.

Problem:
- some leaders are on separate fronts, communications damaged and no full charter legally active.

## Scene 92.3 — Why Rian could take it

07 Authority A–B + core anomaly/future experience:
- can bridge/translate one emergency path others cannot access quickly.

Rian would be the most efficient temporary central commander.
- his tactical/strategic competence makes this credible.
- several allies and opponents ask him to take/keep command until the crisis ends.

Temptation:
- he could stop more immediate loss and shape every transfer.

## Scene 92.4 — Access route opens

Action:
- Rian/07 and local teams begin approach to central safety/service interface.
- other fronts must hold long enough.
- central claimant starts irreversible transfer/lock steps.

Final hook:
- Rian enters the command path with enough authority to save the school—or own it.

Reward:
- central access opportunity and distributed plan.

Cost:
- strong evidence that one commander is safer right now.
