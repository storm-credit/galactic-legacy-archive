## 1. Matrix Rule

Each grand act must satisfy all six conditions:

1. it solves a visible problem promised at the act opening;
2. the solution leaves a material reward;
3. the same solution creates or accelerates the next grand-act problem;
4. at least one earlier person, machine, institution or right is reused at larger scale;
5. at least one autonomous actor other than P-001 determines a decisive result;
6. the act contributes directly to the final distribution ending.

If a grand act can be removed without changing the next act or ending, it must be merged or cut.

## 6. Final Causal Integrity Verdict

The GA1–10 chain is causally closed at grand-act and act/subact level:

- each victory creates the next material problem;
- no ending technology or law appears without an earlier operational prototype;
- early people, machines, institutions and rights return at larger scale;
- the final centralized answer has demonstrated benefits;
- P-001’s growth culminates in relinquishment rather than coronation;
- the ending can remain open without breaking a promised central mystery.
