## 1. Grand-Act Contract

Working title:
- `주인 없는 함대`.

Range:
- Episodes 451–570.

Primary promise:

> 함대는 배 숫자가 아니라, 서로 다른 주인·서약·급여·정비망을 가진 승무원들이 어떤 임무를 위해 얼마나 오래 명령을 빌려주는가로 만들어진다.

Grand-act question:

> P-001은 자신에게 충성하는 함대를 만들지 않고도, 서로 다른 배들이 함께 싸우고 살아 돌아갈 수 있는 지휘체계를 만들 수 있는가?

Visible goals:

1. keep Vesper Depot/pay/repair network from being seized or abandoned.
2. organize ships with conflicting oaths, appointments and supply chains.
3. establish crew rescue/parole, pay and lawful-refusal rules.
4. survive provincial recalls and local-command rivalry.
5. execute one multi-front campaign where subordinate commanders decide independently.
6. protect Vesper/Talren/Outer Front while preventing personal fleet ownership.

Final reward:
- Common Fleet Charter operational across multiple formations.
- 55–90 combat ships and 50–100 support/rescue ships connected in a distributed fleet network, not all under one permanent command.
- P-001 recognized as bounded theater/task commander.
- pooled maintenance, pay/family, authentication and rescue systems.
- local/Provincial/Protector/Crown/industrial commanders remain autonomous participants.

Final cost:
- one formation or province withdraws/refuses at critical time.
- fleet is depleted in missiles, repair capacity and crews.
- local commander/FC-005 retains rival legitimacy.
- civilian movement concentrates toward Orpheus Approach.
- capital/Compact forces also concentrate there, creating GA6 dilemma.
- charter can hide responsibility or become military guild if not checked.

# ACT 5A — 배는 누구의 명령을 듣는가 / Episodes 451–480

Act question:

> 같은 국가의 함선들이 서로 다른 합법적 명령을 받을 때, 어떤 임무까지만 함께할 수 있는가?

Act result:
- temporary Vesper mission force forms under shared intent rather than claimant ownership.
- first command, authentication and crew-oath rules are tested.

Act cost:
- one ship/unit refuses, mutinies, grounds or departs.
- P-001 receives bounded task authority but cannot compel all ships.
- Protector/Compact/Provincial/Crown tensions sharpen.

## 3. Subact 5A-1 — 함대가 아닌 정박지 / Episodes 451–457

Entry:
- Vesper Depot crowded with formations waiting on pay, repairs, orders and recognition.

Goal:
- prevent depot seizure, uncontrolled departure or crew unrest.

Immediate conflicts:
- Protector-aligned officers demand unified emergency command.
- provincial ships require mandate and recall protections.
- Crown Guard protects Crown Passage/civilians.
- yard/Helix actors prioritize repair contracts.
- unpaid crews question deployment.

P-001 status:
- recognized strategic actor but commands only own task/route group.

Required action:
- a depot incident, disputed resupply or armed standoff forces practical cooperation.

Reward:
- 7–10 day shared defensive/service mandate for Vesper Depot and Talren routes.

Cost:
- no offensive/theater command.
- supplies rationed; some ships excluded/grounded.

Hook:
- one disputed destroyer receives three valid but incompatible departure/mission orders and its crew blocks the bridge/locks.

## 4. Subact 5A-2 — 세 개의 선서 / Episodes 458–464

Goal:
- resolve disputed ship without massacre or simple captain victory.

Conflict layers:
- captain commission.
- crew oath.
- pay/pension.
- weapons/keys.
- mission legitimacy.
- family/home province pressure.

CR-001 enters:
- crew demand guarantees and right not to become claimant property.

P-001 temptation:
- use authority/07 boarding to seize ship for urgent mission.

Alternative:
- temporary mission charter, mixed command succession, crew vote/representation and legal review.

Required cost:
- some officers/crew leave or are detained/transferred.
- ship becomes R1/R2 instead of fully ready.

Reward:
- first ship-specific oath/mission agreement and crew representative.

Hook:
- authentication systems reject or delay a legitimate shared order during an external raid/sabotage.

## 5. Subact 5A-3 — 명령이 늦은 전투 / Episodes 465–472

Mission:
- defend Vesper Depot or civilian approach from raiders/rejecter probe/sabotage while orders conflict.

Primary tension:
- centralized Protector order is fast and effective.
- shared authentication/mandate delays deployment.

Action:
- P-001 commands one task group.
- FC-001 independently stabilizes another front.
- one captain acts under standing rules without waiting.

Outcome:
- threat repelled or objective partially saved, but delayed response costs cargo, workers, ship or lives.

Reward:
- standing defensive intent and emergency authentication path.

Cost:
- central-command advocates gain credibility.
- one ship blames charter process and defects/grounds.

Hook:
- fleet cannot sustain another action: repair tender, missiles and payroll become decisive.

## 6. Subact 5A-4 — 빌린 지휘권 / Episodes 473–480

Goal:
- define temporary formation/task command for upcoming operations.

Negotiations:
- mission scope and duration.
- captain safety/surrender.
- crew refusal and audit.
- authentication and courier fallback.
- provincial/crown limits.
- support allocation.

P-001 offer:
- FC-001/Protector or compact grants broader command if he accepts clearer hierarchy.

Required choice:
- P-001 accepts bounded task-group authority only, while FC-001 or another commands separate formation.

Reward:
- first Common Fleet Charter v0.1 and task force.

Cost:
- slower coordination, exit clauses and no guarantee of reinforcement.

Act-ending trigger:
- mobile repair tender and payroll convoy are attacked/claimed; losing them grounds more ships than losing a cruiser.

# ACT 5B — 전함보다 비싼 수리선 / Episodes 481–510

Act question:

> 배를 싸우게 하는 사람과 공장을 누가 우선 수리할지 결정하는 사람이 다를 때, 함대의 주권은 어디에 있는가?

Act result:
- maintenance, pay, rescue and parole systems become part of fleet charter.
- disputed/surrendered ships can join without becoming personal prizes.

Act cost:
- several combat ships remain grounded or cannibalized.
- worker/crew/yard conflicts create real delay and casualties.
- Helios/Deme/Helix actors gain ongoing leverage.

## 7. Subact 5B-1 — 수리선 호송 / Episodes 481–487

Mission:
- protect/recover a mobile repair tender, engineers, payroll/family records and high-value parts.

Threat:
- claimant force, opportunists, FC-005-linked seizure, sabotage or competing legitimate claim.

Battle novelty:
- tender cannot maneuver/fight like warship; escort must preserve equipment/people.
- destroying attackers may lose evidence/crew integration opportunity.

Decisive actor:
- FC-004/yard crews choose route/repair triage.

Reward:
- tender and partial repair/pay capacity.

Cost:
- convoy cargo split or one combat ship sacrificed/abandoned.
- tender condition worse than expected.

Hook:
- yard/tender can restore only a fraction of ships; allocation becomes political.

## 8. Subact 5B-2 — 어느 배를 고칠 것인가 / Episodes 488–494

Goal:
- allocate limited yard/tender time, parts, missiles and crews.

Competing priorities:
- largest combat value.
- civilian/convoy defense.
- provincial equity.
- Crown/Protector mission.
- ships with paid/ready crews.
- open-standard compatibility.

H-002/ENG-001/FC-004:
- create readiness and maintenance-pool proposal.

Conflict:
- H-002’s open repair can return more ships at lower certified confidence.
- Helios/Helix certified repair protects workers/long-term readiness but favors certain hulls.

Required outcome:
- mixed repair pool and transparent priority, leaving some powerful ships grounded.

Cost:
- one faction withdraws/retaliates.
- workers face overload or unsafe pressure.

Reward:
- pooled maintenance rules and distributed parts inventory.

Hook:
- grounded/surrendered crews threaten mutiny/desertion when pay/family support fails.

## 9. Subact 5B-3 — 싸우지 않는 승무원 / Episodes 495–502

Goal:
- establish pay, pension, family, rescue/parole and lawful-refusal system.

Conflict:
- Treasury/regent funds incomplete.
- provinces support own crews.
- Crown/Protector oaths differ.
- deserters/prisoners fear purge/identity loss.
- crews may exploit solidarity to avoid risk/accountability.

CR-001/M-001/S-001/H-001 roles:
- pay/identity, casualty care, family guarantees and records.

Required case:
- crew refuses a mission for mixed legitimate/selfish reasons.
- captain/command must respond without execution or costless refusal.

Reward:
- provisional crew guarantee and review/parole structure.

Cost:
- delayed deployment, funds diverted and guilty actors partly protected.

Hook:
- a defeated/disputed enemy formation offers surrender/joining under conditions, creating title/prize crisis.

## 10. Subact 5B-4 — 나포하지 않는 함대 / Episodes 503–510

Goal:
- integrate or neutralize surrendered/captured ships without claimant/personal ownership.

Conflict:
- title, crew crimes, pay, repair, weapons and oath.
- victims demand punishment/return.
- Protector/claimants want useful hulls.
- crews want protection and status.

Required decision:
- trust/lease/parole ownership with audits, crew choice and mission limits.
- some officers tried; crews not collectively inherited.

Reward:
- additional 3–8 ships/support capability and legal precedent.

Cost:
- political backlash, security risk and repair burden.
- one ship/crew rejects integration and leaves/grounds.

Act ending:
- provincial systems issue recalls because home routes/civilians are threatened, just as common fleet needs those ships for Talren/Vesper operations.

# ACT 5C — 돌아갈 권리 / Episodes 511–540

Act question:

> 함대에 참가한 지방과 승무원이 언제든 돌아갈 수 있다면 공동방위는 가능한가, 돌아갈 수 없다면 자발적 연합인가?

Act result:
- recall/exit rules gain enforceable costs and minimum obligations.
- fleet expands through voluntary but binding mission commitments.
- P-001 and FC-005 cooperate/compete over Outer Front legitimacy.

Act cost:
- one province/formation withdraws at damaging time.
- civilian route or local front suffers.
- FC-005 remains credible alternative and may reject final charter.

## 11. Subact 5C-1 — 고향의 명령 / Episodes 511–517

Trigger:
- provincial home systems demand ships return due attacks, shortages or political fear.

Goal:
- determine which recalls are real emergencies and what obligations remain.

Conflict:
- FC-002 defends provincial mandate.
- P-001/FC-001 need ships for theater.
- H-001 models weak-region and collective effects.
- crews/families pressure captains.

Required decision:
- some ships return under arranged replacement/support; others remain under time-limited mandate.

Cost:
- common force weakens and one route/front loses coverage.

Reward:
- first recall/exit framework with notice, replacement and remaining obligations.

Hook:
- one province/commander withdraws without fulfilling terms, causing civilian/convoy crisis at Talren/Civilian Chain.

## 12. Subact 5C-2 — 떠난 함대의 자리 / Episodes 518–524

Mission:
- protect Talren/Civilian Chain with reduced forces and multiple convoy destinations.

Battle/operation:
- escort, evacuation, route defense and misinformation.
- Crown Guard may divert from household route; provincial/local ships decide independently.

Decisive actor:
- FC-003 or a provincial subordinate saves civilians outside expected loyalty.

Reward:
- public/civil legitimacy for fleet charter.

Cost:
- military objective or depot position weakened.
- one convoy/group delayed/lost/forced to change route.

Hook:
- Outer Front commander FC-005 accuses Core compact fleet of protecting symbols/civilians while abandoning actual war line—or vice versa.

## 13. Subact 5C-3 — 지킨 자의 나라 / Episodes 525–533

Goal:
- negotiate/cooperate with FC-005 and Outer Front forces.

FC-005 claim:
- defended region during Core abandonment and earned lasting command legitimacy.

P-001 conflict:
- shares irregular/performance legitimacy but seeks temporary mission command.

External/third threat:
- rejecter force/opportunist/separatist assault or route collapse forces cooperation.

Action:
- two command systems operate side by side.
- one performs better in one domain and worse in another.

Required result:
- tactical cooperation and exchange of support without personal recruitment.

Reward:
- access to Outer Front ships/routes and main rejecter relationship.

Cost:
- FC-005 demands recognition/resources or seizes local assets.
- P-001 coalition accepts local/theater conditions it dislikes.

Hook:
- evidence shows a larger multi-front offensive/route operation will hit Vesper, Talren and Outer Front nearly together.

## 14. Subact 5C-4 — 떠날 수 있는 약속 / Episodes 534–540

Goal:
- revise fleet charter with enforceable recall/exit, burden, support and command terms before offensive.

Terms:
- mission-specific contribution.
- minimum duration/objective.
- recall notice/emergency exceptions.
- replacement or resource compensation.
- crew/family guarantees.
- rescue/parole.
- provincial/civilian standing.

Conflict:
- strict obligations resemble central command.
- loose terms risk collapse.

Decisive actors:
- FC-002, CR-001, H-001 and local/provincial representatives.

Reward:
- Common Fleet Charter v0.2 with burdens and exit.

Cost:
- some formations refuse and remain outside.
- P-001 cannot count on full strength.

Act-ending trigger:
- multi-front campaign begins before all signatories arrive or repair completes.

# ACT 5D — 공동전단 / Episodes 541–570

Act question:

> 여러 지휘관이 서로 다른 전선을 맡고 P-001이 모든 판단을 내릴 수 없을 때, 함대는 승리와 책임을 어떻게 나누는가?

Act result:
- Common Fleet Charter survives a multi-front campaign.
- multiple formations coordinate through shared intent, standing rules, support and autonomous command.
- P-001 becomes bounded theater/task commander, not fleet owner.

Act cost:
- one formation refuses/withdraws or acts against intent.
- fleet and support network are depleted.
- civilian movement and strategic concentration create Second Orpheus.
- responsibility for losses is distributed and disputed.

## 15. Subact 5D-1 — 하나의 목표, 다섯 개의 전선 / Episodes 541–547

Campaign fronts:

1. Vesper Depot and support line.
2. Talren/Civilian Chain protection.
3. Outer Front relief/denial.
4. Crown Passage/Core reinforcement.
5. Orpheus Approach route/security.

Goal:
- define shared intent and allocate formations/resources.

P-001 role:
- bounded theater commander or coordinating commander under mandate.

Constraint:
- cannot directly command all fronts or pilot constantly.

Required choice:
- one front knowingly under-resourced.
- one commander receives broad autonomy despite disagreement.

Reward:
- operational plan and delegated authority.

Cost:
- political backlash and no perfect coverage.

Hook:
- authentication/communication attack or route damage isolates one front early.

## 16. Subact 5D-2 — 보이지 않는 함대 / Episodes 548–554

Crisis:
- one formation loses comm/authentication or receives forged/conflicting orders.

Command test:
- subordinate commander acts on shared intent/standing rules without P-001.

Possible decisive front:
- FC-003 protects civilians/crown route.
- FC-004 preserves tender/depot.
- FC-002 holds/withdraws provincial front.
- FC-001 executes disciplined counterattack.
- FC-005 defends Outer Front under own legitimacy.

Outcome:
- at least one autonomous success and one failure/misinterpretation.

Reward:
- charter proves it can function without central real-time control.

Cost:
- casualties, lost ships or objective due ambiguity.
- audit/blame conflict begins before campaign ends.

Hook:
- P-001 must choose where to commit remaining reserve/07/first ship.

## 17. Subact 5D-3 — 예비대의 주인 / Episodes 555–563

Goal:
- commit reserve/support to one front while others suffer.

Temptation:
- Protector/Regent/Custodian offers stronger unified override.
- P-001 can seize broader command through emergency authority.

Required refusal/limited use:
- he uses only time-bound mission authority or delegates reserve to another commander.

Decisive success:
- another commander wins major front or saves fleet/population autonomously.

Major loss:
- one under-resourced route/front suffers, and responsibility cannot be assigned to one person alone.

Reward:
- campaign objective achieved: Vesper/Talren/Outer Front remain connected enough.

Cost:
- missiles, repair ships, crews and routes depleted.
- FC-005/one formation rejects P-001’s allocation and preserves separate command.

Hook:
- civilian convoys and damaged state/capital fleets converge on Orpheus Approach with limited route windows and rescue capacity.

## 18. Subact 5D-4 — 함대가 남긴 길 / Episodes 564–570

Goal:
- assess campaign, formalize charter and respond to emerging Orpheus crisis.

After-action conflicts:
- who bears losses.
- whether refusal/withdrawal was lawful.
- pay/pension and family compensation.
- prize/title and surrendered ships.
- commander removal/review.
- public narrative of victory.

Reward:
- Common Fleet Charter v1.0 and distributed formation network.
- P-001 receives limited theater/task command standing.
- rescue/pay/repair/authentication and audit systems operational.

Cost:
- not all formations sign.
- one commander/formation remains rival or leaves.
- fleet cannot immediately recover.

Grand-act ending:
- distress and command messages from Orpheus Approach reveal mutually exclusive windows:
  - state/capital fleet and strategic institutions require passage/defense;
  - mass civilian/refugee convoys require the same route, escorts and supplies.
- no force has enough capacity for both under current clocks.

GA6 launch:
- `두 번째 오르페우스`.

## 19. Character Progression

### P-001

Start:
- recognized strategic commander but no fleet ownership.

Pressure:
- fragmented command causes real loss.
- unified authority is tactically superior.

Change:
- builds mission-lent command, standing intent and subordinate autonomy.
- accepts another commander’s victory and one formation’s refusal.

End:
- bounded theater/task commander whose next decision affects state fleet vs civilians.

### H-001

- builds pay/family/support and provincial burden mechanisms.
- confronts withdrawal and weak-region abandonment.
- fleet logistics power becomes political and ethically dangerous.

### H-002

- pooled maintenance and yard/worker governance.
- chooses which ships remain grounded and bears consequences.
- open/certified hybrid becomes fleet institution.

### S-001

- authentication/after-action/oath records.
- cannot eliminate forged orders or blame ambiguity.
- designs standing intent and plural audit.

### M-001

- rescue/parole, crew fitness and casualty/family systems.
- rejects expendability ranking but must allocate scarce medical capacity.

### CAP-001

- first ship’s captain/mission separation becomes fleet model.
- may disagree with P-001 reserve choice or refuse one order under charter.

### Fleet Commanders

- FC-001: unity vs bounded command.
- FC-002: voluntary province vs enforceable burden.
- FC-003: civilian crown legitimacy vs wider mission.
- FC-004: readiness/repair leverage vs public obligation.
- CR-001: crew rights vs mission responsibility.
- FC-005: earned local command vs transferable/reviewable authority.

## 25. Drop-Off and Repetition Controls

### Risk: fleet law/charter replaces action

Control:
- every clause emerges from a mutiny, delayed order, repair choice, recall, surrender or casualty.
- no more than one full charter meeting per act.

### Risk: GA5 becomes logistics-only and loses spectacle

Control:
- four distinct ship/fleet action shapes plus multi-front campaign.
- large strategic formations visible without number spam.
- 07/frames support boarding, repair, capture and infrastructure rather than replace ships.

### Risk: P-001 still owns every victory

Control:
- at least three decisive autonomous commander successes.
- one valid refusal/withdrawal.
- one front lost/under-resourced due P-001 allocation.

### Risk: too many fleet leaders

Control:
- active 3–5 per subact; others represented by formations/briefings.
- front-stage six include FC-005; not all in every act.

### Risk: 120 episodes too long

Contractible GA5:
- 90–105 episodes.
- merge 5A-1/5A-2 or 5B-3/5B-4 if needed.
- episode ranges are ceilings.
