## 1. Grand-Act Contract

Working title:
- `수집가 없는 은하`.

Range:
- Episodes 1001–1100.

Primary promise:

> P-001이 가장 안전한 중앙권력을 손에 쥔 뒤, 그것을 파괴하지도 영원히 보유하지도 않고 기능별·지역별로 넘기면서 발생하는 실패와 전쟁을 끝까지 책임진다.

Grand-act question:

> 중앙권력이 더 안전한 순간에도 누구도 모두의 미래를 영구 소유하지 못하게 하려면, 무엇을 남기고 무엇을 넘기고 무엇을 잃어야 하는가?

Visible goals:

1. stabilize the failed first handoff without recentralizing permanently.
2. disclose dependency, staff, key, service and hidden backdoor structure.
3. allow different regions/entities to choose different transition states under shared obligations.
4. preserve minimum services, routes and defense during attacks and local capture attempts.
5. distribute current status, service, route, military, technical, record and corrective powers separately.
6. end P-001’s exclusive future index and central command.
7. settle major Archive/Orpheus/Blood Admiral/07/hero-collection payoffs without one final master history.
8. show years of imperfect reconstruction and new lives without Archive labels.

Final reward:
- no single galaxy-wide sovereign Seed operator or master key.
- multiple regional/local/hybrid systems under thin interoperability and minimum obligations.
- bounded residual Continuity, reserve, translation and emergency services.
- P-001 relinquishes exclusive future index and central command.
- people, ships, machines, records and institutions remain with their own custodians/constituencies.
- core characters hold independent institutions/roles.
- regression/reset capability is disabled/distributed beyond repeat use.

Final cost:
- some regions suffer worse service, conflict or elite capture.
- some regions retain temporary central synchronization.
- one transition front/region fails or delays.
- records, identities, AI personhood and compensation remain unresolved in some cases.
- P-001 loses memory access/command capacity and cannot guarantee the future.
- no universal reconciliation or canonical account.

# ACT 10A — 첫 번째 이양 실패 / Episodes 1001–1025

Act question:

> 첫 권한이양에서 사람들이 실제로 죽고 서비스가 끊기면, 중앙키를 다시 쥐지 않고 어떻게 실패를 고칠 것인가?

Act result:
- first handoff region survives/corrects through local institutions plus bounded emergency support.
- transition dependency and hidden-central-control map becomes public/usable.
- regions gain choice/readiness process rather than one universal schedule.

Act cost:
- named people/service/infrastructure lost.
- P-001 uses temporary central override again, strengthening arguments to retain him.
- local elite/hostile/hardliner exploit is proven real.

## 3. Subact 10A-1 — 넘긴 키가 열지 못한 문 / Episodes 1001–1006

Entry:
- first RS-1/RS-2 handoff suffers authentication, payroll, hospital, route or elite-capture failure/attack.

Goal:
- prevent mass service collapse while identifying cause.

Cause stack:
- incomplete local staff/training.
- hidden central dependency/backdoor.
- local actor exploits appeals/current status.
- hostile/hardliner attack.
- transition schedule pressure.

P-001 response:
- temporarily reactivates bounded central correction to save named people, with explicit expiration/trace.

Reward:
- immediate collapse stopped.

Cost:
- one irreversible death/loss and public proof that P-001 central control is safer.

Hook:
- transition region demands local ownership of correction rather than another central takeover.

## 4. Subact 10A-2 — 실패를 돌려주지 않는 법 / Episodes 1007–1012

Goal:
- correct the failure locally instead of cancelling handoff.

Actors:
- TR-L02 regional leader.
- beneficiaries/excluded residents.
- technical/medical/archive staff.
- P-001/A-001 transition team.

Required decision:
- local authority accepts external audit/affected standing and removes/limits one captured official/system.
- P-001 transfers tools/knowledge rather than personally remain operator.

Reward:
- local service/current-status system resumes under corrected plural/hybrid authority.

Cost:
- local autonomy compromised by shared obligations; central residual support remains necessary.

Hook:
- dependency audit reveals many supposedly transferred systems still rely on central staff, certificates, reserves or hidden override paths.

## 5. Subact 10A-3 — 은하가 의존하는 목록 / Episodes 1013–1019

Goal:
- disclose/inventory real dependencies and readiness by function/region.

Includes:
- identity/current status.
- medicine/service.
- routes/nodes.
- fleet/security.
- technical/AI standards.
- records/mirrors.
- central corrective/backdoors.

Conflict:
- disclosure helps attackers/local elites.
- institutions hide dependencies to retain power or prevent panic.
- beneficiary regions fear being assigned burdens.

Required action:
- A-001/transition officials reveal or withhold a critical dependency; consequence follows.

Reward:
- public transition map, readiness bands and failure thresholds.

Cost:
- security incidents, market/political panic and bargaining.

Hook:
- regions demand different transition schedules/states, while maximalists/hardliners reject unequal paths.

## 6. Subact 10A-4 — 같은 날 자유로워지지 않는다 / Episodes 1020–1025

Goal:
- establish regional choice/readiness/obligation process.

Representative choices:
- immediate plural transfer.
- hybrid synchronized autonomy.
- delayed central continuity.
- opt-out compact.
- coerced/collapsed-region trust.
- AI/nonterritorial model.

Conflict:
- choice affects dependent outsiders and migration.
- central/transition maximalists accuse process of preserving oppression or enabling chaos.

Reward:
- Transition Convention and first region-specific compacts.

Cost:
- galaxy formally diverges; no one timeline or standard.

Act-ending trigger:
- hardliner remnants, local elites and external opportunists coordinate/independently attack several different transition states to prove only their model survives.

# ACT 10B — 서로 다른 은하들 / Episodes 1026–1050

Act question:

> 지역마다 다른 체제를 선택할 때, 자유로운 선택과 다른 사람에게 비용을 떠넘기는 선택을 어떻게 구분할 것인가?

Act result:
- plural, hybrid, delayed-central, opt-out, AI/community and coerced-region transition models operate simultaneously.
- thin interoperability/minimum-service compact takes practical form.
- beneficiary and excluded groups gain standing/resources in transition.

Act cost:
- one local elite/warlord captures authority temporarily.
- one delayed-central region postpones full handoff.
- migration/service inequality and armed conflict increase.

## 7. Subact 10B-1 — 남기로 선택한 도시 / Episodes 1026–1031

Goal:
- negotiate a region that voluntarily wants continued central synchronization for a fixed term.

Beneficiary argument:
- services/security and lack of local capacity.

Opposition/minority argument:
- delayed rights and central classification continue.

Required outcome:
- time-limited central continuity with local/minority standing, independent mirrors, training/funding and automatic review/exit triggers.

Reward:
- legitimate delayed path and beneficiary trust.

Cost:
- central authority persists and may resist expiration.

Hook:
- immediate-transfer/opt-out region suffers local power capture or service exclusion.

## 8. Subact 10B-2 — 지방의 독재자 / Episodes 1032–1037

Goal:
- stop local elite/warlord capture without inviting permanent central return.

Conflict:
- local leader claims democratic/operational legitimacy.
- weak/migrant/excluded groups seek outside help.
- central forces offer fast stabilization.

P-001 role:
- cannot simply appoint replacement or take keys back.

Required solution:
- local opposition, affected outsiders, Common Fleet/route/claims institutions and temporary bounded intervention remove/limit capture.

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:
- medical/technical systems across regions become incompatible or under-resourced, threatening patients and trade.

## 9. Subact 10B-3 — 다르게 작동하는 병원과 기계 / Episodes 1038–1044

Actors:

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:
- identity/current-status divergence strands people between regions and gives hardliners/opportunists a target.

- establish cross-region provisional identity/service/migration rules.

Actors:

- excluded coalition.
- beneficiary regions.

- right to forget vs evidence/claims.

Reward:

Cost:

Act-ending trigger:

## 24. Drop-Off and Ending Controls

### Risk: ending becomes constitutional lecture

Control:
- every rule shown through handoff, service, conflict, people and physical systems.
- no final grand convention speech resolves everything.

### Risk: too many epilogues

Control:
- select representative regions/characters and use linked objects/services/messages.
- not every side character receives equal closure scene.

### Risk: P-001 sacrifice feels abstract

Control:
- exclusive index loss changes what he remembers/knows and causes one real failure.
- central command physically/legal expires and cannot be retaken secretly.
- iconic assets/people visibly leave his custody.

### Risk: 100 episodes too long

Contractible GA10:
- approximately 78–92 episodes.
- merge 10A-2/10A-3 or 10D-1/10D-2 if needed.
- episode ranges are ceilings.
