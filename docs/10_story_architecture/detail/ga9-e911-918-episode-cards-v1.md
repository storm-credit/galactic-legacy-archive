# Episode 911 — 점수는 신분이 아니라고 적혀 있다

Date:
- CY 746-09-24 to 09-28.

Goal:
- map the formal credential architecture and its practical use.

Formal rule:
- confidence and compatibility scores guide route/service verification only;
- no score creates citizenship, personhood, family status, cultural legitimacy or technology permission.

Practical finding:
- route access, insurance, repair queues and employer checks increasingly reuse the same score;
- manual review time creates de facto status even where the treaty denies legal status creation.

Decision:
- prohibit unlogged secondary use and require each downstream decision to name its legal basis.

Reward:
- the gap between formal scope and practical power becomes auditable.

Cost:
- operators argue that separate verification will erase efficiency gains.

Hook:
- plural AI identities expose the schema’s first structural mismatch.

# Episode 912 — 한 루트에 여러 사람이 산다

Date:
- CY 746-09-29 to 10-03.

Goal:
- test AI roots, forks, communities and shared substrates against one-record credential assumptions.

Evidence:
- 29,600 AI-plurality flags across decisions;
- flags overlap and do not equal 29,600 people;
- independent forks are sometimes merged into one risk record;
- common substrate dependencies are sometimes mistaken for shared consent;
- service tools are sometimes inflated into identity conflicts.

Decision:
- create multi-seat and multi-consent credential structures with community-controlled mappings.

Reward:
- Lumen, Orison and other AI communities can enter routes without surrendering internal plurality.

Cost:
- verification takes longer, and several coalition operators continue preferring root-only shortcuts.

Hook:
- nonstandard engineering communities face the same pressure through safety certification.

# Episode 913 — 표준 밖의 기계

Date:
- CY 746-10-04 to 10-08.

Goal:
- distinguish genuine safety gaps from prejudice against nonstandard technology.

Evidence:
- 18,200 nonstandard-technology flags;
- 6,400 involve missing test evidence;
- 4,900 involve incompatible but safe local standards;
- 3,700 involve modified reproductive, medical or life-support systems;
- remaining flags are duplicate, stale or unresolved and overlap with other classes.

Decision:
- create bounded equivalence tests and local expert attestations rather than automatic rejection.

Reward:
- useful technology retains access while real safety limits remain enforceable.

Cost:
- equivalence review is expensive and insurers price uncertainty before completion.

Hook:
- names and kinship structures reveal that administrative compatibility also encodes culture.

# Episode 914 — 이름이 맞지 않는 가족

Date:
- CY 746-10-09 to 10-13.

Goal:
- expose cultural and household mismatches without treating them as clerical trivia.

Evidence:
- 16,400 naming, kinship, household and language flags;
- collective households split into unrelated individuals;
- memorial names fail live-identity checks;
- adoption, distributed parenthood and AI-human family links are downgraded;
- translation certainty is mistaken for legal certainty.

Decision:
- accept plural household proofs and community translation attestations under appealable rules.

Reward:
- family, pay, care and travel chains reopen for many cases.

Cost:
- fraud investigators demand more surveillance and longer retention.

Hook:
- mobile and unregistered settlements cannot produce even alternative proofs quickly.

# Episode 915 — 주소가 없는 사람들

Date:
- CY 746-10-14 to 10-18.

Goal:

Evidence:

Reward:

Cost:

# Episode 916 — 이의제기가 빠른 항로를 늦출 때

Date:
- CY 746-10-19 to 10-24.

Goal:
- measure whether appeal rights are real under operational pressure.

Appeal ledger:
- 51,200 appeals;
- 18,600 granted;
- 12,400 modified or conditionally resolved;
- 14,800 upheld;
- 5,400 pending;
- total 51,200.

Finding:
- urgent medical and family cases improve when impact advocates can pause a decision;
- standard commercial operators complain that manual cases occupy scarce slots;
- pending cases concentrate among low-resource communities.

Decision:
- separate urgent human-impact review from routine commercial appeals and finance claimant support.

Reward:
- appeal is not merely a paper right.

Cost:
- the coalition’s headline speed declines slightly, and leaders debate hiding manual-lane time from public dashboards.

Hook:
- a stratified audit tests false positives and false negatives.

# Episode 917 — 틀린 거절과 놓친 위험

Date:
- CY 746-10-25 to 10-30.

Goal:
- audit accuracy without collapsing every mismatch into harm or every clear into safety.

Stratified sample:
- 80,000 decisions;
- 75,280 supported by later evidence;
- 2,960 false-positive restrictions or escalations;
- 1,140 false-negative clears;
- 620 unresolved;
- total 80,000.

Finding:
- the system is highly useful but errors are unevenly distributed;
- AI plurality, mobile settlement and nonstandard technology cases have higher false-positive rates;
- hostile automation and forged cargo remain significant false-negative sources.

Decision:
- publish class-specific error rates and prohibit one global accuracy number from proving fairness.

Reward:
- safety and discrimination can be discussed with the same evidence base.

Cost:
- insurers demand class-specific surcharges, potentially reproducing the audited inequality.

Hook:
- three members invoke exit clauses to test whether withdrawal is genuinely possible.

# Episode 918 — 나갈 수 있는 연합

Date:
- CY 746-10-31 to 11-05.

Goal:
- conduct a real exit and function-withdrawal test.

Tests:
- one conditional body exits common insurance settlement but retains route scheduling;
- one founder suspends automatic credential reuse for fourteen days;
- one small jurisdiction prepares full withdrawal but pauses after service-impact review.

Measured exit costs:
- 14–22 days projected service and settlement disruption;
- 9,800 workers/crew require recredentialing;
- 480m credit-equivalent collateral is repriced or frozen;
- no legal penalty for exit, but dependency costs are real.

Decision:
- create portability packages, open audit exports and a funded transition reserve.

Reward:
- exit remains possible in practice, though not painless.

Cost:
- common efficiency itself becomes bargaining power over members.

Final hook:
- E919 asks whether conditional participation is becoming compulsory through markets and service dependence rather than law.
