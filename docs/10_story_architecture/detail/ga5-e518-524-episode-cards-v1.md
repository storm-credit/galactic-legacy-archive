# Episode 518 — 왕관이 아니라 통로

Date:
- CY 744-07-03.

Goal:
- decide whether Crown Passage receives escort during falling fleet availability.

Decision:
- define mission around current civilians, courts, hospitals, witnesses and service continuity, not Elian’s fleet ownership or ceremonial priority alone.

Reward:
- Oren commits a bounded Crown/civil escort formation.

Cost:
- five combat and four support ships leave Vesper/Outer screens.

Hook:
- military planners warn that the opening will expose a munitions relay.

# Episode 519 — 누구를 먼저 태울 것인가

Date:
- CY 744-07-05.

Goal:
- allocate limited passenger/service capacity without making court rank the dominant criterion.

Decision:
- current medical dependence, essential service continuity, witness/evidence risk, family unity and route vulnerability rank seats/cargo;
- ceremonial household receives a bounded share.

Reward:
- 24,800 people and 6,460 t manifest become current and reviewable.

Cost:

Hook:
- one escort is recalled before departure under the new framework.

# Episode 520 — 소환권이 있는 호위함

Date:
- CY 744-07-07.

Goal:
- preserve escort without breaking Recall Framework v0.1.

Decision:
- honor the recall after a 72-hour handover and replace the ship with a lower-readiness Outer-route escort plus revised convoy geometry.

Reward:
- framework proves usable under political pressure.

Cost:

Hook:
- six hostile/probe ships and twelve drones enter Crown Passage approach.

# Episode 521 — 오렌 로만의 전선

Date:
- CY 744-07-09.

POV:
- Oren Roman.

Goal:
- keep the civilian/service convoy intact rather than answer a simultaneous Vesper outer alert.

Decision:
- Oren retains all six escorts on the convoy and refuses to detach toward Vesper; he records the choice as his own current command responsibility.

Reward:
- convoy screen remains coherent.

Cost:
- Vesper outer picket fights with reduced support; Oren’s legitimacy becomes contested by military blocs.

Hook:
- hostile drones drive one escort into a service ship’s thermal wake.

# Episode 522 — 사람들이 탄 배의 전투

Date:
- CY 744-07-11.

Goal:
- defeat/separate the probe without turning civilian ships into bait.

Action:
- Oren controls escort geometry;
- civilian captains follow standing dispersal routes;
- Rian’s limited route element provides current navigation/courier support but does not command Oren;
- 07 assists exterior rescue/debris marking.

Outcome:
- convoy remains together;
- one escort damaged 4–6 months;
- seven injured, two serious;
- no death;
- hostile group withdraws after one craft disabled.

Cost:
- 380 t cargo is permanently lost in thermal/debris damage.

Hook:
- Vesper reports munitions and relay damage during the same hours.

# Episode 523 — 지키지 못한 탄약창

Date:
- CY 744-07-13.

Goal:
- account for the under-resourced Vesper/Outer front.

Result:
- 920 t munitions/countermeasure stock destroyed or made permanently unusable;
- one route relay damaged 3–5 months;
- three workers/crew injured, none serious;
- no death;
- hostile scouts obtain current response observations.

Finding:
- the Crown convoy draw was a material contributing factor, not the sole cause; prior recalls and tender/repair limits also mattered.

Decision:
- publish both protected-person result and uncovered-front cost without making Oren sole villain or hero.

Reward:
- allocation tradeoff remains visible.

Cost:
- Outer Front and Ardo supporters demand stronger theater priority rules.

Hook:
- convoy reaches destination, but Outer Front sends a direct demand for relief and recognition.

# Episode 524 — 모두 도착한 호송선

Date:
- CY 744-07-15.

Final convoy result:
- 20 ships total;
- 24,800 people, all accounted/alive;
- original cargo 6,460 t;
- permanent loss 380 t;
- delivered current cargo 6,080 t;
- one escort damaged 4–6 months;
- seven injured, two serious;
- no death.

Fleet state after scheduled recalls/departures and damage:
- 23 combat connected;
- 21 support connected;
- 14 combat R2+;
- one damaged convoy escort remains connected but unavailable.

Decision:
- classify Crown/civilian mission as valid success with explicit Vesper/Outer cost, not a claim to Crown fleet command.

Reward:
- Crown/civilian escort commitment enters Charter precedent.

Cost:
- 1,300 t total new cargo/munition loss across convoy and uncovered front; route relay damaged; Outer Front trust worsens.

Final hook:
- independent Outer Front commander Asel Varon offers fourteen-day tactical cooperation but rejects Compact/Charter ownership.
