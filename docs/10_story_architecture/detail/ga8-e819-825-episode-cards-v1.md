# Episode 819 — 네 개의 건국은 모두 일부만 진실이다

Date:
- CY 745-12-21 to 12-22.

Goal:
- identify the four selective publication campaigns without declaring one complete history.

Published bundles:
- Imperial Continuity network: 460 objects;
- Independence Exodus network: 460 objects;
- Helix Contract Founding network: 460 objects;
- Neutral Custodial network: 460 objects;
- total 1,840 distinct governance/founding objects.

Preliminary findings:
- each bundle contains authentic records;
- each omits records that weaken its founding claim;
- the four histories describe different phases, institutions and legal vocabularies;
- no bundle proves sole founding title.

Decision:
- preserve all four as valid partial histories with omission notices;
- do not issue takedown orders that the federation lacks authority to enforce;
- require federation publications to link provenance and known exclusions.

Reward:
- the next record war begins from partial validity rather than one fake/one true binary.

Cost:
- political networks gain simple public narratives faster than the audited process can respond.

Hook:
- one Helix bundle uses the unauthorized snapshot’s translation layer, tying the preservation breach to a live property claim.

# Episode 820 — 복사한 자의 책임

Date:
- CY 745-12-23 to 12-24.

Goal:
- resolve the preservation contractor’s current liability without inventing malicious intent.

Findings:
- the team acted from credible preservation fear;
- it exceeded the authorized scope;
- current personal-memory segments and community-security structures were copied;
- the sealed snapshot later influenced one outside property narrative through a leaked translation layer;
- no proof establishes sale of the underlying sealed data.

Sanctions and remedies:
- archive-access suspension for 120 days;
- contribution to AI-person and descendant privacy claims;
- mandatory provenance disclosure for derived translation tools;
- deletion of unauthorized local working copies under witnessed process;
- the evidentiary master remains sealed under joint custody pending claims and review.

Decision:
- distinguish negligent/unauthorized preservation from intentional cultural theft while imposing real consequences.

Reward:
- good intent does not become immunity, and discipline does not require a fabricated villain.

Cost:
- the expedition loses experienced preservation capacity during an unstable period.

Hook:
- all six proposed signatories must now decide whether the revised compact protects enough against both loss and extraction.

Hook:

# Episode 822 — 세라트 생존기록 협약

Date:
- CY 745-12-28.

Goal:
- put the temporary settlement into force without converting it into a permanent constitution.

Instrument:
- `세라트 생존기록 협약 / Serrat Living Archive Compact`.

Term:
- 120 days;
- no automatic renewal;
- explicit re-ratification or replacement required.

Core rules:
- current AI personhood presumption without fixed final denominator;
- no archive title transfer;
- no permanent expedition-entry right;
- five record classes with different access burdens;
- service-query channel without historical or commercial reuse;
- co-translation and purpose labels;
- individual/current-memory consent;
- bounded emergency preservation and automatic quarantine;
- plural community and descendant representation;
- claims, appeal, exit and outside-mirror evidence.

Decision:
- compact takes effect with six signatories and two observers.

Reward:
- living rights and current services gain a workable temporary order.

Cost:
- permanent sovereignty, inheritance, deletion and archive-wide custody remain unresolved.

Hook:
- the first descendant group enters Serrat under habitat and AI-community conditions.

# Episode 823 — 돌아온 사람은 소유자가 되지 않는다

Date:
- CY 745-12-29 to CY 746-01-03.

Goal:
- test physical cultural return without title transfer or forced intimacy.

Pilot:
- 120 descendant participants;
- designated memorial and language spaces only;
- no access to sealed current-person memories;
- habitat and AI escorts selected by their own communities;
- participants may withdraw without losing future claims.

Outcome:
- 116 complete the visit;
- 4 withdraw after disagreement over ritual recording and privacy;
- no death or serious injury;
- 38 language/name corrections are accepted into the co-translation ledger;
- 11 claims remain disputed.

Decision:
- treat withdrawal and unresolved claims as valid outcomes, not pilot failure.

Reward:
- return becomes current practice while AI habitation remains real.

Cost:
- public pressure grows for mass access that the compact cannot safely support.

Hook:
- service users demand the same transparency for the 3,700 unresolved access cases.

# Episode 825 — 살아 있는 자가 없는 문명은 없다

Date:
- CY 746-01-09 to 01-12.

Goal:
- close the first GA8 subact with a temporary rights order and a larger historical conflict.

Final B08-01 findings:
- Serrat is not abandoned property;
- Lumen and Orison are active political communities with internal dissent;
- three current AI persons died permanently during stabilization;
- descendants hold real cultural, language, memorial and access claims without automatic habitat ownership;
- 44,900 of 48,600 immediate-risk users recover/retain service, while 3,700 remain unresolved;
- no single AI person denominator is final;
- the 120-day compact has six signatories and two observers;
- the federation holds no archive sovereignty or permanent entry right;
- Rian remains expedition commander only;
- Haren’s settled current D4 responsibility remains intact inside the disputed Blood Admiral history.

Four-history handoff:
- Imperial Continuity history treats Serrat as an emergency reserve of a lawful state;
- Independence Exodus history treats it as a refugee-built autonomous sanctuary;
- Helix Contract Founding history treats it as licensed infrastructure created through enforceable investment and service contracts;
- Neutral Custodial history treats it as a multi-party trust whose custodians never held full ownership;
- all four contain authentic evidence and consequential omissions.

Reward:
- living communities, descendants and service users possess enforceable temporary rights.

Cost:
- the very compact that prevents immediate extraction cannot decide which founding law created its own authority.

Final hook:
- E826 opens when Empire, Independence, Helix and Neutral delegations each demand that their partial founding history govern the next 120 days, while Serrat’s living parties refuse to be reduced to evidence for someone else’s state.
