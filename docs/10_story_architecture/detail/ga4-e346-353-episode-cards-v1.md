# Episode 346 — 이름을 계속 쓰게 하는 왕관

Date:
- CY 743-10-26.

POV:
- Elian Argen, Registry workers, cross-provincial families/care users and hearing observers.

Visible goal:
- demonstrate what the registered-heir seal preserves without using it as an executive or military command.

Hidden pressure:
- millions of records need one recognized continuity source, and every accepted seal can be framed as accession.

## Scene units

1. **Bounded test set** — 1,240 cross-jurisdiction identity, inheritance, guardianship and care records; 86 are time-critical medical/family cases.
2. **Registry action** — Elian’s seal validates provenance and continuity of person/family records while local current-person proof and Regency/Continuity checks remain.
3. **Human result** — urgent care transfers, family authority and inheritance/household access proceed for the 86 critical cases; 1,087 of 1,240 clear inside two days, 153 require appeal or conflicting-record review.
4. **No accession clause** — Elian signs a public statement that use of the Registry seal does not constitute obedience, taxation, fleet command or coronation recognition.

Decisive choice:
- use dynastic/Registry legitimacy as a bounded evidentiary service rather than a claim to immediate total rule.

Reward:
- identity and family continuity work across institutions that otherwise reject each other’s codes.

Cost:
- duplicate fees and appeal burdens remain; people without recognized lineage/current proof benefit less; Crown supporters demand broader recognition.

Hook:
- a household Guard unit asks Elian to use the same seal to validate a military obedience order.

Decisive choice:
- Elian preserves the Crown’s evidentiary and ceremonial function by refusing premature military command.

Reward:
- one claimant demonstrates self-limitation and prevents a household-force escalation.

Cost:
- he cannot guarantee payroll, fleet defense or executive coordination; hardline dynasts seek a stronger proxy or forged broader seal.

Function ruling:
- Elian offers real continuity of identity, symbol and lawful inheritance but not a complete state apparatus.

Hook:
- Mara Velen’s civil service clears records but now faces a multi-province payroll and hospital shortfall that a seal alone cannot fund.

Function ruling:
