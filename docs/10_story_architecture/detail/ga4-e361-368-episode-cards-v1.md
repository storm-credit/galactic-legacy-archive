# Episode 361 — 여섯 개의 명령이 멈춘 회랑

Date:
- CY 743-11-25.

POV:
- Ora Fenn, hospital/depot/ship operators, Vern Soa and four-function mission staff.

Visible goal:
- establish a current mission map before claimant offices issue competing fixes.

Hidden pressure:
- central offices see one stalled corridor; local people see three physical lanes, four care chains, two settlement belts and incompatible service minima.

## Scene units

1. **Physical map** — three corridors have different mass, recovery, security and pressure limits; one cannot take hospital and grain traffic in the same recovery cycle.
2. **Service map** — hospital reserves, depot issue, mobile settlement sanitation and worker/pay clocks do not share one priority order.
3. **Authority map** — Ora controls physical movement; hospital/depot/user bodies control current service acceptance; six armed commands retain separate chains under Dor’s freeze; claimant teams control only their function.
4. **Mission boundary** — no office may treat its data/command as current physical truth without local confirmation; no local body may use urgent service as accession leverage.

Decisive choice:
- Graybridge issues one current-state board and keeps physical/service control local while admitting all four claimant functions.

Reward:
- the mission begins with actual dependencies, stop rights and current clocks rather than claimant speeches.

Cost:
- central optimization slows and every office must expose data/limits to local review.

Hook:
- Elian’s Registry team can identify the patients and households fastest, but 3,200 mobile/unregistered people have no recognized cross-protocol record.

# Episode 362 — 이름은 찾았지만 침대는 없다

Date:
- CY 743-11-27.

POV:
- Elian Registry staff, hospital admission teams, Vern/mobile households and current identity reviewers.

Visible goal:
- reconcile patient, caregiver, worker and household records for admission and cargo release.

Hidden pressure:
- recognized identity can clear legal barriers but cannot create beds, oxygen, transport or current service priority.

## Scene units

1. **Registry run** — Elian’s seal/provenance chain reconciles 21,400 of 24,600 service-worker/household records; 3,200 mobile/unregistered remain outside ordinary codes.
2. **Current proof** — Vern/local services provide present-person, care, work and household evidence; 2,480 of the 3,200 receive provisional mission standing, 720 require further appeal/safety review.
3. **Physical limit** — hospitals accept identities but still lack 186 suitable beds/transfer places and current oxygen/cold-chain capacity.
4. **No dynastic allocation** — Elian refuses a household request to prioritize registered Crown dependents over current clinical/service need.

Decisive choice:
- combine Registry provenance with local current-person proof and leave physical/care allocation to affected services.

Reward:
- 23,880 of 24,600 records become currently usable; mobile/unregistered people gain a real provisional path.

Cost:
- 720 remain delayed; no bed, payroll or safe corridor appears from identity alone.

Hook:
- Mara’s civil system can pay staff, beds and carriers, but two provinces refuse each other’s clearing codes.

# Episode 364 — 안전한 회랑이 틀린 순서일 때

POV:
- Dor military coordinators, six armed commands, Ora, hospital/depot vessel masters and local defense/service observers.

Visible goal:
- create protected movement lanes and prevent armed claimant units from fighting around the service convoy.

Hidden pressure:

## Scene units

1. **Freeze/separation** — Dor assigns six armed commands to distinct holding/escort lanes and verifies weapon states; two commands accept only after pay/legal recognition.
2. **Initial order** — military geometry places three grain/utility ships first and hospital ships later to minimize armed crossing.
3. **Service stop** — hospital/Ora/Vern actors show two care chains fail before the later slot; they halt the sequence under the Four-Function Order.
4. **Revised screen** — Dor accepts one higher-risk but bounded hospital-first lane with additional rescue/physical watchers and keeps the remaining force freeze intact.

Decisive choice:
- Dor changes the militarily optimal order to respect current service priority rather than treating defense safety as total command.

Reward:
- armed commands remain separated and a viable hospital-first corridor exists.

Cost:
- escort fuel/crew risk rises; one grain ship loses its priority window; armed hardliners accuse Dor of subordinating defense to local bodies.

Hook:
- Isa’s service network authenticates the revised movement but flags 1,060 current users as classification conflicts that could stop medical release.

POV:

Visible goal:
- approve a combined operation that uses all four central functions without creating a hidden common sovereign.

Hidden pressure:
- a joint secretariat or chair can become the real tie-break; local actors can also evade responsibility by forcing every conflict back to the hearing.

## Scene units

1. **Five operating fields** — Registry/person/provenance; civil/pay/order; Protector/security; Continuity/service/authentication; Graybridge physical/affected-service custody.

3. **Manifest** — 9 service ships, 1,180 t cargo and 96 people; three phases under hospital-first, grain-second, utility/housing-third sequence.
4. **Standing alternates** — local and claimant alternates named; no loss of one officeholder transfers all fields.

Decisive choice:
- formalize Graybridge as a five-field mission rather than treating four claimant offices as a complete government.

Reward:
- a legally funded, physically safe, authenticated and defended operation becomes executable.

Cost:
- preparation takes most of the remaining care reserve; no spare capacity for claimant/political cargo.

Hook:

Hidden pressure:

Cost:

Reward:

Cost:
