# Episode 311 — 육천이백스물두 개의 다음 주소

Date:
- CY 743-08-30.

POV:
- Ari Ven, T-19 households, care/work/service contacts and receiving districts.

Visible goal:
- create current placement paths for all 6,222 survivors without treating the destroyed habitat as one permanent community or political bloc.

Hidden pressure:
- every placement shifts school, clinic, work, transport and representation costs onto another district or ship.

## Scene units

1. **Five pathways** — survivor households choose among Transit-adjacent clustered modules, Civic/Garden placements, route/ship/Surface temporary work-residence, Neutral/outside relocation and long-care/hospital pathways.
2. **Exact current distribution** — 2,410 choose Transit-adjacent modules; 1,780 Civic/Garden; 1,146 route/ship/Surface; 516 Neutral/outside; 370 long-care/hospital. Total: 6,222.
3. **Different reasons** — care proximity, school continuity, work, household separation, distrust of local command, desire to stay near T-19 and desire to leave Ardis all appear.
4. **Representation** — each pathway selects or rotates service/claim contacts; Ari coordinates shared evidence and safety claims only.

Decisive choice:
- survivors retain multiple destinations and political positions rather than being resettled as one controllable constituency.

Reward:
- every survivor has a current placement, care or transfer path.

Cost:
- families/households split, receiving queues worsen and no path restores home or equal service.

Hook:
- Imperial command supporters offer faster transport and recognized housing/pay if survivor groups endorse full emergency authority.

Decisive choice:

Reward:

Cost:
- political fracture deepens; outside convoy clocks worsen while terms remain unsettled.

Hook:

# Episode 313 — 명령이 오지 않아도 할 일

Date:
- CY 743-09-02.

POV:
- Rema Dor, Ari/Savo, Mobile Rescue, defense and public-record contacts.

Visible goal:
- convert T-19 lessons into standing delegated actions that reduce delay without creating hidden local sovereignty or reckless autonomy.

Hidden pressure:
- standing rules can be exploited by forged local status, political actors or commanders avoiding responsibility.

## Scene units

1. **Four delegated actions** — direct physical pressure/fire isolation; staged evacuation for defined high-risk groups; Mobile Rescue response under physical distress + two-source confirmation; local command-field quarantine when signed packets carry unconfirmed target/location data.
2. **Thresholds** — action begins after current physical triggers and 90 seconds without a valid conflicting stop, not after full political agreement; fire/weapon/denial actuation still needs separate authority.
3. **Audit/withdrawal** — every action logs source, physical witness, affected people/service and who may stop or review; no delegated rule becomes permanent law automatically.
4. **Live exercise** — a Garden pressure fault is contained in 2 minutes 18 seconds without waiting for five-front command; one unnecessary evacuation lane is cancelled by current local evidence.

Decisive choice:
- authorize bounded standing service/rescue actions while retaining stricter fire, route and denial thresholds.

Reward:
- the specific 11-minute T-19 confirmation gap cannot repeat unchanged.

Cost:
- local operators carry more responsibility and liability; centralizers argue the patch remains slower and less recognized than full command.

Hook:
- with command delay reduced, the final obstacle is physical: B-2 and B-4 cannot support the outside convoy at current state.
