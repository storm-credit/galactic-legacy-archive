# Episode 943 — 예측을 쓰지 않는 것도 선택이다

Date:
- CY 747-02-24 to 02-28.

Goal:
- confront the case for retaining continuity profiles.

Aven’s evidence:
- profile-assisted verification still detects forged cargo and hostile automation;
- broad rollback increases matched attack and delay risk;
- current reforms depend on the same shared data spine.

Affected response:
- prediction can remain evidence without becoming inherited status;
- the problem is reuse, opacity, circular proof and absent expiry;
- communities must correct the model before harm, not only appeal afterward.

Decision:
- retain prediction under purpose separation, expiry and affected correction;
- prohibit cross-domain reuse without a new current legal basis.

Reward:
- the useful model survives.

Cost:
- separate systems lose some speed and data-network effects.

Final hook:
- E944 tests the reform during a coordinated hostile operation when Rian is again offered wider authority but no central key.
