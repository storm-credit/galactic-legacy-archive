# Episode 698 — 냉장이 끊긴 약

Date:
- CY 744-12-28 to 12-30.

Goal:
- restore a medical cold-chain route broken by two independent capacity/security closures.

Affected:
- 74,000 patients/caregivers across six receiving/holdout sites;
- 2,900 t temperature-controlled medicine and equipment.

Decision:
- use three smaller reciprocal routes, Neutral cold storage and local priority transfer rather than force one corridor open.

Outcome:
- 2,330 t delivered/secured;
- 570 t destroyed/downgraded;
- 63 confirmed downstream deaths and 214 serious treatment harms.

Reward:
- most treatment chains survive and route substitution is proven.

Cost:
- smaller routes lose capacity for food, missing search and family movement.

Hook:
- payroll and identity services fail when one Continuity/Registry corridor suspends cross-region records.

# Episode 699 — 월급을 받지 못한 도착자

Date:
- CY 744-12-31 to CY 745-01-02.

Goal:
- keep earned pay, treatment identity and family benefits active without political recognition or one central root.

Affected:
- 241,000 arrived workers/dependents;
- 38,000 holdout/missing/captured-linked family claims;
- multiple regions use incompatible records and authority.

Decision:
- deploy purpose-limited mirrors, local current proof and reciprocal settlement; earned pay/urgent care cannot be withheld for recognition.

Outcome:
- 206,000 current cases restored;
- 73,000 remain partial/manual/unresolved with overlap;
- no route ownership transfer.

Reward:
- service identity travels without a single sovereign registry.

Cost:
- fraud/error review and unfunded obligations grow; some regions refuse nonurgent benefits.

Hook:
- food convoys divert toward better-paying regions and leave two overloaded receiving zones short.

# Episode 700 — 식량이 비싼 곳으로 간다

Date:
- CY 745-01-03 to 01-05.

Goal:
- prevent market/insurance diversion from becoming famine or forced political alignment.

Affected:
- 315,000 people under seven-day ration risk;
- 19 food/service ships and 14,800 t cargo.

Decision:
- create minimum delivery obligations, transparent price/credit limits and current receiver/crew protection rather than total requisition.

Outcome:
- 12,400 t delivered;
- 1,600 t diverted under lawful commercial terms;
- 800 t lost/spoiled;
- 118 confirmed deaths and 1,420 severe malnutrition/medical harms.

Reward:
- minimum food chain remains and private/crew actors are not fully expropriated.

Cost:
- wealthy regions still buy more resilience; poorer routes demand stronger federation power.

Hook:
- one power/habitat region needs parts trapped behind a local environmental closure.

# Episode 701 — 문을 열면 꺼지는 도시

Date:
- CY 745-01-06 to 01-07.

Goal:
- move power/habitat repair parts without overloading the closing region’s own environment.

Affected:
- 86,000 people in three habitat/receiving sites;
- 4,200 t couplers, filters and thermal parts.

Decision:
- staged low-mass transfer, local worker stop and reciprocal replacement cargo; no heavy convoy.

Outcome:
- 3,480 t reaches intended work;
- 720 t remains delayed;
- 42 confirmed deaths and 390 severe harms before restoration;
- closing region preserves its own thermal margin.

Reward:
- service restarts without infrastructure cascade.

Cost:
- both sides remain below safe reserve and blame each other for the deaths.

Hook:
- missing-person search corridors are being used as the only remaining legal passage for other traffic.

# Episode 702 — 실종자 이름으로 움직이는 배

Date:
- CY 745-01-08 to 01-09.

Goal:
- protect missing-person search/status while stopping commercial, military and political misuse of search corridors.

Current search set:
- 150,000 original missing;
- new information places 28,000 alive in unstable/captured/current routes;
- 6,400 confirmed dead are moved from missing to death claims;
- remaining missing status is not presumed dead.

Decision:
- separate search, rescue, family contact, evidence and other cargo fields; misuse triggers mission-specific suspension, not total search closure.

Reward:
- 28,000 people gain current location/custody evidence and family contact paths.

Cost:
- search traffic slows; one rescue craft is seized and two are damaged, with nine injuries.

Hook:
- captured/unstable regions demand direct supply even when their controlling authority is unrecognized or hostile.

# Episode 703 — 적의 구역에 보내는 약

Date:
- CY 745-01-10 to 01-11.

Goal:
- deliver current care to captured/unstable populations without legitimizing coercive custody or funding armed control.

Affected:
- 132,000 known hostile custody;
- 104,000 unstable destination/service;
- 74,000 route detention/unconfirmed custody;
- total 310,000.

Decision:
- use monitored humanitarian corridors, receiver/medical attestations, prisoner/civilian separation and no political-recognition clause.

Outcome:
- supplies/contact reach 54,000 people;
- 21,000 additional current locations become verified;
- armed controllers seize part of one shipment;
- no recognition or full corridor guarantee.

Reward:
- captured/unstable people are not erased from service obligations.

Cost:
- aid may prolong coercive control; local/open regions argue scarce supplies are being diverted.

Hook:
- reciprocal diversion causes one previously open corridor to impose emergency quotas.

# Episode 704 — 한 길을 열어 세 길을 닫다

Date:
- CY 745-01-12.

Goal:
- trace how emergency substitution overloads other corridors and causes secondary closures.

Finding:
- medical, food and captured-aid substitutions add 43% traffic to two small routes;
- one receiving region reaches housing/medical saturation;
- three neighboring authorities announce quotas or closure.

Decision:
- create a network dependency model with current downstream loss, not corridor-by-corridor decisions only.

Outcome:
- one corridor remains open under 36-hour quota cycles;
- two close pending support;
- 29 confirmed downstream deaths and 176 serious harms occur during diversion gaps.

Reward:
- cascade becomes measurable and shared.

Cost:
- regions fear that opening any route creates obligations they cannot exit.

Hook:
- Haren proposes minimum mutual obligations and exit costs rather than a central master route.

# Episode 705 — 하렌의 최소의무표

Date:
- CY 745-01-13 to 01-14.

POV:
- Haren Doan and affected route/service actors.

Goal:
- build an emergency obligation table before any formal route federation exists.

Fields:
- current medical/food/power minimum;
- holdout/captured/missing contact;
- local safety and service capacity;
- escort/inspection and weapons conditions;
- pay/credit/insurance contribution;
- notice, duration, appeal and residual duty after closure.

Decision:
- Haren proposes reciprocal minimums scaled to current capacity and benefit, not one central command or unconditional opening.

Reward:
- regions can compare what they owe and what closing a route pushes onto others.

Cost:
- Haren becomes associated with route-denial logic and receives access to cross-corridor consequence models that can later enable D4-level power.

Hook:
- emergency operations must stabilize enough corridors to test the obligation table.

Date:
- CY 745-01-15.

Goal:
- publish the first regional dependency and loss state.

Arrived-dependent baseline:
- 1.14m.

By E706:

Confirmed post-Orpheus route/service cascade deaths through E706:

Reward:

Cost:

Final hook:
- E707 convenes a temporary multi-region route table while several authorities refuse participation or demand vetoes.
