# Episode 169 — 다섯 계약

Date:
- CY 742-09-05.

POV:
- 테오, with group contacts holding final consent.

Visible goal:
- replace the rejected unified insurance policy with five compatible but separate destination/transport agreements.

Hidden pressure:
- splitting contracts raises total premiums and creates gaps between jurisdictions.

## Scene 169.1 — Unified policy offer

Offer:
- one community policy at lower premium;
- one representative signature;
- common destination-change restrictions;
- shared medical/identity data pool;
- insurer may redirect individuals within covered destinations.

Benefit:
- lower cost and simpler claims.

Coercive shadow:
- erases private destination, individual caregiver and stay/exit choices.

## Scene 169.2 — Five contract stacks

Separate agreements:
- care compact and medical passage;
- Ardis provisional work/residency and transport;
- route employment/mutual-credit and crew agreements;
- K-13 continuing-service/claims compact;
- private-route escrow and safety verification.

Action:
- each group contact reviews only granted scope;
- competent adults sign individual/household terms.

## Scene 169.3 — Interoperability clauses

Shared minimums:
- emergency contact;
- medical transfer summary by consent;
- claim portability;
- right to appeal placement;
- communication/record continuity;
- no automatic disclosure across all contracts.

## Scene 169.4 — Premium and funding split

Decisive choice:
- accept higher combined cost and shared contingency pool rather than unified control.

Immediate result:
- all five contract paths become legally executable.

Reward:
- choice is translated into enforceable rights, not only preferences.

Cost:
- insurance/legal/escrow cost rises by 38,000–72,000 BSC;
- shared fund requires multi-group approval.

Relationship state:
- Theo’s authority is to reconcile contracts, not optimize people into one cheaper package.

Carried state:
- ships and seats must be assigned to the signed contracts.

Final hook:
- the Neutral medical passage advances departure by thirty-six hours, forcing the care group to leave before the others can gather for a common farewell.

Specialist panel:
- P02/P03, M05, C02/C04, X01/X03/X04.

Date:
- CY 742-09-07.

POV:
- 마렌/루카 through actual vessels and route windows.

Visible goal:
- build a transport network that can move the groups without pretending the charter owns a fleet.

Hidden pressure:
- multiple small carriers reduce single-point risk but increase coordination, certification and abandonment risk.

## Scene 170.1 — Vessel roster

Front-stage vessels:
- 파루스 — escort/relay/workshop and limited passengers;
- Neutral medical passage vessel — DG-CARE;
- Ardis-bound cargo/service convoy — DG-ARDIS later segment;
- two independent route crews — parts of DG-ROUTE;
- protected private transport — DG-PRIVATE;
- K-13 local services — DG-STAY.

No vessel is subordinate to Rian except inside its accepted mission agreement.

## Scene 170.2 — Route schedule conflict

- care vessel leaves first through stable Lumen/Glasswater window;
- private route departs separately with sealed timing;
- Ardis convoy waits for Silex window and cargo/service assembly;
- route-group crews depart according to employers/households.

## Scene 170.3 — Support allocation

파루스 can provide only two of:
- medical escort to first transfer;
- Ardis convoy preparation;
- route-group training/crew placement;
- private-route security support.

Decision:
- Neutral handles care security;
- 파루스 prioritizes Ardis convoy and route-placement support;
- private transport uses its own protection/escrow.

## Scene 170.4 — Joint command refusal

Decisive choice:
- create a coordination board and emergency signals rather than one convoy commander;
- each vessel master retains safety/withdrawal authority.

Reward:
- real distributed transport plan.

Cost:
- slower communication and no ability to redirect all ships in one emergency;
- one group may face trouble without protagonist access.

Relationship state:
- Maren refuses “fleet command” language; Rian holds only assigned mission coordination.

Carried state:
- exact seats, mass and cargo displacement must be finalized.

Final hook:
- the Ardis convoy can take all nine people or the cooling/filter cargo promised to one damaged transit habitat, but not both at the current mass and route window.

# Episode 171 — 좌석의 가격

Date:
- CY 742-09-09.

POV:
- 테오/하렌.

Visible goal:
- create enough capacity for DG-ARDIS without shifting hidden harm onto another service population.

Hidden pressure:
- treating cargo as less important than people ignores that filters/cooling parts keep other people alive.

## Scene 171.1 — Mass and service conflict

Ardis convoy shortage:
- all nine people + household tools/care load exceed current protected mass by approximately 14.8 t;
- promised cooling/filter cargo serves a damaged transit habitat;
- removing it risks reserve shortfall before next shipment.

## Scene 171.2 — False simple choices

A. remove life-support cargo;
B. split the nine across later ships;
C. reduce household/work tools and increase dependence after arrival;
D. use 파루스 cargo margin and alter its own service/workshop schedule;
E. pay for an additional small carrier/route slot.

## Scene 171.3 — Combined solution

Plan:
- three Ardis travellers and 6.2 t critical cargo move on 파루스 for the first leg;
- six travel on the Ardis cargo convoy;
- noncritical household cargo follows later under insured freight;
- additional route slot purchased with shared contingency;
- destination housing/tools adjusted.

## Scene 171.4 — Who loses allocation

Cost ownership:
- 파루스 delays one paid workshop/service job;
- shared contingency spends 31,000–48,000 BSC;
- Ardis group arrives in two parts;
- some personal tools/property delayed;
- transit habitat retains critical filters.

Decisive choice:
- accept split arrival and delayed property rather than sacrifice another habitat’s current service.

Reward:
- all nine retain transport path and external service obligation is preserved.

Cost:
- reduced income, family separation for days/weeks and greater arrival dependence.

Relationship state:
- “people before cargo” is refined into current people on both sides of the allocation.

Carried state:
- care group departs first; one member is reconsidering the chosen caregiver arrangement.

Final hook:
- minutes before medical boarding, one patient withdraws consent for their registered caregiver to accompany them.

Specialist panel:
- P02/P04, M05/T03, C04, X01/X03/X04.

# Episode 175 — 호송대가 갈라지는 순간

Date:
- CY 742-09-16.

POV:
- ensemble through Haren’s coordination board and multiple vessel departures.

Visible goal:
- execute the split movement and preserve communication/claims after physical separation.

Hidden pressure:
- Helix certification withdrawal can strand non-파루스 vessels and re-centralize control over the route.

## Scene 175.1 — Five current states

- DG-CARE: departed and accepted into first medical chain;
- DG-ARDIS: eight moving in two current vessels, one protected later slot;
- DG-ROUTE: two on 파루스 trial work, two with other crews, three Open Passage/Varka arrangements;
- DG-STAY: four in K-13 compact;
- DG-PRIVATE: sealed route underway or completed beyond community visibility.

All 36 remain accounted for.

## Scene 175.2 — Communication compact

Action:
- group contacts establish:
  - emergency channel;
  - shared claim/contingency voting;
  - optional household communication;
  - private-route non-disclosure;
  - no automatic centralized archive of all lives.

## Scene 175.3 — Certification withdrawal

Affected:
- three coolant/filter assemblies on independent convoy/support craft;
- parts physically functional but warranty, diagnostics and insurance support withdrawn due mixed-standard/Jena Annex configuration.

Benefits claimed by Helix:
- prevents unsafe use after Marn failure.

Costs:
- independent carriers may be grounded or forced to replace modules they cannot afford;
- Ardis and route groups face delay/service loss.

## Scene 175.4 — Separate departures

Decisive choice:
- groups and vessel masters continue only where current safety evidence supports operation;
- one craft is held back rather than hidden as safe;
- 파루스 uses its relay to provide bounded state/audit translation, not unauthorized certification;
- Haren coordinates rerouting without recalling everyone to one ship.

Immediate result:
- community physically fragments across destinations;
- no mass casualty or clean reunification;
- one support craft and cargo remain behind, creating the next convoy/sanctions crisis.

Reward:
- the 36 retain chosen paths and continuing cross-group obligations;
- mutual-route coordination seed becomes real.

Cost:
- separation, delays, higher expenses, one held-back craft and dependence on contested certified/open-service layers.

Collection state:
- community relationship changes from one protected unit to a distributed network;
- no person/faction/territory ownership.

Mystery state:
- no new mystery; next conflict is visible industrial safety, insurance and control.

Carried state:
- E176 opens with mixed-standard coolant/filter embargo, held-back vessel and active destination routes.

Final hook:
- the grounded support craft’s captain asks the only question the new relay cannot answer: “작동하는데, 누가 안전하다고 책임질 겁니까?”
