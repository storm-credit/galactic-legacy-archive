# Episode 541 — 같은 작전을 다섯 번 설명한다

Date:
- CY 744-08-24.

Goal:
- define shared campaign intent that each formation can execute without one owner.

Shared intent:
- prevent route/depot seizure;
- preserve inhabited service and civilian movement;
- keep V08/V11 available;
- deny monopoly over supplies/keys/hulls;
- protect reciprocal rescue and lawful exit.

Decision:
- publish front-specific objectives and prohibited actions rather than one vague “defend the theater” order.

Reward:
- formations can act under isolation.

Cost:
- objectives compete for the same ready ships, ammunition, repair and rescue capacity.

Hook:
- requested force totals exceed 48 combat and 42 support, above the 40/34 envelope.

# Episode 542 — 한 척도 두 전선에 있을 수 없다

Date:

Goal:

# Episode 544 — 각자 지휘하는 같은 작전

Date:
- CY 744-09-01.

Goal:
- define formation succession and autonomous action under communications loss.

Decision:
- each front has its own commander, alternate, standing intent, safety/withdrawal rules and reporting boundary;
- Rian coordinates Grey Route/mobile mission and cross-front route timing only;
- no real-time overall tactical command.

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:
- command-authentication simulations show shared network failure for 17–43 minutes under attack.

# Episode 546 — 남겨둔 탄약

Date:
- CY 744-09-05.

Goal:
- allocate missiles/countermeasures after prior 920 t loss and quarantine.

Decision:
- preserve a shared reserve rather than fully arm every front;
- Ardo and Asel receive highest immediate combat loads;
- Oren/Civilian Chain receives 0.7 major-action equivalent plus stronger disengagement/rescue rules;

Hook:

# Episode 547 — 출항한 공동전단

Date:
- CY 744-09-07.

Goal:
- launch all five fronts under Charter v0.2 and Asel’s separate annex.

Final mission envelope:
- 40 combat + 34 support;
- 28 combat R2+;
- 2 combat + 4 support reserve included in totals but not front-stationed;
- no ship counted twice.

Decision:
- accept lawful home-defense trigger, but require current evidence, notice and rescue/asset handover before withdrawal when possible.

Reward:
- first multi-front ownerless-fleet campaign begins.

Cost:
- formations retain exit and local priorities; Civilian Chain remains knowingly under-resourced; 720 pay/family cases remain.

Final hook:
- coordinated authentication interference isolates four of the five fronts within the first campaign day.
