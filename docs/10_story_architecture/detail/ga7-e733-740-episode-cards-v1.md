# Episode 733 — 다섯 층의 서명

Date:
- CY 745-03-17.

Goal:
- publish the provisional composite anatomy of the Blood Admiral pattern.

Five layers:
1. useful predictive denial/risk doctrine and model;
2. staff manuals, weight choices and recurring omissions;
3. credential, delegation and office-succession lineages;
4. current human/local signers, reviewers and physical implementers;
5. later propaganda/history compressing distinct acts into one admiral.

Decision:
- legal and institutional inquiries must assign responsibility inside each layer and for interaction between layers;
- no layer automatically absorbs or erases another.

Reward:
- victims can pursue concrete offices, staff, signers and remedies without relying on one mythic culprit.

Cost:
- no simple arrest or single confession can resolve the harm.

Hook:
- an old combat/traffic record used as a signature control reopens Rian’s memory of Ern firing through 07 at White Dock.

# Episode 734 — 에른 바르카가 쏘지 않은 탄환

Date:
- CY 745-03-18 to 03-19.

Mystery payoff:
- M-012 `Ern withheld fire` decisive payoff.

Goal:
- reconstruct the White Dock firing geometry from physical telemetry, traffic logs, pressure-module damage, civilian tug movement and Ern’s current testimony/records.

Rian’s remembered/future account:
- Ern aimed at or through 07 and betrayed the cell during the White Dock conflict.

Recovered current geometry:
- 07 stood between Ern and a hostile/compromised relay-boarder route;
- a civilian tug and pressure-separated medical module crossed the same firing cone;
- Ern tracked the shot, delayed and shifted aim rather than fire through 07 and the civilians;
- the final discharge/weapon action targeted the other route/threat after the tug cleared;
- his separate departure and later opposition were real, but this specific moment was withheld fire, not attempted murder.

Independent choice:
- Ern protected the civilian/pressure route without asking Rian or rejoining the protagonist group;
- he chose another route/person and accepted being read as hostile.

Decision:
- correct the current record and Rian’s causal model while preserving Ern’s other conflicts and autonomy.

Reward:
- M-012 is decisively reconstructed; future-memory motive error is exposed.

Cost:
- Rian loses another apparently certain betrayal memory; Ern does not become loyal, absolved or collectible.

Hook:
- the same reconstruction method shows several Blood Admiral cases contain accurate physical acts wrapped in false later motive summaries.

# Episode 736 — D4라는 빈 칸

Date:
- CY 745-03-22.

Goal:
- identify the highest denial class and what must exist before it can be used.

D4 class characteristics:
- multi-corridor or theater-scale denial/priority effect;
- predictive downstream-loss model;
- executive/escalation field beyond local physical or military stop;
- duration and residual-duty decisions affecting other regions;
- explicit named authority and later review requirement.

Finding:
- D4 is a capability/decision class in several inherited doctrines, not proof of one historical signer;
- no current valid master D4 key exists;
- components could be assembled from route-table coordination, executive mandate, military guarantees and Continuity/model services.

Decision:
- publish the class and prohibit silent composite activation.

Reward:
- the future danger becomes governable before use.

Cost:
- regions facing real cascades now demand that someone receive the power.

Hook:
- current route forecasts show a multi-region crisis where D4 could plausibly save lives.
