# Episode 473 — 누가 지휘를 빌려주는가

Date:
- CY 744-04-14.

POV:
- Rian, Ardo Rev, Sira Keon, Oren Roman, Tav Oren, Messa Rin and captains.

Visible goal:
- determine what authority a ship or formation can lend for the next mission without transferring ownership.

Hidden pressure:
- every negotiator uses “command” to mean a different bundle: objective, battle order, discipline, weapons, route, support or political allegiance.

Scene units:
1. **Bundle separation** — mission intent, theater allocation, formation tactics, captain safety, weapons release, support priority and political recognition are listed separately.
2. **Ardo’s demand** — one battle commander for active alert windows and a clear succession chain.
3. **Sira/Oren limits** — provincial recall/home defense and Crown/civilian protection cannot disappear inside battle efficiency.
4. **Tav/Messa limits** — repair/safety and crew/family/rescue obligations cannot become subordinate favors.

Decisive choice:
- use the principle `command is lent for a mission` and forbid title/pay/root-key transfer through mission acceptance.

Reward:
- the charter’s object becomes a named mission authority rather than a new navy.

Cost:
- no agreement yet on emergency default, recall or support priority; two formations leave the table as observers.

Hook:
- Dor offers Rian a broader temporary task command if he accepts General Staff default authority.

# Episode 474 — 제독이 되지 않는 조건

Date:
- CY 744-04-16.

POV:
- Rian, Dor Kein, Ardo and Maren Dove.

Visible goal:
- decide whether Rian should accept a broader command that can prevent another thirteen-minute delay.

Hidden pressure:
- the offer is operationally credible and popular after the losses; refusing it may cost lives.

Offer:
- 21-day Vesper–Talren task command;
- authority over up to 24 accepted combat ships and supporting elements;
- General Staff authentication/default battle sequence;
- no formal hull title, but strong practical control over missions and allocation.

Scene units:
1. **Real benefit** — one common command staff and current picture reduce expected alert response by 6–10 minutes.
2. **Capture path** — the same staff would prioritize ammunition, escorts and repair, indirectly controlling ships that never accepted Rian personally.
3. **Maren’s boundary** — ship captains must retain safety/surrender and mission acceptance; `회랑새` cannot become ceremonial flagship of a personal fleet.
4. **Rian’s answer** — accept command only for one named route/flight task group and one alert window under external audit; reject 24-ship standing command.

Decisive choice:
- take bounded operational responsibility without accepting default ownership-like allocation power.

Reward:
- Rian receives a defined 6-combat/5-support route group appointment for the next mission-planning phase.

Cost:
- broader task command remains with Ardo/Dor-aligned proposal; critics argue Rian avoided responsibility.

Hook:
- Charter drafters discover no shared rule for who pays and repairs a ship damaged under borrowed command.

# Episode 475 — 헌장에 없는 청구서

Date:
- CY 744-04-18.

POV:
- Haren, Tav, Messa, casualty/family representatives and formation quartermasters.

Visible goal:
- complete a Charter v0.1 draft that assigns command without hiding costs.

Hidden pressure:
- formations will lend ships only if loss, repair, pay, family and ammunition debts are recognized; no actor can fund all guarantees.

Draft v0.1 agreed fields:
1. shared mission intent;
2. geography and duration;
3. alert/battle command and succession;
4. formation/captain tactical and safety authority;
5. prohibited-order/technical/medical/physical stops;
6. crew pay/oath/audit standing;
7. purpose-limited authentication and courier fallback;
8. immediate rescue/casualty record;
9. minimum support allocation and debt ledger;
10. exit/sunset and after-action review.

Still unresolved:
- enforceable provincial recall costs;
- full pension/family guarantee;
- repair-priority board;
- surrendered/prize ship title;
- parole and victim claims;
- permanent ammunition pool;
- default command outside named alerts.

Scene units:
1. **Loss invoice** — `오래된 계절`, 4 deaths, 19 injuries and 3,060 t cargo loss enter the draft as current obligations.
2. **Funding split** — Treasury, provinces, mission users, yards and insurance accept only partial shares.
3. **No free heroism** — Haren rejects recording unpaid support as voluntary contribution; Messa requires named shortfalls.
4. **Draft publication** — the incomplete charter is made public before signature so crews, workers and local users can object.

Decisive choice:
- publish and carry a visibly incomplete Charter v0.1 draft rather than hide unfunded obligations or declare a finished fleet.

Reward:
- ten executable core clauses and a shared debt/loss record exist.

Cost:
- Charter is not enacted; family, repair, recall and surrender systems remain open; several ships condition future participation.

Final hook:
- a mobile repair/pay convoy is located under disputed claim, and the unfinished Charter cannot yet decide who protects or owns it.
