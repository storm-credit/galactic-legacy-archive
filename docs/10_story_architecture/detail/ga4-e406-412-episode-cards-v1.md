# Episode 406 — 왕좌 없는 협약에 왕의 자리를 만든다

Date:
- CY 744-01-28.

POV:
- claimant officeholders, seven-field participants, Single Emergency Sovereign petitioners and hearing drafters.

Visible goal:
- draft a structure that can later host a sovereign without rewriting every operating field or repeating emergency rule.

Hidden pressure:
- an apparently ceremonial reference office can dominate through money, appointments, recognition and default rules.

## Scene units

1. **Vacant office** — future sovereign may issue public mandates, receive foreign/provincial recognition and represent state continuity; no direct physical, civil, military, Continuity, Registry, affected-service or evidence actuation.
2. **Claimant positions** — Elian accepts symbolic/Registry reference; Mara wants executable budget/appointment link; Dor wants bounded emergency declaration; Isa demands no root/service transfer.
3. **Pilot/local concern** — participants ask who controls budgets, field appointments, timeouts and external treaties when the sovereign cannot actuate directly.
4. **No candidate decision** — draft creates a vacant structural office, not Elian, Mara, Dor, Isa or Rian’s accession.

Decisive choice:
- examine the office as a power system before debating who might occupy it.

Reward:
- final sovereign selection is separated from operating-field design.

Cost:
- every claimant bloc sees a different path to future control inside the same vacant office.

Hook:
- the first budget simulation lets the nonactuating sovereign starve three fields without issuing one operational order.

# Episode 407 — 예산으로 누르는 버튼

Date:
- CY 744-01-29.

POV:
- Mara civil treasury staff, pilot fields, hospitals/operators and public auditors.

Visible goal:
- determine whether sovereign budget authority can coexist with field independence.

Hidden pressure:
- separate budgets can prevent capture but make emergency redistribution slow and fiscally incoherent.

## Scene units

1. **Budget simulation** — sovereign reference office receives one consolidated emergency appropriation and allocates among seven fields.
2. **Capture result** — direct physical maintenance, affected-user appeal and evidence/audit can be underfunded while civil/military/Registry/Continuity remain formally independent.
3. **Current users** — hospitals and operators show delayed maintenance/appeal can change outcomes without any direct actuation order.
4. **Revision** — core field minimums, separate appropriations, public transfer ledger and affected/physical emergency reserve; sovereign may propose but not unilaterally withhold or redirect protected minima.

Decisive choice:
- deny the sovereign consolidated discretionary control over field survival.

Reward:
- operating fields cannot be silently captured by starvation.

Cost:
- budget transfers require more public procedure and may respond slower than one treasury/executive chain.

Hook:
- even with protected budgets, a sovereign who appoints field heads can shape every future decision.

# Episode 408 — 임명권은 명령보다 오래간다

Date:
- CY 744-01-30.

POV:
- field workers/users, claimant offices, provincial bodies and appointment-law drafters.

Visible goal:
- prevent sovereign appointment/removal from converting seven fields into one patronage chain.

Hidden pressure:
- fully independent domain selection can create unaccountable guilds and fragmented legitimacy.

## Scene units

1. **Appointment model** — sovereign nominates heads/alternates for all seven fields and may remove for emergency incapacity.
2. **Capture result** — formal actuation remains separate, but every field anticipates renewal, removal and career dependence.
3. **Domain response** — each field defines its selecting/confirming bodies: Registry institutions, civil/provincial services, military commands, Continuity custody, physical operators, affected users and evidence/appeal bodies.
4. **Revision** — sovereign may nominate external representatives or one candidate list; domain confirmation/removal and term limits control field office; emergency suspension requires evidence + alternate activation + rapid review.

Decisive choice:
- split nomination, confirmation, removal and succession rather than granting one sovereign personnel control.

Reward:
- fields keep operational independence and external legitimacy can still enter appointments.

Cost:
- appointments become slower and contested; domains may entrench insiders or reject needed reform.

Hook:
- external governments and provinces refuse to negotiate with seven field signatures and demand one recognized representative.

# Episode 409 — 대표하는 사람이 서명하는 조약

Date:
- CY 744-01-31.

POV:
- foreign/provincial envoys, claimant officeholders, route/service institutions and hearing counsel.

Visible goal:
- define sovereign external representation without letting treaties reassign internal fields and people.

Hidden pressure:
- counterparties need one recognized signature; internal ratification can make agreements too slow or unreliable.

## Scene units

1. **External need** — defense, trade, debt, care and route agreements require one recognizable state counterpart.
2. **Capture path** — sovereign could bind budgets, military access, root/service standards, local assets or people through external treaty even without domestic actuation.
3. **Field classification** — ceremonial/recognition statements; bounded current agreements; permanent asset/person/key/territorial commitments receive different ratification.
4. **Revision** — sovereign may sign representation instruments only after relevant field and affected/provincial approval; emergency agreements expire and cannot convert into permanent transfer automatically.

Decisive choice:
- preserve one external voice while denying it power to bypass internal field/affected-body conditions.

Reward:
- external recognition and negotiation remain possible.

Cost:
- counterparties face slower ratification and may prefer central rival governments or demand higher guarantees.

Hook:
- drafters propose a timeout rule: if fields fail to respond, the sovereign’s public mandate becomes temporary default.

# Episode 410 — 응답하지 않으면 왕의 답이 되는 규칙

Date:
- CY 744-01-31, later session.

POV:
- emergency planners, Meridian/GL-4/M-4 witnesses, physical/service fields and centralists.

Visible goal:
- create a response to deadlock and silence without recreating the withdrawn Secretariat or common emergency sovereign.

Hidden pressure:
- no default risks lethal delay; sovereign default is a hidden tie-break even if time-limited.

## Scene units

1. **Proposed default** — after 12 minutes without field concurrence, sovereign mandate becomes provisional order unless a direct safety stop exists.
2. **Failure replay** — Meridian shows current physical information can be late, absent or compressed; silence is not consent and direct stops do not cover every service/rights conflict.
3. **Central case** — GL-4/M-4 show faster common decisions can reduce spoilage and restore services.
4. **Revision** — timeout produces narrowest preauthorized safe/service action or hold, explicit cost and emergency review; sovereign may publish recommendation and accountability statement but gains no default actuation.

Decisive choice:
- reject silence-as-sovereign-consent even at the cost of slower unresolved emergencies.

Reward:
- the vacant office cannot become a hidden tie-break through timeouts.

Cost:
- the compact still lacks one actor who can force a choice when safe/service minima conflict.

Hook:
- centralists shift to the last capture path: let the sovereign define when an emergency exists and which fields are relevant.

# Episode 411 — 비상사태를 정의하는 사람이 범위를 정한다

Date:
- CY 744-02-01.

POV:
- emergency-law counsel, claimant offices, loss families/crews, provinces and affected-service users.

Visible goal:
- define sovereign emergency declaration and scope without allowing permanent field suspension or jurisdiction capture.

Hidden pressure:
- one declaration can expand military, civil or technical authority faster than any actuation order.

## Scene units

1. **Declaration model** — sovereign may declare emergency, name affected territory/services and activate preauthorized field duties.
2. **Capture result** — broad scope can convert Dor, Mara or Isa’s bounded functions into station/province-wide government and exclude nonrecognized local actors.
3. **Loss-family evidence** — one MUT-6 family member supports a single declaration/commander for accountability; another crew representative supports direct physical/affected fields because Yara died inside overlapping commands. Neither speaks for all.
4. **Revision** — sovereign may request/declaratively recognize emergency, but scope requires current physical/service evidence and field-specific activation; automatic sunset, public grounds and affected/provincial review apply.

Decisive choice:
- retain one visible emergency voice without allowing it to define every active field or suspend them by declaration alone.

Reward:
- accountability and public recognition gain a focal office.

Cost:
- emergency activation remains multi-source and can still be slower than one sovereign command.

Hook:
- the seven-field pilot expires at the end of the day, forcing every participant to choose what survives without automatic extension.
