# Episode 390 — 한 사람에게 네 개의 신분

Date:
- CY 744-01-12.

POV:
- Kano Riss, rotating crews/households, Registry/civil/local officials and route operators.

Visible goal:
- build a lawful movement manifest without forcing every person into one jurisdiction’s identity or political status.

Hidden pressure:
- the same worker may be registered in Zone A, paid through Zone B, temporarily admitted in Zone C and physically housed in Zone D.

## Scene units

1. **Record collisions** — 9,480 current records produce 11,260 legal/service entries because people and households cross zones/functions.
2. **Zone A** — seven fields accept current-person proof, Registry provenance and affected-service appeal.
3. **Zone B** — Elian/Mara codes accepted, but local physical/security/evidence remain separate; some Continuity tokens are refused.
4. **Zones C/D** — C uses one emergency-sovereign code and clears registered entries fastest; D recognizes only local/current proof plus urgent-service guarantees.

Decisive choice:
- issue a layered route credential showing current person, work/pay, physical access, care and legal-zone status separately rather than one universal identity.

Reward:
- all 176 people receive a current route/service path without forced accession or citizenship conversion.

Cost:
- duplicate fees, appeals and manual checks rise; the layered credential is not automatically portable outside PC-7.

Hook:
- Zone C clears its registered payroll/manifest in four hours while Zone A’s wider current-proof process takes almost eight.

Cost:

Hook:
