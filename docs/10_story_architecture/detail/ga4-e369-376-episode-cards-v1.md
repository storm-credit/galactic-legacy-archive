# Episode 369 — 첫 번째 승계협약

Date:
- CY 743-12-11.

POV:
- four claimant offices, provincial/affected bodies, hearing drafters and Graybridge observers.

Visible goal:
- turn the field-tested four functions plus local authority into a compact that can operate beyond one corridor.

Hidden pressure:
- adding every local physical/service actor directly appears unscalable; aggregating them creates a new central office.

## Scene units

1. **Five fields** — claimant functions remain separate; provincial/local/affected authority is compressed into one fifth field for ratification and current conditions.
2. **Secretariat** — coordinates common manifests, orders and emergency cross-field action; no formal sovereign title but real current agenda and timing power.
3. **Emergency rule** — three central-function concurrences plus Secretariat confirmation may issue a 12-minute provisional route/service order; medical/technical stops direct, physical/local stop routed through Secretariat.
4. **Officeholder conditions** — Elian rejects common Crown seal; Mara supports executable secretariat; Dor seeks rapid emergency coordination; Isa requires root/service boundaries and audit.

Decisive choice:
- release the first five-field compact for ratification rather than declaring the Graybridge model universally sufficient.

Reward:
- a concrete cross-region structure exists for negotiation and field testing.

Cost:
- the compressed fifth field may not represent current physical operators, affected users and political provinces at the same time.

Hook:
- seven of twenty-four ratification bodies demand those local functions be separated before any emergency use.
