# Episode 161 — 서른여섯 개의 목적지

Date:
- CY 742-08-27.

POV:
- 하렌, with his information explicitly limited to granted mandates.

Visible goal:
- replace the old single evacuation list with individual/household destination needs and choices.

Hidden pressure:
- sponsors and media want one community destination because it is cheaper, easier to explain and easier to control.

## Scene 161.1 — The deleted column

Location:
- K-13 community hall/ship mission room linked to receiving institutions.

Action:
- Haren removes the single `destination` column and creates separate fields for:
  - current care;
  - household/caregiver links;
  - work/education;
  - identity exposure;
  - preferred governance;
  - travel tolerance;
  - refusal/private destination;
  - right to revise.

State change:
- one community manifest becomes 36 persons/12 units with different constraints.

## Scene 161.2 — Five provisional paths

Provisional groups emerge:
- medical-care corridor;
- Ardis work/residency;
- mobile/route households;
- K-13 stayers;
- private/undisclosed routes.

Action:
- group categories are treated as planning buckets, not binding votes.

## Scene 161.3 — Cost of separation

Theo/route planners calculate:
- one common convoy appears cheaper but exceeds medical/passenger limits and delays care;
- multiple routes cost 443,000–721,000 BSC and split legal/claim work;
- separation also reduces single-point casualty risk.

## Scene 161.4 — Coordinator, not representative

Decisive choice:
- Haren publicly renounces blanket authority to sign for the 36;
- each group/household grants bounded mandates for specific negotiations.

Reward:
- five executable planning paths and consent structure.

Cost:
- higher price, longer schedule and loss of one visible “community victory.”

Relationship state:
- Haren’s authority narrows while his workload and responsibility increase.

Carried state:
- care-route clinical and caregiver assessment begins first because delay creates current harm.

Final hook:
- Glasswater accepts eight patients but recognizes only three of the five requested caregiver places automatically.

Specialist panel:
- C02/C04, P03/P04, P02/M05, X03/X04.

- immune/medication continuity;

Carried state:

# Episode 163 — 아르디스의 빈자리

Date:
- CY 742-08-29.

POV:
- ensemble through the nine applicants and Ardis representatives; no Rian decision authority.

Visible goal:
- determine whether the Ardis offer is genuine residence/work or disguised emergency labor recruitment.

Hidden pressure:
- Ardis needs workers quickly and can offer more future than immediate safety.

## Scene 163.1 — The offer

Terms:
- 12-month provisional residence;
- paid technical/service training and work;
- shared housing/ration access;
- local registry review;
- possible resident standing after performance and local process.

Not promised:
- permanent citizenship;
- one employer/district;
- stable route or complete medical service.

## Scene 163.2 — Work need and coercion

Ardis representative admits:
- Foundry/utility shortages are real;
- first six qualified workers receive faster placement;
- dependents may wait for housing/registry;
- leaving early can create debt/transport problems.

Action:
- applicants demand wage floor, independent safety stop, dependent status and exit/appeal clauses.

## Scene 163.3 — Current skill test

Physical/remote test:
- three applicants demonstrate repair, cargo/service and administration skills;
- one expected worker is better suited to care/logistics, changing the group plan;
- Ardis cannot count all nine as productive workers.

## Scene 163.4 — Conditional acceptance

Decisive choice:
- nine people accept individual/household provisional offers, contingent on transport and current housing confirmation;
- they reject group employment under Haren or the Academy.

Reward:
- DG-ARDIS path becomes executable.

Cost:
- hiring window, damaged node, shared housing and uncertain portability;
- 102,000–168,000 BSC envelope activated.

Relationship state:
- Ardis gains people only through current terms; group retains right to withdraw before boarding.

Carried state:
- mobile/route group must decide whether ship work is freedom or another tied service system.

Final hook:
- two candidates qualify for 파루스’s missing watch positions, but one receives a better offer from another route crew.

Specialist panel:
- P02/P03/P04/P06, C02, M05, X01/X03/X04.

POV:

Visible goal:
- preserve local schooling, care, work and claims without treating staying as refusal of rescue.

- current treatment/service;

Relationship state:

# Episode 167 — 한 배에 다 못 탄다

Date:
- CY 742-09-02.

POV:
- 테오/루카, through capacity, mass and route schedules.

Visible goal:
- create a transport plan for all selected paths without exceeding ship, medical or receiving capacity.

Hidden pressure:
- political/media desire for one departure ceremony encourages unsafe simultaneous movement.

## Scene 167.1 — Passenger-equivalent model

Requirements:
- ordinary seats;
- caregivers and mobility support;
- medical equipment/continuous power;
- protected/private transport;
- household cargo/tools;
- escorts and documentation staff.

Result:
- 36 people require 43 ordinary-equivalent and 9 medical-equivalent places for the first movements.

## Scene 167.2 — Available capacity

- 파루스: limited after crew/cargo/relay configuration, not a medical passage;
- Neutral medical ship: care group capacity but fixed schedule/data rules;
- Ardis convoy: cargo/work places, limited medical support;
- route crews: scattered places and different destinations;
- sealed private transport: separate schedule.

## Scene 167.3 — What is displaced

To carry more on 파루스 would require:
- removing relay/service cargo or workshop tools;
- reducing emergency reserve;
- delaying ship inspection/repairs;
- using temporary sleeping and sanitation beyond current safe duration.

Decision:
- no overload and no ceremonial common departure.

## Scene 167.4 — Staggered movement

Plan:
- DG-CARE first via Neutral medical passage;
- DG-PRIVATE separately/sealed;
- DG-STAY remains;
- DG-ROUTE spreads across ships/jobs;
- DG-ARDIS joins a later joint convoy with 파루스 support.

Reward:
- every person receives a route/holding plan without one ship carrying all.

Cost:
- separation begins before departure; higher fees and longer uncertainty.

Relationship state:
- “community” shifts from co-location to continuing obligations and communication.

Carried state:
- who negotiates cross-group funding, appeals and information after separation?

Final hook:
- the five groups reject Haren’s proposal for one permanent central representative.

Specialist panel:
- M05/T02/T03/H05, P02/P03, C02, X01/X03/X04.

# Episode 168 — 대표자는 누구인가

Date:
- CY 742-09-03.

POV:
- 하렌.

Visible goal:
- establish representation and coordination after the community splits.

Hidden pressure:
- sponsors, institutions and Haren himself prefer one accountable signature.

## Scene 168.1 — Five group mandates

Each group establishes contacts for:
- care/service;
- contract/claim;
- transport;
- consent/appeal.

No contact receives blanket authority over competent adults.

## Scene 168.2 — Haren’s proposed office

Proposal:
- cross-group route/destination coordinator;
- manage shared funds and claims;
- maintain communication and emergency rerouting;
- no power to choose destination, employer, treatment or disclosure.

## Scene 168.3 — Opposition

Critiques:
- one group fears Haren will become a permanent gatekeeper;
- another fears fragmentation will lose bargaining power;
- private-route group refuses information centralization;
- K-13 stayers want equal voice despite not traveling.

## Scene 168.4 — Bounded coordination compact

Decisive choice:
- 180-day renewable coordination compact;
- group contacts can withdraw mandate;
- shared fund requires multi-group approval;
- emergency action expires and is audited;
- no “community leader” title in external contracts.

Reward:
- coordination without blanket representation.

Cost:
- slower negotiation and recurring meeting/record burden;
- Haren loses symbolic status while retaining responsibility.

Relationship state:
- first concrete seed of future mutual-route federation, but based on limited mandates.

Carried state:
- separate contracts and transport sequence must be executed; care group moves first.

Final hook:
- five destination compacts are ready, but one insurer will cover only a single unified community policy.

Specialist panel:
- P01/P03/P04, C02/C04, N08, X01/X03/X04.
