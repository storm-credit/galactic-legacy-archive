# Episode 716 — 제독이 없는 서명

Date:
- CY 745-02-06 to 02-08.

Goal:
- define what investigators mean by a `Blood Admiral signature` without presuming a signer.

Candidate layers:
1. downstream-risk model and time horizon;
2. priority ordering and omitted populations/services;
3. threshold/escalation choice;
4. command phrasing and redaction style;
5. credential/key/staff lineage;
6. current field implementation and exceptions;
7. later public/official summary.

Decision:
- investigate each layer separately and prohibit one composite similarity score from becoming attribution.

Reward:
- person, office, doctrine, key and propaganda hypotheses remain distinguishable.

Cost:
- public demand for a named culprit intensifies while investigators refuse a simple answer.

Hook:
- the oldest strong-looking document predates Haren’s emergency obligation table.

Goal:

  - immediate corridor failure;
  - downstream service dependency;
  - hostile exploitation;

- Haren’s E705 table adds reciprocal obligation and residual-duty fields but is not the origin of the entire pattern.

Reward:
- retrospective guilt-by-similarity is blocked.

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 718 — 같은 표, 다른 죽음

Date:
- CY 745-02-10 to 02-11.

Goal:
- compare two decisions using nearly identical risk fields but opposite priority orders.

Case A:
- physically justified closure preserves a damaged relay and allows bounded medical exceptions.

Case B:
- political/master-control closure preserves the same infrastructure score but omits unrecognized residents, family contact and earned-pay exceptions.

Finding:
- model structure alone does not determine the harmful outcome;
- priority weights, omitted fields, current exceptions and implementation authority matter.

Decision:
- separate model authorship from decision responsibility.

Reward:
- lawful safety reasoning and coercive use remain distinguishable.

Cost:
- no single technical artifact can bear all blame; field signers and institutions retain responsibility.

Hook:
- copied command wording appears across unrelated offices with different legal keys.

# Episode 721 — 보지 못한 승인

Date:
- CY 745-02-17 to 02-18.

Goal:
- test whether local signers can be responsible for fields hidden by higher-level or automated assembly.

Case:
- local physical and military signers approved bounded denial;
- final packet adds a wider duration and downstream-priority field through an authenticated escalation service;
- signers received only a summary state.

Decision:
- preserve separate responsibility for local approval, system/office assembly, final execution and failure to review current effect.

Reward:
- neither automatic exoneration nor total signer blame.

Cost:
- victims face multiple defendants and uncertain remedy; institutions resist exposing assembly logs.

Hook:
- Meira’s provenance mirror identifies an omission pattern more stable than wording or key lineage.

# Episode 722 — 항상 빠지는 사람들

Date:
- CY 745-02-19 to 02-21.

Goal:
- identify which people/services are repeatedly omitted or discounted in strong/partial matches.

Recurring omissions:
- downstream deaths beyond the declared decision horizon;
- unregistered and disputed-status people;
- holdout/late operators after route closure;
- family/contact and earned-pay continuity;
- nonparticipating regions that still bear diverted traffic;
- captured/unstable people treated as already lost or already preserved.

Decision:
- make omission fields first-class evidence rather than absence of data.

Reward:
- signature analysis becomes ethically and causally stronger.

Cost:
- some omissions reflect genuinely unavailable data, while others reflect doctrine or deliberate narrowing; intent remains open.

Hook:
- the first comparative scoring pass divides the 97 usable documents into strong, partial and control groups.

Date:

Goal:
- publish a reproducible signature comparison without converting it into guilt.

Usable corpus:
- 97 documents.

Results:
- 29 strong pattern matches;
- 41 partial matches;

Strong-match requirements:
- shared risk/time-horizon structure;

Reward:

Cost:
