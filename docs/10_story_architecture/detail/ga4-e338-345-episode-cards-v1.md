# Episode 338 — 공동신탁의 첫 창

Date:
- CY 743-10-12.

POV:
- Rema/Ardis window operators, vessel masters, service recipients and evidence custodians.

Visible goal:
- execute the first independently authorized Joint Trust window and launch the evidence mission without exceeding recovery or political scope.

Hidden pressure:
- success can be used to claim the Trust no longer needs Imperial/Continuity support; failure can justify external takeover.

## Scene units

1. **Physical start** — B-2 holds 14%; B-4 direct/direct/physical sources agree; no claimant or protagonist root key is used.
2. **Inbound phase** — 324 t and 58 people enter under food/medical/service priority; no dense module, weapons or claimant court cargo.
3. **Outbound phase** — three mission vessels, 86 people and 28 t evidence/repair equipment leave under separate masters and custody chains.
4. **Hard close** — total 352 t and 144 people clear; operators close rather than use the remaining 8 t nominal capacity.

Decisive choice:
- preserve the approved mission and node margin instead of adding politically valuable cargo.

Reward:
- the Joint Trust proves it can authorize a bounded routine window without Rian, Iris, Haren or one claimant’s signature.

Cost:
- B-2 falls to 11–12%; no repeat window for an estimated 11–17 days; route timing is exposed to all claimant blocs.

Hook:
- four official retrieval teams and a civilian salvage cooperative converge on Baseline from different directions.

Decisive choice:

Reward:
- immediate fire-control and approach confusion are reduced; each team’s useful capacity becomes available.

Cost:
- no team accepts the final custody proposal yet; convoy speed falls and the missing courier’s life-support clock continues.

Hook:
- Baseline is already under tow by a civilian salvage cooperative holding a valid distress-recovery lien.

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 344 — 나눠 가진 원본

Date:
- CY 743-10-22.

POV:
- vessel masters, claimant custodians, Vesper, Tera’s advocate and Ardis/Neutral evidence officers.

Visible goal:
- create a custody structure that preserves original evidence and witness safety through the remaining journey.

Hidden pressure:
- a single vault is physically simple but politically capturable; splitting evidence can weaken context and admissibility.

## Scene units

1. **Witness custody** — Tera and living survivors remain under Neutral medical/legal care with personal consent and no claimant interrogation access outside advocate-approved process.
2. **Physical originals** — receipt strip and dual-clock module enter a six-lock mobile vault; no claimant holds enough locks to open it alone; Vesper retains documented hull/lien access under escort.
3. **Specialized evidence** — hull/drive logs go to a joint accident board with public hashes; key-challenge residues stay in disconnected Continuity analysis with mirrored checks; Serin/Ardis maintain public provenance index and copies.
4. **Seizure orders** — military and Continuity orders are satisfied only inside their domains; neither receives the whole original set.

Decisive choice:
- preserve context through synchronized hashes/provenance while physically and legally splitting custody.

Reward:
- no claimant, broker, protagonist or single institution can erase, alter or monopolize the full evidence chain.

Cost:
- review becomes slower and every future movement requires coordinated custodians; one missing lock or witness can stall the hearing.

Hook:
- the designated hearing station reports that four claimant delegations have already established incompatible rules of standing.
