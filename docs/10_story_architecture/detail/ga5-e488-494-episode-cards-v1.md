# Episode 488 — 마흔일곱 척의 응급실

# Episode 489 — 순양함 한 척과 아홉 척의 배

Date:
- CY 744-05-07.

Goal:
- choose between restoring heavy cruiser `철의 권리` and a distributed escort/rescue package.

Equal-resource comparison:
- cruiser path: one R3 heavy hull in 24–31 days, approximately 2,200 combat ship-hours in first 30 days;
- distributed path: seven escorts, two rescue/support hulls and tender stabilization, approximately 9,600 combined mission ship-hours.

Decision:
- defer the cruiser and select the distributed package.

Reward:
- convoy, rescue and route coverage can expand.

Cost:
- `철의 권리` remains grounded 5–7 months unless new parts arrive; its formation withdraws two officers from Charter staff.

Hook:
- cruiser supporters accuse Tav/Nera/workers of creating an unelected technical government.

# Episode 490 — 정비위원회의 의자

Date:
- CY 744-05-09.

Goal:
- create a maintenance/readiness board that cannot quietly command missions.

Seven seats/fields:
1. yard/tender engineering;
2. current workers/safety;
3. formation/captain mission need;
4. crew/pay/fatigue;
5. rescue/medical/service users;
6. title/lien/contributor claims;
7. public audit/current evidence.

Limits:
- no independent chair actuation;
- board allocates named repair capacity, not battle orders;
- technical stop remains with current operators;
- mission board may state need but cannot falsify readiness.

Decision:
- approve the board for two 7-day cycles with review after serious injury, grounding or political retaliation.

Reward:
- pooled repair can begin reproducibly.

Cost:
- slower than Tav’s direct schedule; creditors and combat formations retain appeal/withdrawal pressure.

Hook:
- workers discover a fatigue/heat plan that would meet the published schedule only by violating safe shifts.

# Episode 491 — 여섯 시간의 작업중지

Date:
- CY 744-05-11.

Goal:
- resolve a legal work slowdown without losing the entire repair cycle.

State:
- 186 workers across tender/yard lines stop high-heat work;
- the schedule used an impossible overlap between radiation cooldown and shift recovery;
- no sabotage is found.

Decision:
- accept a six-hour stop, rewrite shifts and move two low-risk tasks to trained ship crews under worker supervision.

Reward:
- a likely coolant/radiation incident is prevented; work resumes with current consent.

Cost:
- three ships miss preferred departure windows; Ardo loses one escort for an alert cycle.

Hook:
- to recover schedule, the board proposes cannibalizing two grounded hulls with disputed titles.

# Episode 492 — 부품이 된 배

Date:
- CY 744-05-13.

Goal:
- use parts from two grounded hulls without converting them into unclaimed salvage.

Hull states:
- one provincial frigate with evacuated crew and province return claim;
- one Imperial/Helix-lien corvette with unpaid worker and insurer claims.

Decision:
- create reversible component custody records, replacement debt and crew/title notice;
- prohibit weapons/core-key transfer;
- remove only 94 t of certified-compatible components.

Reward:
- six priority projects gain needed parts.

Cost:
- donor hull recovery is delayed 8–14 months; province/creditors gain quantified replacement claims.

Hook:
- one component fails inspection after removal, threatening four linked projects.

# Episode 494 — 움직이는 열세 척

Date:
- CY 744-05-17.

Goal:
- close the first maintenance cycle and account for readiness gained/lost.

First-cycle selected combat projects:
- 13.

Result:
- 9 reach physical R2 or mission-useful R1+/R2;
- 4 remain in work due parts/quarantine;
- 3 support/rescue hulls also regain current service through linked work;
- 2 donor hulls are delayed 8–14 months;
- `철의 권리` remains grounded.

Charter force state:
- 21 combat ships conditionally connected;
- 18 support/rescue/tender ships;
- only 14 combat ships currently R2 or better;
- remaining accepted hulls are local-defense, transit or conditional.

Decision:
- retain the board for another cycle but require mission/casualty and worker review; no permanent technical authority.

Reward:
- maintenance-supported formation begins to exist.

Cost:
- prestige/heavy capability remains low, workers/tender are fatigued and repair debt deepens.

Final hook:
- crews now ask why repaired ships should deploy when 8,640 old and thousands of new pay/family records remain unresolved.
