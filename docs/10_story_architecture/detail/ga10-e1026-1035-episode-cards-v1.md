# Episode 1026 — 중앙이 사라진 다음 날

Date:
- CY 747-07-04 to 07-06.

Goal:
- show why some communities request continued central coordination after the transition expires.

Evidence:
- two large nodes experience schedule conflicts;
- one repair consortium loses common demand forecasts;
- family/pay portability slows;
- local authorities remain lawful but unevenly staffed.

Petitions:
- five nodes request a time-limited common stability compact;
- three request immediate full local return;
- others demand referenda or delayed decisions.

Decision:
- no central office is recreated by petition alone;
- open a choice process with service and exit guarantees.

Reward:
- stability preference is treated as a current political choice.

Cost:
- the absence of a standing composer creates real delay.

Hook:
- voters demand comparable plans rather than slogans about freedom or safety.

# Episode 1027 — 선택지마다 비용을 적는다

Date:
- CY 747-07-07 to 07-09.

Goal:
- produce equal-assumption choice packages.

Options:
- immediate local/distributed custody;
- gradual transfer with reciprocal support;
- 120-day opt-in stability compact;
- parallel/outside verification;
- delayed decision during repair or displacement;
- function-split participation.

Every package states:
- service expectation;
- transition and insurance cost;
- command and credential scope;
- refusal and exit;
- local/affected rights;
- unresolved claims and capacity limits.

Decision:
- no package may advertise certain saved lives or costless freedom.

Reward:
- local choice is informed by common evidence.

Cost:
- factions accuse the comparison board of hiding values inside assumptions.

Hook:
- the five stability-preferring nodes form a negotiating bloc.

# Episode 1028 — 안정협약을 원하는 다섯 노드

Date:
- CY 747-07-10 to 07-12.

Goal:
- define central stability without restoring one sovereign key.

Five-node envelope:
- 6.4m service-dependent people;
- high medical, repair and family-pay interdependence;
- two nodes have weak local composer capacity;
- all retain local physical/medical stops.

Requested compact:
- 120 days;
- common schedules, service matching and narrow credentials;
- three rotating composer cells;
- no standing Rian commander;
- no civil-status, culture, reproduction, technology-value or deletion authority;
- referendum/review at day 45 and day 90;
- no automatic renewal.

Decision:
- draft the `Opt-In Stability Service Compact`.

Reward:
- continued coordination need not equal restored dictatorship.

Cost:
- network effects may pressure neighboring nodes to join.

Hook:
- Rian is asked to become permanent chief composer and refuses.

# Episode 1029 — 리안이 맡지 않는 중앙

Date:
- CY 747-07-13.

Goal:
- keep Rian useful without reconstructing his office.

Request:
- stability bloc offers Rian a 120-day chief-composer post.

Rian’s response:
- refuses standing office;
- agrees to one public training and readiness review;
- may accept named emergency mandates under ordinary approval;
- requires three rotating composer cells and independent expiry.

Decision:
- compact proceeds without a permanent Rian role.

Reward:
- central coordination becomes institutional rather than personal.

Cost:
- confidence in the compact falls among communities that trusted Rian more than the new cells.

Hook:
- local referenda begin with unequal media, service and financial pressure.

# Episode 1030 — 투표 전에 끊긴 보험

Date:
- CY 747-07-14 to 07-17.

Goal:
- prevent material pressure from masquerading as free consent.

Incident:
- insurers announce higher premiums for nodes considering parallel or outside systems;
- repair suppliers offer priority to compact members;
- one local campaign uses projected service loss as certainty.

Decision:
- freeze referendum-period discriminatory contract changes;
- require dependency disclosures and independent correction;
- preserve ordinary risk pricing based on current evidence.

Reward:
- the vote is less distorted by sudden coercion.

Cost:
- insurers and suppliers carry temporary uncertainty.

Hook:
- one damaged node cannot safely hold a standard referendum.

# Episode 1034 — 안정이 실제로 더 나을 때

Date:
- CY 747-07-27 to 07-29.

Goal:
- show a real benefit of opt-in central stability.

Crisis:
- medical filter and repair shortage across five compact nodes.

Outcome:
- rotating composers and common forecasts reallocate stock;
- 1.28m affected service users retain minimum care;
- median urgent delay 6.8h versus 12.1h in matched gradual-transfer systems;
- no Rian activation;
- no local stop overridden.

Reward:
- stability preference gains evidence-based legitimacy.

Cost:
- surrounding nodes request access without accepting all compact obligations.

Hook:
- compact must define associate and opt-out corridors.
