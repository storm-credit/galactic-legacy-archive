# Episode 901 — 열세 기관의 열쇠

Date:
- CY 746-08-02 to 08-04.

Goal:
- create a treaty-scoped continuity key without reviving a galaxy-wide Seed authority.

Proposal:
- one coalition treaty key may authenticate package versions, corridor schedules and cross-node service credentials only inside participating jurisdictions;
- local physical, medical, ship and receiving stops remain independently current;
- no personhood, citizenship, culture, reproduction or technology-value authority;
- no deletion and no permanent status;
- automatic expiry after 180 days unless explicitly renewed.

Decision:
- thirteen founding bodies authorize a 90-day operational phase within a 180-day treaty term.

Reward:
- common schedules and credentials can operate across fragmented post-Seed systems.

Cost:
- participants become dependent on a common verification spine whose later withdrawal may itself cause disruption.

Hook:
- the first baseline audit shows piracy, delay, shortage and maintenance burdens large enough that several conditional bodies reconsider joining.

# Episode 902 — 가입은 자발적인가

Date:
- CY 746-08-05 to 08-08.

Goal:
- distinguish legal consent from material dependence and political pressure.

Participation categories:
- full: common scheduling, credential verification, insurance and service settlement;
- conditional: selected functions only, with local override and short renewal;
- observer: audit access without operational key use;
- outside/reject: no coalition authority, but ordinary bilateral cooperation remains possible.

Pressure findings:
- two conditional regions rely on coalition repair parts;
- one outside region’s insurers raise premiums because coalition routes are becoming the lower-risk standard;
- three founders face domestic opposition despite formal votes.

Decision:
- require public dependency statements and separate recorded consent for each function.

Reward:
- accession is not represented as pure free choice when refusal has real economic costs.

Cost:
- the coalition’s expansion slows and markets begin pricing outsiders as risky.

Hook:
- the baseline audit publishes comparable pre-operation figures.

# Episode 903 — 완벽해지기 전의 항로

Date:
- CY 746-08-09 to 08-11.

Goal:
- establish the pre-operation reference without inventing a counterfactual certainty.

Ninety-day reference window:
- 214 verified piracy or armed-seizure events;
- 47 high-severity cargo or passenger losses;
- median cross-region delay 31.4 hours;
- critical shortage burden 18.6 days per 100,000 service-days;
- maintenance backlog 23.8% of registered mission/service hulls;
- insured-loss settlement median 29 days.

Population and service envelope:
- 18.6m people currently dependent on the 24 nodes and 37 corridors;
- no forecast-saved count is created.

Decision:
- use the reference as a comparison baseline, not proof of what would certainly happen without the coalition.

Reward:
- later efficiency claims can be tested.

Cost:
- weakly recorded outsider losses remain underrepresented.

Hook:
- coalition operators load the first common schedule and credential package.

# Episode 904 — 조약키의 첫 회전

Date:
- CY 746-08-12 to 08-14.

Goal:
- activate the treaty key within its limited domains.

Authorized domains:
- package version authentication;
- named corridor sequencing;
- convoy and service-slot matching;
- narrow cross-node credential verification;
- insurer and repair settlement references.

Excluded domains:
- local physical or medical override;

- citizenship or personhood;

- deletion and permanent exclusion.

Decision:

Reward:

Cost:
- a common credential schema becomes the practical gateway to the fastest routes.

Hook:

# Episode 906 — 서른한 시간에서 아홉 시간으로

Date:
- CY 746-08-22 to 08-28.

Goal:
- prove the common schedule’s operational value.

Outcome:
- median cross-region delay drops from 31.4 hours to 9.2 hours;
- 90th-percentile delay drops from 88 hours to 31 hours;
- emergency medical transfer matching improves by 41%;
- crew rest violations decrease by 28% among participating fleets.

Cause:
- compatible schedules;
- live receiving capacity;
- authenticated package versions;
- fewer duplicated inspections;
- named exception handling.

Decision:
- retain local stops but require a reason code and current review for delays over six hours.

Reward:
- patients, perishable cargo and rotating crews receive visible benefit.

Cost:
- systems unable to issue coalition-compatible reason codes are increasingly pushed into slower manual lanes.

Hook:
- shortage and ration ledgers become the next political proof point.

# Episode 910 — 공통형식의 바깥

Date:
- CY 746-09-18 to 09-23.

Goal:
- reveal the first administrative edge of the successful system.

Friction classes:
- incomplete or low-trust identity chains;
- plural AI roots/forks and shared substrate;
- nonstandard repair or reproductive technologies;
- cultural names, kinship and household structures not mapped by the schema;
- unregistered or mobile settlements.

Finding:
- none are automatically unlawful or dangerous;
- coalition automation handles them more slowly and assigns higher review burdens;
- insurers and route operators treat delay as risk even before a formal denial.

Decision:
- open an independent friction ledger before expanding the key.

Reward:
- exclusion becomes measurable before it hardens into doctrine.

Cost:
- coalition leaders fear that visible error data will weaken accession.

Final hook:
- E911 begins the credential architecture audit, where speed, confidence and legal status have started to merge in practice.
