# Episode 236 — 첫 주간보고

Date:
- CY 742-11-15.

POV:
- 세린, Toma and six-constituency report participants.

Visible goal:
- publish the first honest weekly trial report before funders or factions publish selective numbers.

Hidden pressure:
- every faction needs one headline: success, failure, theft, or proof of governability.

## Scene units

1. **Count the nonvictory** — second transfer improved, but the board carries the first 14–18 t loss, displaced Foundry cooling, delayed Anchor spare and unequal recipient outcomes.
2. **Three measures** — immediate lives/services protected, material moved, and services displaced are reported separately; no single success percentage.
3. **Central-package comparison** — Iven Kor shows that the 90-day package could clear two payroll cycles, install certified cooling seven to eleven days earlier and unify diagnostic staffing; the benefits are real and current.
4. **Public release** — Serin refuses both heroic and failure-only summaries; local data stewards publish source/confidence and missing fields.

Decisive choice:
- the trial publishes a mixed result that makes both continued local work and centralized restoration politically defensible.

Reward:
- public trust in the report format and a usable target list for the next seven days.

Cost:
- no faction receives a clean victory; creditor and populist campaigns intensify.

Relationship state:
- Toma accepts public responsibility for the window and spare decisions instead of assigning the cost to Rian’s model.

Hook:
- a Transit pressure/fire compartment requests an emergency housing clearance, but the trial has no authority over Civic housing title or district patrol access.

# Episode 237 — 권한 밖의 불

Date:
- CY 742-11-16.

POV:
- Savo Nir, Transit fire/utility crews and Mia.

Visible goal:
- stop a cable-duct fire and move residents before a pressure boundary becomes unsafe.

Hidden pressure:
- the trial can coordinate utilities and medical service but cannot order title clearance, patrol access or permanent rehousing.

## Scene units

1. **Physical incident** — recycled-insulation fire spreads through an old service duct behind three temporary habitat blocks; smoke and pressure-door faults divide the evacuation route.
2. **Mandate collision** — Savo can stop power and open service corridors, Mia can triage, but Civic title officers and district patrol cannot legally clear one occupied storage block without separate authority.
3. **Local workaround** — Transit crews cut a pressure-safe opening and use Garden cargo sleds as evacuation platforms; 회랑새 provides masks and portable pumps, not command.
4. **Aftermath** — no mass death, but one block loses approximately 180 usable berths for at least 18–30 days; 46 residents need medical observation and 11 are admitted, including 2 serious smoke injuries.

Decisive choice:
- Savo violates a property seal to preserve the evacuation route, records the action and submits to local review rather than asking Rian for emergency override.

Reward:
- residents survive and the trial’s utility/medical coordination proves useful.

Cost:
- the trial visibly fails to provide lawful rehousing or patrol/title decisions; 180 berths are lost and Civic liability conflict deepens.

Institution state:
- the 30-day trial is not a government and cannot silently absorb housing sovereignty.

Hook:
- the lost berths force a broader service-residency decision, while Anchor operators report that Spine B’s reference drift has doubled in two shifts.

Date:
- CY 742-11-17 to 742-11-18.

POV:
- Toma, Luka, Nera, Luma Pell and local Custodian operators.

Visible goal:
- inspect Spine B’s exterior and service interfaces without opening a traffic window or transferring node control.

Hidden pressure:
- external actors need proof of recoverability; local actors fear that the same map will become a seizure plan.

## Scene units

1. **Inspection geometry** — four bounded work fronts are defined: B-1 exterior alignment collar, B-2 thermal-recovery bus, B-3 authentication fork, B-4 service conduit and sensor chain.
2. **Split teams** — local utility frames own approach lanes and stop authority; 07 carries shield, tether and extraction support; Luka and Nera provide model/test limits.
3. **Physical findings** — B-1 is misaligned but repairable; B-2 can carry only about 18–22% of former recovery load; B-3 contains current/legacy signature disagreement; B-4 has contamination and two blind sensor segments.
4. **Record conflict** — one old shutdown record lists B-1 as structurally lost, while current physical evidence shows a repairable collar; the edit source remains unproven.

Decisive choice:

Reward:
- Spine B becomes a bounded repair problem rather than a myth or total loss.

Cost:
- Helix, Imperial and local seizure factions now know the same weak points and recoverability.

Power state:
- no traffic window, no command key and no capability reward.

Hook:

# Episode 239 — 토마의 한 시간

Date:
- CY 742-11-19.

POV:
- Toma, Luma Pell and exterior frame teams.

Visible goal:
- finish B-4 direct sensor verification before the exterior window closes.

Hidden pressure:
- waiting costs twelve to sixteen days and strengthens the case for a full certified/centralized takeover; continuing requires crossing one unverified segment.

## Scene units

1. **Current evidence** — two direct sensors are good, one indirect estimate is stale and a service-conduit brace shows acceptable load in the model but no current internal image.
2. **Toma’s decision** — Toma authorizes a one-hour bounded continuation with extra tether and 07 extraction coverage; local teams may stop at any new discrepancy.
3. **Failure** — hidden fatigue and a partly cut brace rotate under Luma’s utility frame; the frame becomes pinned, one manipulator and suit umbilical are damaged and B-4 debris strikes a sensor cluster.
4. **Extraction** — Luma orders 07 not to pull against the conduit; local fitters cut two load paths, then Rian performs the timed extraction. Luma survives with a fractured pelvis, nerve injury and a projected 7–12 month return-to-field limit.

Decisive choice:
- Toma stops the entire inspection, accepts that his continuation decision was wrong and preserves all logs for worker/civic review.

Reward:
- Luma is rescued alive; the hidden brace failure is physically known.

Cost:
- serious lasting injury, 07 tether/tool wear, loss of two B-4 sensors, inspection delay and a direct accountability claim against Toma/trial authority.

Character state:
- Toma is neither infallible local wisdom nor Rian’s correct proxy; his fear of losing time caused current harm.

Hook:
- Foundry workers refuse further Spine parts until pay, injury responsibility and technical-stop terms are signed; Civic issues an emergency requisition notice for Line 3.

# Episode 240 — 최소 서비스, 최대 충돌

Date:
- CY 742-11-20 to 742-11-21.

POV:
- Kera Mott, Toma, Haren and minimum-service constituencies.

Visible goal:
- close Act 3A with a 30-day minimum-service and diagnostic pact that survives Luma’s injury and the housing loss.

Hidden pressure:
- every new protection consumes budget or leverage needed for Spine B, defense and payroll.

## Scene units

1. **Terms from harm** — Luma’s care/wage/independent review, technical stop, food/air/medical minima, displaced Transit housing, public clock reporting and external package conditions are placed in one costed ledger.
2. **Funding cut** — the pact protects current medical/food/air and two payroll bands but suspends one Surface Works expansion and one Civic discretionary construction program; the cuts have named workers and service consequences.
3. **Foundry dispute** — Civic and Helix issue a bounded requisition/remote scheduling order for Line 3 to produce Spine A and defense parts before negotiated Spine B components.
4. **Occupation** — Kera’s shift completes the current safety cycle, then physically tags and occupies the line; emergency medical/cooling parts continue, noncritical and requisitioned production stops.

Decisive choice:
- six constituencies approve the minimum-service and diagnostic pact but refuse automatic control of Foundry production; Kera begins an essential-only strike/occupation.

Reward:
- minimum food, air, medical, worker stop and public reporting survive for thirty days; 회랑새 remains one paid contractor.

Cost:
- Surface/Civic projects are cut, Luma’s claim becomes public, nonessential production stops and the city enters a labor confrontation.

Act 3A result:
- accurate multi-source damage map;
- limited service role for the protagonists;
- no savior command;
- external actors learn Ardis is recoverable;
- the first industrial strike begins.

Final hook:
- Line 3’s remote scheduler rejects the occupation state and begins an automatic purge/cooldown sequence that could damage both the line and the emergency-part queue.
