# Episode 397 — 두 비상정부가 같은 정거장에 선다

Date:
- CY 744-01-19.

POV:
- M-4 West seven-field bodies, East Cordon administration, Dor garrison command and Neutral zones.

Visible goal:
- keep one station/service ring operating when two lawful emergency-government models overlap physically.

Hidden pressure:
- each model can claim current jurisdiction over some people, ships, services and evidence without controlling the whole station.

## Scene units

1. **West Ring** — seven fields, direct physical/affected-service/evidence authority and pilot participation.
2. **East Cordon** — local council adopts Mara as 30-day Single Emergency Sovereign administrator for its zone, appointing the four functions under one accountable chain.
3. **Garrison** — recognizes Dor’s military emergency authority for armed movement but refuses civil, Registry or Continuity absorption.
4. **Neutral zones** — witness/evidence/medical custody remains outside both local political models.

Decisive choice:
- map people, assets, corridors and functions by current zone rather than declaring one emergency government station-wide.

Reward:
- immediate jurisdictional firing/seizure is avoided and both models can be compared under the same crisis.

Cost:
- staff, users and ships receive parallel orders and cannot assume one permit/payment/security token works everywhere.

Hook:
- 41,800 service entries begin expiring or contradicting as the Four-Function Order approaches its January 22 end.
