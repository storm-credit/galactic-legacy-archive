# Episode 293 — 열두 분 안에 하나가 되는 함대

Date:
- CY 743-01-12.

POV:
- Iris Kaun, Sella, local/Imperial fleet staffs and node-authentication operators.

Visible goal:
- establish safe holding and identify what the Imperial key can actually coordinate.

Hidden pressure:
- a demonstration can become de facto consent if local systems begin depending on it before political terms exist.

## Scene units

1. **Arrival** — task group enters a verified external holding zone without immediate weapons lock or armed docking; Doman’s two cutters identify under Imperial recognition but remain outside local sectors.
2. **Key demonstration** — on a disconnected test net, the Imperial key reconciles seven ship credentials, three ammunition authorities and two node-defense states in 12 minutes; the local segmented process estimates 71–104 minutes.
3. **Real limit** — Transit/service-user stops, Foundry physical custody and Helix facility terms are absent from the Imperial model; the key can authenticate command faster than it can decide whose service is sacrificed.
4. **Offer delivery** — Iris offers full 30-day authority or a 72-hour bounded demonstration with explicit end conditions.

Decisive choice:
- local actors permit only disconnected evidence testing while political and service terms are reviewed.

Reward:
- central command’s real speed and current missing fields are both measured.

Cost:
- officers, registered residents and some package workers begin openly supporting full emergency authority.

Hook:
- an incoming Neutral medical/claims craft and a local patrol both detect an accelerating debris/attack cluster on intersecting lanes.

# Episode 294 — 네 개의 허가가 필요한 요격

Date:
- CY 743-01-13.

POV:
- Sella, sector commanders, Neutral craft and Imperial staff.

Visible goal:
- protect the medical/claims craft and inhabited approach without firing through uncertain lanes.

Hidden pressure:
- local command requires four sector/service confirmations; the threat closes faster than the standing handoff time.

## Scene units

1. **Threat** — six guided debris/munition bodies and two spoofed rescue beacons approach; origin remains uncertain.
2. **Local delay** — Outer tracking, Habitat lane confirmation, Mobile Rescue custody and fixed-defense fire authority arrive out of sequence; one local cutter moves early under standing rescue rules.
3. **Imperial action** — Iris requests and receives one bounded local fire corridor, then uses Imperial command to coordinate destroyer sensors, two corvettes and 8 frames within 4 minutes.
4. **Outcome** — seven bodies/beacons are intercepted or diverted; one debris body strikes local cutter `ARC-17`, killing 3 crew, seriously injuring 7 and disabling the cutter for at least 5–8 months. The Neutral craft and inhabited lane survive.

Decisive choice:
- Sella grants a single current fire corridor rather than overall command; Iris acts efficiently inside it.

Reward:
- 61 people on the Neutral craft and the inhabited approach are protected; central coordination proves materially superior in the immediate engagement.

Cost:
- 3 deaths, 7 serious injuries and permanent campaign loss of one cutter; analysis estimates a fully integrated command might have redirected ARC-17 6–9 minutes earlier.

Hard ruling:
- distributed delay contributes to the loss; Imperial effectiveness is not author-rigged propaganda or automatically sufficient for political surrender.

Hook:
- families and crews demand to know why the city refused the key that could have reduced the loss.

# Episode 296 — 하나의 키가 막은 두 명령

Date:
- CY 743-01-15.

POV:
- Eda, Sella, Imperial authentication staff and fixed-defense crews.

Visible goal:
- prevent incompatible denial/battery orders from producing friendly fire or unauthorized isolation.

Hidden pressure:
- using the Imperial key operationally creates dependency before the city finishes its vote.

## Scene units

1. **Order conflict** — one valid local defense order prepares a battery; one delayed Imperial contingency tells the same lane to remain clear; a copied legacy lineage attempts to pair D-A isolation.
2. **Local process** — split authorities can identify the conflict but require physical confirmation and multiple callbacks.
3. **Bounded Imperial query** — with current consent from Sella/Eda/affected sector, the test key resolves source time, command hierarchy and expiration in 3 minutes, quarantining the copied pairing.
4. **No actuation** — local physical operators still decide the battery and D-A states; the key authenticates and quarantines, not fires or isolates.

Decisive choice:
- allow a single live authentication use under local physical control and full audit.

Reward:
- no friendly fire or denial activation; copied-lineage use is contained faster than local process alone.

Cost:
- the Imperial key gains live access and produces a current reliance precedent; one local authentication team is sidelined from the urgent decision.

Hook:
- Iris offers full immediate integration or a 72-hour narrow coordination compact with local physical/service vetoes.

POV:

Decisive choice:

Reward:
- the actual authority transfer becomes inspectable rather than rhetorical.

Cost:
- another day passes under fragmented command; Imperial hardliners prepare to bypass negotiation if attack escalates.

Hook:
- a hospital-transfer convoy loses navigation synchronization during a defense drill, giving both systems one chance to prove their rescue method.

# Episode 298 — 제국의 네 분

Date:
- CY 743-01-17.

POV:
- hospital-transfer crews, Sella/Iris staffs, Transit navigation and medical teams.

Visible goal:
- recover a four-craft patient convoy after spoofed timing separates it from its safe lane.

Hidden pressure:
- the local standing system protects patient/service custody but cannot synchronize every military sensor quickly.

## Scene units

1. **Separation** — four craft carry 83 patients and 29 staff/crew; one craft has 11 high-dependency patients. Spoofed timing and battery drill traffic split the convoy.
2. **Imperial coordination** — Iris’s group authenticates craft, clears military sensors and assigns escort vectors in 4 minutes after Sella grants one rescue domain.
3. **Local correction** — Transit navigator rejects one Imperial vector through a currently unsafe pressure-service approach; local route changes add 3 minutes but avoid a service tug collision.
4. **Outcome** — all 112 people arrive; one patient requires emergency stabilization but no permanent new injury; local and Imperial systems each correct a real blind spot.

Decisive choice:
- combine central external coordination with current local route authority.

Reward:
- a concrete mixed model saves the full convoy faster than the prior segmented process.

Cost:
- the rescue strengthens both narrow-integration supporters and full-command advocates; Imperial systems collect current coordination evidence.

Hook:
- the board vote now has a working alternative, but the full-command offer includes ammunition release and Doman’s cutters that the narrow compact cannot automatically command.
