# Episode 766 — 명령 뒤에 남은 일흔두 시간

Date:
- CY 745-05-09 to 05-15.

Goal:
- reconstruct the D4 execution minute by minute without treating the signed order as the only cause.

Evidence fields:
- Haren’s order and 12/24/48-hour reviews;
- model versions and corrected outer data;
- protected-trunk and capped/closed-corridor traffic;
- residual mission assignments, inspections and escort changes;
- local physical/medical stops and microconvoys;
- receiving, shelter and household outcomes.

Decision:
- create a common event timeline with separate source confidence and disputed links.

Reward:
- the inquiry can test personal, office, model, military, local and receiving responsibility against the same chronology.

Cost:
- no single narrative satisfies beneficiary or victim constituencies; several records remain incomplete.

Hook:
- the first source comparison shows Haren’s staff summary understated two residual-mission delays during the 24-hour review.

# Episode 767 — 보고받지 못한 것과 보지 않은 것

Date:
- CY 745-05-16 to 05-22.

Goal:
- distinguish information unavailable to Haren from information available but discounted or summarized away.

Finding:
- one escort reassignment and one receiver refusal reached staff but not Haren before the 24-hour review;
- Lin’s corrected population/reserve evidence and the overall outer-risk increase did reach Haren;
- one residual-mission failure warning was present in the full packet but omitted from the short executive summary;
- Haren chose not to delay the review for the full packet because of core transition risk.

Decision:
- preserve separate staff-summary, office-design and Haren-review responsibility.

Reward:
- neither total ignorance nor omniscient intent is manufactured.

Cost:
- staff and office defenders accuse the inquiry of hindsight; victims see another layer of avoidable omission.

Hook:
- model analysts rerun the order with the exact current data available at each review.

# Episode 768 — 그때 알 수 있었던 결과

Date:
- CY 745-05-23 to 05-31.

Goal:
- separate foreseeable harm at signature, 24-hour review and 48-hour review from later knowledge.

Findings:
- at signature, severe outer harm and 1.9k–5.2k deaths/irreversible losses were foreseeable but not the exact outcome;
- at 24 hours, outer risk had moved upward and rotating transition was materially more available;
- at 48 hours, the risk of early handoff remained real, but continued concentration and Lin-zone failure were more foreseeable than at signature;
- exact 2,318 deaths were not knowable in advance.

Decision:
- use time-specific foreseeability rather than final-outcome hindsight.

Reward:
- the later legal finding can distinguish signing from continuation.

Cost:
- no binary “necessary” or “reckless from the start” answer survives intact.

Hook:
- beneficiary hospitals and power networks present evidence that early termination could have restarted the core cascade.

# Episode 769 — 살아남은 중심부의 증언

Date:
- CY 745-06-01 to 06-08.

Goal:
- hear beneficiary-region workers, patients and service operators without turning them into Haren’s political shield.

Evidence:
- two receiving/power systems crossed back below failure thresholds after D4;
- treatment, food, shelter and identity chains for large populations remained active;
- 4,860 people still died during the core crisis;
- early transition at 24/48 hours carried real reconfiguration and hostile-exploitation risk.

Decision:
- recognize D4’s material protective effect and beneficiary standing while prohibiting subtraction of outer victims from a hypothetical saved count.

Reward:
- Haren’s defense rests on real current benefit rather than office prestige.

Cost:
- victim groups fear that large beneficiary numbers will dominate the inquiry.

Hook:
- Lin’s household, coworkers and other outer communities present evidence on foreseeable residual-mission failure.

# Episode 770 — 린 오사가 남긴 네 장부

Date:
- CY 745-06-09 to 06-16.

Goal:
- admit Lin’s evidence as current work, household, appeal and service records rather than a single death image.

Four ledgers:
1. corrected shelter/current population;
2. oxygen/filter/cold-chain reserve and shift logs;
3. appeal, receipt and residual-mission trail;
4. household/caregiver and local service decisions.

Finding:
- Lin’s evidence reached Haren before signature;
- the specific residual mission was elevated after the 24-hour review but remained structurally secondary to protected-trunk traffic;
- her death depended on D4 allocation plus infrastructure depletion, data/summary failure, escort/security scarcity and local conditions.

Decision:
- grant Lin’s household and affected region full standing without making them universal victim representatives.

Reward:
- ordinary current agency and causality survive the legal process.

Cost:
- no single remedy can restore the service network, household or lost time.

Hook:
- credential and staff evidence shows the D4 office was designed to concentrate decision but not enough implementation control to guarantee residual duties.

# Episode 771 — 강한 명령과 약한 구호

Date:
- CY 745-06-17 to 06-23.

Goal:
- test the institutional design that made denial mandatory while residual support remained conditional and fragmented.

Finding:
- corridor closure/cap instructions had direct authenticated effect;
- the fourteen residual missions still depended on separate escort, captain, physical, medical and receiving approvals;
- Haren could elevate missions but could not compel every implementation field;
- office designers knew this asymmetry but accepted it to preserve local/ship authority.

Decision:
- classify the asymmetry as an institutional design failure and a known limit Haren was required to account for, not an excuse for every failed mission.

Reward:
- later reform can preserve local stops while strengthening residual obligations.

Cost:
- responsibility spreads across office design, guarantors, receivers, staff and the signer.

Hook:
- military and local actors dispute whether failed missions were preventable or safely executable.

# Episode 772 — 호위하지 않은 배와 열지 않은 문

Date:
- CY 745-06-24 to 06-28.

Goal:
- assign current responsibility for escort reassignment, inspection delay, receiving refusal and local physical stops.

Findings:
- one escort reassignment materially delayed Lin-zone parts but protected a core medical convoy;
- one security hold was reasonable initially but exceeded its current evidence window;
- one receiver refusal was lawful because the destination itself lacked treatment capacity;
- local heavy-load stop prevented a separate relay cascade while small-craft passage remained possible.

Decision:
- record distinct justified, negligent and contested implementation acts; no collective military/local acquittal or blame.

Reward:
- field actors remain responsible agents rather than tools of Haren’s order.

Cost:
- victim compensation and institutional discipline require multiple proceedings and funds.

Hook:
- the inquiry must decide whether Haren’s offense is the existence of D4, the signature, the continuation or the foreseeable externalization of harm.

# Episode 773 — 유죄가 될 수 있는 세 가지

Date:
- CY 745-06-29 to 06-30.

Goal:
- frame the legal issues before public hearing.

Candidate findings:
1. unlawful creation or possession of authority;
2. abuse of lawfully granted emergency route authority;
3. negligent or reckless review/continuation after foreseeable downstream harm increased.

Preliminary ruling:
- office and initial authority were lawfully and transparently created;
- D4 had a legitimate protective purpose and real benefit;
- the central legal issue is whether Haren abused bounded authority by continuing concentrated foreseeable harm without sufficient residual protection and timely transition.

Decision:
- proceed on abuse of emergency route authority, review/continuation responsibility and related civil/political/institutional claims; no intentional-killing charge from the current record.

Reward:
- the case is neither necessity worship nor murder-by-statistics.

Cost:
- some victims see the charge as too narrow; some beneficiary regions see any prosecution as punishment for saving lives.

Hook:
- parties submit remedies ranging from acquittal to permanent imprisonment and model prohibition.

# Episode 774 — 처벌보다 먼저 필요한 것

Date:
- CY 745-07-01 to 07-02.

Goal:
- define remedy categories before selecting punishment.

Remedies considered:
- liberty restriction and inquiry detention;
- executive route-command disqualification;
- permanent unilateral high-level denial/master-key ban;
- reparations contribution and institutional funds;
- affected-region approval for later nonexecutive oversight;
- staff/model/credential governance reforms;
- victim, beneficiary and local-operator review seats;
- no total predictive-model prohibition.

Decision:
- separate punishment, prevention, compensation, institutional reform and future civic participation.

Reward:
- one sentence need not pretend to solve every causal layer.

Cost:
- no remedy restores the dead or eliminates future emergency tradeoffs.

Hook:
- E775 closes the consequence phase with an agreed statement of facts and contested legal positions.

# Episode 775 — 인정된 사실, 다투는 책임

Date:
- CY 745-07-03 to 07-04.

Goal:
- publish the joint pre-hearing fact statement.

Agreed facts:
- Haren lawfully held a bounded one-use office;
- he knew core benefit, outer forecasts, Lin’s correction and alternatives;
- he personally signed and continued D4 at later reviews;
- D4 materially stabilized core systems;
- residual protections were weaker and underperformed;
- 2,318 preliminary downstream deaths and 7,860 serious irreversible harms are strongly linked under current standards;
- Lin is a named current victim with pre-order evidence;
- model, staff, credentials, military, receivers and local systems also contributed.

Contested:
- whether continuation was reasonable necessity or abuse;
- how much later harm was avoidable at each review;
- criminal/liberty, civil, political and institutional remedy;
- Haren’s possible future nonexecutive role.

Reward:
- E776 public/institutional inquiry begins from shared facts.

Cost:
- polarization remains and several regions threaten to reject any verdict they view as erasing either the core benefit or outer dead.

Final hook:
- the inquiry opens with separate panels for personal offense, compensation, institutional design and future route governance.
