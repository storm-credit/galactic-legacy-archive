# Episode 440 — 열여덟 달짜리 왕관

Date:
- CY 744-02-24, later session.

POV:
- compact drafters, sixteen negotiators, claimant institutions and outside observers.

Visible goal:
- produce the ratifiable final GA4 compact with terms, review and emergency-executive rules.

Hidden pressure:
- a short term may destabilize external recognition; a permanent term can freeze an emergency compromise into hereditary constitution.

## Scene units

1. **Compact term** — initial 18 months or ordinary constitutional settlement, whichever occurs first; first full review at 90 days, then every 180 days; no automatic renewal or hereditary transfer.
2. **Crown Reference term** — same maximum term, subject to immediate suspension, recall, vacancy and compact-scope limits; ceremonial/external/public-accountability only.
3. **Seven operating fields** — Registry, civil, military, Continuity, direct physical, affected service-user and public evidence/appeal remain separately budgeted, appointed and reviewable.
4. **Emergency executive clause** — may be newly created for maximum 30 days by at least 8 of 11 full ratifiers plus relevant field and affected/provincial evidence; one fresh extension maximum; cannot absorb root, direct physical, affected-service or evidence fields.

Decisive choice:
- issue `Crown Reference Seven-Field Compact v1` as an 18-month partial compact rather than permanent empire-wide constitution.

Reward:
- a limited Crown and current operating structure become ratifiable.

Cost:
- future constitutional settlement, emergency powers and outside-jurisdiction relations remain unresolved.

Hook:
- sixteen negotiators file final compact positions, while observers and nonparticipants decide whether to adhere by function.
