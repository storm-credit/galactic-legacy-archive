# Episode 481 — 가장 비싼 배는 포를 쏘지 않는다

Date:
- CY 744-04-21.

Goal:
- choose whether the charter’s first major escort protects a damaged cruiser or the repair/pay convoy.

Decision:
- prioritize `기워진 별` convoy because it restores more total capability, while publishing the cruiser delay and crew risk.

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 482 — 채권자가 내리는 항로명령

Date:
- CY 744-04-23.

Goal:

Finding:

Cost:

Hook:
- two provincial escorts receive home orders to detach before the threat corridor.

# Episode 487 — 기워진 별이 도착한다

Date:
- CY 744-05-03.

Goal:
- complete the convoy and account for every person/cargo/claim.

Final mission arithmetic:
- original cargo 8,240 t;
- permanent loss 680 t;
- 210 t downgraded/reinspection but delivered to controlled custody;
- usable/current delivered cargo 7,560 t;
- all 1,128 people accounted;
- five injured, two serious; no death;
- one escort disabled 8–12 months;
- `기워진 별` output reduced 28% for 6–9 weeks.

Decision:
- classify the mission as strategic success with major capacity debt, not a clean victory.

Reward:
- mobile repair/pay capability reaches Vesper/Deme network.

Cost:
- repair demand exceeds capacity; the Charter must decide who remains grounded.

Final hook:
- 47 combat ships submit priority claims, while only 13 can receive meaningful work in the first board cycle.
