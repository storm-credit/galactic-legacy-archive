# Episode 653 — 이백육십오만의 거짓 합계

Date:
- CY 744-12-12 06:00–12:00.

Goal:
- reconcile the four physically earned envelopes and their incompatibilities.

Reference midpoints:
- A 520,000;
- B 810,000;
- C 1,050,000;
- Grey 270,000;
- arithmetic total 2,650,000.

Incompatibilities:
- shared thermal and traffic recovery;
- duplicate tugs/shuttles/crews;
- overlapping destinations/beds;
- defense readiness and hostile windows;
- local/holdout power;
- refusal and command authority.

Decision:
- treat 2.65m only as an individually tested nominal total, not simultaneous usable capacity.

Reward:
- later final capacities have physical evidence and explicit assumptions.

Cost:
- no plan can move all 3.33m and no constituency can claim its route alone solves Orpheus.

Final hook:
- last arrivals and final destination/medical/holdout updates push the population toward the locked decision range.
