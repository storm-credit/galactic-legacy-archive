# Episode 151 — 죽은 사람보다 먼저 온 청구서

Date:

POV:
- 테오.

Visible goal:
- secure immediate care, wages, funeral/memorial choice and habitat repair before parties freeze assets for liability.

Hidden pressure:
- every party wants to preserve evidence and limit responsibility, which can block the cash and parts needed now.

## Scene units

1. **Current obligations** — Jena’s caravan council identifies dependents/community obligations, injured workers, missed client jobs and habitat B emergency needs; no protagonist assumes beneficiary authority.
2. **Asset freeze** — Kael/Neutral/Helix place holds on the controller, caravan accounts and related claims; technically proper evidence preservation threatens payroll and cooling parts.
3. **Interim fund** — Theo separates liability admission from emergency contribution; establishes a 168,000 BSC provisional pool funded by caravan mutual reserve, Helix field safety reserve, Kael claims advance, habitat emergency budget and mission parties.
4. **Local control** — caravan/habitat representatives decide allocation order: injured care, current cooling, wages/dependents, delayed clients, then investigation costs.

Decisive choice:
- 회랑새 accepts a reduced/no immediate mission share so the emergency pool reaches local people first.

Reward:
- care and payroll continue; evidence remains preserved.

Cost:
- 회랑새 gains no accident windfall and must fund its own repairs through later work.

Collection/loss state:
- Jena legacy becomes compensation/service obligation, not a collectible record.

Hook:
- Helix produces an older certified software image showing the missing routine was removed during a safety-standard migration.

# Episode 152 — 돌아온 도망 모듈

Date:
- CY 742-08-15.

POV:
- 세린.

Visible goal:
- authenticate the older software image and locate the escaped module/tug.

Hidden pressure:
- “certification removed the safety routine” can become a convenient single-corporation blame narrative.

## Scene units

1. **Version comparison** — original worker-built routine monitored local queued actuator state but assumed slower hardware and manual watchers; later certified architecture removed it because modern isolated controllers handled queue state differently.
2. **Copy error** — rebuilders copied the newer clean software into mixed old/local hardware, creating an unmodeled gap; no one document describes the final assembled system.
3. **Escaped tug contact** — the tug that fled sends a protected distress/settlement request: its removed service collar is unstable and its crew fears both seizure and prosecution.
4. **Conditional return** — workers/crew agree to return the module for inspection if occupied status, wage claims and no automatic criminal confession are recognized.

Decisive choice:
- inquiry accepts conditional safe harbor instead of pursuit/seizure.

Reward:
- escaped service collar returns physically; worker testimony and software lineage connect.

Cost:
- creditor/salvage claim strengthens; no exclusive Academy custody.

Mystery state:
- the missing routine is explained as architecture migration plus mixed rebuild, not intentional murder code.

Hook:
- the returned collar matches NR72-118 Bay 2 mounting geometry but its cooling spine has been cut into three separately claimed pieces.

# Episode 154 — 제나 규격

Date:
- CY 742-08-17.

POV:
- caravan/habitat worker panel, with Nera as technical participant.

Visible goal:
- turn the accident findings into enforceable safety requirements without using Jena’s name against her community’s wishes.

Hidden pressure:
- naming a standard can honor labor or convert a dead worker into corporate marketing.

## Scene units

1. **Community authorization** — caravan council authorizes public use of Jena’s name only for failure/worker-stop requirements, not product branding; private medical data remains restricted.
2. **Required rules** — local queued-state discovery, physical branch confirmation, worker stop authority, user/medical representative, test envelope, training, incident feedback and shared liability.
3. **Helix objection/acceptance** — Helix argues some requirements reduce automation efficiency and transfer liability unpredictably; accepts them for mixed legacy systems, not all certified systems.
4. **Enforcement carrier** — Kael/Neutral condition future certification/insurance on compliance; Neris and habitat users receive formal review seats.

Decisive choice:
- create the `Jena Mixed-Service Safety Annex`, governed jointly rather than owned by Nera/Helix.

Reward:
- concrete safety governance and named worker contribution.

Cost:
- higher training, staffing and inspection expense; slower deployment.

Hook:
- applying the annex makes the proposed relay too large for the remaining Bay 2 space unless cargo or workshop capacity is cut again.

Cost:

Collection state:
- G2-S10 relay-service capability advances; G2-S07 Bay 2 remains lost/deferred.

Hook:
