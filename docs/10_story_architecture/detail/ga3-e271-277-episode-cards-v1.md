# Episode 271 — 명령 계통을 적어 내라

Date:
- CY 742-12-21.

POV:
- Sella Kove, unit commanders and civilian oversight contacts.

Visible goal:
- identify who each ship, frame unit and fixed battery will actually obey before the first external convoy arrives.

Hidden pressure:
- legal title, pay source, maintenance control, ammunition custody and current crew loyalty point to different authorities.

## Scene units

1. **Force roll call** — every deployable unit reports hull/readiness, pay/service source, legal chain, current commander, withdrawal conditions and prohibited missions.
2. **Fragmented loyalties** — one corvette answers Civic law but Helix maintenance; cutter groups hold old Imperial recognition; armed tugs answer yards/workers; frames split among defense, police and contract systems.
3. **No paper unity** — Sella refuses to count politically sympathetic assets as commanded; readiness falls below public assumptions.
4. **Temporary obligation** — units agree only to immediate rescue, collision prevention and current fixed-defense safety until an interim command compact exists.

Decisive choice:
- publish actual chain-of-authority and readiness rather than claiming a unified defense force.

Reward:
- false force totals and hidden refusal conditions become visible.

Cost:
- opponents learn fragmentation; public confidence drops; patrol gaps widen during review.

Hook:
- Rian’s future-based deployment could concentrate the ready ships within six hours, but only if local commanders accept him as operational commander.

# Episode 272 — 준비된 배와 싸울 수 있는 배

Date:
- CY 742-12-22.

POV:
- Sella, Maren, Vera and local fleet maintenance/crew representatives.

Visible goal:
- convert the published inventory into a credible 72-hour readiness map.

Hidden pressure:
- “ready” can mean can undock, can patrol, can fight once or can sustain defense.

## Scene units

1. **Readiness classes** — the reliable frigate can fight one bounded engagement but lacks sustained ammunition/repair; 3 corvettes have different sensor, missile and crew limits; 9 cutters cannot all share ammunition or encrypted command.
2. **Frame truth** — 28 ready military/security frames include incompatible weapons, police limits and contract restrictions; 64 utility frames can rescue, tow, screen and hold infrastructure but are not free combat mass.
3. **Fixed defense** — functional range varies 45–60%; two batteries can track but not fire safely without current lane confirmation.
4. **Public map** — readiness is published by mission: rescue, patrol, escort, boarding, habitat defense, outer engagement and sustained operation.

Decisive choice:
- define mission readiness and endurance rather than one combat percentage.

Reward:
- sector planning can use real capabilities and stop conditions.

Cost:
- apparent fleet power shrinks; maintenance and ammunition disputes become immediate political conflicts.

Hook:
- the provisional board asks Rian for a deployment plan and several officers assume the request grants command.

# Episode 273 — 지휘관이 되지 않는 계획

Date:
- CY 742-12-23.

POV:
- Rian, Sella, local commanders and the three-body board.

Visible goal:
- use Rian’s experience without transferring overall command or making him responsible for a city he does not govern.

Hidden pressure:
- the fastest response plan depends on one commander reallocating ammunition, lanes and ships across local domains.

## Scene units

1. **Rian’s plan** — four-sector defense, mobile reserve, early patrol concentration and current Spine B denial boundary; technically coherent but assumes shared command and ammunition.
2. **Sella’s refusal** — accepts threat geometry and rejects outsider overall command; demands unit withdrawal rights, civilian rules and local sector commanders.
3. **Role split** — Rian becomes bounded task/flight adviser for route geometry, 07 and 파루스 operations; Sella retains interim local operational coordination.
4. **Changed plan** — slower sector handoffs, explicit no-fire lanes and split ammunition custody replace the unified plan.

Decisive choice:
- Rian refuses the implied overall command and submits the plan to local modification.

Reward:
- future experience becomes usable without personal sovereignty.

Cost:
- mobilization requires 11–16 more hours and cannot concentrate all ready assets.

Hook:
- one cutter detachment states it will not accept orders from Transit/Foundry delegates or a local acting commander not recognized by Imperial authority.

# Episode 274 — 네 구역의 방위

Date:
- CY 742-12-24.

POV:
- sector commanders, civilian contacts and service operators.

Visible goal:
- establish interim sectors, rules of engagement and civil/service constraints.

Hidden pressure:
- sector autonomy can preserve legitimacy but fail during cross-sector attack or pursuit.

## Scene units

1. **Four sectors** — Outer/Anchor Route; Civic/Garden Habitat; Foundry/Surface Infrastructure; Mobile Rescue/Reserve.
2. **Authority fields** — operational command, fire permission, boarding, pursuit, denial, civilian evacuation and service stop remain separately assigned.
3. **Rules** — no fire through inhabited approach lanes without current confirmation; no unilateral node denial/mining; medical/rescue custody protected; pursuit cannot strip a habitat sector below minimum.
4. **Oversight** — three-body board holds bounded civilian review; Garden/medical and technical stop authorities can halt actions that cross current service minima.

Decisive choice:
- adopt segmented sectors and transparent rules rather than one emergency commander.

Reward:
- units can deploy under known constraints and civilian/service safeguards.

Cost:
- slower ammunition sharing, cross-sector pursuit and target handoff; commanders fear political delay.

Hook:
- Fourth Cutter Detachment receives a delayed Imperial recognition packet ordering it to hold independent from the new compact.

# Episode 275 — 떠나는 두 척

Date:
- CY 742-12-25.

POV:
- Doman Rek, cutter crews, Sella and affected patrol sectors.

Visible goal:
- resolve Fourth Detachment’s refusal without mutiny violence or fictitious unity.

Hidden pressure:
- Doman’s order is delayed and politically useful but not obviously forged; his crews rely on Imperial pay/recognition and distrust the provisional board.

## Scene units

1. **Refusal** — Doman rejects civilian oversight from bodies he considers temporary and unrecognized; he accepts rescue obligations but not sector combat orders.
2. **Crew split** — one cutter and two frames choose the local compact; two cutters and four frames remain with Doman; personnel may transfer without collective punishment.
3. **Withdrawal corridor** — Sella permits Doman’s group to move to an Imperial recognition beacon outside current defense lanes under no-hostile-action terms.
4. **Material consequence** — Outer/Anchor patrol loses two ready cutters and four frames; one sector patrol gap expands from 7 to 13 hours.

Decisive choice:
- allow bounded withdrawal instead of arresting crews or pretending command.

Reward:
- no internal firefight; actual loyalties and future negotiation path remain.

Cost:
- local readiness falls; Doman may provide external actors with current defense information and return under another authority.

Hook:
- an unidentified damaged courier approaches through the new patrol gap with a medical distress code and no current authentication.

# Episode 276 — 열세 분 늦은 구조

Date:
- CY 742-12-26.

POV:
- Mobile Rescue sector, Sella, Rian as route adviser and medical custody.

Visible goal:
- identify and recover the damaged courier without opening a spoofed attack lane.

Hidden pressure:
- segmented authority and the cutter withdrawal slow confirmation while life support declines.

## Scene units

1. **Distress** — courier carries 23 people, 4 requiring urgent care; current authentication fails but physical emissions and passenger messages partly match.
2. **Handoff delay** — Outer sector tracks, Mobile Rescue holds the recovery craft and Habitat sector controls the safe lane; no one can unilaterally clear all three.
3. **Costly caution** — recovery starts 13 minutes later than a unified-command model; one patient deteriorates and requires invasive stabilization but survives.
4. **Benefit of limits** — physical inspection finds a dormant tracking/command repeater in cargo; segmented boarding prevents it from entering Anchor systems automatically.

Decisive choice:
- accept the delay and complete physical/medical recovery without granting the courier network access.

Reward:
- 23 people recovered alive; hostile or unauthorized repeater isolated.

Cost:
- one patient suffers a longer recovery and possible persistent respiratory impairment; critics use the delay against segmented command.

Hook:
- the courier’s route data confirms the Helix security/supply convoy will arrive first, carrying medicine, parts and a receivership enforcement order.

# Episode 277 — 가장 먼저 온 구조대

Date:
- CY 742-12-27.

POV:
- Sella, Iven, Helix convoy command, dock/medical/Foundry actors.

Visible goal:
- receive urgent Helix supplies without allowing force posture and creditor order to decide custody before negotiation.

Hidden pressure:
- the convoy can materially improve medicine, parts and defense readiness, and it is strong enough to enforce limited seizure if local forces resist badly.

## Scene units

1. **Arrival force** — 1 security corvette, 2 armed technical tugs, 3 cargo/medical support ships and 12 frames/utility units (6 security, 6 technical).
2. **Cargo promise** — medicine, certified cooling/node parts, hospital systems, frame/defense service and payroll continuity.
3. **Enforcement order** — receivership claims cover Line 3 scheduler/service assets, one certified cold-chain depot, B-4 sensor packages and selected collateral facilities.
4. **Interim posture** — local sectors permit medical distress and safe holding lanes but deny armed docking/remote lock activation pending facility-level terms.

Decisive choice:
- accept medical/service approach under segmented escort while holding security frames and enforcement authority outside critical facilities.

Reward:
- supplies remain available and no immediate battle occurs.

Cost:
- Helix force gains physical presence; local defense sectors expose coordination limits; receivership confrontation becomes unavoidable.

Final hook:
- one cold-chain depot reports that its remote service lock has already accepted the convoy’s enforcement credentials.
