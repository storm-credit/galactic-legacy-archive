# Episode 263 — 열여덟 분의 항로

Date:
- CY 742-12-13 to 742-12-14.

POV:
- local Spine B operators, inbound crews and cross-zone receiving teams.

Visible goal:
- complete the first 412 t / 47-person live passage and stop before Spine B exceeds verified state.

Hidden pressure:
- every extra minute could improve cargo recovery but consume the first route’s remaining thermal and sensor margin.

## Scene units

1. **Live start** — B-1 holds, B-2 begins at 18%, B-3 validates the bounded manifest and B-4 uses direct/physical/derived state in parallel.
2. **Sensor disagreement** — derived state reports normal recovery while the physical watcher sees lag and collar vibration; local stop authority slows the sequence and drops one automatic acceleration step.
3. **Passage** — 412 t and 47 people cross within the 18-minute active interval; no cargo/patient loss and no hull collision.
4. **Hard stop** — operators close after the approved manifest rather than adding waiting local/outbound cargo. B-2 settles at 16–17% immediate post-window recovery, B-4 derived confidence falls and the external diagnostic signature expires after evidence capture.

Decisive choice:
- close the window after one approved manifest despite requests to use remaining apparent time.

Reward:
- first limited Spine B cargo/medical passage succeeds;
- medical, Garden, technical and habitation support arrive;
- Ardis gains an alternative diagnostic/service path.

Cost:
- next window is uncertain for 6–10 days;
- B-2 recovery is temporarily lower;
- B-4 remains sensor-limited;
- 47 arriving people include claims, technical and political actors;
- the dense defense module remains outside under disputed custody.

Hard ruling:
- Spine B is **limited live service**, not restored normal traffic and not L2 capacity.

Final hook:
- Civic wants the medicine, Foundry wants certified parts, Transit wants housing/fire stock, Defense wants the retained security load and every group claims the first unloading priority.
