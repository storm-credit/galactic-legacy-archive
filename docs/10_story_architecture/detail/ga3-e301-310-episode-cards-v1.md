# Episode 301 — 다섯 전선에 같은 배는 없다

Date:
- CY 743-01-20.

POV:
- Sella, Iris, provisional board and five-front leaders.

Visible goal:
- assign current authority, ships, frames, power, parts and service reserves before the simultaneous alerts become one cascade.

Hidden pressure:
- every front requests the same ready cutters, utility frames, technicians and authentication bandwidth.

## Scene units

1. **Front board** — each front reports objective, minimum assets, stop authority, failure cost and what it can surrender.
2. **Scarcity** — only 8 ready/mixed cutters are available after one Doman cutter returns; 24 local ready frames plus Imperial/Helix assets cannot all enter inhabited or node lanes.
3. **Authority assignment** — Sella/Iris coordinate external and cross-sector missions; local leaders retain front execution; Rian receives mission/flight command for one linked Anchor–approach operation only.
4. **Known insufficiency** — Transit/Civic service front receives 6 fewer utility/security frames than its requested minimum because Anchor and regional approach are prioritized.

Decisive choice:
- publish the knowingly under-supported Transit/Civic front rather than hiding the tradeoff or placing every asset under one commander.

Reward:
- all five fronts have named authority, current objective and standing rules.

Cost:
- one front is knowingly vulnerable; political blame and evacuation anxiety begin before battle.

Hook:
- Records/command detects three authenticated orders assigning the same mobile reserve to different fronts.
