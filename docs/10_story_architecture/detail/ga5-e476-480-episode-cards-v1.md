# Episode 476 — 서명하지 않은 사람들의 조항

Date:
- CY 744-04-19.

Visible goal:
- process public objections before any formation signs Charter v0.1.

Key objections:
- families: no complete death/pension guarantee;
- workers: repair and physical stops can be overruled indirectly by schedule pressure;
- provinces: exit costs may become hidden central taxation;
- captains: unclear surrender and command succession;
- service users: civilian routes can be deprioritized without standing.

Decision:
- add a mandatory affected-party notice, named shortfall ledger and post-loss review trigger, but do not promise funding that does not exist.

Reward:
- objections alter executable text instead of becoming ceremonial consultation.

Cost:
- two ships delay signature; charter start compresses into the remaining two days of Act 5A.

Hook:
- Ardo demands a battle-succession clause before his formation joins.

# Episode 477 — 지휘관이 쓰러진 뒤

Date:
- CY 744-04-19.

Visible goal:
- define mission-command succession without transferring ownership to the successor.

Conflict:
- Ardo wants automatic senior-officer succession;
- Sira wants formation withdrawal/reconfirmation;
- Messa wants current crew and casualty obligations preserved;
- captains reject a succession rule that can expand mission scope.

Decision:
- succession transfers only the current mission intent, geometry, accepted ships and remaining duration;
- no new offensive objective, hull title, pay, key or political recognition passes;
- formation commanders may detach under published catastrophic/home-defense rules.

Reward:
- alert/battle command can continue after incapacitation.

Cost:
- succession remains slower than a permanent navy and may shrink the force during crisis.

Hook:
- support ships refuse to sign unless combat formations accept minimum rescue/repair obligations.

# Episode 478 — 지원함의 거부권

Date:
- CY 744-04-20 03:00–11:00.

Visible goal:
- bring tenders, rescue ships and medical craft into the charter without making them expendable auxiliaries.

Conflict:
- combat commanders want support allocation under mission command;
- Tav and service captains demand safety stops, minimum escort and no forced cannibalization.

Decision:
- support hulls lend capacity by named task;
- technical/medical stops act directly;
- combat ships owe minimum escort/rescue duty;
- no support ship may be cannibalized without title, worker, safety and replacement review.

Reward:
- three additional support ships conditionally join.

Cost:
- one cruiser loses preferred repair priority and withdraws from initial formation.

Hook:
- the formation still lacks a funded loss-share formula.

# Episode 479 — 손실을 누가 갚는가

Date:
- CY 744-04-20 11:00–18:00.

Visible goal:
- create an executable minimum loss/debt rule for the initial mission.

Funding fields:
- Treasury earned-pay and death minimum;
- provincial family/home contribution;
- mission-user cargo/service levy;
- yard labor/repair credit;
- insurance/Neutral rescue claim;
- unfunded residual published rather than hidden.

Decision:
- enact only minimum immediate death, serious injury, rescue and first-stage repair support;
- long-term pension, full hull loss and cargo compensation remain claims, not guarantees.

Reward:
- ships can calculate minimum consequences before joining.

Cost:
- 38–46% of projected worst-case long-term loss remains unfunded under current contributors.

Hook:
- final signature count shows a smaller force than public speeches promised.

# Episode 480 — 공동전단헌장 0.1

Date:
- CY 744-04-20 18:00–23:50.

Visible goal:
- enact Charter v0.1 and close Act 5A without declaring a permanent fleet.

Chartered term:
- 14 days;
- V03 Vesper, V06 Talren and named connecting lanes;
- defense, rescue, convoy and depot-service missions only;
- no claimant enforcement, territorial seizure or Outer Front offensive.

Accepted assets:
- 15 combat ships;
- 14 support/rescue/tender ships;
- 92 frames/utility craft;
- not all nearby formations.

Command:
- Ardo: external battle formation lead during defined alerts;
- Rian: 8-combat/7-support route and mission task group;
- Sira/Oren/Tav: separate formation/route/support authority;
- captains retain safety/surrender/internal discipline;
- Messa holds crew/pay/rescue/audit standing;
- physical/technical/medical stops remain direct.

Charter clauses:
1. shared intent;
2. geography/duration;
3. command/succession;
4. captain/formation authority;
5. prohibited-order and physical/medical/technical stops;
6. crew pay/oath/audit;
7. authentication/courier fallback;
8. rescue/casualty record;
9. minimum support/debt allocation;
10. exit/sunset/review.

Decision:
- sign a limited mission charter with explicit unfunded claims and no permanent admiralty.

Reward:
- Initial Vesper Task Force becomes operational.

Cost:
- only 15 combat ships join; recall, full family funding, maintenance governance and surrendered-ship title remain unresolved.

Act 5A closing hook:
- a repair/pay convoy carrying the mobile tender `기워진 별` is attacked and claimed by two authorities; losing it would ground more ships than losing a cruiser.

## End State E480
