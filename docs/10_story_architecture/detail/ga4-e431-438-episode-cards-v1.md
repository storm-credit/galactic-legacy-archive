# Episode 431 — 후보보다 먼저 직위를 표결한다

Date:
- CY 744-02-20.

POV:
- sixteen negotiators, observers/nonparticipants, claimant officeholders and public evidence panel.

Visible goal:
- separate the structure of the Sovereign Reference Office from the person who may occupy it.

Hidden pressure:
- candidate popularity can silently expand the office; a weak office can be strengthened later by precedent and emergency interpretation.

## Scene units

1. **Structure ballot first** — public/ceremonial/external/accountability functions; no operating field, budget capture, appointment chain, timeout default, root/common seal or direct actuation.
2. **Candidate ballot later** — occupant can only be selected after structure, incompatibility, ratification and recall rules pass.
3. **No personal mandate transfer** — claimant forces, civil systems, Registry, Continuity, local physical/service/evidence authority do not travel with a candidate into the office.
4. **Observers/outside** — may file evidence/conditions but are not counted as ratifiers unless they explicitly join.

Decisive choice:
- bind future candidates to a preexisting office rather than letting a candidate define the office after selection.

Reward:
- no person-first shortcut or charismatic emergency expansion.

Cost:
- final selection slows with only eight chronological days left in GA4.

Hook:
- the next issue is whether a candidate may keep an existing claimant field while occupying the reference office.

# Episode 432 — 왕관과 원래 직위를 동시에 쥘 수 없다

Date:
- CY 744-02-20, later session.

POV:
- Elian, Mara, Dor, Isa, field institutions and appointment-law counsel.

Visible goal:
- define incompatibility and separation plans for any candidate.

Hidden pressure:
- forcing total resignation may destroy competence and continuity; allowing dual office turns the reference office into field control.

## Scene units

1. **Elian case** — personal Registered-Heir status may support candidacy, but Registry field remains institutional and separately governed.
2. **Mara case** — candidacy would require civil executive powers to pass to a named bounded alternate/board during the reference term.
3. **Dor/Isa cases** — military command and root-key custody are incompatible with reference-office occupancy unless separately transferred under existing field law.
4. **Separation rule** — candidate must publish field relinquishment/alternate/recusal plan before nomination is valid; reference office cannot remove or command the successor field holder.

Decisive choice:
- require functional separation without erasing a candidate’s prior lawful history or eligibility.

Reward:
- no candidate carries a hidden field bundle into the Crown Reference Office.

Cost:
- strong candidates may refuse or weaken their current useful function during transition.

Hook:
- nomination standing can still let one claimant bloc fill the ballot with controlled candidates.

# Episode 433 — 누가 왕을 지명할 수 있는가

Date:
- CY 744-02-21.

POV:
- negotiating bodies, provincial/affected representatives, Registry/civil/military/Continuity institutions and observers.

Visible goal:
- establish nomination standing without making hereditary registration or negotiator majority sufficient alone.

Hidden pressure:
- broad nomination invites symbolic spoilers; narrow nomination predetermines the winner.

## Scene units

1. **Nomination bundle** — at least two negotiating bodies from different post-pilot arrangement types; candidate consent; valid separation plan; public evidence/provenance file.
2. **Registry role** — validates identity/eligibility/provenance, not political selection.
3. **Affected/provincial challenge** — may challenge office-scope or candidate-conflict facts; cannot veto purely by opposition to the person without evidence/standing.
4. **Outside bodies** — observers/nonparticipants may endorse or challenge evidence but do not create binding nominations unless they join the negotiation/compact process.

Decisive choice:
- distribute nomination across body types and public evidence rather than one dynasty, claimant office or simple majority.

Reward:
- ballot remains open but bounded by real standing and separation duties.

Cost:
- coalition-building and procedural challenges become part of selection.

Hook:
- negotiators now dispute whether eleven votes can bind only themselves or claim a wider state office.

# Episode 434 — 열한 표가 스물넷을 묶지 않는다

Date:
- CY 744-02-21, later session.

POV:
- sixteen negotiators, four observers, four nonparticipants and compact counsel.

Visible goal:
- set structure and occupant ratification thresholds without fabricating empire-wide authority.

Hidden pressure:
- too low a threshold creates a factional crown; too high lets outside bodies veto a compact they refuse to join.

## Scene units

1. **Structure threshold** — at least 11 of 16 negotiators, including support from multiple post-pilot arrangement types and explicit affected/provincial conditions.
2. **Compact effect** — binds full ratifiers only; function-specific adherents accept named fields/certificates; observers/nonparticipants remain outside.
3. **Occupant threshold** — after full compact ratifiers are known, at least 8 full ratifiers plus Registry eligibility and cleared scope/conflict challenges.
4. **No empire claim** — public title must state compact scope; no automatic jurisdiction over outside forces, budgets, keys, people or territory.

Decisive choice:
- create a real but partial compact office rather than an allegedly universal crown.

Reward:
- participants can settle succession without coercing nonparticipants.

Cost:
- parallel states and external recognition disputes remain after selection.

Hook:
- a limited office still needs recall, suspension and vacancy rules that cannot be controlled by the occupant’s supporters.

# Episode 435 — 왕을 세우는 규칙보다 왕을 멈추는 규칙

Date:
- CY 744-02-22.

POV:
- loss families/crews, field bodies, public evidence/appeal institutions and claimant teams.

Visible goal:
- define immediate suspension, recall, vacancy and alternate without creating a second hidden sovereign.

Hidden pressure:
- easy recall makes the office symbolic and unstable; hard recall permits prestige/representation capture.

## Scene units

1. **Immediate suspension** — illegal direct actuation, root/key seizure, field-budget starvation, armed/civil command claim or evidence tampering triggers temporary suspension through public evidence + affected/relevant field findings.
2. **Recall** — 8 of current full compact ratifiers after evidence hearing; candidate/occupant retains defense and appeal but no automatic stay of direct safety protections.
3. **Vacancy** — reference functions revert to separate Registry/external/compact attestations; no facilitator or Regent automatically becomes sovereign.
4. **Alternate** — ceremonial/external alternate only, preconfirmed and unable to inherit claimant fields or extend term.

Decisive choice:
- make the office survivable without an occupant and removable without transferring power to one rival office.

Reward:
- succession does not collapse into emergency inheritance at vacancy.

Cost:
- external counterparties face uncertainty and repeated certification if the office is suspended or vacant.

Hook:
- with procedure defined, formal nominations can finally be filed.

# Episode 437 — 왕관 묶음이라는 가정

Date:
- CY 744-02-23.

POV:
- evidence/law panel, Elian/Mara teams, Dor/Isa institutions, provinces/affected bodies and Tera’s protected record.

Visible goal:
- test the claim that the sovereign’s offices, keys, forces, services and histories formed one inheritable Crown bundle.

Hidden pressure:
- centuries of practice and external recognition treat the Crown as one state, even though emergency protocols separated functions.

## Scene units

1. **Protocol text** — Registered-Heir chain transfers Crown/Registry recognition; Regency, Protector and Continuity protocols have separate triggers, custodians, limits and termination rules.
2. **Physical/institutional history** — budgets, commands, root keys, local custody and evidence were never held or transferred by one identical mechanism.
3. **Practice evidence** — past rulers coordinated fields politically, but coordination practice is not proof of one legal master key or automatic succession bundle.
4. **Affected/local evidence** — physical, care, service and evidence authority cannot be inherited as property through Crown registration.

Decisive choice:
- frame E438 as an office-definition ruling, not a preference between Elian and Mara.

Reward:
- final succession question becomes legally answerable from the authentic protocols.

Cost:
- both dynastic and central-executive narratives lose the simplicity of one inherited state.

Hook:
- the panel is ready to rule whether any master heir exists.

# Episode 438 — 마스터 후계자는 없다

Date:
- CY 744-02-23, decisive ruling session.

POV:
- joint evidence/law panel, claimant officeholders, negotiating/affected bodies and public record.

Visible goal:
- issue the decisive succession ruling required to proceed to final compact ratification.

Hidden pressure:
- rejecting a master heir can be misread as abolishing the Crown, invalidating all claimants or declaring permanent plural government.

## Scene units

1. **Registered-Heir ruling** — Elian validly inherits Crown Reference, Registry and ceremonial/external succession standing subject to compact scope/ratification; not civil, military, root/service, local physical, affected-user or evidence authority.
2. **Separate-office ruling** — Mara civil, Dor military and Isa Continuity functions arise from their own authentic triggers and do not become property of the Crown Reference occupant.
3. **Noninheritable fields** — direct physical, affected-service and public evidence/appeal authority remain current institutional/people functions, not sovereign inheritance.
4. **Stronger sovereign path** — a single executive sovereign may still be newly created by explicit constitutional/compact ratification, but cannot claim automatic inheritance from Registered-Heir succession.

Decisive choice:
- reject the `Master Succession Claim`: no heir or office automatically inherits all fields, keys, forces, services, people, records or histories.

Reward:
- the authentic six-protocol contradiction resolves without declaring documents fake or giving one claimant a master key.

Cost:
- no empire-wide command appears; selection, ratification, opposition and parallel jurisdictions remain.

Payoff lock:
- M-011 `no master heir / no master succession` receives its decisive E438 institutional payoff.

Final hook:
- Elian can now accept a limited Crown Reference Office, Mara can preserve civil authority or seek a newly ratified executive, and the sixteen negotiators must choose the compact rather than pretend succession chose for them.
