# Episode 220 — 환승지대의 냉장고

Date:
- CY 742-10-28 to 742-10-29.

POV:
- Transit Belt receiving workers and mutual-aid coordinators.

Visible goal:
- receive and distribute six cargo pods without losing perishables or letting identity failure become an excuse for seizure/eviction.

Hidden pressure:
- the official receiving system recognizes cargo title and temperature class more reliably than the people and temporary habitats waiting for it.

## Scene units

1. **Physical load** — six pods, three tugs, approximately 186 t of food/water/biological goods; two pods lack current berth authorization after a controller restart.
2. **Cold-chain conflict** — one local cold loop is functional but assigned to an authenticated creditor/market load; a mutual-aid loop is physically available but outside current insurer records.
3. **Manual unload** — local workers use physical temperature checks, portable cooling and bounded relay state; one pod is opened/distributed under witnessed emergency process.
4. **Visible loss** — 11–16 t of temperature-sensitive goods are downgraded, spoiled or diverted from intended medical/market use; no mass starvation is solved.

Decisive choice:
- prioritize current safe distribution and disclose the loss rather than preserve cargo title/value while people and habitats wait.

Reward:
- four pods enter current distribution; two remain delayed but stable enough for follow-up.

Cost:
- 11–16 t value/service loss, insurer/creditor claims and unequal distribution among Transit sections.

Collection state:
- relay proves bounded receiving utility but cannot create berth, cooling or legal standing.

Hook:
- Foundry reports that the first valve bodies from the restarted line are being loaded for a creditor convoy instead of the city trial.

POV:

Visible goal:
- determine who receives the restarted cooling/valve output and whether the line keeps running.

Hidden pressure:

## Scene units

1. **Output claim** — creditor contract claims the first 60% of conforming output; city trial needs parts for Transit/Anchor service.
2. **Worker condition** — delayed injury pay, overtime and stop rights remain unresolved; continuing full output without settlement repeats emergency exploitation.

4. **Worker vote/decision** — Shift Council authorizes a partial run only after immediate wage/injury reserve and technical stop terms; 40% current output goes to public service, 60% remains creditor-pledged.

Decisive choice:
- workers choose a bounded split rather than heroic unpaid production or total seizure.

Reward:
- enough valve/filter bodies for one Transit receiving repair and one test article.

Cost:
- creditor payment/claim worsens; output remains insufficient for Anchor and broader city demand.

Relationship state:
- local workers, not Nera/Rian, decide whether the line runs.

Hook:

POV:

Reward:

Cost:

Relationship state:
- Transit residents participate as current users, not faceless beneficiaries.

Hook:

# Episode 225 — 공동 상태판

Date:
- CY 742-11-03.

POV:
- 세린, Toma and zone-level data custodians.

Visible goal:
- create a current-state board useful for cross-zone decisions without building one total city database or pretending missing data is safe.

Hidden pressure:
- centralized restoration offers faster integration if Ardis transfers broader data and command rights.

## Scene units

1. **State fields** — zone/service, current value/range, source, confidence, current dependent people/service, stop authority, next update and missing/withheld data.
2. **Data boundaries** — Transit household/identity, medical data, worker/pay records and security/route information remain separately governed.

4. **Local participation** — each zone keeps its own operator/data steward and may withhold nonrequired detail while exposing current limits.

Decisive choice:
- establish a shared current-state board with source/confidence/unknowns, not a central control dashboard.

Reward:
- cross-zone decisions can identify stale/missing state and current dependencies.

Cost:
- slower updates, incomplete prediction and recurring labor; external funders see less total control.

Hook:
- during the first full Garden–Transit trial, the board correctly flags an unknown receiving branch—but cargo sponsors ask the operators to continue because the physical temperature remains stable.
