# Episode 776 — 네 개의 재판

Date:
- CY 745-07-05 to 07-10.

Goal:
- establish separate standards so one verdict does not absorb every causal layer.

Personal offense:
- authority, knowledge, foreseeability, alternatives, review choices and bounded office duty.

Civil/reparations:
- causation, beneficiary/institutional gain, victim loss, shared liability and fund capacity.

Political/office:
- trust, future concentration risk, mandate and victim-region standing.

Institutional:
- model horizon/omission, staff summaries, credential succession, residual-duty enforcement, physical/current authority and review design.

Decision:
- evidence can support different findings across panels; acquittal or liability in one does not automatically control the others.

Reward:
- Blood Admiral’s five layers receive actual procedural separation.

Cost:
- the process is slower and emotionally unsatisfying to constituencies demanding one total judgment.

Hook:
- Haren’s defense begins with D4’s real protective benefit and the legality of his appointment.

# Episode 777 — 필요했지만 남용일 수 있다

Date:
- CY 745-07-11 to 07-16.

Goal:
- test whether necessity and legal appointment eliminate abuse.

Defense:
- office lawfully created;
- core cascade was current and severe;
- D4 materially stabilized 1.668m people;
- alternatives were slower, incompletely authorized or operationally risky;
- residual missions and reviews existed.

Affected/victim response:
- legal power can be abused through continuation and externalization;
- Haren had corrected outer data and knew residual protection was structurally weaker;
- the 24/48-hour transition alternatives were materially different from the signature moment.

Interim finding:
- lawful creation, legitimate purpose and real benefit do not immunize later review choices from abuse.

Reward:
- inquiry rejects both “necessary means innocent” and “harm means authority was always unlawful.”

Cost:
- no simple moral category remains.

Hook:
- the personal-offense panel must identify which review choice crosses the legal threshold.

# Episode 778 — 스물네 시간과 마흔여덟 시간

Date:
- CY 745-07-17 to 07-23.

Goal:
- determine the legally decisive conduct.

24-hour state:
- outer forecast worsened;
- quota transition more available but still incomplete;
- core reconfiguration risk remained material;
- Haren continued, modified one cap and elevated support.

48-hour state:
- core stable but fragile;
- rotating transition substantially prepared;
- 3–6 hour reconfiguration and hostile risk remained;

- 24-hour continuation is seriously contested but not independently sufficient for the final offense;
- 48-hour continuation, without stronger residual protection or partial transfer despite a materially available alternative, crosses into abuse of emergency route authority with foreseeable downstream harm.

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 779 — 죽지 않은 사람은 배상금이 아니다

Date:
- CY 745-07-24 to 07-31.

Goal:
- set civil/reparations principles without subtracting hypothetical saved lives from actual victims.

Ruling principles:
- real core benefit affects public/institutional context and proportionality, not the existence of outer claims;
- 44k–73k no-D4 forecast is not a proven saved-person ledger or monetary offset;
- Lin and other victim claims require actual causation, loss and current evidence;
- office, beneficiary-service funds, model/service institutions, responsible implementers and Haren may bear different shares.

Initial liability architecture:
- emergency-route victim and recovery fund;
- beneficiary-region contribution based on protected capacity/benefit and ability;
- institution/model/credential contributions for proven design/review failures;
- Haren personal reparations contribution over fifteen years;
- no family payment conditioned on political recognition or waiver of public findings.

Reward:
- victims do not finance the core benefit through uncompensated loss.

Cost:
- contributions burden already damaged beneficiary and route systems; exact individual awards require later claims work.

Hook:
- political panel addresses whether Haren can ever hold route office again.

# Episode 780 — 다시는 같은 열쇠를 갖지 못한다

- permanent ban on unilateral high-level denial/master key;

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 782 — 모델을 없애지 않는 판결

Date:
- CY 745-08-15 to 08-21.

Goal:
- issue institutional findings for doctrine, staff, credentials, residual missions and current stops.

Findings:
- predictive denial/dependency modeling is legitimate and materially life-saving when governed;
- recurring short horizons, omission patterns and summary compression increased harm;
- direct denial commands were stronger than residual-duty execution;
- credential/office succession controls were inadequate;
- current physical/medical/local evidence must remain independently actionable;
- victim/affected-region review entered too weakly and too late.

Remedies:
- model cards with horizon, omitted groups, confidence and counterfactual limits;
- separated proposal, authorization, implementation and review keys;
- residual mission capacity secured before high-level denial where physically possible;
- mandatory affected-region/victim advocate and public/protected evidence;
- no silent D4 composition or inherited master key.

Reward:
- useful tools survive with visible governance.

Cost:
- future emergency decisions become slower, costlier and still imperfect.

Hook:
- E783 issues the combined final judgment and preserves appeal/review rights.

# Episode 783 — 하렌 도안 판결

Date:
- CY 745-08-22 to 08-31.

Final personal finding:
- **guilty of abuse of emergency route authority with foreseeable downstream harm**, centered on the 48-hour continuation decision and inadequate residual protection/transition despite a materially available alternative.

Not found:
- intentional killing;
- sole cause of Lin/outer deaths;
- original author or sole historical Blood Admiral;
- unlawful creation of the initial office;
- proof that D4 had no real benefit.

Final sanctions:
- total 14 months liberty restriction/inquiry detention, with credit for time already served;
- 12-year ban on executive route command;
- permanent ban on unilateral high-level denial/master-key authority;
- 15-year personal reparations contribution under ability and victim-fund rules;
- no automatic office in the future route federation;
- later nonexecutive commissioner/oversight eligibility only through explicit affected/victim-region approval and an ordinary current mandate.

Separate institutional/civil findings:
- beneficiary-region, office, model/service, credential/implementation and residual-mission actors contribute to compensation/reform according to proven responsibility and benefit;
- Lin’s household and other victims retain individual claims and appeals;
- predictive modeling remains lawful under the new controls.

Haren’s response:
- accepts the factual responsibility and sentence while preserving legal disagreement over the 48-hour necessity finding;
- does not claim martyrdom, innocence by benefit or sole guilt for the system.

Reward:
- personal and institutional responsibility are both real without a one-villain history.

Cost:
- verdict intensifies regional fracture and makes the coming federation design politically harder.

Final hook:
- E784 begins a route-federation charter designed to coordinate mutual obligations without recreating Haren’s unilateral D4 office.
