## Episode 525 — 버려진 전선의 사령관

Date:
- CY 744-07-17.

Front-stage actor:
- `아셀 바론 / Asel Varon`;
- code `OFC-001`;
- commander of the Outer Front Eighth Screen and locally recognized emergency defense administration.

Current Outer Front force:
- 42 combat ships;
- 34 support/converted ships;
- 26 combat ships R2 or better;
- approximately 310 frames/craft;
- not Compact/Charter property.

Current population/service claim:
- approximately 620 million people depend directly on the protected front systems/routes;
- local civil authorities, worker networks and defense councils recognize Asel’s current mission command but not a permanent sovereign title.

Visible goal:
- respond to Asel’s demand for relief, supplies and recognition without transferring Outer Front sovereignty or subordinating the Charter.

Asel’s request:
- 9,000 t missiles, countermeasures, parts and medical support;
- eight combat ships and six support/rescue ships for 14 days;
- recognition of current local operational command inside V09 approaches;
- no Crown, Dor, Rian or Charter claim to permanent command/title.

Asel’s offer:
- fourteen-day tactical cooperation;
- shared threat/evidence map;
- protection of V08/V10 approaches during the agreed mission;
- local repair/refuel and civilian-route access;
- no automatic later Charter entry.

Conflict:
- Vesper cannot provide the full request after recalls, tender damage and Crown mission losses;
- Ardo sees a credible military partner but wants common battle succession;
- Sira supports local standing but demands reciprocal burden;
- Rian’s future memory labels one Outer formation as a later rebel fleet, but current evidence does not establish Asel’s identity or motive.

Decisive choice:
- accept fact-finding and negotiate a smaller mission package rather than recognize or reject Asel by future label.

Immediate outcome:
- no ships or supplies transfer yet;
- shared physical/threat data begins;
- Asel permits one Neutral/Charter survey team under local escort;
- Rian is not named overall commander.

Reward:
- Outer Front cooperation becomes a real option.

Cost:
- Asel publicly states that Vesper protected Crown Passage while the Outer Front lost patrol coverage; local opinion turns against Core/Charter priorities.

Final hook:
- survey data shows the Outer Front threat and Orpheus Approach civilian movement are already beginning to converge.
