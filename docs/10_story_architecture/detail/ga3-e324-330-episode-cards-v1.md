# Episode 325 — 네 개의 아르디스

Date:
- CY 743-09-22.

POV:
- Civic, Foundry, Transit, Custodian, Defense, Garden/medical, Helix and Imperial participants.

Visible goal:
- compare four real post-siege structures by services, rights, money, defense and route capacity.

Hidden pressure:
- every proposal solves a real weakness and creates a different monopoly.

## Scene units

1. **Imperial administration** — recognized pay, defense, route and succession chain; cost is emergency-governor/node/denial authority and provincial dependence.
2. **Helix receivership/service trust** — certified maintenance, debt restructuring and faster repair; cost is collateral/management/data and service-priority control.
3. **Federalist denial federation** — local armed/technical custody and capture resistance; cost is uneven service, weak external recognition and district destruction risk.
4. **Ardis Node Joint Trust** — split operation/service/labor/defense/evidence authority for a fixed term; cost is slower decisions, incomplete funding and recurring coordination.

Decisive choice:
- no proposal is dismissed as villainous or selected by one public mood; each affected body must ratify or reject by current domain.

Reward:
- governance choice becomes a concrete service/authority comparison.

Cost:
- full-command, receivership and Federalist blocs organize credible opposition rather than disappearing.

Hook:
- the Joint Trust draft still lacks exact thresholds for routine windows, emergency stop, denial and external participation.

# Episode 326 — 스물네 달짜리 신탁

Date:
- CY 743-09-23.

POV:
- legal/technical/service drafters and affected-domain contacts.

Visible goal:
- write a 24-month Ardis Node Joint Trust that can operate without pretending to be a permanent constitution.

Hidden pressure:
- vague plural authority recreates delay; rigid thresholds recreate one key through procedure.

## Scene units

1. **Term/review** — 24 months; mandatory review every 6 months; immediate review after mass casualty, route monopoly attempt or authority breach; no automatic renewal.
2. **Eight fields** — physical node operation; traffic/schedule; technical stop/maintenance; affected service/user; labor/production; defense safety; public evidence/audit; external certification/recognition for participating assets.
3. **Function thresholds** — routine window requires physical + traffic + technical + affected-service concurrence and public evidence; any physical/technical/medical stop can halt; denial requires physical + defense + affected service + technical + evidence, with no external/protagonist unilateral activation.
4. **Funding/claims** — local service revenue, public budget, bounded creditor/Helix contracts, external/Imperial support and claim reserves remain separated; T-19, Toma, ARC-17, Miren and worker claims cannot be converted into ownership shares.

Decisive choice:
- create function-specific thresholds and stops rather than one council vote or master key.

Reward:
- routine operation, emergency halt, denial and external participation have executable current rules.

Cost:
- no actor controls all information or resources; funding and urgent command remain slower than a centralized administration.

Hook:
- Imperial and local supporters propose Rian as first Trust governor/neutral master-key holder to solve the coordination problem.

Cost:

Relationship state:

Hook:

Reward:

Cost:
