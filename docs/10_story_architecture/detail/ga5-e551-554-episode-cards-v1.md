# Episode 551 — 예비대가 향한 곳

Date:
- CY 744-09-15.

Goal:
- release the held reserve between Vesper relay defense and Civilian Chain.

Current evidence:
- Vesper relay threat is serious but local Ardo formation retains 7 R2+ combat hulls and fixed defense;
- V10 has 46,200 people, weak point defense, 0.7 major-action ammunition and no alternate route within 21 hours.

Decision:
- affected-service threshold and physical route data send the held 1 combat +2 support reserve to V10;
- Vesper receives only the already dispatched 1 combat +2 support.

Authority:
- decision made by current mission evidence, affected-service and reserve rules;
- Rian supplies route timing but does not decide alone.

Reward:
- V10 gains late reinforcement and rescue capacity.

Cost:
- Vesper remains below its requested reserve and Ardo must hold the relay with local resources.

Hook:
- Talren receives a home-threat alert that could pull Sira’s formation away.

# Episode 552 — 시라 케온이 추격하지 않은 이유

Date:
- CY 744-09-18.

POV:
- Sira Keon and provincial captains.

Goal:
- decide whether to pursue a hostile scout group or protect a food/guardianship convoy through V07.

Current protected set:
- 9 service/transport ships;
- 12,600 people;
- 3,800 t food, legal/family and medical cargo.

Decision:
- Sira rejects pursuit and uses her formation to cover the convoy and verify the home alert before recall.

Outcome:
- all 12,600 people and 3,800 t arrive;
- one provincial escort damaged 3–5 months;
- four injured, none serious;
- no death;
- scout group escapes with route observations.

Reward:
- a second autonomous-front success under Charter standing intent.

Cost:
- threat reconnaissance survives; provincial critics accuse Sira of avoiding decisive combat.

Hook:
- Vesper relay jamming turns into an armed attempt to seize repair-traffic authentication.

# Episode 553 — 아르도가 지킨 중계기

Date:
- CY 744-09-21.

POV:
- Ardo Rev and Vesper formation.

Goal:
- preserve relay/repair traffic without the held reserve.

Decision:
- Ardo uses the arriving 1 combat +2 support reserve as rear rescue/repair cover and commits his own high-readiness ships to a short centralized intercept.

Outcome:
- relay remains operational at 82% current capacity;
- two hostile ships disabled, three withdraw;
- one Charter combat ship damaged 6–9 months;
- six injured, one serious;
- no death;
- 18% of Vesper campaign missile/countermeasure reserve spent.

Reward:
- Ardo’s fast local chain proves materially useful.

Cost:
- Vesper cannot send further reinforcement; repair traffic operates with delay and reduced redundancy.

Hook:
- Rian’s Grey Route front finds the route used to feed both the V10 attackers and Recovery Column remnants.

# Episode 554 — 길을 닫고 전투를 끝내지 못하다

Date:
- CY 744-09-24.

POV:
- Rian, Grey Route captains and local operators.

Goal:
- cut hostile reinforcement through V11 without closing rescue/civilian access or claiming theater command.

Decision:
- close two armed transit vectors, preserve one verified rescue/service corridor and mark physical/courier challenges for allied fronts.

Outcome:
- hostile reinforcement arrival slows 14–19 hours;
- one Charter support ship damaged 2–4 months during relay capture;
- three injured, none serious;
- no death;
- no enemy command element trapped yet.

Reward:
- campaign fronts gain time and evidence.

Cost:
- the same closure delays friendly medical/ammunition transfers by 6–11 hours.

Final hook:
- V10’s contact resolves into 12 armed ships, 26 drones and boarding craft converging on the 18-ship civilian/service group.
