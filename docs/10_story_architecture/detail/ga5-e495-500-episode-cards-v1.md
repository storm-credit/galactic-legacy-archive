# Episode 496 — 한 장부의 가격

Date:
- CY 744-05-21.

Goal:
- clear payroll/family records without giving one office permanent control of fleet identity and deployment eligibility.

Decision:
- use a multi-authority current ledger with purpose-separated fields: person/current service, earned pay, family/dependent support, injury/death claim and mission eligibility;

Result:
- 16,800 records receive full current authorization;
- 2,140 receive provisional/partial support;
- 4,080 remain appeal/manual/unfunded;
- 16,800 + 2,140 + 4,080 = 23,020.

Reward:
- 18,940 people/households gain some current support.

Cost:
- records remain slower and more expensive than one classification; fraud/duplicate and contributor debt persist.

Hook:
- ammunition loaders refuse a rushed loading order on one repaired destroyer.

Goal:

Reward:

Hook:

# Episode 499 — 먼저 살리고 나중에 묻는다

- one rescue craft damaged 2–3 months;

- three ships grounded/sealed under split custody.

Decision:

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 500 — 구조는 복종계약이 아니다

Date:
- CY 744-05-29.

Goal:
- establish preliminary rescue/parole status without completing prize/title integration early.

Current status:
- 298 rescued;
- 31 command/security personnel under individualized investigation/restriction;
- 267 protected temporary residents/crew claimants;
- no forced return and no automatic Charter enlistment;
- three hulls grounded/sealed;
- title, liens, crimes, victim claims and future use unresolved.

Preliminary protocol:
1. rescue regardless of claimant/formation when physically possible;
2. treatment and basic identity not conditioned on political oath;
3. individual risk/crime review, not collective guilt;
4. temporary housing/pay/work options;
5. evidence/privacy layers;
6. no ship becomes personal/faction prize;
7. parole/service/title require later affected/victim and legal process.

Decision:
- adopt `Rescue/Temporary Protection Protocol v0.1` and defer hull/prize/parole settlement to E503–510.

Reward:
- crews can surrender without automatic disappearance or ownership transfer.

Cost:
- 4,080 pay/family records remain unresolved; rescued-person support is additional; victims fear accountability dilution.

Final hook:
- creditors, provinces, victims and Charter formations submit incompatible claims to the three grounded hulls.
