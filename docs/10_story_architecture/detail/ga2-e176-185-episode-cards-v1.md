# Episode 176 — 작동과 안전은 다르다

Date:

POV:
- 베라/네라 through three physical inspections.

Visible goal:

Hidden pressure:

Reward:

Cost:
- no immediate common release; all three remain delayed for different reasons.

Collection state:
- relay evidence gains credibility but no certification authority.

Hook:
- A17’s replacement seal exists in regional stock, but its insurer reserves it for a higher-priority certified customer.
