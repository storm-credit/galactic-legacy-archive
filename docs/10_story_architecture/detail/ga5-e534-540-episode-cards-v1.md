# Episode 535 — 같은 비율이 공평하지 않다

Date:
- CY 744-08-07.

Goal:
- create enforceable burdens without making poor/small provinces pay identical hull or money shares.

Decision:
- contribution can be ships, support, route access, repair labor, family service, food/medical cargo or replacement credit;
- minimum obligation scales by current capacity and mission benefit;
- wealthy members cannot buy total command, weak members cannot claim zero burden.

Reward:
- four provincial groups reopen Charter negotiations.

Cost:
- valuation becomes contentious and slower; some support contributions count less visibly than combat hulls.

Hook:
- civilian/service bodies demand standing beyond consultation because they bore the Crown and Outer route costs.

# Episode 537 — 명령은 기간을 넘지 않는다

Date:
- CY 744-08-13.

Goal:
- keep mission command from becoming permanent through repeated emergency continuation.

Decision:
- every campaign names geography, objectives, accepted ships, start/end, succession, review triggers and extension procedure;
- commander succession transfers only remaining mission authority;
- one extension maximum without full resignature;
- no timeout default into permanent command.

Reward:
- Ardo accepts a stronger operational chain inside named campaigns.

Cost:
- formations can still leave or shrink at renewal; campaign planners face visible clocks.

Hook:
- ammunition and support allocation reveal the network is still too small for five fronts.

# Episode 540 — 공동전단헌장 0.2

Date:
- CY 744-08-23.

Goal:
- enact Charter v0.2 and close Act 5C without creating a permanent navy or sovereign admiralty.

Term:
- CY 744-08-23 through 10-31;
- review every fourteen days and after mass casualty, major withdrawal, mission breach or support collapse;
- one named-operation extension maximum inside the term;
- no automatic renewal after GA5.

Core additions beyond v0.1:
1. scaled enforceable contribution/burden;
2. Recall & Exit Framework with replacement/residual obligations;
3. civilian/route/worker/affected-service standing;
4. campaign duration, succession and extension rules;
5. maintenance/support pool governance;
6. minimum current pay/family and rescue/parole/title annexes;
7. independent formation command under shared intent;
8. public loss/debt audit;
9. no hull/person/title/root ownership transfer through mission acceptance.

Charter network:
- 31 combat + 27 support;
- 21 combat R2+.

Outer Front annex:
- 9 combat + 7 support for a named campaign;
- 7 combat R2+;
- Asel retains formation/local authority;
- no full Charter membership or permanent command recognition.

Rian:
- eligible for one named campaign/task command only;
- no fleet title, pay, keys, appointment or universal command.

Reward:
- Charter v0.2 becomes operational and Act 5C closes.

Cost:
- 720 pay/family cases remain;
- formations/outside bodies refuse or condition participation;
- a five-front campaign must knowingly under-resource at least one front.

Final hook:
- threat movements at V03, V06, V08, V09 and V11 force immediate campaign allocation.
