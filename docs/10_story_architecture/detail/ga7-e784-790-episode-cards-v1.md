# Episode 784 — 항로연방이 소유하지 않는 것

Date:
- CY 745-09-01 to 09-05.

Goal:
- define the provisional federation by what it cannot own.

Nonownership clauses:
- no ownership of member routes, ships, receiving systems, local physical controls or people;
- no common master route/denial key;
- no automatic command over military, Neutral or private/creditor fleets;
- no majority power over outsiders;
- no inherited D4 office or Haren succession.

Positive functions:
- common dependency facts;
- reciprocal minimum obligations;
- named cross-region mission agreements;
- contribution/repair/insurance settlement;
- affected-region/victim review;
- emergency proposal and separated authorization.

Decision:
- adopt `상호항로연방 / Mutual Route Federation` as a provisional treaty network, not a route state.

Reward:
- coordination can persist without transferring title or total authority.

Cost:
- enforcement remains dependent on member mandates, assets and current local cooperation.

Hook:
- delegates dispute whether a high-level denial can ever be fast enough without one executor.

# Episode 785 — 여덟 개의 작동필드

Date:
- CY 745-09-06 to 09-10.

Goal:
- separate ongoing route governance into fields that cannot be silently merged.

Fields:
1. dependency facts/model and source confidence;
2. local physical/technical stop;
3. medical/current-care stop and consent;
4. ship/captain/crew safety and mission acceptance;
5. military threat/escort/weapons separation;
6. receiving/service capacity and affected-user standing;
7. victim/public evidence, appeal and review;
8. finance, repair, insurance, crew/family and residual-duty resources.

Decision:
- every cross-region mission or denial identifies which fields act, which abstain and which can stop only their own domain.

Reward:
- no office or model output automatically becomes executable total policy.

Cost:
- coordination and documentation increase; fields can block or delay one another.

Hook:
- delegates design a denial ladder that preserves direct local stops while splitting multi-region power.

# Episode 786 — D1에서 D4까지

Date:
- CY 745-09-11 to 09-14.

Goal:
- govern denial by scale and effect rather than one undifferentiated emergency power.

Denial ladder:
- D1: immediate local physical/medical/ship stop, direct and current;
- D2: named single-corridor quota/inspection/service condition with notice and residual duty;
- D3: multi-corridor mission/priority plan requiring participating and affected-field approvals;
- D4: theater-scale temporary denial/priority proposal requiring separated keys, current crisis threshold, victim/affected standing, secured residual capacity, sunset and mandatory inquiry.

D4 separated roles:
- fact/model proposal;
- legal/current authorization;
- affected/beneficiary and receiving conditions;
- military/ship/physical implementation acceptance;
- independent review/expiry.

Decision:
- no one person may propose, authorize, execute and review D4.

Reward:
- Haren’s single-executor structure cannot recur unchanged.

Cost:
- less than two-hour crises may outrun the full D4 process.

Hook:
- members demand an emergency short bridge for sub-two-hour threats.

# Episode 787 — 여섯 시간만 유효한 다리

Date:
- CY 745-09-15 to 09-18.

Goal:
- create a fast emergency bridge without restoring a hidden master key.

Bridge rule:
- maximum six hours;
- only current physical/medical safety, named humanitarian/service preservation and military deconfliction;
- requires three separated current keys from fact/model, affected/receiving and implementation domains;
- cannot create broad manifest ranking, political recognition, permanent route reassignment or follow-on denial;
- automatic expiry and public/protected log;
- any continuation requires D2/D3/D4 process.

Decision:
- accept the six-hour bridge as a narrow response tool.

Reward:
- immediate cascades can be interrupted before full procedure.

Cost:
- bridge may preserve the wrong temporary configuration and consume residual resources.

Hook:
- residual missions need protected capacity before any D3/D4 can be approved.

# Episode 788 — 구호가 명령보다 약하지 않게

Date:
- CY 745-09-19 to 09-22.

Goal:
- correct the D4 asymmetry between directly executable denial and weak conditional relief.

Rules:
- proposed D3/D4 must identify residual missions, cargo, escort, receiving and current stop conditions before approval;
- secured minimum resource/mission capacity is reserved where physically possible;
- if residual capacity falls below the published threshold, denial narrows, pauses or triggers immediate review;
- local micro-routes and current care/contact cannot be silently erased;
- impossible residual promises must be disclosed, not used as moral decoration.

Decision:
- create a shared residual-duty reserve financed by member, beneficiary, insurer/creditor and recovery contributions.

Reward:
- relief obligations gain real ships, cargo and money.

Cost:
- route capacity is withheld from ordinary commerce and even beneficiary systems before a crisis.

Hook:
- victim and affected-region bodies demand authority over review without a permanent veto over all emergency action.

# Episode 789 — 피해자의 자리는 거부권이 아니다

Date:
- CY 745-09-23 to 09-26.

Goal:
- define victim/affected standing that is meaningful but not a universal hereditary veto.

Standing:
- current evidence and omission correction;
- notice, counsel, protected/public records and appeal;
- review-seat participation and post-use claims;
- ability to block false use of their identity/consent or noncurrent data;
- no automatic power to command unrelated routes or permanently prohibit every denial.

Decision:
- establish rotating affected-region/victim review panels selected per operation and by ordinary mandate.

Haren eligibility:
- excluded from executive roles under sentence;
- future nonexecutive oversight remains possible only after liberty term and through explicit affected/victim-region approval;
- no current seat is granted.

Reward:
- affected people become decision participants without creating a new unaccountable class.

Cost:
- representation disputes and unequal organizational capacity remain.

Hook:
- E790 asks the 23 bodies to provisionally ratify the charter before a real corridor crisis tests it.

# Episode 790 — 아직 완성되지 않은 연방

Date:
- CY 745-09-27.

Goal:
- provisionally launch the Mutual Route Federation without claiming final GA7 settlement.

Participation among 23 bodies:
- 11 full provisional ratifiers;
- 6 function-specific adherents;
- 2 observers;
- 4 outside/reject;
- total 23.

Term:
- six-month provisional charter;
- review after 30 and 90 days;
- no automatic renewal or compulsory accession.

Current route map, 48 corridors:
- 15 open/limited;
- 19 conditional/quota/inspection;
- 9 closed;
- 5 contested/unknown;
- total 48.

Provisional institutions:
- dependency fact service;
- mission/obligation clearing;
- residual-duty reserve;
- separated D1–D4 process;
- affected/victim review and evidence;
- finance/repair/insurance settlement;
- no executive route sovereign.

Reward:
- first durable coordination structure exists without a master key.

Cost:
- four outside bodies and several military/private/Neutral actors remain outside full authority; the charter is untested under a live high-speed crisis.

Final hook:
- a two-region relay failure and armed convoy collision warning arrives, forcing the six-hour bridge and D2/D3 processes to operate before the ink dries.
