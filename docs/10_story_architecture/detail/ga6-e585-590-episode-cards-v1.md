# Episode 585 — A는 사람을 확인하고 열을 잃는다

Date:
- CY 744-11-11.

Goal:
- test State-certified Trunk A dependencies without opening a passenger window.

Test:
- two empty large-hull geometry passes;
- authentication/receiving handshake;
- medical equipment mass simulation;
- thermal return measurement.

Finding:
- physical geometry is stronger than other routes;
- current authentication/reception confidence fails on mixed/disputed identities and split institutional packages;
- repeated large-hull alignment consumes thermal margin faster than public models assume.

Decision:
- keep A in dry-test status and require current destination/identity and thermal recovery before live certification.

Reward:
- State claims a credible future route but not exclusive first priority.

Cost:
- one large State ship must change loading design; projected capacity remains unknown.

Hook:
- Distributed B accepts more craft but exposes destination and traffic-separation failures.

# Episode 586 — B는 길이 많아서 부딪힌다

Date:
- CY 744-11-12.

Goal:
- test civilian/Neutral distributed routing and independent branch limits.

Test:
- 18 small/medium craft equivalents across three simulated branch paths;
- mixed destination and provisional-identity packets.

Finding:
- branch flexibility improves identity/current-household acceptance;
- two destinations reject medical/dependency loads;
- traffic separation fails when independent captains use different safety margins.

Decision:
- require branch-specific captain covenants, destination proof and shared collision timing while preserving refusal.

Reward:
- B remains viable for mixed smaller craft.

Cost:
- no common passenger-equivalent capacity can be claimed yet; independent limits reduce headline throughput.

Hook:
- Convergence C test requires temporary reduction of shelter power and defense readiness.

# Episode 587 — C를 시험하는 동안 꺼지는 불

Date:
- CY 744-11-13.

Goal:
- test whether C can synchronize node, route, traffic and defense without opening a live window.

Required temporary reductions:
- 8% shelter/shipboard noncritical power;
- one defense sensor sector to standby;
- halt noncritical State data replication;
- worker/thermal teams on emergency shifts.

Finding:
- C can form a short reference convergence but loses stability after 17 minutes;
- heat-sink and traffic timing, not raw route math, are primary opening constraints.

Decision:
- end the test at 17 minutes under local technical stop.

Reward:
- C’s high-capacity promise is physically credible but unearned.

Cost:
- shelter temperatures rise, defense classification weakens and workers accrue fatigue; no passenger capacity certified.

Hook:
- Grey-route survey teams report a passable segment but uncertain destination and relay ownership.

# Episode 588 — 회색길의 끝에는 누가 있는가

Date:
- CY 744-11-14.

Goal:
- survey Grey/local routes without presenting them as free overflow.

Finding:
- several small-craft segments are physically usable;
- large State/habitat ships cannot pass;
- endpoints can receive only limited people and lack reliable medical, identity, food and insurance support;
- some relay owners demand local service or defense guarantees.

Decision:
- keep Grey as a current small-craft/local/late-rescue possibility with endpoint-by-endpoint consent and no central booking promise.

Reward:
- disputed/unregistered and local craft retain a noncentral path.

Cost:
- dispersal, disappearance, exploitation and later service failure risks become explicit.

Hook:
- an unauthorized shelter intake uses preliminary route projections to bring thousands more people into a weak local module.

# Episode 589 — 숫자에 없는 두 사람

Date:
- CY 744-11-14 to 11-15.

Goal:
- rescue and account for people after an overloaded shelter/power transfer fails.

Event:
- unauthorized intake and contradictory capacity messages add approximately 6,800 people to a temporary module designed for 4,600 current occupants;
- a power coupler/fire/pressure event occurs during alternating trunk load.

Outcome:
- 2 confirmed deaths;
- 37 injured, 8 serious;
- all remaining current occupants accounted;
- module unavailable 3–5 months;
- 4,900 people displaced again to ships/other shelters;
- no route structure destroyed.

Causality:
- unauthorized organizers, false/old capacity claims, local admission under pressure, outside demand and weak infrastructure all contribute;
- no single population category is blamed.

Decision:
- publish the incident before revising all shelter capacities downward/current.

Reward:
- local/current capacity becomes auditable.

Cost:
- permanent deaths/injuries, reduced shelter and deeper local resentment.

Hook:
- constituencies agree facts must be governed by a formal board with source, uncertainty and correction rules.

# Episode 590 — 사실만 결정하는 위원회

Date:
- CY 744-11-15.

Goal:
- constitute the Orpheus Common Fact Board without giving it priority or manifest authority.

Fields:
1. physical person/current-location counts;
2. ship/readiness/capacity;
3. route/thermal/traffic state;
4. destination/treatment/function dependency;
5. local shelter/service cost;
6. source, uncertainty, appeal and correction;
7. public release and protected personal layers.

Members/standing:
- State/Compact;
- civilian convoy councils;
- institutional distribution;
- Neutral/merchant;
- local node/workers;
- holdout/stay;
- medical/current-status and evidence actors.

Limits:
- no priority ranking;
- no manifest assignment;
- no route opening;
- no command or Authority G override.

Reward:
- one reproducible fact process exists.

Cost:
- facts remain contested, slower and politically explosive; no one can promise evacuation.

Final hook:
- the board’s first consolidated pass shows the physical population is already above two million and still rising.
