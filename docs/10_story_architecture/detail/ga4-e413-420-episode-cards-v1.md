# Episode 413 — 자정에 끝난 협약

Date:
- CY 744-02-02.

POV:
- twenty-four bodies, current service users/operators and claimant offices.

Visible goal:
- terminate the pilot cleanly and identify which missions, tokens, budgets and responsibilities remain.

Hidden pressure:
- letting useful functions continue without new authorization can quietly extend the compact; shutting everything down harms current people.

## Scene units

1. **Expiry ledger** — pilot-wide reviews, facilitator schedule, participation status and cross-body defaults end; current missions already underway close under their own terms.
2. **Surviving authority** — original Registry, civil, military and Continuity protocols remain; direct physical/service/evidence authority returns to current local/mission law unless separately renewed.
3. **Urgent-service protection** — medicine, earned pay, rescue, current care and existing identity records do not vanish solely because the pilot ended.
4. **No automatic membership** — each body files a new post-pilot arrangement or explicitly defers.

Decisive choice:
- end the pilot as promised while preserving only separately authorized current services and missions.

Reward:
- temporary authority does not become permanent through useful inertia.

Cost:
- common review, interoperability and withdrawal rules fragment overnight.

Hook:
- the twenty-four filings produce five different legal arrangements, not one revised compact.

Hook:

POV:

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 416 — 헌법이 아니라 임무등록부

Date:
- CY 744-02-05.

POV:
- eight multi-field bodies, local operators/users, public evidence officers and claimant functions.

Visible goal:
- preserve tested mission coordination without pretending the pilot became a constitution.

Hidden pressure:
- a mission registry can slowly become a de facto central compact through repeated templates and shared staff.

## Scene units

1. **Registry scope** — each mission names assets, seven current fields, alternates, stops, budgets, evidence, withdrawal and expiry.
2. **No standing government** — no common chair, budget, police, treaty or permanent field appointments; templates are optional and mission-bounded.
3. **Capture controls** — shared staff cannot issue orders; every template change is public and participating bodies retain current confirmation.
4. **First entries** — four care/service, two route, one defense/rescue and one evidence mission register under separate terms.

Decisive choice:
- use a common mission registry as infrastructure, not as a sovereign or permanent compact.

Reward:
- eight bodies retain interoperable seven-field practice for real work.

Cost:
- repeated mission negotiation, staffing and audit remain expensive; nonparticipants are not covered.

Hook:
- seven function-specific bodies need to move one care/repair convoy through three different arrangements without the expired pilot’s common rules.

# Episode 417 — 기능만 이어 붙인 구호선

Date:
- CY 744-02-06.

POV:
- function-specific bodies, vessel masters, local physical/service actors and current users.

Visible goal:
- move six service ships, 280 t and 52 people through a civil/Registry zone, a Continuity-only zone and an independent physical/security zone.

Hidden pressure:
- no body accepts all seven fields or one common review/withdrawal rule.

## Scene units

1. **Functions** — Elian manifests and Mara payment cover first zone; Isa service authentication covers second; local physical/security/evidence cover the independent zone.
2. **Interoperability gap** — one care group’s current proof and one repair pallet lack a common appeal/claim path.
3. **Mission accord** — vessel masters and affected-care users gain direct current stop/appeal for this movement only; no permanent field adoption.
4. **Outcome** — all six ships and 52 people complete after 11 hours; 278.2 t arrives for intended use, 1.8 t cold-chain cargo is downgraded and released for narrower use; no death or injury.

Decisive choice:
- create one bounded mission accord rather than force jurisdictions into full compact participation.

Reward:
- function-specific arrangements can cooperate without accession.

Cost:
- 11-hour delay, 1.8 t downgrade, duplicate fees and no reusable common legal remedy outside the mission.

Hook:
- one independent body refuses the next route entirely while a deferred body requests urgent-service access without joining any arrangement.
