# Episode 356 — 동의하지 않을 권리의 범위

Date:
- CY 743-11-15.

POV:
- founding-covenant counsel, provincial bodies, protected-service users, claimant teams and hearing officers.

Visible goal:
- decide whether provinces and affected people may block an otherwise valid succession remedy.

Hidden pressure:
- unlimited blocking can paralyze wages, medicine and defense; no blocking lets central offices transfer local people, assets and services without consent.

## Scene units

1. **Three categories** — immediate life/service continuity; temporary administrative/defense operation; permanent accession, asset, person, key or local-custody transfer.
2. **Affected-body proof** — provinces and service/user bodies show current physical, economic and care costs from each remedy; they do not claim one universal veto over the empire.
3. **Claimant objections** — civil/military/Continuity teams argue that urgent systems need predictable authority; Elian’s Registry team accepts local person/record challenge but rejects province-by-province dynastic nullification.
4. **First scope ruling** — affected bodies cannot block bounded medicine, payroll, rescue or ceasefire solely as accession leverage; they may condition or block permanent local asset/person/service-custody transfer and must receive evidence, appeal and nonretaliation.

Decisive choice:
- recognize domain-specific affected-body blocking and ratification rather than universal veto or total central transfer.

Reward:
- permanent remedies cannot silently absorb local people/services; urgent care and safety remain operable.

Cost:
- every remedy now carries local conditions, documentation and possible delay; central claimant blocs organize to narrow the ruling.

Hook:
- the hearing turns from who may object to which event legally activated each office.

# Episode 357 — 죽음보다 먼저 시작된 섭정

Date:
- CY 743-11-17.

POV:
- medical/Registry experts, Mara and Elian teams, Tera’s advocate and evidence panel.

Visible goal:
- separate biological event, written entry, receipt and legally sufficient notice.

Hidden pressure:
- a single precise timeline would simplify succession but the records were created by different clocks, people and office rules.

## Scene units

1. **Medical event band** — irreversible incapacity is medically certified before the Registry death/receipt entry; exact biological transition remains a bounded range rather than one perfect second.
2. **Regency trigger** — Mara’s protocol activates on certified incapacity for civil continuity; it does not require later death receipt and is valid before heir recognition.
3. **Heir trigger** — Elian’s Registered-Heir protocol activates on death plus Registry receipt/recognition; it does not retroactively erase valid incapacity-period civil action.
4. **No crown ruling** — the panel admits a sequential overlap: Regency civil authority can begin first and Registered-Heir continuity can begin later without either becoming total sovereign automatically.

Decisive choice:
- recognize distinct event/notice triggers and reject retrospective invalidation of services performed under the earlier valid state.

Reward:
- payroll, care, Registry and family acts already performed remain legally defensible.

Cost:
- two authentic central authorities now coexist by time and function; hardliners accuse the hearing of inventing shared sovereignty.

Hook:
- Dor and Isa argue that the later conflict acknowledgment triggered emergency powers that neither Regency nor heir can simply override.

# Episode 358 — 충돌을 알게 된 순간

Date:
- CY 743-11-19.

POV:
- Dor/Isa teams, General Staff/Continuity operators, provincial defense and service observers.

Visible goal:
- determine when military freeze and root-key suspension became valid and how long they may remain.

Hidden pressure:
- “conflict known” can justify indefinite military/technical emergency rule if no termination condition exists.

## Scene units

1. **Conflict acknowledgment** — the third write event proves authorized operators knew two valid succession states conflicted before some downstream orders propagated.
2. **Protector trigger** — Dor’s freeze becomes valid for armed movement, ceasefire and defense recognition when contradictory command creates current force risk; it does not gain civil/evidence control.
3. **Continuity trigger** — Isa’s suspension becomes valid when disputed succession could transfer root/command/service keys; bounded service modules remain separate from root ownership.
4. **Duration conditions** — both emergency functions require periodic factual review, public/affected-domain evidence and termination or narrowing when the triggering risk ends.

Decisive choice:
- validate Protector and Continuity emergency functions from the conflict acknowledgment while rejecting permanent emergency supremacy.

Reward:
- ceasefires, root protection and cross-code service performed after the conflict remain valid.

Cost:
- the state now has four valid authority clocks and no single office can terminate every other one.

Hook:
- hearing officers must design a remedy that keeps all four functions running without creating a hidden fifth sovereign.

POV:

Visible goal:

Hidden pressure:

1. **Four fields** — Elian: Registry/ceremonial provenance; Mara: civil administration; Dor: military ceasefire/defense; Isa: root suspension/service continuity.

Decisive choice:
- choose functional coexistence and visible deadlock over a single emergency sovereign.

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

POV:

Visible goal:

Cost:
