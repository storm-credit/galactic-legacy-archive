# Episode 603 — 사십구만 육천 명의 국가기능

Date:
- CY 744-11-23.

Goal:
- reconcile the six packets without double counting and distinguish function-linked people from ordinary State-associated persons.

Reconciled minimum viable function-linked people:
- core staff/patients/operators: 312,000;
- mandatory caregivers/dependents/current maintainers: 184,000;
- total: **496,000**.

Packet totals before overlap correction exceed 496,000 because some people/equipment support multiple functions.

Decision:
- use 496,000 as a dependency-linked planning population, not an automatic priority block;
- all other State-associated people remain ordinary current persons with equal claim to person-based guarantees.

Reward:
- State continuity becomes decomposable and numerically honest.

Cost:
- no route or destination can receive all six packets intact; institutional leaders and ordinary dependents now compete inside the same scarcity.

Final hook:
- civilian/community manifests must now prove destination, household, care and long-term viability under the same standards.
