# Episode 571 — 새 헌장의 첫 시간

Date:
- CY 744-11-01.

Goal:
- establish a common Orpheus clock and current command boundaries before the first large arrival wave.

Conflict:
- State/Compact, Charter, provincial, Crown, Outer, Neutral and local node chains all issue valid but incompatible arrival/berthing instructions.

Decision:
- adopt Orpheus coordinated traffic time and temporary berth/safety rules;
- no priority, manifest or theater command assigned.

Reward:
- ships share one clock and collision-avoidance language.

Cost:
- twelve ships remain in holding patterns; two hospitals delay planned docking.

Hook:
- population dashboards differ by more than 180,000 people before any new convoy arrives.

# Episode 572 — 숫자보다 많은 사람

Date:
- CY 744-11-02.

Goal:
- explain why current population lists cannot simply be added.

Finding:
- people appear as passenger, worker, dependent, patient, crew, local resident or future booking in multiple systems;
- some unregistered people appear only in food, oxygen or school records.

Decision:
- publish three physical groups and a separate conflict/overlap ledger rather than one false exact total.

Reward:
- opening physical baseline 1.26m becomes usable.

Cost:
- no faction can claim all its listed people are additional priority seats; public distrust rises.

Hook:
- State escort requests immediate priority berths for advanced hospital and payroll functions.

# Episode 573 — 국가가 먼저 도착해야 하는 이유

Date:
- CY 744-11-03.

POV:
- Ardo Rev and State/Compact convoy staff.

Goal:
- secure berths and protected power for central hospital, payroll, identity and command ships.

Ardo’s case:
- losing operational teams can cause deaths across multiple regions, not only among passengers present.

Decision:
- grant minimum current hospital/power berths after dependency proof, but reject blanket State-first approach lanes.

Reward:
- critical State functions begin safe intake.

Cost:
- civilian ships wait longer; Ardo records that fragmented berthing may break team integrity.

Hook:
- civilian convoy captains reject being represented by one queue or one coordinator.

# Episode 574 — 난민선단은 하나가 아니다

Date:
- CY 744-11-03 to 11-04.

POV:
- Nada Soren and civilian captains.

Goal:
- create civilian coordination without erasing ship/community refusal.

Decision:
- form five convoy councils by life-support, destination, mobility, cargo/service and current risk;
- Nada coordinates information and joint demands, not tactical command.

Reward:
- organized convoys can communicate and expose missing/isolated groups.

Cost:
- documented/organized ships gain faster visibility than isolated craft; internal civilian conflict becomes public.

Hook:
- local node workers stop an outside power connection that both State and civilian ships need.

# Episode 575 — 문을 여는 사람의 고향

Date:
- CY 744-11-04.

POV:
- Deon Har, Muren Kas and local workers.

Goal:
- determine whether the physical stop is protectionism or valid node safety.

Finding:
- outside fleets already consume 18% of emergency energy and 23% of certified dock/repair shifts;
- connecting another high-load intake line risks a thermal cascade with one heat-sink field offline.

Decision:
- Deon maintains the stop and authorizes only a 36% bounded intake through alternating trunks.

Reward:
- thermal cascade avoided and current shelters remain powered.

Cost:
- approximately 16,000 arrivals remain in exposed shipboard holding for 9–17 additional hours.

Hook:
- Neutral rescue ships announce they will refuse boarding beyond current covenant capacity.

# Episode 576 — 구조선이 거절하는 사람들

Date:
- CY 744-11-05.

POV:
- Ora Niv and member captains.

Goal:
- publish real Neutral medical/rescue capacity and refusal conditions.

Current capacity:
- 14 major Neutral/merchant vessels and 28 small craft physically available for immediate rescue/service;
- approximately 42,000 temporary passenger/medical equivalents under severe limits;
- member captains retain final safety/refusal authority.

Decision:
- Ora publishes ship-level capacity, disarmament, medical and destination limits and refuses political overbooking.

Reward:
- no heroic false promise or hidden reserved capacity.

Cost:
- 11,000 urgent applicants remain outside Neutral commitments; factions accuse Ora of monetized neutrality.

Hook:
- holdout/stay groups demand a seat before planners treat them as future leftovers.

# Episode 577 — 남겨질 사람이라는 이름

Date:
- CY 744-11-05.

POV:
- Lea Nor and local/convoy shelter groups.

Goal:
- establish standing for people unable or unwilling to transit.

Current groups:
- medically nontransportable;
- local workers needed to operate windows;
- residents refusing abandonment;
- people with destination, guardianship or identity reasons to stay;
- people likely forced to remain by scarcity.

Decision:
- create a Holdout/Stay seat and require supplies, shelter, defense, surrender/contact and later claims in every plan.

Reward:
- staying is treated as a current path with agency and coercion risks.

Cost:
- every transit plan loses cargo, escort or labor capacity to holdout support.

Final hook:
- the six constituencies agree to a Common Fact Board but refuse to give it manifest or priority authority.

## End State E577

- common clock and physical baseline active;
- State, civilian, Neutral, local and holdout positions visible;
- Common Fact Board authorized for facts only;
- no final population, manifest, window capacity or plan.
