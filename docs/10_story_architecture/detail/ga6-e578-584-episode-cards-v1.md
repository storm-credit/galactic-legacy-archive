# Episode 578 — 기관을 한 배에 싣지 않는다

Date:
- CY 744-11-06.

POV:
- Karin Ves and State institution teams.

Goal:
- determine which State functions require intact co-location and which can be decomposed.

Function examples:
- hospital specialty teams and equipment;
- payroll/identity records and current signers;
- command/communications;
- courts/legal custody;
- archive/education/technical maintenance.

Decision:
- create minimum viable function-packet candidates with staff, equipment, data, destination and replacement dependencies;
- reject the assumption that moving headcount preserves an institution.

Reward:
- future manifest can compare functions instead of labels.

Cost:
- departments lose seniority-based seats and intact hierarchy; some packages prove too large for one route.

Hook:
- one advanced hospital packet depends on caregivers and unregistered family members outside the State manifest.

# Episode 579 — 가족은 행정단위가 아니다

Date:
- CY 744-11-07.

POV:
- Tiren O, aunt and registered parent.

Goal:
- resolve a guardianship/movement conflict without making the child either State property or convoy property.

Conflict:
- registered parent has a State-function destination with better long-term medical/education service;
- aunt and current household are aboard a civilian habitat convoy with stronger present attachment;
- both routes are plausible and risky.

Decision:
- record Tiren’s preference to remain with the aunt while retaining parent contact/legal recognition;
- movement authority and guardianship/contact authority are separated.

Reward:
- child preference and current household enter the facts.

Cost:
- neither adult gets a complete legal victory; future destination and school continuity remain unresolved.

Hook:
- cargo captains reject a passenger-maximizing proposal that would discard agricultural cultures and tools.

# Episode 580 — 사람 대신 씨앗을 싣는 배

Date:
- CY 744-11-08.

POV:
- Soa Rin and civilian cargo crews.

Goal:
- decide how much cargo can be removed to increase passenger capacity.

Current ship group:
- four civilian cargo/passenger vessels;
- 11,800 current passengers/crew/dependents;
- 9,400 t agricultural cultures, tools, water/food processing and family property;
- passenger-max plan would discard 6,100 t and add approximately 18,000 short-range passenger equivalents across the group.

Decision:
- preserve 4,800 t of strategic agricultural/service cargo, release/redistribute 4,600 t and accept fewer seats.

Reward:
- receiving regions retain a credible food/work startup package.

Cost:
- 9,000–13,000 additional passengers must find other paths; civilians accuse Soa of valuing goods over people.

Hook:
- local thermal workers show that every extra shelter/ship load also consumes the node’s future window capacity.

# Episode 581 — 열이 남아야 길이 열린다

Date:
- CY 744-11-09.

POV:
- Muren Kas, Deon Har and node engineers.

Goal:
- quantify how current shelter, docking and data replication reduce later route capacity.

Finding:
- current outside load uses 18% emergency energy;
- an additional 100,000 people sheltered for 72 hours can reduce later high-capacity convergence margin by 4–7% unless heat-sink/repair work succeeds;
- noncritical data replication and armed-ship hot readiness consume comparable thermal capacity.

Decision:
- publish a current thermal budget and require every constituency to name what present service it will reduce or what repair labor/resources it contributes.

Reward:
- local cost becomes measurable rather than moral rhetoric.

Cost:
- shelter groups face rationing; military and State actors must consider reducing readiness/replication.

Hook:
- Authority G/Aurel offers to merge all current people, treatment, destination and function records to optimize the trade.

# Episode 582 — 정답을 내지 않는 통합장부

Date:
- CY 744-11-09 to 11-10.

Goal:

Offer:

- predicted destination/treatment mismatch;
- dynamic capacity model;

Hook:

# Episode 583 — 약속된 수용지는 없다

Date:
- CY 744-11-10.

Goal:
- verify destination capacity instead of counting written acceptance as safe arrival.

Initial audit:
- 47 major destination commitments reviewed;
- 11 lack current medical capacity;
- 8 have identity/entry limits inconsistent with listed passengers;
- 6 cannot accept the promised institutional equipment/mass;
- several capacities overlap the same beds/docks.

Decision:
- downgrade written promises into current verified, conditional, disputed and unavailable categories.

Reward:
- false safe-arrival assumptions are removed before loading.

Cost:
- at least 74,000 current listed people lose a previously promised destination path and reenter planning.

Hook:
- ship counts reveal that many inbound vessels exist only as future assignments or are physically incapable of the routes claimed.

# Episode 584 — 아직 오지 않은 배의 좌석

Date:
- CY 744-11-10.

Goal:
- separate present capacity from promised/inbound ship capacity.

Finding:
- public planners count 22 combat and 18 support staging notices as if already present;
- several transports are repair-, crew-, fuel- or route-limited;
- small craft cannot substitute for large-route medical/habitat capability.

Decision:
- publish present, inbound, conditional and unavailable ship columns;
- no promised seat enters current capacity.

Reward:
- E571 present OOB 25 combat/47 major support/96 small craft is preserved as physical fact.

Cost:
- current available transport appears far below public expectation; panic purchasing and unauthorized departures begin.

Final hook:
- Common Fact Board schedules separate dry tests of A, B, C and Grey dependencies without passengers.
