# Episode 122 — 계약이 바뀐 날

Date:
- CY 742-07-18, pre-departure.

POV:
- 테오.

Opening state:
- certificate issued;
- first-contract advance can be withdrawn if the package is not accepted immediately.

Visible goal:
- accept a mission package the ship can legally and physically perform.

Hidden pressure:
- the package was assembled by several institutions that each assume their own obligation is primary.

## Scene 122.1 — Contract objects

Location:
- cargo bay, infirmary and mission room through linked inspection.

Action:
- physical relay assemblies, patient equipment, sealed evidence and passenger manifests are inspected together;
- mass/space alone fits, but heat, route timing, quarantine and liability do not all fit simultaneously.

State change:
- “one contract” is exposed as four different service obligations.

## Scene 122.2 — Payment and penalty table

Base:
- relay cargo/records 130,000 BSC.

Conditional:
- tug assistance up to 90,000;
- patient/witness transport 45,000;
- evidence preservation up to 45,000.

Penalties:
- late cargo;
- manifest breach;
- evidence contamination;
- unauthorized acceleration or route deviation;
- preventable medical harm;
- offensive escalation.

Action:
- 테오 shows that full success yields only 42,000–71,000 BSC usable surplus after mandatory distributions.

## Scene 122.3 — Passenger consent and destination

Actors:
- patients/caregivers choose treatment destinations and risk conditions;
- appeal witnesses insist on remaining with sealed evidence;
- inspectors retain authority to stop certificate-invalid action.

Conflict:
- one patient’s condition may not tolerate the fastest route profile;
- moving evidence separately would reduce medical risk but weaken the appeal.

## Scene 122.4 — Clause-by-clause acceptance

Decisive choice:
- crew accepts the relay, patient/witness and bounded tug-search obligations;
- rejects automatic hull salvage, forced passenger transfer and unrestricted pursuit clauses.

Immediate result:
- mission becomes payable but cannot achieve every optional reward through one action.

Reward:
- 150,000 BSC advance activates repairs, wages and departure.

Cost:
- repayment/penalty exposure begins;
- inspectors and witnesses physically limit ship freedom.

Collection state:
- G2-S01 operational hull first external use begins;
- G2-S09 temporary medical/passenger configuration: `T → C`;
- route/claims relationships activated.

Relationship state:
- 테오 gains authority by refusing apparently profitable clauses;
- patients/witnesses remain decision-makers, not cargo.

Carried state:
- exact manifest and route threshold locked;
- departure must occur before a queue window closes.

Final hook:
- while the last cargo seal is verified, the overdue KT-441 beacon returns for eleven seconds.

Specialist panel:
- P02/P03, M05, H05/T03, C02/X03, N04/X04.
