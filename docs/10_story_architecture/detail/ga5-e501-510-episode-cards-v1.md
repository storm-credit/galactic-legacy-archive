# Episode 504 — 피해자의 배

Date:
- CY 744-06-06.

Goal:
- determine whether victim claims permit permanent seizure or destruction of the hulls.

Decision:
- victims receive evidence, restitution priority and a bar on immediate armed reuse;
- they do not automatically receive title or collective punishment authority.

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:
- the `저녁의 담보` province offers to accept crew review and restitution escrow if the hull returns.

# Episode 505 — 돌려보내는 함선

Date:
- CY 744-06-08.

Goal:
- return `저녁의 담보` without forced crew return, evidence loss or victim-right erasure.

Decision:
- return hull title/physical custody after weapons seal, evidence mirror, 12-month no-offensive-use condition and restitution escrow;
- people choose separately.

Outcome:
- 52 people across the three rescued cohorts choose supervised return/transfer, including 39 associated with this hull;
- remaining persons are not transferred with the ship;
- hull exits Charter inventory and cannot count as fleet growth.

Reward:
- title law is honored without treating crew as cargo.

Cost:
- Charter loses a potentially useful combat hull; province disputes the no-offensive-use condition.

Hook:
- `빈 깃발` has no uncontested owner capable of repair, crew support and victim security.

# Episode 506 — 백팔십 일의 빈 깃발

Date:
- CY 744-06-10.

Goal:
- create temporary title/custody for `빈 깃발` without awarding it to Rian, Dor, the Charter or rescued crew.

Decision:
- establish a 180-day Ship Title & Mission Trust;
- trustees represent title/creditor, crew/work, victims, repair/current custody and public mission/audit;
- no independent chair actuation;
- no sale, strategic-key transfer or permanent military assignment.

State:
- physical R1;
- weapons remain partly disabled/sealed;
- mission use limited to escort/rescue after repair and crew consent.

Reward:
- repair and legal work can begin under bounded custody.

Cost:
- trust carries debt, audit and withdrawal conditions; it adds no immediate combat-ready ship.

Hook:
- `늦은 구조` is too damaged for economical armed restoration.

Date:

- Rescue/Parole Protocol v0.2;
- Ship Title & Mission Trust rule;
