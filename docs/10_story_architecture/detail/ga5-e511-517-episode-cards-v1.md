# Episode 511 — 집으로 오라는 합법적인 명령

Date:
- CY 744-06-19.

Goal:
- authenticate recall orders and determine which obligations remain.

Finding:
- all three recall chains are authentic;
- home threats differ in severity;
- Charter v0.1-R has no complete recall-cost framework.

Decision:
- freeze punitive action and require each ship/province to disclose current mission, people/cargo aboard, replacement possibilities and home emergency evidence.

Reward:
- recall is treated as lawful claim, not defection.

Cost:
- fleet planning freezes around thirteen ships; Outer/Vesper coverage uncertainty rises.

Hook:
- one province orders immediate departure without unloading shared rescue equipment.

# Episode 512 — 배가 떠나도 남는 것

Date:
- CY 744-06-21.

Goal:
- separate the right to leave from current rescue, cargo, classified equipment and dependent-person obligations.

Decision:
- ships may leave, but must complete immediate safety handover, return/replace mission-specific pooled assets and protect embarked persons’ choices.

Reward:
- no asset hostage or crew-as-cargo rule.

Cost:
- departure slows 48–72 hours; provinces accuse Charter of disguised detention.

Hook:
- crew on one recalled support ship votes/requests to remain while title and home mandate demand return.

Hook:

# Episode 514 — 소환 비용

Date:
- CY 744-06-25.

Goal:
- define obligations that make voluntary coalition meaningful without abolishing exit.

Draft framework:
- 72-hour notice when physically possible;
- current rescue/safety/cargo handover;
- return of pooled ammunition/parts or replacement debt;
- remaining casualty/family/pay obligations;
- emergency home-defense evidence;
- replacement/route/service credit;
- no forced extension beyond named catastrophic window.

Decision:
- accept costs as reciprocal obligations, not penalties for political disloyalty.

Reward:
- exits become predictable enough for planning.

Cost:
- weaker provinces struggle to provide replacement credit and view the framework as wealthy-region bias.

Hook:
- Doan Mir receives medical clearance and a seat home for the sibling guardianship hearing.

# Episode 516 — 거짓말한 지방도 지켜야 하는가

Date:
- CY 744-06-29.

Goal:
- address inflated threat evidence without declaring the entire recall illegitimate.

Finding:
- one province overstated projected attack probability but has real food/family and patrol shortages.

Decision:
- correct the record, reduce emergency priority and preserve ordinary recall/exit rights with full replacement debt.

Reward:
- fraud does not erase genuine local needs or create collective punishment.

Cost:
- trust in provincial reporting declines; Charter audit gains more intrusive data demands.

Hook:
- final ship decisions divide the thirteen recalled hulls into three timelines.
