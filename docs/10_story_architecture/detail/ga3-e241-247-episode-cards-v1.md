# Episode 241 — 자동으로 파업을 부수는 법

Date:
- CY 742-11-22, immediate continuation from E240.

POV:
- Kera Mott, Nera, Vera and Iven Kor inside Line 3.

Visible goal:
- prevent the remote purge/cooldown from damaging the line, emergency blanks and worker-safe access.

Hidden pressure:
- the fastest certified freeze command requires a Helix management token that could establish control precedent.

## Scene units

1. **Purge sequence** — hopper isolation, coolant dump, scheduler credential revocation and automatic removal of unrecognized work orders begin in a sequence meant to protect an abandoned line, not an occupied one.
2. **Two bad stops** — cutting main power risks trapped heat and blank deformation; accepting the full management token allows remote scheduling/worker credential control for ninety days.
3. **Bounded token** — Iven offers a 70-minute certified emergency-freeze token if local operators expose exact actuator state and accept Helix incident audit; Kera permits the single function but refuses management activation.
4. **Physical cutover** — local workers hand-lock two feed gates while Nera/Vera isolate a mixed controller. Eleven percent of current catalyst/blank value is lost, but the line and emergency queue survive.

Decisive choice:
- Kera and Iven jointly use a narrow certified freeze while preserving local occupation and physical stop authority.

Reward:
- Line 3 survives without a full takeover; current emergency orders remain recoverable.

Cost:
- material loss, two-day recalibration, one moderate hand injury and a legal precedent that Helix can demand incident data/payment for certified intervention.

Hook:
- the workers can keep the line, but payroll escrow has only nine days of essential-shift wages left.

# Episode 242 — 일하지 않을 권리와 멈추지 않을 의무

Date:
- CY 742-11-23.

POV:
- Kera, Haren, worker households and current service users.

Visible goal:
- define which production remains essential during the strike without turning every public need into forced labor.

Hidden pressure:
- hospitals, Garden loops, Anchor repair and defense can all claim life-protecting urgency.

## Scene units

1. **Order board** — current requests are separated into immediate life service, time-bounded prevention, strategic restoration and ordinary/creditor production.
2. **Household reality** — worker representatives expose unpaid care, transport and injury time; Haren’s first model undercounts workers absent because of dependent care.
3. **Essential band** — the strike agrees to hospital cold-chain couplings, Garden pump seals, current fire/pressure parts and Luma’s injury-support fabrication; Spine B blanks receive a diagnostic-only quota.
4. **Refusal** — defense reserve and creditor export parts are delayed despite political pressure; one hospital request is reduced because its inventory report double-counted reserved stock.

Decisive choice:
- workers accept a capped essential shift with rotating consent, paid hours and independent technical stop, not unlimited emergency duty.

Reward:
- minimum-service production restarts lawfully.

Cost:
- nonessential output, defense reserve and planned Spine work remain delayed; households still face wage uncertainty.

Hook:
- Iven publishes a payroll bridge: seventy percent of verified arrears in thirty-six hours for workers who enter the centralized 90-day service contract.

Reward:

Cost:

Reader rule:

Hook:

# Episode 244 — 하나의 파업은 없다

Date:
- CY 742-11-25.

POV:
- Kera, Tir, three shift blocs and subcontract crews.

Visible goal:
- prevent the workforce split from becoming violence or a total production shutdown.

Hidden pressure:
- each bloc controls a different physical link: operator credentials, cranes, test benches, materials or transport.

## Scene units

1. **Three blocs** — essential-only occupation, full centralized-contract workers and a mixed bloc demanding wages now but local scheduling later.
2. **Physical interdependence** — no bloc can run the whole line: certified workers lack one local jig history; occupation workers lack fresh diagnostic support; subcontract transport can stop both.
3. **Near clash** — two shifts arrive for the same crane/bench slot; Kera and Tir establish separate physical lanes and a shared emergency stop rather than competing pickets.
4. **Split governance** — one old union/guild council fractures; line-level delegates and household/service contacts replace its claim to represent everyone.

Decisive choice:
- workers preserve a shared safety and emergency-production layer while allowing different labor contracts and political positions.

Reward:
- no violent clearance and no total plant shutdown.

Cost:
- bargaining power fragments; unemployment and nonessential shortages rise; administrative burden multiplies.

Hook:
- a Surface Works oxygen-compressor seal fails, and the required blank sits in the proprietary queue scheduled six days later.

# Episode 245 — 여섯 날을 기다릴 수 없는 부품

Date:
- CY 742-11-26.

POV:
- Surface Works crew, Kera, Tir, Nera and Iven.

Visible goal:
- produce an oxygen-compressor seal within sixteen hours without invalidating the line’s safety state.

Hidden pressure:
- the local substitute can be made now with uncertain repeat life; the proprietary part is certified but late.

## Scene units

1. **Current consequence** — compressor failure cuts reserve oxygen/industrial-gas margin for approximately 38,000 Surface Works residents and workers; there is no immediate suffocation but the safety clock is real.
2. **Two production paths** — local blank and worker jig can deliver a 9–14 day provisional seal; Helix path delivers a 120-day certified seal in six days and offers temporary gas support for only part of the load.
3. **Mixed build** — Tir’s certified shift provides current material scan and Kera’s occupation shift uses the local jig; Surface operators set the acceptance/stop limit.
4. **Installation** — 07 transports the oversized part only after local pressure crews isolate the compressor. The provisional seal holds under reduced load.

Decisive choice:
- use a locally governed provisional seal now and keep the certified replacement order active.

Reward:
- current oxygen/gas reserve stabilizes and both labor blocs contribute.

Cost:
- reduced industrial output, mandatory daily inspection and payment for the later certified part; no ideological winner.

Hook:
- during recalibration, Line 3’s feed carriage moves under an authorization no current bloc admits issuing.

# Episode 246 — 파업 안의 록아웃

Date:
- CY 742-11-27.

POV:
- Kera, Tir, Iven, Vera and onsite safety teams.

Visible goal:
- stop an unauthorized feed-carriage movement before it crushes a calibration crew or destroys the mixed production state.

Hidden pressure:
- the event can justify violent clearance, full receivership or accusations of worker sabotage.

## Scene units

1. **Uncommanded movement** — a feed carriage releases from local hold while six people work inside the marked safe envelope; physical alarm and one worker stop save seconds.
2. **Causal chain** — a copied old management token, remote rollback after E241, local bypass left for emergency production and stale occupied-zone state combine; one deliberate token use is likely but not yet attributable.
3. **Rescue/containment** — Tir uses certified scheduler knowledge to freeze the downstream axis; Kera’s crew physically blocks the carriage; Vera isolates the mixed safety relay. Two workers suffer minor injuries, no death.
4. **Evidence choice** — Kera refuses to purge all remote access immediately because that would disable certified safety support; Iven accepts joint custody of the token and logs instead of demanding sole control.

Decisive choice:
- classify the event as a joint lockout/safety incident with unresolved deliberate contribution, not proof that one side must own the line.

Reward:
- plant survives, evidence is preserved and both sides’ real safety capacities remain available.

Cost:
- output stops for 31 hours; trust collapses further; remote/local access complexity becomes a public security issue.

Hook:
- Transit utility workers announce that they will not cover the Foundry outage unless their own service-standing and stop rights enter the pact.

POV:

Visible goal:

Reward:

Cost:
