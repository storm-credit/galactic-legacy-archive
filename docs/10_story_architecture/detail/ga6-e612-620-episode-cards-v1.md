# Episode 612 — 전투함은 좌석이 아니다

Date:
- CY 744-11-29.

Goal:
- define how military/escort ships and crews enter manifests without becoming passenger capacity or automatic State priority.

Decision:
- separate defense/escort, transport/service, sacrificial/repair and later withdrawal roles;
- combat crews remain operational persons unless explicitly reassigned;
- no armed hull receives passenger priority merely for power.

Reward:
- military capability and people count stop producing phantom seats.

Cost:
- some crews/operators become late/holdout-linked; Ardo warns insufficient escorts will collapse every route.

Hook:
- medical planners demand cross-route triage rules that commanders cannot override casually.

# Episode 613 — 치료순서와 이동순서

Date:
- CY 744-11-29.

Goal:
- distinguish medical urgency, transportability, destination compatibility and current consent.

Decision:
- create four independent medical fields:
  - urgent current treatment;
  - transport risk/required equipment;
  - destination specialty/bed compatibility;
  - patient/caregiver consent and continuity.

Reward:
- medical priority cannot be reduced to “sickest first” or “easiest to move.”

Cost:
- some highly urgent patients remain local because movement would kill them; some stable people move earlier to preserve later care capacity.

Hook:
- Neutral captains reject a central medical assignment that exceeds individual vessel covenants.

# Episode 619 — 같은 가정으로 다시 돌린 계획

Date:
- CY 744-12-02 to 12-03.

Goal:
- normalize selected assumptions enough to identify real disagreements.

Equalized factors:
- current 3.16m population;
- present OOB and route readiness;
- destination verification rules;
- current medical and operator requirements;
- same known hostile pressure range;
- explicit refusal/override authority.

Finding:
- S preserves more central functions in several models but moves fewer distributed civilian communities;
- C moves more people in favorable cases but increases destination/holdout/service fragility;
- L protects local/Neutral refusal and irregular paths but underuses high-capacity convergence;
- G is usually fastest/highest-capacity if broad override is accepted, with appeal/refusal and concentration risks;
- M distributes authority and provenance but is slower and does not minimize deaths in every model.

Decision:
- no winner; advance all five to military/route stress testing.

Reward:
- plan differences are no longer hidden by unequal inputs.

Cost:
- Authority G gains credible support because its benefit remains real.

Hook:
- military reconnaissance reports hostile convergence will compress the remaining preparation period.
