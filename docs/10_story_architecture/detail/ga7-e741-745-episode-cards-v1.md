# Episode 743 — 네 개의 실행안

Date:
- CY 745-04-04 to 04-06.

Goal:
- compare realistic alternatives under the same 97-minute final-decision horizon expected if the crisis triggers.

Alternatives:
1. **Distributed rotating quota** — local/affected control, slower 11-hour full setup, lower outer concentration risk.
2. **Full G/central optimization** — fastest total reallocation and likely lowest combined near-term loss in several models, requires broad override not currently granted.
3. **Independent local closures** — preserves current local authority but risks uncoordinated cascade and duplicated denial.
4. **D4 targeted denial** — one named 72-hour regional traffic-priority decision with residual missions, reviews and current physical/medical stops.

Model findings:
- no action/fragmented delay risks tens of thousands of core service deaths;
- D4 may avoid roughly 44k–73k core deaths relative to severe cascade cases while foreseeably imposing 1.9k–5.2k downstream deaths/irreversible losses if residual missions fail;
- rotating quota likely lowers downstream harm but may not be ready before core failure;
- full G often performs best numerically but lacks lawful affected/ship/local grant.

Decision:
- keep all four live; require affected-region evidence and named responsibility before any D4 office is created.

Reward:
- D4’s benefit and harm are explicit before a person holds it.

Cost:
- no solution is both fastest, fully authorized and low-harm.

Hook:
- downstream regions dispute the reserve data used in the D4 model.

# Episode 745 — 빈 의자에 적힌 이름

Date:
- CY 745-04-11 to 04-12.

Goal:
- create a bounded decision process without selecting or precommitting the final order.

Temporary office design:
- one **Route Emergency Executor** for the seven-corridor crisis only;
- appointment term maximum 96 hours;
- can issue at most one D4 order, maximum 72-hour effect;
- cannot override direct physical/medical stop, weapons safety or receiving refusal;
- must publish predicted core/downstream effects and residual missions;
- reviews at 12/24/48 hours;
- no renewal, master key or authority outside the cluster;
- mandatory victim/affected-region inquiry after use.

Appointment body:
- 15 current bodies representing beneficiary regions, affected regions, route/service, military and evidence/appeal standing;
- appointment requires 9 affirmative and no concealment of affected-region objections.

Candidates/structures remain open:
- Haren as dependency/obligation specialist;
- a military/route-security executive;
- a rotating three-person panel;
- full G human-corrective proposal if new grants arrive;
- leaving the office vacant and using local/rotating quotas.

Decision:
- create the vacant office/process only; no appointee or D4 order yet.

Reward:
- the crisis cannot silently assemble D4 through software or merged credentials.

Cost:
- one person may soon receive power to knowingly externalize severe harm.

Final hook:
- E746 begins the appointment as the trigger forecasts worsen and Haren must decide whether to accept the office.
