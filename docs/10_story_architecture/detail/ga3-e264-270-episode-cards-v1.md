# Episode 264 — 첫 번째 하역 순서

Date:
- CY 742-12-14.

POV:
- Toma, dock operators, Savo, Kera and receiving service teams.

Visible goal:
- unload without letting the first physical position determine permanent custody or priority.

Hidden pressure:
- cold-chain, Garden inputs, certified parts, fire/housing repair and security equipment all have real clocks.

## Scene units

1. **Dock geometry** — only two safe recovery/inspection lanes are available; cold-chain needs immediate power, biological inputs need temperature control and certified parts require chain-of-custody scan.
2. **Priority claims** — Civic hospitals demand all medical first, Foundry requests technical crates, Transit requests fire/housing stock and Defense requests secured handling.
3. **Temporary intake** — cargo is unloaded by physical survival need but enters neutral/current service custody rather than claimant ownership; each pallet receives source, current condition, stop and appeal contact.
4. **First compromise** — cold-chain and biological inputs move first; one technical pallet and all security cargo remain sealed at dock until representation/custody terms exist.

Decisive choice:
- separate unloading sequence from allocation rights.

Reward:
- no spoilage or custody seizure during intake.

Cost:
- technical work and fire/housing distribution lose a shift; dock labor and power costs rise.

Hook:
- the 88 t medical shipment cannot cover both registered hospital replenishment and Transit/mobile clinic gaps.

POV:

Decisive choice:

Reward:
- food continuity, fire recovery and registered repair all receive bounded material.

Cost:
- Garden output improvement is lower than modeled maximum; all housing queues remain incomplete.

Character state:
- Haren’s model is useful evidence, not representation or final authority.

Hook:

# Episode 269 — 세 번째 도시의 자리

Date:
- CY 742-12-19.

POV:
- Civic, Foundry/technical labor and Transit/service-user blocs with Garden/medical and defense contacts.

Visible goal:
- establish operational representation for post-window allocation and Spine/defense decisions.

Hidden pressure:
- each “city” is internally divided and could turn one seat into false blanket representation.

## Scene units

1. **Three bodies** — Civic/registered institutions, Foundry/technical labor and Transit/service users receive separate current representation channels.
2. **Internal limits** — each body uses multiple delegates/contacts, recall and minority appeal; central-package workers may remain within Foundry representation without surrendering contract position.
3. **Guaranteed domains** — Garden/medical minimums and local defense safety gain protected review/stop roles but not a fourth sovereign chamber.
4. **Authority scope** — supply allocation, live-window consent, temporary service rules and emergency budget only; no permanent constitution/citizenship or total defense command.

Decisive choice:
- create a provisional three-body service/allocation board with revocable mandates and public cost reports.

Reward:
- a body exists that can authorize Phase 3 supply/service decisions without one faction owning Ardis.

Cost:
- decisions require cross-body agreement or bounded emergency rules; excluded/populist/federal/creditor blocs organize opposition.

Hook:
- the first major board vote is whether to spend scarce technical capacity on another Spine B window or immediate defense readiness.

POV:

Visible goal:

Reward:
