# Episode 377 — 현지를 다시 한 칸으로 묶지 않는다

Date:
- CY 743-12-27.

POV:
- Meridian survivors/operators, Graybridge delegates, compact drafters and claimant officeholders.

Visible goal:
- redesign the compact around the exact structural cause of the 97-second delay.

Hidden pressure:
- too many co-equal fields may reproduce paralysis at a larger scale.

## Scene units

1. **Three separations** — direct physical state/stop, affected service-user priority and provincial/founding ratification become distinct rather than one local delegate.
2. **Seven fields** — four claimant functions + direct physical + affected service/user + public evidence/appeal; province/covenant remains a condition on permanent transfer and participation.
3. **Immediate stop** — physical, technical and medical stops act directly and are logged; they do not wait for a Secretariat or majority.
4. **No emergency majority** — cross-field conflict becomes narrowest safe action, explicit cost, public evidence and rapid review; rotating facilitator has no independent actuation.

Decisive choice:
- accept operational complexity rather than recreating a scalable but structurally blind central field.

Reward:
- the exact Meridian failure mode cannot recur through the same stop-routing rule.

Cost:
- no one can promise twelve-minute empire-wide decisions; every mission needs current field assignments and alternates.

Hook:
- centralist families, officers and administrators file a Single Emergency Sovereign Petition citing ARC-17, T-19 and Meridian deaths.

# Episode 378 — 한 명을 세우자는 청원

Date:
- CY 743-12-29.

POV:
- loss families/crews, centralist/dynastic/military/civil blocs, provincial and affected-body representatives.

Visible goal:
- hear the strongest argument for one emergency sovereign before ratifying the revised pilot.

Hidden pressure:
- rejecting the petition as authoritarian would erase real deaths caused partly by delay; accepting it can convert grief into blanket consent.

## Scene units

1. **Petition case** — one recognized emergency sovereign for 90 days, able to appoint or coordinate the four functions and override deadlock; cites real command/service delays and compensation needs.
2. **Support** — some ARC-17/T-19/Meridian families, officers, registered workers and hospital administrators support speed, recognized pay and accountability.
3. **Opposition/conditions** — other families/survivors reject speaking as one bloc; provinces, local operators and service users demand current physical/affected rights and no permanent transfer.
4. **Officeholder response** — none of Elian, Mara, Dor or Isa claims the petition alone; each accepts that the pilot must be compared against the single-sovereign option with published outcomes.

Decisive choice:
- keep the Single Emergency Sovereign Petition as a lawful competing remedy and require the seven-field pilot to prove itself rather than morally dismissing centralization.

Reward:
- central-command benefits and grief-based support remain legitimate evidence.

Cost:
- pilot ratification weakens; hardline blocs prepare parallel orders and may withdraw assets/services.

Hook:
- twenty-four ratification bodies file final pilot decisions under five different participation conditions.

# Episode 379 — 열셋은 들어오고 넷은 기능만 맡는다

Date:
- CY 743-12-31.

POV:
- twenty-four provincial/route/protected bodies, claimant offices and hearing administrators.

Visible goal:
- determine the actual scope of a thirty-day pilot without fabricating unanimity.

Hidden pressure:
- a narrow pilot may be too small to test; broad participation may coerce holdouts through service dependence.

## Scene units

1. **Ratification result** — 13 full thirty-day participants; 4 function-specific participants; 4 reject; 3 defer. Total: 24.
2. **Function-specific terms** — some accept only civil clearing, Registry, military ceasefire or Continuity modules while retaining their own physical/affected/evidence rules.
3. **Nonretaliation/service rule** — rejection or deferral cannot alone terminate medicine, payroll already earned, rescue or identity access; participants cannot claim the compact is empire-wide.
4. **Asset/withdrawal rule** — every mission names field assets and current custody; institutional withdrawal requires six-hour notice except immediate safety/illegal-order stop.

Decisive choice:
- launch a limited, uneven pilot with visible boundaries instead of counting partial participation as full accession.

Reward:
- enough real jurisdictions/functions enter to test the revised structure.

Cost:
- parallel legal zones, duplicated systems and holdout corridors remain; compact opponents retain lawful alternatives and external allies.

Hook:
- the seven-field compact requires all four claimant officeholders to sign their limited function without signing accession to one another.

Date:
- CY 744-01-02.

POV:
- Elian, Mara, Dor, Isa, participating bodies and public evidence officers.

Visible goal:
- activate the revised pilot while preserving the unresolved sovereign question and the existing hearing.

Hidden pressure:
- a joint signature page can become the common seal or collective sovereign that the compact denies.

2. **Pilot term** — effective CY744-01-03 through CY744-02-01; seven-day public reviews, immediate casualty/monopoly/authority-breach review and no automatic extension.

4. **First assignments** — participating corridors submit civil, Registry, defense, service and physical missions; rejecting/deferring bodies retain lawful outside status and urgent-service protections.

Reward:

Cost:
