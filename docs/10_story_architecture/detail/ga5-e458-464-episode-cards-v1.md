# Episode 458 — 세 개의 선서

Date:
- CY 744-03-15.

POV:
- Kev Raon, Captain Rami Ode and current watch sections.

Visible goal:
- identify what the ship’s people actually promised before deciding which order they can obey.

Hidden pressure:
- officers, enlisted crew, technical contractors and frame personnel use different oath texts and family/pay systems.

Scene units:
1. **Oath inventory** — Crown/old state, Empire/law/ship, province/home-defense and contract/safety commitments overlap.
2. **Not one crew** — 412 people divide into several legal and practical cohorts without becoming factions of good and evil.
3. **Kev’s limit** — he can speak for an elected crew council on labor/pay/safety, not for officers, contractors or tactical decisions.
4. **Rami’s limit** — captaincy controls ship safety and current discipline but cannot convert old commission into political ownership.

Decisive choice:
- negotiate a mission agreement by authority layer rather than hold a single loyalty vote.

Reward:
- each cohort’s standing and nontransferable rights become visible.

Cost:
- the ship remains unavailable; public rumor mislabels the process as mutiny referendum.

Hook:
- Chief Engineer Leda Voss fails one of the two thermal safeties during a live low-load test.

# Episode 459 — 움직일 수 있는 배와 싸울 수 있는 배

Date:
- CY 744-03-17.

POV:
- Leda, Tav Oren, Nera and engineering watches.

Visible goal:
- determine whether the destroyer can deploy before certified replacement parts arrive.

Hidden pressure:
- open-service replacements can restore limited function, but Helios certification and insurance do not cover sustained missile/thermal load.

Scene units:
1. **Physical state** — propulsion and maneuver are stable; sustained weapons/thermal cycling risks converter isolation.
2. **Repair choices** — wait 9–13 days for certified parts, cannibalize another escort or install bounded open replacements.
3. **Worker credit** — Leda and ship engineers require written limits and refuse a heroic “engineers will make it work” assumption.
4. **Bounded repair** — one certified salvage unit plus one open replacement permits R2 mission profile, not R3.

Decisive choice:
- reduce the ship’s accepted combat envelope rather than cannibalize a provincial escort or hide the uncertainty.

Reward:
- safe propulsion, point defense and limited missile/flight capacity become available.

Cost:
- sustained pursuit and heavy-barrage roles are prohibited; Helios retains later certification claim.

Hook:
- 48 people file incompatible requests to transfer, stand down or remain under conditions.

# Episode 460 — 떠날 권리와 남을 의무

Date:
- CY 744-03-19.

POV:
- Messa Rin, Kev, Rami, affected officers/crew and family-service staff.

Visible goal:
- process departures and objections without stripping the ship or coercing political loyalty.

Hidden pressure:
- allowing every immediate departure would make the urgent mission impossible; forcing everyone to remain treats crew as inherited property.

Scene units:
1. **Departure categories** — conscientious political refusal, expired contract, medical unfitness, family emergency, discipline case and simple preference.
2. **Standing obligations** — current watch safety and safe handover must be completed before departure except medical/immediate danger.
3. **Bad solidarity** — the crew council initially shields two ammunition-handling violations because the accused supported the lockout.
4. **Correction** — Messa separates labor protection from safety accountability and permits independent investigation.

Decisive choice:
- approve staged transfer/stand-down for 48 people while requiring safe replacement and accountability for two safety cases.

Outcome:
- 11 officers transfer/stand down;
- 23 enlisted/technical crew transfer after handover;
- 8 people receive medical/family emergency release;
- 6 remain temporarily to complete irreplaceable safety duty and leave after mission.

Reward:
- no forced political oath; the remaining ship complement is current and explicit.

Cost:
- active complement falls from 412 to 364 for the mission; watch/fatigue burden rises.

Hook:
- the home province offers immediate family support if the ship accepts its return order in full.

Date:
- CY 744-03-21.

POV:
- Kev, Haren and households in two provincial systems.

Visible goal:
- secure family allotments without allowing a province to purchase command of the whole ship.

Hidden pressure:
- 126 households face rent, care or food interruption within 20 days; abstract constitutional purity will not solve it.

Scene units:
1. **Provincial offer** — full family payments and legal defense in exchange for immediate return.
2. **Treasury gap** — civil bridge covers earned pay but not all provincial housing/care systems.
3. **Reciprocal package** — Haren proposes split contributions tied to completed Vesper mission hours and a later review of home-defense obligation.
4. **Household dissent** — some families prefer return and reject being used as proof of broader duty.

Decisive choice:
- accept province funding only as a current family obligation and recorded mission contribution, not a command purchase.

Reward:
- 111 of 126 urgent households gain 30-day protection; 15 enter individualized appeal/alternate support.

Cost:
- the province records a quantified future service/return claim; other ships demand equal treatment.

Hook:
- Dor offers Rami a direct Protector recommission that would unlock ammunition immediately.

POV:

Visible goal:
- decide whether a direct Protector recommission solves the command/authentication conflict.

Hidden pressure:

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

Date:
- CY 744-03-25.

POV:
- Kev, weapons crews, Serin and Messa.

Visible goal:
- design crew refusal/representation without making tactical fire a shipwide vote.

Hidden pressure:
- commanders fear paralysis; crews fear political or prohibited orders.

Scene units:
1. **Refusal categories** — prohibited civilian attack, claimant seizure, impossible unsafe condition, expired mandate and disputed tactical judgment are separated.
2. **Immediate rule** — technical/medical/physical stops can act at once; prohibited-order refusal goes to captain/alternate and later audit; ordinary tactical disagreement does not stop action.
3. **Crew representation** — Kev receives pay/safety/oath/audit standing, not bridge command.
4. **Weapons release** — current mission packet binds targets and geography to Vesper/Talren defense.

Decisive choice:
- create bounded refusal and audit rules rather than unanimity or total captain discretion.

Reward:
- the crew oath block clears for defined missions.

Cost:
- disputed cases will still be judged after risk has been taken; two sections record formal dissent.

Hook:
- all pieces are ready except how the three authentic orders rank after the seven-day mandate expires.

POV:

Visible goal:

- province/Treasury: split family and earned-pay contribution;
- Helios/Tav: maintenance limits and later certification;

- 6 delayed departures after safety handover;
- no person is detained solely for political refusal.

Readiness:

Decisive choice:
- sign the agreement without declaring who ultimately owns or permanently commands the ship.

Reward:
- `세 번째 종` joins the next bounded defense mission as the first ship-specific charter precedent.

Cost:

Final hook:
