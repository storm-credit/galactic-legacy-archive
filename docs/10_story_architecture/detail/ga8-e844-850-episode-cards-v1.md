Reward:

Cost:
- seven serious harms become claims against the failed negotiation and underfunded manual system.

Hook:
- 1,920 of the prior 3,700 unresolved service cases can be resolved if the replacement separates query authority from archive possession.

- Independence: evacuation survival and community-reconstruction registry;

Cost:

Hook:
