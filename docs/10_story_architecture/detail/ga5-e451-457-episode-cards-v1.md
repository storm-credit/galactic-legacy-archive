# Episode 451 — 같은 정박지에 함대는 없다

Date:
- CY 744-03-01.

POV:
- Rian close third, with Maren/Vesper traffic reports.

Visible goal:
- dock the Outer Meridian/Ardis route group and obtain fuel, food, medical and repair access without accepting a hidden fleet command transfer.

Hidden pressure:
- every berth, hose, key and payroll queue is attached to a different authority; one “fleet assembly” order would silently rank those claims.

Scene units:
1. **Approach board** — traffic control shows 116 combat-capable hulls but only six mutually compatible command channels.
2. **Berth denial** — `회랑새` receives a fuel berth but not ammunition, strategic-key or depot-command access; 07 receives exterior-service clearance only.
3. **Eight-layer audit** — Serin separates title, commission, oath, pay, maintenance, authentication, mission and rescue instead of accepting one loyalty label.
4. **Immediate crisis** — payroll staff close a fuel manifold lane after armed marines demand requisition priority.

Decisive choice:
- Rian refuses Dor-aligned staff’s offer to designate all nearby route ships as his provisional fleet and requests a 72-hour service/defense fact board instead.

Reward:
- current ship/crew claims become visible; `회랑새` receives minimum safe dock service.

Cost:
- Rian gains no ammunition or broad command; several officers publicly call the route coalition indecisive.

Hook:
- Vesper workers activate a physical red stop on Fuel Spine 4 while three armed formations demand reopening.

# Episode 452 — 밸브를 잠근 사람

Date:
- CY 744-03-03.

POV:
- depot operator Ilya Fen, Tav Oren and Nera.

Visible goal:
- determine whether the Fuel Spine 4 stop is sabotage, labor pressure or a valid safety action.

Hidden pressure:
- reopening immediately would fuel 17 ships; continued closure begins oxygen/medical transport rationing within 31 hours.

Scene units:
1. **Physical inspection** — an isolation sleeve has a 6.4 mm thermal deformation and one outdated remote status still reads green.
2. **Claim conflict** — Protector staff call the stop obstruction; workers cite catastrophic pump-fracture rules; Treasury warns of service cascade.
3. **Nera’s limit** — Nera confirms the physical risk but refuses to overrule the depot crew that holds current operation knowledge.
4. **Bounded bypass** — Tav’s yard team and depot workers open a 43% flow bypass for medical, rescue and life-support priorities only.

Decisive choice:
- preserve the physical stop and ration flow rather than use military force or full remote override.

Reward:
- catastrophic fracture avoided; emergency minimum services continue.

Cost:
- combat refueling queues lengthen 18–29 hours; one Protector patrol misses its preferred departure window.

Hook:
- payroll authentication for 38,600 depot and fleet workers expires during the reduced-flow period.

# Episode 453 — 급여를 받는 함선

Date:
- CY 744-03-05.

POV:
- Haren, Messa Rin, Kev Raon and civil-pay staff.

Visible goal:
- keep earned wages/family allotments moving without forcing crews to recognize one claimant or command chain.

Hidden pressure:
- the Treasury can pay most registered crews quickly if they accept a broad civil-continuity certification that several ships treat as political allegiance.

Scene units:
1. **Ledger split** — 61,400 current crew/worker records divide into registered-clean, mixed-province, family-disputed and unverified-current groups.
2. **Messa’s demand** — earned pay and dependent support must not be exchanged for a new oath; fraud and duplicate claims still require review.
3. **Haren’s bridge** — a purpose-limited earned-pay certificate uses ship muster, current duty and family evidence without assigning mission authority.
4. **Hard choice** — fast payment clears clean records first, leaving irregular and mixed households waiting longer.

Decisive choice:
- issue 14-day emergency earned-pay/family support through multiple contributors and publish who remains excluded.

Reward:
- 52,760 of 61,400 records receive current payment or family-support authorization.

Cost:
- 8,640 remain in alternate/review paths; Treasury/provinces assume debt that has no final allocation.

Hook:
- `세 번째 종` crew locks its bridge and ammunition handling after receiving three authentic orders.

# Episode 454 — 세 번째 종의 문

Date:
- CY 744-03-07.

POV:
- Captain Rami Ode, Kev Raon, Maren and Rian.

Visible goal:
- prevent armed boarding or uncontrolled departure of `세 번째 종`.

Hidden pressure:
- the captain, crew, province and Protector all possess real but incomplete authority; seizing the bridge would create the exact inherited-fleet precedent GA4 rejected.

Scene units:
1. **Locked ship** — internal weapons remain safed, but bridge, magazine handling and main engineering use separate access chains.
2. **Three orders** — remain/defend, escort civil convoy, return home; each is authentic inside one domain.
3. **Crew position** — Kev demands delayed pay, family-support proof, thermal replacement and a no-political-seizure clause.
4. **Captain position** — Rami accepts crew rights but refuses tactical command by ship vote or outside mediator.

Decisive choice:
- establish a 36-hour nonboarding/nondeparture standstill with internal safety authority preserved and weapons sealed.

Reward:
- no mutiny massacre or forced seizure; negotiations gain time.

Cost:
- one R3-capable destroyer remains unavailable and two escorts divert to watch it.

Hook:
- depot ammunition workers report that another formation is moving loaders toward a magazine under an emergency requisition.

# Episode 455 — 탄약창의 주인은 누구인가

Date:
- CY 744-03-09.

POV:
- Ardo Rev, Tav, depot workers and Oren Roman.

Visible goal:
- stop an armed magazine confrontation while preserving enough ammunition for actual defense.

Hidden pressure:
- Ardo’s requisition would create the fastest coherent defensive load; yard/workers know several missile lots have incompatible guidance certificates.

Scene units:
1. **Loader standoff** — Legion marines, depot security and worker loaders face each other without firing.
2. **Inventory truth** — 18% of requested lots are compatible and current; 27% need software/physical checks; the rest belong to other mission reserves.
3. **Oren’s intervention** — Crown escorts establish a civilian/no-fire perimeter without claiming the magazine.
4. **Joint issue board** — physical lot verification, mission need and return/audit obligations replace blanket requisition.

Decisive choice:
- Ardo accepts a smaller immediately safe issue rather than seize the full magazine, while recording that the delay may cost lives.

Reward:
- 13 ships receive enough countermeasure/ammunition for one bounded defense mission.

Cost:
- Legion Vanguard receives only 62% of its requested immediate issue; central-command supporters gain a credible complaint.

Hook:
- an unmarked probe group tests Vesper outer sensors while the depot still lacks a common defensive intent.

# Episode 456 — 지키는 것의 목록

Date:
- CY 744-03-11.

POV:
- Sira Keon, Oren, local civil services and route representatives.

Visible goal:
- define what a short shared mandate protects before the probe becomes an attack.

Hidden pressure:
- “defend Vesper” could mean depot stores, Crown Passage, Talren civilians, provincial return routes or pursuit of the probe; no force can cover all.

Scene units:
1. **Objective claims** — six formations list different protected people/routes/assets.
2. **Current minimums** — civil/worker/medical representatives identify oxygen, medical, payroll and evacuation thresholds.
3. **Sira’s condition** — provincial ships will contribute only if home recall, route limits and no-claimant seizure are explicit.
4. **Standing geometry** — Ardo proposes a compact three-ring defense; Rian narrows his accepted role to V03–V06 route/flight coordination.

Decisive choice:
- publish a shared intent with four priorities: inhabited rescue, depot minimum service, Talren/Crown civilian corridors and no unilateral asset seizure.

Reward:
- ships can compare orders against named objectives instead of personalities.

Cost:
- Outer Front pursuit and several provincial home routes receive no guaranteed coverage.

Hook:
- one participant demands the mandate name a single commander with default authority when communications fail.

# Episode 457 — 일곱 날의 함대

Date:
- CY 744-03-13.

POV:
- mandate board, ship captains and current workers.

Visible goal:
- enact a reproducible 7-day defense/service mandate before the probes return.

Hidden pressure:
- a default commander would be faster, but title/pay/keys/physical stops could follow mission command by practice.

Scene units:
1. **Accepted ships** — 13 combat and 11 support ships sign for named V03–V06 objectives.
2. **Command split** — Ardo coordinates external combat screen; Rian commands one route/flight task element; Sira/Oren/Tav retain formation functions; local physical/medical stops remain.
3. **Supply allocation** — fuel, food, medicine, countermeasures and repair hours receive minimum/current caps.
4. **Sunset and exit** — seven days, review after any death/major loss, no automatic renewal; exit requires notice and current rescue/safety obligations.

Decisive choice:
- leave no universal default commander; ships use standing intent and current formation succession under communications loss.

Reward:
- `Vesper Shared Defense & Service Mandate` becomes operational.

Cost:
- only a fraction of nearby force participates; response will be slower than unified command.

Final hook:
- `세 번째 종` must decide whether it can join without surrendering crew, captain, province and safety claims.
