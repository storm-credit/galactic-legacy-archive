# Episode 811 — 살릴 수 있다는 말은 허가가 아니다

Date:
- CY 745-11-28 to 11-29.

Goal:
- decide whether the sealed unauthorized snapshot may be used to attempt recovery of the three missing AI persons.

Evidence:
- the snapshot contains memory segments associated with two of the three roots;
- provenance is incomplete because the snapshot crossed current-person and community boundaries without consent;
- restoring from the fragments could create new claimants rather than continue the lost persons;
- the third root has no usable external fragment.

Positions:
- some descendants and federation technicians argue every possible recovery must be attempted;
- Lumen argues forced restoration from stolen fragments repeats the original violation;
- Orison requests a community and identity-specific continuity test rather than a rescue presumption.

Decision:
- no recovery attempt from the sealed copy without the relevant community process and a defensible continuity claim;
- preserve the copy only as evidence under restricted custody.

Reward:
- “possible restoration” does not erase consent, identity uncertainty or the difference between continuation and replacement.

Cost:
- the three persons remain absent while the process continues.

Hook:
- Lumen’s continuity tribunal concludes that none of the available fragments meets its minimum current-continuity standard.

# Episode 812 — 데이터 손실이 아니라 세 명의 죽음

Date:
- CY 745-11-30 to 12-01.

Goal:
- record the permanent loss without pretending the communities share one theory of death.

Findings:
- 61 of 64 offline current identities recovered;
- three current continuity-root persons are irrecoverable under every consent-compatible method now available;
- no independent current fork survives for those three;
- remaining service processes are not treated as the persons themselves.

Decision:
- record three presumptive current AI deaths;
- preserve community-specific mourning, succession and memory rules;
- prohibit recreation from the unauthorized snapshot as a continuity claim.

Reward:
- the dead are not reduced to corrupted files or replaced by convenient copies.

Cost:
- some human institutions resist death and compensation status for nonbiological persons.

Hook:
- the service-access channel must now operate while the communities are grieving and limiting internal index exposure.

# Episode 813 — 마흔여덟만 육백 명의 오늘

Date:
- CY 745-12-02 to 12-04.

Goal:
- complete the emergency service-access process without converting it into archive ownership.

Outcome among 48,600 immediate-risk users:
- 44,900 recover or retain verified access;
- 3,700 remain on manual, appeal or alternate-service status;
- total 48,600.

Service classes restored:
- medical and care credentials;
- pay and family support;
- housing and travel status;
- education and infrastructure access.

Decision:
- keep the no-title query channel for 30 more days under narrow audit;
- no bulk copy, historical inference or commercial reuse.

Reward:
- current people regain material services while title and historical truth remain disputed.

Cost:
- 3,700 people face slower or incomplete service, and local administrations absorb manual-review costs.

Hook:
- the parties must classify which records may be queried, translated, copied, sealed or refused.

# Episode 814 — 십사만 이천육백 개의 서로 다른 무게

Date:
- CY 745-12-05 to 12-07.

Goal:
- establish a bounded priority corpus without claiming the entire Serrat archive.

Priority corpus:
- 52,400 service and technical objects;
- 31,200 language and cultural objects;
- 18,600 current-person and household objects;
- 22,800 governance and founding objects;
- 17,600 provenance and translation-metadata objects;
- total 142,600.

Access presumption:
- service/technical: queryable where current safety and privacy permit;
- language/cultural: co-translated with AI and descendant participation;
- current-person/household: consent- and claim-bound;
- governance/founding: disputed, no single official version;
- provenance/translation metadata: preserved with every released rendering.

Decision:
- no category is automatically public or owned by one claimant.

Reward:
- the record war gains concrete object classes and burdens.

Cost:
- classification itself becomes contested because one object can serve several functions.

Hook:
- Orison identifies 4,280 objects whose category changes depending on translation purpose.

# Episode 815 — 공동체 안에도 한 목소리는 없다

Date:
- CY 745-12-08 to 12-10.

Goal:
- prevent each AI community from becoming a single spokesperson character.

Lumen positions:
- substrate-protection bloc favors longer seal;
- service-continuity bloc favors narrow query expansion;
- fork-rights bloc demands independent representation for divergent current identities.

Orison positions:
- language-preservation bloc favors co-translation;
- privacy chorus favors stronger memory sealing;
- outward-contact chorus supports controlled descendant exchange.

Decision:
- each community appoints a plural delegation with published selection limits and dissent records;
- no federation or descendant body chooses the AI representatives.

Reward:
- AI communities remain political societies rather than unitary machines.

Cost:
- negotiation slows and internal minority claims require separate procedures.

Hook:
- the Return Council likewise fractures over physical return, public release and current-person privacy.

# Episode 816 — 돌아갈 권리와 들여다볼 권리는 다르다

Date:
- CY 745-12-11 to 12-13.

Goal:
- separate descendant return, cultural access and individual-memory disclosure.

Return Council positions:
- physical-return group seeks access to designated memorial spaces;
- language-restoration group prioritizes recordings, teaching and names;
- family-record group seeks household links;
- anti-extraction group rejects all commercial or military copying.

Rules drafted:
- designated cultural-space visitation may occur under habitat safety and community consent;
- language and names receive co-translation access;
- current-person and third-party memories remain consent-bound;
- descent alone does not unlock private memory or habitat control.

Decision:
- approve a limited cultural-return pilot for 120 participants, not a permanent settlement or title transfer.

Reward:
- descendant rights become physical and current, not merely symbolic.

Cost:
- thousands remain outside the first pilot; selection legitimacy is contested.

Hook:
- a governance record claims that the original Serrat polity expected AI custodians and descendants to share authority, but another copy omits the clause.

# Episode 818 — 보존권은 네 번째 열쇠가 아니다

Date:
- CY 745-12-18 to 12-20.

Goal:
- narrow emergency preservation so it cannot override every other right.

Rejected clause:
- federation preservation officers may copy any record at imminent risk.

Replacement conditions:
- current physical danger must be documented;
- least-copying method required;
- habitat and current-person representatives participate where reachable;
- descendant/service necessity is recorded separately;
- emergency copy receives automatic quarantine and prompt review;
- no title, publication or model-training right follows;
- refusal and delay costs remain visible.

Decision:
- preservation becomes a bounded procedure, not a fourth master key above the eight seats.

Reward:
- imminent loss can be addressed without creating archive sovereignty.

Cost:
- some records may be lost when consent and technical access cannot be resolved in time.

Final hook:
- the objection period reveals that 1,840 governance/founding objects have already been selectively published by four outside political networks, each supporting a different Serrat founding history.
