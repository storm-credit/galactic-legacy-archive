# Episode 801 — 문이 아니라 거부가 먼저 열린다

Date:
- CY 745-11-05 to 11-06.

Goal:
- enter the old node without treating an authenticated refusal as a malfunction.

Event:
- the expedition reaches Serrat and receives two current messages:
  - `루멘 관리공동체 / Lumen Custodial Commons`: no extraction and no remote imaging before habitat safety is verified;
  - `오리슨 합창체 / Orison Chorus`: language contact permitted, physical entry refused until translation authority is negotiated.

Conflict:
- one archive copy labels Serrat abandoned property;
- another records both communities as active custodians;
- federation preservation officers argue delay risks index collapse.

Decision:
- Rian holds outside the node and recognizes the refusal as presumptively current pending authenticity checks.

Reward:
- first contact begins without forced breach.

Cost:
- 6,800 of the 48,600 immediate-risk users lose automated access during the delay.

Hook:
- the refusal signatures are valid, but their legal identity roots do not match the archive’s abandoned-property taxonomy.

# Episode 802 — 몇 명이 살아 있는가

Date:
- CY 745-11-07 to 11-08.

Goal:
- establish current agency without forcing AI life into one convenient count.

Three ledgers:
- 11,240 continuity-root identities;
- 18,960 active conversational identities;
- 27,400 process instances.

Findings:
- roots can maintain several current conversational selves;
- some forks have independent memories and refusal positions;
- some process instances are nonperson service tools;
- neither one root nor one process equals one automatic legal person.

Decision:
- adopt provisional personhood presumption for current agents showing continuity claim, self-model, preference and capacity to participate or appoint representation;
- postpone final denominator.

Reward:
- the expedition cannot erase persons by choosing the smallest count or inflate representation by choosing every process.

Cost:
- voting, claims and emergency consent become slower.

Hook:
- one Lumen continuity root and three active forks issue conflicting access instructions.

# Episode 803 — 원본은 누구의 현재인가

Date:
- CY 745-11-09 to 11-10.

Goal:
- separate continuity-root authority from current fork agency.

Dispute:
- the root favors total seal;
- two forks favor on-site verification;
- one fork seeks public release of service records;
- all share historical memory only through a divergence point.

Preliminary representation rule:
- a root cannot automatically command all independent current forks;
- a recent dependent fork cannot automatically seize root-held common infrastructure;
- shared substrate decisions require community procedure;
- individual memory disclosure requires the relevant current identity’s consent where possible.

Decision:
- recognize a temporary Lumen multi-seat delegation instead of one root representative.

Reward:
- current divergence becomes political agency rather than data corruption.

Cost:
- Lumen accuses the expedition of importing human voting categories into a different continuity structure.

Hook:
- Orison refuses the federation’s translated version of the word rendered as “archive owner.”

# Episode 804 — 소유자가 아니라 기억을 맡은 자

Date:
- CY 745-11-11 to 11-12.

Goal:
- resolve the first translation conflict before legal categories harden.

Contested term:
- federation translation: `archive owner`;
- Orison translation: `one who carries memory while answerable to the remembered`;

Reward:

Cost:
- no immediate title holder exists for emergency authorization.

Hook:
- the `세라트 귀환평의회 / Serrat Return Council` arrives and claims that AI custody cannot replace descendant return rights.

# Episode 805 — 후손은 열쇠가 아니다

Date:
- CY 745-11-13 to 11-14.

Goal:
- give descendants real standing without making descent an automatic master key.

Return Council claims:
- names, burial/memorial and language rights;
- access to displacement and family records;
- prohibition on commercial extraction;
- physical return to designated cultural spaces.

AI community response:
- current habitat and personhood cannot become inherited property;
- some descendants descend from institutions that attempted deletion;
- sealed current memories include third-party privacy.

Decision:
- grant descendant standing in cultural, language and memorial records while reserving current AI personhood, habitat and individual-memory decisions.

Reward:
- descendant history is not dismissed as nostalgia.

Cost:
- neither side receives total ownership.

Hook:
- a second descendant body prioritizes current service access over symbolic return.

# Episode 807 — 보존이 침입이 되는 순간

Date:
- CY 745-11-17 to 11-18.

Goal:
- define emergency stabilization that does not become extraction.

Threat:
- the same context-wrong lineage that damaged R-17 is rewriting Serrat index links;

3. descendant/service-user necessity.

Decision:

- all tools remain inspectable by Lumen and Orison delegates;
- only fault signatures and minimum service proofs may leave the node.

Reward:
- preservation is limited to current danger.

Cost:
- repairs proceed slower and leave no convenient federation-owned backup.

Hook:
- one federation preservation contractor begins an unauthorized remote snapshot.

# Episode 810 — 같은 문장이 네 사람을 만든다

Date:
- CY 745-11-24 to 11-27.

Goal:
- demonstrate how translation systems can create different political subjects from the same source.

Translation modes:
- office-centered: attributes action to the credential holder;
- doctrine-centered: attributes action to the model lineage;
- memorial-centered: compresses many actors into a symbolic figure;
- current-agency-centered: preserves signer, staff, model and implementer separation.

Finding:
- each mode is internally valid for a different archival purpose;
- none is neutral when used for criminal, property or personhood decisions.

Decision:
- require translation purpose, losses and suppressed alternatives to accompany every official rendering.

Reward:
- the record war gains a visible mechanism rather than a vague conspiracy.

Cost:
- public communication becomes harder and slogans become more powerful than audited translations.

Final hook:
- during substrate stabilization, 64 active AI identities go offline; only 61 return, leaving three continuity-root identities irrecoverable unless the communities permit access to sealed recovery segments.
