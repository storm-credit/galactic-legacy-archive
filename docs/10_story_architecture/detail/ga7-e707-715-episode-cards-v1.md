# Episode 707 — 스물세 개의 문

Date:
- CY 745-01-16 to 01-18.

Goal:
- convene a temporary route/service table without creating a federation or master route office.

Bodies:
- 23 regions, route authorities, service compacts and ship/Neutral representatives.

Initial positions:
- 12 full emergency-table participants;
- 5 function-specific participants;
- 3 observers;
- 3 reject/nonparticipants.

Decision:
- establish a 45-day Emergency Mutual Route Table for facts, named missions, minimum obligations and dispute notice;
- no permanent command, master key, compulsory membership or majority binding on nonparticipants.

Reward:
- corridor problems can be negotiated as a network.

Cost:
- participation is partial and urgent routes still require separate physical, military, service and financing agreements.

Hook:
- participants disagree whether minimum obligations should follow population, capacity, benefit, fault or prior contribution.

Goal:

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

# Episode 709 — 닫은 뒤에도 남는 의무

Date:
- CY 745-01-22.

Goal:
- enforce notice and residual duties after a participant closes for valid current safety reasons.

Required residuals:
- current distress/contact response;
- already committed patients/cargo/crews;
- safe diversion data;
- minimum local/receiving support;
- downstream warning;
- later claims/evidence.

Decision:
- accept the physical closure but require a 36-hour residual-service package and substitute-route contribution.

Reward:
- local safety authority remains while downstream abandonment is reduced.

Cost:
- closing region bears costs after losing traffic/benefit; critics call it coerced federation.

Hook:
- the first full obligation test targets the 93k critical holdout group.

Mission:

- 8,900 t cargo;

Cost:

Hook:

- CY 745-01-27 to 01-29.

Decision:

- medical/contact cargo and ship command remain outside military ownership;

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:
- creditors refuse the temporary mission liens created for damaged rescue and service hulls.

# Episode 713 — 열지 않는 것도 참가다

Date:
- CY 745-02-01 to 02-02.

Goal:
- prevent political retaliation while preserving lawful nonparticipation and dissent.

Decision:
- participant can reject nonurgent/common missions but cannot retaliate against current emergency medicine, earned pay, family contact or already accepted obligations;
- withdrawal requires notice/residual duties and does not transfer keys.

Outcome:
- threatened total suspension narrows to nonurgent and political traffic;
- three emergency missions continue;
- issuing authority remains a lawful opponent, not expelled or seized.

Reward:
- table can survive dissent without compulsory federation.

Cost:
- political/commercial fragmentation deepens and supporters demand stronger enforcement.

Hook:
- one overloaded substitute corridor fails despite compliance with the table.
