Working title:

## 7. Ghost-Ship Core Mystery

Recommended truth:

- `NR72-061` was not simply destroyed.
- after a relay disaster and disputed rescue, its registry was closed and hull sections/modules were redistributed under multiple insurance, military and worker claims.
- its identity/transponder was reused to move parts, people or losses off books.
- several “061 parts” are genuine, some are forged, and some were rebuilt by undocumented workers.
- the apparent ghost is a chain of claims and labor, not one intact legendary ship.

Why it matters:
- mirrors person identity closure and Archive compression.
- second frame/service module lineage connects to 07/AUXILIA but is not a hidden superweapon.

### Risk: ship repair arc feels like maintenance spreadsheet

Control:
- every defect tied to authority, debt, crew or mission choice.
- repair reveals past harm/claim.
- visible first departure by episode 121–123 at latest.

### Risk: ghost parts feels like unrelated treasure hunt

Control:
- directly supplies second bay/Silex service and mirrors identity/Archive compression.
- current residents/workers create moral ownership conflict.

### Risk: convoy becomes meetings

Control:
- fixed deadlines, destination capacity changes, packing/boarding/route actions and split convoy threats.
- not all debates occur around one table.

### Risk: standard war is abstract

Control:
- named injury/failure, actual machines/medicine/routes, worker and user decisions, Silex physical mission.

### Risk: P-001/admiral scale delayed

Control:
- first ship command conflicts and regional force/reinforcement clocks show admiral architecture.
- one ship-scale engagement and one multi-faction infrastructure operation.
- P-001 cannot yet command fleet, but learns fleet-relevant authority/logistics.
