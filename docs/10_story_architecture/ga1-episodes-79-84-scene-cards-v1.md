# Episode 79 — 제국은 학교를 살릴 수 있다

Date:
- CY 742-06-04.

Episode function:
- present the Imperial package at full strength and cost.
- separate voluntary Imperial supporters from forced transfer.
- make Rian confront Darin’s informed choice.

## Scene 79.1 — The operational demonstration

POV:
- Rian at Ring A/Dock/security briefing.

Imperial offer demonstrates:
- payroll/food/security schedule.
- recognized military qualification/citizenship routes.
- medical/protected identity continuity.
- rapid restoration of command and node confidence.

Physical proof:
- supplies and personnel already present.
- one current security/medical problem is solved faster than the Academy’s plural structure.

## Scene 79.2 — National-security terms

Terms:
- classified curriculum/missions.
- protected state custody of 07/ORA/continuity records.
- expanded service obligations.
- advisory rather than binding student/patient representation.
- restricted exit.

Imperial observer distinguishes:
- cultivation/protection from Purge/hardline control.
- cannot guarantee future internal politics.

## Scene 79.3 — Darin’s decision

Rian privately warns Darin of broad future risks without revealing full ranking/history.

Darin:
- recognizes manipulation/omission.
- chooses Imperial service for squad/family/citizenship/security.
- demands current written protections rather than Rian’s future promise.

Rian choice:
- could expose Darin’s future importance to recruit/pressure actors.
- refuses and records/defends Darin’s current consent.

Cost:
- a likely future hero leaves Rian’s reach.

## Scene 79.4 — Voluntary and disputed lists

Yori/Serin/Darin identify:
- some listed students clearly consent.
- some did not respond or have incomplete appeal.
- some consent only if their cell/family remains together.

Imperial commander:
- agrees to review limited cases but refuses to suspend the whole transfer.

End hook:
- the first departure manifest still includes 17 disputed cases mixed with volunteers.

Reward:
- Imperial package understood and Darin autonomy protected.

Cost:
- transfer remains imminent and attractive.

# Episode 80 — 가장 안전한 계약

Date:
- CY 742-06-05.

Episode function:
- present the Helix package through work, treatment and contract detail.
- split Nera/Sera/Helix internal interests.
- make technical/medical safety an argument for control.

## Scene 80.1 — Full operating plan

POV:
- Rian visiting Ring C/Helix presentation.

Helix shows:
- 180-day full-equivalent funding.
- payroll, certification, modern tools and treatment.
- exact job/placement tracks.
- debt restructuring.

Current benefit:
- one Black Ward/maintenance supply or safety issue resolved immediately under Helix procedure.

## Scene 80.2 — Contract ownership

Terms:
- long service obligations.
- data and technical licensing.
- proprietary standards and restricted open workshop work.
- family/dependent benefits tied to continued contract.
- expensive exit/buyout.

Sera:
- argues these are known costs for real benefits.

Nera:
- challenges ownership of worker/student design and medical dependency.

## Scene 80.3 — Helix divides itself

Manufacturing/Frontier staff:
- willing to accept local worker/design credit, bounded standards council and safe adapter licensing.

Finance/Security:
- wants management control and asset/data certainty before investing 49 units.

Sera:
- sides with certification/work but opposes blanket remote lock or erasure of local labor.

No single corporate voice.

## Scene 80.4 — Conditional signers

Students/staff sign letters of intent with conditions.
- some are voluntary and informed.
- some are pushed by family debt/treatment.

Rian/Nera cannot offer equivalent immediate resources.

End hook:
- Helix Finance adds a clause allowing technical seizure if the secret Imperial transfer begins, to protect assets from state capture.

Reward:
- viable medical/technical funding and internal Helix allies.

Cost:
- competing seizure authority and debt/data control.

Date:
- CY 742-06-06.

Episode function:
- present Independence and Neutral packages through actual capacity and destinations.
- expose rescue inequality and weak defense.
- give Aven/Ern/Amel conflicting goals.

## Scene 81.1 — Independence capacity board

POV:
- Rian/Haren at a witnessed route meeting.

Ern/Federalist contacts provide:
- ships, departure windows, destinations and obligations.
- 480–720 first-14-day capacity; 900–1,300 over 45 days if routes hold.

Limits:
- not enough for everyone.
- medical/high-dependency and family loads reduce numbers.
- some routes require labor/defense/militia obligations.

Aven:
- still prefers exit but demands transparent priority instead of future-value selection.

## Scene 81.2 — Who boards first

Competing criteria:
- immediate custody danger.
- medical/route compatibility.
- family/cell unity.
- skills useful at destination.
- political/faction priority.

Ern’s network and radicals disagree.

- sees how easily his future hero rankings could become boarding priority.
- refuses to submit such a list.

## Scene 81.3 — Neutral trusteeship

- 6 immediate / 18 over 90 days.
- medical, claims, evidence and transfer review.
- combat training and weapons restricted.
- no full defense/funding for the whole station.

Amel:
- supports medical/legal protection but asks who pays staff and defends patients after 90 days.

Neutral representative:
- admits limits.

## Scene 81.4 — Radical deadline

Radical Independence element decides:
- if Imperial/Helix takeover proceeds, key records/core/route systems should be denied or destroyed to prevent permanent capture.

Ern opposes destruction with people inside but cannot fully control them.

End hook:
- a hidden demolition/denial route is activated in Old Yard/Spine infrastructure.

Reward:
- real exit and rights options.

Cost:

# Episode 82 — 학교를 유지할 수 없는 학교

- CY 742-06-07.

Episode function:
- test internal continuation honestly and extract hybrid components.
- show current workers/students can operate parts of the system but not fund everything.
- create the first concrete charter draft ingredients.

## Scene 82.1 — Emergency internal week

POV:
- Rian moving through school during reduced operation.

Results:
- students/workers keep food, maintenance, clinic and selected training running.
- payroll reduced and fatigue rising.
- one deferred repair/shortage causes a real interruption or injury risk.

Bram/Kara:
- demonstrate local knowledge and the cost of unpaid/understaffed continuity.

## Scene 82.2 — Budget wall

Haren/administration:
- internal resources 2.7 + uncertain 2.0–3.3 cannot meet 8.7 immediate minimum reliably.
- current plan survives 24–38 days, not 180.

Rian:
- cannot solve with a heroic mission before closure unless there is legal/ship/route capacity.

## Scene 82.3 — Components worth keeping

Representatives identify package components:

- Imperial base funding, citizenship and defense.
- Helix treatment, equipment and jobs.
- Neutral audit/medical/claims.
- Independence exit/route/federal options.
- internal local staff, patient/student/worker authority.

Conflict:
- each external party wants enough control to justify its contribution.

## Scene 82.4 — First hybrid numbers

Initial 180-day target:
- need 49.
- possible coalition reaches 46 with a 3+ contingency gap.

Meaning:
- even hybrid charter starts underfunded and dependent on future external missions.

End hook:
- Operations accelerates the Imperial transfer because the hybrid draft has no legal operator or full funding yet.

Reward:
- viable charter components and transparent gap.

Cost:
- internal plan proven insolvent; transfer deadline advances.
