# Episode 93 — 중앙키

Date:
- CY 742-06-12, first emergency window.

Episode function:
- make Rian take real central power and save lives.
- prove holding it is effective, not merely tempting.
- expose the inseparability of safety and coercive control.

## Scene 93.1 — Approach to the central interface

POV:
- Rian in 07.

Physical objective:
- reach/hold the central safety/service interface while local teams preserve power/doors/routes.

Opposition:
- current central claimant’s security frames/locks.
- infrastructure damage and degraded 07.
- radical or opportunist interference.

07:
- no prolonged duel; uses tools/tether/tags and precise movement.

## Scene 93.2 — Rian takes command

Authority A–B/core anomaly bridges access.

Immediate actions:
- resolves one conflicting rotation/door/life-support command.
- prevents a shuttle/structure collision or pressure failure.
- reopens a medical/current-person corridor.

Visible result:
- centralized command saves people faster than distributed negotiation could.

People across fronts ask Rian to keep it until all danger ends.

## Scene 93.3 — The other controls

The same interface offers:
- lock Ring B sectors.
- complete or cancel transfer manifests.
- seize/disable frames.
- copy/close records.
- prioritize Black Ward/technical assets.

Rian could impose his own best outcome.

Future-memory temptation:
- select/retain high-value people and prevent rival departures.
- destroy radical/hostile routes.
- command every front.

## Scene 93.4 — First limited order

Rian uses central power only for declared immediate safety/anti-seizure objectives and announces/logs scope.

He orders preparations to segment authority rather than expanding personal control.

Opposition:
- allies say relinquishing now will cause additional casualties.
- external actors call the segmentation unlawful/sabotage.

End hook:
- the central system gives Rian 12–20 minutes before renewal/overload or a permanent claimant retakes it.

Reward:
- central key and immediate lives saved.

Cost:
- Rian becomes responsible for central orders and faces a real safer-control argument.

# Episode 94 — 키를 나누는 동안

Date:
- CY 742-06-12.

Episode function:
- execute physical/technical distribution under fire.
- give every specialist a domain and local dependency.
- show no abstract “decentralize” command can work without hardware, people and time.

## Scene 94.1 — Segments

POV:
- Rian through central feed/07.

Target segments:
- life support/logistics → local operations/Haren network.
- power/technical → Nera/Bram/operators.
- medical → licensed clinicians/Mia/patient oversight.
- security/doors → Kara/professional Security under limits.
- records/comms → Serin/Neutral/local witness network.
- Dock D/frames → field/technical/mission split.

Each requires:
- physical/local key or control station.
- current operator.
- signed scope and fallback.

## Scene 94.2 — Haren’s minimum service

Haren/local operations:
- chooses which zones/systems receive air, power, food and movement priority.
- publishes/records minimum service.
- cannot preserve every abandoned workshop/room/asset.

Conflict:
- one faction/sector accuses him of sacrificing its people/assets.

He relies on Yori/local current-person counts, not only central sensors.

## Scene 94.3 — Nera and the physical keys

Nera/Bram/engineers:
- separate technical links and install/manualize control.
- one shortcut could finish faster but bypass worker/maintenance safety.
- Bram refuses and forces a slower route.

Rian chooses not to override Bram from central command.

## Scene 94.4 — Clock loss

Segmentation runs slower than centralized control.
- one system/route deteriorates before its local key is ready.
- a serious casualty, patient deterioration or lost asset occurs.

Rian can reverse by retaining/renewing more central power.
- he continues segmentation while using only immediate safety action.

End hook:
- Ring B transfer corridor loses coordination as Darin’s Imperial-aligned squad receives orders from the old/new systems simultaneously.

Reward:
- first authority segments physically established.

Cost:
- visible delay harm and accusations Rian chose ideology over lives.
