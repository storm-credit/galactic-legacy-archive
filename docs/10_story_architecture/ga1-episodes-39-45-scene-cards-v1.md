# Episode 39 — 법적으로 도착한 물건

Date:
- CY 742-04-24.

Episode function:
- distinguish legal custody, physical custody and operational control.
- trace the core through bounded evidence rather than omniscient hacking.
- reveal the diversion was protective/strategic, not simple resale theft.

## Scene 39.1 — Empty vault chain

POV:
- Rian with Serin leading a narrow inquiry.

Evidence chain:
- Neutral handoff record.
- transport container administrative seal.
- Imperial legal receipt.
- absent physical vault mass/thermal intake.
- obsolete protocol signal from K-13/Aca­demy-connected infrastructure.

Serin conclusion:
- custody changed legally at the handoff.
- the physical object was redirected before vault intake.
- no current evidence identifies the person/order with full certainty.

Security response:
- classification and continuity procedures permit nonstandard evidence movement.
- students lack standing to inspect the destination.

## Scene 39.2 — Where the signal can exist

Nera:
- narrows possible locations based on core power, cooling and service interface needs.
- it cannot be hidden in an ordinary office or unpowered container for weeks without degradation/evidence.

Possible sites:
- relay evidence-isolation cradle.
- low-gravity medical/records annex.
- Imperial service craft.

Haren:
- maps access, witnesses and consequences.
- only the first two preserve a path to public/joint custody; the craft route could remove it entirely.

Rian:
- wants immediate covert search.

## Scene 39.3 — The protected witness cost

Discovery:
- the same classified procedure used to move the core also relocated or sealed a White Dock witness/identity subject.
- the person is physically safer from Security/radicals but unavailable to the inquiry and family/community.

Meaning:
- the unknown Imperial actor is not simply stealing assets.
- “protection” is removing people/things from decisions.

Mia:
- notes relocation may preserve treatment or life.

Serin:
- notes the person cannot correct or challenge the new record status.

## Scene 39.4 — Invitation to one person

Action:
- Rian receives a controlled message through medical/administrative channels.
- invitation offers proof and core access if he attends alone.

Condition:
- no team, no public record, no Academy escort.

Rian:
- considers withholding the meeting to preserve bargaining power.

End hook:
- message contains a core response phrase only Rian/07 service interactions should have produced.

Reward:
- physical diversion narrowed.

Cost:
- classified witness relocation and exclusive contact.

# Episode 40 — 국가가 숨기는 방식

Date:
- CY 742-04-25.

Episode function:
- introduce Imperial observer’s real motive and bounded authority.
- make exclusive patronage materially attractive.
- advance seven-authority/continuity mystery without revealing the final Archive origin.

## Scene 40.1 — Medical-record cover

POV:
- Rian.

Location:
- controlled low-gravity medical/records annex or relay evidence room.

Imperial observer:
- appears through the existing assistant medical-record examiner cover.
- states they diverted the core after it responded to an obsolete continuity/succession service protocol.

Reason:
- Helix exclusive claim could lock it into proprietary analysis.
- Archive/Imperial hardliners could erase or seize students/witnesses.
- Academy could dismantle it as old equipment.

Limit:
- observer cannot legally grant permanent ownership or free the students alone.

## Scene 40.2 — The core alive but not free

Physical proof:
- core held in a low-power isolation cradle.
- no hidden weapon mode.
- response logs show service-layer recognition and an unregistered/mismatched operator state around Rian.

Mystery:
- system identifies seven service authorities/categories.
- it does not identify seven legendary machines.
- Authority A relates to local service/emergency access; Authority B relates to protected continuity/credential functions.

Rian future conflict:
- his memory associated “seven” with later relic/weapon myths.
- current evidence contradicts that simplification.

## Scene 40.3 — The offer

Imperial package:
- return/use of core under official protection.
- stronger education/medical support.
- possible Doran identity/treatment correction channels.
- protection from Academy/Helix purge.
- access to classified future-relevant records.

Price:
- secret Imperial service.
- exclusive disclosure of Rian’s anomaly/future knowledge/Archive behavior.
- acceptance of classified witness handling.
- Imperial priority over deployment and data.

Why attractive:
- real medicine, safety, records and power.
- could prevent larger future deaths.

## Scene 40.4 — A piece of true information

Observer provides:
- one verified event/record discrepancy relevant to Rian’s future assumptions or Academy cohort.
- enough to prove access/competence, not enough to resolve the series mystery.

Rian:
- does not reject immediately.
- asks for time and leaves without core.

Observer warning:
- if the matter becomes public before a custody structure exists, hardliners will take control and the observer cannot protect everyone.

End hook:
- Rian returns carrying an advantage he has not yet disclosed to the team.

Reward:
- core location, continuity clue and credible Imperial protection.

Cost:
- secret-service temptation and classified witness cost.

# Episode 41 — 혼자 가진 정답

Date:
- CY 742-04-26 to CY 742-04-27.

Episode function:
- test the opening rule against Rian’s instinct to centralize information.
- let each core member reject or reshape exclusive patronage for different reasons.
- preserve Imperial observer as a credible continuing actor.

## Scene 41.1 — Rian’s private plan

POV:
- Rian.

Plan:
- accept limited secret service long enough to recover the core and classified information.
- later renegotiate or escape after the team/frame is stronger.

Rational case:
- immediate Doran/medical support.
- core protection.
- larger future disaster intelligence.
- fragmented committee approvals could lose the opportunity.

Flaw:
- Rian decides others can be informed after the asset is secured.

## Scene 41.2 — Detected omission

Trigger:
- Serin sees an unexplained medical/records access gap or classification mark.
- Nera identifies core telemetry changed during Rian’s absence.
- Haren sees Rian’s resource assumptions suddenly include unavailable Imperial capacity.
- Mia notices stress/medical effects from the meeting.

Confrontation:
- they do not know the full offer but know relevant information is withheld.

Rian:
- initially gives only operationally necessary details.
- the team invokes the E17–20 disclosure/process rule.

## Scene 41.3 — Different refusals

Rian discloses the core location and offer.

Haren:
- rejects Doran/community correction negotiated through one person’s secret service.
- demands affected people/representatives have standing.

Nera:
- rejects core return without maintenance/design and component rights.

Serin:
- rejects a custody state that cannot be externally audited/corrected.
- also recognizes public disclosure now could trigger seizure.

Mia:
- accepts that classified protection may be medically safer for some people.
- rejects using treatment as automatic service consent.

Result:
- no simple unanimous moral rejection.

## Scene 41.4 — Counteroffer instead of denunciation

Decision:
- reject exclusive service.
- do not immediately expose the observer/core location.
- design a time-limited joint custody that gives each claimant a bounded real benefit.

Rian cost:
- loses exclusive information advantage and fastest path.

Team cost:
- assumes responsibility for a complex arrangement that may respond too slowly.

End hook:
- Academy reformers offer nominal collective ownership but cannot protect the core from Imperial/Helix seizure without external parties.

Reward:
- shared relevant information and team autonomy.

Cost:
- slower path and observer distrust.

# Episode 42 — 네 개의 자물쇠

Date:
- CY 742-04-28.

Episode function:
- design the provisional custody as operational machinery, not abstract compromise.
- give every party a benefit and a limit.
- show why distributed authority is slower and vulnerable.

## Scene 42.1 — Requirements before ownership

POV:
- Rian in planning/negotiation preparation.

Required functions:
- physical security/storage.
- activation/legacy authorization.
- certified power/cooling safety.
- mission/data purpose and audit.
- technical and medical stop.

Nera/Haren/Serin/Mia:
- translate moral demands into access, signatures, stop conditions and expiry.

Rian:
- pushes for an emergency path that does not require a full meeting every time.

## Scene 42.2 — Provisional structure

Proposed 30-day custody:

Lock 1 — Academy:
- physical cradle/hangar custody and mission-board scope.

Lock 2 — Imperial:
- core/legacy activation and protected-continuity monitoring.

Lock 3 — Helix:
- certified radiator/power safety envelope, inspection and liability.

Lock 4 — Student/Neutral audit:
- stated mission purpose, provenance/evidence mirror and no unrestricted data export.

Separate stops:
- Nera technical stop.
- Mia medical stop.
- Kara field/mission stop within assigned operation.

Emergency rule:
- limited immediate action can begin with predeclared conditions, but continuation/expansion requires missing approvals and post-action audit.

## Scene 42.3 — What each party hates

Academy:
- cannot deploy its own hull freely.

Imperial observer:
- cannot contain anomaly/continuity data exclusively.

Helix:
- cannot own all design/performance data or remotely lock whole frame.

Neutral:
- assumes audit burden without permanent enforcement capacity.

Students:
- cannot use the frame at will and inherit debt/oversight.

Haren:
- notes no party accepts because it is ideal; they accept because others can veto worse outcomes.

## Scene 42.4 — First rejection

One party, likely Helix Finance/Security or Academy Security, rejects the draft as too slow and leak-prone.

Counterpressure:
- core degradation/relocation deadline.
- radiator contract expiry.
- Academy wants the public symbol/frame.
- Imperial observer fears hardliner intervention.

End hook:
- the observer agrees to a physical transfer meeting only if Rian personally signs responsibility for unauthorized future use.

Reward:
- concrete custody architecture.

Cost:
- personal liability and slow response.

- recognizes Authority A and a partial Authority B pattern.

- Helix technician certifies bounded systems.
- Serin logs provenance/data scopes.

## Scene 44.2 — Mismatch test

Problem:
- abandoned manifold means the certified control model and current bypass disagree during combined movement/tool load.

Nera:
- lowers stable output and accepts 12–16-minute mixed envelope.
- refuses a higher performance setting offered through exclusive Helix/Imperial control.

Rian:
- must relearn a weaker configuration instead of assuming future 07 behavior.

Reward:

Cost:

Procedure:
- each lock/authority is exercised.
- one delayed approval nearly misses the test window, showing the cost of distributed control.
- emergency preauthorization is tested but remains narrow.

Core behavior:
- no hidden weapon.
- service diagnostics, identity mismatch and Authority A/B response.

## Scene 44.4 — External license proposal

Academy/parties agree in principle:
- conditional external team license tied to mission purpose, maintenance, medical and audit terms.

Student reaction:

End hook:

Reward:
