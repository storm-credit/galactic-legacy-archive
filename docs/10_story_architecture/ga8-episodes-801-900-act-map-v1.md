## 1. Grand-Act Contract

Working title:
- `기록전쟁`.

Range:
- Episodes 801–900.

Primary promise:

> 과거를 밝히는 탐험이 아니라, 어떤 기록을 보존·공개·수정·삭제하고 현재 신분·명령·항로·인격에 사용할지를 두고 싸우는 전쟁 속에서 은하유산록의 정체가 드러난다.

Grand-act question:

> 하나의 실행 가능한 기록이 없으면 사회가 무너지고, 하나의 기록이 있으면 사람이 지워질 때 어떤 기록체계를 작동시킬 것인가?

Visible goals:

1. stop immediate record seizures, deletions and identity/command collapse.
2. secure affected-person, source and custodian rights to key archives.
3. reach and stabilize Palimpsest regional mirrors.
4. determine which AI/composite/witness systems are persons, evidence or tools.
5. confirm Continuity Seed architecture and P-001 selection/regression mechanism.
6. prevent one faction from monopolizing Authority F/G and all current identity/command.
7. decide whether to activate a bounded Seed consensus during crisis.

Final reward:
- Archive/Continuity Seed origin and compression/value rules substantially confirmed.
- Authority F/plural archive access distributed with consent/provenance/audit.
- P-001 understood as unrecorded connector rather than chosen greatest hero.
- record rights framework established in partial operational form.
- several suppressed people/institutions/AI actors regain names and standing.

Final cost:
- bounded Authority G/Seed consensus is activated because plural systems cannot stop current collapse fast enough.
- activation restores real services and command while ranking/freezing current people, routes and technologies.
- some records are deleted, lost, exposed or forcibly synchronized.
- one AI/composite/person/community suffers irreversible copy/merge/deletion or identity fracture.
- P-001’s Archive connection becomes potential human corrective/central key.
- GA9 preservation regime begins with broad, sympathetic support.

# ACT 8A — 이름을 가진 열쇠 / Episodes 801–825

Act question:

> 피의 제독 기록을 고쳤더니 현재의 직책·배상·항로키와 다른 사람들의 신분까지 흔들릴 때, 누가 정정할 권한을 갖는가?

Act result:
- record correction becomes current material conflict.
- Authority F / Record Translation access path is identified.
- plural source/custodian coalition forms.

Act cost:
- identity, payment, office and route systems fail or diverge.
- one record/person/evidence set is irreversibly deleted, exposed or seized.
- Continuity central current-status layer gains support.

## 3. Subact 8A-1 — 정정된 이름, 멈춘 급여 / Episodes 801–806

Consequences:

- families/crews lose or gain claims.

Cost:

Hook:

Required access domains:

P-001 temptation:

Required refusal:

Reward:

Cost:

Hook:

## 6. Subact 8A-4 — 기록을 지키는 전투 / Episodes 820–825

- secure departure/routes/people and stop first mirror/source seizure.

Mode:
- fleet/courier/record-node operation using known ships/routes/07.

Conflict:

Reward:

Cost:

Act-ending trigger:
