## 1. Grand Act 1 Contract

Working title:
- `다섯 번째 답`

Range:
- Episodes 1–100.

Grand-act question:

> P-001은 미래의 인재와 기체를 자신의 전력으로 차지하지 않고, 그들이 스스로 선택하고 소유할 수 있는 첫 제도를 만들 수 있는가?

Grand-act visible goals:

1. survive the academy’s tests and contracts.
2. form a functioning cell around P-001, H-001, H-002, and two additional specialists.
3. recover and operate 07호 without surrendering all ownership to one patron.
4. protect H-001’s community and other students from clean erasure.
5. replace black-mission control with an unstable multi-party charter.

Grand-act final reward:
- limited external mission autonomy.
- reconstructed but still imperfect 07호.
- student representation and multi-party oversight.
- first durable team/institutional foothold.

Grand-act final cost:
- school becomes public political battleground.
- student body fragments among factions.
- P-001’s future timeline for academy cohort is unusable.
- team inherits debt, scrutiny, and enemies rather than freedom.

# ACT B — 기체는 누구의 것인가 / Episodes 21–45

Act question:

> 전투에서 구한 기체와 기록을 실제로 누가 소유하고 사용할 권리가 있는가?

Act result:
- 07호’s core and key components are recovered across different methods.
- no single party gains total ownership.
- the team receives conditional mission/maintenance rights.

Act cost:
- a new student scapegoat is punished for redirected institutional pressure.
- team signs debt/oversight obligations.
- public “fifth answer” myth creates imitators and critics.

## 7. B1 — 돌아온 죄수들 / Episodes 21–26

Entry:
- joint medical protection expires; team must return for inquiry.

Goals:
- prevent immediate isolation and preserve shared testimony.
- identify where 07호 components and data were taken.

Opposition:
- Operations blames procedural violation.
- Security alleges Independence collusion.
- Helix files property claim.
- Neutral Assembly only protects medical evidence, not criminal acts.

Key choices:
- H-001 refuses one unified story designed by P-001.
- each member gives separate testimony, preserving contradictions.

Rewards:
- public hearing instead of secret disposal.
- component-location list.
- field instructor becomes possible ally.

Costs:
- contradictions weaken legal defense.
- another cell’s student is selected as likely data thief/scapegoat.

Collection tease:
- 07 core at imperial security vault.
- radiator at Helix lab.
- industrial arm retained by academy.
- service-key copy with Neutral medical record.

Episode 26 hook:
- scapegoat student confesses to a crime they could not have committed.

## 8. B2 — 가짜 범인 / Episodes 27–32

Goals:
- determine why the student confessed and whether exposing truth worsens their family sentence.

Primary mode:
- investigation, student social networks, contract coercion.

New core supporting character candidate:
- `S-001 Data/Comms Cadet`, initially suspected informer.

Conflict:
- saving the scapegoat may expose D-001 evidence and H-001 community.
- scapegoat may prefer punishment to family harm.

Choice:
- team does not “rescue” by force; builds an alternative guarantor through Neutral/education staff and student mutual fund.

Reward:
- S-001 access to audit trail.
- evidence that trial data was sold before test completion.

Cost:
- student mutual fund incurs collective debt.
- Security shifts to hidden transfers rather than open punishment.

Collection:
- data authentication tool.
- student network and first noncombat institutional resource.

Episode 32 hook:
- component auction/transfer convoy departs before legal decision.

## 9. B3 — 부품 경매 / Episodes 33–38

Goals:
- keep Helix from dismantling/selling 07 components.

Primary mode:
- legal auction, salvage rights, industrial competition, limited infiltration.

Three acquisition paths:
- buy/assume debt for radiator.
- prove academy salvage labor claim for industrial arm.
- use imperial security interest to delay core transfer.

Character conflict:
- P-001 wants to steal everything during convoy vulnerability.
- H-002 insists physical theft without tools/rights recreates dependency.
- H-001 calculates which asset is actually needed to protect people next.

Reward:
- radiator and arm recovered under different ownership terms.
- H-002’s family-workshop design mark recognized in one component.

Cost:
- long-term debt and inspection access granted to Helix.
- one less flashy but vital component must be abandoned to another bidder.

Battle novelty:
- brief zero-g salvage/auction sabotage where destroying assets loses the case.

Episode 38 hook:
- imperial vault reports the 07 core “was never received.”

## 10. B4 — 사라진 코어 / Episodes 39–45

Goals:
- locate core without triggering imperial purge.

Truth:
- imperial observer diverted core because it responded to succession/continuity protocol.
- not simple theft; observer fears Helix and Archive Bloc.

Conflict:
- observer offers P-001 official protection and core return in exchange for secret service.
- academy reformers offer weaker collective ownership.

Choice:
- team rejects exclusive patronage but accepts a time-limited joint research custody with audit.

Climax:
- core placed back in frame under multiple physical/cryptographic locks.
- 07호 can operate, but mission authorization requires several parties.

Reward:
- first reconstructed frame and conditional external team license.
- imperial observer relationship established.

Cost:
- slow approvals; P-001 cannot deploy whenever he wants.
- scapegoat case ends in partial victory: student spared disappearance but transferred/chooses another faction.

Act ending:
- rumor of “fifth answer team” spreads to other cells; one imitation causes severe accident or hostage escalation.

Mystery advance:
- core recognizes a Continuity-era phrase and identifies seven service authorities, not seven machines.

# ACT C — 검은 병동 / Episodes 46–72

Act question:

> 불법·강압적 연구가 실제로 사람을 살리고 있다면, 폭로와 파괴만으로 피해자를 구할 수 있는가?

Act result:
- Black Ward cannot continue secret coercive trials under one bloc.
- shared medical oversight and patient representation established.
- H-001 protector/community truth partially recovered.
- two additional team roles emerge.

Act cost:
- patients’ identities and rare conditions become politically visible.
- treatments/parts disrupted.
- Archive/Continuity protocol detects P-001 more clearly.

## 11. C1 — 실패한 모방자들 / Episodes 46–51

Trigger:
- another cell copies the fifth-answer myth, attacks test apparatus, causes injury/security lockdown.

Goal:
- stop collective punishment and understand changed coercion method.

Conflict:
- P-001’s symbolic influence caused unintended harm.
- some students resent his special treatment and public myth.

New character candidate:
- `M-001 Medical/Rescue Cadet`, treating injured imitators and challenging heroic risk-taking.

Reward:
- medical network access.
- P-001 publicly states the fifth answer is not a trick, weakening his myth but accepting responsibility.

Cost:
- popularity and easy recruitment decrease.
- Security gains support for stricter monitoring.

Episode 51 hook:
- one injured student’s neural record shows Black Ward treatment not listed in public medicine.

## 12. C2 — 살아 있게 만든 감옥 / Episodes 52–58

Goals:
- determine what Black Ward does and whether patients can survive removal.

Discovery:
- some students have unstable neural conditions that public systems cannot treat.
- research mixes genuine therapy, military optimization, and identity/data extraction.

Conflict:
- M-001 refuses destructive rescue.
- H-002 finds treatment hardware dependent on proprietary Helix parts.
- H-001 learns protector/community members may be inside or linked to trials.

Reward:
- patient identities and treatment dependency map.
- medical/biotech hero trace.

Cost:
- evidence requires continued access cooperation with Research Bloc.

Ordinary-life anchor:
- patients’ routines, friendships, boredom, and agency; not horror-lab objects.

Episode 58 hook:
- a bounded AI/patient interface greets 07 core with obsolete continuity protocol.

## 13. C3 — 기억하는 환자 / Episodes 59–65

Goals:
- communicate with AI/witness without allowing either Research or Imperial actors to seize it.

Primary mystery:
- AI stores fragments from transferred identities/medical logs but is not a complete person archive.

Conflict:
- some patients want records erased.
- families want proof and restoration.
- Archive wants to categorize the AI/witness as legacy.

Choice:
- P-001 refuses automatic Archive registration/publication; patient consent limits what is copied.

Reward:
- continuity-protocol phrase, protector transfer route, evidence of administrative death identities.
- AI/witness becomes independent conditional ally, not inventory.

Cost:
- missing data cannot be recovered because consent or deletion is respected.
- Archive set remains incomplete.

Episode 65 hook:
- protector is alive under a legally closed identity but will die if treatment/supply chain is cut.

## 14. C4 — 누구를 먼저 치료할 것인가 / Episodes 66–72

Goals:
- save protector/patients during Helix supply freeze and security purge.

Pressure:
- insufficient treatment units.
- revealing patients triggers seizure by several agencies.
- P-001 cannot save all personally.

Independent plans:
- H-001 designs triage with patients/community representatives.
- H-002 creates temporary open adapter but warns of unequal risk.
- M-001 owns medical priority and may reject P-001’s preferred target.

Climax:
- patients vote/choose risk and data sharing terms.
- team secures Neutral medical corridor and temporary multi-party oversight.

Reward:
- protector survives but remains in treatment/community role, not simply reunited.
- Black Ward coercive mission authority suspended.
- M-001 and AI/witness enter wider cast under conditions.

Cost:
- research data leaks partially; outside powers now know valuable subjects exist.
- Helix/Imperial/Independence teams prepare intervention.
- school’s financial survival worsens.

Act ending hook:
- funding, supply, and legal collapse will close school in 30 days unless one patron takes full control.

Mystery advance:
- Archive’s “dead” records are legal identity closures, not only physical death.

# ACT D — 열세 번째 학교의 포위 / Episodes 73–100

Act question:

> 학교를 파괴하거나 한 선한 세력에게 넘기지 않고, 학생이 실제 권리를 가진 제도로 바꿀 수 있는가?

Act result:
- temporary multi-party charter with student/staff representation, exit and audit mechanisms, mission limits.
- academy continues but no longer one patron’s hidden asset.
- P-001 cell gains external mission autonomy.

Act cost:
- students choose different factions and some are forcibly/voluntarily transferred before charter applies.
- campus damaged/partly lost.
- black missions continue elsewhere.
- all factions now openly compete for school graduates, records, and hardware.

## 15. D1 — 폐교는 해방이 아니다 / Episodes 73–78

Trigger:
- budget/supply cutoff and closure order.

Consequences of simple closure:
- students returned to prisons, military units, corporate labor, or stateless status.
- treatments stop.
- staff/jobs/nearby node defense collapse.

Conflict:
- Independence radicals call for mass breakout.
- Helix offers full funding for data/contract control.
- Imperial Intelligence offers national-security takeover.
- Neutral Assembly cannot finance the institution alone.

Choice:
- student cells survey actual preferences rather than assume all want escape.

Reward:
- first student assembly and preference map.

Cost:
- divisions become visible; no united oppressed class.

Episode 78 hook:
- one internal bloc has already signed a secret takeover/evacuation contract.

## 16. D2 — 다섯 개의 인수안 / Episodes 79–84

Each proposal gives real benefits and costs:

1. Imperial takeover: citizenship/order, secret-service control.
2. Helix contract school: technology/jobs, data/debt ownership.
3. Independence liberation: freedom/land, military instability and conscription.
4. Neutral trusteeship: law/medical protection, high cost and limited defense.
5. Internal continuation: familiarity, existing coercion and insolvency.

Goal:
- negotiate components of a charter rather than pick one package.

P-001 flaw pressure:
- tempted to secretly rank students by future value and place them in best proposal.

Choice:
- refuses to reveal/act on future ranks; supports individual/collective choice even when losing heroes.

Reward:
- provisional coalition terms.

Cost:
- valuable future hero chooses rival faction and leaves P-001’s reach.

Episode 84 hook:
- secret contract triggers armed transfer before negotiations finish.

## 17. D3 — 학교는 전장이다 / Episodes 85–92

Primary mode:
- multi-sided academy siege/evacuation across rings, service spine, hangars, Black Ward.

Victory conditions:
- prevent mass casualty/forced transfer.
- preserve life support, medical patients, records, and exit routes.
- avoid destroying node/campus.

Battle roles:
- P-001/07호: movement control and defense, not total victory.
- H-001: evacuation/logistics and faction route negotiation.
- H-002: power/repair and denial of remote lockouts.
- M-001/AI witness: medical zones and identity authentication.
- S-001 data cadet: communications and public record.
- field instructor: commands staff/students independently.
- enemy ace/Independence representative: can oppose both forced transfer and central seizure.

Key reversals:
- an ostensibly hostile faction protects a ring for its own constituency.
- a reformer may sacrifice another group to preserve institution.
- 07호 cannot be everywhere; another pilot/team must win a decisive front.

Reward:
- armed transfer stalled.
- multiple blocs recognize need for joint authority.

Cost:
- campus damage, deaths/injuries, records lost, students depart.
- P-001’s unusual command identity exposed widely.

Episode 92 hook:
- central reactor/lattice safety and legal authority will fail unless one entity takes emergency command within hours.

## 18. D4 — 권한을 나누는 전투 / Episodes 93–100

Problem:
- single emergency command is efficient and legally available to Imperial/Helix/Operations actors.
- plural control risks delayed catastrophe.

Plan components:
- H-001: segmented life-support/logistics command and minimum service.
- H-002: multiple signed technical controls and manual fallback.
- Neutral representative: temporary trusteeship, audit, liability.
- student/staff assembly: consent and exit representation.
- P-001: limited defense coordination, sunset clause, no permanent master key.

Climax:
- simultaneous defense, repair, evacuation, and public legal transmission.
- P-001 can seize one central key through 07/Archive but chooses not to retain it.
- another actor’s autonomous decision prevents failure, proving system can work beyond him.

Grand-act victory:
- `제13학원 임시공동헌장` established.
- black missions require multi-party approval/independent audit.
- student representatives and exit/contract review rights.
- mission team receives limited external license under multiple sponsors.

Grand-act cost:
- charter is slow and underfunded.
- some powerful actors refuse and establish competing programs elsewhere.
- P-001’s cohort no longer follows known history.
- school myth and heroes become public commodities.

Final collection state:
- 07호 physically restored enough for external missions, but ownership/design/command split.
- core team of five roles formed without permanent loyalty guarantee.
- records preserved in fragments.

Final mystery/payoff:
- Continuity protocol recognizes charter as unstable/low-preservation solution.
- Archive’s thirteen count changes again based on people who left, institutions created, and unnamed labor.

Episode 100 hook / GA2 launch:
- charter’s first independent mission contract arrives with an obsolete utility escort vessel, an impossible debt, and a salvage manifest containing another 07 service-key trace.
