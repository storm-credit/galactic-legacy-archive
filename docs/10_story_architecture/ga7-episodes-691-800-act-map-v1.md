## 1. Grand-Act Contract

Working title:
- `피의 제독이 없는 전쟁`.

Range:
- Episodes 691–800.

Primary promise:

> H-001이 미래의 학살자라는 기록을 피하는 이야기가 아니라, 항로를 열고 닫고 파괴하는 권한을 실제로 갖게 된 뒤 어떤 선택을 자기 이름으로 책임지는지 보여준다.

Grand-act question:

> 점령을 막기 위해 항로를 파괴하는 것이 정당할 수 있다면, 누가 그 결정을 내리고 항로 밖에서 죽는 사람에게 어떻게 책임질 것인가?

Visible goals:

1. stabilize post-Orpheus routes, holdouts and minimum services.
2. form or reform the Mutual Route Federation.
3. prevent Continuity monopoly and indiscriminate scorched denial.
4. identify who controls denial keys, titles and delegated orders.
5. survive a cascading route war across several nodes/regions.
6. decompose the Blood Admiral record without erasing H-001’s real harmful choice.

Final reward:
- route federation with bounded closure/denial authority, minimum obligations and distributed key custody.
- several routes/regions survive capture or total centralization.
- Blood Admiral record decompressed into H-001, office/title, doctrine, successor commanders, signatures and unresolved incidents.
- H-001 accepts specific responsibility while rejecting total mythic guilt.
- public/technical record systems become the direct object of GA8 conflict.

Final cost:
- one D4 denial ordered by H-001 strands or exposes a large population and damages dependent regions.
- routes/nodes and shared infrastructure are permanently lost.
- H-001 loses authority, allies, office or relationships even if federation survives.
- Continuity corridors gain support because federation/denial failures are real.
- some incidents and victims remain unresolved.
- war becomes a fight over records, identity and historical authority.

# ACT 7A — 닫히는 항로들 / Episodes 691–715

Act question:

> 오르페우스 이후 각 지역이 스스로 항로를 닫기 시작할 때, 자치권을 존중하면서도 연쇄 기아·의료붕괴를 막을 수 있는가?

Act result:
- post-Orpheus route/service facts are linked across regions.
- Mutual Route Federation gains a temporary emergency mandate.
- H-001 receives bounded coordinator authority rather than personal control.

Act cost:
- secure members resist burdens or close routes.
- one local closure/priority decision causes named downstream suffering.
- central Continuity corridor demonstrates superior reliability.
