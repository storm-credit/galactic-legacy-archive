# Episode 73 — 남은 날짜는 서른

Date:
- CY 742-05-28.

Episode function:
- convert the Black Ward victory into a whole-school survival clock.
- make closure’s immediate effects physical and numerical.
- stop Rian from treating the school as a finished battlefield.

## Scene 73.1 — The closure notice

POV:
- Rian in Ring A/campus broadcast.

Notice:
- current external funding/authority will not support the Academy beyond the 30-day emergency period without a recognized operator/package.
- assets, students, patients and staff must be transferred, closed or funded.

Public reaction:
- relief from some students who believe school will finally end.
- panic among patients/staff/families.
- recruiters/patrons begin moving.

Rian:
- initially sees closure as a chance to break Academy control.

## Scene 73.2 — What closes first

Haren/operations accounting:
- payroll, food, medical, repair and legal transfer clocks differ.
- shutting training does not instantly save enough because life support, patients and staff remain.
- staff departure reduces ability to keep the station safe.

Physical examples:
- a meal/ration shift cut.
- workshop hours reduced.
- a patient treatment procurement delayed.
- a staff family housing/credit notice.

## Scene 73.3 — People returned to where

Possible simple closure routes:
- prison/correction return.
- military assignment.
- corporate labor/medical contract.
- stateless/unrecognized release without transport.
- family/community return that may not accept/support them.

Yori/Aven/student voices:
- “release” is not one safe outcome.

Amel:
- asks who keeps 112 patients/wards alive during closure.

## Scene 73.4 — The first recruiters

Imperial and Helix preliminary teams publish benefits/eligibility.
- some students sign interest forms immediately.
- others call it takeover.

End hook:
- Academy administration says it needs one package/operator, not a student debate, before payroll and security authority fail.

Reward:
- exact closure clock and material stakes.

Cost:
- outside recruitment starts before collective process.

# Episode 74 — 자유롭게 버려지는 사람들

Date:
- CY 742-05-29.

Episode function:
- show closure consequences through several legal/social categories.
- make different people prefer different external packages.
- establish why staying can be a rational choice.

## Scene 74.1 — Darin’s package

POV:
- Rian visiting/encountering Darin’s squad.

Darin:
- chooses Imperial interest process.
- reasons: squad continuity, citizenship, family movement, credible defense and qualification.

Rian future memory:
- identifies Darin as high future value.
- temptation to warn/manipulate the choice.

He withholds the rank/future label for now but tries strategic persuasion.

Darin:
- asks who protects the squad if Rian’s experimental structure fails.

## Scene 74.2 — Sera’s contract

Sera:
- accepts Helix screening/contract negotiation because of dependents, treatment and technical career.
- demonstrates a current Academy tool/safety shortage Helix can solve.

Nera:
- challenges ownership/data terms but cannot honestly offer equivalent pay/treatment/certification.

No villain conversion:
- Sera’s decision is rational and not final total loyalty.

## Scene 74.3 — Aven’s exit

Aven:
- wants an Independence/federalist route or enforceable right to leave.
- says another temporary internal process can be canceled after danger passes.

Constraint:
- C-9 disciplinary/medical status limits movement.
- Independence cannot guarantee treatment/transport for everyone.

## Scene 74.4 — People who want the school

Bram/Kara/ordinary staff:
- want to preserve the institution because jobs, teaching, maintenance, treatment and current relationships are real.
- admit current structure harmed students.

Rian realization:
- “destroy the school” and “free the students” are not equivalent.

End hook:
- staff/operations begin preparing category-based transfer lists before students have given current preferences.

Reward:
- package preferences embodied in people.

Cost:
- transfer bureaucracy moves faster than reform.

# Episode 75 — 누가 누구를 대표하는가

Date:
- CY 742-05-30.

Episode function:
- build a preference process without pretending perfect democracy or anonymity.
- limit Rian/Haren’s representational monopoly.
- expose missing/nonresponding groups.

## Scene 75.1 — Rian’s assembly proposal

POV:
- Rian.

Proposal:
- one mass student vote on takeover package.

Problems raised:
- staff/workers/patients also keep the school operating.
- patients cannot have treatment decided by majority.
- people need conditional/multiple preferences, not one faction identity.
- closed identities and fear make perfect anonymous voting impossible.
- receiving capacity changes the meaning of choice.

Rian accepts a more complex process.

## Scene 75.2 — Survey design

Haren/Yori/Serin/patient/worker/staff representatives define separate questions:
- stay/leave.
- acceptable sponsors.
- family/cell/treatment/work conditions.
- military/industrial obligations.
- data/identity terms.
- unacceptable outcomes.

Serin:
- designs bounded access and evidence that responses exist without publishing vulnerable individual choices.

Risk:
- authorities/recruiters can still infer groups and pressure them.

## Scene 75.3 — Representation challenges

Yori:
- challenged as C-9 participant.

Amel:
- states no patient representative can authorize another patient’s transfer.

Bram:
- rejects a student-only plan that assumes workers will operate unpaid.

Kara:
- accepts instructor authority must shrink and staff may choose to leave.

Rian:
- assigned as witness/technical/mission figure, not chair or student representative.

## Scene 75.4 — First missing responses

Nonresponse categories:
- fear of retaliation.
- closed/missing identity.
- incapacity.
- no acceptable package.
- distrust of all organizers.

End hook:
- someone accesses or leaks a partial preference grouping, allowing targeted recruitment and intimidation.

Reward:
- legitimate but imperfect preference mechanism.

Cost:
- data vulnerability and loss of Rian control.

Episode function:

- show every package has supporters and concrete gaps.

- some receive citizenship/service pathways.

## Scene 76.4 — Internal continuation ration/work shift

Students/staff run an emergency work/ration plan.
- demonstrates local capability.
- also shows exhaustion, deferred maintenance and missing money.

End hook:
- leaked preference categories trigger family/sponsor pressure and one student changes/withdraws a response.

Reward:

Cost:

# Episode 77 — 삼십 퍼센트의 다수

Date:
- CY 742-06-02.

Episode function:
- reveal no majority and conditional overlaps.
- give key representatives distinct interpretations.
- create a hybrid-negotiation mandate without claiming consensus.

## Scene 77.1 — Student result

POV:
- Rian at witnessed summary.

Valid student responses:
- 1,442.

Primary results:
- internal/hybrid 436 / 30.2%.
- Imperial 331 / 23.0%.
- Helix 249 / 17.3%.
- Neutral 182 / 12.6%.
- Independence 137 / 9.5%.
- other/undecided 107 / 7.4%.

Meaning:
- the largest group is not a majority.

## Scene 77.2 — Conditional overlaps

Findings:
- many internal respondents accept Imperial/Helix money under changed rights.
- many Imperial/Helix respondents demand cell/family/appeal terms.
- Independence supporters disagree on destination/military duty.
- Neutral respondents reject indefinite underfunded limbo.

Haren:
- argues the useful result is a requirements map, not winner selection.

## Scene 77.3 — Other groups

Staff/workers and patient patterns differ.
- workers prioritize payroll/safety/control.
- patients split among Neutral, Helix, Imperial and current care.
- staff are more willing than students to accept stable external control.

No student mandate can override them.

## Scene 77.4 — Hybrid bargaining decision

Representatives agree:
- negotiate components across packages.
- preserve individual departures already chosen.
- seek temporary joint operation rather than one winner.

Rian:
- recognizes a hybrid is slower and may cause more immediate loss.

End hook:
- preference data reveals that 286 priority cadets already appear on a separate Imperial transfer schedule.

Reward:
- real preference map and hybrid mandate.

Cost:
- factions know exactly whom to recruit/pressure; secret list exposed.

POV:

Signers:

- waiting risks node, patients, payroll and faction seizure.

Rian:
- sees future-value classifications behind part of the list.

- identify incomplete current-person/medical/appeal data.

Negotiation deadline:

Reward:
