## 3. Scale and Travel

Approximate dimensions:

- White Dock overall span: 1.4 km.
- Archive structure width: 260–340 m.
- each repair arm: 420–620 m usable exterior length.
- Worker Habitat ↔ Archive pressure connector: 180–260 m.
- School Security Berth ↔ Archive exterior: 350–520 m.
- Repair Arm B truss clearance: 20–60 m around dense equipment; wider outside.

Frame movement:

- open exterior crossing of 300 m is physically fast, but safe acceleration/braking near structure takes tens of seconds and major heat/propellant.
- full-output direct chase is prohibited by civilians, docked tug, cables and structure.
- 07 and Ern can reposition across one local exterior zone in 20–60 seconds under combat stress.
- interior archive movement requires dismounted teams, utility frames or drones.

Human/pressure movement:

- one pressure segment evacuation through a controlled connector: 3–12 minutes depending injury and door cycle.
- manual bulkhead override: 40 seconds–4 minutes.
- medical movement through damaged connector requires repeated stabilization, not one instant opening.
