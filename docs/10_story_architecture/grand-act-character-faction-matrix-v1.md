## 1. Operating Rule

- 주인공 아크와 세력 아크는 같은 사건을 다른 목표로 통과한다.
- 주요 인물과 세력은 P-001이 없는 동안에도 계획·실패·승계를 가진다.
- 한 대액트에서 모든 핵심 세력이 전면 등장하지 않는다. `전면 / 보조 / 배경시계 / 휴면`으로 노출 강도를 조절한다.
- 동맹은 충성상태가 아니라 현재 목표의 일치다.
- 지도자 교체 후에도 세력의 자원·부채·이념은 남는다.

Exposure legend:
- `F`: front-stage conflict or viewpoint.
- `S`: supporting active force.
- `C`: clock moves mostly off-stage.
- `D`: dormant/legacy influence.

## 12. Independence Test

A core actor passes only if removing P-001 for one grand act still leaves:

- a goal.
- a conflict with another actor.
- an irreversible choice.
- a gain and a cost.
- consequences that P-001 later encounters.

Examples:
- H-001 GA4 builds federalist network without P-001.
- H-002 GA7 chooses supply conditions between rival camps.
- Neutral Assembly GA6 decides corridor access based on debt/trust.
- Helix GA9 splits over subordination to Seed.
- Academy GA8 confronts continuity-selection roots.

## 13. Spotlight Limits

Per grand act:
- 3–5 front-stage core actors/factions.
- 2–4 supporting.
- others move by clock, report, consequence, or representative.

No act may give all nine major actors equal scene time.

Suggested front-stage:
- GA1: P-001, H-001, H-002, Academy, Helix/Independence representatives.
- GA2: P-001, H-002, H-001/community, Helix, Neutral routes.
- GA3: H-001, H-002, P-001, local government, Independence/Imperial claims.
- GA4: P-001, Imperial Intelligence, claimants, A-001 seed, Neutral mediators.
- GA5: P-001, captains, H-002, fleet factions, Helix.
- GA6: P-001, H-001, Neutral Assembly, A-001, civilians.
- GA7: H-001, P-001, Independence successors, H-002, memory factions.
