# Episode 52 — 검은 병동은 병원이다

Date:
- CY 742-05-08.

Episode function:
- break the expectation of a simple torture lab.
- show real treatment, routines, staff limits and access control.
- make the team’s presence intrusive to current patients.

## Scene 52.1 — Transfer through BW-A

POV:
- Rian.

Physical process:
- identity/access check, decontamination, low-gravity transfer and restricted devices.
- team separated by role and only reunites in approved common area.

Rian expectation:
- searches for guards, hidden cells and signs of imminent abuse.

What he sees:
- medical staff managing symptoms, exercise/rehab and ordinary supplies.
- patients arguing over meals, schedules, privacy and visitors.
- security exists but is not the only visible function.

## Scene 52.2 — Patients do not welcome rescuers

Amel Rook and other patients:
- challenge why students/officials are suddenly allowed to inspect their care after creating a public incident.

Mia:
- states the narrow purpose and what the team cannot promise.

Rian:
- begins explaining the wider coercive system.
- Amel stops him and asks whether he can replace tonight’s treatment if the Ward is shut.

Effect:
- rescue/destruction plan loses moral simplicity.

## Scene 52.3 — Leta’s current choice

Leta:
- stabilized but weak, capable of limited decision.
- confirms she previously received Ward treatment and was told the public record would remain simple to preserve placement.
- did not understand every data/identity consequence.

Current options:
- remain for stabilization.
- transfer to public clinic with higher risk/insufficient protocol.
- consent to specific review while refusing full history copy.

Choice:
- stays temporarily and permits only compatibility/current-treatment information.

## Scene 52.4 — A door with patients behind it

Haren sees:
- protected/closed-identity transfer list categories.
- cannot access names without patient/custody permission.

Rian:
- recognizes one future “dead” hero/support record pattern but is unable to identify the person cleanly.

End hook:
- a maintenance/medical supply alert shows some patients cannot survive a rapid Ward closure or mass transfer.

Reward:
- real patient access and Leta’s current consent.

Cost:
- limited data and moral/operational dependency.

# Episode 54 — 치료와 실험 사이

Date:
- CY 742-05-10.

Episode function:
- map overlapping treatment/research programs.
- prevent one clean line between care and exploitation.
- give Nera/Mia/Serin separate questions and limits.

## Scene 54.1 — The dependency map

POV:
- Rian in a bounded review meeting, but Mia leads.

Mia/clinicians classify:
- who needs what current treatment and when.
- which systems are essential care, optional optimization or physically shared.
- what would happen if specific programs stop.

Finding:
- research rigs and treatment units share power, models, parts and staff.
- shutting “experiments” off immediately would reduce care capacity.

## Scene 54.2 — Nera’s hardware audit

Nera:
- examines interface hardware with Ward engineers.
- finds proprietary Helix consumables, older local modifications and undocumented emergency service interfaces.

Truth:
- the proprietary system is safer for known profiles.
- undocumented modifications kept obsolete patients/equipment working.
- some research data improved treatment, while ownership hides labor and makes alternatives difficult.

## Scene 54.3 — Serin’s record layers

Serin distinguishes:
- current clinical record.
- protected identity pointer.
- research data rights.
- custody/sentence order.
- family/public record.

One patient can have contradictory effective identities across these layers.

Risk:
- merging everything into one public file can expose or invalidate treatment/custody protections.

Rian:
- realizes “restore the true record” is not a single safe action.

## Scene 54.4 — The military value list

A restricted program shows patients classified for future frame/command/technical value.

Rian future knowledge:
- recognizes names/roles compressed into later hero/support histories.

Conflict:
- he wants to preserve people from future loss.
- patients may not want heroic/military restoration.

End hook:
- one listed program uses the same obsolete continuity phrase/authority family as 07.

Reward:
- treatment/research/identity architecture.

Cost:
- no clean shutdown and increased strategic interest.
