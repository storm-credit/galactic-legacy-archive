## 1. Grand-Act Contract

Working title:
- `왕관 없는 계승전`.

Range:
- Episodes 331–450.

Primary promise:

> 제국의 왕좌를 누가 차지하는지가 아니라, 서로 진짜인 명령들이 동시에 내려왔을 때 어떤 기관과 사람이 무엇을 계속해야 하는지 결정하는 정치·정보·군사전이다.

Grand-act question:

> 하나의 정답이 없는 승계위기에서, P-001은 자신이 아는 미래를 근거로 왕을 고르지 않고도 제국의 붕괴를 늦출 수 있는가?

Visible goals:

1. deliver and preserve Ardis/provincial evidence through the Core.
2. determine what each authentic succession protocol actually grants.
3. keep civil services, payroll, medicine and military orders functioning.
4. protect witnesses/records from Intelligence internal war.
5. prevent any claimant from monopolizing crown, executive, military, Registry and anchor keys.
6. build a time-limited continuity compact without pretending succession is settled.

Final reward:
- public evidence that multiple protocols are genuinely valid.
- bounded roles for crown, regency, military protector, continuity custody and provincial consultation.
- temporary Imperial Continuity Compact.
- strategic rank/recognition for P-001’s coalition without personal kingmaker authority.
- access to core military/administrative networks and fleet relationships needed for GA5.

Final cost:
- some fleets/provinces/claimants reject compact.
- Intelligence and military institutions fracture.
- witnesses/records are lost or remain sealed.
- one claimant’s supporters are violently or administratively excluded.
- civil war becomes delayed and structured rather than prevented.
- P-001’s future history of the Empire is no longer reliable.

# ACT 4A — 여섯 개의 명령 / Episodes 331–360

Act question:

> 서로 다른 기관이 모두 진짜 인장을 가진 명령을 보냈을 때, 무엇을 먼저 확인하고 무엇을 일단 멈춰야 하는가?

Act result:
- six-message packet and key witnesses are verified as genuinely contradictory.
- protagonists enter the Core as protected but politically contested courier/witness group.
- claimant solutions become visible.

Act cost:
- evidence and people are split across institutions for safety.
- Intelligence blocs identify Archive/07/Ardis network as strategic threat/asset.
- one witness or record channel is lost/compromised.

## 3. Subact 4A-1 — 도착하기 전에 결정된 것들 / Episodes 331–337

Entry:
- Ardis receives six messages and must respond before P-001 can reach the Core.

Goal:
- choose what Ardis recognizes, delays or transmits while preserving local compact.

Conflict:
- each message demands incompatible action: accession recognition, regency obedience, military freeze, Continuity suspension, provincial consultation or covenant review.
- Ardis factions differ.
- P-001 is still physically distant or preparing transit.

Required local agency:
- Ardis—not P-001—issues a narrow response: no final accession recognition, continued services, acceptance of provincial consultation, preservation of evidence and limited lawful orders.

P-001 role:
- advises source confidence and operational consequences; cannot dictate.

Reward:
- Ardis’s authenticated response and evidence courier mandate.

Cost:
- Core labels Ardis either rebellious, strategically essential or both.
- one faction inside Ardis records dissent/alternative recognition.

Hook:
- a Coreward courier carrying a missing timestamp/witness is intercepted or disappears before rendezvous.

## 4. Subact 4A-2 — 증거가 먼저 죽는다 / Episodes 338–345

Goal:
- recover/protect witness/evidence chain while traveling toward C11/C03/C06.

Primary mode:
- route pursuit, courier transfer, boarding, counterintelligence and legal custody.

Evidence classes:
- sovereign medical/incapacity record.
- designation signing/transmission record.
- Registry correction.
- General Staff emergency threshold logs.
- Continuity conflict hashes.
- founding-covenant institutional lineage.

Conflict:
- different Intelligence blocs want different pieces preserved, sealed or destroyed.
- Neutral/Ardis custody conflicts with Imperial warrants.
- first ship is outclassed in Core security environment.

Action:
- P-001/07 protects transfer without destroying pursuers.
- S-001 must choose between duplicating evidence broadly and protecting witnesses.

Reward:
- one crucial witness or physical archive reaches protected custody.

Cost:
- another evidence chain is lost, sealed or publicly leaked without context.
- crew/courier identity exposed.

Hook:
- the leaked fragment appears to prove one claimant, triggering partial mobilization/ceremony before full review.

## 5. Subact 4A-3 — 네 명의 합법 / Episodes 346–353

Goal:
- meet or observe the four front-stage claimants through active institutions rather than exposition.

Scenes:
- Registered Heir addresses public/court after leak.
- Regent keeps payroll/medical cabinet working.
- Protector prevents unit clash or route seizure.
- Custodian blocks an irreversible key transfer/forged order.

P-001 realization:
- each claimant’s competence solves a different real problem.
- Archive/future labels are contradictory and curated.

Conflict:
- each seeks endorsement, evidence or custody.
- H-001/H-002/S-001/M-001 receive separate offers aligned to their expertise.

Reward:
- limited access/relationship with all four solutions.

Cost:
- coalition unity strained; accepting one service/protection creates visible alignment.

Ordinary-life anchor:
- Core civilian impacts: salary, medicine, pension, family and route access under conflicting authorities.

Hook:
- Registry and Sacred Archive schedule a joint protocol hearing, but General Staff and Continuity offices demand security restrictions.

## 6. Subact 4A-4 — 진짜 문서들의 재판 / Episodes 354–360

Goal:
- authenticate scope, timing and conditions of six protocols.

Not a courtroom-only sequence:
- physical archive access.
- witness protection.
- system reconstruction.
- competing public broadcasts.
- security and route threats.

Discovery:
- no core protocol is a simple forgery.
- death/incapacity timing and designation scope genuinely diverge.
- emergency and provincial thresholds are partly met.

Required decision:
- institutions certify contradiction publicly enough to prevent one claimant from claiming settled truth.

Reward:
- `다중정통성 인증 / Multiple Legitimacy Finding` or equivalent public result.

Cost:
- markets/fleets/provinces panic or choose sides.
- Purge bloc attacks/isolates one witness/institution.
- Continuity suspension expands temporarily.

Act-ending trigger:
- state services begin failing because nobody knows which orders/payments/appointments to obey; Regent proposes emergency executive continuity while Heir prepares limited accession and Protector mobilizes.

# ACT 4B — 황제 없이 돌아가는 나라 / Episodes 361–390

Act question:

> 왕이 정해지지 않아도 병원·급여·식량·법원은 돌아가야 한다면, 누가 임시 행정을 맡고 언제 내려와야 하는가?

Act result:
- civil-service continuity framework keeps core services operating.
- claimant roles and public constituencies sharpen.
- partial accession/emergency measures reveal how temporary power becomes permanent.

Act cost:
- executive/treasury/registry controls expand.
- provinces and military units receive unequal treatment.
- one major service or payment crisis creates unrest and coercive response.

## 7. Subact 4B-1 — 월급을 주는 사람이 황제다 / Episodes 361–367

Trigger:
- Treasury and ministries receive conflicting appointment/payment authority.

Goal:
- keep salaries, pensions, hospitals and food contracts functioning.

Regent plan:
- emergency civil-service continuity order under SP-02/03.

Heir plan:
- accession/royal warrant to restore a clear signatory.

Custodian:
- freezes irreversible account/key changes.

Protector:
- prioritizes military/frontier payroll.

Conflict:
- every choice privileges constituencies.
- provinces/Ardis demand transparent criteria and local continuation authority.

Required event:
- one service sector accepts the wrong or incomplete authority and creates human consequences.

Reward:
- temporary dual/multi-signature payment and service scheme.

Cost:
- slower access, exclusions and emergency executive data control.

Hook:
- one claimant’s supporters organize a public accession/recognition event to break paralysis.

## 8. Subact 4B-2 — 반쪽 대관식 / Episodes 368–375

Goal:
- manage or prevent partial accession from becoming irreversible while respecting public legitimacy.

Event:
- Registered Heir completes some but not all ceremonial/legal steps.

Why it matters:
- public/order confidence improves in aligned districts/institutions.
- rival institutions cannot ignore visible crown claim.

Conflict:
- stopping ceremony by force validates military/Continuity domination.
- allowing it pressures Registry/General Staff/provinces.

P-001 choice:
- refuses to publicly endorse sole accession but protects ceremony/civilians from attack/purge.

Helpful claimant act:
- Heir limits retaliation and agrees ceremony does not settle all executive/military powers.

Harmful effect:
- supporters treat partial accession as complete and target rivals.

Reward:
- ceremonial legitimacy separated from immediate total control.

Cost:
- public split deepens; one royal/court adviser uses pressure/violence or evidence suppression.

Hook:
- provincial/field commands refuse or condition recognition; food/route flows are threatened.

## 9. Subact 4B-3 — 지방은 누구에게 세금을 내는가 / Episodes 376–383

Goal:
- secure provincial food, tax, route and administrative continuation without coercive recognition.

Provincial Compact:
- demands voting/consultation and limits on Core emergency powers.

Regent:
- needs revenue and service compliance.

Protector:
- wants logistics/command.

Heir:
- wants recognition.

Custodian:
- demands authenticated local keys and may suspend noncompliant routes.

H-001 role:
- designs burden-sharing/minimum obligations but faces wealthy-region veto and weak-region dependence.

Conflict:
- provinces bargain selfishly; some threaten secession or withhold food.
- Core public sees provincial pressure as blackmail.

Reward:
- provisional provincial consultation/continuation mechanism.

Cost:
- concessions, unequal burdens and delayed shipments.
- one weak province is deprioritized or exposed.

Hook:
- military units/fleets begin choosing payment/recognition chains; Protector invokes stronger command.

## 10. Subact 4B-4 — 유능한 섭정 / Episodes 384–390

Goal:
- test Regent’s emergency administration under real simultaneous crises.

Crisis stack:
- hospital/food/credit disruption.
- fleet payroll/order split.
- public unrest/propaganda.
- witness/evidence security.

Regent competence:
- resolves several problems faster than plural process.
- M-001 and H-001 see lives materially saved.

Harmful move:
- classification, fund freeze, appointment or compulsory service used to maintain execution.

P-001 conflict:
- Regent offers strategic office and an efficient chain.
- accepting would stabilize operations but make him exceptional central commander again.

Outcome:
- Regent gains temporary executive legitimacy but must face audit/sunset demands.

Cost:
- opposition arrested/excluded or services tied to compliance.
- Continuity/Protector offices demand equal emergency powers.

Act-ending trigger:
- coordinated Intelligence/Purge/military move seeks to settle crisis through “lawful emergency” seizure of key institutions and P-001/07/records.

# ACT 4C — 가장 합법적인 쿠데타 / Episodes 391–420

Act question:

> 법에 근거한 비상조치가 모든 경쟁자를 동시에 무력화하면, 그것은 국가보존인가 가장 합법적인 쿠데타인가?

Act result:
- no claimant/office gains all crown, executive, military, Registry and anchor powers.
- Intelligence internal split becomes public/operational.
- key institutions and evidence survive in distributed custody.

Act cost:
- arrests, defections, casualties and destroyed/sealed records.
- one claimant/solution loses important institutional base.
- military/provincial fracture becomes unavoidable.

## 11. Subact 4C-1 — 보호 명령 / Episodes 391–397

Trigger:
- several Intelligence blocs issue contradictory protection/capture orders for claimants, witnesses and P-001 network.

Goal:
- determine who is protecting whom and preserve independent custody.

Mode:
- safe houses, institution corridors, route transfers, comm compromise and small-team action.

Conflict:
- R-IMP-001 and field offices must choose bloc/order.
- S-001 sees valid credentials on harmful commands.
- Custodian may support seizure as preservation.

Required choice:
- coalition splits evidence/people across trusted but conflicting institutions rather than keep one master archive.

Reward:
- some witnesses/records survive outside any one claimant.

Cost:
- one person/record is captured, disappears or voluntarily enters protected custody.
- team cannot know all locations.

Hook:
- Protector announces emergency military command to prevent institution attacks and fleet split.

Goal:
- prevent military units from fighting while limiting Protector’s authority.

Protector success:
- one unified order prevents a catastrophic clash/attack.

Temptation:
- officers/public demand expanded command.

## 13. Subact 4C-3 — 멈춘 제국 / Episodes 405–412

Goal:
- address Continuity suspension that stops forged commands but also freezes movement, funds and identities.

Helpful effect:
- prevents one coordinated coup/irreversible seizure.

Human cost:
- hospitals, refugees, salaries, appointments and routes stall.
- unverified people suffer most.

Core-team conflicts:
- S-001 respects provenance logic but demands published uncertainty.
- M-001/H-001 confront service harms.
- H-002 sees technical/industrial locks.

Custodian offer:

Required refusal/compromise:
- accept narrow verification service but split keys/data and require confidence publication/sunset.

Reward:

Cost:

- some identities/records remain frozen.

Hook:
- Purge/Archive hardliners launch final attempt to destroy or monopolize evidence during multi-institution transfer/hearing.

## 14. Subact 4C-4 — 누구의 역사인가 / Episodes 413–420

Goal:
- preserve enough evidence and public access to prevent permanent manufactured truth.

Mode:
- Sacred Archive/Registry/route/data operation with physical and public fronts.

Conflict:
- Founding Covenant coalition wants old originals.
- Intelligence Archive bloc wants predictive stable outcome.
- claimants want selective evidence.
- witnesses/sources demand privacy or destruction.

Required choice:
- no complete public dump and no single sealed custody.
- create plural evidence sets, public findings, protected personal layers and independent institutional copies.

P-001 sacrifice:
- withholds/limits Archive future labels rather than use them to settle legitimacy.

Reward:
- enough public evidence to support temporary compact; one historical suppression exposed.

Cost:
- records/witnesses lost, privacy compromised or truth remains incomplete.
- one claimant/support base severely damaged.

Act-ending trigger:
- services and fleets cannot wait; all factions must negotiate a compact while field forces/provinces set deadlines and begin independent alignment.

# ACT 4D — 왕관 없는 협약 / Episodes 421–450

Act question:

> 왕위가 결정되지 않은 채로도 국가가 전쟁과 행정을 계속하려면, 권력을 누구에게 얼마나 나누고 언제 되돌릴 것인가?

Act result:
- Imperial Continuity Compact separates ceremonial, executive, military, authentication and provincial powers.
- immediate Core collapse is prevented.
- enough forces/institutions accept to operate.

Act cost:
- some fleets/provinces/claimants reject compact.
- compact ambiguity creates ownerless/contested fleets and GA5 war.
- no final historical truth or crown settlement.

## 15. Subact 4D-1 — 다섯 개의 권한 / Episodes 421–427

Goal:
- negotiate authority categories rather than one ruler.

Categories:
1. ceremonial crown/household.
2. civil executive/regency.
3. military protection/General Staff.
4. Continuity authentication/suspension.
5. provincial recognition/consultation.
6. judicial/public audit and founding limits.

Conflict:
- every actor wants emergency powers and fears decorative role.
- H-001 pushes provincial burden obligations.
- S-001 pushes evidence/uncertainty rules.
- M-001 pushes service minimums.
- H-002 pushes industrial/technical governance limits.

Reward:
- draft compact with sunset/review/removal and key splits.

Cost:
- no agreement on one or two critical clauses: fleet command, anchor keys or succession timing.

Hook:
- one claimant attempts final unilateral accession/appointment or external field fleet refuses the draft.

## 16. Subact 4D-2 — 마지막 대관식 / Episodes 428–434

Event:
- final attempt to turn partial legitimacy into complete control: ceremony, military recognition, executive appointment or anchor transfer.

Not necessarily villainous:
- supporters believe only decisive completion prevents civil war.

Goal:
- prevent irreversible monopoly without humiliating/destroying claimant constituency.

Action:
- simultaneous public ceremony, legal/registry operation, military security and evidence broadcast.

P-001 role:
- protects civilians/claimant and blocks key transfer/force escalation.
- refuses to name a “true ruler.”

Outcome:
- accession/appointment remains partial; claimant accepts, is forced into or rejects compact terms.

Reward:
- political space for final agreement.

Cost:
- casualties, schism or one institution/claimant withdraws.

Hook:
- field fleets and provinces demand executable command within hours/days or will act independently.

## 17. Subact 4D-3 — 제국연속협약 / Episodes 435–443

Goal:
- enact compact across several sites/institutions, not one signature table.

Operational requirements:
- Registry recognizes temporary offices without final sovereign.
- Treasury pays services/forces through multi-signature rules.
- General Staff receives bounded military authority/standing intent.
- Meridian Anchor splits authentication/suspension.
- Crown/Old Court complete public/ceremonial acknowledgment.
- provinces submit recognition/conditions.
- public evidence and review schedules transmitted.

Decisive autonomous choices:
- Heir accepts bounded role or protects rival institution.
- Regent signs sunset/removal.
- Protector obeys civilian limit or loses some forces.

Reward:

Cost:
- several major fleets/provinces do not recognize all terms.

Hook:
- first test: a major field fleet/shipyard refuses conflicting compact order, forcing each ship/task group to choose command ownership.

## 25. Drop-Off and Repetition Controls

### Risk: legal exposition overload

Control:
- each protocol introduced through an order that changes pay, movement, protection, ceremony or command.
- no scene explains all seven protocols at once.
- reader-facing focus remains four personal claimants plus compact/provincial alternative.

### Risk: claimant meetings feel repetitive

Control:
- each claimant first appears solving a different crisis.
- offers target different core-team members.
- alliances shift through institutions, not endless private debates.

### Risk: no clear “win” because throne unresolved

Control:
