# Episode 21 — 안전구역 안의 적

Date:
- CY 742-04-05, 10:20–10:50.

Episode function:
- cross from school test into real civilian operation.
- establish White Dock zones, map discrepancy and actors with separate objectives.
- begin Rian–Ern contact before any clean enemy explanation.

## Scene 21.1 — Docking without the promised support

POV:
- Rian in transport/07 cockpit preparation.

Goal:
- activate 07 and confirm mission envelope.

Physical setup:
- transport enters WD-G School Security Berth.
- white reflective dock panels, repair heat and lattice noise distort sensor baselines.
- promised external cooling cart is absent because Security reassigned it.

Contributions:
- Nera recalculates 07 to an 8–10-minute high-demand envelope.
- Mia sets a stricter pilot stop because E17 damage/recovery is incomplete.
- Serin finds the live dock manifest and route package were signed at different times.
- Haren sees the official target order leaves WD-B worker routes below response priority.

Choice:
- refuse launch and fail the evaluation, or deploy with reduced capability and documented limitations.
- cell chooses deployment only after Serin records the missing support and Nera/Mia limits.

Reward:
- real 07 sortie.

Cost:
- reduced thermal margin before combat begins.

## Scene 21.2 — A secure zone that is not secure

Action:
- White Dock reports an unauthorized maintenance lock cycle in WD-B.
- Academy command initially labels it a training inject or contractor error.
- Rian recognizes movement timing inconsistent with ordinary worker traffic.

Information limit:
- Ern’s frame is partly hidden by structure and heat.
- Rian cannot identify pilot or objective yet.

Local agency:
- a White Dock pressure warden closes one route against Academy instruction because civilians are still inside it.

Conflict:
- Security orders cadets to remain at WD-G/WD-F while its frames investigate.
- the hidden evaluation punishes independent pursuit and independent rescue differently.

## Scene 21.3 — First exterior movement

Action:
- Rian launches 07 and uses low output to move along the school berth exterior.
- Juno’s cell takes WD-A/WD-F assignment.
- Haren establishes common route/people board rather than a single tactical channel.

Ace payoff:
- Rian predicts a security frame’s blocked approach and uses one precise tether/brace movement to avoid collision without spending full output.

Limit:
- 07’s sensor classification remains poor.
- Nera warns that a fast crossing would consume the reserve needed for rescue.

## Scene 21.4 — White Line

Action:
- Ern’s modified response frame emerges from WD-B service geometry.
- Ern disables a security sensor rather than firing at a cockpit.
- Rian moves to block access toward WD-C.

Recognition:
- Ern sees a prisoner/student signature moving with veteran timing.
- Rian sees a future-record silhouette/call-sign association that does not match the current mission.

End hook:
- radical breachers with a separate forged contractor clearance enter WD-E East while Rian and Ern face each other.

Reward:
- enemy ace and real external stakes.

Cost:
- two threats; 07 cannot pursue both.

# Episode 22 — 쏘지 않은 적

Date:
- CY 742-04-05, 10:50–11:15.

Episode function:
- deliver first capture-not-destroy frame clash.
- prove Ern’s restraint is costly and not allegiance.
- give Juno/Haren independent operational roles.

## Scene 22.1 — Structure instead of open sky

POV:
- Rian in 07.

Spatial objective:
- prevent Ern from reaching WD-C service collar.

Combat:
- Ern uses cables, truss shadows and a worker-built anchor route absent from Rian’s future/official map.
- Rian uses 07’s tether and manipulator to limit Ern’s choices rather than chase speed.
- low-power training emitter marks/forces route but cannot penetrate military armor.

Future-limit beat:
- Rian predicts the standard maintenance corridor; Ern uses a local repair bypass built after the record Rian remembers.

Cost:
- 07 spends 5–7 minutes of high-demand movement and approaches Red heat if rescue tools continue immediately.

## Scene 22.2 — The withheld shot

Action:
- Rian’s cockpit/shoulder line is briefly exposed.
- Ern has a lethal or disabling firing opportunity.
- student custody signatures and nonstandard training telemetry confirm Rian may be witness/victim.

Ern choice:
- withholds cockpit-kill fire and instead cuts 07’s sensor/approach line.

Rian response:
- does not treat restraint as friendship.
- exploits the hesitation to pin Ern’s support route long enough for Security to close.

Cost to Ern:
- retrieval support frame/tug is exposed.
- one operative becomes trapped/injured later because extraction timing slips.

## Scene 22.3 — Different fronts

Haren:
- identifies WD-F relay interface and WD-A evacuation as the only route preserving distress/evidence traffic.
- asks Juno’s cell to stay there instead of reinforcing Rian.

Juno:
- accepts after demanding explicit authority and credit; refuses to become invisible support.
- moves to protect local tugs and relay interface.

Command conflict:
- Academy Security orders Juno to support archive capture instead.
- Kara authorizes Juno’s safety/relay role within her limited authority, accepting blame.

Result:
- Juno’s front remains independent and cannot later save Rian.

## Scene 22.4 — The second intruder

Action:
- Serin receives a White Dock interior alert: radical breachers are not following Ern’s extraction route.
- Mia sees first pressure/fire casualties from WD-E.
- Ern tries to communicate a short warning about charges without surrendering target information.

Rian choice:
- break contact and respond to pressure threat, or continue toward Ern capture.
- he delays one beat, seeking a way to do both, then loses the clean capture window.

End hook:
- a shaped charge/power action separates WD-B/C/E pressure segments; 147 people become trapped at peak.

Reward:
- enemy motive complication and Juno autonomy.

Cost:
- Ern escapes immediate pin; pressure crisis begins.

# Episode 23 — 추격을 버리는 법

Date:
- CY 742-04-05, 11:15–12:20.

Episode function:
- make rescue, evidence and capture physically incompatible.
- give Nera, Serin, Mia, Haren and local workers decisive actions.
- force Rian to abandon the ace pursuit.

## Scene 23.1 — One hundred forty-seven

POV:
- Rian through damaged sensor/voice channels.

State:
- WD-B/C/E separated.
- worker habitat clinic receiving casualties.
- Academy command orders archive perimeter held; trapped cargo workers are secondary.

Haren:
- produces a zone-by-zone plan:
  - Juno/locals preserve WD-F and WD-A evacuation.
  - Mia/clinic own triage and movement order.
  - Nera accesses the pressure/service collar.
  - Serin preserves proof without copying all private contents.
  - Rian holds/opens one physical connector.

Tradeoff:
- Ern cannot be pursued during the operation.

Rian decision:
- formally gives up immediate pursuit and switches 07 to rescue configuration.

## Scene 23.2 — Human-scale access

Action:
- Nera, Serin and a White Dock worker team approach WD-C through a maintenance/pressure route.
- full-size 07 cannot enter.

Nera:
- identifies an old service interface compatible with 07’s authority fragment.
- use will expose unauthorized service capability to every claimant.

Serin:
- creates external authentication hashes and current casualty/transfer markers.
- refuses a full uncontrolled copy because patient/family/private identities are mixed into the records.

Local workers:
- provide the actual route and operate manual locks.
- one local decision overrides an unsafe remote Security command.

## Scene 23.3 — The weight of the door

Action:
- 07 anchors outside a damaged WD-C/worker connector.
- Rian uses manipulator and tether to hold structure while people move.

Machine limit:
- low/medium output after partial cooldown.
- pursuit, high-speed reposition and structural hold cannot occur together.
- left joint and radiator approach Red.

Pilot limit:
- Rian’s body begins tremor/nausea.
- Mia sets a hard time/condition stop while continuing triage.

Loss:
- at least one person dies before the route can be opened, preventing a clean rescue fantasy.

## Scene 23.4 — Partial truth

Ern:
- reaches a bounded archive section and extracts only part of the custody/transport chain.
- discovers the personal target/destination is incomplete.

Security:
- moves to capture Ern and close the archive even if pressure rescue is unfinished.

Radicals:
- prepare destructive denial because full extraction is failing.

Serin warning:
- the archive is not one server; destroying it will also destroy ordinary identity/traffic functions and some records that exist nowhere else.

End hook:
- radical denial timer/physical charge is discovered in a zone that cannot be cleared before 07’s thermal limit expires.

Reward:
- most trapped people now have a route; authenticated evidence is distributed.

Cost:
- one death, exposed service authority and incomplete data.

# Episode 24 — 기체를 남겨두고

Date:
- CY 742-04-05 to CY 742-04-06.

Episode function:
- complete White Dock climax.
- create temporary Rian–Ern physical cooperation without alliance.
- sacrifice 07 readiness/components to preserve people and evidence.

## Scene 24.1 — Denial path

POV:
- Rian.

Problem:
- radical denial can trigger pressure/fire/data destruction across WD-C and WD-E.
- Security’s preferred solution is lethal clearance/fire that risks workers and archive structure.
- Ern’s preferred solution preserves extraction route but may let radicals escape.

Haren plan:
- split physical tasks among Rian, Ern, Juno/local tugs and interior workers.
- no shared command; each actor sees a reason to prevent total destruction.

Rian condition:
- 07 has one short high-demand action left.

## Scene 24.2 — Temporary overlap

Action:
- Rian blocks/holds the damaged connector and denial mechanism’s structural path.
- Ern cuts or drags the charge carrier/weaponized utility frame away from the archive.
- neither gives the other full access or trust.

Combat detail:
- Rian uses future tactical timing but current tools.
- Ern uses local infrastructure knowledge.
- a Security frame attempts to capture Ern during the overlap, worsening geometry.

Choice:
- Rian marks/blocks Security’s firing line, making himself liable for collusion.

Cost:
- Ern’s remaining operative/support route is exposed; one operative is critically injured/captured or left behind.

## Scene 24.3 — Nera’s component

Nera:
- determines the certified radiator wing cannot survive the heat/structural load.
- commands manual release, sacrificing the part and her immediate control claim.

Rian:
- releases it despite knowing 07 will be immobilized and claimed.
- uses the detached wing as heat/structure protection while the manipulator holds the route.

Mia/Kara:
- enforce shutdown at the end of the rescue window.

Machine outcome:
- radiator detached.
- manipulator shoulder and attitude group Red.
- sensor head/telemetry damaged.
- core records abnormal continuity/pilot response.
- 07 cannot self-deploy.

Pilot outcome:
- sync overload and grounding.

## Scene 24.4 — No complete winner

Outcome:
- radical cell fails to destroy the entire archive.
- Ern escapes with a bounded partial record set, not the target’s complete destination.
- White Dock workers restore pressure and account for survivors.
- Juno’s cell preserves WD-F and the civilian route.
- three deaths and serious injuries make the operation a real incident, not a school victory.

Custody split:
- Academy tows hull/industrial arm.
- Helix claims detached radiator.
- Neutral medics isolate core/telemetry as medical/evidence material.
- records exist in several incomplete copies.

Neutral entry:
- distress and conflict-of-interest conditions allow rescue cutters to dock.

End hook:
- while Rian is removed from the cockpit, four institutions issue incompatible claims on his body, the core and the surviving evidence.

Reward:
- people and most evidence survive; total denial prevented.

Cost:
- 07 lost as an operable unit, Ern escapes, collusion suspicion and deaths.

# Episode 25 — 구조선은 망명선이 아니다

Date:
- CY 742-04-06 to CY 742-04-07.

Episode function:
- show body/casualty consequences.
- establish Neutral custody’s real protection and strict limits.
- prevent a simple “outside organization saves them” resolution.

## Scene 25.1 — Rian without the frame

POV:
- Rian on Neutral medical deck.

Body state:
- tremor, nausea, muscular injury, sensory afterimage and incomplete memory of final minutes.
- Mia/Neutral staff ground him.

Goal:
- learn what happened to team, people, 07 and records.

Constraint:
- medical staff release information by patient/evidence role, not Rian command rank.

Human consequence:
- casualty names/conditions and the worker death are presented before component status.

## Scene 25.2 — Four claims

Claimants:
- Academy demands return of sentenced cadets and physical hull.
- Security alleges collusion and demands interrogation/separation.
- Helix asserts certified radiator, telemetry and liability rights.
- Imperial authority seeks core/evidence preservation.
- Neutral claims temporary patients/evidence chain only.

Neutral representative:
- can hold injured students for 24–72 hours.
- cannot grant citizenship, erase sentences or keep military contraband indefinitely.

Rian temptation:
- ask Neutral to keep the team together by concealing the core or identities.

Refusal:
- Neutral representative says concealment would collapse the very evidence protection they need.

## Scene 25.3 — One story or several

Rian:
- proposes a single coordinated account to prevent contradictions being weaponized.

Haren:
- refuses; one unified story would make Rian the owner of everyone’s reasons and conceal differences.

Nera:
- will not describe the radiator release as unavoidable if another slower option existed.

Serin:
- separates confirmed action, individual intent and unknown command source.

Mia:
- records medical stop and casualties independently.

Decision:
- each member preserves a separate account, linked by shared evidence rather than one script.

Cost:
- their legal defense becomes less clean.

## Scene 25.4 — Conditional return proposal

Terms proposed:
- no secret separation before initial testimony.
- independent medical presence.
- evidence mirrors and written charges.
- White Dock/Neutral witnesses at preliminary inquiry.
- separate component seals remain.

Why accept:
- Neutral refuses indefinite custody.
- Academy wants students back without public rescue-law violation.
- Imperial/Helix want preserved claims.

End hook:
- Academy accepts the return terms but adds that the hearing will examine whether a student cell supplied Ern the internal route.

Reward:
- time, medical care and public procedure.

Cost:
- return to custody and unavoidable inquiry.

# Episode 26 — 돌아온 죄수들

Date:
- CY 742-04-08.

Episode function:
- return team to Academy under conditional protection.
- establish inquiry arena and component-location list.
- redirect institutional pressure onto Juno.

## Scene 26.1 — Return corridor

POV:
- Rian in medical transport.

Physical movement:
- Neutral launch→Academy dock→medical/public corridor under escort.
- crowds/students see the damaged team and separated 07 tow.

Public myth:
- fragments of the White Dock event become “the fifth answer team beat the terrorists” and “the traitor cell helped them escape.”

Rian:
- realizes he cannot control the story while unable to control his own body/frame.

## Scene 26.2 — Separation attempt

Action:
- Security tries to route Nera/Serin/Haren into different interview rooms before witnesses arrive.

Counter:
- Serin invokes written return terms.
- Mia confirms Rian and another student cannot be separated from medical monitoring yet.

- hull and arm: Academy.
- radiator: Helix lab/claim.
- core: Neutral-to-Imperial evidence transfer.
- sensor/telemetry: damaged and mirrored.
- Service Authority A: Neutral evidence copy.

Rian goal:
- recover core first.

Nera/Haren disagreement:
- Nera argues a core without cooling, tools and rights is a hostage, not a machine.

## Scene 26.4 — Impossible confession

Action:
- Juno Hess is brought into the preliminary inquiry.

Contradiction:
- Juno’s cell was physically holding WD-F/WD-A at the relevant time.
- the claimed credential event occurred outside Juno’s actual access window.

Juno motive not yet stated:
- confession protects cellmates/placement or linked guarantees from collective action.

End hook:
- Juno asks Rian not to disprove the confession.

Reward:
- public hearing and component map.

Cost:
- new scapegoat and internal distrust.
