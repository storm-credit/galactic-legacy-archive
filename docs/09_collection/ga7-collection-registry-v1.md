## 3. Reader-Facing Consolidation

GA7 should read through five tracks:

1. which routes are open, controlled, closed or destroyed.
2. who holds closure/denial authority and keys.
3. which local/dependent populations bear the decision.
4. what H-001 personally ordered versus office/doctrine/successors.
5. whether the federation can operate without him.

Do not display every office, signature and claim as a separate codex item.

Reader-facing names:
- one federation name.
- one Continuity corridor name.
- one scorched doctrine/faction name.
- one H-001 office/title.
- one target D4 node/route and 2–3 dependent regions.

## 4. Loss and Non-Completion Rules

- H-001’s real D4 harm is not reversed.
- no complete Blood Admiral incident decomposition.
- no universal acceptance of corrected record.
- no elimination of scorched/central/local monopoly risk.
- no total federation membership or reliable minimum service.
- at least one route/node remains permanently destroyed/degraded.
- dependent-region deaths/claims remain active beyond GA7.
- H-001 loses or narrows singular emergency authority.
- P-001 does not decide H-001’s final guilt/office.
- Continuity corridor remains a credible successful rival.

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- a leader only voices ideology and makes no route decision.
- a denial authority rule changes no timing/evacuation/custody.
- an incident does not change responsibility or current rights.
- an identity layer can be removed without affecting the mystery/politics.
- a route battle repeats GA6 evacuation rather than closure/denial/downstream causality.
- an investigation scene does not change aid, office, evidence, claims or federation design.

Likely consolidation:
- denial registry and key custody appear as one evolving system.
- title/office/signature records can be one reader-facing case.
- victim claims and reparation obligations can be shown through 2–3 focal cases rather than many documents.
