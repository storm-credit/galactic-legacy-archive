## 1. Final Registry Rule

- GA10 does not complete the collection by putting everything in P-001’s possession.
- every major people, machine, ship, record, route and institution target must end in one of four states:
  1. independent living custody;
  2. plural/shared institutional custody;
  3. deliberate retirement/loss/destruction with lineage preserved;
  4. unresolved/contested state explicitly acknowledged.
- no major item/relationship silently disappears.
- final rewards and losses must be visible through operations and ordinary life, not only legal text.

Status codes:
- `T`: teased.
- `I`: identified.
- `C`: conditionally connected/authorized.
- `G`: integrated and used.
- `L`: lost/retired/destroyed/contested.
- `R`: reinterpreted/distributed/relinquished.

## 3. Reader-Facing Consolidation

GA10 should read through five tracks:

1. whether handoffs keep people alive and who corrects failure.
2. which regions choose which transition path and who bears outside costs.
3. which functions/keys remain central or transfer.
4. what P-001 permanently gives up.
5. what ordinary life and history look like years later.

Do not enumerate every compact, region or character ending.
Use representative systems and recurring people.

## 4. Final Loss and Non-Utopia Rules

- one region/front fails or delays distribution.
- one region voluntarily remains temporarily central.
- one service/technical transition causes a named death/loss.
- one local elite/actor captures power before being challenged.
- one identity/personhood/history case remains unresolved.
- compensation/reparation remains incomplete.
- P-001 cannot recover exclusive index or central command.
- no universal praise, reconciliation or one official history.
- no sequel reset.

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- a transition institution never changes service/rights/authority.
- a regional example duplicates another path without distinct cost.
- a final character scene merely reports role without choice/work/relationship.
- a mystery payoff is only explained and lacks material aftermath.
- an epilogue resolves a conflict more cleanly than the established institution permits.

Likely consolidation:
- functional distribution appears through 5–7 fronts, not all ten legal documents.
- final role closures can be grouped by shared places/institutions.
- mystery payoffs should emerge through actions/records/ordinary life, not a checklist in prose.
