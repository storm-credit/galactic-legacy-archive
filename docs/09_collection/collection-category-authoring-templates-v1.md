## 1. Purpose

These templates prevent late improvisation, free rewards and category drift. They are author-side tools, not screens that must appear in the novel.

A target is approved only when it has:

- a prior trace;
- a current holder or responsible constituency;
- a category-specific acquisition verb;
- a practical integration use;
- a cost and support burden;
- a loss, refusal or recovery path;
- later reuse or an explicit ending state.

## 3. People / Hero Relationship Template

People are never owned inventory.

Required status language:
- unknown;
- contacted;
- mutually recognized;
- conditional collaborator;
- represented party;
- allied for a defined objective;
- institutionally connected;
- independent rival;
- estranged;
- defected;
- deceased / identity-closed / missing — separately defined.

Do not use:
- obtained;
- owned;
- loyalty complete;
- permanent party member;
- rank as moral value.

Additional fields:

- Present want:
- Present fear:
- Constituency / dependents:
- Current competence and limits:
- Future reputation or compressed identity:
- Why that future may be wrong:
- Independent offer from another faction:
- Conditions for cooperation:
- Non-negotiable refusal:
- Reason to leave the protagonist:
- Institution they may create or join:
- Decision they make without the protagonist:
- Relationship cost of recruitment attempt:
- Later disagreement that must survive:

Approval tests:

- Would this person still have an arc if the protagonist disappeared?
- Can they rationally choose another faction?
- Does cooperation require consent and current evidence?
- Can they contradict the Archive’s future label?
- Is one useful skill balanced by a real weakness and support need?

## 13. Reader-Facing Memory Limits

At any one time:

- 3–5 active pursuit targets;
- 1 principal set and at most 1 secondary set;
- 4–6 visible ship/frame system tracks;
- 3–7 meaningful fleet formations;
- 3–5 front-stage factions in one mini-arc;
- 3–5 new proper nouns per episode cluster, not per scene.

Author database size may be much larger. Reader-facing display must remain selective.

## 14. Approval Workflow

1. Fill universal and category fields.
2. Link at least two prior clues or one explicit act setup.
3. Identify competing faction and counter-collection method.
4. Define integration and cost.
5. Define refusal, loss and permanent ending state.
6. Check memory limits and category overlap.
7. Run power-creep, ethics, continuity and similarity review.
8. Register in the appropriate act inventory before prose or scene-card use.
