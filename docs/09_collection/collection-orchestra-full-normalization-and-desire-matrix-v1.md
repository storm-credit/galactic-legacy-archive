## 5. Category Integration Rulings

### C1 — Heroes and relationships

- Keep `영웅·관계` as the author-side working tag until the reader-facing label is decided.
- A future title is a narrative promise, not the character's present identity or guaranteed destiny.
- Person entities require present want, fear, constituency, current limit and future-label conflict.
- Relationship records require both endpoints, scope, consent, competing offer, refusal, exit and a protagonist-independent decision.
- People, AI persons and communities are never owned inventory.

### C2 — Maneuver frames

- The current registry's frame anchor is `AUX-07 / 07호`, not a catalogue of many complete frames.
- F-001–F-006 are body or system components; F-004 and F-007–F-010 route primarily through C3 as mounted tools; service keys and logs route through claims or C4/C6.
- Hangars, workshops, operators, certification councils and production cells are support relations, not additional frames.
- `LFX-01 AXIOM` is not in current repository canon and is excluded from the 415-row count.
- `CTF-13/07` versus `AUX-07` designation and dimension conflicts remain an O01/X04 issue.

### C3 — Weapons and modules

- A C3 entity needs a host, independent operation, independent cost and an independent loss path.
- Acquisition never grants unlimited ammunition, energy, compatibility, training or legal authority.
- Required operational fields include range, sensor dependency, ammunition or energy, heat, reload, recoil and structural load, collateral, countermeasure, storage, doctrine, supply, compatibility and nonlethal use.
- Old tools retain rescue, low-signature, repair or standard-specific utility.

### C4 — Relics and treasures

- C4 is limited to physical or cultural evidence, not every right or record in the setting.
- Physical key, document, mirror or archive holding can be an entity; its legal, social and interpretive effect is a separate claim.
- Existing positive reward tests should use keys, logs, originals, mirrors, translation and responsible return before inventing one new relic per grand act.
- Community heritage cannot become a private museum item or unexplained combat buff.

### C5 — Ships

- `NR72-118` is the confirmed GA2 operating anchor; access to its hull does not equal operational ownership.
- `NR72-061` has no intact legendary hull available for recovery. Its lineage is distributed among records, components, labor, residence and claims.
- Hull, crew, captain authority, title, debt, insurance, wages, route license, mission module and loss state are separate records.
- A charter, scuttle plan, formation or sacrifice set is not a ship entity.
- Mobile repair tender, disputed destroyer, `Vow of Bastion` and `Warmth` remain entity candidates subject to canon and loss crosswalk.

### C6 — Technologies

- Technology follows theory or architecture, prototype or profile, process, tooling, standard or certification, training or diffusion, security or version and failure or obligation layers.
- A blueprint does not create materials, tools, skilled labor, certification, doctrine or an industry.
- `OSR-0.7` and `Emergency Open-Service Specification v0.1` must remain separate provisional subjects until their version relationship is resolved.
- `07 Original Frame and Open Service Spine` is split into an `AUX-07` entity candidate and an Open Service Spine technology subject candidate.

### C7 — Factions and institutions

- Only organizations with constituents, resources, services, decisions and succession receive institution entity IDs.
- Charters, mandates, authority and representation are control claims unless a distinct institution is independently evidenced.
- Leadership defeat or death does not erase the organization.
- Required fields include constituency, social base, funding, services, internal factions, offices, succession, enforcement, appeal, entry, exit and action without P-001.

### C8 — Territory, nodes and civilizations

- A place entity is separate from passage, jurisdiction, service and defense claims.
- `current_holder` must split jurisdiction, operation, service, defense and meaning; it is not an owner field.
- Occupation, coercion and failed integration are negative state and obligation records, never positive acquisition completion.
- Front-stage C8 anchors should concentrate on GA3 node restoration, GA7 route federation and GA10 regional choice rather than exposing every geographic row.

## 7. Trap Sweep and Corrections

The orchestra and red team found and corrected the following implementation traps:

1. `frame` inside `framework` was incorrectly treated as a maneuver frame; term matching now uses boundaries.
2. `ship` inside `Relationships` incorrectly produced C5 tags; section matching now uses boundaries.
3. Source-level tags were copied to every split record, making person entities look like frames or technologies; source and split tags are now separate.
4. The first correction removed place and institutional context from relationship and claim records; cross-domain links are now preserved without automatically adding C7.
5. `Ship Command Charter` was treated as a hull while `Mobile Repair Tender` was not; both are manually corrected.
6. `07 Original Frame and Open Service Spine` was one entity record; it now exposes two unresolved subject slots.
7. Sacrifice plans, principles, oaths and responsibility splits were false entity records; representative cases are removed.
8. Missing community, AI-person, claim and set records identified by A16 were added to the provisional split.

These corrections improve the queue but do not replace row-level semantic review.
