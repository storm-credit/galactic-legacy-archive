- GA8 rewards must change current identity, service, command, personhood or institutional authority.
- active pursuit remains 3–5 reader-visible targets per mini-arc.
- origin reveals are registered only when supported by multiple earlier/current sources.

Status codes:
- `T`: teased.
- `I`: identified.
- `C`: conditionally connected/authorized.
- `G`: integrated and used.
- `L`: lost/deleted/exposed/merged/contested.

3. which archive systems are sites, tools, evidence or persons.

## 4. Loss and Non-Completion Rules

- no complete original history or mirror set.
- one record/mirror/source class permanently lost/exposed.
- one AI/composite/person suffers irreversible copy/merge/deletion/identity harm.
- no universal AI personhood test.
- no full solution to preserve/correct/forget/refuse conflict.
- no exclusive P-001 curator role, but unique corrective connection remains.
- no safe/repeatable regression capability.
- bounded activation expands beyond intended scope.
- plural factions survive but fail to prevent some central classifications/freezes.

---

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- a record right does not alter current access, service, personhood or accountability.
- an archive site adds lore without a living constituency/current operation.
- an AI case repeats another without changing classification/rights.
- a Seed layer is explained but never used in an active choice.
- an origin reveal only rewards curiosity and does not cause activation/authority conflict.

Likely consolidation:
- record-right categories can appear through 2–3 focal cases.
- S0–S5 layer names may remain project-facing; prose uses local/institutional/mirror/consensus/curator/correction concepts.
- Authority F/G legal/access documents appear as one evolving operation rather than separate paperwork arcs.
