## 1. Rules

- ships, crews and formations are not collected as personal property.
- fleet progression requires mission mandate, crew acceptance, pay/family, maintenance, ammunition, authentication, route and rescue systems.
- active pursuit remains 3–5 targets per mini-arc.
- fleet size is secondary to readiness, support and command cohesion.
- every formation retains exit/refusal and independent constituency.

Status codes:
- `T`: teased.
- `I`: identified.
- `C`: conditionally connected/chartered.
- `G`: integrated and used.
- `L`: lost/withdrawn/grounded/contested.
- `R`: reinterpreted.

## 3. Reader-Facing Consolidation

Visible GA5 progression should be five tracks:

1. who can command which ships for which mission.
2. whether crews accept, are paid and can be rescued.
3. whether ships can be repaired/fueled/armed.
4. whether provinces/formations may recall/leave.
5. whether independent fronts can act under shared intent.

Do not expose every legal/logistics document as a collectible.

Formation readability:
- track 3–7 named formations.
- name individual ships only when characters, losses or tactical roles require it.

---

## 4. Loss and Non-Completion Rules

- no universal fleet obedience.
- no personal P-001 fleet title/ownership.
- no immediate command of hundreds of ships.
- no fully repaired/resupplied fleet at ending.
- at least one valid refusal/withdrawal.
- at least one autonomous success and one distributed-command loss.
- FC-005 or another major formation remains independent/rival.
- Orpheus route/civilian concentration remains unresolved.

---

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- a charter clause never affects a mission/crew/repair decision.
- a formation commander duplicates another authority claim.
- a support/logistics target is acquired but unused within 10–15 episodes.
- a mutiny/recall/refusal produces no lasting rule or relationship.
- a battle can be removed without changing readiness, alignment or GA6 setup.

Likely consolidation:
- pay/pension/family ledger can be one reader-facing guarantee.
- maintenance board/parts pool/readiness map can be one evolving support system.
- task-force growth stages need not be explicitly numbered in prose.

---

## 6. Archive Display Policy — GA5

Allowed major moments:

1. hero-fleet attribution conflicts with distributed records.
2. one subordinate commander/formation becomes historically central after autonomous choice.
3. unified-command models outperform charter in immediate simulation.
4. Orpheus structural match appears.

Limits:
- Archive never selects correct reserve/front allocation.
- no loyalty/ownership roster.
- fleet state comes from commanders/logistics/readiness systems.
- average Archive display less frequent than once every 5–7 episodes.
