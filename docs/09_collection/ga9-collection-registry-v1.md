## 3. Reader-Facing Consolidation

GA9 should read through five tracks:

1. what services the regime restores.
2. how classifications/appeals affect named lives and regions.
3. whether opt-out/hybrid systems work or fail.
4. who controls Seed, mirrors, protection forces and exceptions.
5. why P-001 must begin distribution despite being the safest operator.

Do not display one numeric social score.

## 4. Loss and Non-Completion Rules

- central Seed remains active at E1000.
- millions/billions remain dependent on synchronized services.
- P-001 retains real transition authority/key.
- some classifications/closed identities/technology deletions cannot be reversed.
- one opt-out/hybrid region fails or commits serious abuse.
- one forced synchronization remains popular/beneficial.
- no complete defeat of Continuity institution or all security forces.
- no immediate universal distribution.
- beneficiaries and opponents both resist parts of the handoff.
- first distribution attempt fails or is attacked.

---

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- a classification case does not change service/rights/authority.
- an actor only supports or opposes the regime without independent benefit/harm.
- an appeal/reform scene does not alter key, evidence, status or transition capacity.
- a battle can be removed without changing regime scope, services or central-key state.
- P-001 corrects a case with no resource/precedent/dependency cost.

Likely consolidation:
- classification domains appear through 3–5 focal cases.
- internal coalition keys/mirrors/staff operate as one evolving transition network.
- trusteeship terms and forced-synchronization ledger can share reader-facing case language.
