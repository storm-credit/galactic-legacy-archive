## 1. Registry Rules

- GA3 collection represents access, public service, authority, production, route and defense states—not ownership of the city.
- no protagonist or external faction can receive an `Owned` state for Ardis, its node, residents or defense.
- active pursuit remains 3–5 visible targets per mini-arc.
- project IDs stay mostly invisible to readers.
- every acquired right/system must alter food, medicine, movement, work, traffic, defense or representation within 10 episodes.

Status codes:
- `T`: teased.
- `I`: identified.
- `C`: conditional connection/claim.
- `G`: integrated and used.
- `L`: lost/contested/dispersed.
- `R`: reinterpreted.

## 4. Loss and Non-Completion Rules

- no full L2 upgrade in GA3.
- no complete ownership or technical restoration.
- no elimination of all external claims.
- no unified permanent local political consensus.
- one major loss remains physically/socially visible.
- one local faction remains credible opposition.
- protagonists receive no permanent master key or governing office.
- Service Authority E remains local/split and cannot command the node alone.

---

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- a service right changes no access or resource.
- a local leader exists only to represent one policy and has no autonomous decision.
- a Spine distinction does not produce different physical/authority choice.
- a defense document does not alter orders or sector control.
- a mystery fragment only repeats “records were edited” without changing current action.
- a local group has no later GA4–7 reuse and no distinct GA3 choice.

Likely consolidation after scene testing:
- city data-sharing + failure map may be one reader-facing investigation.
- three-body board may be narrated as the precursor to joint trust rather than separately named.
- defense readiness map and segmented charter may share one public crisis arc.
