## 1. Rules

- succession records, offices and protocols are not simple collectible proof of a “true ruler.”
- every authentic item has scope, condition, custodian, consequence and uncertainty.
- no claimant/person is owned or completed.
- active pursuit remains 3–5 targets per mini-arc.
- reader-facing progression is consolidated into evidence, services, claimants, military/continuity limits and compact.

Status codes:
- `T`: teased.
- `I`: identified.
- `C`: conditionally connected/claimed.
- `G`: integrated and used.
- `L`: lost/sealed/contested.
- `R`: reinterpreted.

## 3. Reader-Facing Consolidation

Visible progression should be understood through five tracks:

1. which succession claims are authentic and what each grants.
2. whether public services and salaries continue.
3. who commands the military.
4. who authenticates/suspends orders and records.
5. whether provinces and institutions accept the compact.

Do not present every protocol/document as a popup or standalone quest.

Front-stage claimant limit:
- four personal claimant solutions.
- Provincial Compact and Founding Covenant remain coalition/legal alternatives.

---

## 4. Loss and Non-Completion Rules

- no final uncontested sovereign.
- no complete public archive or evidence set.
- no restored unified Intelligence agency.
- no total fleet obedience.
- no permanent P-001 royal/constitutional office.
- one claimant/institution loses significant people/base/records.
- compact remains temporary, slower and contested.
- Continuity Office retains useful power and future danger.

---

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- a protocol creates no operational difference.
- a claimant relationship repeats another’s offer/conflict.
- a record only repeats that history was edited without current consequence.
- an administrative scheme does not change services/pay/movement.
- an Intelligence bloc has no independent action or later reuse.
- a compact clause has no enforcement, cost or future conflict.

Likely consolidation after scene testing:
- several evidence items can be one hearing/investigation set.
- payment scheme and audited regency can appear as one evolving executive framework.
- Anchor split keys and Continuity charter may be one reader-facing package.

## 6. Archive Display Policy — GA4

Allowed major Archive moments:

1. conflicting future labels for claimants/outcomes.
2. unnamed P-001 command trace in sealed history.
3. Archive treats one future constitutional outcome as canonical despite current valid alternatives.
4. final fleet/hero set changes after compact and claimant choices.

Limits:
- Archive does not identify the true ruler.
- no future casualty table used to choose claimant.
- public evidence/records and living institutions drive plot.
- average Archive display remains lower than once every 4–6 episodes.
