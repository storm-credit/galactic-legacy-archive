## 1. Registry Rules

- GA6 progression is measured by verified people/functions/routes, distributed authority and surviving obligations—not a single evacuation percentage.
- no person, community, civilian convoy or state institution is “collected” by P-001.
- losses and unresolved holdouts are registered with the same weight as rewards.
- active pursuit remains 3–5 reader-visible targets per mini-arc.
- every route, institution or rescue gain must consume capacity, time, hulls, supplies or political authority.
- no new technology or law introduced in GA6 may create the mixed plan; all required foundations must come from GA1–5.

Status codes:
- `T`: teased.
- `I`: identified.
- `C`: conditionally connected/authorized.
- `G`: integrated and used.
- `L`: lost/destroyed/stranded/contested.
- `R`: reinterpreted.

## 3. Reader-Facing Consolidation

GA6 reader-facing progression should be understood through five tracks:

1. how many people/ships/functions exist and how urgent they are.
2. who controls Windows A/B/C/Grey/local shelter.
3. which State functions and ships are dismantled or sacrificed.
4. which convoys/fronts survive, fail or stay behind.
5. how responsibility/history is recorded afterward.

Do not expose every manifest, function package or legal document as a separate collectible.

Recurring foreground limit:
- 5–8 named ships/communities/institutions.
- 5–7 decision actors/commanders active across the grand act, only 3–5 per subact.

---

## 4. Loss and Non-Completion Rules

- no option is proven mathematically or morally optimal.
- no complete evacuation.
- no intact State/Capital continuity group.
- no full civilian rescue.
- no universal fleet obedience or manifest consent.
- no complete casualty list or causal attribution.
- at least one recurring ship/institution/supporting character or community is permanently lost/transformed.
- at least one holdout remains outside rescue.
- one wider front/region suffers directly from resource diversion.
- P-001’s original responsibility remains after reconstruction.
- full Continuity override remains unchosen but demonstrably more efficient in some models/actions.

---

## 5. Cut/Merge Triggers

Merge/cut if:
- an evacuation document does not change route, capacity, authority or loss.
- a decision actor only asks P-001 for rescue and makes no independent choice.
- an institutional package survives without reducing capacity or creating destination conflict.
- a ship sacrifice has no named crew/function/emotional or strategic consequence.
- an aftermath scene merely restates casualty scale without changing claims, relationships or reform.
- original Orpheus evidence only absolves or condemns P-001 without exposing systemic choices.

Likely consolidation:
- census + dependency map may appear as one evolving operations board.
- Window B plan and bounded Continuity accord can interlock rather than require separate exposition.
- several distributed State functions can be represented by 2–3 recurring institutions/ships in prose.

## 6. Archive Display Policy — GA6

Allowed major Archive moments:

1. recognition of original Orpheus structural match.
2. contradictory option/continuity rankings with explicit uncertainty.
3. post-operation historical-loss classification despite higher immediate survival.
4. Blood Admiral and hero/fleet record drift.

Limits:
- Archive never produces the final manifest or correct option.
- no precise future-death list used to select individuals.
- displays remain short and contested by living evidence.
- average Archive display less frequent than once every 6–8 episodes outside the four major moments.
