# Package 1 — Imperial Security Academy Administration

## 3. Offer

Immediate funding/supply:
- `12` budget units within 10 days.

180-day commitment:
- up to `42` budget units plus military logistics in kind.

Benefits:
- essential payroll at approximately 80–90%.
- secure food, medicine, node/security and frame maintenance.
- recognized citizenship/service routes for many graduates.
- unified command and rapid restoration of discipline/route confidence.
- protected identities and continuity materials kept from uncontrolled sale or destruction.

Control terms:
- Academy redesignated a national-security training/continuity facility.
- Imperial appointment controls Security, curriculum, records and mission priorities.
- student/patient representation advisory only.
- classified transfer and service obligations expanded.
- ORA-3/07/continuity data placed under protected state custody.

Population effects in first 180 days:
- 240–360 cadets selected for military/intelligence/continuity tracks.
- 90–160 cadets returned or transferred to other correctional institutions.
- remaining students continue under stricter service terms.
- 14–25 protected patients/identity cases likely moved to state facilities.

Exit:
- limited through approved discharge/placement.
- desertion treated as national-security offense.

Why good people support it:
- it is the strongest immediate defense and service-continuity package.
- citizenship, salary, treatment and recognized careers are real.
- some students prefer clear authority to experimental plural governance.

Primary harm:
- secret-service ownership and classified disappearance remain structurally possible.

# Package 2 — Helix Contract School

## 4. Offer

Immediate funding/supply:
- `15` budget units plus proprietary medicine/parts.

180-day commitment:
- `49` budget units equivalent in cash, equipment, treatment and contracts.

Benefits:
- 90–100% essential payroll and medical supply.
- modern frame/ship equipment and certification.
- technical careers, apprenticeships and family-debt restructuring.
- safer treatment and maintenance than the current underfunded system.
- no need for broad military nationalization.

Control terms:
- Helix receives management authority over technical curriculum, treatment/equipment standards, data and placement contracts.
- debt and training costs converted into long-term labor/service obligations.
- 07/ORA/open-interface data licensed to consortium programs.
- non-Helix workshop/adapter work restricted or licensed.

Population effects in first 180 days:
- 310–460 cadets offered/assigned long technical, security or industrial contracts.
- 80–140 nonmatching students transferred or returned to public correction systems.
- medical patients retain high-quality care under data/identity/contract conditions.
- worker staffing improves but independent labor claims weaken.

Exit:
- contract buyout/transfer possible but expensive and often unrealistic for indebted students.

Why good people support it:
- strongest full operating package and highest technical/medical quality.
- offers concrete employment rather than indefinite student status.

Primary harm:
- contracts price coercion and convert treatment/education into ownership leverage.

# Package 3 — Independence Breakout & Distributed Relocation

## 5. Offer

Immediate on-site support:
- `3.5` budget units equivalent, mostly transport, food, forged/alternative identities and route access.

Transport capacity:
- 480–720 people in the first 14 days.
- 900–1,300 total over 45 days if routes remain open and factions cooperate.

Benefits:
- breaks Academy/Imperial/Helix custody for participants.
- gives some communities immediate physical exit.
- local/federalist routes can offer land, work and political participation.
- records/evidence can be removed from central seizure.

Control/conditions:
- not one unified receiving government.
- transport and destinations differ by Liberation, Federalist, Localist and Radical networks.
- some routes require defense, labor, militia or political obligations.
- advanced treatment and recognized identity may be weak or unavailable.

Population effects:
- cannot move everyone before supplies/authority fail.
- 350–600 people likely remain stranded or transferred under another package if the Academy closes quickly.
- high-dependency patients and large families are difficult to move.
- staff/worker departure can accelerate life-support collapse for those remaining.

Exit:
- physical mobility may be greater, but route/faction obligations and lack of recognized status remain.

Why good people support it:
- custody and coercive schooling end immediately for those successfully relocated.
- local communities gain direct choice and political agency.

Primary harm:
- unequal rescue capacity, instability and potential conscription/militarization.

# Package 4 — Neutral Medical-Legal Trusteeship

## 6. Offer

Immediate guarantee/support:
- `6` budget units equivalent over 30 days.

90-day support ceiling:
- `18` budget units, requiring co-funders and severe service limits.

Benefits:
- medical minimums and evidence/custody protections.
- temporary suspension of secret/mass transfer.
- independent claims, contract and identity review.
- disarmament/neutral rules reduce some violence.
- students/patients gain legal time and witnessed choices.

Control/conditions:
- Neutral trustees control medical, claims, transfer and audit scopes.
- combat training/black missions suspended.
- budgets, weapons and movement strictly limited.
- fees, insurance and future repayment remain.
- Neutral Assembly does not guarantee military defense against determined state/corporate seizure.

Population effects:
- 300–600 vulnerable people can receive direct protection/transfer review within 90 days.
- the whole 3,000+ station population cannot be fully supported.
- staff payroll 45–65%, training/industry heavily reduced.
- some students must accept external placements or remain in limbo.

Exit:
- individual transfer/appeal improves, but receiving capacity and legal status remain limited.

Why good people support it:
- best immediate rights/evidence framework and lowest secret-transfer risk.

Primary harm:
- underfunded, weak defense and prolonged uncertainty.

# Package 5 — Internal Emergency Continuation

## 7. Offer/Plan

Immediate internal resources:
- `2.7` budget units from reserves, seized/unfrozen claims, student/worker output and deferred obligations.

Estimated additional 30-day income/credit:
- `2.0–3.3` if mission/industrial work and creditors cooperate.

Benefits:
- preserves familiar staff, local knowledge and existing mixed reforms.
- avoids immediate outside takeover.
- keeps students/patients near current relationships and infrastructure.

Required cuts:
- staff payroll 30–45%.
- training activity below 40%.
- noncritical maintenance deferred.
- medical/research capacity rationed.
- external missions/work increased despite fatigue/risk.
- debt and unpaid claims accumulate.

Runway:
- 24–38 days depending failures and external pressure.

Population effects:
- no receiving system for all legal categories.
- staff departures and supply uncertainty grow.
- Security/Operations can recapture emergency authority under crisis.

Why good people support it:
- every takeover package has structural ownership costs and many people want time to build another arrangement.

Primary harm:
- mathematically insolvent without a new coalition; familiar coercion continues.

## 9. Preference Process Requirements

The survey/assembly must ask separately:

- desired immediate location.
- acceptable sponsor/authority.
- treatment/family/work constraints.
- willingness for military/industrial obligation.
- acceptable data/identity terms.
- whether the person wants to remain with current cell/community.
- what risk they accept if no package can meet all needs.

No one person chooses “a faction” as a complete identity.

Multiple-choice/conditional answers are expected.
