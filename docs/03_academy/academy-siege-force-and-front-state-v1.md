## 1. Siege Definition

This is not a conventional enemy fleet bombarding a school until the heroes win.

It is a simultaneous armed transfer, contract seizure, breakout/evacuation and institutional-control crisis in which:

- several actors hold lawful or contractual claims;
- many students/staff voluntarily prefer the arriving actors;
- life support, medical care and records must remain active;
- heavy fire would destroy the asset and kill the people every faction claims to protect;
- no one force can occupy every ring, spine, dock and civilian/medical zone.

Narrative start:
- E84 secret transfer activates.

Open multi-front phase:
- E85–92.

Emergency-command/charter phase:
- E93–96, with legal/implementation aftershocks through E100.

## 7. Six Simultaneous Fronts

### Front 1 — Ring B Student Habitat

People:
- largest student concentration.

Objectives:
- prevent indiscriminate lockdown/forced boarding.
- maintain food, air and current-person manifests.
- separate voluntary departures from disputed/forced transfers.

Key actors:
- Yori, Darin, Aven, student cells, mixed Security units.

Threats:
- crowd panic, false lists, internal fights, door/rotation control.

Decisive autonomous action:
- Darin’s Imperial-aligned squad refuses to seal/abandon a route containing unlisted students/workers.

### Front 2 — Ring C Industrial/Workshop

Objectives:
- prevent remote lockout and unsafe asset stripping.
- keep power, recyclers and frame maintenance operating.
- distinguish willing Helix transfers/contracts from forced wholesale seizure.

Key actors:
- Nera, Bram, Sera, workers, Helix Manufacturing/Security.

Threats:
- power/software lock, sabotage, fires, industrial frames and volatile systems.

Reversal:
- Sera/Helix technical faction protects certified systems from both unsafe student bypass and Finance/Security repossession.

### Front 3 — Central Spine / Black Ward

Objectives:
- maintain patient treatment.
- preserve ORA-3 consent/data state.
- allow patient-specific voluntary transfers, prevent blanket seizure.

Key actors:
- Mia, Amel, clinicians, Serin, ORA-3, mixed Imperial/Helix/Security/Neutral teams.

Threats:
- power/coolant coupling, sealed identities, medical movement, data purge.

### Front 4 — Dock D / Exterior

Objectives:
- control frame bays, shuttles and external approach.
- keep one Neutral/evacuation corridor.
- prevent core/frame removal and radical demolition.

Key actors:
- Rian/07, Kara, Bram’s workers, Ern/White Line, Imperial/Helix frames.

Threats:
- multi-sided maneuver around structure, remote locks, shuttles and limited fire arcs.

07 role:
- movement control, nonlethal disable, rescue and access denial.
- cannot defeat all external frames/ships.

### Front 5 — Ring A Civic/Legal/Broadcast

Objectives:
- keep witnesses, preference data, claims and charter negotiation visible.
- prevent one actor from declaring uncontested legal command.

Key actors:
- student/staff/patient reps, Neutral trustee, Imperial/Helix reps, Serin-linked record team.

Threats:
- communications cut, false orders, selective evidence and seizure of representatives.

### Front 6 — Spine South / Life Support and Central Safety

Objectives:
- power, rotation, air/water, emergency command and segmentation.

Key actors:
- Haren, Nera/engineers, local operators, professional Security, central-system claimants.

Threats:
- all fronts overload shared systems.
- one central command can coordinate faster but creates control monopoly.

E92 trigger:
- safety/authority will fail within hours unless central or segmented emergency command is established.

## 11. Information and Command State

Communications:
- main command network partially compromised/contested.
- local wired/manual/short-range networks remain.
- Ring A public/legal feed intermittently available.
- false/late orders circulate.

Central safety command:
- can coordinate rotation, life support, doors, Dock D and emergency routing rapidly.
- legal/technical access disputed among Academy Operations, Imperial and Helix systems.
- 07/Authority A–B can help bridge/translate one emergency path but cannot create missing power or repair.

Segmented alternative:
- slower local commands with manual fallback and cross-signing.
- requires Haren/Nera/Mia/Serin/Kara/operators/representatives.
- delay causes one visible loss but prevents permanent master seizure.

## 12. Victory Conditions

The protagonists do not need to defeat every external actor.

Success requires:

- mass forced transfer stalled.
- voluntary departures remain possible.
- medical and life-support systems continue.
- core records/07/ORA not captured or destroyed by one faction.
- preference/claims/charter process remains visible enough to bind actors.
- radical denial fails.
- emergency command transitions to a time-limited split structure.

Failure remains:

- some forced/disputed people already gone.
- deaths/injuries and campus damage.
- parts/records lost.
- external factions establish competing programs elsewhere.
- charter underfunded and revocable.
