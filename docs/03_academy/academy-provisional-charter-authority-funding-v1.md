## 1. Working Legal Name

`제13학원 임시공동운영·보호헌장`

Short name:
- `제13학원 임시헌장`.

Duration:
- 180 days.

Review points:
- emergency review at 7 days.
- operational audit at 30 days.
- funding/rights review at 90 days.
- expires or requires renewed legal settlement at 180 days.

Purpose:
- prevent uncontrolled closure, mass forced transfer, exclusive asset seizure and life-support/medical collapse.
- preserve education, treatment, node/service and limited mission capacity while individual placement/exit rights are reviewed.

It is not:
- full student sovereignty.
- cancellation of all sentences/debts/contracts.
- permanent constitution.
- guaranteed funding.
- universal reform of other academies/programs.

## 3. Nine-Seat Emergency Board

Seats:

1. student representative A.
2. student representative B.
3. patient/medical representative.
4. staff/education representative.
5. worker/maintenance representative.
6. Academy/correction legal authority.
7. Imperial/state observer.
8. Neutral trustee/auditor.
9. industrial/contract partner seat, initially Helix with recusal/rotation rules.

No permanent Rian seat.

Rian may serve:
- mission/defense commander only for assigned operations.
- technical/Archive/07 witness where relevant.
- nonvoting adviser in specified emergency sessions.

Selection:
- student/staff/patient/worker seats are selected through provisional group processes and can be recalled/replaced.
- external seats derive from funding/legal/contract authority.

Quorum:
- 6 of 9.

Ordinary decisions:
- simple majority of present quorum, except reserved domains.

## 4. Reserved Domains and Thresholds

### Mass transfer or closure

Requires:
- 6 of 9.
- at least one student seat.
- Neutral trustee or medical/patient seat for vulnerable/medical cases.
- written individual/aggregate impact and appeal route.

### Black/live external mission

Requires:
- 6 of 9.
- student mission/cell consent under contract.
- technical and medical sign-off.
- independent risk/evidence record.
- emergency exception limited by declared rescue/defense conditions.

### Patient/research/data action

Requires:
- clinical authority and patient/consent rules.
- patient representative/Neutral audit for class-wide or program action.
- Helix/Imperial/Academy rights only within stated data/custody scope.

### Major technical changes

Requires:
- licensed technical sign-off.
- worker/maintainer contribution and stop rights.
- owner/contract inspection where valid.
- version/provenance record.

### Emergency security command

Can be invoked for:
- up to 6 hours by recognized safety/security authority during immediate threat.

Must:
- state objective and scope.
- preserve medical/technical stops where physically possible.
- log orders/actions.
- receive board review/renewal before extension.

Cannot automatically:
- permanently transfer students/patients/assets.
- erase records.
- convert emergency control into permanent appointment.

## 6. Student Rights

- current risk summary before missions where possible.
- mission contract copy and stated compensation/debt effects.
- independent medical stop/appeal.
- transfer/discipline notice and representative counsel.
- access to personal debt/placement ledger.
- data access/correction request and consent tiers.
- alternative placement/exit application.
- right to refuse one sponsor/package without automatic collective punishment, subject to underlying sentence law and capacity.
- cell/community choice does not override individual consent automatically.

Limits:
- sentence/debt does not vanish.
- dangerous missions may still be chosen or required within law/contract.
- exit depends on receiving capacity and legal status.
- emergency restrictions remain possible.

## 7. Enforcement Mechanisms

The charter is enforceable because authority is linked to real systems:

- budget release and payroll keys.
- Dock D/frame launch approvals.
- medical custody and supply access.
- external mission licenses and insurance.
- evidence/claims mirrors.
- node/security recognition.
- physical worker/operator control.
- multiple signed command/technical/data keys.

No single party signs out of kindness.

If one party defects:
- other parties can freeze only specified funding/access/authority.
- essential service minimums and emergency procedures continue where possible.
- the charter may still fail if enough funders/operators withdraw.

## 8. Central Emergency Key Transition

During E92–96:
- a central safety command can coordinate rotation, life support, doors, security and Dock D rapidly.
- Rian/07/Authority A–B can temporarily bridge or seize access.

Accepted transition:

1. Rian uses the central path only to stop immediate cascade/seizure.
2. authority is divided into signed segments:
   - life support/logistics.
   - technical/power.
   - medical.
   - security/doors.
   - records/communications.
   - Dock D/frames.
3. local leaders/operators receive time-limited control in their domains.
4. emergency central access expires automatically after a short window unless multi-party renewed.
5. every use is logged and reviewed.

Cost:
- slower coordination and one visible loss/failure.
- no guarantee against future capture.

Rian cannot keep a permanent master key.

These must recur later; charter is not a perfect answer.
