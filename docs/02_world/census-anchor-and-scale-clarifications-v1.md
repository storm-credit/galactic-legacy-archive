## 1. K-13 Scale Clarification

The values refer to different physical scopes.

### K-13 system census

- registered system-wide population at R0: **620,000**;
- includes traffic workers, industrial habitats, food/water production, local civil settlements, security, dependents, transient-service residents and the Academy station;
- primary node: L3 near a larger corridor;
- governance: mixed Imperial claim and local administration.

### 13th Academy station

- permanent normal population: approximately **2,940–3,100**;
- peak operational population: approximately **3,300–3,600**;
- students: approximately 1,280;
- one station/institution inside the K-13 system, not the whole system.

Ratio:
- Academy permanent population is approximately 0.5% of registered K-13 system population.

Story consequence:
- accidents at the Academy are socially visible but do not mean every K-13 resident lives at the school;
- closure affects local contracts and employment beyond the station;
- the local system cannot instantly replace specialized Academy services;
- K-13 civilians and authorities can act independently of school factions.

## 3. Joraas Inner L0 Rationale

Joraas is the one primary L0 outside the Core macroregion.

History:
- originally established as an early relay/continuity anchor before the current Core boundary;
- later demilitarized and placed under a Neutral Commercial Charter after several succession crises;
- physical anchor status remained while political ownership changed.

Functions:
- high-volume Inner route reference;
- fleet traffic and data synchronization;
- emergency alternative when Core anchor politics block access;
- neutral clearing and inspection.

Limits:
- does not possess the complete central Archive or Imperial command root;
- multilateral guarantees constrain unilateral military use;
- neutral status can delay emergency concentration;
- Helix, Imperial and route members all hold partial service/inspection leverage.

Story value:
- proves node class and sovereignty are separate;
- prevents all civilization-scale infrastructure from being physically inside the political Core;
- creates a real neutral-benefit and neutral-coercion case.

## 4. Primary Node Interpretation

A census system's `primary_node` means the highest strategically relevant inhabited-system node.

It does not mean:
- the only relay in the system;
- every habitat sits beside it;
- all routes are healthy;
- the listed government has uncontested physical control;
- the system population can evacuate through it immediately.

## 5. Population Precision

Census point estimates are author-side planning values.

- macroregion totals are locked for scale consistency;
- ordinary system values may shift ±10% through logged corrections/migration;
- front-stage system values require explicit chronology updates;
- reader-facing narration should round unless administrative precision is itself the conflict.
