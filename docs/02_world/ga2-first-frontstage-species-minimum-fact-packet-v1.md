## 0. Purpose

A species codex can be hundreds of fields deep. A first webnovel appearance cannot.

This packet defines the **minimum fact surface** required to put S1-03 and S1-05 on page without:
- lore dump;
- accidental new canon;
- species-as-tool writing;
- reader noun overload;
- inventing biology during prose drafting.

It separates three levels:

1. **ON-PAGE NOW** — facts the first appearance may actually show;
2. **AUTHOR-SIDE SUPPORT** — facts writers/continuity should know but normally not explain yet;
3. **HOLD** — unresolved facts that must not be invented in prose.

The point is not to make these species shallow.
The point is to reveal depth in the correct order.

---

# 1. Global first-exposure limits

For the first 5-episode window of one new species:

- new species public name: max 1;
- new individual name: max 1 if recurring;
- new biological fact: max 1–2;
- homeworld: do not name;
- species state/faction: do not introduce unless current plot requires it;
- self-name/native-language term: do not introduce;
- first-contact date: do not introduce;
- reproduction: do not explain;
- dedicated mecha/ship: do not introduce;
- historical grievance: do not lecture.

Reader should remember:

> person → current goal → one material difference

not:

> species → encyclopedia entry → job specialization.

# 2. S1-03 — low-g pressure/vibration-sensitive people

Tier recommendation:
- **STRONG S1 CANDIDATE** under [[core-species-demand-and-tier-pruning-audit-v1]].

Current preferred first front-stage role:
- **existing caregiver/family-support role in GA2 E122–130**, if later approved.

Correction to older branch wording:
- an earlier prototype card named `route/ship structural specialist` as First Strong Exposure;
- later anti-RPG and GA2 impact audits supersede that **exposure preference**;
- current preferred first strong exposure is **caregiver/ordinary family role**;
- biology remains unchanged.

No scene-card assignment has been executed.

## 2.1 ON-PAGE NOW — maximum first bundle

A first ordinary scene may establish only:

### Fact A — body/rest geometry

Reader-visible:
- elongated/light body;
- resting posture is more reclined/anchored than human chair posture;
- portable rest support or damping wrap can be used in crowded ship accommodation.

No anatomy lecture.

### Fact B — current ship comfort contrast

On Parus-like cruise conditions:
- approximately 0.35–0.45g can be comfortable for many S1-03 people;
- unresolved heavy structural vibration can still make sleep/recovery painful or exhausting.

Important:
- the person does **not** discover the vibration fault;
- engineering already knows the ship defect;
- their physiology merely makes an existing ship condition personally costly.

### Fact C — person’s actual goal

First scene goal should be something such as:
- keep a patient/family member on the promised treatment route;
- protect destination/appeal rights;
- get enough rest to continue caregiving;
- preserve work/family obligations after arrival.

Their species physiology is a constraint on that goal, not the goal itself.

## 2.2 AUTHOR-SIDE SUPPORT — known, usually unspoken

Writers may rely on:
- comfortable gravity planning band roughly 0.35–0.65g;
- sustained high acceleration is medically more dangerous than for common human baseline;
- pressure/vibration mechanoreception has normal neural filtering;
- intense structural vibration can cause overload/pain;
- sensing range is local/noisy;
- trained interpretation can notice clues, but biology cannot diagnose machinery by itself;
- standard common atmosphere is assumed workable for the current NONCANON test unless later chemistry design says otherwise;
- ordinary public communication can use common speech/text translation;
- restraint/rest geometry needs U2-level adaptation more often than doors/terminals;
- emergency transport should distribute load across the elongated body.

These are operational support facts, not exposition obligations.

## 2.3 FIRST-SCENE PROHIBITIONS

Do not:
- have them identify engine bearing failure by touch;
- have them predict decompression before instruments;
- explain evolutionary history;
- describe them as naturally gentle/calm because they sense vibration;
- introduce a special S1-03 ship/frame;
- make Rian the first person to recognize their accommodation need;
- make the crew praise their species as useful;
- turn their physiology into the reason the existing E124 medical veto exists.

## 2.4 HOLD — must not be invented in prose

Unresolved:
- final reader-facing species name;
- self-name;
- homeworld/system;
- origin chronology;
- Imperial contact/incorporation history;
- exact atmospheric chemistry;
- reproduction/gestation/development details;
- exact demographic share;
- exact species-specific language/sensory grammar;
- interspecies reproduction;
- species-majority polity;
- dedicated vehicle/frame lineage.

If prose seems to require one of these, stop and open a design/change request rather than improvising.

## 2.5 Reveal ceiling after first scene

### R0
- recognizable nonhuman person in ordinary shared space.

### R1
- rest/acceleration/vibration accommodation affects daily life.

### R2 later
- evacuation standard, housing or labor policy creates institutional cost.

### R3 much later
- internal political disagreement about separate versus universal accommodation standards.

### R4
- contact/homeworld history only if story demand exists.

# 3. S1-05 — obligate symbiotic one-person biology

Tier recommendation:
- **STRONG S1 CANDIDATE / HIGH DESIGN OBLIGATION**.

Current preferred first front-stage role:
- ordinary paid support/galley/habitation/general-service worker in GA2 E131–145, if later approved.

First appearance must be **work/pay/personality first**, symbiosis second.

## 3.1 ON-PAGE NOW — maximum first bundle

### Fact A — socially one person

Normal interaction uses:
- one personal name;
- one legal/social person;
- ordinary direct conversation.

Do not stage a reveal like:
> “There are actually two beings inside them.”

The biological partnership is normal embodiment, not horror or twist.

### Fact B — one routine support difference

At most one practical detail in the first window, such as:
- one medication/sterilization warning;
- one food compatibility note;
- routine medical inventory marks a symbiont-safe alternative.

This should be presented as mundane ship procedure.

### Fact C — person’s actual goal

Possible first goals:
- get paid correctly;
- keep a work shift;
- secure berth/meal assignment;
- pay family debt;
- refuse an unfair task;
- maintain an ordinary relationship or obligation.

No philosophical discussion is required.

## 3.2 AUTHOR-SIDE SUPPORT — known, usually unspoken

Writers may rely on:
- one normal S1-05 person develops through a primary mobile host organism plus an obligate integrated symbiotic biological partner;
- normal cognition/personhood is one integrated life history, not default dual personality;
- some antibiotics/sterilization/immune treatments can damage the symbiotic partner and therefore the person;
- medical records need integrated-status awareness;
- common seating and atmosphere can be near shared-standard for this NONCANON test;
- medical/food/quarantine compatibility needs more care than ordinary seating;
- chemical sensitivity is bounded and not a toxin detector superpower;
- standard conversation can use ordinary common-language translation;
- unusual separation cases exist only as rare later legal/medical problems.

## 3.3 FIRST-SCENE PROHIBITIONS

Do not:
- call them a hive mind;
- give the symbiont a separate speaking personality by default;
- ask “which one of you is real?” merely to dump lore;
- make them detect poison/illness automatically;
- use a biometric split as the first hook;
- tie their first appearance to Archive mystery;
- have Rian explain their personhood to others;
- make them a legal test case before they have existed as an ordinary person;
- make their symbiosis solve the salvage/061 evidence problem.

## 3.4 HOLD — must not be invented in prose

Unresolved:
- final reader-facing species name;
- self-name;
- evolutionary or engineered origin;
- homeworld/system;
- reproduction and how both biological lineages transmit/develop;
- juvenile symbiosis acquisition/inheritance model;
- exact atmosphere/food chemistry;
- interspecies reproduction;
- exact legal history;
- exact Imperial contact/incorporation date;
- demographic share;
- species-majority state;
- rare separation-case legal doctrine;
- dedicated vehicle/frame lineage.

Especially important:
> reproduction remains a deliberate HOLD. Do not solve it casually inside family dialogue.

## 3.5 Reveal ceiling after first scene

### R0
- ordinary worker/person.

### R1
- one mundane medical/food-support difference.

### R2 later
- customs/medical software mismatch or quarantine procedure.

### R3 GA4/8
- S1-05 people disagree about legal remedy and classification.

### R4
- biological deep origin only if plot requires it.

# 4. Shared GA2 ship rules

If S1-03/S1-05 are later approved for Parus/GA2 use:

### Manifest

Record **service needs**, not just species label:
- restraint/rest geometry;
- acceleration limit;
- medical contraindications;
- food/medication compatibility;
- caregiver/dependent obligation.

This follows the multi-species infrastructure architecture.

### Captain authority

Species accommodation does not erase existing ship authority:
- Maren retains captain/safety authority;
- medical authority retains transportability/medical stop authority;
- Rian does not gain accommodation authority because he knows future facts.

### Cost

Accommodation must be materially visible but not every time as crisis.

Examples:
- different berth choice;
- slower acceleration window;
- one inventory substitution;
- sleep loss;
- extra transfer time.

Avoid:
- every nonhuman presence causing a major emergency.

### Agency

The first nonhuman people must be capable of:
- saying no;
- choosing destination/work;
- demanding payment/service;
- disagreeing with Rian/Haren/Maren;
- leaving or refusing later involvement.

Rian does not recruit the species.

# 5. Dialogue and translation minimum

First appearance default:
- ordinary fluent trade/service conversation through common language and/or MT1 support;
- no translator exposition unless a misunderstanding matters;
- reader-facing prose uses one simple public species term only after final name approval.

Do not introduce:
- self-name;
- native-language grammar;
- untranslated ritual terms;
- multiple exonyms.

High-stakes later scenes may escalate to certified interpretation under the language architecture.

# 7. First-character memory test

After 5–10 episodes, a reader should be able to answer:

### S1-03 person
- Who are they caring for / what do they want?
- What ship condition makes their life harder?
- Did they solve a technical problem because of biology? **Answer must be no.**

### S1-05 person
- What job/obligation do they have?
- What ordinary support fact is different?
- Are they one social/legal person? **Yes.**
- Was their first purpose to explain species philosophy? **No.**

Failure signal:
- reader remembers only “vibration alien” and “two-organism alien.”
