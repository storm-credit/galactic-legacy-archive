## 2. Epistemic Rule

No actor possesses a perfect postwar census.

Figures combine:

- local registries;
- service-usage estimates;
- route and ration manifests;
- survey sampling;
- witness and missing-person records;
- Aurel/Preservation data where available;
- independent regional audits.

Use three confidence bands:

- `H` high confidence: ±1–3%;
- `M` medium confidence: ±5–10%;
- `L` low confidence: ±15–30%.

Reader-facing scenes should use human consequences and rounded figures. Exact values remain author-side.

## 17. Region Outcome Examples

### A T0 medical core

- emergency mortality below R0;
- identity resolution rapid;
- dissent focuses on model access and data refusal;
- many residents voluntarily retain synchronization.

### A T3 local-plural industrial region

- strong local labor and appeal rights;
- repair innovation and ordinary civic life return;
- imported medicine and route insurance remain worse;
- one preventable winter service crisis becomes a lasting political wound.

### A T6 re-centralized frontier hub

- piracy and food interruption fall quickly;
- former local commanders are removed;
- press and identity appeals narrow;
- many residents still support the result because children and medicine survive.

### A T4 asynchronous refusal region

- high autonomy and strong privacy;
- delayed specialized care and identity portability;
- physical couriers and local caches create resilience and inequality simultaneously.
