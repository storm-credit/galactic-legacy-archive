## 1. Existing Canon/Review Facts Used

Ardis is not one planet-city.

Existing zones:
1. Anchor Crown — nonrotating node/traffic complex.
2. Civic Ring — rotating habitat, governance/market/hospital/education.
3. Foundry Cylinder — ship/frame industry + worker communities.
4. Garden Chain — food/water/biological processing.
5. Transit Belt — cargo/temporary habitats/refugees/service platforms.
6. Surface Works — mining/propellant/energy/thermal systems.
7. Outer Defense & Salvage Field.

Existing social identity:
> `the city that repairs what others abandon.`

Existing ordinary culture:
- repair/shift contribution public boards;
- systems with multiple repair marks rather than one owner logo;
- Restart Day;
- registered memorial vs worker/transit memorial conflict;
- rationed fresh greens, fermented protein, rare fruit;
- mixed route cuisine in transit districts;
- school enrollment, ration queue, shift safety, housing claim, identity correction, funeral, wage payment.

Existing physical clocks:
- food, advanced medicine, cooling, payroll, transit life support all fail on different schedules.

These are the visual identity source. No new tourism lore is required.

## 2. Four Art-Direction Lenses

### A — `City of Repair Layers`

Macro principle:
- 새것/헌것 이분법이 아니라 **여러 세대의 수리층**이 보인다.
- 패널, 지지구조, 통로, 공공설비가 교체될 때 이전 규격 흔적이 완전히 지워지지 않음.

Reader anchor:
- `누가 소유했는가`보다 `누가 마지막으로 고쳤는가`가 표면에 남음.

Good use:
- Anchor Crown service truss;
- Foundry machinery;
- public repair/shift boards;
- Transit modules.

Strength:
- 도시의 핵심 문장과 직접 연결.

Risk:
- 전부 낡고 패치된 scrap city로 만들면 실패.

### B — `Six Cities In One Orbit`

Macro principle:
- zone별 중력/산업/주민/이동 방식 차이를 강하게 보여준다.

Anchor Crown:
- nonrotating, handhold/rail/service geometry 중심.

Civic Ring:
- 회전중력과 인간 생활 동선.

Foundry:
- 큰 공작/yard volume.

Garden:
- water/air/biological density.

Transit:
- 임시성, 모듈 연결, 표식의 중첩.

Surface:
- 열/채굴/propellant heavy infrastructure.

Strength:
- `도시 전체를 한 배경처럼 그리는 오류` 방지.

Risk:
- zone마다 완전히 다른 테마파크처럼 보여 하나의 도시 정체가 사라질 수 있음.

### C — `Service Spine Civilization`

Macro principle:
- 도시의 아름다움이 skyline ornament가 아니라 **사람·물·열·화물·데이터가 움직이는 연결구조**에서 나옴.

Visual emphasis:
- shuttle/tether transfer;
- queue and docking flow;
- life-support/service corridors;
- cargo and hospital movement;
- repair access.

Strength:
- GA3 전쟁/경제/노동과 직접 연결.

Risk:
- 너무 기술도면처럼 보일 수 있음.

### D — `Lived-In Civic Memory`

Human-scale principle:
- 도시를 대표하는 것은 거대 node가 아니라 같은 장소가 `출근 / 급식 / 시위 / 피난 / 추모`에 반복 사용되는 모습.

Existing-source anchors:
- public repair board;
- registered memorial vs worker/transit memorial;
- ration/market line;
- school/identity center;
- mixed transit food areas;
- worker hall / machine access.

Strength:
- 거대한 세계관을 사람 기억으로 축소.

Risk:
- orbital-scale uniqueness가 약해질 수 있음.

## 3. Recommended Hybrid

**A + C for macro identity, B for spatial distinction, D for reader emotion.**

One-line recall:
> `멀리서는 여러 거주·산업 구조가 하나의 고장 난 노드 주위에 매달린 도시이고, 가까이서는 모든 벽과 사람의 동선에 누가 무엇을 고쳤는지가 남아 있는 곳.`

This is proposal language, not new canon prose.

## 4. Spatial Memory — One Image Per Existing Zone

### 4.1 Anchor Crown

What reader must feel:
- 교통/권한/위험이 집중되지만 `도시 전체`는 아님.

Visual memory:
- node/service structure + docking/traffic corridors + access/repair layers.

Motion:
- nonrotating environment; handholds/tethers/controlled motion.

Conflict visible without exposition:
- local physical operation;
- Imperial/creditor/authentication layers use different access/seal surfaces.

Hard veto:
- throne-room-like central command center.

### 4.2 Civic Ring

What reader must feel:
- `사람이 사는 도시`.

Visual memory:
- curved long sightline from rotation;
- housing/market/hospital/education sharing infrastructure.

Human memory anchors:
- queue, school commute, medical transfer, public board, memorial.

Hard veto:
- spotless utopian cylinder.

### 4.3 Foundry Cylinder

What reader must feel:
- city survival is physical labor.

Visual memory:
- large cradle/machine halls;
- worker access paths;
- replacement panels and old standards coexisting.

Sound:
- work cycles, transport, heavy lift, shutdown silence.

Hard veto:
- every surface sparks/steams for industrial atmosphere.

### 4.4 Garden Chain

What reader must feel:
- food/air/water is infrastructure, not pastoral escape.

Visual memory:
- biological production and care spaces tied to power/water service.

Ordinary sensory anchor:
- fresh greens/fermented protein/rare fruit distribution already exists in city bible.

Hard veto:
- luxury nature resort.

### 4.5 Transit Belt

What reader must feel:
- temporary people become permanent enough to build lives without full standing.

Visual memory:
- joined cargo/hab modules;
- multiple route marks;
- informal market/message surfaces;
- repair layers from different origins.

Hard veto:
- chaotic slum shorthand.

### 4.6 Surface Works

What reader must feel:
- energy/propellant/thermal survival has a cost far from civic visibility.

Visual memory:
- heavy anchor/thermal infrastructure;
- difficult evacuation routes;
- worker-scale against massive systems.

Hard veto:
- generic mine planet.

## 5. Material Grammar — No Final Palette Yet

Exact color palette remains AUTHOR HOLD.

The material hierarchy should be function-derived:

1. `long-life structural` — main habitat/node/yard skeleton;
2. `replaceable service` — panels, conduits, inspection covers;
3. `local repair` — mismatched but documented replacement material;
4. `public human layer` — signs, handrails, seating, market/clinic/school surfaces;
5. `emergency layer` — temporary barriers, route strips, triage/evacuation marks.

Rule:
- patchwork is organized history, not random visual noise.

## 6. Public UI / Signage Memory

Do not invent hundreds of futuristic holograms.

Use existing civic conflicts:

- repair/shift contribution board;
- identity/claim correction status;
- transport/transition queue;
- cooling/energy restriction notice;
- ration/service eligibility;
- memorial list.

Reader-facing principle:
> 같은 UI가 GA3 초반에는 생활정보, 공성전에서는 생존정보, 결말에는 복구기록으로 바뀌어야 한다.

## 7. Sensory Grammar

### Motion / gravity

Ardis cannot have one gravity feel.

- Anchor: nonrotating work/docking movement.
- Civic/Foundry/Garden: each habitat rotation/operation context.
- shuttle/tether transfers make `도시 이동` an event.

### Sound

Use causes, not generic sci-fi hum:
- docking/traffic cycle;
- foundry shift machinery;
- ventilation/water service;
- temporary Transit Belt modules;
- emergency shutdown silence.

### Temperature / air

Cooling/energy shortage changes comfort and work conditions.

### Food

Existing canon-review life:
- rationed greens;
- fermented protein;
- rare fruit;
- route-mixed transit cooking.

These are enough. Do not invent a cuisine encyclopedia.

## 8. Landmark Policy

No new monumental landmark is required.

Instead convert existing functional spaces into recurring landmarks.

### Candidate L1 — Public Repair/Failure Board

Existing basis:
- public boards record repair/shift contributions and unresolved failures.

Story reuse:
- ordinary work;
- political argument;
- casualty/strike context;
- siege priorities;
- postwar memory.

### Candidate L2 — Dual Memorial Contrast

Existing basis:
- official registered-casualty memorial vs worker/transit memorial with aliases/unknowns.

Story reuse:
- identity conflict;
- Serin/record arc;
- later plural-history payoff.

### Candidate L3 — Transfer Bottleneck

Not a new named site; select an existing Civic/Anchor transfer point when episode card demands it.

Story reuse:
- commute;
- cargo dispute;
- medical transfer;
- riot;
- evacuation.

Rule:
- landmark earns memory by repeated function, not by architectural extravagance.

## 9. Three Visual States

### A0 — Arrival / strained normal

- city operates at degraded capacity;
- repair layers visible but routines exist;
- queues and delays, not apocalypse.

### A1 — Siege / denial

- some routes closed;
- emergency markings and redirected flows accumulate;
- damage affects actual travel/service.

### A2 — Joint-service recovery

- repairs do not reset surfaces;
- some emergency marks become permanent historical layers;
- local/new standards coexist rather than one faction repainting the city.

## 10. Collectible Desire Beats

Reader should want to `collect` Ardis by understanding more of it:

1. first sight — `이게 한 도시가 맞나?`
2. first transfer — `왜 같은 도시인데 이동에 이렇게 오래 걸리나?`
3. first public board — `누가 실제로 이 도시를 살리고 있나?`
4. first shortage choice — `어느 zone을 먼저 살릴까?`
5. siege — `익숙해진 장소가 어떻게 전장이 되나?`
6. recovery — `수리했지만 무엇은 되돌아오지 않나?`
7. later return/reference — `Ardis가 Rian 없이 무엇을 선택했나?`

## 11. Anti-Generic-SF City Red Team

FAIL if:
- 모든 zone이 파란 홀로그램과 금속복도;
- skyline만 거대하고 사람이 사는 공간이 없음;
- 노동자/피난민을 dirty slum visual로 단순화;
- Civic Ring만 `진짜 도시`처럼 보임;
- Garden은 초록색, Foundry는 주황색처럼 color zoning만 함;
- siege damage가 다음 장면에서 사라짐;
- Rian 도착 전 도시가 멈춰 있는 것처럼 보임;
- 한 시장/시장실이 도시 전체를 대표.
