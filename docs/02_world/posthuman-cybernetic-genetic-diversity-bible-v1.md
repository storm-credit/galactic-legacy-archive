## 1. Core principle

《은하유산록》에서 “강화인간”과 “사이보그”는 종족명이 아니다.

They describe different combinations of:

- treatment;
- adaptation;
- elective augmentation;
- occupation;
- military requirement;
- inherited biology;
- current embodiment;
- vendor/service dependence.

A person with prosthetics remains a person, not an equipment class.

No augmentation may provide free power. Every major gain must carry costs in at least two of:

- energy;
- heat;
- maintenance;
- medicine;
- immune management;
- data/privacy;
- certification;
- replacement parts;
- psychological adaptation;
- training;
- legal restriction;
- failure mode;
- social stigma or dependency.

## 2. Classification axes

Do not use a linear `normal → enhanced → superhuman` ladder.

Every augmented person is described on separate axes:

### A. Biological inheritance

- B0 baseline/local natural variation;
- B1 non-heritable somatic treatment;
- B2 heritable disease prevention;
- B3 stable engineered adaptation;
- B4 divergent engineered clade;
- B5 provenance disputed/experimental lineage.

### B. Mechanical integration

- C0 external equipment only;
- C1 removable prosthetic/assist;
- C2 implanted but replaceable subsystem;
- C3 distributed cybernetic integration;
- C4 majority synthetic embodiment with biological continuity;
- C5 body-continuity legally/medically disputed.

### C. Neural integration

- N0 ordinary interface;
- N1 wearable/temporary neural access;
- N2 implanted sensory/motor bridge;
- N3 high-bandwidth occupational interface;
- N4 persistent distributed neural co-processing;
- N5 experimental continuity-dependent architecture.

### D. Purpose

- medical;
- environmental;
- industrial;
- cognitive/sensory;
- military;
- reproductive/adaptive;
- aesthetic/cultural;
- mixed.

### E. Support dependence

- S0 locally maintainable;
- S1 common certified service;
- S2 specialist regional service;
- S3 proprietary network dependence;
- S4 unique/experimental support;
- S5 degrading/no viable replacement ecology.

No single axis determines legal personhood or social value.

## 3. Major augmentation families

### 3.1 Therapeutic prosthetics and organ replacement

Purpose:
- restore or support current function.

Examples:
- limb prosthetics;
- cloned/engineered organs;
- artificial joints;
- sensory restoration;
- cardiac/respiratory support;
- neural stabilization.

Rule:
- “restorative” is not a neutral category; the person decides whether restoration to an earlier body is desirable.

### 3.2 Occupational adaptation

Purpose:
- survive or work in specific environments.

Possible uses:
- radiation shielding implants;
- pressure adaptation;
- low-g musculoskeletal support;
- industrial sensory overlays;
- maintenance interface ports;
- extended vacuum/emergency tolerance.

Cost:
- specialized medicine, body strain and off-duty inconvenience.

### 3.3 Pilot/interface augmentation

Purpose:
- reduce control latency, improve sensor integration or protect against neural feedback.

Controls:
- does not grant tactical genius;
- high bandwidth increases injury and spoofing risk;
- cockpit/machine compatibility still matters;
- trained non-augmented pilots can outperform poorly supported augmented pilots.

Black Ward is existing evidence that interface medicine and optimization can save lives while creating coercive dependency.

### 3.4 Military enhancement

Possible domains:
- acceleration tolerance;
- reflex pathway support;
- clotting/trauma stabilization;
- sensory expansion;
- fatigue management;
- toxin/radiation resistance;
- neural command interfaces.

Hard prohibitions:
- no universal supersoldier package;
- no loyalty gene;
- no intelligence gene;
- no painless permanent combat mode;
- no enhancement without recovery, logistics and long-term health consequence.

Military red-team question:

> if an enhancement is so useful that every army should adopt it, why have they not?

The answer must be material, medical, legal, tactical or economic—not “tradition.”

### 3.5 Civilian elective augmentation

Possible motivations:
- sensory art;
- sport;
- profession;
- communication;
- beauty/body identity;
- disability adaptation;
- cultural membership.

Rule:
- visible cybernetics cannot be coded automatically as poverty, villainy or military history.

### 3.6 Majority-synthetic embodiment

Possible future range:
- biological brain/continuity with largely synthetic body;
- distributed life-support body;
- interchangeable occupational shells under strict continuity controls.

This area remains proposal-level because existing canon strongly separates personhood from hardware ownership.

Key legal principle:
- owning or financing a body component does not create ownership of the person.

## 4. Heritable lineages versus augmentation

A cybernetic person is not a new species simply because their body is heavily modified.

A population becomes a candidate `human-derived clade` only if several conditions persist across generations:

- heritable biological divergence;
- stable developmental pathway;
- materially different medicine/environment;
- recognized community continuity;
- not merely a purchased product generation.

Reproductive compatibility may be:

- fully compatible;
- medically assisted;
- partially compatible;
- incompatible.

No category implies superiority or purity.

## 5. Failure modes

### Medical

- immune rejection;
- scar/nerve overgrowth;
- endocrine imbalance;
- chronic pain;
- sensory overload;
- thrombosis/infection;
- developmental side effects.

### Mechanical

- wear;
- seal failure;
- actuator fatigue;
- connector damage;
- unavailable materials.

### Neural

- phantom feedback;
- desynchronization;
- latency instability;
- interface seizures;
- identity/body-map distress;
- dependency on calibration models.

### Cyber/security

- data extraction;
- spoofing;
- malicious updates;
- revoked certificates;
- manufacturer lockout;
- compromised diagnostic ports.

### Institutional

- insurer refuses maintenance;
- military owns service contract;
- employer controls treatment access;
- border authority rejects implant certification;
- parts sanctions make a healthy person medically vulnerable.

Major story incidents should combine at least two failure layers.

## 6. Economy and class

Augmentation creates unequal access but not one-directional class coding.

Possible class patterns:

- wealthy citizens with elective premium augmentation;
- workers whose employer-funded implants tie them to a contract;
- veterans whose military hardware is expensive to civilianize;
- frontier people with crude but maintainable systems;
- patients with excellent implants but no recognized identity;
- elite pilots with high-performance systems and short service life;
- communities that deliberately reject proprietary augmentation.

Critical cost fields:

- purchase;
- surgery;
- calibration;
- consumables;
- replacement cycle;
- software/diagnostics;
- rehabilitation;
- downtime;
- insurance;
- travel to compatible clinic.

## 7. Law and rights

Required questions:

- who owns implanted hardware after contract termination?
- can a company remotely disable non-life-critical functions?
- what counts as assault when software changes a body?
- does customs inspection require body-data access?
- may military command mandate removal/replacement?
- who pays after service injury?
- can creditors repossess external components?
- what happens when removing hardware risks death?

Working proposal:

1. personhood outranks hardware title;
2. life-supporting integrated hardware receives anti-repossession protection;
3. remote disablement that threatens health is prohibited outside narrowly defined emergency law;
4. body telemetry is medical/personal data, not ordinary equipment telemetry;
5. military/employer access expires or narrows after duty unless current safety requires it;
6. replacement and maintenance obligations can survive contract termination.

No canon promotion until P03/legal audit.

## 8. Culture and identity

Do not assume people want to “become normal.”

Potential identities:

- prosthetic culture;
- veteran modification community;
- body-design art movements;
- industrial maintainer lineages;
- high-g pilot subculture;
- anti-implant/refusal communities;
- open-hardware body rights movements;
- proprietary-certified professional guilds.

Conflict can occur within the same biological species and faction.

## 9. Military organization consequences

A force using heavily augmented personnel requires:

- medical maintenance;
- compatible evacuation;
- spare modules;
- cyber-defense;
- implant-certified armor/suits;
- long-term veteran care;
- duty rotation;
- legal responsibility for upgrades and removal.

Therefore an elite augmented unit is a **logistics institution**, not just better soldiers.

Possible organization types for later design:

- Imperial high-g interception cadres;
- Helix certified recovery/security specialists;
- frontier augmentation cooperatives;
- knightly/heirloom units whose body-interface traditions are institutional rather than genetic destiny;
- illegal debt-bound enhancement crews;
- rescue/medical cybernetic specialists.

These are role seeds only, not named canon factions.

## 10. Maneuver-frame implications

Augmented pilots may change:

- cockpit restraint;
- control bandwidth;
- sensory channel count;
- emergency disconnect;
- medical monitoring;
- acceleration envelope;
- heat/cooling interface;
- authentication.

But a pilot enhancement does not automatically justify a new frame model.

New chassis threshold remains:
- different load-bearing frame;
- at least two major systems redesigned;
- independent parts/certification/loss path.

Potential blind spot:
- if high-bandwidth pilots dominate too strongly, unaugmented pilots become obsolete and the existing cast ecology collapses.

Control:
- high-bandwidth interface should trade versatility and long-term safety for local performance;
- service/open-standard frames like 07 can retain advantage through broad compatibility and manual fallback.

---

## 11. Archive and identity

Archive must not classify people by enhancement as collectible rarity.

Potential record errors:

- one person appears as separate identities after body replacement;
- a military call-sign persists after the original body/interface changes;
- vendor serials are mistaken for identity continuity;
- collective unit achievements are attributed to an “enhanced ace.”

Story rule:
- augmentation can complicate evidence but cannot become a cheap immortality mechanism.

---

## 12. Red-team stop conditions

Reject or redesign if:

- augmentation has no maintenance burden;
- all poor people are visibly cybernetic;
- all elites look biologically pristine;
- military augmentation creates superpowers with no doctrine response;
- gene editing determines personality;
- disability exists only to motivate revenge;
- body replacement silently equals resurrection;
- hacking an implant becomes instant mind control;
- one vendor can disable billions of people without existing politics preventing it;
- every named ace requires illegal enhancement;
- enhancements erase the strategic value of frames/ships/logistics.
