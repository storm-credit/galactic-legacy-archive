## 1. Theater Purpose

GA5 needs a theater where:

- several fleets are close enough to cooperate but not already unified;
- no single claimant controls all routes, depots and yards;
- civilians, shipyards and military fronts compete for protection;
- a successful ownerless-fleet charter can be proven operationally;
- the victory commits ships/supplies and creates the later Second Orpheus dilemma.

Working theater:
- `베스퍼 접경전역 / Vesper March Theater`.

Location:
- between Crown Meridian, Lumen/Ardis corridor and two major provincial/frontier fronts.

Population affected:
- 4.8–7.2 billion across systems directly or indirectly dependent on the theater.

## 3. Key Sites

### V01 — Concord Gate

Class:
- L1/L2 military-civil junction under compact authority dispute.

Function:
- Core entry, order authentication and fleet assembly.

Control:
- compact General Staff, Continuity key staff and Crown/Regent observers.

Conflict:
- Protector-aligned forces want unified command.
- provinces require mandate/recall terms.

### V02 — Helios Spur

Function:
- shipyard supply branch and repair staging connected to Helios industry.

Control:
- yard consortium, Helix contracts, compact industrial agreement.

Conflict:
- which formations receive repairs, missiles and parts.

### V03 — Vesper Depot

Function:
- main fuel, ammunition, food, medical and fleet-support depot.

Population:
- 11–18 million military/civilian workers and dependents across facilities.

Control:
- disputed Treasury/Protector/provincial/worker authority.

Strategic value:
- fleet that holds it can sustain theater operations.

Weakness:
- fixed, politically visible and vulnerable to sabotage/blockade.

### V04 — Crown Passage

Function:
- protected route for Crown Habitat, diplomatic traffic and civilian administrative movement.

Control:
- Crown Guard and civil authorities.

Conflict:
- military wants route for reinforcement; Crown Guard prioritizes civilian/household continuity.

### V05 — Deme Yard

Function:
- medium naval yard, mobile tender base and salvage integration.

Population:
- 0.6–1.0 billion in system/industrial habitats.

Control:
- provincial government, workers, Helix/Imperial liens.

Strategic value:
- can restore escorts/support ships faster than Helios but lacks capital-ship capacity.

GA5 role:
- maintenance-pool and worker-right conflict.

### V06 — Talren Habitats

Function:
- dense civilian/industrial habitat chain and convoy junction.

Population:
- 1.2–1.8 billion.

Control:
- civil authorities, provincial compact and local defense.

Strategic value:
- food/labor/civilian legitimacy; cannot be sacrificed without major political break.

### V07 — Provincial Fork

Function:
- routes diverge toward several provincial members and home defenses.

Control:
- provincial fleets/councils.

GA5 role:
- recall/withdrawal and burden-sharing crisis.

### V08 — Orpheus Approach

Function:
- military/civil route junction leading to the later Orpheus node/refugee-capital conflict zone.

At GA5:
- not yet in full crisis; signs of population movement, route congestion and competing military priorities begin.

Strategic value:
- whoever controls it can move fleets or civilian convoys toward/away from coming disaster.

### V09 — Outer Front

Function:
- active combat frontier against compact rejecters, opportunists, separatists or rival field command.

Control:
- FC-005/theater fleet and local populations.

Conflict:
- Core compact appears distant; local commander claims earned authority.

### V10 — Civilian Chain

Function:
- habitats, refugee/merchant routes, food/medical convoy line.

Population:
- hundreds of millions moving/depending on route.

GA5 role:
- tests rescue/parole and civilian mission limits.

GA6 link:
- becomes one source of mass evacuation toward Second Orpheus.

### V11 — Grey Route

Function:
- lower-capacity Ardis/Neutral/Independence route bypass.

Control:
- mixed local operators and informal networks.

Strategic value:
- alternative courier/rescue/repair route, not enough for a grand fleet.

## 4. Theater Force Distribution at GA5 Opening

### Compact/Protector-aligned near V01–V03

- FLT-01 elements: 18–28 combat ships + support.
- FLT-02 elements: 20–30 combat ships + support.
- total not under one uncontested commander.

### Crown Guard near V04

- 10–15 combat/escort ships + royal/civilian support.

### Provincial Concord near V07/Talren

- 25–40 combat ships, 25–60 support/transport; dispersed among provinces.

### Helios/Deme yard forces

- 12–22 combat/security ships, tenders and armed tugs.

### Outer Front / FC-005

- 30–50 combat ships, 25–45 support/converted ships, strong local legitimacy.

### P-001/Ardis route coalition

- 8–15 combat ships + 15–30 support/rescue/courier.

No force can secure Vesper Depot, Talren, Outer Front, Crown Passage and Orpheus Approach simultaneously.

## 5. Campaign Strategic Problems

1. **Vesper Depot Control** — pay, fuel, ammunition and maintenance allocation.
2. **Deme Yard Access** — repair title, labor and readiness priority.
3. **Provincial Recall** — home systems demand ships return.
4. **Outer Front Command** — FC-005 refuses compact ownership but needs support.
5. **Civilian Chain Protection** — rescue/convoy missions reduce combat strength.
6. **Authentication and Order Trust** — compact/protector/province/crown/continuity commands diverge.
7. **Crown Passage** — civilian legitimacy vs military route use.
8. **Orpheus Approach** — early strategic choice between capital/front forces and growing civilian movement.

## 6. Campaign Phases

### Phase 1 — Assembly Without Ownership

Location:
- V01–V03.

Goal:
- form temporary mission force and keep depot functioning.

Problem:
- ships accept different orders and conditions.

### Phase 2 — Repair and Pay War

Location:
- V02/V03/V05.

Goal:
- restore readiness and allocate scarce parts/ammunition/pay.

Problem:
- yard/worker/Helix/claimant leverage.

### Phase 3 — Provincial Withdrawal Crisis

Location:
- V06/V07/V10.

Goal:
- protect civilians/routes while provinces recall ships.

Problem:
- voluntary coalition vs enforceable burden.

### Phase 4 — Outer Front Choice

Location:
- V09/V11/V08.

Goal:
- cooperate or confront FC-005 and prevent theater collapse.

Problem:
- performance legitimacy and local sovereignty.

### Phase 5 — First Common Fleet Operation

Location:
- multi-front V03/V06/V09/V08.

Goal:
- execute shared intent with subordinate autonomous fronts.

Problem:
- one front must be under-resourced and one commander may refuse/withdraw.

End:
- coalition proves charter but commits reserves, damages route and drives civilians/fleets toward Orpheus Approach.

## 7. GA5 Major Mission Shapes

### Mission A — Depot Mutiny/Seizure Prevention

- secure supplies/pay records without treating workers/crews as enemies.
- limited interior/ship/frame action.

### Mission B — Repair Tender Rescue

- one mobile tender carrying parts/engineers becomes more valuable than cruiser.
- escort/capture under heat/ammunition constraints.

### Mission C — Crown Civilian Passage

- protect symbolic/civilian convoy while military front demands ships.
- FC-003 owns key decision.

### Mission D — Provincial Recall Convoy Split

- ships choose home recall vs common mission; families/food/routes involved.
- H-001/FC-002/CR-001 conflict.

### Mission E — Surrender/Parole Fleet

- enemy/disputed ships can join only with crew/legal/rescue terms.
- tests no-personal-prize doctrine.

### Mission F — Outer Front Relief

- P-001 and FC-005 cooperate/conflict against a third threat.
- no simple duel decides legitimacy.

### Mission G — Multi-Front Charter Test

- three to five formations execute shared intent independently.
- one subordinate commander wins decisive front without P-001.

## 8. Reinforcement and Communication Clocks

Core to V01:
- 0.8–1.8 days physical after mobilization; authorization/political delays vary.

V01 to V03/V04:
- 1–2 days.

V03 to V06/V07:
- 1–3 days.

V07/V11 to V09/V08:
- 1–4 days.

Priority short orders:
- hours to 1 day across inner theater.
- 1–3 days to Outer Front under damage/jamming.

Full evidence/after-action:
- 1–5 days or courier.

Major fleet concentration:
- 1–3 weeks minimum and visible.

## 9. GA6 Causal Link

GA5 ending should create Second Orpheus conditions through success, not random disaster:

- charter fleet protects Vesper/Talren/Outer Front but expends missiles, crews and repair capacity.
- several civilian chains relocate toward V08 Orpheus Approach because the ownerless fleet route appears safer.
- Compact/Protector/capital forces concentrate to secure strategic junction and state institutions.
- FC-005/local commanders and provincial fleets retain separate priorities.
- route damage/denial leaves capital fleet and civilian convoys in competing windows.
- neutral/rescue capacity is limited by accumulated obligations/debts.

Thus GA6 asks whether to save state fleet/institutions or mass civilians under conditions GA5 created.
