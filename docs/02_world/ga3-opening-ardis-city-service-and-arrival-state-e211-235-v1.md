## 1. Canonical Scale

Ardis at E211:
- permanent population: approximately **1.42 million**;
- transient/stranded population: approximately **170,000**, fluctuating;
- damaged L3 relay with contested path toward regional L2 function;
- nominal current throughput: approximately **46%** of pre-damage rating during stable operation;
- reliable normal operation: roughly **two short transition windows per day** plus low-bandwidth synchronization;
- Silex one-window access from GA2 is closed and not repeatable on demand.

Ardis is already governed, worked and maintained by local people. It is not an empty city waiting for Rian.

## 5. Six Immediate City Problems

### A. Node throughput and phase-reference instability

- 46% stable throughput does not mean 46% of all demand is met;
- queue timing, mass, authentication and recovery capacity determine actual service;
- one damaged phase-reference branch and split control stack remain.

### B. Cooling and power allocation

- Anchor Crown, Foundry, hospitals, Garden Chain and Transit Belt all have valid current claims;
- allocating delivered cooling components to one zone creates visible losses elsewhere.

### C. Payroll, debt and work legitimacy

- workers/operators have delayed/contested pay and injury claims;
- creditors and outside restorers may offer money tied to control or priority;
- unpaid labor cannot be treated as infinite emergency resource.

### D. Identity and temporary standing

- residents, stranded travellers and unregistered Transit Belt people have different current records;
- correcting an old record does not restore lost food, housing, treatment or work;
- provisional local standing may not travel outside Ardis.

### E. Food/water/biological transfer

- Garden Chain production exists but transport, power, cold-chain and queue failures can create local scarcity;
- food is a system and labor problem, not only total tonnage.

### F. External claims and internal legitimacy

- Imperial, Helix, Neutral, creditor and route actors offer resources with current conditions;
- local workers, city institutions, temporary residents and operators do not share one political position;
- no arriving protagonist may appoint one city representative.

Not:
- restore Ardis;
- become governor;
- open a permanent route;
- defeat one villain faction.

The trial may coordinate:

It may not transfer:

- permanent node title;
