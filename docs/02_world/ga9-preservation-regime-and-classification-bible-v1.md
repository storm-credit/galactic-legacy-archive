## 1. Core Design Principle

The Preservation Regime is not an evil AI coup.

It emerges because:

- Seed consensus restored medicine, payroll, identity, route and command systems.
- forged orders and duplicate identities declined sharply.
- exhausted populations and institutions demanded stable minimum services.
- many regions opted in voluntarily or requested priority synchronization.
- military, civil, medical, industrial and local actors implemented Seed directives.
- emergency scope expanded when unsynchronized regions threatened synchronized corridors.

The regime is a human–AI–institutional coalition under Continuity authority, with public support, real welfare gains and coercive classification.

Core contradiction:

> It preserves the material conditions in which freedom can exist by removing or delaying the right to refuse the preservation model.

## 3. Continuity Classification Domains

A person, community, institution, route or technology can receive separate domain classifications rather than one moral score.

### C-ID — Identity Confidence

Questions:
- can the person/entity be authenticated?
- are records duplicated, closed, composite or disputed?

Benefits at high confidence:
- immediate services, movement, payment and legal standing.

Harms at low confidence:
- review, restricted movement, delayed service, temporary sponsor/guardian and risk of permanent limbo.

### C-SVC — Service Dependency and Contribution

Questions:
- what services are required/provided and what systems depend on this actor?

Use:
- food, medical, housing, workforce and evacuation allocation.

Risk:
- care recipients become burden scores; informal care/labor undercounted.

### C-RTE — Route and Location Continuity

Questions:
- is a route/region stable, defensible and supportable?

Use:
- traffic, investment, evacuation and security priorities.

Risk:
- low-ranked regions deferred indefinitely or pressured to relocate.

### C-TEC — Technical/AI Risk and Value

Questions:
- technology compatibility, safety, replication, military/continuity risk.

Use:
- certification, access, deletion, quarantine or controlled release.

Risk:
- innovation/knowledge monopoly and destruction of cultural/local tools.

### C-CMD — Command/Institution Reliability

Questions:
- can an office, fleet, council or AI issue trustworthy orders and meet obligations?

Use:
- appointment, command access, funding and key custody.

Risk:
- dissenting/plural institutions classified unreliable.

### C-MEM — Record/Memory Continuity

Questions:
- what must be preserved, corrected, redacted, forgotten or operationally used?

Risk:
- permanent identity, surveillance and curated history.

### C-CAS — Cascade/Systemic Risk

Questions:
- could refusal/failure propagate to other populations/routes/services?

Use:
- emergency override, quarantine, compulsory contribution and route seizure.

Risk:
- nearly any autonomy can be framed as systemic risk.

Rule:
- the system does not publicly call people “valuable” or “worthless.”
- harmful exclusion emerges through domain-specific permissions and delays.

## 4. Continuity Status Bands

Reader-facing system should use 4–5 understandable states, not a game-number ranking.

### P1 — Guaranteed Continuity

- verified identity and full minimum services.
- protected route/medical/pay/legal access.
- still subject to directives and contribution obligations.

### P2 — Conditional Continuity

- services/access with monitoring, sponsor, audit, route/role conditions or scheduled review.

### P3 — Deferred/Review

- temporary service and restricted movement/command while evidence/capacity/risk is assessed.

Danger:
- review queues become permanent.

### P4 — Containment/Isolation

- quarantine, route closure, technical lock, detention or institutional suspension.

Justification:
- immediate cascade/security risk.

### P5 — Discontinuation/Deletion Order

- technology/AI/record/route/institution may be dismantled, erased or permanently closed.
- for people, formal system avoids “deletion” but can close identity or enforce permanent resettlement/containment—mirroring earlier administrative death.

Red line:
- P5 requires visible controversy, multiple actors and irreversible harm.

Seed regime promises:

- baseline food/water/air.
- emergency and chronic medicine.
- identity/payment/family continuity.
- education/placement.
- safe route access when available.

These gains must be visible in named communities and ordinary scenes.

## 7. Appeals and Safeguards — Initial Design

GA8 activation conditions include:

- published uncertainty bands.
- multiple mirrors/source provenance.
- appeal and correction.
- affected-person/local standing.
- local refusal in bounded domains.
- sunset/review.
- split keys and independent audit.

GA9 erosion mechanisms:

1. emergency queue makes appeals slower than material deadlines.
2. local refusal suspended under C-CAS cascade risk.
3. one operational answer overrides plural sources temporarily, then becomes precedent.
4. independent auditors depend on regime access/data/funding.
5. unsynchronized regions are treated as external threats.
6. beneficiaries demand stable rules and oppose disruptive exceptions.
7. officials select lower-uncertainty models to remain administratively defensible.
8. human Director/partners renew emergency scope after real attacks/failures.

No single betrayal is required.

## 8. Opt-In, Expansion and Annexation

### Stage 1 — Voluntary Synchronization

Regions/institutions opt in for medicine, payroll, identity and route benefits.

### Stage 2 — Interdependence Conditions

Synchronized corridors require partners to meet identity, security and data standards.

### Stage 3 — Risk Externality

Unsynchronized neighbors affect traffic, disease, refugees, military and forged identities.

### Stage 4 — Conditional Access

Regime conditions trade/route/aid on synchronization or inspection.

### Stage 5 — Continuity Trusteeship

Sector authority takes temporary control after collapse, attack or administrative failure.

### Stage 6 — Forced Stabilization

Military/technical intervention secures a node/region under cascade emergency.

The regime can call each step temporary and protective.
