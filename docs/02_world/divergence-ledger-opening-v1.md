# D-001 — Betrayal Trial Data Captured

Intervention:
- P-001 and H-001 seize a fragment of the conditional betrayal trial’s relay data and avoid the four official answers.

Original Event:
- OT-02: H-001 follows the coerced leak path, team deaths occur, blame attaches to him.

Changed Event:
- no clean betrayal result; trial manipulation evidence exists outside the examiner’s immediate control.

Scale:
- B2 organization-level.

Immediate Winners:
- H-001 avoids immediate sole-blame classification.
- P-001 gains leverage and a shared secret.
- field instructors can question the trial result.

Immediate Losers:
- Operations Bloc loses clean behavioral data.
- Security Bloc cannot identify its internal leak with confidence.
- Helix receives incomplete trial metrics.

Affected People:
- H-001 protector/community.
- the other teams used as test variables.
- examiner and data technician.
- future internal informant.

Affected Resources:
- trial log fragment.
- access path through the service spine.
- student risk profiles.

Affected Institutions:
- academy Operations, Security, Research.
- imperial observer’s secret selection process.

Public Interpretation:
- students exploited a flaw or cheated.

Secret Interpretations:
- Operations: an examiner or student altered the data.
- Security: possible Independence Front penetration.
- Imperial Intelligence: a high-value command mind may be present.

Transmission Paths:
- Data: missing packets and copied relay fragment.
- Institutional: exam scores affect placement and contracts.
- Emotional: H-001 experiences a shared alternative to obedience.
- Symbolic: rumor of a ‘fifth answer’ spreads among students.

First-Order Effects — Episodes 5–15:

1. H-001’s protector contract is escalated from routine leverage to secure review.
2. Operations designs later tests with fewer visible loopholes.
3. Security increases surveillance of service shafts and student communications.
4. Other students seek P-001 for rule-breaking solutions, raising attention.
5. Imperial observer flags P-001/H-001 as a linked variable rather than separate candidates.

Second-Order Effects — Episodes 16–50:

1. the academy stops relying on voluntary betrayal and uses contract/legal traps.
2. a field instructor may copy or protect other manipulated test records.
3. student folklore around the fifth answer encourages cooperation but also reckless imitation.
4. H-001’s original stigma is weakened, so a different scapegoat may be chosen.
5. Helix requests raw neural data to recover the lost behavioral result.

Third-Order Risks — Episodes 50–150:

1. academy reforms its coercion methods and becomes harder to expose.
2. a surviving original scapegoat could become an antagonist who blames P-001 for redirecting punishment.
3. the fifth-answer myth becomes a political slogan beyond P-001’s control.
4. early proof of test manipulation may trigger premature academy shutdown, scattering students into worse contracts.

Knowledge Invalidated:
- K1 memory of who betrays whom in the first cohort.
- K3 official record of H-001’s first betrayal.
- K4 Blood Admiral causal summary.

New Opportunities:
- evidence set for academy legal challenge.
- H-001 agency arc.
- student cooperative networks.

Unintended Cost:
- protector transfer accelerated.
- pressure transferred to other students and staff.

Next Review:
- episode 25, 45, 90.

# D-002 — H-001 Chooses a Joint Record Operation

Intervention:
- H-001 does not simply accept or reject the Independence Front’s escape offer; he proposes using the White Dock mission to seize distributed custody records.

Original Event:
- OT-05/10 path: H-001 remains passive until protector loss, then later joins liberation forces under extreme conditions.

Changed Event:
- H-001 begins political and operational agency before the traumatic break.

Scale:
- B2 with B3 potential.

Immediate Winners:
- H-001 gains a plan that includes his community rather than only himself.
- P-001 gains a strategist, not a rescued subordinate.
- Liberation Network may obtain broader evidence.

Immediate Losers:
- academy loses the ability to predict H-001 through simple leverage.
- Independence contact loses control of the recruitment narrative.

Affected Resources:
- custody lists, ration contracts, transport schedules.
- H-001’s trust and independent network.

Affected Institutions:
- academy, Liberation Network, neutral legal channels, refugee cooperative.

Public Interpretation:
- none initially; looks like ordinary mission cooperation.

Secret Interpretations:
- Independence contact: H-001 may be a future organizer or double agent.
- P-001: the original future is already becoming unusable.
- H-001: P-001 is useful but may try to own the plan.

Transmission Paths:
- Information: distributed records.
- Relationship: H-001 begins direct negotiation.
- Institutional: refugee cooperative becomes a political actor rather than private hostage.
- Ideological: liberation through evidence and shared control competes with destruction.

First-Order Effects — Episodes 12–25:

1. H-001 negotiates separate conditions with P-001, Independence, and neutral observers.
2. his protector/community learns that he is acting, changing their own behavior.
3. the academy moves the protector earlier and fragments the contract records.
4. Independence factions debate whether evidence or immediate extraction is more valuable.

Second-Order Effects — Episodes 25–80:

1. H-001 builds a small logistics-information network among students, workers, and families.
2. the Scorched Bloc loses exclusive access to his future grievances.
3. Federalist and Liberation blocs court him earlier.
4. P-001 must tolerate H-001 withholding information and leading separate operations.
5. the protector/community may refuse rescue if escape destroys everyone else’s ration status.

Third-Order Risks — Episodes 80–250:

1. H-001 becomes a political organizer much earlier and may rival P-001.
2. centralized enemies may target entire distributed networks instead of one protector.
3. evidence-based reform may fail publicly, making later destruction doctrine appear more justified.
4. if P-001 manipulates H-001’s network using future knowledge, their eventual break becomes earlier and deeper.

Knowledge Invalidated:
- H-001 recruitment date and faction.
- original order of protector loss, liberation, and command rise.
- future Blood Admiral ideology timeline.

New Opportunities:
- alternate node governance model.
- H-001-led civilian logistics arc.
- internal faction competition within Independence.

Unintended Cost:
- more civilians become visible to state and corporate surveillance.

Next Review:
- episodes 30, 75, 150.

# D-003 — Custody Records Distributed Across Factions

Intervention:
- H-001’s plan sends authenticated fragments and hashes to the academy team, Independence, and the Free Route Neutral Assembly instead of allowing one side to seize the entire ledger.

Original Event:
- OT-04: evidence erased; OT-06 placements proceed without public challenge.

Changed Event:
- no faction has full proof, but no faction can completely erase the existence of the records.

Scale:
- B3 seed.

Immediate Winners:
- students/families gain potential witnesses.
- Neutral Assembly gains legal and information leverage.
- moderate academy staff gain a reason to preserve records.

Immediate Losers:
- Operations cannot quietly settle the incident.
- Helix cannot control all neural-contract records.
- Independence cannot use the full list for unilateral recruitment or propaganda.

Affected Resources:
- data fragments, authentication keys, medical records, insurance claims, transport logs.

Affected Institutions:
- neutral courts, academy administration, Helix legal office, Imperial counterintelligence, refugee organizations.

Public Interpretation:
- initially a data breach and safety incident.

Secret Interpretations:
- Neutral Assembly: possible systemic involuntary contract case.
- Helix: trade-secret theft and contract sabotage.
- Imperial Intelligence: evidence can destabilize multiple agencies.
- Independence: proof of forced recruitment, but incomplete.

Transmission Paths:
- Legal: records can be subpoenaed or authenticated.
- Financial: insurance and debt contracts depend on their validity.
- Political: selective leaks change public narratives.
- Intelligence: each faction tries to locate missing fragments.

First-Order Effects — Episodes 17–40:

1. joint jurisdiction protects the team temporarily.
2. Helix freezes some equipment and family accounts.
3. academy blames rogue officials rather than system design.
4. Neutral Assembly demands medical access and witness interviews.
5. Independence leaks one fragment, causing both sympathy and panic.

Second-Order Effects — Episodes 40–120:

1. legal cases slow student transfers and create a backlog inside the academy.
2. competing factions recruit witnesses, creating betrayal and protection arcs.
3. forged fragments appear, reducing public trust.
4. P-001 must choose whether to release records that could expose vulnerable families.
5. record custody becomes a collectible set with ethical restrictions.

Third-Order Risks — Episodes 120–350:

1. the Empire creates a more sophisticated centralized identity system, accelerating future authoritarian control.
2. open records inspire other schools and prisons to revolt or destroy evidence.
3. Neutral Assembly’s data power grows, making ‘neutral memory’ a new monopoly.
4. the Archive may classify the fragmented records as a historical set and tempt P-001 to complete it at human cost.

Knowledge Invalidated:
- official academy placement lists.
- exact careers and disappearances of multiple future heroes.
- Helix standardization timetable.

New Opportunities:
- legal/investigative arcs.
- noncombat collection and faction alliance.
- early open-standard movement.

Unintended Cost:
- families and witnesses become targets.
- incomplete proof allows every faction to claim a different truth.

Next Review:
- episodes 40, 100, 220.

# D-004 — White Dock Casualties and Evidence Erasure Prevented

Intervention:
- P-001’s team, the enemy ace, and neutral responders preserve civilians and distribute evidence; 07호 is sacrificed rather than allowing the dock and records to be destroyed.

Original Event:
- OT-04: students and civilians die, incident is hidden, Independence portrayed as indiscriminate attackers, P-001/H-002/H-001 paths separate.

Changed Event:
- visible survivors and multi-faction records contradict the official narrative; the first team remains connected.

Scale:
- B3 cluster.

Immediate Winners:
- surviving civilians and students.
- neutral responders and moderate factions.
- enemy ace gains proof that some academy students are not willing instruments.

Immediate Losers:
- Security and Operations lose a clean terror narrative.
- Helix loses immediate control of the frame core and evidence.
- radical Independence factions lose justification for total destruction at the dock.

Affected Resources:
- 07호 core and scattered components.
- dock control key.
- survivor testimony.
- school/Helix contracts.
- enemy ace’s identity and mission record.

Affected Institutions:
- all five opening factions.

Public Interpretation:
- contested: student heroism, internal corruption, terrorist attack, industrial accident.

Secret Interpretations:
- Imperial Intelligence: P-001 may be a strategic anomaly.
- Helix: open-service lineage is active.
- Independence: a cross-faction student cell may be useful or dangerous.
- Neutral Assembly: an enforceable joint-custody precedent may exist.

Transmission Paths:
- Symbolic: students and enemy cooperated to save civilians.
- Legal: survivor and medical evidence.
- Military: enemy ace lives; tactics and identities are shared.
- Industrial: 07호 parts disperse among owners.
- Emotional: team formed through joint sacrifice, not command assignment.

First-Order Effects — Episodes 19–50:

1. team faces inquiry rather than immediate separation because too many witnesses exist.
2. 07호 restoration becomes a multi-location recovery arc.
3. enemy ace becomes recurring negotiator/rival.
4. academy internal blocs fight over blame and reform.
5. students imitate P-001’s mission-first-over-orders behavior.

Second-Order Effects — Episodes 50–150:

1. school mission rules and contracts change to prevent future joint jurisdiction.
2. Helix accelerates deletion of open-service hardware elsewhere.
3. Independence moderates and radicals argue over whether infrastructure can be captured rather than destroyed.
4. Neutral Assembly gains public legitimacy but incurs financial and military pressure.
5. P-001’s team becomes a symbol before it has the organization to bear that role.

Third-Order Risks — Episodes 150–500:

1. early symbolization draws talented people to P-001 and recreates hero concentration sooner.
2. surviving enemy ace alters several original battles and may save or kill different leaders.
3. open-service lineage can destabilize Helix standardization, affecting fleet readiness during civil war.
4. joint jurisdiction precedent may evolve into a new federal order—or become a loophole for endless foreign intervention.

Knowledge Invalidated:
- OT-04 casualty list and propaganda.
- enemy ace death/defection timing.
- 07호 destruction record.
- H-002 forced-contract schedule.
- first cohort placements.

New Opportunities:
- frame recovery and component-set arc.
- rival/ally enemy ace.
- academy reform and legal struggle.
- early neutral-route relationship that can transform OT-16.

Unintended Cost:
- team loses anonymity.
- history changes faster than P-001 can model.
- multiple factions now seek the same people and hardware.

Next Review:
- episodes 35, 80, 160, 300.

## Cross-Divergence Interaction

### D-001 × D-002

- proof of coercion plus H-001 agency can create student organizing.
- risk: academy shifts from overt leverage to covert family transfers.

### D-002 × D-003

- H-001 gains a distributed network and legal evidence.
- risk: more civilians become legible targets.

### D-003 × D-004

- witnesses plus fragmented records prevent easy erasure.
- risk: forged records and selective leaks create truth fatigue.

### All four

Potential structural change:
- the school does not simply collapse; it becomes a contested institution earlier.
- Helix standardization is challenged before war, but fleet compatibility may suffer.
- Independence develops a capture/reform option alongside scorched doctrine.
- Neutral Assembly’s relation with P-001 may change OT-16, but only if debts and trust are repaired over years.

Archive reliability effect:
- academy/early-career records fall from high to low reliability.
- distant imperial succession and outer-front events remain more stable temporarily.
