## 1. Use Rule

The roster prevents every local problem from being solved by the Empire, Helix, Independence or the protagonist.

Each institution must have:

- a service people actually need;
- a constituency;
- resources and limits;
- an internal disagreement;
- a coercive or exclusion risk;
- a reason to act without Rian.

No more than 3–5 new organizations may remain active in one reader-facing arc.

## 10. Reader-Facing Activation Rules

- introduce organization through a person and service, not a paragraph;
- use stable short name after first introduction;
- one institution may represent several background equivalents;
- do not activate two organizations with near-identical service in one arc;
- retire the name when its function leaves the front stage;
- show at least one beneficiary and one excluded/pressured person for major institutions.

## 11. Independence Rule

At least 18 organizations in this roster must make one consequential decision without Rian's proposal, approval or presence.

Examples:
- a union stops a yard despite military urgency;
- a patient council refuses a useful data transfer;
- a claims hall recognizes a worker family over the protagonists;
- a media desk publishes a correction damaging allies;
- a rescue board denies an unsafe heroic plan;
- a sport/art body preserves ordinary life rather than becoming a clue.

## 12. Completion Ruling

> Named minor organizations and public-life institutions are no longer globally deferred.

Additional local organizations may be added only when functionally distinct and within reader-memory limits.
