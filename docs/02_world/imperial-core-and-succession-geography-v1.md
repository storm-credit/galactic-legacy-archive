## 1. Design Principle

- 황도권은 하나의 수도행성이 아니라 여러 노드·행정·기록·군수·의례기관이 분산된 핵심권이다.
- ‘황제를 차지한다’는 것은 사람 한 명과 궁전을 차지하는 것이 아니라, 후계 인증·행정급여·군령·노드키·의례·세금·기록·외교를 동시에 묶는 일이다.
- 어느 한 요구자도 모든 핵심자원을 장악하지 못한다.
- GA4의 전투는 대함대 결전보다 **어떤 명령이 합법이고 어느 기관이 그 명령을 실행할지**를 둘러싼 기동·방첩·호송·점거가 중심이다.

## 2. Core Region Summary

Working region:
- `관정권 / Crown Meridian`.

Systems:
- 11 major inhabited systems and 23 secondary habitats/relay sites within the immediate succession theater.

Population:
- approximately 19–23 billion registered residents.

Normal travel:
- most core systems are 0.4–1.8 days apart by priority route.
- dense traffic and security queues can make civilian movement slower than frontier routes.

Communication:
- authenticated short orders typically 1–8 hours between major core nodes.
- full evidence/archives still require longer transmission or physical courier.

Strategic reality:
- messages move fast enough for political whiplash but not instant enough to prevent contradictory orders from becoming operational facts.

## 4. Core Sites

### C01 — Meridian Anchor

Class:
- L0 Continuity Anchor.

Function:
- high-capacity route synchronization, military/civil authentication roots, deep continuity hardware.

Control:
- joint operator college, Imperial continuity officials and military security.

Succession importance:
- can validate high-level command and route priority but does not crown a ruler by itself.

Risk:
- whoever controls it can accelerate or choke core/provincial movement.

### C02 — Crown Habitat

Function:
- current royal household, court, ceremonial institutions, diplomatic residences and protected dependents.

Population:
- approximately 85–130 million across linked habitats and service zones.

Succession importance:
- public visibility, royal person, regalia and ceremonial accession.

Weakness:
- dependent on other systems for registry, treasury and military compliance.

### C03 — Central Registry

Function:
- citizenship, noble/house, succession, appointment, pension, tax and legal identity roots.

Population:
- 0.4–0.7 billion including administrative city/habitats.

Succession importance:
- validates genealogy, office and legal continuity.

Risk:
- multiple legitimate emergency snapshots and sealed corrections exist.

### C04 — Imperial General Staff

Function:
- strategic command, officer appointments, theater orders and military communications.

Population:
- 0.6–0.9 billion in command/defense complexes and associated cities.

Succession importance:
- armed forces act through recognized chains, but field commanders can refuse disputed orders.

### C05 — Treasury Arc

Function:
- currency clearing, pensions, salaries, war bonds, provincial transfers and emergency credit.

Population:
- 0.9–1.4 billion.

Succession importance:
- pays the state and can make one claimant operational without declaring them lawful.

### C06 — Sacred Archive

Function:
- imperial founding documents, sealed oaths, memorial and continuity records, ceremonial history.

Population:
- 40–80 million.

Succession importance:
- holds oaths and sealed protocols used by several claimants.

Risk:
- archive is curated and politically layered, not one perfect original.

### C07 — Helios Shipyards

Function:
- major core naval construction/overhaul and Helix/Imperial industrial nexus.

Population:
- 1.8–2.6 billion across system and orbital industry.

Succession importance:
- fleet readiness, parts and industrial bargaining.

### C08 — Civil Service Hub

Function:
- ministries, examinations, provincial administration, public health, education and logistics bureaus.

Population:
- 2.8–3.8 billion.

Succession importance:
- can continue government under an acting cabinet or regency even without settled crown.

### C09 — Old Court Ring

Function:
- former capital/court, hereditary houses, judicial chambers and retired officials.

Population:
- 1.0–1.6 billion.

Succession importance:
- competing legal schools, noble coalitions and historical protocols.

### C10 — Legion Muster

Function:
- major military base and reserve/rapid-response staging.

Population:
- 0.7–1.1 billion including military families and support industry.

Succession importance:
- visible armed legitimacy and potential coup center.

### C11 — Outer Meridian Gate

Class:
- L1 trunk node linking Core to provincial routes including the Lumen/Ardis corridor.

Population:
- 0.5–0.8 billion.

Succession importance:
- controls arrival of provincial fleets, evidence and representatives.
- Ardis’s new route can bypass or complicate this gate.

## 5. Succession Resource Layers

A claimant needs enough of the following to rule:

1. **Person/Lineage** — blood, adoption, designation or legal successor status.
2. **Ceremony** — regalia, oath, public accession and court recognition.
3. **Registry** — legal identity and appointment chain.
4. **Administration** — ministries and civil-service compliance.
5. **Treasury** — salaries, pensions, credit and taxes.
6. **Military** — General Staff and field-command recognition.
7. **Node/Authentication** — trusted route/command keys.
8. **Industrial/Logistics** — shipyards, parts, food and medicine.
9. **Provincial Consent** — governors, node cities and local forces.
10. **Public Narrative** — evidence, memorial and legitimacy.

No claimant controls more than 4–6 layers at GA4 opening.

## 6. Entry from Ardis

Ardis to C11 Outer Meridian Gate:
- 7–15 days priority under stable Silex/Ardis route and onward corridor.
- 2–5 weeks if forced through Lumen and security queues.

C11 to major core sites:
- 0.8–2.4 days priority.

Communication from Core to Ardis:
- short authenticated orders 2–6 days depending route state.
- full registry/archive evidence 5–18 days or courier.

Why P-001 cannot arrive before politics forms:
- contradictory messages and local decisions begin days before his physical arrival.
- Core factions can seize institutions while he is in transit.

Why Ardis matters:
- its joint-trust authentication and alternate route can validate/transport provincial representatives/evidence outside one Core gate.
- several claimants seek Ardis recognition, route access or keys.

## 7. Core Force Envelope at GA4 Opening

Forces in immediate Crown Meridian:
- 3 battleship/heavy-carrier groups, politically divided.
- 11–17 cruisers.
- 28–45 destroyers.
- 70–120 frigates/corvettes.
- extensive patrol/support/yard forces.

Readiness:
- 50–65% deployable immediately.
- many forces assigned to protect institutions and routes rather than form one fleet.

Major alignment categories:
- Crown Guard / Household.
- General Staff loyalists.
- Legion/field commands.
- Continuity emergency command.
- provincial-aligned units.
- Helix yard/security interests.

Rule:
- no single claimant can immediately concentrate all forces without exposing another institution or triggering defection.

## 8. Civilian and Economic Stakes

Succession conflict affects:
- salary and pension continuity.
- medical/supply contracts.
- provincial tax/food flows.
- legal identity and court judgments.
- military orders and conscription.
- route priority and refugee movement.
- trade/credit confidence.

Core residents are not passive palace audience.

Named public crises to use:
- two salary authorities issue conflicting pay.
- hospitals/food systems reject one authentication chain.
- retired soldiers/pensioners lose access.
- provincial cargo detained as politically suspect.
- diplomatic missions demand recognized signatory.
