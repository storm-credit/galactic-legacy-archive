# S1-02 — 브락시 / Braxi

Tier: S1 candidate
Biological Classification: independent biological species, provisional BIO-1
Working Reader Name: 브락시
Self-name: HOLD
First Strong Exposure: GA2 yard/ship ordinary worker candidate
Primary Regions: HOLD
Population Band: HOLD

## One-screen card

**Silhouette:** 낮은 중심의 넓은 몸통과 네 개의 체중지지·조작 사지를 가진 고중력 적응종.

**Environment:** 약 1.25–1.55g에서 가장 편안하며, 인간 표준 중력에서도 생활 가능하지만 장기 저중력에서는 근골격·순환 관리가 필요하다.

**Memorable body fact:** 네 사지를 상황에 따라 보행·고정·정밀 작업에 모두 쓸 수 있다.

**Ship requirement:** 높은 열부하와 칼로리 수요, 다점식 좌석/안전고정, 저중력 장기근무용 능동 하중 운동장치.

**Wrong stereotype:** “타고난 돌격병/중장비 노동자.” 실제 강점은 전투성이 아니라 하중 환경에서의 신체 안정성이며, 직업·성격은 광범위하다.

## Biology

Body plan:
- bilateral-ish four-limb load-bearing architecture;
- low torso, broad internal support frame;
- all four major limbs end in manipulatory structures with different fine-control ranges;
- locomotion alternates between four-point bracing, three-point support + one manipulator, and low two-limb transitional posture;
- average braced height roughly 1.0–1.25 m;
- extended reach roughly 1.5–1.8 m;
- provisional adult mass 70–115 kg.

Gravity envelope:
- comfortable: 1.25–1.55g;
- routine adapted: 0.8–1.8g;
- long-term below 0.6g requires loading therapy;
- short emergency exposure above 2g does **not** imply fighter-pilot immunity to acceleration.

Physiology:
- compact vascular loops;
- strong venous valves and distributed pressure regulation;
- high muscle/bone maintenance cost;
- relatively high basal caloric requirement;
- high activity produces substantial heat that must be rejected.

Acceleration red-team correction:
- home gravity adaptation may reduce some orthostatic/structural limits;
- spacecraft acceleration injury still depends on vector, duration, restraint, organs and circulation;
- Braxi are not universally superior high-G pilots.

Senses:
- broadly human-comparable visual/audio bandwidth;
- stronger proprioceptive/contact feedback through limbs;
- no special truth/sensor ability.

Life cycle:
- provisional adult lifespan similar order to humans, perhaps moderately longer under good medicine;
- no extreme longevity advantage;
- juvenile motor development emphasizes stable multi-point movement.

## Medicine

Risks:
- overheating during sustained exertion;
- low-g bone/muscle deconditioning;
- circulatory dysregulation if sedentary under very low gravity;
- joint overuse in human-standard workplaces that assume two dedicated legs/two dedicated arms.

Emergency medicine:
- blood-pressure interpretation requires Braxi reference ranges;
- crash restraint injuries distribute across four limb girdles;
- cooling must be treated as a first-class trauma variable.

Prosthetics:
- modular limb prostheses mature because industrial injuries historically affected load-bearing workers;
- losing one limb changes movement and work but does not remove personhood/independence.

## Ordinary life

Home:
- broad low furniture;
- floor/rail surfaces count as work surfaces;
- vertical human chairs are often annoying rather than inaccessible if adapters exist.

Food:
- frequent calorie-dense meals are common for physically active adults;
- stereotype that Braxi “eat constantly” ignores occupation/activity variation.

Clothing:
- four-limb harness garments;
- heat shedding often matters more than insulation in standard habitats.

Work:
Historical concentration may exist in:
- yards;
- cargo handling;
- high-load maintenance;
- security/military.

But ordinary population must also visibly include:
- teachers;
- accountants;
- artists;
- doctors;
- shopkeepers;
- parents;
- administrators.

Leisure:
- braced cooperative sports can exploit four-point movement;
- shared zero-g games are also popular precisely because they reverse home-gravity assumptions.

## Infrastructure

Human-standard compatibility:
- doors: U1;
- seating: U2;
- restraints: U2;
- suits: U2;
- toilets/sanitation: U1–U2;
- terminals: U1;
- medical beds: U2;
- standard ship atmosphere: likely U0/U1, final chemistry HOLD;
- frame cockpit: U2.

Political issue:
- “one-size safety certification” can exclude Braxi by requiring human seating geometry rather than outcome-based safety.

## Culture/politics controls

Do not define a single Braxi culture.

Required eventual examples:
- Imperial urban Braxi family;
- Neutral/route Braxi technician;
- Braxi critic of occupational stereotyping;
- Braxi officer who rejects physiology-based recruitment propaganda.

Historical wound candidate:
- occupational channeling into high-load industry/service, not finalized.

## Military/logistics

Useful:
- braced close maintenance;
- heavy-EVA stabilization;
- damage-control work under load.

Costs:
- ration/cooling load;
- larger restraint system;
- low-g deployment conditioning.

Frame implication:
- standard two-arm/two-leg frame can use translated control;
- four-limb mapping may justify specialized industrial/service machines;
- no automatic Braxi combat frame lineage.

## Reveal ladder

R0: GA2 worker/crew appearance without lecture.
R1: restraint/cooling/meal issue.
R2: certification or work-assignment dispute.
R3: Braxi characters disagree about recruitment stereotypes.
R4: homeworld/contact history much later if needed.

# S1-03 — 세이아 / Seia

Tier: S1 candidate
Biological Classification: independent biological species, provisional BIO-1
Working Reader Name: 세이아
First Strong Exposure: GA2 route/ship structural specialist

## One-screen card

**Silhouette:** 길고 가벼운 몸, 세 개의 주요 이동지지 사지와 두 개의 섬세한 조작지를 가진 저중력 적응종.

**Environment:** 0.35–0.65g에서 편안하고 고중력·급가속에 취약하다.

**Memorable body fact:** 피부와 내부 압력기관으로 공기 흐름·선체 진동·압력 변화를 정밀하게 느끼지만 강한 구조진동은 통증과 감각과부하를 일으킨다.

**Ship requirement:** 넓은 하중분산형 휴식공간, 진동저감 구역, 고가속 시 의료/운용 제한.

**Wrong stereotype:** “조용하고 신비로운 감각종.” 감각 특성은 생리일 뿐 성격·철학과 무관하다.

## Biology

Body plan:
- elongated flexible torso;
- three primary locomotor/support limbs;
- two smaller fine manipulators;
- adult length roughly 1.7–2.3 m when extended;
- mass provisional 35–60 kg.

Gravity:
- comfortable: 0.35–0.65g;
- routine with conditioning: 0.25–1.0g;
- 1g is survivable but tiring for many individuals;
- sustained >1.2g is medically significant.

Pressure/vibration sense:
- mechanoreceptor web detects airflow, surface vibration and pressure gradients;
- useful ranges are local and noisy;
- cannot identify machine faults magically without training/context;
- structural alarms can overwhelm the same sense.

Vision/hearing:
- adequate but not necessarily dominant;
- tactile/pressure information supplements ordinary senses.

Life cycle:
- provisional lifespan 70–110 human-comparable years depending medicine;
- no special cognitive speed.

## Medicine

Key risks:
- high-g syncope/organ strain;
- blunt trauma;
- sensory overload during explosions or vibration-heavy machinery;
- chronic connective-support problems in high-g residences.

Emergency care:
- acceleration casualty threshold lower than human average;
- transport litter must support elongated body;
- vibration isolation can be as important as noise control.

## Ordinary life

Home:
- reclining/anchored nests rather than upright chair-centered layout;
- walls/ceilings used as normal surfaces in low-g architecture.

Food:
- moderate energy demand;
- meal culture not biologically predetermined.

Privacy:
- footsteps, ventilation and machinery may be perceptible at ranges humans ignore;
- architecture can require vibration privacy, not merely soundproofing.

Work stereotype:
- often recruited into leak detection, hull inspection, navigation support and structural diagnostics;
- must show Seia in unrelated professions.

Leisure:
- low-g spatial dance/coordination sports possible;
- some Seia deliberately enjoy loud/rough environments with protective gear, contradicting stereotype.

## Infrastructure

Doors/terminals: U0–U1.
Seats/restraints: U2.
High-g emergency craft: U2/U3.
Alarm systems: U2 due vibration intensity.
Medical beds: U2.
Frame cockpit: U2–U3 depending acceleration profile.

Political issue:
- emergency evacuation standards may prioritize acceleration throughput that is dangerous to Seia.

## Military/logistics

Advantages:
- hull inspection;
- local vibration anomaly detection;
- low-g EVA maneuver efficiency.

Costs:
- high acceleration;
- bombardment vibration;
- crash tolerance.

Command abuse risk:
- treating Seia sensory ability as expendable biological equipment.

Frame implication:
- remote service/sensor craft stronger fit than high-G ace role by default;
- individual exceptions possible through training/medical support.

## Reveal ladder

R0: GA2 route worker or contract client.
R1: ship reduces acceleration because of passenger/crew physiology.
R2: schedule/payment conflict reveals real cost of accommodation.
R3: Seia disagree over whether separate service standards are protection or occupational segregation.
R4: contact history later.

# S1-04 — 누마르 / Numar

Tier: S1 candidate
Biological Classification: independent biological species, provisional BIO-1
Working Reader Name: 누마르
First Strong Exposure: GA2 late or GA3 city infrastructure

## One-screen card

**Silhouette:** 유체 환경에서 유연하게 움직이는 양측대칭 신체와 두 개의 주요 조작지, 강한 하부 추진 구조를 가진 수륙성 지성종.

**Environment:** 고습도 기체 환경에서 제한 활동이 가능하지만 정기적인 유체지지 휴식과 피부·호흡 관리가 필요하다.

**Memorable body fact:** “물속 외계인”이 아니라, 유체가 몸의 지지·열관리·호흡표면 유지에 관여하는 종족이다.

**Ship requirement:** wet-rest module, hydration control, contamination-managed fluid, fluid-support medical pod.

**Wrong stereotype:** 수중환경 때문에 느리거나 비전투적이라는 생각. 실제 성능은 환경에 따라 크게 바뀐다.

## Biology

Body plan:
- bilateral torso;
- two principal manipulators;
- ventral/caudal locomotor structures for fluid propulsion and braced dry movement;
- adult body length provisional 1.5–2.1 m;
- mass 60–100 kg excluding support fluid.

Environment:
- comfortable in dense fluid/shallow aquatic environments;
- gas-atmosphere activity possible with high humidity and respiratory/skin support;
- dry exposure duration varies by individual/technology;
- not instantly incapacitated out of water.

Thermal regulation:
- fluid exchange aids cooling;
- dry habitats require dedicated skin moisture/heat management.

Respiration:
- final chemistry HOLD;
- recommended to keep mixed-habitat atmospheric compatibility possible with aid, rather than requiring exotic toxic gas in every scene.

## Medicine

Requirements:
- wet medical bed/pod for serious care;
- dehydration/skin barrier injury;
- contamination management;
- fluid-borne infection protocols;
- drugs delivered through species-specific routes.

Evacuation burden:
- carrying support fluid adds mass;
- triage cannot count body mass alone.

Death/body handling:
- must distinguish physiological death from irreversible drying/respiratory collapse; exact cultural rites remain separate.

## Ordinary life

Home:
- mixed dry/wet zones;
- fluid-rest basins are private space, not decorative aquariums.

Food:
- diet chemistry HOLD but should permit some shared processed foods;
- communal eating can happen in both wet and dry social spaces.

Sanitation:
- water quality is sanitation, respiratory health and housing at once;
- contamination disputes are politically serious.

Family:
- childcare may require different fluid depth/chemistry than adults depending developmental design; final life-cycle HOLD.

Leisure:
- fluid three-dimensional sport/art;
- dry-space social culture also exists in diaspora.

## Infrastructure

Public transport: U2.
Seating: U2.
Life support: U2/U3.
Sanitation: U3 in full-time residence.
Medical: U3.
Prison/detention: U3 if long duration.
School: U2/U3.
Rescue pod: U3.
Frame cockpit: U2 or U3 fluid-supported.

Political issue:
- “water allocation” is a civil-rights and life-support issue, not luxury consumption.

## Military/logistics

Strengths:
- fluid/coolant/flooded industrial environments;
- pressure changes if correctly supported;
- fluid-supported acceleration can distribute some loads.

Costs:
- support-fluid mass;
- contamination;
- dehydration during suit failure;
- damaged wet modules create immediate casualty risk.

Frame implication:
- fluid cockpit can help load distribution but raises mass/service cost;
- separate chassis only if mission profile justifies it.

## Reveal ladder

R0: wet-module resident/worker appears normally.
R1: water module repair has passenger consequences.
R2: GA3 budget/governance conflict over public multi-environment service.
R3: Numar groups disagree over integrated vs separate habitat districts.
R4: homeworld ecology later.

# S1-05 — 이브라 / Ivra

Tier: S1 candidate
Biological Classification: obligate symbiotic dual-organism person lineage, provisional BIO-1/4 boundary
Working Reader Name: 이브라
First Strong Exposure: ordinary person before GA4 legal conflict

## One-screen card

**Silhouette:** 한 개체처럼 보이는 이동성 숙주체 안팎에 작은 공생 조절생물이 평생 결합해 발달하는 종족.

**Personhood fact:** 정상적인 이브라 한 사람은 “두 인격”이 아니라 두 생물학적 파트너의 장기 공생으로 형성된 한 삶/한 법적 사람이다.

**Ship requirement:** 의료·격리 시스템이 두 생리계와 공생 상태를 함께 추적해야 한다.

**Wrong stereotype:** hive mind, possession, split personality.

## Biology

Architecture:
- primary mobile host organism;
- distributed smaller symbiotic partner integrated through skin/gut/vascular-adjacent niches;
- partner contributes chemical sensing, immune calibration and metabolic regulation;
- host provides mobility, central nervous system and most deliberate motor behavior.

Identity control:
- normal cognition emerges from the integrated developmental organism-person;
- do not assign separate conscious dialogue to the symbiont by default.

Separation:
- temporary partial separation can be medically survivable under controlled conditions;
- long separation causes cascading illness;
- unusual cases can raise personhood questions but should remain rare.

Senses:
- broad chemical sensitivity;
- trained Ivra may detect environmental changes but cannot diagnose substances magically.

## Medicine

Critical risks:
- broad-spectrum sterilization/antibiotics can injure the symbiont;
- immune therapies can destabilize partnership;
- quarantine rules designed for “one organism = one patient” can misclassify them;
- poison may affect partner before host symptoms appear.

Medical records need:
- host state;
- symbiont state;
- integration index;
- treatment compatibility.

Reproduction:
- HOLD; must define how both organisms pass to/develop in offspring without requiring mystical inheritance.

## Ordinary life

Food:
- diet affects both host and partner;
- some fermented/biotic foods may be medical/ordinary staples without turning culture into “gut religion.”

Family:
- children may require staged symbiont acquisition or inherited integration; final choice HOLD.

Body etiquette:
- touching/sampling surface symbiont areas may be medically intimate;
- outsiders can misread it as hygiene issue.

Work:
- chemical/ecological professions plausible but cannot dominate representation.

Leisure/culture:
- ordinary arts/politics unrelated to biology required.

## Infrastructure

Standard seating: U1.
Atmosphere: likely U0/U1.
Food/medical: U2/U3.
Quarantine: U3 procedural adaptation.
Identity scanner: U2 if biological markers otherwise appear as multiple entities.
Frame cockpit: U1/U2.

Legal issue:
- customs/medical systems may generate two biometric identities;
- Archive may mistake lineage or symbiont markers for separate person/object provenance.

## Politics

Potential internal positions:
- integration-rights advocates;
- medical-standard reformers;
- groups protecting traditional symbiont-care practice;
- citizens who oppose species-specific political mobilization.

No universal “collective consciousness” ideology.

## Military/logistics

Useful:
- chemical hazard response if trained;
- some toxin/environment monitoring.

Costs:
- field medicine complexity;
- sterilization protocols;
- quarantine delays.

POW/detention:
- denying symbiont-support treatment is denial of essential medical care.

## Reveal ladder

R0: Ivra clerk/worker/crew member treated as ordinary.
R1: medical or food-support difference.
R2: customs/biometric identity failure.
R3: GA4/8 legal argument where Ivra disagree about remedy.
R4: evolutionary origin only if plot-relevant.

# S1-06 — 테사르 / Tesar

Tier: S1 candidate
Biological Classification: independent biological species, provisional BIO-1
Working Reader Name: 테사르
First Strong Exposure: GA3/4 ordinary professional, not elder sage

## One-screen card

**Silhouette:** 중앙 몸통을 기준으로 다섯 개의 주요 사지가 방사형으로 배치된 장수·완만한 성장 지성종.

**Environment:** 인간과 크게 겹치는 생활환경을 가질 수 있어 혼합도시 적응은 쉽지만, 좌석·작업대·출입구는 방향성이 없는 신체에 맞춰 조정해야 한다.

**Memorable body fact:** 긴 수명보다 더 중요한 것은 긴 성장기와 낮은 인구 대체속도다.

**Wrong stereotype:** 오래 살기 때문에 모두 보수적이고 현명하다.

## Biology

Body plan:
- approximately radial five-limb arrangement;
- limbs can alternate locomotion/manipulation with specialization by individual/culture/training;
- central torso with upper sensory cluster;
- provisional adult span 1.4–1.8 m across extended limbs;
- mass 65–120 kg.

Environment:
- recommended atmosphere/temperature close enough to shared public space for strong integration;
- gravity comfortable 0.8–1.15g provisional.

Longevity:
- provisional healthy lifespan 160–240 standard years with modern medicine;
- maturity significantly slower than humans;
- full physical/social adulthood may occur around 24–35 years depending final biology and law;
- do not map human legal ages directly.

Cognition:
- no superior memory by default;
- long life creates more opportunity to remember events, but memory still distorts/fades.

## Medicine

Risks:
- long cumulative chronic disease exposure;
- slow tissue turnover may complicate some healing/cancers;
- limb redundancy reduces some disability impacts but does not trivialize loss.

Population reality:
- wartime adult deaths are demographically expensive because replacement/maturation is slow;
- this can shape politics without implying cowardice.

## Ordinary life

Home:
- central work/rest spaces with multi-directional access;
- human corridor furniture can force one “front” that Tesar bodies do not naturally privilege.

Family:
- long juvenile/dependent stages make education and care major institutions;
- intergenerational households can include very large age spans.

Politics:
- one Tesar may personally remember three regime eras;
- another may be young enough to know them only from school.

Work:
- long apprenticeships possible but must not become universal caste inheritance.

Leisure:
- multi-direction coordination arts/sports;
- rapid media and fashion cycles can be embraced, not rejected.

## Infrastructure

Doors: U1.
Seating/restraint: U2.
Workstations: U2.
Standard atmosphere: U0/U1 recommended.
Medical beds: U2.
Frame cockpit: U2.

Legal issue:
- sentencing, office terms, pension and minority/majority ages need proportional/legal reconsideration.

## Military/logistics

Potential doctrine preference from institutions, not biology:
- casualty-conscious remote systems;
- high training investment;
- experienced maintenance/command continuity.

Counterexample required:
- impulsive or radical young/old Tesar actors.

Frame:
- five-limb mapping can benefit specialized industrial systems;
- common combat frames remain translated standard unless support base justifies alternate design.

## Reveal ladder

R0: ordinary Tesar professional/citizen.
R1: seating/age/status difference.
R2: GA4 succession evidence includes a living witness whose memory is useful but not infallible.
R3: Tesar political blocs disagree strongly.
R4: species history later.

# S1-07 — 켈릭 / Kelik

Tier: S1 candidate
Biological Classification: independent biological species, provisional BIO-1
Working Reader Name: 켈릭
First Strong Exposure: GA2 salvage/maintenance or GA3 production

## One-screen card

**Silhouette:** 낮은 분절형 몸체와 여러 고정지, 두 주요 조작지를 가진 폐쇄 지하·정거장형 환경 적응종.

**Environment:** 중저중력, 어두운 먼지/광물 환경에 적응했으며 시각보다 접촉·근거리 전기/자기장 감각 비중이 높다.

**Memorable body fact:** 가까운 전류 누설과 장 변화를 감지할 수 있지만 강한 전자기 잡음은 오히려 감각 과부하와 생리적 스트레스를 일으킨다.

**Wrong stereotype:** 기계를 “본능적으로 읽는 엔지니어 종족.” 실제 능력은 센서 하나가 더 있는 것뿐이고 해석에는 교육이 필요하다.

## Biology

Body plan:
- compact segmented torso;
- multiple anchoring/locomotor limbs;
- two high-dexterity manipulators;
- adult length provisional 1.2–1.6 m;
- mass 45–80 kg.

Gravity:
- comfortable 0.5–0.9g provisional;
- anchoring limbs useful in microgravity but do not eliminate inertia.

Senses:
- low-light vision;
- contact vibration;
- close-range electric/magnetic gradient sense;
- exact sensitivity bounded to local fields and trained interpretation.

EM limits:
- strong industrial motors, transmitters and active EW can be physically unpleasant or disorienting;
- shielding and filtered interfaces are accessibility tools.

Thermal cycle:
- physiology benefits from scheduled temperature variation/rest phases;
- constant human-comfort temperature is survivable but may impair sleep/metabolic health long-term.

## Medicine

Key issues:
- EM-sensitive diagnostics;
- implanted electronics must be carefully shielded/calibrated;
- thermal-cycle disruption;
- industrial electrocution may cause sensory-organ injury below human-visible burn thresholds.

Prosthetics:
- can integrate tactile/electrical feedback, but proprietary signal filters create vendor-dependency risk.

## Ordinary life

Home:
- EM-quiet rest alcoves;
- tactile signage;
- controlled warm/cool cycles;
- darkness is not inherently gloomy.

Work stereotype:
- engineering, maintenance, mine detection and electronics.

Required visible alternatives:
- cook;
- teacher;
- lawyer;
- artist;
- political organizer.

Art:
- tactile/electrical-field art possible, but avoid turning all Kelik culture into technology worship.

Privacy:
- powered devices can leak detectable activity at short range; social rules around shielding matter.

## Infrastructure

Visual displays: U1–U2.
EM environment: U2.
Seating/restraint: U1/U2.
Habitat thermal schedule: U2 for dedicated residence.
Medical: U2.
Frame cockpit: U2 due sensor filtering.

Political issue:
- “standard safe EM emission” based only on human thresholds can be exclusionary.

## Military/logistics

Useful:
- local current leakage detection;
- mine/electrical fault work;
- anchoring in microgravity maintenance.

Vulnerability:

## 9. Portfolio Red Team

Control:
- final origin classification must decide whether Verin are an independent engineered species or human-derived clade.

### Risk 5 — Tesar longevity can dominate historical testimony

Control:
- memory is fallible;
- living witness does not equal legal truth;
- no individual spans the whole Imperial history.
