## 2. Background

- grew up around a minor relay-node dismantling yard.
- learned industrial-frame operation during decompression rescue and salvage, not formal military academy.
- a contract dispute turned into a security crackdown after workers refused unsafe node decommissioning.
- several relatives/coworkers were classified as administratively dead and transferred through correctional labor channels.
- joined the Liberation Network to trace people whose identities vanished across schools, medical wards, and corporate facilities.

Why E-001 attacks White Dock:
- White Dock contains custody manifests and transport authentication fragments connecting several disappeared groups.
- the Independence Front lacks legal standing; stealing authenticated data may be the only way to prove they exist.
- a radical escort cell is prepared to destroy part of the dock if records cannot be extracted.

Why E-001 does not kill students casually:
- students may be victims or future witnesses.
- killing them reproduces the disappearance system.
- operational doctrine prioritizes retrieval and disabling force.

Limit:
- E-001 will use lethal force against armed staff/ships when necessary.
- restraint is costly, not effortless moral superiority.

---

## 3. External Want and Internal Need

### External want

- recover authenticated identity/custody records.
- find a specific missing worker/kin/community member linked to correctional transfers.
- prove the Liberation Network can rescue people without becoming a scorched-terror faction.

### Internal need

- accept that records and rescue cannot restore every former identity or relationship.
- stop treating everyone inside imperial institutions as coerced victims waiting to defect.
- build accountable authority instead of permanent emergency cells.
- distinguish refusing central law from refusing all law.

### False belief

> If the truth is extracted and shown, people will choose freedom once they understand what was done to them.

### Truth to learn

> Evidence changes possibilities, but people may rationally choose safety, work, treatment, family, or imperfect institutions over E-001’s liberation plan.

## 4. Competence Map

### Exceptional

- close-structure movement around docks, rotating habitats, cable arrays, and debris.
- disabling sensors, joints, weapons, and propulsion without immediate cockpit kill.
- rescue under decompression and structural collapse.
- reading industrial facilities and worker improvisations.
- rapid extraction under pursuit.

### Strong

- small-cell leadership.
- informal route contacts.
- worker/security procedures.
- identifying which infrastructure can be disabled safely.

### Weak

- open-space fleet combat.
- long political negotiation.
- managing large mixed constituencies.
- accepting delay when people remain imprisoned.
- trusting official legal processes.

### Blind spots

- underestimates institutional employees who genuinely protect people.
- assumes public evidence creates unified outrage.
- can treat neutral caution as cowardice/complicity.
- personal disappearance case can distort broader priorities.

## 5. Frame and Combat Style

Working frame type:
- modified industrial-response frame with narrow profile, multiple anchor limbs/cables, white heat-resistant work coating partly stripped.

Combat doctrine:
- penetrate through maintenance paths.
- cut sensors and communication before weapons.
- anchor enemies to structure or force surrender through mobility loss.
- use facility systems as terrain rather than destroy them.
- preserve data, people, and tools.

Signature contrast with P-001:

- P-001 edits opponent choices to create team victory.
- E-001 edits infrastructure access so the mission can be completed despite military defeat.

Weakness/counter:
- low ammunition and armor.
- restraint makes patterns predictable.
- relies on maps and insider data that can be falsified.
- cable/anchor systems vulnerable to heat, cutting, and open space.

White Dock withheld-fire logic:
- student combat signatures match custody records and nonstandard training telemetry.
- E-001 recognizes that shooting would erase witnesses and contradict mission.
- pauses long enough to communicate, exposing their cell to capture.

Cost of restraint:
- a fellow operative is injured/captured or the radical escort obtains leverage.

## 6. Relationship with P-001

Initial impression:
- P-001 moves like a veteran command pilot but wears a prisoner identity.
- he protects infrastructure and students yet obeys enough imperial procedure to be dangerous.

What E-001 wants from P-001:
- access to records and students.
- proof that school resistance exists.
- later, a corridor or ceasefire.

What P-001 remembers/assumes:
- E-001 may be listed under a future atrocity, defection, or death with uncertain identity.
- initially tries to place E-001 in a known future faction role.

Core conflict:
- P-001 protects the school from capture because students/patients need it.
- E-001 believes continued operation sustains coercion.

Relationship ladder:

1. armed recognition.
2. temporary joint rescue.
3. evidence exchange with betrayal risk.
4. recurring rival over methods.
5. GA2 convoy/identity rescue cooperation.
6. GA3 political split over joint-trust node.
7. possible successor leader independent of both P-001 and H-001.

No recruitment rule:
- E-001 does not join P-001’s team in GA1.
- even after alliance, retains separate command and constituency.

## 7. Relationship with H-001

Conflict:

Long arc:

## 9. Voice Bible

Default speech:
- concrete, dry, mission-focused.
- uses worker/facility terms rather than formal military ranks.
- asks who owns/maintains a system before discussing ideology.
- distrusts promises without route, fuel, bed, medicine, and witness.

Under pressure:
- becomes sharper and more directive.
- drops political explanation.
- counts exits and people aloud.

When lying:
- tells the mission purpose truth but hides target person, extraction limit, or radical escort orders.

With students:
- avoids heroic speeches.
- offers exact options and warns what cannot be guaranteed.

Distinct from H-001:
- H-001 calculates systems and future flows cautiously.
- E-001 acts through immediate routes and physical extraction, with stronger impatience.

## 10. Moral Lines

Will not:
- intentionally kill correctional students as category.
- destroy irreplaceable life-support or identity records without evacuation/copy.
- return rescued people to coercive owners for political exchange.

May cross:
- lethal force against security/contract troops.
- falsified identities and data theft.
- coercive extraction if E-001 believes a person cannot consent safely.
- temporary alliance with radicals to gain access.

Fall condition:
- decides anyone remaining inside the system has chosen complicity and may be treated as enemy.

Growth condition:
- accepts individuals’ informed refusal and invests in institutions capable of supporting that choice.
