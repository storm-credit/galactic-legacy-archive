## 1. Representation Rule

No one “student leader” speaks for approximately 1,780 students.

The preference process uses:

- individual conditional survey/requests;
- cell/cohort meetings;
- patient and worker/service groups;
- staff departments;
- contested guardians/family/contract data;
- representatives who can be challenged, recalled or contradicted.

Rian’s cell contributes systems/evidence but receives no automatic majority or permanent leadership.

## 2. Survey Design

Survey is confidential but not perfectly anonymous because legal/medical/placement matching is required.

Questions separate:

- immediate stay/leave preference.
- acceptable sponsor(s).
- unacceptable authority/contract terms.
- cell/family/community separation constraints.
- medical/work/identity needs.
- willingness for military/industrial obligation.
- acceptable risk if no package satisfies all conditions.

Respondents can select multiple conditional packages.

Eligible/located:

| Preference | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|

Important overlap:

- Neutral supporters often accept reduced training but not indefinite limbo.

Main concerns:

No patient “vote” authorizes treatment or transfer for another patient.
