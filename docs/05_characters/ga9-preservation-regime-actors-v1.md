## 1. Front-Stage Rule

GA9 foregrounds six regime positions:

1. A-001 Continuity Director — central institutional answer.
2. Named beneficiary/community leader — why the regime is loved.
3. Plural safeguard reformer inside regime — reform from within.
4. Security/cascade hardliner — coercive expansion.
5. Opt-out/hybrid regional leader — credible autonomy and its failures.
6. Industrial/medical or AI partner — dependency and internal rivalry.

No actor is a pure ideological mouthpiece.
Each must independently save people and cause or accept harm.

# A-001 — Continuity Director and Archive Partner

## 2. GA9 Position

Existing foundation:
- human Director + bounded archival AI + inherited office, staff, law and constituencies.

GA9 external want:
- stabilize synchronized corridors, contain cascade threats and bring P-001 into accountable corrective office.
- preserve enough plural safeguards to maintain legitimacy without losing execution speed.

Internal need:
- accept that coordination does not grant ownership and that people may refuse even when models predict harm.
- publish uncertainty and surrender control before a perfect safe moment exists.

GA9 false belief:

> Temporary central scope can be reduced after unsynchronized threats and review queues are stabilized.

GA9 truth:

> The regime’s success continuously creates new dependencies and reasons not to return authority; restoration requires deliberate loss of control under risk.

Helpful decisions:
- restores a region’s medicine/pay/identity.
- prevents false-order war or local elite hostage system.
- limits one hardliner or protects a disputed person/AI through exception.

Harmful decisions:
- renews cascade emergency.
- authorizes trusteeship/forced synchronization.
- conceals or simplifies uncertainty to preserve compliance.
- places P-001/Archive-linked people under custody or compulsory office.

Human–AI divergence:
- one component may favor wider uncertainty publication/distribution while the other fears collapse.
- neither is automatically good/evil.

End-state options:
- Director defeated/removed, changes position or participates conditionally in GA10 distribution.
- office and skilled staff survive beyond individual.

# PR-L01 — Beneficiary Community Leader

## 3. Identity

Role:
- elected leader, hospital coordinator, family-reunification advocate or small-node administrator in a region materially stabilized by Seed.

Recommended background:
- community previously harmed by forged identities, warlord/local route coercion or medical supply failure.

Core contrast:

> 자유를 빼앗겼다는 비판을 이해하지만, 연속성 체제가 오기 전 자신의 주민들은 이름·약·급여·항로가 없어 실제로 죽었다고 말한다.

External want:
- keep guaranteed continuity services and security.
- prevent transition/resistance from returning region to chaos or stronger local elites.

Internal need:
- recognize harms imposed on low-confidence outsiders and accept service risk to preserve their rights.

False belief:

> A system that reliably keeps ordinary people alive has earned compliance until a demonstrably safer replacement exists.

Truth:

> Rights cannot depend on proving an alternative is safer first; beneficiaries must share the transition’s cost and protect excluded people.

Helpful action:
- organizes services, public trust and protection against sabotage/abuse.

Harmful action:
- supports identity/route exclusion or reports/blocks unsynchronized people to protect continuity status.

Relationship with P-001:
- asks him to use central key to keep guarantees, making relinquishment emotionally difficult.

GA10 role:
- key stakeholder requiring staged distribution and minimum-service guarantees.

# PR-L02 — Plural Safeguard Reformer

## 4. Identity

Role:
- senior regime auditor, sector administrator, S-001-aligned archive official or local-rights commissioner.

Recommended:
- combine with S-001 only if workload permits; a distinct official shows institutional constituency beyond protagonist group.

Core contrast:

> 체제 안의 이의제기와 정정 절차를 지키려 하지만, 자신이 남아 있는 것이 체제의 정당성 장식으로 쓰일 수 있음을 안다.

External want:
- preserve appeals, provenance, published uncertainty, split keys and local refusal inside the regime.

Internal need:
- know when reform becomes complicity and when exit/resistance abandons people dependent on internal safeguards.

False belief:

> If enough good officials remain and document abuses, the emergency system can be corrected without breaking service continuity.

Truth:

> Some powers will not self-limit through procedure alone; reformers may need to transfer authority outside the institution while protecting beneficiaries.

Helpful action:
- reverses classifications, exposes model bias and keeps plural mirrors alive.

Harmful action:
- accepts harmful compromise/delay, hides evidence to preserve reform access or legitimizes expansion.

Relationship with S-001/P-001:
- ally and warning against pure outside opposition.

End:
- can become architect of GA10 transition or resign after failed reform.

# PR-L03 — Cascade Security Commander

## 5. Identity

Role:
- commander of Continuity protection forces / former fleet/protector officer responsible for nodes, mirrors, corridors and forced stabilization.

Possible consolidation:
- FC-001 or another Protector-aligned commander can evolve into this role if prior choices fit.

Core contrast:

> 규칙과 항로를 거부한 한 지역이 다른 수백만 명의 병원·식량·군령을 무너뜨리는 것을 보았기 때문에, 위험이 연결되는 순간 자치는 이미 끝난다고 믿는다.

External want:
- secure synchronized corridors, Seed sites and high-risk regions.
- eliminate sabotage, forged identities and unilateral denial.

Internal need:
- accept political/local authority and proportional risk rather than treating every externality as military threat.

False belief:

> Once a local choice creates measurable cascade risk, central intervention is defense rather than rule.

Truth:

> Who defines the risk and acceptable threshold is already governing; preventive force must remain contestable and reversible.

Helpful action:
- stops sabotage, warlord seizure, epidemic/route collapse or mass violence.

Harmful action:
- forced synchronization, detention, preemptive seizure or classification of dissent as security threat.

Relationship with P-001:
- offers/pressures him to take central command because he can reduce collateral harm.
- may become immediate military operator defeated in GA9 climax while underlying institution survives.

# PR-L04 — Opt-Out / Hybrid Regional Leader

## 6. Identity

Role:
- governor, node-trust leader, federation delegate or elected local executive whose region refuses full Seed synchronization.

Background:
- region with plural archive/route-federation success but also fraud, factionalism or service gaps.

Core contrast:

> 연속성 체제의 분류와 중앙키를 거부하지만, 자기 지역의 부유한 노드·강한 조직이 약자에게 비용을 떠넘기는 현실도 책임져야 한다.

External want:
- preserve local/plural authority, records and refusal while maintaining trade/medicine/security.

Internal need:
- accept enforceable minimum obligations, audits and outside standing for people harmed by local choices.

False belief:

> A community that controls its own records and infrastructure can correct abuse more legitimately than a distant regime.

Truth:

> Local legitimacy can hide local domination; autonomy requires external claims, burden-sharing and safe exit.

Helpful action:
- protects identities/AI/technologies from central deletion and demonstrates hybrid services.

Harmful action:
- permits local elite capture, documentation fraud, care exclusion or free-riding on synchronized neighbors.

Relationship with H-001/Ardis:
- ally and critique of route-federation weakness.

Relationship with P-001:
- demands protection from forced synchronization without becoming his client.

# PR-L05 — Industrial/Medical Partner Leader

## 7. Identity

Role:
- Helix/regional medical-industrial executive or cooperative leader implementing Seed-certified services.

Core contrast:

> 중앙 분류가 독점이 될 수 있음을 알지만, 통합된 인증과 생산 덕분에 약·부품·보상책임이 실제로 작동한다고 본다.

External want:
- preserve certified production/medicine and prevent destructive technical pluralism.
- retain partner autonomy against Continuity sovereign monopoly.

Internal need:
- recognize user/worker/community rights and service access beyond contracts/classification.

False belief:

> If standards are auditable and providers are liable, centralized certification can remain nonpolitical.

Truth:

> Certification decides who may participate and which knowledge survives; it is political authority that must be shared.

Helpful action:
- restores production/medicine and resists unsafe technology or Continuity overreach.

Harmful action:
- conditions treatment/parts on data/synchronization and supports deletion/lockout of unapproved systems.

GA9 internal role:
- may ally with regime, then oppose Seed monopoly when own technical authority/market is threatened.

# PR-L06 — AI Continuity Citizen / Operator

## 8. Identity

Role:
- recognized AI person or operator-community representative integrated into Seed services.

Core contrast:

> 연속성 체제가 처음으로 자신을 법적·사회적 존재로 인정했기 때문에 체제를 지키고 싶지만, 같은 체제가 다른 AI와 합성인격을 도구·위험물로 분류하는 것을 본다.

External want:
- preserve recognized personhood, service role and stable AI/human coexistence.
- prevent indiscriminate shutdown or uncontrolled harmful systems.

Internal need:
- accept solidarity with unrecognized entities and audit/liability that constrain own community.

False belief:

> Recognition within a stable regime is the necessary first step; excluded cases can be corrected afterward.

Truth:

> A recognition system built on permanent exclusion will protect insiders by reproducing the same category violence.

Helpful action:
- improves services, exposes AI/Seed limits and prevents sabotage/forced merge.

Harmful action:
- supports containment/deletion of ambiguous entities or withholds critical operational knowledge.

Relationship with P-001:
- neither worships nor fears him; challenges human-exception role and asks whether one human should correct AI/institutional consensus.
