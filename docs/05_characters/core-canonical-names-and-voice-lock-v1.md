## 1. Naming Policy

Reader-facing names use:
- 2–3 syllable given name + 1–2 syllable family/route name in Korean transliteration.
- one stable everyday name per character.
- rank, office and call-sign are introduced only when socially necessary.
- aliases do not replace the everyday name for whole arcs.

Formal display:
- `이름 성` order in dialogue/narration for mixed frontier society.
- legal registries may display family/charter name first, but prose does not repeatedly reproduce it.

Prohibited:
- multiple similar `카엘/라엘/라온`-type names in the same active cast.
- titles used as substitutes for personality.
- hidden name changes as cheap twists.

## 2. Opening Core — Final Lock

### P-001 — 리안 칼데르 / Rian Calder

> 개명 반영 2026-08-07 ([[canonical-name-errata-005]], PR #99). 구명은 해당 errata에 기록돼 있으며 역사 텍스트에서만 유지된다. 이 주석은 구명을 직접 표기하지 않는다 — 개명 스윕이 개명 설명문 자체를 덮어쓰는 사고가 실제로 발생했다.

Everyday call:
- `리안`.

Formal/late titles:
- 학원 셀 비행책임자.
- 07호 임무지휘관.
- 공동함대 임무지휘관.
- 오르페우스 공동지휘관.
- 임시 중앙교정권 보유자.

Forbidden title drift:
- permanent emperor, permanent supreme admiral, master curator.

Voice anchor:
- short conclusion after silently calculating several consequences.
- asks for time, distance, ammunition and who pays the cost.
- rarely gives inspirational speeches; when he does, uses concrete tasks and names.
- under fear becomes too calm and omits his own condition.

Typical syntax:
- “가능합니다. 대신 셋을 포기해야 합니다.”
- “누가 남는지부터 정하죠. 배는 그다음입니다.”

Avoid:
- omniscient exposition in dialogue.
- constant cold genius register.
- repeating that he already lived this future.

Private flaw visible in speech:
- frames moral choices as operational variables until another person forces him to name the human loss.

## 8. Ship and Machine Reader-Facing Names

### 07

Canonical everyday name:
- `07호`.

Formal registry:
- `AUX-07 다목적 상호운용 훈련 프레임`.

Do not rename it into a grand mythical sword/mecha name.
Its plain number is part of the theme: institutions and people give it meaning through use.

### NR72-118

Canonical official registry:
- `NR72-118`.

Canonical crew/public name:
- `파루스`.

English working rendering:
- `Parus`.

Name origin:
- Latin `Parus`, the tit/chickadee genus — a small bird that winters near
  inhabited service spaces, not a predator or royal symbol.
- the crew chose it for a laid-up wreck that had never earned an honour name;
  imperial Latin, but the one word the empire would never pick for itself.
- retired earlier name: `회랑새` (D-20260813-02).

### First ship reader rule

- narration uses `파루스` after the crew-name scene.
- legal/technical scenes use `NR72-118` when title, damage or identity matters.

## 9. Dialogue Distinction Matrix
