## 1. Community Baseline

Working designation:
- `K-7 배급공동체` or similar frontier registry code, final name open.

Size:
- 28–44 legal/irregular members at R0.

Composition:
- displaced industrial workers, children/young adults, two elderly/disabled residents, caregivers, one ration/accounting group, temporary boarders.

Legal status:
- recognized as one support/guarantee unit under an emergency settlement contract.
- several members have incomplete or shared identity documents.
- food, medicine, oxygen/habitat fees and citizenship applications are bundled.

Why H-001 is leverage:
- his academy contract contributes credits/status and guarantees one part of the bundle.
- institutions can freeze the entire account while claiming not to punish unrelated people.

Community internal conflict:
- preserve bundled status and wait for recognition.
- dissolve and disperse before authorities seize everyone.
- accept Helix/Imperial sponsorship.
- join Independence route.
- remain at current node despite risk.

Rule:
- H-001 cannot represent all 28–44 people by himself.

# PC-001 — Guardian / Ration Coordinator

## 2. Identity

Code: PC-001  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: 38–60 provisional  
Culture: Frontier Route with some Core registry literacy  
Role: registered guardian/sponsor contact for H-001; community ration/accounting coordinator  
Relation: not required to be biological parent; recommended former coworker/family friend who assumed legal guardianship

Core contrast:

> H-001을 학교에 보내 공동체를 살렸지만, 그 선택이 아이를 인질로 만들었다는 사실을 안다.

## 3. Want and Need

External want:
- keep community ration/medical status intact until stable relocation or citizenship.
- prevent H-001 from sacrificing himself for them.
- recover missing identity/contract records.

Internal need:
- stop making major choices for H-001 while calling them protection.
- accept that preserving the community bundle can imprison people who want separate futures.
- reveal their own compromise with Academy/Helix/officials.

False belief:

> If the adults absorb the guilt and hide the full cost, the young can survive without carrying it.

Truth:

> Hidden sacrifice becomes a debt the young cannot understand or consent to; shared survival requires shared information and choice.

## 4. Past Compromise

- signed or accepted H-001’s correctional education contract because it stabilized community status.
- may have understated danger or forged consent age/status.
- withheld an Independence escape offer because it could not take everyone.
- paid/redirected supplies toward the most vulnerable, creating resentment from able workers.

## 5. Agency in GA1

- refuses H-001’s simple extraction plan if community treatment/status would collapse.
- may deliberately remain in Black Ward/transfer system to protect other identities or gather evidence.
- sends a message that contradicts both P-001 and H-001 assumptions.
- demands community representatives be included in medical/contract decisions.

Helpful decision:
- authenticates H-001’s history and shares contract evidence.

Harmful decision:
- withholds a person/location or accepts an institutional bargain that moves others sooner.

GA1 target state:
- alive but not fully reunited/free.
- gains voice in oversight/community decision, remains accountable for original contract.

## 6. Voice and Life

Voice:
- practical, measured, skilled at making inadequate resources sound manageable.
- avoids dramatic guilt; uses meal, medicine, and schedule details.

Ordinary anchor:
- keeps handwritten or physical ration tallies because digital accounts changed before.
- makes one community dish and remembers individual portions.
- enjoys a quiet craft/story practice unrelated to survival, but rarely permits time for it.

Moral line:
- will not trade another child/student for H-001.
- may still prioritize the whole community over one person.

## 7. Identity

Code: PC-002  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: near H-001, 15–19  
Culture: Frontier Route / settlement youth  
Role: friend, foster sibling, or former work partner  
Current status: remains in community, low-level dock/repair work or unregistered courier  
Future paths: Liberation Network organizer, localist defender, radical escort, or community representative

Core contrast:

> H-001에게 공동체를 살리라고 요구하면서도, 학교에서 얻는 기회와 능력을 부러워하고 배신당했다고 느낀다.

## 8. Want and Need

External want:
- break bundled contract and leave before adults negotiate another delay.
- rescue H-001 or force him to choose the community openly.
- gain personal legal identity/work route independent of the group.

- stop turning H-001’s absence into proof of disloyalty.
- own their desire for freedom/status rather than disguise it as communal duty.

False belief:

> Anyone who succeeds inside the system has chosen it over us.

Truth:

> Survival inside a compromised institution is not consent, but neither does shared suffering create permanent ownership over a person.

## 9. Agency in GA1–2

- sends/receives messages through Independence contact.
- may reveal the escape proposal to E-001 or radical bloc without H-001 permission.
- can help locate transfer routes while creating security exposure.
- rejects PC-001’s bundle-first strategy.
- may choose not to board a rescue route because their own political commitment differs.

Helpful decision:
- provides current community conditions and exposes false institutional message.

Harmful decision:
- leaks timing/identity to radical rescuers, accelerating transfer or violence.

GA1 target state:
- not safely collected into school team.
- becomes independent bridge/conflict with Liberation Network.

GA2 role:
- focal participant in convoy/relocation dispute.
- forces H-001 to negotiate rather than command community.

## 10. Voice and Life

Voice:
- faster, more emotional and openly accusatory than H-001.
- uses shared childhood/work references as leverage.
- distrusts abstract plans and asks “who leaves first?”

Ordinary anchor:
- likes a competitive low-gravity sport, loud music, or fashion/object tied to port youth.
- spends scarce credits on one visible personal item, angering PC-001/H-001’s ration logic.

Moral line:
- will not abandon a person physically present in immediate danger.
- may endanger a larger plan to rescue someone now.

Culture: Industrial/Frontier mixed  
Role: former node technician, union recorder, medic, or transport clerk

Core contrast:

## 12. Want and Need

External want:
- restore or deliberately replace legal identity on chosen terms.
- keep treatment without becoming evidence property.
- reveal one transfer/contract truth while protecting other patients.

Internal need:
- accept that the former legal/person identity cannot be perfectly restored.
- allow younger people to choose futures not defined by preserving the old community.
- decide which memories/records should remain private or be forgotten.

False belief:

> If the original record is restored exactly, the injustice and lost years can be corrected.

Truth:

> Correction needs accountability and rights, but a person is not identical to a record; restoration can become another demand to perform the past.

Harmful/limiting decision:
- deletes, withholds, or requests destruction of information P-001 needs.

GA1 target state:

Late payoff:

## 14. Voice and Life

Ordinary anchor:

Moral line:
