## 1. Roster Rule

Ordinary focals are not “the public” or a moral chorus.

Each focal must have:

- a livelihood, household and personal interest;
- an immediate goal not reducible to helping or opposing the protagonists;
- a current institution they value;
- a reason to disagree with people from the same region/class;
- at least one action with material consequence;
- a later status or ending beyond appearing as a victim.

Front-stage limit:
- 2–4 ordinary focals per mini-arc;
- other roster members appear through messages, institutions or later reuse.

## 31. Anti-Instrumentality Checklist

Before using a focal:

- what do they enjoy or dislike outside the main conflict?
- what institution/service do they rationally defend?
- what choice do they make without the protagonists?
- how do people close to them disagree?
- what material consequence follows?
- do they survive or persist beyond the illustrative scene?
- are they being asked to represent an entire class/region/species?
- if harmed or killed, who owns the grief and aftermath?
