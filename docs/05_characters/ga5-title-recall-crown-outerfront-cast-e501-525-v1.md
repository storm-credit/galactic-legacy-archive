## 1. Asel Varon

Code:
- OFC-001.

Role:
- commander of Outer Front Eighth Screen and current locally recognized emergency defense administration.

Age band:
- early-to-mid 50s.

Background:
- maintained V09 routes during Core succession paralysis using provincial stores, converted hulls, worker repair and local civil ration systems.

Immediate want:
- obtain enough supplies and relief to hold the front without surrendering command to a distant Compact/Charter coalition.

Internal need:
- distinguish earned current operational legitimacy from permanent personal/regional sovereignty.

False belief:
- the command that actually defended a population during abandonment has earned durable priority over outside claims until the crisis ends by its own judgment.

Useful truth:
- current local trust, route knowledge and coherent command cannot be replaced by legal title alone.

Danger:
- relief, supply and emergency government can harden into a personal theater state.

Voice:
- what arrived, what did not, days of stock, civilians behind the screen, which order failed to come.

Independent opening action:
- offers shared data and local escort before receiving recognition or supplies;
- refuses Rian/Dor/Crown command but does not attack or close negotiations.

E525 state:
- separate force 42 combat + 34 support/converted, 26 combat R2+;
- 14-day tactical proposal open;
- no Charter/Compact accession.
