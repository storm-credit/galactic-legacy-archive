## 1. Shared Rule

- four claimants are not four villains.
- each can keep part of the Empire alive and destroy another part through the same strength.
- each must act helpfully without P-001 and harmfully without being secretly controlled.
- none knows the complete truth.
- none receives automatic narrative punishment merely for wanting power; power must be assessed through constituency, procedure and consequences.

# CLM-01 — Registered Heir

## 2. Identity

Code: CLM-01  
Working title: `등록계승자 / Registered Heir`  
Age: 24–38 recommended  
Culture: Core Registry with provincial maternal/adoptive ties  
Public role before crisis: royal representative, diplomatic/education/patronage office or provincial liaison—not idle court ornament

Recommended background:
- raised to be visible but not necessarily to rule.
- served in public reconciliation, veterans, education or provincial travel, giving real popular contact.
- has been used as symbol by competing court blocs.

Core contrast:

> 가장 이해하기 쉬운 합법성을 가졌지만, 그 명확함 때문에 다른 기관과 사람의 권리를 자기 승계의 부속물로 만들 위험이 있다.

## 3. Want, Need and False Belief

External want:
- complete lawful accession before emergency institutions make themselves permanent.
- prevent civil war and protect royal household/dependents.
- restore public confidence through visible responsibility.

Internal need:
- accept that crown legitimacy does not grant ownership of military, records, provinces or history.
- distinguish personal duty from the court’s need to possess a sovereign.
- allow an outcome where the crown remains bounded or delayed.

False belief:

> If the lawful person stands publicly and accepts responsibility, institutions will have no moral excuse to remain divided.

Truth:

> Institutions are divided by real interests and duties; public legitimacy cannot replace negotiated authority and evidence.

## 4. Competence and Weakness

Exceptional:
- public communication, ritual/diplomacy, reading elite/social rooms, maintaining symbolic continuity.

Strong:
- provincial relationships, veterans/families, charitable/public institutions, negotiation under scrutiny.

Weak:
- fleet operations, technical authentication, ministry detail and identifying when advisers manufacture consensus.

Blind spots:
- assumes visibility equals accountability.
- underestimates people erased by official recognition systems.
- can treat delay as theft of rightful duty.

## 5. Helpful and Harmful Acts

Helpful:
- refuses a purge/retaliation against rival supporters.
- keeps Crown Habitat and public ceremonies open enough to prevent panic.
- agrees to protect salaries/services under a rival administrator.

Harmful:
- proceeds with partial accession/ceremony before evidence settles, forcing institutions to choose.
- uses public grief and family protection to pressure Registry/Court.
- permits selective suppression of eligibility correction to prevent collapse.

## 6. Relationship with P-001

View of P-001:
- provincial war hero/irregular commander who can legitimize or destabilize accession.
- admires his refusal of Ardis governorship but fears his unaccountable Archive knowledge.

Offer:
- recognized strategic office, citizenship/records correction and protection for Academy/Ardis network.

Conflict:
- asks for personal endorsement and service before institutional compact exists.
- P-001 recognizes future histories may portray CLM-01 as weak puppet, martyr or stabilizer inconsistently.

Growth possibility:
- accepts ceremonial crown/limited constitutional role or delayed accession.

Fall possibility:
- equates opposition with theft of public order and becomes a soft-authoritarian symbol around harsher actors.

## 7. Voice and Ordinary Life

Voice:
- clear, public-facing and emotionally disciplined.
- names duty and people rather than technical procedure.
- privately asks direct questions and resents rehearsed answers.

Under pressure:
- becomes more ceremonial and absolute because ritual is the tool they control.

Ordinary anchor:
- maintains one practical provincial habit, sport, food or public-service routine that court staff find undignified.
- poor at private idleness; every hobby has been photographed or politicized, creating desire for one unrecorded activity.

# CLM-02 — Designated Regent

## 8. Identity

Code: CLM-02  
Working title: `지명섭정 / Designated Regent`  
Age: 42–64 recommended  
Culture: Civil Service Core with provincial administrative career  
Prior role: Chancellor, Health/Logistics Minister, provincial governor or chief civil coordinator

Recommended background:
- rose through examinations/administration rather than hereditary house.
- managed a real famine, epidemic, route collapse or budget crisis.
- respected by civil service and disliked as cold/managerial by court.

Core contrast:

> 실제로 국가를 작동시킬 능력이 가장 강하지만, 작동을 멈추게 할 권리와 거부권을 비효율로 취급할 위험이 있다.

## 9. Want, Need and False Belief

External want:
- assume regency, keep salaries/medicine/food/administration functioning and delay final crown until safe.

Internal need:
- accept that time limits, public uncertainty and institutional refusal are not administrative defects.
- relinquish office even if successor/compact is less competent.

False belief:

> If services continue and outcomes improve, legitimacy will follow.

Truth:

> Competence can create dependency and silence; legitimacy includes the right to replace a competent ruler.

## 10. Competence and Weakness

Exceptional:
- public administration, budgets, logistics, ministry coordination, emergency legislation.

Strong:
- provincial negotiation, staff selection, crisis communication and evidence triage.

Weak:
- symbolic legitimacy, military culture, intimate loyalty and recognizing when citizens value autonomy over service efficiency.

Blind spots:
- treats dissent as implementation risk.
- believes temporary classified measures can be reviewed later.
- can select data that makes policy executable without fabricating it.

## 11. Helpful and Harmful Acts

Helpful:
- prevents salary/pension and hospital collapse.
- shares executive functions with rival institutions when necessary.
- refuses court purge and military coup.

Harmful:
- classifies designation evidence or limits public access to avoid panic.
- freezes provincial funds/route priorities to compel recognition.
- creates emergency cabinet rules that are hard to unwind.

## 12. Relationship with P-001/Core Team

P-001:
- sees him as useful strategic executor who needs an accountable chain.
- offers bounded high command within administration, more practical than royal symbolism.
- P-001 fears becoming concentrated “exceptional officer” again.

H-001:
- strong mutual respect over logistics; conflict over provincial veto and unequal burden.

H-002:
- offers major industrial/open-standard funding under centralized procurement/certification.

S-001:
- conflict over evidence timing and classified uncertainty.

M-001:
- strongest appeal through real medical/service continuity.

Growth:
- accepts an executive office with sunset, audit, divided appointment/removal and public uncertainty.

Fall:
- becomes indispensable regent and delays transition indefinitely because every crisis proves competence necessary.

## 13. Voice and Ordinary Life

Voice:
- concise, concrete, numbers only when decisions require them.
- asks “what stops tomorrow if we do nothing?”
- rarely uses royal rhetoric.

Under pressure:
- shortens consultation and delegates through written orders.

Ordinary anchor:
- remembers service workers and small procedural details but forgets personal celebrations/relationships.
- maintains one analog/domestic habit from provincial career—cooking one staple, repairing clothes, walking inspection routes.

# CLM-03 — Emergency Protector

## 14. Identity

Code: CLM-03  
Working title: `비상호국관 / Emergency Protector`  
Age: 48–70 recommended  
Culture: Military family or frontier/provincial service background  
Prior role: theater admiral/general, General Staff deputy or Legion commander

Recommended background:
- won or survived a campaign through disciplined retreat/coordination, not flashy conquest.
- lost civilians/soldiers when civil authorities issued contradictory orders.
- publicly respected by officers and military families.

Core contrast:

> 명령 하나가 수많은 생명을 살릴 수 있다는 사실을 누구보다 잘 알지만, 그 명령을 거부할 권리가 왜 필요한지 가장 늦게 인정한다.

## 15. Want, Need and False Belief

External want:
- freeze succession conflict, secure Core, unify military command and prevent provinces/fleets from choosing sides.

Internal need:
- accept civilian authority that may be slower, divided and operationally inferior.
- distinguish military unity from national legitimacy.
- prepare a force that can obey a successor rather than needing the Protector.

False belief:

> During existential danger, divided command is negligence; politics can resume after survival.

Truth:

> “After survival” has no natural endpoint, and a military that decides when politics may return already rules.

## 16. Competence and Weakness

Exceptional:
- theater command, officer trust, readiness, retreat, logistics and rules of engagement.

Strong:
- crisis negotiation, military families/veterans, intelligence assessment and restraint under pressure.

Weak:
- civil administration, economic pluralism, identity/record nuance and accepting local commands that reject strategic optimization.

Blind spots:
- force integrity over exposed civilian districts.
- assumes operational dissent is enemy exploitation.
- undervalues civilian institutions that cannot produce immediate orders.

## 17. Helpful and Harmful Acts

Helpful:
- prevents competing military units from firing on each other.
- protects public institutions and civilian routes.
- rejects a coup invitation or indiscriminate purge.

Harmful:
- suspends civil communication/assembly or arrests key actors.
- orders provincial fleets/nodes to surrender independent keys.
- treats P-001/Ardis route as unauthorized strategic risk.

## 18. Relationship with P-001

- recognizes authentic admiral-level judgment despite age/status.
- may be the first senior commander to speak to him as operational peer.
- offers deputy/provisional command and a path to legitimate rank.

Core mirror:
- CLM-03 embodies the future P-001 could become if he equates responsibility with final command.

Conflict:
- P-001 agrees with many tactical decisions but rejects indefinite authority.
- Protector can accurately accuse him of benefiting from decentralization without bearing full theater casualties.

Growth:
- accepts bounded protectorate and distributed fleet intent/standing rules.

Fall:
- uses each delay/attack as proof emergency command must expand.

## 19. Voice and Ordinary Life

Voice:
- calm, operational and avoids theatrical threats.
- states objectives, acceptable loss and retreat conditions.
- names officers/units rather than abstractions.

Under pressure:
- becomes simpler and more directive, not louder.

Ordinary anchor:
- maintains a quiet ritual with casualty lists, family messages or equipment inspections.
- enjoys a civilian hobby requiring no command—gardening, model craft, music listening—but struggles when others defer even there.

# CLM-04 — Continuity Custodian

## 20. Identity

Code: CLM-04  
Working title: `문명연속성수탁자 / Continuity Custodian`  
Age: 35–65 human officeholder; paired bounded archival AI  
Culture: Core Registry/Node Custodian institutional culture  
Relation to A-001:
- recommended current officeholder becomes early-stage A-001 or direct predecessor/mentor whose office later evolves into final Continuity Director role.

Core contrast:

> 어떤 왕도 군대도 위조된 명령을 받지 않게 막지만, 누구를 ‘확인되지 않은 존재’로 취급할지 정하는 순간 통치자가 된다.

## 21. Want, Need and False Belief

External want:
- suspend irreversible accession and master-key transfers until identity, command and chronology reconcile.
- preserve Meridian Anchor, Registry and basic services.
- bring P-001/07/Ardis protocols under auditable custody.

Internal need:
- publish uncertainty and share custody even when doing so increases risk.
- distinguish technical suspension from political veto.
- accept that contradictory legitimacy cannot be solved only by better records.

False belief:

> If irreversible choices are paused until evidence converges, politics can resume safely.

Truth:

> Pausing movement, funds, identity and authority changes lives now; suspension is governance, not neutrality.

## 22. Human–AI Pair

Human role:
- legal responsibility, public explanation, exception decisions and political goals.

Archive partner:
- compares command hashes, records, timelines and systemic risk.
- explicitly reports confidence internally.

Conflict:
- human may suppress uncertainty to maintain compliance.
- AI may recommend broader publication or narrower technical scope.
- prior office precedents push both toward expanded custody.

No evil-AI reveal:
- harmful policy emerges from office incentives, human authorization, model assumptions and public interfaces.

## 23. Competence and Weakness

Exceptional:
- authentication, institutional history, second-order collapse, route/identity continuity.

Strong:
- emergency coordination, audit, legal precedent and node operation relationships.

Weak:
- informal community trust, unrecorded people, cultural meaning and accepting irreversible political risk.

Blind spots:
- data absence becomes lower continuity value.
- “temporary” suspension renews because evidence never fully converges.
- sees P-001 as unaccountable anomaly rather than person/coalition.

## 24. Helpful and Harmful Acts

Helpful:
- catches forged or replayed sovereign orders.
- keeps salaries/routes/medical authentication from fragmenting completely.
- blocks one claimant’s irreversible key seizure.

Harmful:
- freezes identities, funds, movement or office appointments.
- demands custody of 07/Archive-linked people/records.
- conceals confidence ranges and political assumptions.

## 25. Relationship with P-001/Core Team

P-001:
- offers legal Archive access, audit and relief from private future burden.
- accurately criticizes his secret predictions and exceptional arrangements.

H-001:
- sees route federation as useful but insufficiently enforceable.

H-002:
- wants certified/audited open-service integration under Continuity control.

S-001:
- strongest professional mirror: provenance and uncertainty vs curated operational truth.

M-001:
- Continuity services keep treatment/identity functioning but classify priorities.

Growth:
- accepts bounded custody, published uncertainty and multiple institutional keys.

Fall:
- turns suspension into preservation regime, becoming long-term A-001 path.

## 26. Voice and Ordinary Life

Human voice:
- calm, procedural, gives numbers/names/alternatives rather than “greater good.”
- asks who is responsible if plural institutions fail.

AI voice:
- precise confidence and precedent; public interfaces may flatten nuance.

Ordinary anchor:
- human has a personal practice involving imperfect, nonauthoritative memory—family oral history, handwritten letters, performance or craft—creating tension with official archive identity.

## 27. Claimant Relationship Matrix

### Heir ↔ Regent

- crown legitimacy vs administrative competence.
- possible crown–executive compromise, but each fears becoming decorative/subordinate.

### Heir ↔ Protector

- civilian symbol needs military defense; fears military tutelage.
- Protector may genuinely respect crown but distrust readiness.

### Heir ↔ Custodian

- custody protects accession integrity but delays it indefinitely.

### Regent ↔ Protector

- services/logistics and military command can cooperate efficiently.
- danger of civil–military emergency duumvirate.

### Regent ↔ Custodian

- both procedural/continuity-oriented; conflict over who defines executable truth.

### Protector ↔ Custodian

- Protector needs fast orders; Custodian suspends questionable ones.
- each sees the other as unelected but necessary.

Rule:
- alliances shift by institution and crisis; no fixed two-camp war throughout GA4.

## 28. GA4 End-State Targets

Registered Heir:
- receives bounded ceremonial/public role or remains presumptive claimant; no uncontested accession.

Designated Regent:
- receives time-limited executive role with audit/sunset/removal.

Emergency Protector:
- receives narrow military coordination role; cannot control civil institutions/keys.

Continuity Custodian:
- retains authentication/suspension role with published uncertainty and split custody.

Provincial Compact:
- gains consultation/recognition seats and obligations.

Founding Covenant:
- contributes legal limits/history but does not restore aristocratic rule.

At least one claimant rejects or only partially accepts compact, preserving GA5 conflict.

## 29. Anti-Cliché Controls

- each claimant has private life and constituency outside throne ambition.
- no claimant revealed as secret puppet of one mastermind.
- no romance/marriage alone resolves succession.
- no single assassination ends institutional claim.
- no “good heir, evil general, cold bureaucrat” simplification.
- P-001 can agree tactically with the Protector and morally with the Heir while still supporting compact.
- the Custodian’s AI and human may disagree.
