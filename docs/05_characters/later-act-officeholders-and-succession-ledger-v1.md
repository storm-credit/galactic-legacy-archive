## 1. Naming and Succession Rules

- Existing locked names and canonical errata take precedence.
- `메사 린 / Messa Rin` supersedes the old FC-005 name.
- A successor inherits a defined office or unit, never the deceased person’s total authority, reputation or relationships.
- Staff, budget, keys, force, public legitimacy and external recognition may pass to different people.
- Every front-stage later office has a deputy or alternate before crisis.
- Death, resignation or defeat changes policy and coalition balance; it does not erase the institution.
- Rian cannot appoint every successor. Local/crew/civic bodies must own selection.
