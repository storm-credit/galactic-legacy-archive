- allows Doman’s bounded withdrawal rather than forcing internal battle;
- stops Helix corridor confrontation before weapons fire.

- no permanent supreme command;
- external/denial conflict will test her authority.

Prohibited use:

- flawless neutral professional;
- automatic future dictator or martyr.
