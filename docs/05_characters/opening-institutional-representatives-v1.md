## Operating Rule

- each representative is a person with constituency, career risk, and private limits—not a mouthpiece who perfectly represents the entire organization.
- they can lose office while their institution continues.
- their agreements bind only the authority they actually possess.
- each must make at least one autonomous choice that helps the protagonists and one that materially harms or constrains them.

# R-IMP-001 — Imperial Observer

## 1. Identity

Code: R-IMP-001  
Working role: covert cultivation officer / medical-administrative observer  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: 27–40 provisional  
Faction: Imperial Intelligence, Cultivation Bloc with Continuity ties  
Cover options: medical records officer, assistant instructor, legal clerk, or visiting aptitude examiner  
Recommended cover: assistant medical-record examiner, allowing access to neural data and legal identity without dominating training scenes

Core contrast:

> 정권보다 국가의 연속성을 중시한다고 말하지만, 누가 국가의 일부로 기록될지 결정하는 기관에 속해 있다.

## 2. External Want and Internal Need

External want:
- identify strategic cadets, hidden legacy protocols, and institutional infiltration.
- keep 07 core/Archive anomaly away from Helix monopoly and Independence capture.
- cultivate rather than purge useful irregular talent.

Internal need:
- accept that legal invisibility and classified protection are forms of erasure.
- choose whether continuity serves people or merely the apparatus that names them.
- stop assuming competent secret guardians can correct public institutions from above.

False belief:

> If dangerous people and truths are kept inside the right compartment, they can be used without destabilizing everyone else.

Truth:

> Compartments protect secrets by removing those affected from the decisions; eventually the guardian cannot distinguish protection from ownership.

## 3. Background and Constituency

- from an administrative or node-operations family affected by a local records collapse.
- witnessed false identities, forged orders, and fragmented authority kill people.
- joined Intelligence because authenticated state continuity seemed more humane than warlord/local record chaos.
- career depends on producing useful candidates and avoiding public scandal.
- responsible to superiors who may belong to Dynastic, Continuity, Purge, or Archive blocs.

Protected:
- state administrators, node operators, selected cadets, sealed witnesses.

Harmed/excluded:
- people made unofficial for their own “protection,” families denied records, candidates manipulated into service.

## 4. Competence and Limits

Exceptional:
- reading inconsistencies across identity, medical, military, and contract records.
- recruitment/cultivation and controlled disclosure.
- recognizing strategic talent without relying only on school scores.
- navigating imperial authority overlaps.

Strong:
- counterintelligence, negotiation, witness handling, limited AI/Archive protocol literacy.

Weak:
- field command and mecha combat.
- local worker/community trust.
- admitting institutional ignorance.
- acting without legal/operational cover.

Limits:
- cannot order school closure or mass arrest alone.
- requires warrants, superior approval, or covert deniability.
- cover identity restricts visible intervention.
- Helix and Academy have their own protected data domains.

## 5. Relationship with P-001

Initial interpretation:
- illegal training, memory implant, enemy infiltrator, or rare command talent.

Offer:
- secret protection, core access, education, family/identity correction in exchange for service and disclosure.

Conflict:
- P-001 knows secret patronage creates future ownership.
- R-IMP-001 sees multi-party control as leak-prone and dangerous.

Autonomous helpful choice:
- diverts 07 core from Helix/Archive hardliners and prevents immediate Black Ward transfer.

Autonomous harmful choice:
- accelerates protector/witness relocation or withholds evidence to preserve wider operation.

GA1 end:
- revealed to P-001 team but not necessarily public.
- accepts joint custody as temporary experiment, continues covert investigation.

Long arc:
- GA4 key witness/actor in succession records.
- can join or oppose A-001 Continuity Office depending on whether state continuity overtakes rights.

## 6. Voice

- courteous, careful, rarely uses direct threats.
- speaks in authority scopes: “I can delay this,” “I cannot protect that identity once public.”
- asks what evidence would change a person’s mind.
- under pressure becomes more formal and omits the human subject from sentences.

Moral line:
- will not fabricate raw evidence; may classify, omit, relocate, or create cover identities.

Fall condition:
- permanently closes identities and calls that protection.

# R-NEU-001 — Neutral Medical-Legal Representative

## 7. Identity

Code: R-NEU-001  
Working role: rescue-vessel chief / medical auditor / neutral claims officer  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: 35–60 provisional  
Faction: Free Route Neutral Assembly, Humanitarian and Insurance blocs  
Recommended role: rescue-vessel chief with dual medical-audit and limited claims authority

Core contrast:

> 눈앞의 생명을 구하는 사람인 동시에, 다음 구조선을 띄우기 위해 누구를 받을 수 없는지 계산하는 사람이다.

## 8. External Want and Internal Need

External want:
- preserve neutral rescue rights and vessel safety.
- document violations without letting any faction seize the ship/network.
- make the academy pay/guarantee future care for people it deploys.

Internal need:
- recognize that neutral rules can reproduce exclusion when only documented/insured people qualify.
- distinguish refusing unlimited liability from hiding moral choice behind capacity formulas.
- share decision authority with rescued communities rather than acting as benevolent gatekeeper.

False belief:

> A rule applied equally is the only way neutrality survives.

Truth:

> Equal rules can preserve prior inequality; neutrality needs transparent exceptions, burden-sharing, and accountability.

## 9. Background and Constituency

- survived or served during a prior rescue-vessel seizure by imperial/other forces.
- saw unrestricted humanitarian commitments bankrupt or destroy a neutral fleet.
- protects crew, current patients, insured routes, future rescue capacity.
- may carry records of imperial unpaid debt that later shape Orpheus refusal.

Protected:
- rescued civilians, medical staff, neutral crew, contracted routes.

Excluded/harmed:
- unverified identities, politically toxic passengers, requests beyond capacity.

## 10. Competence and Limits

Exceptional:
- triage at vessel/community scale.
- neutral law, custody, evidence chain, rescue corridor negotiation.
- determining what a neutral vessel can physically sustain.

Strong:
- medical logistics, insurance claims, hostage/transfer negotiation, public credibility.

Weak:
- offensive combat.
- understanding internal faction culture beyond contracts.
- trusting secret evidence.
- long-term responsibility after disembarkation.

Limits:
- can grant temporary medical custody, not full asylum/citizenship.
- ship capacity, fuel, supplies, crew consent, and neutral member rules bind action.
- must avoid carrying military contraband or lose neutrality.

## 11. Relationship with P-001/Core Team

Initial:
- sees P-001 as a cadet who saved civilians but violated command and may be using students as shield.
- respects H-001’s logistics but rejects undocumented passenger expansion.
- respects H-002’s repairs only after safety disclosure.
- works closely/conflicts with M-001 over triage and S-001 over identity evidence.

Helpful choice:
- records students as injured civilians/education personnel long enough to block secret transfer.

Harmful constraint:
- refuses permanent protection, weapons/core custody, or unlimited community evacuation without guarantees.

GA1 charter role:
- provides temporary trusteeship, audits, emergency medical minimums, and sunset terms.
- charges costs/debts and requires external review.

Long arc:
- GA2 route/insurance negotiation.
- GA6 Second Orpheus corridor decision shaped by accumulated trust/debt, not personal friendship alone.
- GA8–10 Memory/Neutral Assembly risk of becoming master archive custodian.

## 12. Voice

- blunt about capacity and consequences.
- names current patients/crew before abstract humanitarian principles.
- avoids faction honorifics on neutral ship.
- uses “we can take X for Y hours” rather than “we will save everyone.”
- under moral accusation asks who will supply, staff, and protect the next rescue.

Moral line:
- will not surrender a patient to immediate torture/execution or allow combat on neutral medical deck.
- may refuse boarding, separate families, or require disarmament/identity conditions.

Fall condition:
- reduces neutrality to solvent customers and treats undocumented people as nonexistent.

# R-HEL-001 — Helix Contract and Technical Evaluator

## 13. Identity

Code: R-HEL-001  
Working role: contract engineer / asset evaluator / junior consortium negotiator  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: 25–42 provisional  
Faction: Helix Consortium; Manufacturing Bloc with obligations to Finance/Human Performance  
Recommended background: former frontier technician who rose through Helix certification and genuinely believes standards saved lives

Core contrast:

> 학생을 계급보다 정확하게 평가하지만, 그 정확한 평가를 계약가격과 소유권으로 바꾼다.

## 14. External Want and Internal Need

External want:
- recover/secure 07 components, neural data, and design claims.
- prevent unsafe open-service technology from creating accidents/security breaches.
- keep academy contract profitable and politically defensible.
- identify H-002 as recruitable talent before rivals.

Internal need:
- accept that a contract accepted under debt, family leverage, and legal invisibility is not fully voluntary.
- distinguish standard safety from proprietary control.
- recognize worker/user knowledge as ownership and governance, not only input data.

False belief:

> The fairest system is one that prices every risk and pays every contribution according to contract.

Truth:

> Pricing does not make unequal bargaining power consensual, and some rights cannot be sold away for immediate survival.

## 15. Background and Constituency

- grew up with unsafe local repairs or lost someone to counterfeit/incompatible equipment.
- Helix training/certification provided real mobility and competence.
- responsible to factory workers, investors, customers, insurers, and superiors—not only profit caricature.
- career endangered if 07 open bus or student contracts expose standardization abuses.

Protected:
- certified workers, customers relying on parts, contract cadets receiving treatment/equipment.

Exploited:
- indebted pilots, independent mechanics, frontier customers locked into subscriptions.

## 16. Competence and Limits

Exceptional:
- technical valuation tied to maintenance, liability, and supply.
- contract structuring and identifying ownership layers.
- recognizing practical talent outside formal status.
- comparing safety/performance data.

Strong:
- frame systems at evaluator level, industrial networks, negotiation, corporate politics.

Weak:
- frontline tactical command.
- informal community economies.
- understanding why people reject materially beneficial contracts.
- admitting when safety language masks monopoly.

Limits:
- cannot simply seize state-owned core without claim.
- Helix internal blocs and insurers restrict generosity.
- technical claims require provenance, patents, labor records, and legal jurisdiction.

## 17. Relationship with Core Team

P-001:
- sees rare operational talent but high liability and opaque source.
- offers better equipment/medical support for exclusive contract.
- is frustrated that P-001 values flexibility over standard safety.

H-002:
- genuine professional recognition and strongest conflict.
- offers resources and legal career that would also absorb family workshop rights.
- may mentor, compete with, or attempt to buy her.

H-001:
- values logistics talent but sees community obligations as unpriced external risk.

S-001:
- debates provenance, trade secrets, and public evidence.

M-001:
- Helix treatment/supply can save patients; contract terms threaten consent.

Helpful choice:
- provides safe component/medical information and opposes crude seizure/destruction.

Harmful choice:
- freezes accounts/supplies, files ownership claims, or selects data over people under contract authority.

GA1 end:
- loses sole control but gains audit/inspection rights and continuing debt claims.
- remains recurring negotiator, not defeated villain.

Long arc:
- GA2 embargo/open standard conflict.
- GA3 factory/worker split.
- GA4 corporate bloc politics.
- GA9 surprising anti-Seed ally because Continuity Seed is a greater certification monopoly.

## 18. Voice

- technical and contractual, but not jargon for its own sake.
- praises specific competence directly.
- avoids moral language; asks what failure costs and who pays.
- uses “voluntary” according to signed process until challenged by coercive context.
- under pressure reveals frontier accent/habits or anger at unsafe improvisation.

Moral line:
- will not knowingly certify lethal defect or falsify safety results.
- may enforce crushing contract, data ownership, supply freeze, or legal transfer.

Fall condition:
- decides those unable to afford certified safety must be barred from technology/life-support access entirely.

## 19. Representative Interaction Matrix

### Imperial Observer ↔ Neutral Representative

- both value authenticated records.
- Imperial wants secrecy/continuity; Neutral wants chain-of-custody and public enforceability.
- can cooperate on witness protection and clash over classified relocation.

### Imperial Observer ↔ Helix Representative

- state needs industry; industry needs legal protection.
- each suspects the other is hiding Archive/core data.
- both may temporarily protect 07 from the other.

### Neutral Representative ↔ Helix Representative

- insurance/safety alliance and conflict over proprietary treatment/parts.
- Neutral can enforce payment/audit; Helix can withdraw supplies.

### Trio and Academy

- none can fully control Academy without paying institutional, military, or legitimacy cost.
- multi-party charter emerges from mutual veto, not shared virtue.

## 20. GA1 Independent Choices

R-IMP-001:
- diverts/protects core but relocates witness/protector.

R-NEU-001:
- grants temporary medical custody but refuses permanent asylum/contraband.

R-HEL-001:
- blocks unsafe destruction and recognizes H-002 labor, while enforcing debt and inspection rights.

Required rule:
- each helpful act advances their own institutional goal.
- each harmful constraint remains credible and cannot be solved by friendship.
