## 1. Front-Stage Rule

GA5 may contain many captains, but only five fleet/crew figures remain consistently front-stage:

1. a Protector-aligned veteran formation commander.
2. a provincial compact commodore.
3. a Crown/ceremonial squadron captain.
4. a Helios yard/technical fleet commander.
5. an elected/rotating crew representative from disputed ships.

CAP-001 remains the protagonist coalition’s ship-master perspective.
P-001 must not absorb all five functions.

# FC-001 — Protector-Aligned Vanguard Commander

## 2. Identity

Code: FC-001  
Role: commander of Legion Vanguard or major field formation  
Age: 40–62 recommended  
Background: combat-experienced officer who owes lives and career to CLM-03’s unified command

Core contrast:

> 함대가 한 명의 명령에 움직여야 살아남는다는 사실을 증명해왔지만, 그 명령이 누구의 것인지 묻는 순간 함대가 멈추는 것을 두려워한다.

External want:
- preserve coherent military command and defeat opportunist/rebel/claimant fragmentation.
- keep crews paid and families protected.
- prevent compact politicians from directing battles they do not understand.

Internal need:
- distinguish mission unity from personal/office ownership.
- accept subordinate refusal/exit and civilian limits even when tactically costly.

False belief:

> A commander who accepts responsibility must also hold final authority over every ship committed to the operation.

Competence:
- fleet maneuver, readiness, officer trust, controlled aggression and disciplined retreat.

Weakness:
- heterogeneous civilian/provincial fleets, crew politics, informal maintenance networks and public evidence.

Helpful action:
- saves a rival/compact formation rather than exploit its failure.

Harmful action:
- detains/reassigns captains or seizes depots/keys to prevent fragmentation.

Relationship with P-001:
- recognizes command talent and offers real formation authority.
- sees charter limitations as luxury paid by other crews’ deaths.
- can become mentor-rival rather than simple enemy.

GA5 end options:
- joins charter under strict military terms, remains major opposition, or splits formation.

# FC-002 — Provincial Concord Commodore

## 3. Identity

Code: FC-002  
Role: elected/negotiated commander of several provincial squadrons  
Age: 35–58  
Background: node-defense commander, governor’s naval officer or merchant-defense captain

Core contrast:

> 중앙의 주인이 없는 함대를 원하지만, 각 지방이 필요할 때 떠날 권리를 지키면 공동작전이 가장 위험한 순간 무너진다.

External want:
- protect provincial routes and obtain equal standing in strategic command.
- avoid being used as expendable peripheral force.
- secure pay/repair/return guarantees.

Internal need:
- accept enforceable shared burdens and recall costs.
- defend weak regions that provide little direct value.

False belief:

> Voluntary contribution is legitimate only while every province may withdraw freely.

Truth:

> A coalition that can leave costlessly at the moment of burden is not a shared defense.

Competence:
- coalition bargaining, local route warfare, mixed small-ship formations and provincial politics.

Weakness:
- sustained heavy-fleet battle, unified discipline and prioritizing theater over home.

Helpful action:
- contributes ships to protect a route/region outside own province under fair rules.

Harmful action:
- threatens or executes recall during crisis to force concessions.

Relationship with H-001:
- strong alliance/conflict over minimum obligations, resource allocation and local veto.

Relationship with P-001:
- respects mission-specific command, rejects personal fleet loyalty.

# FC-003 — Crown Guard Captain

## 4. Identity

Code: FC-003  
Role: commander of Crown Guard Squadron or the Heir’s principal naval protector  
Age: 30–52  
Background: household guard, ceremonial/security officer with actual combat experience

Core contrast:

> 왕실을 지키는 충성이 개인숭배가 아니라 군대가 민간권위를 기억하게 하는 마지막 장치라고 믿는다.

External want:
- protect Registered Heir, Crown Habitat and public/ceremonial continuity.
- prevent Protector/Regent/Continuity offices from reducing crown to hostage or decoration.

Internal need:
- accept that civilian legitimacy can be plural and institutional, not embodied only in one protected person.

False belief:

> A fleet without visible civilian sovereign loyalty inevitably becomes its commander’s army.

Competence:
- close protection, disciplined escort, public/civilian rules of engagement and secure transport.

Weakness:
- prolonged logistics, heterogeneous fleets and separating household security from national mission.

Helpful action:
- protects rival claimant/witness/civilians because crown duty includes law.

Harmful action:
- withholds ships or prioritizes household evacuation over wider strategic need.

Relationship with P-001:
- distrusts unappointed charismatic commander.
- may support charter if crown/public civilian role remains real.

# FC-004 — Helios Yard Defense Commander

## 5. Identity

Code: FC-004  
Role: commander/coordinator of yard-defense ships, repair tenders and contract escorts  
Age: 35–60  
Background: naval engineer, yard-security officer or former fleet logistics commander

Core contrast:

> 전투에서 승리한 함대보다 다음 달에도 움직일 수 있는 함대가 진짜 함대라고 보지만, 정비를 통제하는 순간 명령권까지 갖게 된다.

External want:
- keep Helios yards, workers and supply networks neutral/operational.
- prevent any claimant from seizing all production.
- ensure ships receiving repairs pay and follow safety/contract rules.

Internal need:
- recognize repair access as public/strategic obligation, not only priced leverage.
- share technical priorities with crews/provinces/users.

False belief:

> Whoever bears repair and replacement cost has earned the right to decide which missions are sustainable.

Competence:
- readiness, repair scheduling, tenders, industrial security and mixed civilian/military logistics.

Weakness:
- frontline maneuver, public legitimacy and accepting technically wasteful symbolic missions.

Helpful action:
- repairs rival ships and preserves worker lives despite political pressure.

Harmful action:
- grounds/withholds repair or uses software/parts access to force fleet alignment.

Relationship with H-002/ENG-001:
- professional respect and conflict over open/certified pooled maintenance.

Relationship with P-001:
- sees brilliant commander who may spend ships faster than they can be replaced.

# CR-001 — Crew/Oath Representative

## 6. Identity

Code: CR-001  
Role: elected delegate, senior petty officer, shipboard legal/union representative or rotating crew council convener  
Age: 28–55  
Origin: one disputed ship that survived pay/oath/command conflict

Core contrast:

> 장교와 정치가들이 배의 주인을 논할 때, 승무원이 실제로 누구를 위해 죽고 가족에게 누가 책임질지 묻는다.

External want:
- secure pay, pension, family support, rescue/parole and lawful refusal.
- prevent captains/claimants from treating crews as inherited property.

Internal need:
- accept mission risk and collective obligations beyond immediate crew interest.
- prevent crew democracy from becoming short-term self-preservation or pressure rule.

False belief:

> No crew should be required to fight unless a majority accepts the mission and all guarantees are secured.

Truth:

> Some urgent collective defense cannot wait for perfect consent or funding; legitimacy requires standing obligations and accountable emergency authority.

Competence:
- crew morale, practical ship life, informal networks, contract/oath interpretation and collective action.

Weakness:
- theater strategy, classified intelligence and speaking for officers/specialists/embarked civilians.

Helpful action:
- stops suicidal/political order and keeps crew from mutinying or abandoning civilians.

Harmful action:
- work stoppage/refusal during critical operation or protection of guilty crew solidarity.

Relationship with CAP-001:
- captain safety authority vs crew rights and trust.

Relationship with P-001:
- challenges heroic command and demands named consequences/guarantees.

GA5 end:
- crew representation embedded in charter without tactical plebiscite.

# FC-005 — Independent Field Fleet Commander / Main Rejecter Candidate

## 7. Identity

Code: FC-005  
Role: commander of one large unaligned frontier/theater fleet  
Age: 38–65  
Background: defended a distant region while Core factions disputed succession; received contradictory support/orders

Core contrast:

> 중앙의 협약을 약한 타협으로 보면서도, 자신이 지키는 전선에선 이미 사실상 국가를 운영하고 있다.

External want:
- secure supplies and authority for own theater.
- refuse orders that sacrifice region for Core compact.
- build or join a command strong enough to win coming war.

Internal need:
- distinguish justified local autonomy from personal regional sovereignty.
- accept that other fronts/people have claims on their fleet/resources.

False belief:

> The authority that actually defended a population during abandonment has earned permanent command legitimacy there.

Competence:
- theater defense, local political support, supply improvisation and independent command.

Weakness:
- national compromise, other regions’ needs and succession beyond performance legitimacy.

Helpful action:
- protects population/routes despite lack of orders and may cooperate tactically.

Harmful action:
- refuses shared mission, seizes local resources/ships or demands personal recognition.

GA5 function:
- strongest credible alternative to P-001/compact fleet charter.
- may become recurring rival into GA6–7.

## 8. Relationship Matrix

### FC-001 ↔ FC-002

- unity/discipline vs provincial consent/recall.
- each sees the other as irresponsible in different direction.

### FC-001 ↔ FC-003

- military necessity vs visible civilian crown authority.
- can cooperate to block personal dictatorship or conflict over heir safety.

### FC-001 ↔ FC-004

- battle urgency vs readiness/repair sustainability.

### FC-002 ↔ CR-001

- province/ship autonomy allies until burden/withdrawal harms coalition.

### FC-003 ↔ CR-001

- oath to crown vs crew/state/ship; strong debate on inherited service.

### FC-004 ↔ CR-001

- repair/workers/pay solidarity but conflict over operational deadlines.

### FC-005 ↔ P-001

- both irregular commanders with performance legitimacy.
- difference: P-001 tries to make command temporary/reproducible; FC-005 believes defended territory grants durable authority.

## 9. Voice Separation

FC-001:
- concise operational intent, unacceptable risk and decisive orders.

FC-002:
- speaks in contributions, mandates, home routes and reciprocal obligations.

FC-003:
- duty, law, person under protection and public meaning.

FC-004:
- readiness, repair hours, replacement, workers and what remains after battle.

CR-001:
- names crew, families, shifts, pay, rescue and who signs consequences.

FC-005:
- speaks from abandoned front, results and earned authority.

No five-way policy speech scenes; introduce through decisions.

## 10. Ordinary-Life Anchors

FC-001:
- quiet casualty/family routine and difficulty receiving care without command hierarchy.

FC-002:
- provincial food/music/game or practical home habit; receives constant local messages and pressure.

FC-003:
- ceremonial maintenance, public protocol and one private ordinary relationship with protected household staff/family.

FC-004:
- walks repair decks, knows workers and keeps damaged/obsolete component as reminder.

CR-001:
- communal meal/watch/pay ledger, personal family obligation and frustration with officer spaces.

FC-005:
- local memorial or civil-service duty showing regional governance burden.

## 11. GA5 End-State Targets

- FC-001: accepts bounded mission command or remains Protector-aligned opposition with some cooperative rules.
- FC-002: commits provinces to enforceable burdens/recall costs.
- FC-003: preserves civilian/crown role without personal fleet ownership.
- FC-004: joins pooled maintenance with limits and continued industrial leverage.
- CR-001: gains representation, guarantees and refusal rules without tactical vote.
- FC-005: main rejecter/rival or conditional participant whose theater conflict leads to GA6.

At least two figures remain outside P-001’s command at end.

## 12. Anti-Cliché Controls

- no all-wise democratic crew vs evil officers.
- no general becomes tyrant through one bad speech.
- no provincial commander is merely cowardly/local selfish.
- no Crown Guard is ornamental.
- no engineer/logistics commander is noncombat coward.
- no commander joins P-001 after one duel.
- command disagreements create real victories/losses.
