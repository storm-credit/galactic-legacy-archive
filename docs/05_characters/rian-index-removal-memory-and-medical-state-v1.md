## 1. Principle

Rian’s final sacrifice is not general amnesia.
He loses the **exclusive future index and master-query relationship** that made past/future information rapidly searchable, rankable and emotionally privileged.

He retains:
- his identity and present-timeline relationships;
- ordinary autobiographical memory;
- learned piloting and command skills;
- moral/legal responsibility;
- the ability to learn new information.

He permanently loses:
- guaranteed access to the future-history index;
- complete confidence about which memory came from direct life, Archive compression or later contamination;
- several specific high-value future memories listed below;
- continuous high-load 07/Seed synchronization tolerance.

No later cure restores these losses.

## 2. Pre-Removal State

### GA1–3

- vivid future memories arrive by relevance and emotional shock.
- Rian often mistakes familiarity for current certainty.
- present changes progressively invalidate dates, affiliations and outcomes.

### GA4–6

- future memory becomes less like a complete life and more like a ranked strategic index.
- high-stress events trigger involuntary retrieval.
- present relationships begin altering the emotional weight of old memories.

### GA7–8

- Serin/Mia confirm bidirectional contamination:
  - Archive compression affects recall priority and confidence;
  - current experiences affect how the Archive ranks/query-matches future records.
- Rian cannot prove every internal memory independently.

### GA9

- central corrective key makes retrieval faster and more dangerous.
- the same interface that helps coordinate services can turn his judgment into a civilization-scale default.

## 3. Locked Permanent Losses

The following are permanently inaccessible, fragmented or no longer queryable after Episodes 1060–1088.

### L1 — Exact Original Orpheus Casualty Index

Lost:
- names, order and complete count of many individual original-timeline dead/missing.

Retained:
- that mass loss occurred.
- his original State-priority choice and responsibility.
- current reconstructed evidence.

Reason:
- prevents grief/guilt from remaining an all-knowing private graveyard and blocks future casualty ranking by remembered importance.

Emotional consequence:
- Rian can no longer silently recite everyone; he must rely on plural public/private records and living witnesses.

### L2 — Full Future Thirteen-Hero Roster and Rank Order

Lost:
- one complete ranked list and the confidence that it was ever one stable set.

Retained:
- remembered relationships, operations and some names as ordinary memories.

Reason:
- final rejection of people as a collection target.

### L3 — Master Route-Collapse Sequence after the Original Orpheus

Lost:
- exact node-by-node collapse dates, optimal interception points and all later denial outcomes.

Retained:
- general doctrine, warning signs and learned strategic reasoning.

Reason:
- prevents him from permanently dominating route policy through unique future leverage.

### L4 — Aurel/Seed Master Failure Tree

Lost:
- exclusive ability to query all projected outcomes from a central failure state.

Retained:
- known risks, current technical documents and ability to participate like other experts.

Reason:
- no individual can again become the safest omniscient central operator.

### L5 — One Future Personal Relationship Cluster

Locked content:
- detailed future memories of one cohort/crew relationship group that did not form the same way in the current timeline.

Recommended implementation:
- Rian retains affection/grief impressions but loses exact private conversations and final moments of 3–5 original-future companions.

Names:
- to be chosen from original-timeline-only characters, not the current six core survivors.

Reason:
- gives personal emotional cost without erasing current relationships or manufacturing grief toward living altered people.

### L6 — Exact Future Death/Disappearance Dates for Current Core Cast

Lost:
- precise dates/circumstances for Haren, Nera, Serin, Mia and Ern.

Retained:
- broad awareness that their official futures were unstable/compressed.

Reason:
- he must relate to them as present people rather than countdowns.

### L7 — Repeatable Regression Invocation Path

Lost/destroyed:
- phase-index coordinates, authentication sequence and embodied query relationship required to trigger another S5 correction.

Retained:
- evidence that regression happened and bounded scientific/institutional understanding.

Reason:
- series closure and nonrepeatability.

## 4. Locked Retained Memories

Rian retains clearly enough to remain himself:

- childhood and pre-Academy personal history in the current body’s record.
- the subjective fact of living the original future.
- major original decisions, including Orpheus State priority.
- skills learned through practice: piloting, command reasoning, tactics and institutional lessons.
- present-timeline experiences from GA1–10, subject to ordinary trauma and imperfect recall.
- current core characters’ names, relationships and meaningful shared events.
- the ability to recognize that a memory may be contaminated or uncertain.
- all public/current evidence available to other authorized actors.

Rule:
- index removal cannot conveniently erase guilt, promises or information needed for accountability.

## 6. Medical/Operational Limitations

### Neural synchronization

Before final removal:
- repeated high-load 07/Seed sync causes headaches, sleep disruption, tremor, dissociation risk and memory intrusion.

After E1088:
- safe low/medium-load 07 operation remains possible with current interfaces.
- high-load continuous combat synchronization is medically restricted.

Locked limits:
- maximum normal high-intensity frame sortie: approximately 35–55 minutes before mandated evaluation, depending condition.
- no back-to-back high-load sortie without medical clearance and substantial recovery.
- no direct whole-network/Seed central sync.
- no unsupervised experimental interface.

These are design bands, not reader-facing numbers every scene.

### Cognitive effects

- retrieval gaps under fatigue.
- difficulty distinguishing “I once knew” from “I should know.”
- grief/phantom familiarity.
- increased need for notes, colleagues and external records.
- ordinary learning remains intact.

### Physical effects

Possible recurring but non-totalizing:
- fine tremor after overload.
- vestibular/sensor mismatch.
- sleep-cycle disorder.
- migraine/light sensitivity.

Do not depict:
- constant helplessness.
- miraculous cure through emotional realization.
- disability as punishment or moral purification.

## 7. Support and Consent Structure

Mia/medical institution may:
- medically ground Rian.
- set sync/recovery conditions.
- preserve private treatment data.

Serin/archive institution may:
- audit query provenance with consent/mandate.
- hold external records he no longer can access privately.

Rian may:
- refuse nonemergency sync.
- choose what personal uncertain memories to discuss.

Rian may not:
- override medical grounding through former rank.
- demand other people serve as replacements for lost memories.
- keep secret master backups of the index.

## 8. Emotional Arc Controls

- losing exact future memories does not mean relationships were unreal.
- current people do not owe him reconstruction of their alternate selves.
- he must grieve people whose names/details are incomplete without inventing certainty.
- relief at losing constant future pressure may coexist with guilt and loss.
- final ordinary scenes show note-taking, asking, forgetting and being corrected without humiliation.

Key line principle:
- Rian does not say “I remember nothing.”
- he says some variation of “I remember living it. I can’t search it anymore.”

## 9. Epilogue Function

At CY 751:

- Rian uses external schedules/maps and asks local experts.
- he may fly a low-risk rescue/training sortie under licence and medical rules.
- one ordinary person tells him a fact he once would have queried.
- he does not know their future importance.
- he remains competent, responsible and limited.

Final state:
- no permanent central command.
- no repeatable reset.
- no perfect memory.
- no loss of personhood or all relationships.
