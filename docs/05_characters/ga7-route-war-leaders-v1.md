## 1. Front-Stage Rule

GA7 centers H-001, but four independent leaders embody competing route answers:

1. Mutual Route Federation delegate/organizer.
2. Continuity Corridor administrator.
3. Scorched/denial commander.
4. Local node custodian/municipal leader.

E-001, FC-005 or existing characters may fill one role if continuity fits, but no role should be created only to debate H-001.

---

# RF-L01 — Mutual Route Federation Delegate

## 2. Identity

Role:
- elected/rotating federation convener responsible for member mandates, obligations and disputes.

Background:
- provincial/Neutral/local route official, convoy organizer or Ardis-adjacent political operator.

Core contrast:

> H-001의 물류능력을 필요로 하지만, 연합이 한 사람의 계산과 신뢰에 의존하면 이미 새로운 중앙정부가 된다고 본다.

External want:
- formalize mutual obligations without losing local custody/exit.
- prevent scorched closures and Continuity takeover.

Internal need:
- accept enforceable burdens and emergency authority rather than hiding behind endless member consent.

False belief:

> If procedures remain transparent and members retain veto, federation power cannot become coercive.

Truth:

> Veto and delay also coerce the weak; authority must be bounded and accountable, not absent.

Competence:
- coalition politics, local mandates, burden negotiation and public legitimacy.

Weakness:
- military timing, technical node risk and rapid crisis command.

Helpful action:
- limits H-001’s unilateral closure authority and protects member dissent.

Harmful action:
- delays/blocks necessary action or lets secure members shift burdens onto exposed regions.

Relationship with H-001:
- ally, supervisor and political rival.
- may appoint/support him as technical coordinator but oppose doctrine/office expansion.

Voice:
- mandates, constituencies, reciprocal obligations and who consented.

# RF-L02 — Continuity Corridor Administrator

## 3. Identity

Role:
- regional Continuity Office director/administrator for central food, medical, military and authentication corridors.

Relation:
- subordinate, peer or predecessor-stage ally of CLM-04/A-001.

Core contrast:

> 오르페우스 이후 실제로 굶주림과 병원 붕괴를 막은 중앙회랑을 운영하며, 지방의 자유보다 최소서비스가 먼저라고 믿는다.

External want:
- place key nodes/denial systems under Continuity corridor authority.
- guarantee predictable food/medical/defense traffic.
- eliminate unilateral closures and false identities.

Internal need:
- expose allocation uncertainty, recognize local refusal and stop treating low-ranked regions as deferrable.

False belief:

> A route guaranteed to everyone cannot remain subject to local politics; central allocation is the only fair minimum service.

Truth:

> Central guarantees still rank, exclude and defer people; fairness requires affected communities to contest the model and hold keys.

Competence:
- regional traffic, service allocation, authentication, emergency response and institutional logistics.

Weakness:
- undocumented communities, informal repair, local legitimacy and accepting inefficient exceptions.

Helpful action:
- restores hospital/food corridors and prevents radical/local elite hostage tactics.

Harmful action:
- freezes low-confidence identities/routes, diverts supplies and seeks permanent key custody.

Relationship with H-001:
- strongest professional/ideological rival.
- can prove federation failures with real data.

Relationship with P-001:
- offers centralized solution and criticizes H-001/P-001 exceptional arrangements.

Voice:
- service guarantees, failure probabilities, queue fairness and explicit tradeoffs.

# RF-L03 — Scorched Route Commander

## 4. Identity

Role:
- commander/organizer of a radical denial coalition, possibly derived from E-001’s network, FC-005 front or a distinct survivor leader.

Recommended:
- distinct from E-001 unless E-001’s prior arc credibly supports radicalization; E-001 should more likely be a counterexample within Independence.

Background:
- survived a node seizure/occupation where keeping route open enabled mass capture, forced labor or extermination.

Core contrast:

> 항로는 중립 인프라가 아니라 점령자가 더 많은 사람을 통제하는 무기이며, 파괴할 준비가 없는 공동체는 이미 인질이라고 믿는다.

External want:
- distribute denial charges/keys and establish automatic capture-triggered destruction.
- prevent Continuity/Compact/Helix control.

Internal need:
- recognize the right of dependent/local populations to reject destruction and the obligation to support downstream survivors.

False belief:

> A route destroyed by its people is freedom; a route captured intact is slavery.

Truth:

> The power to destroy shared infrastructure can enslave people as completely as the occupier, especially when dissenters cannot leave.

Competence:
- sabotage, local defense, covert networks, demolition and occupation experience.

Weakness:
- long-term services, regional dependency, reconstruction and dissent management.

Helpful action:
- prevents real seizure/hostage system and trains local defense.

Harmful action:
- plants/uses denial without adequate consent/evacuation or pressures communities through fear.

Relationship with H-001:
- sees him as originator who lacks courage to apply doctrine fully.
- may use his title/signature/doctrine to legitimize actions.

Voice:
- occupation memories, chokepoints, hostages and irreversible decisions; distrusts abstract future costs.

# RF-L04 — Local Node Custodian and Civic Leader

## 5. Identity

Role:
- locally selected node/city leader combining or balancing operator and civilian authority.

Background:
- municipality/node that depends on upstream/downstream routes and has experienced both abandonment and central seizure.

Core contrast:

> 자기 주민을 지키려면 항로를 닫을 권리가 필요하지만, 그 항로에 의존하는 다른 지역은 자기 유권자가 아니라는 문제가 있다.

External want:
- preserve local population, node and political autonomy.
- obtain compensation/support for regional obligations.

Internal need:
- accept duties to distant dependents and shared review of local denial.

False belief:

> The authority closest to the risk is the most legitimate decision-maker.

Truth:

> Local legitimacy does not automatically include people downstream who bear the decision’s largest cost.

Competence:
- local services, operator relationships, public politics and practical route limits.

Weakness:
- theater strategy and distant consequences.

Helpful action:
- refuses external monopoly and provides local knowledge/consent.

Harmful action:
- closes or bargains with route capacity, abandoning distant communities.

Relationship with H-001:
- needs his models/coordination but may reject federal obligations.

Voice:
- named districts, workers, local risk and resentment at outsiders’ burden demands.

# RF-L05 — Victim/Dependent-Region Advocate

## 6. Identity

Role:
- representative from a medical/agricultural/holdout region dependent on a route H-001 or others may close/destroy.

Why needed:
- downstream harm must have a person/institutional voice before and after denial.

External want:
- keep passage/minimum supplies or obtain real replacement route/support.

Internal need:
- accept that their survival demand may expose another node/community to occupation.

False belief:

> A route serving millions cannot morally belong to the people living beside it.

Truth:

> Regional dependence creates obligations but does not erase local danger and consent; shared authority must share costs too.

Helpful action:
- provides dependency data, contributes resources/evacuation and supports compromise.

Harmful action:
- pressures local node into suicidal openness or uses public suffering as political coercion.

Relationship with H-001:
- direct claimant against his D4 denial; may become named victim/opponent rather than forgiveness device.

## 8. Independent Decision Requirements

Before GA7 ends:

- RF-L01 limits or removes an H-001 emergency authority despite operational risk.
- RF-L02 opens one exception/admits one model exclusion and also imposes one harmful freeze/diversion.
- RF-L03 prevents one real capture and crosses/approaches a coercive denial line.
- RF-L04 chooses local action that helps residents and harms dependents—or accepts a burdensome federation duty.
- RF-L05 contributes resources/evacuation rather than only demanding passage, and later pursues a claim against denial.
- E-001/FC-005/another existing leader makes an independent route choice not reducible to H-001/P-001.
