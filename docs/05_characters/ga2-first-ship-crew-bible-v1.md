## 1. Operating Principle

- P-001 is **not** captain of the first ship at GA2 opening.
- ship command, tactical mission command, engineering certification, medical authority, logistics/passenger rights and data authentication are separate.
- the crew is undermanned and partly licensed through external institutions, so no role is ceremonial.
- authority conflicts create choices; they are not resolved by P-001 simply being more competent.
- named front-stage shipboard cast is limited to 8–10 during GA2. Other crew exist as functional teams and rotating named members.

# CAP-001 — Ship Master / Captain

## 3. Identity

Code: CAP-001  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: 38–58 provisional  
Culture: Free-Port Neutral or mixed Imperial/Frontier maritime culture  
Background: former relay-tender master, customs/rescue captain or provincial auxiliary officer  
Current status: suspended/underemployed after refusing an unsafe or politically compromised order; holds the license needed to move NR72-118

Recommended canon:
- former master of the same hull or sister ship, but not sentimental owner.
- lost command after choosing crew/ship safety over cargo/mission in a case with real casualties or financial collapse.
- believes a captain must be accountable for decisions others call cowardice.

Core contrast:

> P-001은 임무를 위해 배를 쓴다. CAP-001은 배와 승무원이 살아남아야 다음 임무가 존재한다고 본다.

## 4. Want, Need and Flaw

External want:
- restore professional standing and pay old crew wage/salvage claims.
- prove the hull can work without becoming a cadet weapon or faction asset.
- keep crew alive and ship legally mobile.

Internal need:
- stop using safety authority to avoid morally necessary risk.
- admit that previous “responsible” withdrawal abandoned people/cargo/crew beyond the hull.
- share command information rather than invoke the captain’s final word too early.

False belief:

> The one person who carries legal responsibility must retain final control over every dangerous choice.

Truth:

> Responsibility can be allocated and shared without disappearing; refusing others meaningful authority creates the very hidden failures a captain fears.

Competence:
- ship handling, docking, node procedures, crew discipline, emergency damage control, route bureaucracy.
- strong at retreat and preserving options.

Weakness:
- frame tactics, youth/community politics, open-standard improvisation, trusting unlicensed talent.

Moral lines:
- will not launch with known uncontrollable reactor/life-support failure.
- will not surrender protected passengers without valid authority and review.
- may abandon cargo/objective/another ship when survival model crosses threshold.

GA2 arc:
1. accepts command only with wage/safety/authority terms.
2. clashes with P-001 over first mission and emergency acceleration.
3. makes a retreat that appears to fail mission but preserves evidence/crew—or vice versa.
4. later trusts distributed departments while retaining abandon-ship responsibility.
5. GA2 end: ship command charter revised; CAP-001 remains independent captain, not subordinate chauffeur.

Voice:
- uses exact ship-state language and refuses vague “we can make it.”
- dry shipboard humor, dislikes heroic titles.
- under fear becomes formal and cites names/roles one by one.

Ordinary anchor:
- personally checks a mundane daily ship routine despite rank.
- has one leisure habit—cards, knot/line craft, old radio drama, cooking—that cadets initially misunderstand.

# ENG-001 — Licensed Chief Engineer

## 5. Identity

Code: ENG-001  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: 32–62 provisional  
Culture: Workshop-Industrial / Brann Yard  
Background: certified ship engineer with prior connection to NR72 class, Helix subcontracting or worker guild  
Current status: owed wages/benefits, blacklisted after safety dispute or plant closure

Core contrast:

> H-002 can see a new solution faster; ENG-001 knows which old shortcut killed someone thirty years ago and what certificate keeps the ship from being impounded tomorrow.

## 6. Want, Need and Conflict

External want:
- restore vessel without falsifying certification.
- recover unpaid crew/worker claims and protect engineering department.
- prevent cadet improvisation from creating untraceable risk.

Internal need:
- stop treating certification as the only valid knowledge.
- mentor/share authority without absorbing H-002’s labor or blocking innovation.
- confront own participation in hiding old hull defects.

False belief:

> If a modification cannot be fully documented and certified, it should not be used.

Truth:

> Emergency and frontier survival require provisional knowledge—but responsibility, tests and stop conditions must still be explicit.

Authority:
- final engineering certification and reactor/propulsion technical stop while licensed chief.
- H-002 owns her design work and specific frame/open-service decisions but cannot certify whole ship alone.

Conflict with H-002:
- theory/documentation gaps vs practical brilliance.
- family/workshop rights vs institutional certification.
- mutual respect must emerge through failed tests and shared repair, not instant mentorship.

GA2 arc:
- starts as contracted obstacle/necessary license.
- discovers common coolant trunk was misrepresented in old records, including possible own predecessor/guild complicity.
- co-designs limited open bypass with H-002.
- may leave/retire after establishing a distributed engineering board, or continue as chief with reduced monopoly.

Moral line:
- will not sign false safe state.
- may refuse departure while people outside are in danger.

Voice:
- detailed failure histories, old yard slang, asks who will inspect after them.
- distinguishes “works,” “works once,” “can be maintained,” and “can be certified.”

# NAV-001 — Navigator / Route Pilot

## 7. Identity

Code: NAV-001  
Canonical name: UNLOCKED  
Age: 24–45 provisional  
Culture: Neutral/Route  
Background: courier, tug navigator or former Imperial route officer with license restrictions  
Current status: possesses current Lumen/Kael route qualification; owes debt or seeks access to Taris/Silex information

Core contrast:

> P-001 knows where history says events occurred. NAV-001 knows whether this ship can actually reach that place with today’s queue, mass, heat and credentials.

External want:
- regain/expand route license and control personal navigation data.
- avoid being permanently attached to one faction.

Internal need:
- stop selling route neutrality as personal nonresponsibility.
- disclose when side deals or hidden routes alter passenger risk.

Competence:
- node queue, phase alignment, tug/escort routing, traffic interpretation, damaged-route risk.

Weakness:
- politics beyond immediate corridor, combat command, trusting official forecasts.

Authority:
- recommends and executes navigation under captain.
- may refuse an uncharted transition below safety threshold.

GA2 role:
- route graph made human and practical.
- has contacts at Kael/Taris/Silex but no universal map.
- hidden side contract or family/community route can conflict with mission.

Voice:
- speaks in windows, margins, and alternative routes.
- dislikes “fastest” without asking fastest for whom and under what load.

## 9. Core Student Roles Aboard

### P-001 — Mission and Flight Commander

Primary:
- 07 pilot, external mission planning, boarding/rescue tactical command.

Required limitation:
- reports to captain for ship safety/movement.
- cannot select passengers/cargo/medical risk alone.
- present-body sync and fatigue tracked.

GA2 growth:
- learns to propose missions with resource/crew/contract consequences before asking for action.
- loses at least one objective because captain/department veto is valid.

### H-001 — Logistics and Passenger Planning Lead

Primary:
- manifests, convoy splits, ration/evacuation models, community negotiation.

Required limitation:
- Q-001 controls legal/financial manifest.
- CAP-001 controls ship safety/capacity.
- M-001 controls medical transport.
- passengers/community representatives retain consent.

GA2 growth:
- his protected community refuses unified routing.
- builds separate civilian/logistics authority rather than becoming ship quartermaster subordinate.

### H-002 — Frame/Open-Service Engineer

Primary:
- 07, mission modules, cross-standard adapters, workshop network.

Required limitation:
- ENG-001 certifies reactor/ship systems.
- cannot use all workshop space/power.
- designs require documentation, test and liability owner.

GA2 growth:
- controlled standard release, failure/accident, worker network.

### S-001 — Communications, Data and Audit Cadet

Primary:
- authentication, log provenance, secure relay, privacy/record controls.

Required limitation:
- old hardware, bandwidth and credentials.
- no command authority over content beyond legal/charter rules.

GA2 growth:
- confronts forged ghost-ship record and public truth timing.

### M-001 — Rescue/Medical Cadet

Primary:
- stabilization, passenger health, medical stop, casualty routes.

Required limitation:
- operates under licensed medical officer/Neutral agreement until qualified.
- ship lacks advanced care.

GA2 growth:
- convoy triage, identity/treatment destination conflict, central medical network temptation.

## 11. Opening Watch Structure

Because crew is short, three-watch ideal is impossible.

Opening:
- two main watches + irregular repair/alert teams.
- fatigue accumulates.
- cadets cannot simply work every crisis.

Minimum watch rule:
- command/helm: 2 qualified per active watch.
- engineering: 4–5 per watch including reactor/life support.
- sensors/comms: 2 per watch.
- medical: at least 1 awake/1 on call.
- security/flight: mission-dependent.

By episode 130 target:
- recruit enough for 3-watch partial structure or accept reduced mission tempo.

Crew-fatigue consequences:
- errors, conflict, illness, reduced maintenance, invalid certification and command judgment.

No montage solution:
- training takes weeks/months and cannot instantly produce licensed officers.

## 12. Decision Matrix

| Decision | Final/primary authority | Required consultation/veto |
|---|---|---|
| departure/route execution | CAP-001 | NAV-001, ENG-001, mission contract |
| reactor/acceleration safety | ENG-001/CAP-001 | H-002 for modified systems |
| tactical frame/boarding plan | P-001 | CAP-001 safety, H-001 logistics, M-001 medical, rules of engagement |
| passenger acceptance | CAP-001/Q-001 | H-001, M-001, Neutral/legal terms, community consent |
| cargo/module allocation | Q-001/CAP-001 | ENG-001/H-002/M-001/mission owner |
| medical quarantine/stop | licensed MED-001 | CAP-001 for ship safety; M-001 operational input |
| evidence/data release | S-001 under charter/legal owner | patients/sources, CAP-001 operational security, Neutral/Imperial authority where valid |
| surrender/abandon ship | CAP-001 | department reports; cannot surrender protected people outside law automatically |
| mission change | mission board/contract authority or emergency CAP-001+P-001 | resource/medical/engineering/data impacts |

Rule:
- emergency authority expires and is audited; it does not silently become permanent.

## 15. Anti-Monopoly Controls

- P-001 cannot pilot 07 and direct every ship department simultaneously.
- H-001 cannot decide community/passenger choices alone.
- H-002 is not opening chief engineer.
- S-001 cannot hack without access and cannot own truth.
- M-001 is not fully licensed and cannot heal everything.
- CAP-001 can stop ship but cannot own mission, passengers, data or modifications.
- every major success requires at least three authority domains to align.
