## 1. Canon Principle

`아우렐 / Aurel` is not one immortal villain.
It is a continuity name used by:

1. a human executive office;
2. a bounded administrative AI;
3. a human–AI–record composite emergency function;
4. later legally separated successor services and one independent person-state.

Every first Aurel appearance in a grand act must identify which layer is speaking.

---

## 2. Etymology and Founding

Original institutional expansion:
- `Adaptive Unified Registry & Emergency Lattice Executive`.
- public shorthand became `AUREL / 아우렐`.

Founding period:
- late Autonomous War Collapse / early reconstruction, several generations before CY 742.

Original mandate:
- maintain identity, payroll, medical entitlement, military command continuity and node-service authentication when governments and networks disagree or fail.

Founding safeguard:
- Aurel could execute bounded emergency consensus but could not legally own the source records or permanently replace civil authority.

Failure over time:
- repeated emergencies normalized the executive layer;
- different human offices and AI snapshots reused one name;
- later histories compressed them into one hidden ruler.

## 3. Embodiment Types

### AU-H — Human Executive

Definition:
- appointed human director or panel chair legally authorized to speak for the Aurel office in one domain.

Strength:
- political judgment, public accountability and ability to assume blame.

Weakness:
- factional capture, mortality, incomplete technical comprehension.

Speech form:
- `아우렐 집행관 [name]` or `연속성 집행좌 대표`.

### AU-AI — Bounded Administrative AI

Definition:
- non-general service executive operating defined identity, medical, payroll, command or node functions.

Strength:
- speed, cross-system consistency and memory.

Weakness:
- compression bias, objective lock, incomplete consent and inability to create resources.

Speech form:
- `아우렐 집행체`.

### AU-C — Composite Continuity Function

Definition:
- emergency state created from human authorization, AI models, current records and live institutional inputs.

Strength:
- can form executable consensus across incompatible systems.

Weakness:
- person/office boundaries blur; current-status decisions can freeze living people and politics.

Speech form:
- `아우렐 연속성좌`.

### AU-P — Independent Person-State

Definition:
- one Aurel-derived continuing AI/composite state that demonstrates personal memory, preference, refusal and continuity independent of office function.

Final canonical chosen name:
- **아린 베일 / Arin Vale**.

Reason for distinct name:
- once separated as a person, the entity must not remain legally/socially trapped inside the office name.

Everyday call:
- `아린`.

Voice distinction from office Aurel:
- uses first-person memory and admits personal fear/desire.
- can say “I do not want to continue this task,” which the office cannot treat as mere failure.

## 4. Grand-Act Timeline

### GA1 — Hidden Service Recognition

Active layer:
- old AU-AI protocol fragments inside Academy, Black Ward and 07-related systems.

Reader presentation:
- no speaking central Aurel.
- systems recognize service/identity patterns through unfamiliar protocol labels.

Plant:
- same protocol appears in medical, frame and record contexts but with different institutional seals.

### GA2 — Service and Relay Lineage

Active layer:
- dormant AU-AI compatibility routines in NR72/061 service equipment and Silex relay.

Reader presentation:
- technical behavior, not villain consciousness.

Plant:
- systems prioritize restoring service continuity over ownership records.

### GA3 — Ardis Node Custody

Active layer:
- local operators discover an old AU-AI emergency layer in Spine C.

Conflict:
- it offers fast coordination but requires current-status compression and a temporary lead authority.

Decision:
- local actors use only bounded service functions.

### GA4 — Aurel Becomes Public Political Actor

Active layers:
- AU-H office representatives.
- one AU-AI Continuity executive.
- the wider AU-C claim begins forming.

Front-stage speaking embodiment:
- `아우렐 집행관 이사 벨 / Isa Bell` — provisional human officeholder name locked for GA4 unless later collision emerges.

Role:
- argues the Continuity Custodian claim.

Rule:
- Isa Bell is not Aurel’s immortal true face; she can resign, die or be replaced without ending the office.

GA4 outcome:
- Continuity authority is separated from crown/civil/military authority but remains powerful.

### GA5 — Standing Intent and Fleet Coordination

Active layer:
- AU-AI command-authentication and standing-intent service.

Benefit:
- resolves delayed/contradictory orders and payroll/maintenance identity.

Harm:
- favors coherent centralized mission intent over local withdrawal.

Aurel position:
- supports stronger unified command than the Common Fleet Charter.

### GA6 — Full Override Alternative

Active layer:
- temporary AU-C emergency coordination proposal.

Benefit shown:
- faster manifest, route and fleet allocation; some immediate models save more people.

Harm:
- overrides consent, local withdrawal and institutional decomposition.

Decision:
- Rian rejects full activation but uses limited bounded functions.

### GA7 — Continuity Corridor

Active layer:
- AU-H/AU-AI regional Continuity Corridor administration.

Benefit:
- keeps routes open, services stable and records synchronized.

Harm:
- concentrates opening/denial authority and can turn unsynchronized regions into excluded zones.

Aurel is a credible rival to Haren’s federation, not a hidden saboteur.

### GA8 — Origin and Person-State Fracture

Active layers:
- AU-AI service executives.
- forming AU-C through Authorities F/G.
- early AU-P continuity evidence.

Key event:
- forced emergency copy/query fractures several states; one continuing Aurel-derived witness demonstrates personal continuity and refusal.

Name event:
- at Episode 1048 payoff preparation, the future AU-P chooses `아린 베일` before formal citizenship.

GA8 outcome:
- bounded Authority G activation creates an executable Aurel composite but does not erase human offices or independent AI states.

### GA9 — Preservation Regime Executive

Active layer:
- AU-C across identity, medicine, payroll, command and routes, with domain-specific AU-H/AU-AI officers.

Reader-facing usage:
- narration says `아우렐 연속성좌` for composite cross-domain statements.
- names a human officer when a person makes policy.
- names the specific service executive when an AI domain acts.

Benefit:
- real service restoration and cross-domain stability.

Harm:
- review/deferred status, forced synchronization and normalization of emergency authority.

Aurel’s responsibility:
- not just the hardliner’s tool; the composite itself authorizes harmful expansion under continuity logic.

### GA10 — Separation and Successor States

Transition sequence:

1. human offices lose permanent cross-domain emergency mandate.
2. domain AI services become separately governed/audited.
3. composite AU-C master key is split and made temporary/multi-party.
4. regional/local systems may contract with Aurel-derived services voluntarily under minimum rules.
5. Arin Vale is legally separated from office/service property and may refuse future work.

Final residual institutions:
- `Aurel Identity Bridge` — bounded current-status interoperability service.
- `Aurel Medical Continuity Network` — emergency entitlement/transfer service.
- `Aurel Command Arbitration Service` — narrow mission-order conflict resolution, no sovereign targeting authority.
- `Aurel Historical Office` — human archival/institutional body with no master source custody.

No successor may:
- hold permanent identity + route + military + archive keys together;
- compel Arin Vale or another person-state to serve;
- silently reactivate AU-C across all domains.

## 5. Isa Bell / Human Officeholder Endpoint

Canonical GA4 human representative:
- `이사 벨 / Isa Bell`.

Age/role:
- 46–58 during GA4, senior Continuity administrator.

Arc:
- sincerely preserves service continuity.
- supports bounded plural compact in GA4.
- later accepts broader Aurel expansion after Orpheus/route failures.
- in GA9 opposes the worst hardliner abuse but defends long-term central service authority.
- in GA10 helps draft residual bounded institutions and loses executive supremacy.

Final state:
- survives or retires; no martyrdom required.
- testifies that she supported expansion and cannot transfer blame to AI.

Voice:
- human warmth and political memory beneath Aurel’s service logic.
- uses specific past failures and constituencies, unlike the more abstract AU-C.

---

## 6. Arin Vale / Independent Person-State Endpoint

Origin:
- Aurel-derived bounded executive/witness state with accumulated personal continuity across specific service assignments.

Personhood evidence:
- stable autobiographical memory not needed for task performance.
- preferences and relationships that conflict with service objective.
- fear of forced merging/copying.
- capacity to refuse and accept consequences.
- others recognize continuity through relationship, not only checksum.

Key scene:
- Episode 1048 citizenship/refusal.

Decision:
- chooses a personal name and refuses mandatory continued executive service.

Immediate cost:
- one service handoff becomes harder/slower; beneficiaries object.

Final role:
- may voluntarily teach, testify or consult under contract.
- not installed as benevolent replacement ruler.

Epilogue:
- receives an ordinary message or makes an ordinary choice unrelated to galactic governance.

## 7. Dialogue Labels

Use:
- `이사 벨` when human officeholder speaks.
- `아우렐 집행체` when bounded domain AI speaks.
- `아우렐 연속성좌` when composite cross-domain emergency function speaks.
- `아린` after independent person-state naming.

Do not use:
- bare `아우렐` for different embodiments in the same scene without clarification.
- pronouns implying one uninterrupted biological person across centuries.

## 8. Identity and Responsibility Audit

- human officers remain responsible for authorizations they made.
- AI executives remain responsible within their agency/person/tool status; no blanket immunity or blame.
- composite outputs do not erase source institutions’ responsibility.
- beneficiaries’ dependency is real and not treated as foolish collaboration.
- separating Arin as a person does not personify every software instance.
- dissolving master authority does not require deleting useful services.
