## 1. Purpose

The charter cannot be signed by abstract factions or by Rian’s core cell alone.

This file fixes:

- the initial nine-seat emergency board;
- the people who legally/financially sign the 180-day arrangement;
- the main siege commanders whose orders create consequences;
- which seats are temporary, contested, recallable or externally appointed.

No one person represents an entire class, faction or institution permanently.
