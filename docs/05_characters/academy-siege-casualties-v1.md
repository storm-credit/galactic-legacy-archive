## 1. Rule

The five siege deaths are fixed as separate causal chains. They do not form one heroic casualty montage and cannot be attributed to one faction or one constitutional choice.

# SG-D01 — 이벤 솔 / Iven Sol

Role:
- Academy professional Security/transfer officer at Dock D.

Age:
- 36.

Alignment:
- accepted the Imperial continuity transfer as the safest legal option, but opposed indiscriminate force.

Death chain:

1. Imperial/Academy transfer and resistance/Independence interference create a shuttle-frame collision/boarding hazard.
2. Iven moves current passengers/crew out of the immediate path.
3. conflicting lock/thruster/frame actions cause fatal impact/decompression injury.

Consequence:
- Imperial/Security supporters cite the death as proof armed resistance made lawful protection lethal.
- witnesses also record that Iven questioned mixed voluntary/disputed boarding.

# SG-D02 — 넬리 오름 / Nelli Orm

Role:
- 17-year-old general technical cadet in Ring B, not part of Rian’s cell or major faction.

Preference:
- undecided; wanted to stay with two friends while keeping an individual exit option.

Death chain:

1. Security seals a corridor according to incomplete manifest/transfer order.
2. students/resistance and Independence contacts attempt to reopen/move people.
3. conflicting door/rotation/crowd commands cause crush/impact injury.
4. local route reaches Nelli too late.

Consequence:
- no faction can claim Nelli chose its political outcome.
- Yori/Darin/current-person rules are judged against this death.

# SG-D03 — 루스크 이덴 / Rusk Iden

Role:
- 47-year-old Ring C power/recycler technician and shift safety representative.

Preference:
- internal/hybrid continuation; willing to work under Helix certification but wanted worker authority/pay.

Death chain:

1. Helix remote lock, local bypass and radical/opportunist sabotage interact in an industrial power segment.
2. Rusk stays to isolate a cascading load and clear workers.
3. delayed/competing control leaves the local isolation incomplete.
4. fire/arc/impact causes fatal injury.

Consequence:
- Nera, Sera, Bram and Helix all carry a defensible but incomplete share of the system choices.
- Rusk’s family/work unit pursues pay, disability/death benefits and technical-record access.

# SG-D04 — 베라 콜 / Vera Khol

Role:
- 30-year-old radical/Scorched denial operative.

Goal:
- destroy core/central transfer infrastructure rather than allow Imperial/Helix capture.

Death chain:

- continues the denial operation after Ern/Federalist actors oppose it.
- killed during the Dock D denial craft/frame disable and structural hazard.

Consequence:
- radical propaganda calls Vera a martyr betrayed by moderates.
- Ern’s network faces internal fracture.
- death does not excuse the denial plan or erase Vera’s political motive.

# SG-D05 — 토마 에스 / Toma Ess

Role:
- 34-year-old Black Ward/public-clinic medical technician and patient-transport specialist.

Preference:
- Neutral-backed medical trusteeship with stable Helix supplies.

Death chain:

1. Ring C/Spine power instability and contested patient-transfer commands delay safe movement.
2. Toma remains with a high-dependency patient/module during service interruption/attack.
3. centralized command could have restored one link faster, while blanket seizure would have overridden patient choices.
4. Toma dies from injury or exposure while the patient survives or is stabilized.

Consequence:
- patient/staff families can truthfully argue the segmented transition cost a life.
- charter review must preserve this evidence rather than presenting decentralization as harmless.
