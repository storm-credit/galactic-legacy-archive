## 1. Front-Stage Rule

GA6 foregrounds five decision constituencies in addition to the existing core/GA5 commanders:

1. State/Capital Continuity Group.
2. Civilian Convoy Council.
3. Institutional/Hospital Distribution Advocate.
4. Neutral Rescue Coordinator.
5. Local Orpheus Node/Route Custodian.

Each must make an independent choice that changes the outcome.
No group exists only to plead before P-001.

---

# OR-STATE-001 — State Fleet Continuity Commander

## 2. Identity

Role:
- commander/coordinator of S-STATE military and institutional escort.

Possible consolidation:
- may be FC-001 or another senior officer under Protector/Compact authority.

Recommended:
- use FC-001 if continuity remains strong; add a separate state-fleet chief of staff only if command burden demands it.

Core contrast:

> 수십만 명의 군인만이 아니라 병원·급여·항로·행정의 기능을 보호하고 있어, 자신들의 우선통과가 특권이 아니라 더 많은 생명을 위한 의무라고 믿는다.

External want:
- preserve enough S-STATE ships/functions to keep Compact and wider fronts alive.
- maintain formation discipline and route security.

Internal need:
- accept institutional fragmentation and destruction of valuable hulls/functions rather than treating central integrity as sacred.

False belief:

> A state function divided below operational integrity is equivalent to losing it; therefore the central group must transit intact.

Helpful action:
- releases escorts/medical capacity to civilians and accepts distributed command when evidence proves viable.

Harmful action:
- restricts civilian access/information or attempts to seize Window A.

Voice:
- specific institutional dependencies and theater consequences, not “soldiers first.”

# OR-CIV-001 — Civilian Convoy Council Coordinator

## 3. Identity

Role:
- elected/negotiated coordinator among hundreds of civilian convoy captains and communities.

Background:
- merchant convoy organizer, habitat emergency official, refugee-route leader or former public-service administrator.

Core contrast:

> 수백 척의 민간선을 하나의 ‘난민 함대’로 부르는 순간 각 배의 생명유지·목적지·위험과 사람들의 거부권이 사라진다는 것을 안다.

External want:
- secure maximum civilian transit, fair manifests and transparent route information.
- prevent military/state institutions from repeating original abandonment.

Internal need:
- accept triage, ship-level discipline and that some civilian groups cannot be moved first.
- stop claiming unified civilian will when councils/captains disagree.

False belief:

> If civilians receive full information and representation, they will accept a fair common evacuation plan.

Truth:

> Scarcity and unequal risk produce conflict even under transparency; representation cannot eliminate coercion or loss.

Competence:
- convoy communication, civilian logistics, public legitimacy and negotiation among communities.

Weakness:
- fleet combat, secure authentication and making decisions for incapacitated/isolated ships.

Helpful action:
- prevents panic, redistributes supplies and accepts mixed/distributed transit.

Harmful action:
- blocks military movement/inspection or favors organized/documented convoy groups over unregistered/isolated ships.

Relationship with H-001:
- respects route models, resists his aggregation/optimization and insists on independent captains/communities.

# OR-INST-001 — Institutional Distribution Advocate

## 4. Identity

Role:
- senior civil/medical/Registry/Continuity official responsible for distributing state functions across smaller ships/nodes.

Recommended background:
- mobile hospital/health-network director or civil-service continuity architect, not purely technical archivist.

Core contrast:

> 중앙 기관을 살리려 하지만, 기관을 살리는 방법이 한 덩어리로 보존하는 것이 아니라 사람·기능·기록을 서로 다른 곳에 나누는 것임을 주장한다.

External want:
- preserve minimum viable medicine, payment, identity, command and technical functions.
- prevent both intact central convoy loss and reckless dismantling.

Internal need:
- accept unequal regional reconstruction and loss of institutional identity/status.
- allow local/provincial systems to own functions rather than remain branches.

False belief:

> With enough modular planning, every essential function can survive in distributed form.

Truth:

> Some institutions depend on scale, teams and trust that cannot be copied; distribution saves parts and destroys others.

Competence:
- organizational decomposition, staffing, medical/civil dependencies and continuity planning.

Weakness:
- fleet combat, community consent, ship-level capacity and political legitimacy.

Helpful action:
- identifies functions that can be split and volunteers own institution/status for dissolution.

Harmful action:
- ranks staff/data/functions and pressures individuals into assigned ships/roles.

Relationship with H-002/S-001/M-001:
- central collaborator/conflict for modular systems, records and medicine.

# OR-NEU-001 — Neutral Rescue Coordinator

## 5. Identity

Role:
- regional Neutral rescue/insurance coordinator with several member captains, potentially R-NEU-001 in expanded role or a distinct superior/peer.

Recommended:
- R-NEU-001 remains one key captain/claims officer; OR-NEU-001 coordinates but cannot command all member ships.

Core contrast:

> 더 많은 사람을 태우겠다고 약속하는 순간 이미 구조 중인 환자·승무원·다음 항로를 죽일 수 있다는 것을 안다.

External want:
- maximize rescue without destroying Neutral capacity/credibility.
- enforce disarmament, manifests, medical triage and reciprocal guarantees.

Internal need:
- own moral responsibility for refusal rather than hide behind capacity formulas.
- allow member captains/communities to make costly exceptions while sharing consequences.

False belief:

> Transparent capacity rules are the only protection against political capture and total collapse.

Truth:

> Rules need exceptions and burden-sharing; refusing an exception is still a moral choice, not neutrality.

Helpful action:
- opens additional capacity/route and protects civilians from seizure.

Harmful action:
- refuses boarding or cuts off a group with weak identity/guarantee, contributing to deaths.

Relationship with P-001:
- remembers original/structural debts and rejects heroic unlimited-rescue promises.

# OR-NODE-001 — Orpheus Node Custodian / Local Authority

## 6. Identity

Role:
- local node operations leader and representative of Orpheus workers/residents.

Background:
- survived or inherited records of original node use/abandonment; current community bears maintenance and military occupation costs.

Core contrast:

> 모두가 오르페우스를 통과문으로 보지만, 자신은 그 창을 열 때마다 고향의 전력·열·구조물과 운영자 생명을 소모한다는 것을 안다.

External want:
- prevent node seizure/destruction and protect local workers/residents.
- choose traffic/thermal sacrifice with local standing.

Internal need:
- accept regional/civilizational obligations beyond local survival without surrendering all custody.

False belief:

> Those who physically maintain the node have the strongest claim to decide who passes.

Truth:

> Local custody is necessary but not sufficient when millions depend on the route; authority needs shared burdens and accountability.

Competence:
- node windows, thermal/structural risk, local defense and worker coordination.

Weakness:
- fleet/civilian convoy politics and accepting externally imposed risk.

Helpful action:
- opens Window B/C or Grey-route support at severe local cost.

Harmful action:
- withholds capacity or favors local/state alliance to preserve community.

Relationship with H-002/S-001:
- technical/provenance collaboration and conflict.

# OR-HOLD-001 — Holdout/Stay Representative

## 7. Identity

Role:
- representative of people who cannot or choose not to transit and must survive local holdout/occupation.

Why needed:
- evacuation fiction often treats those left behind as failure objects without agency.

External want:
- secure supplies, defense, surrender terms and future contact for local shelter group.

Internal need:
- accept that staying can be both autonomous and coerced by scarcity.
- avoid claiming moral superiority over evacuees.

Helpful action:
- organizes local shelters and frees transit capacity.

Harmful action:
- conceals vulnerable people or refuses evacuation for ideological/community reasons.

E690 target:
- holdout survives partially or faces unresolved occupation; not all are rescued off-screen.

## 8. Existing Commanders in GA6

### FC-001 / State or Protector Formation

- advocates state continuity priority or central coordination.
- can accept distributed sacrifice while insisting on military integrity.

### FC-002 / Provincial Commodore

- prioritizes provincial/civilian routes and recall obligations.

### FC-003 / Crown Guard

- protects claimant/civilian institutions; may break household mission to save others.

### FC-004 / Yard/Tender Commander

- chooses which ships/hulls/parts are sacrificed and whether repair capacity survives.

### FC-005 / Outer Front Commander

- may refuse P-001 allocation, protect local population/front or offer separate route.
- not automatically antagonist; can save one front while worsening state plan.

### CR-001 / Crew Representative

- crew consent, scuttling, abandonment, rescue and family consequences.

### CAP-001

- first ship safety/scuttle/passenger decisions; potential major sacrifice vessel or surviving relay command.

## 9. Relationship Conflicts

### State Commander ↔ Civilian Coordinator

- long-term institutional deaths vs immediate civilian abandonment.
- both contain civilians and essential workers.

### Institutional Distributor ↔ Continuity Custodian

- distributed imperfect functions vs centralized verified continuity.

### Neutral Coordinator ↔ Civilian Council

- capacity/identity/disarmament rules vs mass need and political exclusion.

### Node Custodian ↔ All Fleets

- local ownership/risk vs regional millions.

### Holdout Representative ↔ Evacuation Planners

- being left behind vs choosing to stay and needing resources/voice.

### P-001 ↔ All

- has strategic command but no legitimate right to reduce groups to one objective.
- must state what cannot be saved and accept refusal/condemnation.

## 10. Independent Decision Requirements

Before GA6 end:

- OR-STATE-001 voluntarily fragments or refuses fragmentation of one institution/formation.
- OR-CIV-001 accepts or rejects a triage/manifest that disadvantages own organized supporters.
- OR-INST-001 dissolves or protects an institution at cost.
- OR-NEU-001 grants one exception and refuses another.
- OR-NODE-001 opens/withholds a window at local cost.
- OR-HOLD-001 chooses a holdout plan and retains later claims.
- at least two fleet commanders make decisions without P-001 that save different groups.

## 12. Anti-Manipulation Controls

- civilians are not a silent moral mass.
- state institutions contain ordinary civilians and essential care.
- military crews are not expendable or privileged category alone.
- Neutral refusal has reasons and consequences.
- local node workers are not infrastructure NPCs.
- holdouts retain agency and aftermath.
- no group’s suffering exists solely to redeem P-001.
