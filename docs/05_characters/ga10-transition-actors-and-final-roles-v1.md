## 1. Front-Stage Rule

GA10 is not P-001’s solo abdication story.
The transition requires at least seven independent constituencies:

1. P-001 — central corrective/key holder.
2. A-001/Continuity staff — expertise and central-service constituency.
3. beneficiary regions — continuity requirements and transition caution.
4. plural/local/hybrid regions — rights and distributed-capacity foundation.
5. fleet/route/security actors — protection and threat response.
6. industrial/medical/AI/record partners — technical/service/personhood handoffs.
7. excluded/closed/forgotten people — remedy and standing.

At least three decisive final fronts succeed without P-001’s direct solution.

# TR-L01 — Transition Convenor / Beneficiary Coalition Representative

## 2. Identity

Role:
- leader/convener chosen by synchronized beneficiary regions and service institutions to negotiate handoff conditions.

Possible consolidation:
- PR-L01 beneficiary leader can fill this role.

Core contrast:

> 중앙권력을 반대하지 않아서가 아니라, 병원·급여·신분이 다시 끊길 사람들을 책임져야 하므로 느린 이양을 요구한다.

External want:
- maintain guaranteed services, public safety and current status through transition.
- give beneficiary communities real veto/standing rather than asking them to trust revolutionaries.

Internal need:
- accept risks and costs to protect excluded people’s rights and prevent permanent central dependency.

False belief:

> Authority should be transferred only after every replacement proves equal reliability.

Truth:

> Requiring equal proof preserves monopoly forever; beneficiaries must help fund and govern imperfect alternatives.

Helpful action:
- supplies staff/resources/legitimacy and protects hospitals during handoff.

Harmful action:
- delays, conditions or blocks rights restoration and regional authority transfer.

End role:
- leader of minimum-service/equalization compact or regional service authority, not P-001 loyalist.

# TR-L02 — Regional Transition Leader

## 3. Identity

Role:
- head/delegate of the first RS-1/RS-2 handoff region.

Possible consolidation:
- PR-L04 opt-out leader, Ardis successor or RF-L01 federation delegate.

Core contrast:

> 중앙분류를 끝내려 하지만, 첫 이양 실패에서 자기 지역의 약한 주민이 지방 엘리트와 기술격차에 먼저 희생되는 것을 본다.

External want:
- receive identity/service/route/record authority and prove local/plural governance.

Internal need:
- accept external claims, minimum obligations and independent oversight rather than defining autonomy as noninterference.

False belief:

> Once keys and records return locally, democratic legitimacy will correct implementation failures.

Truth:

> Capacity and power do not equal legitimacy; distributed authority needs shared resources, safe exit and outside standing.

Helpful action:
- continues handoff after failure and opens local decisions/data to affected outsiders.

Harmful action:
- protects local officials/system or delays aid to preserve political autonomy.

End role:
- one regional model among several, not template for all.

# TR-L03 — Central Continuity Transition Director

## 4. Identity

Role:
- A-001, successor, human/AI component or new jointly appointed director responsible for devolution while maintaining central residue.

Core contrast:

> 체제를 해체하는 사람이 그 체제를 가장 잘 아는 관료라는 사실 때문에 누구에게도 완전히 신뢰받지 못한다.

External want:
- transfer authority without service collapse, hostile capture or loss of expertise.
- preserve bounded residual continuity services.

Internal need:
- surrender keys/staff/data before all risks are controlled and accept external/local governance over former office.

False belief:

> Expertise requires temporary custody until recipients are ready.

Truth:

> Custody shapes readiness and can prevent it; experts must train and hand over authority while remaining advisors rather than owners.

Helpful action:
- reveals hidden dependencies/backdoors and transfers staff/knowledge.

Harmful action:
- withholds one key/function or reasserts control after failure.

End role options:
- bounded residual-service official under plural oversight.
- removed/retired individual while office staff distribute.
- human/AI components choose different end roles.

# TR-L04 — Excluded/Closed Identity Coalition Representative

## 5. Identity

Role:
- representative of low-confidence, administratively closed, contained, relocated and deleted/forgotten communities/persons.

Possible consolidation:
- PC-003, Right-to-Forget representative, AI/composite advocate or new coalition delegate.

Core contrast:

> 중앙체제의 피해자이지만, 즉시 모든 기록과 현재상태를 없애면 자신의 가족·치료·배상증거도 사라질 수 있음을 안다.

External want:
- restore provisional standing, movement, services, records and claims.
- prevent both central permanent identity and reckless deletion.

Internal need:
- accept evidence preservation, review and obligations without reproducing classification violence.

False belief:

> Freedom requires ending every status created by the regime immediately.

Truth:

> Some status/evidence must persist for care, accountability and rights; ownership and operational use must change instead.

Helpful action:
- designs provisional recognition, safe exit and remedy processes.

Harmful action:
- supports deletion/release that harms another group’s claims or safety.

End role:
- independent rights/claims/identity institution with no single permanent identity authority.

# TR-L05 — Transition Security / Common Fleet Commander

## 6. Identity

Role:
- commander under Common Fleet Charter protecting handoffs against hardliners, warlords and opportunists.

Possible consolidation:
- FC-002/003/004/005 or CAP-001 successor, not necessarily P-001.

Core contrast:

> 중앙군령을 해체하는 동안 가장 강한 공통군이 필요하지만, 그 공통군이 새 중앙권력이 되지 않도록 스스로 제한해야 한다.

External want:
- protect Seed sites, regions, staff, routes and civilians during handoff.
- prevent revenge, seizure and fragmentation war.

Internal need:
- relinquish emergency theater command and accept local/political constraints after victory.

False belief:

> Security authority can be handed back after threats are defeated.

Truth:

> Transition itself creates recurring threats; military limits and succession must function before the war is over.

Helpful action:
- defeats hardliner/opportunist attacks and protects opposing communities.

Harmful action:
- suspends local transfer, seizes routes or delays demobilization for security.

End role:
- mission-specific/interregional defense network, no personal permanent fleet.

# TR-L06 — Technical and Service Commons Representative

## 7. Identity

Role:
- H-002/industrial/medical/worker/AI coalition representative managing standards, manual fallback and service transfer.

Recommended:
- H-002 remains central but does not speak for every worker/user/AI; a council/co-representative structure is visible.

Core contrast:

> 권한을 나눠도 기술과 정비능력이 중앙에 남아 있으면 독립은 허상이며, 모든 규격을 즉시 풀면 약한 지역이 사고를 감당한다.

External want:
- train local operators, distribute tools/standards and preserve safety/liability.

Internal need:
- accept unequal implementation and give up technical gatekeeping/hero authorship.

Helpful action:
- creates manual/local fallback and multiple certifiers.

Harmful action:
- delays transfer, blocks unsafe region or releases incomplete systems.

End role:
- interoperable technical commons with plural certifiers and worker/user/AI rights.

# TR-L07 — Plural Archive and Current-Status Convenor

## 8. Identity

Role:
- S-001/plural archive coalition official coordinating current-status translation during transition.

Core contrast:

> 하나의 현재상태가 사라지면 사람을 되찾을 수 있지만, 지역마다 다른 신분과 기록이 생기면 가장 약한 사람이 국경과 시스템 사이에서 다시 지워질 수 있다.

External want:
- distribute identity/record authority while maintaining provisional cross-region recognition and evidence.

Internal need:
- accept thin shared current-status protocols without recreating master history/identity.

Helpful action:
- prevents identity loss, exposes manipulation and keeps plural mirrors interoperable.

Harmful action:
- delays correction, preserves harmful records or enables forum shopping/identity conflict.

End role:
- Archive Translation Commons or independent interregional verification network.

# TR-L08 — Medical Minimum-Service Coordinator

## 9. Identity

Role:
- M-001/medical network representative responsible for patient continuity during handoff.

Core contrast:

> 자유와 지역자치를 지지하지만 약·산소·치료기록은 하루만 끊겨도 죽는다는 사실 때문에 가장 느린 이양을 요구할 수밖에 없다.

External want:
- keep chronic/emergency treatment and patient-controlled records continuous.

Internal need:
- allow patient/community choice and local variation rather than using medical urgency as permanent central authority.

Helpful action:
- phases medical handoffs, reserves and cross-region recognition.

Harmful action:
- supports quarantine, delay or central retention that harms autonomy/identity.

End role:
- interregional medical minimum-service network with local providers and patient standing.

## 10. Core Character Final Role Targets

### P-001

Final role — recommended:
- no permanent central command or exclusive Archive index.
- works as teacher, mediator, expedition/field adviser, rotating emergency participant or public figure with bounded authority.
- cannot automatically retrieve a person’s future label.
- survives and must live with imperfect outcomes.

### H-001

- independent route/political/logistics institution under affected-region and local limits.
- specific Blood Admiral obligations/reparations remain.
- not P-001 subordinate or total federation ruler.

### H-002

- technical commons/open standard institution with multiple certifiers and worker/user rights.
- relinquishes singular design authorship/control.

### S-001

- plural archive/current-status translation institution.
- cannot curate one final history.

### M-001

- medical/rescue/minimum-service network and patient/consent framework.
- remains willing to oppose political/technical actors.

### CAP-001 / First Ship

Possible final role:
- training/rescue/route service ship, retired memorial/workshop, or lineage continued after destruction.
- not P-001 private flagship.

### E-001 / FC commanders / local leaders

- independent polities, fleets, route and institutions; mixed relationships with compact/regions.

## 11. A-001 Human–AI End Options

Recommended asymmetry:

- Human Director may accept removal/transfer, remain bounded adviser or oppose rapid handoff.
- Archive AI partner may support uncertainty publication/distribution, remain residual service person, or choose separate legal identity.
- office staff disperse into regional/residual institutions.

No option shifts all guilt to human or AI.
No total institutional deletion.

## 12. Independent Decision Requirements

Before ending:

- beneficiary coalition accepts one service risk/resource contribution for excluded/less-ready regions.
- first transition region owns correction after handoff failure rather than returning all control to P-001.
- Continuity transition director transfers or withholds a critical function and faces consequence.
- excluded coalition preserves necessary evidence/status while ending central ownership.
- security commander limits/demobilizes authority before all threats disappear.
- technical/service commons transfers tools/training despite risk.
- plural archive convenor accepts thin interoperability without final canonical history.
- medical network permits local/patient choice while protecting minimum treatment.
- at least three fronts succeed without P-001.
