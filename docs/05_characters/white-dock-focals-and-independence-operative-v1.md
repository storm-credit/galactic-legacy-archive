## 1. Purpose

White Dock’s casualties and civilian success cannot remain anonymous proof that the battle was serious.

The following people lock:

- who supplied local route and pressure knowledge;
- who survived with lasting harm;
- who died because response priorities and physical conditions failed;
- which Independence operative paid for Ern’s restraint and incomplete extraction.

They are ordinary workers and operatives, not hidden legendary heroes.
