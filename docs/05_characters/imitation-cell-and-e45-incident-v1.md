## 1. Core Design

The imitators are not foolish fans repeating a catchphrase.

They are a lower-resource provisional cell that correctly observes:

- official tests present coercive closed choices;
- Rian’s cell obtained witnesses, procedure and equipment only after creating a situation institutions could not quietly simplify;
- ordinary students without external value are transferred or punished before review completes.

Their error is to infer:

> If we force every authority to share the physical risk, they will be required to negotiate a fifth answer.

They copy the visible form of the opening victory while lacking:

- White Dock’s independent civil/Neutral witnesses;
- separate technical, record and medical stop authority;
- a current-person manifest;
- a safe exit and treatment plan;
- knowledge that one cell member carries a hidden Black Ward neural-treatment profile.

## 4. Imitation Plan

Declared demand:
- freeze C-9 transfers for 24 hours.
- release placement criteria and risk records.
- allow a witnessed collective hearing.

Method:

1. occupy the simulator/control hall during a scheduled evaluation.
2. place members and instructors inside the same stress/safety envelope.
3. route the official test’s closed-choice script to public/student displays.
4. use the copied adapter to prevent remote deletion/shutdown before evidence is mirrored.
5. surrender after an external witness and written hearing order are confirmed.

Intended nonlethal constraint:
- no firearms or explosives.
- doors can be opened locally.
- environmental systems remain within training limits.
- Aven believes shared exposure prevents violent Security action.

Hidden hazards:
- Kiro’s adapter bypasses an isolation layer.
- Leta’s Black Ward-linked safety response interacts with the simulator.
- current-person manifest omits two workers.
- one frightened student attempts a forced exit/reset.
- Security sends competing remote commands.

## 5. Exact Failure Chain

1. public broadcast and lock succeed.
2. Academy Security attempts remote isolation/reset.
3. copied adapter rejects the reset and routes an authentication conflict through the neural harness.
4. Leta’s hidden treatment protocol responds as if a dangerous sync event is occurring.
5. the simulator increases or repeats a stabilization/feedback cycle instead of cleanly disconnecting.
6. another participant panics and physically disrupts a restraint/console.
7. local power, cooling and door systems enter partial fault.
8. 25 people are held in a deteriorating environment for 31 minutes.

No one actor alone creates the full failure.
