## 1. Purpose

A species becomes believable only when readers meet people who are not there to explain the species.

For each core nonhuman candidate, this document creates four **role bundles**:

1. ordinary civilian/worker;
2. professional/expert;
3. political/institutional actor;
4. stereotype-breaking or oppositional person.

These are not final cast additions. They are anti-tokenism and anti-monoculture stress tests.

Every role bundle must have:
- a want unrelated to Rian;
- a resource or obligation;
- a decision they can make while Rian is absent;
- a relationship/family/community link;
- one scene function beyond species exposition;
- one failure mode.

# 2. Braxi Role Bundle

## BRX-CIV-01 — Yard Canteen Operator / Caregiver

Suggested window: GA2.

Life:
- runs a small canteen/meal contract near a repair yard;
- supports an older relative with low-g deconditioning;
- understands ration economics better than combat doctrine.

Own goal:
- keep the canteen contract independent from a yard consortium that wants bundled food/housing control.

Conflict:
- Braxi calorie requirements make suppliers charge a “heavy species surcharge” even when actual food cost does not justify it.

Story use:
- shows labor/care and ordinary price pressure;
- can refuse service credit to Rian's crew if they do not pay, proving protagonists are not exempt from local economy.

Stereotype break:
- first strong Braxi is not a soldier or mechanic.

## BRX-EXP-01 — Safety Certification Engineer

Suggested window: GA2–3.

Own goal:
- replace body-shape certification with outcome-based restraint and workplace safety standards.

Strength:
- technically rigorous, politically impatient.

Failure:
- sometimes treats local informal solutions as unacceptable before understanding why poor settlements use them.

Independent action:
- can suspend certification even if protagonists need departure clearance.

## BRX-POL-01 — Provincial Labor Arbitrator

Suggested window: GA3/4.

Own goal:
- stop occupational channeling without destroying industries that many Braxi families genuinely depend on.

Political tension:
- criticized by both industrial employers and radical activists.

Species point:
- refuses “Braxi unity” rhetoric; represents a mixed labor constituency.

## BRX-CONTRA-01 — Fine-Arts Conservator / Anti-Militarist

Suggested window: GA3 or later.

Own goal:
- protect fragile civilian archives/art during emergency requisition.

Stereotype break:
- physically well suited to load-bearing environments but hates being treated as naturally suited to violence.

Potential sharp scene:
- uses four-limb stability to save artwork during structural collapse, then rejects military recruitment praise.

# 3. Seia Role Bundle

## SEI-CIV-01 — Route Nursery Teacher

Suggested window: GA2.

Own goal:
- keep a mobile low-g nursery open after route closures force families into a higher-g station.

Conflict:
- temporary emergency housing is “safe” by human code but exhausts Seia children.

Independent action:
- organizes families to refuse relocation unless gravity/rest accommodations are added.

Stereotype break:
- introduces Seia through childcare, not navigation.

## SEI-EXP-01 — Hull-Acoustics Investigator

Suggested window: GA2.

Own goal:
- prove an accident was caused by ignored maintenance vibration rather than pilot error.

Strength:
- trained interpretation of pressure/vibration data.

Failure:
- overtrusts bodily perception before calibration data is complete.

Rule:
- perception gives clue, not automatic truth.

## SEI-POL-01 — Evacuation Standards Advocate

Suggested window: GA5–6 groundwork.

Own goal:
- make evacuation throughput metrics include physiology-specific acceleration injury.

Conflict:
- slower launch profiles can reduce total seats moved per hour.

Moral complexity:
- protecting Seia may cost time that endangers others; there is no free correct answer.

## SEI-CONTRA-01 — High-Acceleration Racing Enthusiast

Suggested window: GA3 ordinary-life scene.

Own goal:
- compete in adapted racing despite family/medical criticism.

Stereotype break:
- not all Seia avoid acceleration; risk appetite differs individually.

Control:
- adaptation/equipment is costly and does not erase biology.

# 4. Numar Role Bundle

## NUM-CIV-01 — Public-Housing Administrator

Suggested window: GA3.

Own goal:
- allocate wet-module capacity fairly among Numar families, mixed households and medical cases.

Conflict:
- water/fluid modules are expensive public infrastructure, not unlimited cultural accommodation.

Independent action:
- can deny a politically connected family's expansion request to preserve emergency capacity.

Stereotype break:
- first Numar can be an administrator, not swimmer/warrior.

## NUM-EXP-01 — Contamination Physician

Suggested window: GA3/5.

Own goal:
- keep a fluid-support infection from causing authorities to seal an entire Numar district.

Failure:
- may prioritize epidemiological caution over residents' economic survival.

## NUM-POL-01 — Integrated-Habitat Councilor

Suggested window: GA3/4.

Position:
- favors mixed districts and shared service budgets.

Opponent:
- another Numar bloc prefers concentrated wet districts for efficiency and cultural autonomy.

Species point:
- both positions are legitimate, preventing one “Numar political view.”

## NUM-CONTRA-01 — Dry-Habitat Courier / Performer

Suggested window: GA2/3.

Own goal:
- build career in gas-atmosphere route circuits despite maintenance burden.

Stereotype break:
- chooses a life physically inconvenient for their species.

Cost:
- skin/respiratory care schedule is non-negotiable.

# 5. Ivra Role Bundle

## IVR-CIV-01 — Customs Clerk

Suggested window: GA2/3.

Own goal:
- keep mixed travelers moving through a system whose old biometric software repeatedly splits Ivra identities.

Conflict:
- personally benefits from knowing workarounds but wants the broken system replaced.

Independent action:
- can legally hold a protagonist cargo entry because identity records do not reconcile.

Stereotype break:
- ordinary bureaucrat, not mysterious symbiotic oracle.

## IVR-EXP-01 — Pediatric Symbiosis Physician

Suggested window: GA4 or earlier background.

Own goal:
- prevent a new sterilization protocol from damaging juvenile symbiotic development.

Moral tension:
- public-health emergency may make broad sterilization attractive.

## IVR-POL-01 — Personhood Procedure Litigator

Suggested window: GA4/8.

Own goal:
- fix legal schemas that misidentify biological symbiosis without turning Ivra into a permanent special legal caste.

Conflict:
- some activists want species-specific protected classification; others want universal person/body separation rules.

## IVR-CONTRA-01 — Anti-Identity-Politics Ship Cook

Suggested window: GA2/5.

Own goal:
- run a kitchen and pay family debt, not represent Ivra politics.

Stereotype break:
- annoyed whenever strangers ask philosophical questions about being “two organisms.”

Character value:
- humor and boundary-setting without reducing the issue to comedy.

# 10. Anti-Tokenism Rules

1. No species' first two named people may share the same occupation.
2. No species' first two named people may share the same political position.
3. No species can be represented only by elites.
4. At least one dependent/caregiver/child/elder/ordinary worker perspective appears across the species layer.
5. A species-specific conflict must show disagreement inside that species.
6. Species should sometimes be irrelevant to a character's immediate scene goal.
7. Rian must not be the person who gives every species political voice.
8. Existing local actors solve local problems while Rian is elsewhere.

## 11. Reader-Load Allocation

Do not activate all 28 bundles.

Recommended first-stage use:
- GA2: 3–5 total nonhuman recurring/support roles across 2 species;
- GA3: expand to 6–10 total across 3–4 species;
- GA4–5: reuse existing people before introducing more;
- GA6: payoff known physiological/logistics differences;
- GA7–10: deepen politics/history through recurring individuals.

Most role bundles can remain design alternatives and never become named characters.
