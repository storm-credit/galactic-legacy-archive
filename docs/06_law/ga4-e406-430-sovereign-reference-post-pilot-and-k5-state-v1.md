## 3. Five Hidden Capture Paths and Controls

### Budget / Allocation

Risk:
- a nonactuating sovereign can starve disfavored fields or appeals.

Control:
- protected field minima;
- separate appropriations;
- public transfer ledger;
- physical/affected emergency reserves;
- sovereign proposal, not unilateral withholding/redirect.

### Appointment / Removal

Risk:
- career, renewal and removal dependence centralizes future decisions.

Control:
- domain nomination/confirmation/removal;
- term limits and standing alternates;
- sovereign may nominate external representatives/candidate lists only;
- emergency suspension requires evidence, alternate activation and rapid review.

### External Recognition / Treaty

Risk:
- one signature can reassign internal assets, people, keys or military access.

Control:
- agreement classification;
- relevant field and provincial/affected ratification;
- emergency agreement expiry;
- no automatic permanent conversion.

### Timeout / Default

Risk:
- silence after 12 minutes becomes hidden sovereign actuation.

Control:
- timeout produces narrowest preauthorized safe/service action or hold;
- explicit cost and rapid review;
- sovereign may recommend and explain, not actuate by default.

### Emergency Declaration / Scope

Risk:
- defining emergency and territory can suspend fields or expand civil/military/technical government.

Control:
- physical/service evidence;
- field-specific activation;
- automatic sunset;
- public grounds;
- affected/provincial review;
- no unilateral field suspension.
