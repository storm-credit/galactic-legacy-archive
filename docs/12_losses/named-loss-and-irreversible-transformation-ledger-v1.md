Last Reviewed: 2026-08-03

Open Risks: exact scene blocking and some secondary names remain open

## 1. Rule

Loss must not exist only to motivate Rian or prove darkness.
Every locked loss requires:

- an independent goal and prior presence;
- a decision or material function in the scene;
- consequences for people/institutions beyond grief;
- no later reversal, secret survival or perfect replacement.

The six opening core characters survive, but supporting people, ships, systems and records do not all survive intact.

## 2. Human Deaths — Locked

### L-H01 — 제나 아크 / Jena Ark

Code:
- ghost-caravan worker/technician focal, GA2.

Introduction:
- Episodes 139–142.

Role:
- current resident and repair technician aboard the reused NR72-061 parts caravan.
- argues that “salvage” is already someone’s home and labor.
- provides genuine service-collar history and distrusts both Helix certification and Nera’s rushed tests.

Death:
- Episode **150**.

Cause:
- forged/reconstructed legacy controller contributes to bay/cooling/actuator failure during a pressured test/rescue.
- multiple understandable errors: undocumented rebuild, copied certification, schedule pressure and inadequate cross-material testing.

Agency:
- Jena recognizes the failure first and manually isolates the caravan’s inhabited section, preventing larger casualties.
- she dies from exposure/injury during the isolation action, not passively as a test victim.

Consequences:
- open-standard accident gains a named worker history.
- current residents receive claims and standing.
- Nera/Vera/Serin/Helix/workers share investigation responsibility.
- Jena’s maintenance notes become a living test practice, not sacred final design.

No reversal:
- no AI copy, secret survival or future clone restores Jena.

Anti-fridging control:
- grief belongs primarily to her residents/coworkers/family and changes worker governance, not only Nera’s motivation.

### L-H02 — 토마 칼 / Toma Cal

Code:
- AR-DEF-001, acting Ardis local defense commander.

Introduction:
- Episodes 221–228.

Role:
- professional officer attempting to remain neutral until legitimate civilian command forms.
- repeatedly protects force readiness but delays aid to exposed districts.

Death:
- Episode **322**.

Cause:
- during the final Ardis siege, a command/communications split leaves one defense sector isolated.
- Toma transfers control to a civilian-bounded distributed command and remains physically at an Anchor Crown defense station to prevent an indiscriminate battery solution.

Agency:
- chooses to disable/redirect the battery and preserve habitats rather than preserve his own command post.

Consequences:
- local defense loses a unifying professional figure.
- joint trust inherits both his restraint doctrine and evidence of neutrality’s earlier harm.
- successor command is plural and less efficient.

No reversal.

Control:
- local actors, not Rian, decide his memorial and succession.

### L-H03 — 엘라 사빈 / Ella Savin

Code:
- MED-001, licensed medical officer aboard/alongside 회랑새.

Introduction:
- Episodes 108–115.

Role:
- Mia’s senior physician and a builder of sustainable frontier medical routes.
- often opposes heroic one-off rescue that destroys long-term care capacity.

Death:
- Episode **680**.

Location:
- Orpheus medical-transfer front during Window C failure.

Cause:
- a hospital/medical module cannot transit with all connected patients and staff after route geometry and towing assumptions collapse.

Agency:
- Ella orders transfer of mobile patients, records and younger staff, remains with nontransportable patients and local caregivers to keep life support/analgesia functioning until capture/destruction.
- she refuses a last-minute extraction that would consume capacity and abandon more patients.

Consequences:
- Mia inherits no magical authority, only duties, incomplete records and institutional grief.
- Neutral/medical minimum-service rules change.
- several patients/caregivers may survive as holdouts/captives; Ella’s choice is not a clean noble death tableau.

No reversal.

Control:
- do not frame her death as solely necessary for Mia’s licence or maturity.

### L-H04 — 아르도 레브 / Ardo Rev

Code:
- FC-001, veteran integrated-command admiral.

Introduction:
- GA5 Episodes 456–465.

Role:
- strongest credible advocate for unified command.
- repeatedly demonstrates that central coordination prevents friendly fire and wasted sacrifice.

Death:
- Episode **683**.

Location:
- Orpheus State/Civilian corridor separation battle.

Cause:
- hostile pressure and communication failure threaten both a State-function packet and civilian convoy.

Agency:
- Ardo accepts Rian’s mixed distributed plan, then independently holds one formation beyond planned withdrawal to preserve the timing of two separate commands.
- he deliberately transmits an imperfect standing intent rather than requesting Rian’s real-time control.

Consequences:
- his formation succeeds in enabling escape but is destroyed/crippled.
- unified-command advocates gain a martyr and legitimate critique of later fragmentation.
- the Common Fleet Charter retains central-coordination tools rather than treating his doctrine as disproved.

No reversal.

Control:
- Rian does not order a personal suicide; Ardo owns the decision.

### L-H05 — 린 오사 / Rin Osa

Code:
- dependent-region medical/route coordinator, GA6–7.

Introduction:
- Episodes 604–612 as coordinator for a region dependent on Orpheus/route-federation medicine and identity traffic.

Role:
- supports distributed evacuation but warns that routes appearing “secondary” carry invisible chronic-care dependencies.

Death:
- Episode **762**.

Cause:
- downstream medical interruption following Haren’s D4 hard denial; replacement route arrives too late.

Agency:
- organizes local rationing, treatment and evacuation during the delay and preserves a dependency/witness ledger.

Consequences:
- converts abstract downstream harm into a named institutional/person cost.
- her ledger exposes undercounted informal dependencies and becomes evidence in Haren’s order.
- affected-region representatives, not the core team, control its use.

No reversal.

Control:
- her death is not caused by villain sabotage added to force Haren’s guilt; it follows the locked dependency chain.

## 3. Permanent Injury/Role Transformation — Locked

### L-T01 — 베라 토른 / Vera Thorn

Event:
- Episode **684**, Orpheus Grey-route/회랑새 damage sequence.

Injury:
- severe left-arm and peripheral neural injury plus radiation/thermal exposure while isolating the ship’s common coolant trunk and enabling the drive-truss sacrifice.

Outcome:
- survives.
- cannot continue full-time high-risk EVA/heavy chief-engineer work.
- uses prosthetic/assistive systems but recurring pain/fatigue and fine-control limitations remain.

Agency:
- chooses the isolation sequence and trains others during the operation.

Later role:
- engineering safety examiner, instructor and board member at 회랑새 항로학교.
- she is not “fixed” into previous capacity.

Institutional consequence:
- maintenance procedures must be teachable/shared rather than dependent on her hands.
