## 1. Reason for Change

The older `first-100-act-map-v1.md` placed most of the White Dock operation inside Episodes 14–20 and began Episode 21 after temporary medical custody had already been granted.

The later and more detailed opening scene-card canon now ends Episode 20 with:

- 07 loaded for real deployment;
- the cell departing the Academy;
- White Dock’s outer door opening;
- Ern already inside the declared secure zone.

Therefore the physical White Dock operation must begin in Episode 21.

The older map also described Serin Or and Mia Lentz as later candidate additions. Later canon has both characters functioning in the opening cell before Episode 20:

- Serin owns bounded logging, provenance and authority records;
- Mia owns medical monitoring and stop authority.

Their Episodes 21–45 function is deepening autonomy, complicity and institutional power, not first introduction.

## 4. Role Consolidation

### Kara Ven

Kara Ven is the reader-facing opening field instructor and safety officer fulfilling the previously separate `I-001` field-instructor function.

This avoids adding another adult with the same authority and moral conflict.

Kara therefore carries:

- technical and field safety authority;
- selective-protection history;
- knowledge of Academy mission allocation;
- the capacity to help secure a public hearing while admitting that other cells previously paid for protected students.

### Serin Or

Serin is not introduced in B2. B2 reveals:

- the limits of her current credentials;
- prior routine reporting used by Security;
- why she cannot simply erase or publish records;
- her transition from delegated operator to conditional independent auditor.

### Mia Lentz

Mia is not introduced in C1. C1 later expands her from opening medical-stop role into casualty/consent/Black Ward authority.
