## 1. Author instruction / correction of completion semantics

The 1100/1100 source-bound Context layer is necessary but not sufficient for the author's requested meaning of **deep**.

A Context entry is not considered fully deep merely because the common six fields exist.

For writer-activation depth, each episode must also make it possible to answer, before prose drafting:

1. whose information/experience carries the episode;
2. who owns the decisive choice or stop;
3. what causal sequence turns the opening pressure into the end-state;
4. what human/relationship/emotional pressure is visible without inventing feelings;
5. what concrete reader payoff occurs this episode;
6. what unresolved question or changed condition pulls the reader into the next episode;
7. what narrative engine family is being used and how this episode differs from nearby episodes;
8. what Rian cannot decide, certify, own or override;
9. what exact mystery/payoff ceiling applies;
10. what fact remains unsupported and therefore must not be improvised.

This is a **workflow/QC activation layer**, not new story canon.

## 2. Non-negotiable source boundary

Writer-depth enrichment may:

- select and order already-approved episode-card material;
- derive an execution route from approved `goal/conflict/choice/action/outcome/cost/hook` blocks;
- recommend a POV/information route when the source does not canonically fix one;
- label an episode's narrative-engine family for anti-repetition QC;
- identify a relationship/emotional pressure carrier from an existing conflict, refusal, cost or current actor goal;
- phrase a reader-facing payoff/question from an approved result/hook.

Writer-depth enrichment may **not**:

- create a new event, scene fact, person, place, prop or number;
- create a new emotion or relationship state and present it as canon;
- promote a recommended POV into story canon;
- assign a technical/medical/legal/local/ship/archive/affected-party choice to Rian when another owner exists;
- move a locked clue/payoff earlier;
- restore a death, permanent injury, destroyed asset or retired configuration;
- add a new power, technology, authority, faction, ship, mecha, relic or master key;
- change the ending;
- use manuscript prose as authority for a fact absent from higher sources.

Unsupported story facts remain `UNRESOLVED FROM APPROVED SOURCES`.

## 4. Field semantics

### 4.1 `POV_INFORMATION_ROUTE`

Purpose:
- tell the writer whose current experience/information should carry the episode.

Priority:
1. explicit episode-card POV/information source;
2. explicit front-stage actor;
3. actor owning the decisive action/stop;
4. workflow recommendation using the existing close-third style and current focal actor.

A workflow recommendation is not canon and may be changed during authorized drafting if the same information ceiling and agency ownership are preserved.

### 4.2 `PRIMARY_DECISION_OWNER`

The person/institution already entitled by source/canon to own the choice that changes state.

If the exact owner is not explicit, use a bounded role description such as:
- `local technical authority`;
- `receiving medical authority`;
- `current captain/crew covenant`;
- `affected-party/custodian decision`.

Do **not** use Rian as a fallback merely because he is the protagonist.

### 4.3 `DECISION_BEAT`

The source-supported action/refusal/choice that makes the episode change state.

A pure discovery with no choice may use the approved operational stop, acceptance, release, refusal, transfer or record action that converts information into consequence.

### 4.4 `SCENE_CAUSAL_CHAIN`

A compact execution ordering, normally:

`current pressure → evidence/obstacle → owned choice/refusal → visible state change → cost → next pressure`

This is not a required six-scene formula. It is a causality check.

### 4.5 `HUMAN_PRESSURE_CARRIER`

An existing person/community/crew/patient/worker/claimant/custodian whose current interest prevents the episode becoming system exposition.

No named exemplar may be invented to fill this field.

### 4.6 `RELATIONSHIP_EMOTIONAL_DELTA`

Only source-supported movement is allowed.

Valid values include:
- explicit trust/friction/refusal/obligation change already in source;
- an institutional relationship change that affects current people;
- `NONE — relationship/emotional delta is not load-bearing this episode`.

Do not manufacture internal emotion merely to make the pack feel literary.

### 4.7 `READER_PAYOFF_THIS_EP`

The thing the reader gets **now**, not only a future promise:
- a physical success/failure;
- a reveal within the allowed information ceiling;
- a named choice/refusal;
- a collection/access/status change;
- a tactical/technical solution;
- a consequence becoming visible;
- a relationship/authority change.

A pure hook with no current payoff is Depth-B unless the source explicitly designs the episode as a bridge and the prior/next payoff window is recorded.

### 4.8 `RETENTION_QUESTION_OR_CHANGED_CONDITION`

Prefer an approved final hook or re-entry state. It must create a concrete next pressure, not a generic “what happens next?”.

E1100 uses endpoint closure rather than an E1101 hook.

### 4.9 `NARRATIVE_ENGINE_FAMILY`

Workflow/QC label only. Allowed families:

- `TACTICAL-COMBAT`
- `TECHNICAL-REPAIR/TEST`
- `RESCUE/SERVICE`
- `ROUTE/LOGISTICS`
- `CLAIM/OWNERSHIP/CUSTODY`
- `MEDICAL/CARE/CONSENT`
- `RECORD/PROVENANCE/MYSTERY`
- `LEGAL/GOVERNANCE/ACCOUNTABILITY`
- `NEGOTIATION/COALITION`
- `COLLECTION/ACCESS/TRANSFER`
- `RELATIONSHIP/TEAM`
- `INVESTIGATION/EVIDENCE`
- `TRANSITION/AFTERMATH`
- `ENDING/HANDOFF`

Multiple source elements may exist, but one family must describe the **dominant episode engine**.

### 4.10 `ENGINE_DIFFERENTIATOR`

State what makes this episode materially different from adjacent episodes, using approved dimensions:

- different decision owner;
- different physical carrier;
- different cost class;
- different type of refusal;
- central solution succeeds here but fails elsewhere;
- local solution has a real cost;
- Rian is absent/not owner;
- reveal vs action vs aftermath;
- acquisition vs use vs loss vs return.

If three or more nearby episodes share the same engine family, differentiator quality becomes a mandatory red-team check.

## 5. Depth grades

### Depth-A — WRITER-ACTIVATED DEEP

All conditions:

- common six-field Context is ready;
- a valid POV/information route exists, explicit or clearly marked workflow recommendation;
- a source-supported decision beat/owner exists;
- causal chain is executable;
- a human pressure carrier exists or the episode is a deliberate non-human/system endpoint with explicit justification;
- current reader payoff exists;
- next-pressure/endpoint condition exists;
- engine family and differentiator are identifiable;
- authority and information ceiling are guarded;
- no unsupported story fact is required;
- `NEW_CANON_REQUIRED: NO`.

### Depth-B — ACTIVATION ENRICHMENT REQUIRED

The story/card is sound but at least one writer-execution dimension is too implicit, for example:

- POV route unclear;
- decision owner/beat only inferable weakly;
- current payoff too diffuse;
- human pressure carrier submerged under procedure;
- adjacent engine differentiation weak;
- relationship/institution delta not visible enough for scene execution.

Depth-B must be repaired by **source-bound Context overlay**, not by redesigning the episode.

### Depth-C — DESIGN/CANON BLOCKER

A writer-ready episode would require a new story fact, authority transfer, missing event, contradictory irreversible state, or unresolvable payoff timing.

Use:

`NEW_CANON_REQUIRED: YES-STOP`

Do not repair C automatically.

## 7. Red-team failure paths

Before completion, explicitly test:

1. **False depth by formatting** — many fields but all repeat the same sentence.
2. **POV invention** — workflow recommendation presented as canon.
3. **Rian gravity** — unclear owner silently defaults to protagonist.
4. **Emotion fabrication** — unsupported feelings added to fill a delta field.
5. **Exposition payoff fraud** — “reader learns a rule” used as payoff when no current consequence changes.
6. **Hook-only episodes** — episode spends its entire value promising the next one.
7. **Engine monoculture** — repeated future-answer correction or institutional rejection loop.
8. **High-watch paperwork drift** — claim/Seed/legal terms displace people/assets/places.
9. **Loss reset** — later Context forgets death, injury, destroyed ship or retired configuration.
10. **Ending over-closure** — E1096–1100 turns plural/incomplete endpoint into a solved master history.

A final PASS requires no S0/S1 finding in these paths.
