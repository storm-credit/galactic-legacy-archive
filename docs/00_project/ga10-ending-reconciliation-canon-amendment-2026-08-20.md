## 0. Why This Amendment Exists

`ENDING-S1-01` identified a hierarchy conflict.

Higher-authority structure already requires:

- E1076–1095 = main-story transition/reconstruction through `CY 748-07-31`;
- E1096–1100 = CY751 epilogue outcomes;
- E1083–1089 = return/distribution of iconic people, 07, first ship, records and institutions;
- E1090–1095 = plural history/accountability and proof that institutions can act without Rian;
- final ordinary/unlabeled person and no destiny-confirming UI.

Older lower detailed cards instead continued ledger/crisis material through E1100 and ended on CY748-01, contradicting both [[master-series-chronology-v1]] and [[ga10-episodes-1001-1100-act-map-v1]].

The author approved the recommended reconciliation package and then instructed the project to continue after a reference/anti-similarity preflight. PR #195 completed that preflight without finding a structural originality blocker.

This amendment therefore fixes **allocation and open endpoints**, not the already-set core theme.
