## 1. Truthful Execution Model

The project uses a **fixed specialist-role orchestra**.

What this means:

- specialist codes, domains, questions and vetoes remain stable across sessions;
- every major document identifies primary and reviewing specialists;
- the PM orchestrator switches into those specialist roles and performs separate review passes;
- continuity, ethics, reader-memory and red-team passes challenge the primary draft;
- the final integrated result is stored in GitHub.

What this does **not** mean:

- the repository does not prove that every code was a separate autonomous AI process;
- an `Owner Agents` header is not evidence of an independent model invocation;
- no claim may be made that a human admiral, engineer, economist, lawyer or scientist externally certified the work unless a separate review record exists.

Official wording:

> **고정 전문역할 기반 PM 오케스트라**

Prohibited wording without separate evidence:

> “모든 전문 에이전트가 각각 독립 실행·독립 승인했다.”

## 10. Fixed Execution Sequence

Every foundational document uses this sequence:

1. primary specialist role drafts the domain logic;
2. adjacent specialists test dependencies;
3. X04 checks chronology/state continuity;
4. X01 checks causal/numerical logic;
5. X03 checks agency/ethics when people or communities are affected;
6. A16 attacks easy answers and author favoritism;
7. X02/N07 check reader load and repetition;
8. O01 resolves source precedence;
9. O02 assigns gate status;
10. A00 integrates and records the result.

A future session may use separate AI processes, but the execution record must say so explicitly. Silence means role-based passes by the orchestrator.
