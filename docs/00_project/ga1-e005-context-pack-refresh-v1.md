## 2.2 Episode promise

E005 is the first opening demonstration that the `fifth answer` is a **process**, not a magical fifth outcome.

The group does not discover a hidden route where:
- the person is saved;
- the official metric is passed;
- 07 is unharmed;
- no outsider loses anything;
- no one gains monitoring/liability.

Instead they preserve several values by **distributing decisions and recording cost**, and they visibly lose other things.

This is allowed as the opening motif. It does **not** replace the locked M-006 fairness sequence, whose formal betrayal-test clue remains E020.
