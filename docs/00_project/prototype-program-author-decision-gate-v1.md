# 1. Decision P-A — What is the numbered program structurally?

## A1 — one manufacturer’s twenty-machine secret project

Pros:
- easy mythology.

Cons:
- too neat;
- creates sibling-machine collection logic;
- weak fit with 07’s mixed-contractor/service ancestry;
- makes later factions derivative of one source.

Verdict:
- **REJECT.**

## A2 — one Imperial military procurement series

Pros:
- clean administrative history.

Cons:
- service-first 07 becomes awkward;
- early Era-1 institutions become too mature/centralized;
- failure/partial transfer ecology becomes too linear.

Verdict:
- **REJECT AS SOLE MODEL.**

## A3 — distributed experimental authorization umbrella

Shape:
- a broader authority/funding/test framework assigns numbered experimental slots;
- multiple contractors, regions, service institutions and later military evaluators participate;
- a slot number identifies experimental authorization/history more reliably than manufacturer/model family;
- some slots never become full frames;
- subsystems/processes can transfer across slots.

Pros:
- best fit with mixed industrial history;
- failure/cancellation becomes natural;
- modern lineages can recombine ancestry;
- AUXILIA can remain one service/intervention workstream rather than the whole program.

Verdict:
- **RECOMMENDED.**

# 2. Decision P-B — Why did the program exist?

Four historical models were tested.

## B1 — relay expansion only

Good root:
- incompatible habitats/yards/service systems create rescue/repair crisis.

Insufficient for:
- acceleration/EW/AI/formation/breach experiments.

## B2 — contractor fragmentation only

Good structural explanation:
- incompatible standards, service rights, diagnostics and licensing.

Insufficient as dramatic/historical pressure by itself.

## B3 — war/node rebellion only

Explains:
- contested rescue;
- breach;
- EW;
- formation;
- high-performance interfaces.

But as sole origin it turns the program into a weapons project and conflicts with 07 service-first history.

## B4 — layered crisis history

Recommended chain:

```text
relay/habitat expansion pressure
→ contractor/regional standard fragmentation
→ numbered competitive experimental authorization
→ node rebellion/security escalation militarizes part of the work
→ reconstruction selects usable lessons
→ later Era-2 Imperial standardization
```

This preserves:
- 07 as service/intervention first;
- civilian/industrial origin;
- later military modification;
- failure and partial-success inheritance;
- later centralized standardization as reaction to a messy earlier era.

### Recommendation

> **P-B4 / H-E Layered Crisis Program.**

### Author decision status

`HOLD — explicit approval required before canon history propagation.`
