# GA5 B05-02 Continuity Clarification 001 — Charter v0.1 Term

Status: CANONICAL DETAIL OVERRIDE
Last Reviewed: 2026-08-04

## Issue

E480 enacts Charter v0.1 for fourteen days, ending CY 744-05-04. E481–500 continue to use Charter institutions and accepted ships. No automatic extension is permitted.

## Canonical Resolution

At the E487 convoy closeout on CY 744-05-03, current signatories conduct the required review and explicitly renew Charter v0.1 for **28 days, CY 744-05-04 through 05-31**.

Renewal scope:
- Vesper/Talren named defense and service lanes;
- repair/pay convoy protection;
- maintenance allocation support;
- rescue and temporary-protection missions;
- no Outer Front offensive, territorial seizure or claimant enforcement;
- no permanent fleet, admiralty, common root key or automatic next renewal.

Renewal participation:
- 15 combat and 14 support ships remain initial signatories;
- later conditionally connected ships are added by ship/mission agreement, not retroactive ownership;
- by E494 the connected network is 21 combat and 18 support, with only 14 combat R2+.

This clarification supersedes any reading that Charter v0.1 auto-renews or silently expires while its institutions continue.
