## Correction

Old provisional name:
- `리아 메스 / Ria Mess`.

Final canonical name:
- `메사 린 / Messa Rin`.

Code:
- FC-005.

Role:
- crew pay/family/surrender representative and later Common Fleet Charter officer.

Everyday call:
- `메사`.

Voice anchor:
- insists crews are citizens, families and contract parties rather than hull components.
- turns abstract victory into unpaid wages, survivor benefits, surrender terms and who informs families.

Typical syntax:
- “함선은 남았습니다. 승무원 절반이 계약 밖에 남았어요.”
- “철수권을 지우면 충성은 늘지 않습니다. 탈영 기록만 늘죠.”

Reason:
- removes the `리안 / 리보 / 리아` Korean phonetic collision in fleet-heavy scenes.

Propagation:
- all future scene cards and prose use `메사 린`.
- existing project-code references remain FC-005.
