# Canonical Haren Sanction Timeline Errata 004

Status: CANON CORRECTION — SANCTION CHRONOLOGY
Owner Agents: O01 Canon / O02 Gates / P03 Law / X04 Continuity
Last Reviewed: 2026-08-05
Applies To: GA8–GA10 project-control and carryover shorthand

## 1. Correction

Some later GA8–GA10 state files use the shorthand:
- `Haren serving sentence`;
- `Haren remains under sentence`.

Canonical interpretation:
- this shorthand means the Haren judgment and sanction regime remain current;
- it does not mean the fourteen-month liberty-restriction term continues indefinitely through CY 747–748.

## 2. Exact Final Chronology

Judgment:
- E783 / CY 745-08.

Liberty sanction:
- total 14 months liberty restriction/inquiry detention;
- credited supervised restriction from executive-authority suspension;
- completed before the late GA9/GA10 dates.

Continuing sanctions after liberty-term completion:
- twelve-year ban on executive route command;
- permanent ban on unilateral high-level denial/master-key authority;
- fifteen-year personal reparations contribution;
- no automatic office in any federation, route body, Archive or transition structure;
- any later nonexecutive role requires ordinary current mandate and explicit affected/victim-region approval.

## 3. Findings Not Changed

Unchanged:
- guilty of abuse of emergency route authority with foreseeable downstream harm;
- offense centered on 48-hour continuation without stronger residual protection or partial transition;
- not intentional killing;
- not sole cause of Lin or outer deaths;
- D4 had real material core benefit;
- Haren is not the sole historical Blood Admiral;
- Lin Osa’s evidence reached Haren before signature;
- Lin remains a named current actor and permanent victim;
- Blood Admiral remains a five-layer composite history.

## 4. Authority Consequence

Completion of liberty restriction does not:
- restore executive route command;
- grant master-key authority;
- grant federation or Archive office;
- transfer Haren authority to Rian, Aven or another actor;
- erase victim/affected-region standing;
- remove reparations obligations.

## 5. Canonical Final State at E1100

Haren:
- liberty-restriction term completed;
- twelve-year executive route-command ban active;
- permanent unilateral denial/master-key ban active;
- fifteen-year reparations contribution active;
- no automatic office;
- possible later nonexecutive civic role only through ordinary mandate and explicit affected-region approval.
