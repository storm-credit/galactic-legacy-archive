## 1. Error

[[ga2-first-ship-crew-bible-v1]] listed departmental legal minimums whose visible sum was 38 while the locked ship-wide legal minimum was 42.

The ship bible independently locks:
- legal minimum transit crew: **42**.

The total remains 42. The department table is corrected below.

## 3. Sustainable Target

The sustainable mixed-mission target remains 62–76, with the crew bible's 72-person planning target retained.

## 4. Opening Interpretation

At E101:
- 34–39 people possess useful current training or prior ship experience;
- not all are willing, medically available or currently licensed;
- cadets/contract workers count partially;
- the vessel therefore remains below legal departure minimum until E114–121 staffing and waivers are complete.

## 5. Downstream Effects

- no departure with only the visible 38-role sum;
- four additional watch/coverage positions must be staffed or explicitly waived;
- fatigue and double-shift cost remain material;
- payroll, bunks, food and medical capacity use the actual 42-person minimum;
- a later casualty may invalidate certification even when the hull remains functional.

## 6. Ruling

> `42` is the canonical legal minimum for NR72-118.

This errata overrides the arithmetic table in the earlier crew bible without changing its authority separation or sustainable crew target.
