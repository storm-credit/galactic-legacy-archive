## 1. Calendar Standard

Official calendar:
- `연속력 / Continuity Year (CY)`.

Series opening:
- `CY 742-03-17`, Episode 1.

Main ending:
- `CY 748-07-31`, Episode 1095.

Epilogue range:
- `CY 751-08-03`, Episodes 1096–1100, approximately three years after the main transfer war.

Main-story elapsed time:
- 6 years, 4 months, 15 days.

Epilogue elapsed time from opening:
- 9 years, 4 months, 17 days.

Rule:
- all travel, recovery, training, promotion, pregnancy/family, ship repair, institutional reform and political legitimacy must fit this elapsed time.
- a character cannot acquire a professional licence merely because many episodes passed; calendar time and supervised hours are required.

## 7. Recovery and Resource Time Rules

- frame pilot neural recovery after severe overload: days to weeks; repeated overload accumulates.
- ship major structural/reactor/cooling repair: weeks to months at capable yard.
- node-spine structural restoration: months to years; emergency windows can be restored in days only through existing hardware and high risk.
- fleet missile/interceptor restock: days locally if stock exists, weeks/months if strategic production/route disrupted.
- training a licensed ship engineer/navigator/medical officer: years; cadets operate under supervised emergency warrants until qualified.
- political charters may be signed quickly but require months of enforcement, payroll, courts, operators and public acceptance.
- population relocation at million scale: weeks/months, not one departure scene.
- identity restoration: immediate provisional status possible; final legal/benefit/relationship consequences take months or years.

## 8. Chronology Red Flags

Mandatory check before episode cards:

1. character age on scene date.
2. travel time and message arrival.
3. ship/node repair elapsed time.
4. licence and supervised-hour eligibility.
5. physical/neural injury recovery.
6. pregnancy/child age/family separation where used.
7. office term, election, inquiry and appeal duration.
8. ammunition, food, medicine, money and crew replacement.
9. whether an institution has staff and local enforcement, not only a signed charter.
10. whether a later historical record could plausibly compress the event.
