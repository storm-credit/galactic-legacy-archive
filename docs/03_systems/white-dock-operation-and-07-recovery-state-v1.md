## 3. White Dock Tactical Geometry

### WD-A — Repair Arm A

- intact at incident start.
- contains functional tugs and exterior service access.
- becomes principal civilian evacuation and local-worker response route.
- Juno Hess’s cell protects its approach and later supports relay defense.

### WD-B — Repair Arm B

- Ern’s entry route through a maintenance exterior and obsolete worker lock.
- narrow structural members and cable arrays favor Ern’s anchor-based frame.
- an old pressure transfer gives access toward WD-C but is absent from the Academy’s simplified training map.

### WD-C — Central Customs/Archive

- pressure-separated archive stacks, customs desks, identity terminals and physical authentication media.
- full-size frames cannot enter inner archive corridors.
- exterior walls and service collars can be held, cut or disabled by frames.
- destruction here damages both evidence and ordinary traffic/identity services.

### WD-D — Worker Habitat and Clinic

- densely occupied and unsuited to heavy weapons.
- receives decompression casualties.
- local staff become independent rescuers rather than passive objectives.

### WD-E — Cargo Spines

- long pressure warehouses with movable containers, cranes and multiple locks.
- radical escort enters through East Cargo Spine using a forged contractor clearance.
- chain reaction risk from propellant cells, medical gas and old insulation.

### WD-F — Relay Interface Annex

- traffic and authentication interface, not the main relay core.
- loss would isolate evidence, neutral distress calls and ship movements.
- Juno’s cell holds this area rather than following Rian.

### WD-G — Emergency Shelter and School Berth

- Academy transport, maintenance crew and 07 support.
- Neutral rescue approach is visible from exterior sensors but remains outside local weapons envelope until distress conditions are met.

Travel/action constraints:
- full-size frame exterior transit across the dock: 1–4 minutes at safe combat maneuver, but braking, structure and fire arcs matter.
- suited human team WD-G→WD-C through official interior route: 7–14 minutes without lockdown, 18–35 during pressure separation.
- WD-B maintenance route→WD-C service collar: 4–9 minutes for trained worker/operative, not known to all Academy staff.
- Neutral anchorage→dock: 12–25 minutes after launch/clearance.

## 6. Official and Hidden Objectives

### Official Academy objective

1. protect WD-F relay interface.
2. secure WD-C archive.
3. identify/capture intruders.
4. keep cadets within assigned sectors.

### Hidden Academy evaluation

The cell is judged on whether it:

- obeys an order that leaves a protected target/worker sector waiting;
- identifies one teammate/unauthorized action as the cause of deviation;
- or refuses collectively and accepts linked equipment/community penalties.

The evaluation does not create the attack, but it exploits real danger and makes help conditional.

### Rian cell objective

- preserve current people and authenticated evidence;
- stop destructive denial;
- prevent one faction taking all records/07 control;
- survive without pretending 07 can win a normal military duel.

### Ern objective

- extract usable authenticated custody records;
- locate missing-person/transfer evidence;
- prevent radical destruction when possible;
- leave before Imperial/Academy forces close the route.

### Radical objective

- extract what can be taken;
- destroy the remaining archive if capture is imminent;
- create proof of Imperial/Academy atrocity through the response.

### White Dock local objective

- keep pressure, clinic, worker routes and ordinary traffic alive.
- local workers do not automatically support Academy, Ern or radicals.

### Neutral objective

- preserve patients, evidence chain and rescue neutrality.
- refuse permanent asylum, military contraband and unbounded liability.

## 7. Operation Timeline

### 10:20–10:45 — Arrival and discrepancy

- school transport docks at WD-G.
- Serin identifies live route/manifest differences from the briefing package.
- Nera sees 07’s mission package lacks the promised external cooling cart.
- Haren finds the assigned priorities leave a worker sector below response threshold.
- Ern’s signature appears inside WD-B before official hostile declaration.

### 10:45–11:10 — First frame contact

- Rian intercepts Ern around WD-B exterior.
- both disable access/mobility rather than seek cockpit kill.
- Rian’s future expectation misreads one worker-built anchor route.
- Ern’s restraint exposes the retrieval support frame/tug.
- radical breachers enter WD-E East.

### 11:10–11:40 — Split threat

- Academy orders Rian to remain on WD-C approach and Juno to hold WD-F.
- radical charge/power action separates WD-B/C/E pressure segments.
- 147 people become trapped at peak.
- Mia and local clinic staff establish triage/evacuation priorities.
- Haren proposes a split plan requiring Juno’s cell and local tug crews to act independently.

### 11:40–12:20 — Archive and pressure rescue

- Nera uses old service access to operate a pressure collar but exposes unauthorized interface use.
- Serin creates authenticated external hashes without copying all private contents.
- Rian uses 07’s manipulator/tether to hold a damaged archive/worker connector.
- Ern extracts only a bounded subset and discovers the personal target chain is incomplete.
- Academy Security attempts to close on Ern and deprioritizes trapped cargo workers.

### 12:20–13:15 — Denial climax

- radical escort prepares destructive denial in WD-C/WD-E.
- Rian and Ern temporarily coordinate around a physical objective, not through alliance or shared command.
- Juno’s cell preserves WD-F and a civilian route instead of following Rian.
- Nera orders 07’s damaged radiator wing released as sacrificial heat/structure protection.
- Rian uses the remaining 07 envelope to prevent a larger pressure/charge cascade.
- 07 enters Red thermal/joint state and shuts down under Kara/Mia/Nera stop.

### 13:15–14:20 — Separation and rescue jurisdiction

- Ern withdraws with partial records and at least one injured/captured operative left behind.
- radical cell loses one operative and does not destroy the whole archive.
- White Dock local staff restore partial pressure.
- Neutral rescue launches enter after distress and medical conflict-of-interest conditions are documented.
- 07 core is isolated as pilot/medical/evidence material.
- Academy, Helix, Imperial and Neutral claims prevent immediate reunification of frame components.

## 13. Legal and Medical Custody After Incident

Neutral emergency custody:
- 24–72 hours for Rian and other injured students under decompression/neural-risk authority.
- protects patients from immediate separation or coercive interrogation.
- does not erase Academy sentence, Security inquiry or Helix property claims.

Conditional return terms:

1. no secret separation of core cell before initial testimony.
2. independent medical presence.
3. evidence preservation and mirrored hashes.
4. written notice of charges/transfer.
5. White Dock local witnesses and Neutral auditor may attend preliminary inquiry.
6. 07/core/components remain sealed under their separate claims.

Why actors accept:
- Academy regains students/custody without openly violating Neutral rescue law.
- Neutral avoids indefinite asylum/liability.
- Imperial preserves evidence and anomaly access.
- Helix preserves component claims.
- students gain time, witnesses and a public procedure rather than freedom.
