## 1. Crisis Trigger

The supply crisis is not a cartoon punishment switch.

It results from several simultaneous events:

1. Black Ward treatment/identity data and ORA-3 compatibility become contested evidence.
2. Nera and Ward staff modify/test a provisional generic adapter without Helix’s full exclusive process.
3. Academy invoices and insurance exposure are already delinquent.
4. Helix Finance/Security orders a liability hold on unverified use and future shipments.
5. Helix Manufacturing/Frontier staff oppose a total cutoff but cannot release all controlled stock without signatures/payment.
6. Academy Security prepares patient/data transfers that would restore one clear responsible owner but remove local/patient control.

The result is:
- new shipments suspended;
- current stocks audited/sealed;
- emergency release offered under exclusive Helix custody/data/contract terms;
- some clinicians and Helix staff continue trying to keep patients alive.

## 4. Full-Protocol Priority Model

Mia and licensed clinicians use current clinical criteria:

1. immediate reversibility and time-to-harm.
2. whether full protocol uniquely changes outcome.
3. transportability and available destination.
4. adapter compatibility.
5. patient refusal/preferences.
6. effect on other patients through staff/bed/resource use.

Explicitly excluded:
- future military/hero value.
- faction or citizenship.
- whether a patient’s evidence helps Rian/Haren.
- punishment or cooperation.

Required disputed allocation:
- a full proprietary cycle is assigned to Leta or another acute reversible patient rather than Orvan.
- Orvan receives a choice among high-risk adapter, delayed treatment with tighter monitoring, or Neutral transfer slot.
- Haren disagrees because Orvan is personally/community significant and his route/identity evidence matters.
- Mia’s decision is clinically defensible but not morally cost-free.

## 5. Helix Emergency Offer

Helix can resume and release enough full-protocol stock to stabilize all 23 time-critical patients if the following are accepted:

- Helix-controlled clinical command/audit for the crisis.
- full access to modified-interface and outcome data.
- transfer of ORA-3 research/compatibility rights into a protected consortium program.
- separate contracts/identity verification for patients.
- suspension of unapproved adapter use.
- Academy debt restructuring and future treatment priority rights.

Genuine benefits:
- lower immediate failure risk.
- known consumables and trained technical support.
- stronger liability and quality-control chain.

Harms/limits:
- treatment/data lock-in.
- identity-closed patients can be reclassified/transferred.
- patient refusal and local oversight weakened.
- Nera/Ward worker knowledge absorbed without equal governance.

No character can truthfully claim the Helix offer is medically worthless.

Capacity limit:

Legal conditions:

- evidence/data separation.
