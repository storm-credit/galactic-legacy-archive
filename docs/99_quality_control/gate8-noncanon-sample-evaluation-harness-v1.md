## 2. Scoring Scale

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

- 5 — clear without outside notes; preserves nuance and pace.
- 4 — understandable with minor density/friction.
- 3 — core idea visible but likely reread/confusion.
- 2 — substantial explanation or memory burden.
- 1 — fails the intended effect.

Internal pass threshold:
- no dimension below 3.
- overall average ≥4.0.
- primary test dimensions ≥4.
- no canon/continuity violation.

Human pass remains separate and uses comprehension/identification rather than these author scores.

---

## 3. Shared Dimensions

1. **Immediate objective** — can the reader state what must be done now?
2. **Physical/current state** — are location, body, machine, people or resource states visible?
3. **Constraint clarity** — is the impossible tradeoff caused by known limits rather than author choice?
4. **Causal progression** — does each action alter the next state?
5. **Character authority separation** — do non-Rian actors own decisions?
6. **Terminology load** — can meaning be followed without memorizing all terms?
7. **Sentence/mobile rhythm** — medium sentence baseline, short impact lines, limited choppiness.
8. **Emotional consequence** — does the cost belong to people/relationships rather than tables only?
9. **Theme without lecture** — is the political/ethical idea demonstrated physically?
10. **End-state/hook** — does the sequence change the story and leave a specific next problem?

Special dimensions:

- Sample A: three-dimensional spatial tracking.
- Sample B: centralization benefit and physical distribution cost.
- Sample C: AI ontology/source/consent distinction.
- Sample D: five-package differentiation and number retention.

## 5. Cross-Sample Findings

### POV

PASS CANDIDATE:
- Rian close third allows limited sensor/knowledge while preserving other actors’ choices through communication and physical results.
- no omniscient jump is required in the four samples.

Risk:
- scenes where Rian is not physically present must use reports, calls, later reconstruction or consciously change viewpoint only if the final series POV policy later permits it.

### Sentence style

PASS CANDIDATE:
- medium sentences carry cause and geometry.
- short sentences mark decisions and impact rather than dominate every paragraph.

Risk:
- frequent one-line paragraphs remain common in mobile style. Human readers should flag whether they feel cinematic or artificially chopped.

### Technical explanation

PASS CANDIDATE:
- technical terms normally appear with a physical function or consequence.

Risk:
- White Dock and central-key samples each introduce multiple infrastructure terms. Canonical prose should reuse stable nouns and avoid synonyms.

### Institutional explanation

PASS CANDIDATE:
- authority is shown through doors, drugs, parts, keys, routes, payroll and ships.
- no sample relies only on abstract law.

Risk:
- Sample D is still a deliberately compressed explanation test, not the intended final episode sequence. Canon scene cards already distribute it across E73–83.

### Protagonist monopoly

PASS:
- Rian makes central decisions but cannot own technical, medical, record, logistics or patient authority.
- other actors change outcomes and sometimes overrule him.

### Centralization balance

PASS CANDIDATE:
- central command has immediate measurable benefits.
- distribution causes unreversed loss.
- the samples do not claim plural control always minimizes casualties.

### Collection/reward

PASS:
- action rewards are not raw output:
  - White Dock preserves people/evidence while losing component.
  - central key is acquired and then relinquished.
  - ORA gives process proof while losing a clue.
  - charter grants operating rights while creating debt/ship obligation.

## 7. Required Human Test Packet

Use blind participants who have not read the full setting bible.

Minimum:
- 5 readers for initial diagnostic.
- preferably 10–20 for more stable signal.
- at least half reading on a phone-sized display.

Collect:
- comprehension answers without allowing reread first.
- confusing names/terms/locations.
- where they stopped or skimmed.
- emotional/continuation interest 1–5.
- character/authority identification.
- centralization/consent interpretation in their own words.

Target:
- sample-specific thresholds listed in each file.
- overall continuation interest ≥3.5/5.
- no recurring false inference in more than 30% of readers.
