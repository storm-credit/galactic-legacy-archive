## 3. S1 Major Risks and Controls

### S1-01. The protagonists may colonize or politically own Ardis

Attack:
- the heroes arrive with scarce cargo, a famous ship, 07 and route knowledge.
- a weaker design would let local factions wait for their solution.

Controls verified:
- E211 begins with Toma/local traffic workers before protagonist disembarkation;
- local queue and medical priorities override hero status;
- all technical stops and branch sequencing require current local authority;
- 회랑새 receives paid conditional work, not command or property;
- the 30-day trial has six constituencies and expires;
- no city, district, worker body or arrival group becomes protagonist-owned.

Verdict:
- PASS.

### S1-02. Toma Cal may become a local proxy for Rian

Attack:
- one named local coordinator can become a convenient mouthpiece.

Controls verified:
- Toma denies arrival priority;
- he allocates windows with costs Rian did not choose;
- his redesign accepts less total data than Rian/central packages prefer;
- local maintainer corrects both remote and Rian models;
- Toma supports a temporary institution that can reject or replace external contributors;
- his future E322 loss is not used to remove current agency.

Required later control:
- Toma must make at least one costly or wrong decision before E260 and remain accountable to multiple local constituencies.

Verdict:
- PASS WITH CONTINUING CONDITION.

### S1-03. The 30-day trial may secretly function as a new permanent government

Attack:
- shared board, constituency seats, stop rights and money could become de facto sovereignty.

Controls verified:
- scope is one bounded cross-zone service chain and related current work;
- 30-day expiry and weekly reports;
- mandates are revocable;
- no title, sovereignty, permanent keys or automatic extension;
- district/zone data and authority remain local;
- centralized 90-day package remains available;
- E236 must show the trial failing to decide issues outside its mandate.

Verdict:
- PASS.

### S1-04. Distributed governance is presented as automatically morally superior

Attack:
- the project’s late-series theme could bias every outcome toward plural authority.

Controls verified:
- first trial loses/downgrades 14–18 t;
- distribution remains unequal;
- decision and update labor is high;
- data boundaries reduce prediction and speed;
- Foundry/Anchor services are displaced;
- centralized package is faster/better for payroll, certification and integrated service;
- trial continues only as measured comparison, not ideological victory.

Required later control:
- at least one resident/worker/service user must rationally prefer the centralized package and gain a real benefit from it.

Verdict:
- PASS WITH CONTINUING CONDITION.

### S1-05. The second successful transfer may erase the first failure

Controls verified:

- second movement uses reduced load, more labor and repaired branch;
