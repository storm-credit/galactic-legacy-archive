## 2. S1 Major Risks

### S1-01. Option 3 may be author-rigged as the obvious moral optimum

Problem:
- mixed distribution currently saves more civilians than State priority while preserving more institutions than civilian priority.

Required controls:
1. ranges overlap and downstream forecasts remain genuinely uncertain.
2. Option 3 loses functions/ships/regions that Option 1 preserves.
3. named downstream deaths and military defeats follow fragmentation.
4. one credible analysis after the event argues Option 1 may have produced lower total deaths.
5. some sympathetic survivors/state workers condemn P-001 through the ending.
6. P-001 cannot claim “the right answer,” only the choice he made under stated values and uncertainty.

Red line:
- do not reveal later that Option 3 was secretly objectively best through Archive or author narration.

### S1-02. State group vs civilian group can still simplify into institution vs people

Controls:
- State group visibly contains hospital patients, families, civil workers and dependents.
- civilian group contains militia, strategic workers, merchants and politically organized actors.
- destroyed State functions produce later human suffering, not abstract statistics.
- civilian convoy councils can exclude, panic, hoard or prioritize their own members.
- mixed manifests use current medical/route/service criteria, not category labels alone.

### S1-03. Population/casualty scale can become manipulative or numbing

Controls:
- foreground 5–8 recurring ships, communities and institutions.
- only 2–4 numbers/clocks active in any scene sequence.
- use ranges and uncertainty rather than false exactness.
- named loss chains connect structural decisions to bodies, families, work and later regions.
- avoid repeated anonymous destruction montages.

### S1-04. P-001 may remain sole moral and operational decider

Required controls:
- he recuses from sole manifest authority.
- civilian, State, node and Neutral actors have real veto/conditions.
- separate commanders own A/B/C/Grey/holdout fronts.
- at least two major successful decisions and one damaging refusal/failure occur without him.
- one allocation he opposes is adopted and later proves beneficial.
- he cannot monitor all outcomes during 6C.

### S1-05. Distributed plan can become a “fifth answer” cheat

Controls:
- every component comes from GA1–5: charter, first ship/fleet, route federation, open standard, plural records, local custody and medical rights.
- no new super-key, AI or route appears.
- planning time is insufficient and implementation fails partially.
- 25–45 ships/support assets and central capacity are lost.
- 450,000–850,000 people remain killed/captured/missing/severe risk in the working range.
- later wars/services pay the cost.

### S1-06. Central Continuity override could be straw-manned as evil coercion

Controls:
- full override produces the best immediate traffic discipline in at least one model/test.
- it catches spoofing and prevents one collision/attack.
- reasonable State, medical, civilian and crew actors support it.
- narrow synchronization is actually used and saves lives.
- refusal rests on authority/rights/future-regime risk, not operational inferiority.
- P-001’s partial use creates dependence/data/political consequences.

### S1-07. The original Orpheus reveal may absolve P-001

Controls:
- no perfect alternative existed, but he knew meaningful uncertainty.
- he chose concentration, accepted civilian restrictions, hid uncertainty and overestimated later rescue.
- systemic manipulation contextualizes rather than removes command responsibility.
- original survivors need not forgive him.
- current better process does not retroactively compensate original dead.

### S1-08. Conversely, P-001 may absorb all blame and erase institutions

Controls:
- node operators, State commanders, information/security offices, Neutral routes and civilian formations made independent choices.
- records assign responsibility by scope and evidence.
- no single “P-001 killed X million” verdict.
- reforms target systems as well as commander conduct.

### S1-09. Holdouts can disappear after the evacuation spectacle

Controls:
- OR-HOLD-001 and named shelters receive supplies, surrender/defense terms and later contact attempts.
- at least one holdout choice is voluntary but constrained.
- E661–667 spends real time/resources on them.
- some remain unresolved or occupied at E690.
- later GA7/8 must revisit at least one holdout community.

### S1-10. Named protagonists may all survive while anonymous masses die

Required controls:
- at least one meaningful recurring supporting character, commander, crew, ship or institution is permanently lost/transformed.
- core protagonists may survive, but they lose authority, relationship, body/health capacity, home/ship or institution—not only sadness.
- sacrifice cannot consist solely of new characters introduced in GA6.
- selected losses must have appeared by GA5 or earlier where possible.

### S1-11. Window mechanics may feel conveniently adjustable

Controls:
- exact per-window mass/ship/thermal/repair state sheets before scene cards.
- actions in A directly change B/C capacity.
- hostile damage, queue disorder and local choices have quantified bands.
- no last-minute extra window without previously registered physical mechanism and cost.
- Grey routes remain low-capacity and medically weak.

### S1-12. Thirty episodes of continuous climax can exhaust the reader

Controls:
- 6C windows have distinct objectives and command owners.
- use focused aftermath/transfer/repair pauses between action peaks.
- no constant cross-cutting among all fronts.
- resolve one front before final manifest where possible.
- preserve quiet ship/community moments during transfer/scuttle.

### S1-13. Aftermath can become an essay or self-trial

Controls:
- E661–690 includes rescue, prisoners, holdouts, broken services, compensation, political decisions and route consequences.
- testimony changes claims, roles and reforms.
- casualty finding is plural and incomplete.
- original reveal is tied to current records/relationships and not a long lecture.
- P-001 does not deliver final moral verdict.

### S1-14. GA7 link can feel forced by suddenly harsher H-001

Controls:
- H-001’s pressure arises from actual failed windows, hostage routes, holdouts and later-front losses.
- route-denial advocates include independent commanders/communities, not only H-001.
- H-001 initially builds safeguards/alternatives before destructive doctrine.
- Blood Admiral record drift precedes any personal crossing.
- his later choices remain independent of P-001 trauma arc.

### S1-15. 120 episodes may be bloated

Contractible GA6:
- approximately 90–105 episodes.

Likely merges:
- 6A-2 + 6A-3 if census/traffic-hold overlap.
- 6B-3 + 6B-4 if route preparation and command allocation can interlock.
- 6D-1 + 6D-2 if rescue and casualty accounting repeat.

Rule:
- every mini-arc must change capacity, authority, route, survival state, responsibility or later-front resources.

## 3. S2 Moderate Risks

### S2-01. Too many decision councils and manifests

Control:
- only one full emergency council per act.
- decisions also occur aboard ships, node structures, hospitals, shelters and traffic lanes.
- reader-facing use one evolving operations board rather than many documents.

### S2-02. Medical triage can be overused as moral shorthand

Control:
- include ship mass, route skill, caregiver, institutional function, family and local shelter constraints.
- M-001 does not own all priority decisions.
- medical priority itself has internal conflicts and uncertain prognosis.

### S2-03. Neutral refusal may repeat previous arcs

Distinct function:
- GA6 concerns millions and accumulated capacity/debt, not one ship/community.
- show member captains disagree and one branch breaks rules at real future cost.

### S2-04. Scuttling ships can become melodramatic sacrificial spectacle

Control:
- ships have crews, transfer time, functions, title and alternatives.
- some crews refuse or survive without heroic death.
- scuttle may be technical/quiet and emotionally delayed.

### S2-05. Archive classification can reveal too much of Seed

Control:
- expose value conflict, not full origin/mechanism.
- classification is one system output contested by records and actors.
- full Continuity Seed truth remains GA8.

### S2-06. Casualty forecasts could appear pseudo-scientific

Control:
- present assumptions/confidence and political model differences.
- avoid single precise downstream-death number.
- later outcomes only partially validate forecasts.

## 4. Character Agency Audit

### P-001

Pass if:
- shares authority and uncertainty before windows.
- refuses full override despite real efficiency.
- loses meaningful asset/relationship/authority.
- accepts public responsibility without absolution or sole blame.

### H-001

Pass if:
- civilian/local actors modify or reject his routing.
- one optimized choice harms a community/relationship.
- GA7 doctrine emerges from independent political responsibility.

### H-002

Pass if:
- cannot modularize all institutions/ships.
- one open conversion fails and local workers/captains own decisions.

### S-001

Pass if:
- manifests/records remain incomplete.
- one privacy/provenance decision reduces rescue or accountability.

### M-001

Pass if:
- central hospital integrity remains a valid option.
- medical authority cannot erase ship/community refusal and resource effects.

### CAP/Commanders

Pass if:
- at least two decide independently and one refuses/loses.
- captain/crew scuttle/surrender rights matter.

### Decision constituencies

Each must:
- protect a real group/function.
- make one beneficial and one harmful/limiting choice.
- retain post-crisis voice/claim.

## 5. Collection Audit

Strengths:
- losses and holdouts registered equally with rewards.
- no complete evacuation or intact State group.
- central override useful but bounded/rejected in full.
- original responsibility and Archive bias have distinct payoffs.

Reader-facing consolidation:
- five tracks: facts, windows, distributed functions/ships, survival/failure and aftermath responsibility.

Cut any target that:
- does not alter capacity/route/authority/loss.
- turns a group into passive plea.
- saves an institution without degradation.
- repeats original-Orpheus information without current consequence.
