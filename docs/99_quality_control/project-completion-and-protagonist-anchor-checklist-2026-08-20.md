# 5. Protagonist Anchor — Updated Target

## 5.1 User-directed direction

The series should be more clearly protagonist-anchored than a loose ensemble, while preserving the project's core rule that other people, factions, fleets and institutions continue acting without Rian.

The following is therefore adopted as the **current QC target band** for future card reconciliation and manuscript execution.

### A. Rian-centered episodes

Target:
- **75–80%**
- 1,100-episode equivalent: **825–880 episodes**

Definition:
An episode is `RIAN-CENTERED` when at least one of the following is true and the episode's primary causal/emotional turn is materially tied to Rian:

1. Rian makes the primary decision.
2. Rian bears the primary immediate cost or reward.
3. the episode's central mission is his direct task/command/attempt.
4. another actor acts independently, but the episode is primarily about Rian confronting or changing because of that outcome.

A cameo, mention, order heard offscreen, or end-of-episode reaction does **not** automatically make the episode Rian-centered.

### B. Episodes with meaningful Rian appearance

Target:
- **93–95%**
- 1,100-episode equivalent: **1,023–1,045 episodes**

Meaningful appearance requires:
- actual on-page scene presence;
- conversation/action/observation/choice that moves the episode;
- not merely being named in a report or quoted by another character.

### C. Episodes with no Rian appearance

Target:
- **5–7%**
- 1,100-episode equivalent: **55–77 episodes**

Hard warning band:
- >10% / >110 episodes requires full-series retention review.

Why 55–77 instead of copying `20–30 episodes`:
- 20–30 absent episodes in a 410-episode project = 4.9–7.3%.
- the proportional 1,100-episode equivalent is approximately 54–80.
- therefore 55–77 is the appropriate working band for this series.

### D. Rian dominant POV / narrative lens

Target remains:
- **55–65% full-series dominant POV/lens**

This is **not** in conflict with the 75–80% centered-episode target.

Example:
- a Haren POV episode can still be Rian-centered if the central conflict is a direct Rian–Haren negotiation/operation and Rian is a major causal participant;
- a Rian POV episode can occasionally be ensemble-centered if Rian is observing another actor's irreversible choice and does not own the causal turn.

This separation is essential.

---

## 5.2 Necessary-absence rule

Every episode with no on-page Rian must pass `NECESSARY-ABSENCE`.

Acceptable reasons:

1. a faction must demonstrate that it acts before Rian knows.
2. an enemy/competitor must make a competent decision Rian cannot witness.
3. simultaneous fronts require a decision elsewhere whose consequence later reaches Rian.
4. Haren/Nera/Serin/Mia/Ern/local actors need an irreversible choice that would be weakened if Rian were present.
5. civilian/crew/worker/patient consequences must be shown without converting them into Rian's observation object.
6. suspense requires the reader to know or feel a changed state before Rian arrives.

Not sufficient:
- worldbuilding exposition.
- a random side-character interlude.
- repeating facts Rian already knows.
- showing an organization only to prove the world is large.
- delaying Rian artificially.

If the same causal content can be shown with meaningful Rian participation **without stealing another actor's decision**, prefer Rian presence.

---

## 5.3 Independence safeguard

The new protagonist target must **not** become a rule that Rian solves 75–80% of all problems.

Mandatory counterweights remain:

- every major operation/front contains at least one decisive non-Rian act.
- fleet/city/route/GA5–GA10 multi-front operations should contain at least two consequential non-Rian decisions where practical.
- Haren, Nera, Serin, Mia, Ern, captains, workers, local institutions, AI/community actors and factions may change state before Rian arrives.
- Rian may be crucial, but the world may move toward a different or worse outcome in his absence rather than freezing.
- Rian cannot be physically present at every decisive front.

Failure tags:
- `PROTAGONIST-INFLATION`
- `FALSE-DELEGATION`
- `SATELLITE`
- `HERO-RECALL`
- `POV-NO-CAUSALITY`
- `FACTION-FROZEN`
- `COMMAND-OMNIPRESENCE`
