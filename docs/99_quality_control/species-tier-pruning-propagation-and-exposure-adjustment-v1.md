## 1. Why this overlay exists

The earlier GA2/GA3 exposure proposals were written while all seven nonhuman S1 slots were still being treated as equal core candidates.

[[core-species-demand-and-tier-pruning-audit-v1]] now recommends:
- strong core: S1-03 / S1-04 / S1-05 / S1-06;
- conditional core: S1-02 / S1-08;
- regional-major demotion: S1-07.

This overlay changes only **NONCANON exposure recommendations** that depended on the old equal-seven assumption.

It does not alter any existing GA scene card, canon character, population number, event, outcome or manuscript.

# 3. GA3 early adjustment — remove pressure to introduce S1-02

The earlier GA3 proposal used S1-02 as the default new species candidate in E213/E216/E222.

After pruning, that is no longer preferred.

Reason:
- S1-02 is conditional core because much of its load/acceleration function can overlap with posthuman high-g adaptation;
- introducing it early creates a new name/body grammar before its independent civic/historical need is proven;
- GA3 already has enough institutional complexity.

### Revised E211–235 recommendation

Use:
1. human/human-derived local citizens;
2. S1-03 already known from GA2;
3. S1-05 already known from GA2.

Do **not require any new S1 name** through E235.

This is stronger normalization:
- nonhuman citizens are already part of Ardis;
- the city does not need to parade a new species every few episodes to prove diversity.

### Existing S1-02 role suggestions

The earlier suggestions such as:
- Civic identity clerk;
- service administrator;
- non-industrial local worker
remain useful **if S1-02 later survives its conditional-core gate**.

They are no longer default early-GA3 assignments.

# 4. GA3 middle/late — S1-04 becomes the next preferred core introduction

S1-04 has stronger irreplaceable infrastructure value than S1-02.

Recommended timing:
- **E236+ or later GA3**, only when public housing/water/sanitation/hospital service is already a readable scene variable.

First role should still be ordinary and non-tokenized.

Preferred candidates:
- public housing/appeal worker;
- teacher;
- parent/caregiver;
- shopkeeper;
- mixed-service budget administrator;
- union/accounting representative.

Avoid first role:
- flooded-zone specialist;
- aquatic commando;
- water-rights victim whose entire identity is wet-support cost.

### First exposure rule

Reader learns:
1. public species name eventually;
2. the person’s immediate civic/work/family goal;
3. only one current fluid-support fact if today’s choice requires it.

History/homeworld/reproduction remain later.

# 5. GA3 late seed — S1-06 before GA4 legal payoff

S1-06 has strong GA4/GA8 value, but introducing it first as a constitutional witness would make the species equal its plot function.

Therefore preferred sequence:

### GA3 late X1

Introduce one S1-06 ordinary young adult as:
- municipal clerk;
- trainee administrator;
- school/permit worker;
- reconstruction junior planner;
- ordinary resident with a personal/family obligation.

Important:
- reader should encounter them as **young/impatient**, not as an ancient sage.

Only one subtle lifespan implication is allowed at first.

### GA4 X2/X3

Later use long maturation/lifespan for:
- office-term design;
- inheritance/guardianship;
- witness credibility;
- punishment duration;
- succession-law time assumptions;
- living memory versus documentary record.

This creates:

> ordinary person first → institutional consequence later.

# 6. S1-02 conditional exposure rule

Do not delete S1-02.

Until its core gate passes:
- keep biology/world design;
- allow background or regional testing;
- do not reserve recurring GA bandwidth for it;
- no mandatory first appearance.

Promotion trigger:
- a concrete arc needs its **independent civilization/civic history**, not merely strong-body ergonomics.

If that never occurs:
- move it to S2 regional-major status.

---

# 7. S1-07 after demotion

S1-07 becomes a regional-major candidate.

Consequences:
- no requirement to appear in GA2/GA3;
- no requirement to recur across the whole series;
- electrosense/EM accessibility can enter through a specific route, settlement, school or fleet region when story demand exists;
- GA7 EW is **not** sufficient by itself to justify core promotion.

This prevents:
- “electric alien = EW specialist” destiny;
- codex obligation driving scene creation.

---

# 8. S1-08 conditional exposure rule

Before ontology decision:
- do not foreground S1-08 merely to foreshadow GA8;
- creator-era asset records remain closed;
- no “mysterious engineered species” tease is required in GA1–3.

If D1 independent engineered nonhuman is later approved:
- ordinary present-day S1-08 person should appear before deep creator-history reveal;
- GA8 may then reinterpret records about a people the reader already knows.

If D2 human-derived clade is approved:
- S1-08 leaves the independent nonhuman core roster and is handled under posthuman/genetic lineage design.
