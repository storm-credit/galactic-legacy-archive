## 2. S1 Major Risks

### S1-01. The regime can read as generic dystopian social scoring

Controls:
- no public numeric worth score.
- classifications remain separate permissions/domains with different evidence and effects.
- show ordinary administrative/medical/route decisions rather than spectacle UI.
- use setting-specific identity, command, node, AI and technical custody problems.
- some high-status people still face contribution/monitoring obligations.

### S1-02. Benefits may feel like temporary bait before obvious evil

Required controls:
- service gains persist through GA9 and into GA10.
- named beneficiaries remain supporters even after harms are exposed.
- opposition/hybrid regions fail in real ways.
- central command saves people during climax.
- some voluntary synchronized regions choose to retain parts of regime after distribution.

### S1-03. Hardliner may absorb all regime guilt

Controls:
- harmful classifications/queues/trusteeships predate and survive one commander.
- A-001, partners, officials, beneficiaries and local implementers make independent choices.
- defeating hardliner stops acute coercive expansion but not institutional dependency or ranking.
- staff/security remain necessary for transition.

### S1-04. A-001 may be softened into harmless reformer or flattened into final villain

Controls:
- A-001 personally/institutionally saves services and authorizes harmful scope.
- human/AI components may disagree but cannot transfer all blame.
- office incentives and constituencies matter.
- outcome may include removal, reform or conditional cooperation, but no clean absolution.

### S1-05. Opt-out region failure may be rigged to justify centralization

Controls:
- hybrid region also preserves rights/technology/identities the regime would harm.
- failure arises from established local power/resource structures, not sudden stupidity.
- external regime pressure contributes to instability where appropriate.
- some opt-out/hybrid systems remain viable.

### S1-06. Sabotage can become convenient excuse for repression

Controls:
- attack has specific independent perpetrators/motives and real victims.
- collective targeting is demonstrably broader than evidence.
- hardliner response genuinely stops additional harm.
- internal reform/targeted security alternatives exist but carry costs/delay.

### S1-07. P-001’s central corrective may become magical override

Controls:
- central key coordinates/authorizes; it does not create resources or instant compliance.
- each correction uses mirrors, evidence, staff and local implementation.
- exceptions consume capacity and create precedent/dependence.
- memory/health/attention limits persist.
- many decisions remain outside synchronized territory.

### S1-08. P-001 may look morally pure because he is the kinder dictator

Required controls:
- he uses seizures, restrictions or classifications during the climax.
- named people are harmed by his targeted command.
- he accepts that central efficiency makes him dangerous, not innocent.
- other actors save fronts/people independently.
- GA10 distribution is not proof his GA9 coercion was harmless.

### S1-09. Internal reformer may be a convenient key-delivery device

Controls:
- reformer has own constituency, successes, compromises and harm.
- transfer of mirrors/keys threatens services/colleagues and may fail.
- reform remains a viable partial path, not only prelude to rebellion.
- some safeguards survive because of insiders.

### S1-10. Beneficiary opposition to distribution can be portrayed as selfish fear

Controls:
- their services and safety truly depend on current system.
- they propose concrete transition requirements and may protect excluded people at risk.
- abrupt handoff would cause measurable deaths/failures.
- GA10 gives them authority rather than asking passive trust.

### S1-11. AI citizen can become token complexity

Controls:
- give ordinary relationships and independent community disagreement.
- recognized AI benefits are real.
- ambiguous entities’ exclusion is not solved through one spokesperson.
- AI actor makes one protective and one harmful decision.

### S1-12. Seed military confrontation may resemble boss battle

Controls:
- conflict occurs across services, regions, mirrors, fleets, hospitals and local authorities.
- no core-destruction shot ends regime.
- victory preserves staff/infrastructure and negotiates surrender.
- political/administrative control is more important than defeating one machine/ship.

### S1-13. P-001 central-key acquisition may contradict refusal in GA8 too neatly

Controls:
- GA8 refusal was permanent/exclusive curator role; GA9 accepts bounded emergency command under new crisis.
- decision has explicit scope, oversight and expiration, then proves difficult to relinquish.
- public/institutional demand and lack of alternatives make it credible.

### S1-14. GA10 transition may feel predetermined and easy after operator defeat

Controls:
- first handoff fails or is attacked.
- services/beneficiaries/security forces remain dependent.
- regions have unequal readiness and preferences.
- opportunists/local elites/foreign forces exploit change.
- P-001 must temporarily keep some central functions while distributing others.

### S1-15. 100 episodes may be bloated

Contractible GA9:
- approximately 78–92 episodes.

Likely merges:
- 9A-2 + 9A-3 if classification and corrective-office exposition overlap.
- 9B-3 + 9B-4 if sabotage and trusteeship are one causal crisis.
- 9C-1 + 9C-2 if occupation and internal coalition can intercut.

Rule:
- every mini-arc changes services, classification, region status, key custody, regime alignment or P-001 authority.

## 3. S2 Moderate Risks

### S2-01. Classification cases may become episodic issue-of-the-week stories

Control:
- focal cases feed the same regions, actors and key/appeal systems.
- consequences recur and aggregate into regime conflict.

### S2-02. Appeals bureaucracy may repeat GA4/8 records

Distinct function:
- GA9 appeals decide current life under an active service regime and expire against material deadlines.
- focus on implementation and queue power, not evidence mystery alone.

### S2-03. Industrial partner can seem opportunistically anti-monopoly

Control:
- preserve genuine safety/service concern and proprietary harm.
- partner’s opposition to Seed monopoly does not erase earlier exploitation.

### S2-04. Forced synchronization language can become abstract

Control:
- show officials, crews, routes, clinics, locks, records, housing and schools implementing it.

### S2-05. Public support scale can be asserted rather than shown

Control:
- use several named communities/sectors and institutional votes/requests.
- do not rely on anonymous cheering crowds.

## 4. Character Agency Audit

### P-001

Pass if:
- accepts corrective office with real limits/cost.
- uses central coercion in climax.
- protects services/beneficiaries and excluded groups.
- initiates handoff before perfect safety.

### A-001

Pass if:
- saves a region/system and authorizes harmful expansion.
- retains independent decision and constituency.
- human/AI/office responsibility remains distinct but shared.

### Beneficiary Leader

Pass if:
- supports services credibly and takes a risk for excluded people or accepts exclusion with consequence.

### Reformer

Pass if:
- reverses cases and faces complicity/transfer cost.

### Hardliner

Pass if:
- stops real catastrophe and crosses coercive line through established logic.

### Opt-Out Leader

Pass if:
- proves autonomy benefit and owns local abuse/failure.

### Partners/AI

Pass if:
- make autonomous choices not reducible to protagonists.

## 5. Collection Audit

Strengths:
- services, harms, keys and handoff states registered together.
- central Seed remains active and beneficial.
- no instant universal distribution.
- P-001 safest-dictator state explicit.

Reader-facing consolidation:
- five tracks: benefits, classifications/appeals, region choice, control/keys and transition.

Cut any target that:
- changes no service/right/authority.
- repeats another classification without cumulative effect.
- gives P-001 correction with no dependency/resource cost.
- makes a region/actor pure regime or pure resistance symbol.

## 6. GA10 Dependency Audit

GA9 must leave:

- active central Seed/current-status services.
- P-001 holding real corrective/command access.
- split mirrors/keys and internal reform staff.
- beneficiary regions requiring continuity.
- opt-out/hybrid/coerced regions with different preferences.
- damaged appeals and unresolved excluded identities/technologies.
- security forces/staff needing new mandates.
- first handoff failure and external/internal threats.
- A-001/Continuity expertise available or contested.

If GA10 can simply broadcast keys and end happily, GA9 has failed.
