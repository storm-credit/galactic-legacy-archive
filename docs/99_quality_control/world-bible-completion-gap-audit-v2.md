## 1. Scope and Method

This audit evaluates whether the repository contains sufficient design authority for:

- world physics and travel;
- society, economy and law;
- technology, mecha, ships and fleet operations;
- collection and progression;
- characters and factions;
- chronology and long-form architecture;
- mysteries, MacGuffins and ending recovery;
- operational continuity and revision controls.

It does not evaluate manuscript prose. Files under `manuscript/` are excluded from evidence by the design-only restoration ruling.

Ratings:

- `PASS` — sufficient working canon exists for downstream design.
- `PARTIAL` — foundation exists, but one or more named packets remain.
- `OPEN` — a required design domain lacks enough concrete canon.
- `DEFERRED` — useful but not required to call the 1100-episode blueprint complete.
