## 2. S1 Major Risks and Controls

### S1-01. White Dock has too many simultaneous actors and channels

Risk:
- Rian, Haren, Nera, Serin, Mia, Juno, Kara, Ern, Security, workers, radicals, Neutral and remote institutions can overload a four-episode battle.

Required controls:
- each scene begins with one zone, one physical objective and one visible clock/limit.
- Rian receives no more than two active external feeds at once.
- Haren’s board compresses off-screen fronts into zone/person status, not full dialogue from everyone.
- E21 foregrounds Rian/Nera/Haren; E22 Rian/Ern/Juno; E23 Rian/Nera/Serin/Mia; E24 Rian/Ern/Nera/Kara.
- White Dock local workers own actions but only 2–3 are named in prose.

### S1-02. Ern can appear morally superior because he withholds fire and helps stop destruction

Risk:
- sympathetic rival cliché or hidden future recruit.

Required controls:
- Ern steals records and withholds the target person/extraction limit.
- his route/operation contributes to the conditions allowing the radical cell inside.
- restraint costs an operative and does not erase lethal actions against armed/security targets.
- he leaves with records instead of staying for casualties/hearing.
- later affected people may reject his liberation plan.
- no recruitment or friendship resolution in GA1.

### S1-03. Three deaths and casualty counts may feel like abstract seriousness

Required before prose:
- lock 2–3 White Dock focal workers/crew and the critically injured Independence operative.
- assign each confirmed death to a clear physical chain, not generic battle noise.
- show one family/work-shift/service consequence after E24.
- do not use a casualty UI or repeat totals for emotion.

Current scene-card status remains valid, but casualty relationships are a prose blocker.

### S1-04. B1–B4 can become legal/ownership exposition after an action-heavy opening

Controls:
- E27 uses live testimony conflict and Kara’s personal cost.
- E28 uses bounded physical audit access.
- E29 uses monitored detention confrontation.
- E30 uses student contributions and refusal.
- E31 accelerates actual transfer.
- E32 occurs at departing shuttle.
- E33–35 include physical inspection/workshop planning.
- E36 is external zero-g salvage action.
- E38 includes physical reconstruction absence.
- E39–40 trace and visit the hidden core.
- E44 assembles the machine.
- E45 tests it and triggers a real accident.

Rule:
- no more than one full hearing/roundtable-dominant episode consecutively.

### S1-05. Juno’s case may repeat the Episode 18 betrayal test

Difference required:
- E18 establishes the cell’s refusal to assign one culprit.
- E27–32 asks whether they can replace the material guarantees that make a confession rational.
- Juno resents Rian’s unequal opportunity and does not join the team.
- victory creates a reusable mutual guarantee and debt rather than simply proving innocence.

Cut condition:
- if prose reduces Juno to “friend falsely accused,” compress the arc and restore the resource/allocation grievance.

### S1-06. Student mutual fund can solve coercion too easily

Controls:
- fund only buys a short bond/ration/medical bridge and cannot purchase freedom.
- requires approximately 72 visible contributors/refusers plus adult/Neutral placement.
- contributors lose scarce credits and become politically visible.
- Juno leaves the Academy team sphere anyway.
- the fund incurs future obligations and can be captured/fail later.

### S1-07. Serin’s audit can become magical hacking

Controls:
- read-only case scope.
- Neutral timestamps and White Dock physical lock logs required.
- Security personnel routing remains withheld.
- delegated-token replay proves timing impossibility, not a complete culprit.
- Serin exposes her own previous reporting and loses credentials.
- no remote deletion, master access or perfect truth.

### S1-08. Component acquisition risks spreadsheet fiction

Reader-facing consolidation:

1. core.
2. cooling/radiator.
3. work arm.
4. eyes/records.

The secondary manifold is shown as the one useful part abandoned; it should not become another recurring named quest in GA1.

Each acquisition must change a scene:
- radiator changes heat and Helix access.
- arm changes rescue/work and labor rights.
- sensor changes evidence/navigation and Neutral audit.
- core changes activation/continuity and Imperial control.

Do not display every title layer as a status screen.

### S1-09. Helix may still read as monopoly villain despite safety competence

Controls:
- Helix evaluator correctly identifies dangerous radiator damage.
- Helix staff help save the radiator/workers during Old Yard sabotage.
- certified repair is genuinely safer and enables E45.
- harmful elements are debt, data, exclusivity and bargaining power—not fake engineering.
- Nera accepts certification in a bounded domain.

### S1-10. Imperial observer can feel like a convenient benefactor who creates the climax

Controls:
- observer diverted the core before team request and for an independent state/continuity goal.
- protection removes a witness/person from family/public process.
- observer cannot free students or own all claims.
- exclusive offer is materially good and politically dangerous.
- core return occurs only because Helix/Academy/Neutral/student vetoes make hardliner seizure costly.
- observer retains access, surveillance and future leverage after Act B.

### S1-11. Four-lock custody can read as an early idealized answer

Controls:
- activation is slower and E45 nearly loses an emergency target.
- one party initially rejects it.
- it lasts 30 days and can be revoked/captured.
- students carry debt and liability.
- protected witness remains excluded.
- local/worker/community representation is incomplete.
- E46–100 must revise/fail it rather than treating it as final constitution.

### S1-12. Rian’s ace-pilot promise may fade after E24

Required payoffs:
- E21 precise launch/avoidance.
- E22 capture-not-kill duel.
- E23 structural rescue under heat/body limit.
- E24 denial-climax timing.
- E36 unarmed utility-frame zero-g rescue.
- E45 reconstructed 07 emergency movement.

Control:
- no hidden output increase or effortless recovery.
- action remains varied: duel, rescue, salvage and constrained test.

### S1-13. 07 reconstruction may erase the cost of White Dock

Controls:
- lost certified manifold creates permanent lower cooling envelope.
- radiator remains patched/debt-inspected.
- sensor data remains externally mirrored.
- core remains multi-party controlled.
- Rian cannot deploy alone.
- E45 output is only 50–60%, not a superior new form.

### S1-14. Fifth-answer imitation accident can feel mechanically timed to start Act C

Controls:
- rumor/resentment appears from E26 onward.
- E30 student debate shows different interpretations.
- E44 special treatment and reconstructed 07 visibly reinforce the myth.
- imitators act for their own grievance/objective, not because plot needs a hospital clue.
- their plan is an understandable but dangerous misreading: create shared risk to force negotiation.
- victims and consequences continue through E46–51.
