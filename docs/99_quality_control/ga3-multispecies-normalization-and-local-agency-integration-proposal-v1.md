## 0. Purpose

This proposal answers one narrow question:

> If the galaxy is genuinely multi-species, how can GA3 Ardis show that fact **without creating a new species subplot, changing existing events, or turning Rian into the person who discovers/validates alien citizens?**

Answer:

> Use already-existing unnamed local workers, residents, clerks, passengers and constituency contacts as the first city-scale normalization layer. Their jobs and decisions already exist in the cards. Species assignment, if later approved, changes embodiment/service details — not event ownership.

This document proposes **where** such assignments could fit.
It does not execute them.

---

## 1. Existing GA3 structural advantage

Ardis at E211 is already:
- ~1.42m permanent residents;
- ~170k transient/stranded;
- a functioning city with traffic, hospitals, schools, markets, foundry, food/water systems, temporary housing and surface works;
- locally governed and maintained before Rian arrives.

E211 begins with Toma Cal and Anchor Crown traffic workers **before the protagonist party disembarks**.

This gives the correct multi-species message:

> nonhuman people did not appear because Rian reached a new map.
> They were already living, working, arguing, paying rent and making decisions here.

## 4. Exposure principle

### Wrong structure

```text
Rian arrives
→ sees alien
→ asks what species it is
→ receives biology lecture
→ alien trait solves problem
```

Reject.

### Preferred structure

```text
local person is already doing job / arguing / refusing / caring
→ reader recognizes familiar or newly bounded body difference
→ current service detail appears only if it changes today's choice
→ person's institutional decision remains the main scene function
→ species history waits
```

# 10. Multi-species infrastructure without new plot

Ardis can feel multi-species through repeated small facts:

- berth/service capability tags;
- adjustable restraint geometry;
- multi-environment clinic bays;
- food label separation;
- wet-rest or pressure-rest modules;
- accessible terminals;
- different emergency transport hardware;
- public sanitation service categories.

Rules:
- one fact per scene at most when not plot-central;
- no tour-guide paragraph;
- do not make every infrastructure difference a discrimination conflict.

Ordinary usability must appear before political breakdown.
