## 1. Core Principle

Do not bolt a species subplot onto the series.

Use existing story engines:

> species difference → ordinary service/constraint → character choice → institutional cost → larger political meaning

A species should enter because the current GA already asks a question that biology/infrastructure makes materially sharper.

## 2. Global Introduction Limits

1. GA1 is not a species showcase.
2. GA2 is the first sustained multi-species ordinary-life window.
3. GA3 normalizes multi-species public infrastructure.
4. GA4 politicizes history/law only after ordinary coexistence is established.
5. GA5–7 reuse existing species more than introducing new ones.
6. GA8 opens S3/deep-time layer.
7. GA9–10 introduce almost no new major peoples.
8. Existing named cast stays unchanged unless separately approved.
9. No retroactive “this character was secretly species X” fix.

## 3. Exposure States

### X0 — world hint

Examples:
- equipment adapter label;
- translated public notice;
- medical category;
- mixed-species media image;
- food/environment service icon.

No named species exposition required.

### X1 — visible ordinary person

A person of another species appears performing an ordinary role.

Reader learns:
- public name;
- one practical body/service fact at most.

### X2 — recurring supporting person

Reader sees:
- own goal;
- relationship/community;
- repeated choices;
- one institutional conflict.

### X3 — species-linked public issue

Infrastructure, law, labor, military or history becomes plot-relevant.

### X4 — deep history/origin

Contact chronology, engineered origin, extinct civilization or Archive taxonomy.

Rule:
- S1 normally reaches X1/X2 before X3/X4.

# 4. GA1 — 다섯 번째 답

Current engine:
- correctional Academy;
- 07;
- contracts/records;
- Black Ward;
- siege/charter.

Species purpose:
- **establish that K-13 is local, not representative of the whole galaxy.**

Recommended maximum:
- X0 strongly allowed;
- X1 at most one minor/background role after continuity review;
- no X2 recurring new species character unless an existing card has a genuine role gap.

Safe X0 examples:
- medical database contains nonhuman physiological profiles;
- dock emergency map shows environment-compatible rescue modules;
- translated Imperial service icon has several body-profile accessibility marks;
- 07 diagnostic compatibility table lists nonhuman adapter categories without explaining them.

Do not:
- insert cantina-style species parade;
- explain Imperial contact history;
- change core five species identity;
- imply 07 is alien technology.

Why:
- GA1's reader load is already Rian/Haren/Nera/Serin/Mia, Academy factions, 07, Black Ward and future-record mystery.

GA1 verdict:
- **X0 only by default.**

# 6. GA3 — 부서진 항로의 도시

Current engine:
- node city;
- public service;
- labor;
- production;
- ownership;
- siege/governance.

Species purpose:
- **prove multi-species society works or fails through budgets and infrastructure.**

Recommended active set:
- 3–4 S1 species including those introduced in GA2;
- 1 S2 regional people at most if ecology/industry requires it.

Strong role bundles:
- Numar housing administrator;
- Kelik school/access actor;
- Braxi labor arbitrator;
- Tesar young municipal clerk;
- Verin migrant parent as late-GA3 seed if history reveal stays closed.

### 3A — service collapse

Species difference turns into:
- who loses safe environment first;
- which hospital module fails;
- which public service cannot simply be rationed equally.

### 3B — ownership/governance

Question:
- who pays for unequal-cost accommodation?

Avoid:
- “special rights versus fairness” straw man.

Show:
- different users fund and depend on shared systems in different ways.

### 3C — mass-production derivative

Species angle:
- public frame/industrial standards need adapters;
- separate chassis only where mission economics justify it.

### 3D — siege

Pay off established needs:
- water;
- EM safety;
- gravity;
- medical support;
- evacuation.

GA3 verdict:
- **multi-species ordinary life becomes normalized fact, not reveal.**

## 17. Audit Result
