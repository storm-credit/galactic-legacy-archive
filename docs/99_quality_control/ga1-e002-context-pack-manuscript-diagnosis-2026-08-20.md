## 5. Finding E2-F02 — `Blood Admiral` is exposed ten episodes too early

Severity: **S2 — reader-information timing / character framing**
Harness: Pass 3 / Pass 5

### Problem

Current v2 repeatedly gives E002 readers information equivalent to:

- specific mass-harm/route-starvation résumé;
- an interpretation that the official future record makes him look bloodthirsty from the beginning.

The E002 Context Pack therefore explicitly forbids promoting the label/metadata early.

[[core-canonical-names-and-voice-lock-v1]] also says Haren's present voice must avoid **bloodthirsty foreshadowing**.

### Cause

Older prose used future-title exposition as a fast contrast device before the fairness-ledger timing was enforced at manuscript level.

Keep Rian's flawed future framing, but reduce it to source-supported function:

- Rian remembers Haren as a future **dangerous / strategically important figure** whose historical image he distrusts or fears;
- do not name `피의 제독` in E002;
- do not list specific future starvation/mass-harm achievements here;
- let Haren's current obligations and anger challenge Rian's category.

This preserves the scene-card value change:

> future enemy/target category → present person with obligations and independent anger.

It also preserves E011's actual first Blood Admiral reveal.

### Ripple

- E011 M-004 gains its intended first-hit force;
- present Haren reads as a person before a label;
- no future responsibility is erased or softened; it is simply not spoiled in E002.
