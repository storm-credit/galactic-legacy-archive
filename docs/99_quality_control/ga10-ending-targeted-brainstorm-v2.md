## 1. Reference-Preflight Consequences

The cross-media research changes the execution method in four ways:

1. do not end with one recognizable `iconic shot` borrowed from another franchise;
2. distribute closure by **function and scale**, not by a parade of fanservice cameos;
3. let different characters visually and institutionally mature in different directions;
4. treat 07/Parus as **working legacy**, not sacred collectibles or protagonist inventory.

Reference lessons are constraints, not scene sources.

# 2. E1076–1100 Macro Presentation Options Inside Approved Option C

## C1 — `Ledger First, Epilogue Last`

Shape:
- E1076–1095 completes almost all reconstruction/accountability in documentary order;
- E1096–1100 becomes a clean epilogue block.

Strength:
- chronology easy to audit.

Risk:
- 20 episodes of procedural cleanup before emotion;
- reader may feel the story already ended twice.

Verdict: **SAFE BUT DRY**.

## C2 — `Object → Institution → Person Funnel`

Shape:
- immediate aftermath closes operational debt;
- 07/Parus and material legacy return before abstract final ledgers;
- institutions prove autonomy;
- CY751 ends at one ordinary person.

Strength:
- scale steadily contracts from civilization to object to people to individual present desire;
- strong fit with collection/legacy theme.

Risk:
- if 07/Parus are too sentimental, middle band becomes nostalgia parade.

Verdict: **RECOMMENDED PRIMARY SHAPE**.

## C3 — `Intercut Reconstruction and CY751`

Shape:
- E1090 onward alternates CY748–750 reconstruction with CY751 current frame.

Strength:
- shows causal payoff immediately.

Risk:
- chronology complexity in the last 10 episodes;
- can feel like prestige-TV editing rather than Korean webnovel clarity.

Verdict: **USE ONLY AS LIMITED INSERTS**.

## C4 — `Five Independent Endings`

Shape:
- each core person/object gets a near-standalone farewell episode.

Strength:
- maximum fan payoff.

Risk:
- anthology/reunion-special feeling;
- structural repetition.

Verdict: **REJECT AS PRIMARY**.

### PM selection

Use **C2** as the backbone, with **small C3 reconstruction inserts** only where needed to prove a CY751 state.

# 3. 07-C Hybrid Retirement — Four Execution Shapes

Locked direction:

- no peak-combat resurrection;
- useful Open Service lineage remains public/nonexclusive;
- unsafe/obsolete material may be dismantled into training/compatible service parts;
- one working instructional/service configuration may remain only if exact canon mapping supports it.

Focus:
- formal handover/farewell.

Risk:

Verdict: **REJECT AS PRIMARY**.

Focus:

Verdict: **STRONG**.

Focus:
- some unsafe/obsolete sections are gone; the important continuity is shared standards, training and compatible service practice across more than one place.

Strength:

Risk:
- original 07 emotional presence may become too diffuse.

Verdict: **STRONG SECONDARY**.

## 07-C4 — `Silent Museum Object`

Focus:
- preserved hull behind glass or equivalent.

Strength:
- easy visual symbol.

Failure:
- contradicts working/service legacy.

Verdict: **REJECT**.

### Recommended execution

**C2 + C3.**

The reader should see that the original machine is no longer `Rian's answer`, while its readable service lineage is more alive than a pristine relic would be.

No exact facility/name/scene is invented here.
