## 5. Finding F-E001-CP-02 — `처음 며칠` after regression is unsupported exact timing

Severity: **S3 — unsupported prose-level temporal specificity**
Status: **OPEN — neutralization recommended**

### Verified current-v2 text function

After the Archive warning, current v2 says in substance:

> 죽었다가 열일곱 살로 돌아온 뒤부터 같은 경고가 반복됐고, `처음 며칠`은 원인을 알아내려 했다.

### Source boundary

Higher sources lock:

- E001 / CY 742-03-17 as the series opening/intake episode;
- the fact of regression to the correctional-academy state;
- one-time regression;
- abnormal/repeated Archive response.

They do **not** sufficiently lock an exact multi-day interval between the regression instant and the E001 hangar cold open.

This is different from the 07 dismantling clock. `입소 사흘 뒤` is source-supported by the opening operational state; `회귀 후 처음 며칠` is not independently established at the same authority level.

### Risk

The wording can quietly create chronology from manuscript staging and can make the episode promise read as if the wake-up/regression happened materially earlier than the E001 opening without a control decision.

### Recommended minimum fix

Remove only the duration.

Functional replacement:

> `경고가 나타난 뒤 몇 번이나 원인을 확인하려 했지만…`

or another duration-neutral equivalent.

Do not decide a new exact regression minute/day in prose.

### Ripple

- no event change;
- no mystery-answer change;
- no chronology change;
- M-009 uncertainty preserved;
- `NEW_CANON_REQUIRED: NO`.
