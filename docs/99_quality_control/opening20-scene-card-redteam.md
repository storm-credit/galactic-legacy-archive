## 2. S1 Risks

### S1-01. Administrative density remains high

Heavy episodes:
- E7 ownership hearing.
- E9 record correction.
- E10 current-status case.
- E13 authorship/closure registry.
- E14 contract negotiation.
- E18 betrayal bargain.

Controls present:
- every episode has physical access, treatment, test, repair or deadline consequence.

Required prose control:
- no legal/record explanation longer than the current physical choice needs.
- open each heavy episode with denied access, moving equipment, medical need or deadline—not a seated briefing.
- dialogue must contain character conflict and concrete objects, not policy summaries.

### S1-02. Too many mystery promises in twenty episodes

Introduced:
- unrecorded Rian.
- 07 service lineage.
- changing hero count.
- Orpheus gaps.
- Haren/Blood Admiral.
- Nera closed/death record.
- Ern withheld fire.
- Black Ward/closed identities.

Controls:
- only three remain active foreground questions at a time:
  1. immediate 07/cell/White Dock eligibility;
  2. Haren community deadline;
  3. one rotating future/Archive contradiction.
- others appear briefly and leave a physical consequence.

Required:
- no episode advertises more than one new CORE mystery.
- E11 and E13 explanations remain deliberately incomplete.

### S1-03. Cast load can exceed reader attachment

Recurring by E20:
- Rian, Haren, Nera, Serin, Mia, Ern.
- Bram, Kara, Juno, Soma.
- community representatives and institutional observers.

Controls:
- Rian/Haren anchor E1–10 emotionally.
- Nera/Serin/Mia each own one clear competence before expanded backstory.
- Bram/Soma/Juno appear only when function/agency matters.
- institutional observers can remain roles rather than named core cast.

Human test question:
- after E5 and E10, ask readers to name/describe characters without prompt.

### S1-04. Rian may reveal “another time/configuration” too early or too vaguely

E17 control:
- he admits his information assumes a later/different configuration.
- he does not fully explain regression mechanism or prove future life.
- cell knows he has dangerous privileged information/memory-like expertise, not the cosmic answer.

Required consequence:
- trust changes; Serin/Mia demand future disclosures be confidence-tagged.
- no one simply accepts him as a time traveler without evidence.

### S1-05. Episode 13 title can become cheap death clickbait

- do not end the episode with a fake corpse tease.

### S1-06. Haren’s community could dominate every choice

Controls:

- E11 shows competence/office fear.
- E19 includes independent community disagreement and Rivo exit pressure.

### S1-07. Helix can feel unusually reasonable early and villainous later

Control:

### S1-08. Rival cell is at risk of being a moral measuring stick

Controls:

Control:
