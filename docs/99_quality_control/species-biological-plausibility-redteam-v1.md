## 1. Audit rule

This is not a demand for strict hard-SF xenobiology. The standard is:

- internally causal;
- no free superpowers;
- body plan affects ordinary life;
- physiology survives ship/medicine/military use;
- no trait exists only for visual novelty;
- unknown evolutionary details may remain speculative, but the operational consequences must be consistent.

Verdicts:
- PASS — strong enough to continue;
- MODIFY — premise useful, mechanism/wording needs correction;
- HOLD — too many unresolved assumptions;
- REJECT — weak or redundant.

## 2. S1-01 Humanity / human-derived spectrum

Verdict: **PASS**.

Strength:
- current setting already supports natural, genetic, cybernetic and regional variation.

Blind spot:
- treating human baseline as biologically uniform across 612 systems would be less plausible than moderate adaptation.

Control:
- keep local medical/physiological variation without turning every colony into a separate species.

Required later work:
- define which human-derived adaptations remain reversible/medical and which become stable clades.

---

## 3. S1-02 High-gravity compact multi-use limb species

Verdict: **MODIFY / STRONG**.

Useful premise:
- higher gravity plausibly favors lower center of mass, stronger support structures, compact body plans and different circulation constraints.

Problem in first pass:
- “four major limbs that alternate between locomotion and fine manipulation” can become mechanically vague and risks simply producing a centaur/spider visual without believable hand/tool evolution.

Recommended refinement:
- use **four load-bearing limbs plus two smaller dedicated manipulators** OR **two anterior limbs with hand-grade distal anatomy and two posterior primary supports**.
- do not claim all limbs are equally dexterous unless developmental/evolutionary reason is built.

High-g advantage limit:
- high-g adaptation does not automatically grant high spacecraft acceleration tolerance. Circulation orientation, chest compression and acceleration vector still matter.

Required cost:
- high caloric/oxygen demand;
- cooling burden;
- reduced comfort in long low-g periods;
- heavier seating/restraint.

Military ruling:
- “naturally better soldiers” = REJECT.
- “some individuals tolerate specific load profiles better” = acceptable.

---

## 4. S1-03 Low-gravity pressure/vibration-sensitive species

Verdict: **MODIFY / PASS AFTER CONTROL**.

Useful premise:
- low-g ancestry can plausibly produce gracile structures and poor high-load tolerance if ecology permits.
- pressure/vibration sensing is physically grounded in mechanoreception.

Blind spot:
- too-sensitive whole-body vibration sense could make living aboard machinery impossible.

Control:
- biology must include neural filtering and adaptation ranges;
- overload occurs only outside normal ecology or under intense industrial/weapon vibration.

Sensor limit:
- they can infer leaks, impacts and structural changes; they cannot magically diagnose an engine by touch without training and instruments.

Body-plan issue:
- the proposed 3 locomotor + 2 manipulator arrangement remains unusual but viable as speculative anatomy; it needs later gait/embryology sketch before visual canon.

Military ruling:
- best used for structural awareness, not precognition.

---

## 5. S1-04 Amphibious/fluid-supported species

Verdict: **PASS / HIGH WORLD VALUE**.

Strength:
- produces clear infrastructure, ship, medicine and evacuation consequences.

Key control:
- “amphibious” must specify whether gas respiration is native, secondary or mechanically assisted.
- do not let the species remain indefinitely dry if fluid-supported skin/respiratory maintenance is a core trait.

Fluid-cockpit issue:
- immersion can distribute body loads but does not erase acceleration physics; respiration, pressure, circulation and restraint remain limits.

Mass cost:
- fluid mass/containment makes small-ship accommodation expensive.

Narrative risk:
- do not reduce them to water-rights victims. Need politics, humor, professions and cultures unrelated to water.

---

## 6. S1-05 Symbiotic dual-organism person lineage

Verdict: **PASS / MOST ORIGINAL IDENTITY SLOT, HIGH COMPLEXITY**.

Strength:
- directly enriches medical consent, personhood, Archive and quarantine themes.

Biological control:
- the regulatory/sensory symbiont should integrate during normal development, not act as a second hidden brain by default.
- it can contribute immune/metabolic/sensory functions without containing a separate personality.

Legal blind spot:
- “two organisms = one legal person” must not be universal metaphysical truth. Law recognizes the current integrated person because that is the stable living entity; unusual separation cases can be contested.

Reproduction:
- needs later answer for transmission of both partner lineages.

Power limit:
- broad chemical sensing does not equal toxin immunity or instant disease detection.

Verdict reason:
- keep, but it demands the deepest medical/family entry.

## 7. S1-06 Long-lived radial species

Verdict: **PASS / MODIFY LONGEVITY MECHANISM LATER**.

Strength:
- lifespan changes law, political memory, casualty value and apprenticeship without becoming a combat power.

Body plan:
- five-limb radial symmetry is speculative but operationally coherent in three-dimensional habitats.

Blind spot:
- radial body cannot be assumed to lack front/back sensory specialization; tool use and locomotion still create functional orientations.

Longevity control:
- long life needs slow maturation/repair tradeoffs and should not imply perfect memory.
- individual recollection remains biased and incomplete.

Demographic consequence:
- low reproduction/replacement can make mass-casualty wars existential at much smaller absolute casualty rates.

Narrative risk:
- “old species = wise conservative elders” is prohibited.

---

## 8. S1-07 Electrosensory enclosed-habitat species

Verdict: **MODIFY / STRONG**.

Electrosense can plausibly detect fields/currents in limited ranges.

Hard limit:
- cannot read encrypted signals, memories, software or device purpose directly.
- shielded systems and distance reduce useful signal.

Overload risk:
- dense machinery should not make cities uninhabitable. Their nervous system requires normal filtering and protective behavior.

Recommended ecology:
- electrosense evolved for navigation/prey/social signaling in conductive mineral/wet substrate and later became useful around machines.

Thermal-cycling trait:
- currently under-justified. Either:
  1. tie it causally to metabolism/ecology; or
  2. remove it to avoid stacking too many gimmicks.

**Recommendation: remove mandatory thermal cycling from the core species premise unless later ecology proves it.**

This keeps the slot focused on body anchoring + nonvisual electrosense.

---

## 9. S1-08 Developmentally engineered free-lineage species

Verdict: **PASS AS STORY SLOT / BIOLOGY HOLD**.

The important distinction is historical origin, not a predetermined anatomy.

Strength:
- connects genetic ownership, industrial history and current autonomy.

Risk:
- engineered origin can become an excuse for optimized superbiology.

Control:
- original design purpose imposed tradeoffs;
- descendants undergo natural/cultural change;
- current people are not “products that escaped.”

Major question:
- independent nonhuman engineered species or human-derived clade?

Recommendation:
- keep this unresolved until S2/S1 overlap audit. If human-derived, it may belong in the posthuman bible rather than consume one S1 nonhuman slot.

## 10. Cross-slot redundancy audit

Potential overlaps:

- S1-08 engineered lineage overlaps with posthuman engineered clades;
- S1-03 mechanosense and S1-07 electrosense both risk “better engineer species” stereotyping;
- S1-02 high-g and S1-04 fluid support both touch acceleration tolerance.

Controls:

- S1-03 main identity = low-g body + pressure/mechanical environment sensitivity;
- S1-07 main identity = contact/field sensing + anchoring body plan, not engineering aptitude;
- S1-02 main identity = load-bearing compact anatomy, not universal acceleration supremacy;
- S1-04 main identity = environmental fluid dependence, not high-g combat.

---

## 11. Missing biological dimensions

Current S1 test has good body/environment diversity but under-covers:

1. dramatically different circadian/temporal biology;
2. truly non-humanoid communication/social sensory channel;
3. species with very small adult scale;
4. species with very large adult scale;
5. reproduction/development difference;
6. airborne/buoyant locomotion;
7. alternative biochemistry.

These are already partly represented in S2 pool. Do **not** cram all into S1.

---

## 12. Evolution-versus-engineering balance

Recommended portfolio:

- majority of S1 current species: naturally evolved or origin uncertain;
- 1 engineered-origin core lineage at most;
- human-derived engineered clades remain separate from independent species count;
- precursor engineering may be suspected in some lineages but cannot become universal truth.

---

## 13. Scientific-detail boundary

Do not overexplain:
- exact alien biochemistry;
- full evolutionary family trees;
- molecular basis of every sensory organ.

Do explain when story-relevant:
- what atmosphere/food they need;
- what injures them;
- what medical systems must know;
- what their senses can and cannot detect;
- what acceleration/environment they tolerate;
- what equipment physically changes.

## 15. Verdict

> **7/8 slots are worth continuing; S1-08’s category placement remains HOLD. The largest corrections are to avoid free acceleration advantages, magical electrosense and over-stacked physiological gimmicks.**
