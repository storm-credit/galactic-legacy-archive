## 2. S1 Major Risks

### S1-01. Identity decompression may still function as H-001 exoneration

Required controls:
- current D4 order is direct, consequential and recorded in H-001’s own name.
- he knew meaningful uncertainty and heard local/dependent dissent.
- he accepts foreseeable doctrine/delegation failures separately from direct acts.
- victims do not need to forgive him after attribution is corrected.
- corrected records do not restore destroyed routes or downstream lives.
- no final narration calls him “actually innocent.”

### S1-02. H-001’s D4 choice can look forced by the plot

Required chain:
1. target route/node and three-region dependencies established at least 10–15 episodes earlier.
2. D1–D3 alternatives attempted or credibly ruled out.
3. capture/occupation threat demonstrated in prior arc.
4. central corridor offer is credible and rejected/limited for stated reasons.
5. local and dependent representatives disagree.
6. H-001 has actual authority and a genuine alternative, not a fake button choice.
7. later evidence does not reveal a hidden safe option.

### S1-03. Blood Admiral mystery may become too complex

Reader-facing model:
- H-001 the person.
- route-admiral office/title.
- denial doctrine.
- delegated/inherited signatures and other commanders.
- propaganda/downstream aggregation.

Control:
- investigate 4–6 representative incidents only.
- use one evolving incident board/case, not dozens of records.
- every identity distinction changes current responsibility, office, compensation or route rights.

### S1-04. Another governance arc may feel repetitive after GA1–5

Distinct stakes:
- authority to destroy shared infrastructure and impose downstream death.
- responsibility across person/office/doctrine/delegation.
- physical route cascades and occupation, not only charter design.

Control:
- each subact includes convoy, node, charge, occupation, closure or route action.
- no more than one full federation hearing/assembly per act.
- rules emerge after concrete failure or denial decision.

### S1-05. Continuity Corridor may seem obviously authoritarian

Controls:
- central corridor reliably moves food/medicine and prevents local/radical hostage tactics.
- some vulnerable communities prefer it.
- RF-L02 grants at least one exception/admission of model error.
- federation/local veto visibly abandons or delays dependents.
- conflict is over access, ranking and permanent custody, not whether the corridor works.

### S1-06. Scorched commander may become a convenient extremist scapegoat

Controls:
- scorched doctrine prevents one real capture/occupation.
- occupation history and local support are specific.
- H-001/Defense/Custodian actors agree with parts of the logic.
- radical misuse is not the cause of H-001’s own D4 order.
- faction has internal dissent and affected communities.

### S1-07. H-001 may eclipse all local and federation actors

Controls:
- RF-L01 authors/limits/removes his mandate.
- local custodian and dependent region hold real standing.
- operators/captains execute/refuse D4 independently.
- final federation operation works without his real-time command.
- H-001 receives no permanent master route/denial key.

### S1-08. P-001 can become H-001’s moral judge and recentralize the story

Controls:
- P-001 may oppose or support tactics but cannot decide H-001’s office/guilt.
- he testifies to facts/choices and protects people/evidence.
- H-001 decision remains outside P-001 command where legally plausible.
- their relationship can fracture without one submitting.
- final proceedings and federation reforms are local/plural.

### S1-09. Downstream casualties can become invisible statistics

Controls:
- RF-L05 and 2–3 named dependent institutions/communities appear before D4.
- replacement-route/service failures shown after the act.
- claims/reparations and later GA8/9 politics continue.
- casualty ranges tied to medicine, food, transport and occupation chains.
- at least one downstream actor remains recurring.

### S1-10. Denial technology can become a convenient dramatic switch

Controls:
- D4 requires physical teams, time, access, evacuation, thermal/structural work and multiple components.
- refusal/delay and partial execution are possible.
- destruction has repair/environmental/debris effects.
- no single universal red button.
- H-002/07 can enable or prevent specific steps but cannot decide policy.

### S1-11. Plural responsibility may erase accountability

Controls:
- separate direct order, delegated authority, foreseeable doctrine, failure to revoke/respond, false attribution and downstream aggregation.
- assign evidence and remedy by category.
- uncertainty does not mean nobody is responsible.
- some cases remain unresolved rather than falsely distributed to everyone.

### S1-12. Public myth persistence could make correction feel pointless

Control:
- decompression changes legal office, compensation, identity, access, histories and victim claims even if popular title persists.
- successor commanders and institutions can no longer hide behind H-001 myth.
- H-001 loses/narrows authority and bears specific obligations.

### S1-13. Final federation success may undermine the D4 tragedy too quickly

Controls:
- E781–790 succeeds at a different route/problem and still harms one group.
- it demonstrates institutional succession, not that earlier H-001 choice was unnecessary.
- destroyed target route remains lost.
- victims/opposition remain outside reconciliation.

### S1-14. GA8 bridge could feel like abrupt lore shift

Controls:
- throughout GA7, record custody changes current identity, command keys, compensation and route authority.
- Archive/Continuity actors actively use compressed records.
- final conflict concerns who may correct/delete/centralize records, naturally requiring deeper archives.
- no sudden expedition solely for lore.

### S1-15. 110 episodes may be bloated

Contractible GA7:
- approximately 82–98 episodes.

Likely merges:
- 7A-2 + 7A-3 if local closure and mandate negotiation overlap.
- 7B-3 + 7B-4 if key custody and title inheritance share one crisis.
- 7D-1 + 7D-2 if incident decomposition and responsibility proceedings repeat.

Rule:
- every mini-arc must change route state, authority, denial capacity, responsibility, evidence or federation design.
