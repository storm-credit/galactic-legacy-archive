## 1. CORR-01 — S1-02 limb architecture

### Problem

[[core-species-codex-prototype-cards-v1]] still describes S1-02 as four major limbs that are all used for locomotion and manipulation with different fine-control ranges.

[[species-biological-plausibility-redteam-v1]] correctly identified that as mechanically vague and too close to giving every limb equal hand-grade dexterity without evolutionary justification.

### Correction

Keep the stable slot identity:

> `S1-02 — high-gravity compact four-limb species`

But interpret the four major limbs as:

- **two anterior limbs** with hand-grade distal manipulation and strong bracing capability;
- **two posterior limbs** specialized primarily for load-bearing, propulsion and stable support;
- all four can participate in emergency bracing/crawling/anchoring;
- posterior limbs may perform coarse manipulation, but they are **not** equally dexterous hands;
- ordinary tool culture therefore remains compatible with mixed workplaces through adapters rather than requiring four simultaneous hand controls.

This preserves the reader-memory label `four-limb` while making the body plan more credible.

### No change to

- high-g adaptation;
- cooling/caloric costs;
- no-universal-high-G-pilot rule;
- no-warrior-species rule;
- infrastructure and restraint costs.

Status: **CORRECTED AT PROPOSAL LAYER**.

## 2. CORR-02 — S1-07 thermal-cycle overstack

### Problem

Early proposal shorthand describes S1-07 as `electrosense / thermal-cycle` and the prototype card includes species-wide scheduled thermal cycling.

[[species-biological-plausibility-redteam-v1]] found the thermal-cycle trait under-justified and warned that it stacks a second gimmick onto an already sufficient electrosensory/anchoring premise.

### Correction

Stable S1-07 identity becomes:

> `S1-07 — electrosensory anchored low-light species`

Core biological premise:

- compact anchored body plan;
- low-light/contact sensing;
- close-range electric/magnetic gradient sense;
- strong EM-noise overload limits;
- interpretation still requires education and instruments.

`thermal-cycle` is **REMOVED from the species-wide core identity**.

It may return only as one of:

- a local population adaptation;
- a home-ecology feature with explicit causal support;
- a cultural rest practice that is not genetically universal.

Until then, any earlier `electrosense/thermal-cycle` shorthand in PR #188 must be read as `electrosense/anchoring`.

Status: **CORRECTED AT PROPOSAL LAYER**.
