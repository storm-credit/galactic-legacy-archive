## 0. Question

Even if `species != faction`, a weak design can still produce the same pattern seven times:

> species → homeworld → one government → diaspora → one stereotype.

This audit asks whether the retained strong-core candidates can have **different civilization shapes**, so the galaxy feels historically grown rather than generated from one template.

---

# 1. Four civilization-distribution directions

## CIV-A — One species, one sovereign polity

Pattern:
- each people has one homeland state and speaks politically through it.

Strength:
- easy reader map.

Failure:
- species becomes nationality;
- mixed cities/factions feel secondary;
- every war risks becoming race war.

Verdict:
- **REJECT as default.**

## CIV-B — Pure diaspora

Pattern:
- every species is scattered with no substantial majority region.

Strength:
- prevents ethnostate mapping.

Failure:
- erases independent pre-Imperial civilization history;
- makes all nonhuman peoples appear absorbed into human institutions.

Verdict:
- **REJECT as universal model.**

## CIV-C — Imperial integration only

Pattern:
- nonhuman peoples matter mainly through Arzen Imperial provinces/institutions.

Strength:
- low continuity cost.

Failure:
- the galaxy becomes a human imperial history with colorful residents.

Verdict:
- **REJECT.**

## CIV-D — Asymmetric layered plurality

Pattern:
- different peoples have different combinations of homeland, habitat networks, diaspora, provincial integration, mixed institutions and lost/secondary centers;
- no single template applies to all.

Strength:
- best historical depth;
- species does not dictate politics;
- supports IMP-D if later approved;
- gives Rian-independent regional clocks.

Verdict:
- **RECOMMENDED.**

# 2. Shared anti-monoculture requirements

For every strong S1 candidate before canon promotion:

1. at least **3 institutional/political contexts**;
2. at least **2 internal political disagreements** not reducible to biology;
3. at least **1 mixed-species institution** where members are ordinary participants, not token envoys;
4. at least **1 region/community** where humans are not the default majority if plausible;
5. at least **1 diaspora or migration history**;
6. at least **1 cultural practice with no survival/military utility**;
7. at least **1 current plan that proceeds without Rian**;
8. no species-wide command hierarchy.

Not every S1 requires a sovereign state.

---

# 3. S1-03 civilization-shape stress test

Biological anchor:
- low-g body;
- pressure/vibration sensitivity.

Recommended civilization shape:
> **distributed low-gravity habitat/commonwealth network + route diaspora + mixed Imperial/local urban populations.**

This is a shape, not a named polity.

## Context A — low-g habitat network

Possible form:
- several moons/cylinders/stations share infrastructure standards and mutual evacuation agreements;
- no single capital required;
- governance may be municipal/federal/contractual rather than dynastic.

Independent agenda:
- keep high-g route operators from defining universal safety standards;
- preserve economic viability without becoming a protected enclave.

Internal split 1:
- universal low-acceleration standards versus species-specific exceptions.

Internal split 2:
- habitat autonomy versus deeper integration with high-throughput Imperial/Neutral routes.

## Context B — Neutral/route diaspora

People live aboard:
- family ships;
- route habitats;
- clinics;
- education networks;
- logistics communities.

Important:
- route identity can matter more than species identity.

Independent agenda:
- route access, childcare, insurance and evacuation standards continue whether Rian appears or not.

## Context C — Imperial/local civic populations

Presence in:
- public service;
- schools;
- courts;
- housing;
- medicine;
- media.

Do not confine them to hull work.

## Non-utilitarian culture test

Potential category only, exact culture HOLD:
- spatial performance/dance tradition;
- quiet tactile storytelling or fashion;
- competitive adapted racing subcultures.

At least one should exist because people enjoy it, not because it improves survival.

### Verdict
**PASS AS DISTINCT CIVILIZATION SHAPE.**

# 4. S1-04 civilization-shape stress test

Biological anchor:
- fluid-supported/amphibious ordinary life;
- wet-rest infrastructure.

Recommended civilization shape:
> **multi-city aquatic/fluid municipal corridor + mixed dry-habitat diaspora + cross-environment trade/service institutions.**

Do not default to one ocean planet monarchy.

## Context A — fluid-habitat municipal network

Possible form:
- multiple city/habitat jurisdictions linked by water/service infrastructure;
- politics organized around municipal budgets, contamination control, trade and local autonomy.

Independent agenda:
- shared fluid-service standards and contamination treaties.

Internal split 1:
- concentrated wet districts for efficiency/community versus integrated mixed districts for access/political influence.

Internal split 2:
- municipal public ownership versus private/contract operation of fluid infrastructure.

These are political-economic disagreements, not biological destiny.

## Context B — dry/mixed diaspora

Many S1-04 people deliberately choose:
- mixed Imperial cities;
- Neutral routes;
- Ardis-like public habitats;
- professional careers in ordinary dry public space.

Their diaspora politics can differ sharply from majority-fluid municipalities.

## Context C — cross-environment commerce/service

Possible institutions:
- habitat construction;
- medical transport;
- sanitation certification;
- ordinary trade unrelated to water;
- education and family support.

Do not turn these into a species guild.

## Non-utilitarian culture test

Potential categories:
- dry-space theatre/performance fashion;
- fluid sculpture/play;
- culinary/social traditions that are not merely hydration management.

### Verdict
**PASS — STRONGLY DISTINCT FROM S1-03 AND HUMAN REGIONAL VARIATION.**

# 5. S1-05 civilization-shape stress test

Biological anchor:
- obligate biological symbiosis forming one ordinary person.

Recommended civilization shape:
> **diaspora-heavy integrated people with several old cultural centers but no requirement for one dominant species-state.**

Why this shape is useful:
- shared atmosphere/body geometry allows wider integration;
- their strongest differences are medical/legal, so making them geographically segregated would be unnecessarily deterministic;
- creates a species whose civilization is not primarily territorial.

## Context A — old cultural/medical centers

Some regions may be historically important for:
- developmental medicine;
- family/community practice;
- language/art;
- legal reform.

They need not control most S1-05 citizens.

## Context B — Imperial/mixed urban integration

Large populations can be ordinary:
- clerks;
- teachers;
- workers;
- doctors;
- administrators;
- soldiers;
- merchants;
- artists.

This makes outdated biometric/legal systems more politically interesting: exclusion occurs inside institutions they helped build.

## Context C — Neutral medical/custody networks

Cross-border care and quarantine makes Neutral routes materially important.

Independent agenda:
- certified treatment access and identity portability.

## Internal split 1

Species-specific protected legal category versus universal body/person rules that apply to everyone.

## Internal split 2

Privacy/refusal politics versus public-health/medical standardization.

## Internal split 3

Creator/evolutionary origin, if ever relevant, must not automatically unify political identity.

## Non-utilitarian culture test

Potential categories:
- cuisine, comedy, performance, family ritual or competitive sport unrelated to symbiotic function.

Do not make all culture about “two becoming one.”

### Verdict
**PASS — USEFUL NON-TERRITORIAL CORE CIVILIZATION MODEL.**

# 6. S1-06 civilization-shape stress test

Biological anchor:
- long life + slow maturation;
- radial five-limb body.

Recommended civilization shape:
> **one or more old regional political traditions + substantial Imperial/Neutral diaspora + strong generational reform conflicts.**

Unlike S1-05, some long-lived institutions can plausibly retain very old local continuity.

## Context A — old regional polity/institutions

Possible characteristics:
- offices, courts, schools and property law developed around long maturation and long service lives;
- institutional continuity is real but contested.

Important:
- not an “ancient wise council race.”

Independent agenda:
- reform old law without surrendering regional autonomy to faster-changing outside institutions.

## Context B — Imperial/provincial communities

Long-lived individuals may personally remember:
- prior administrations;
- earlier wars;
- previous versions of law.

This does not make their memory correct.

## Context C — Neutral/route and veteran communities

Casualty and service politics differ because replacement/maturation is slow.

Some members may favor extreme caution.
Others may argue that long life creates a greater obligation to risk oneself for younger populations.

## Internal split 1

Long office terms as continuity versus long office terms as entrenched power.

## Internal split 2

Personal witness prestige versus documentary/audited evidence.

## Internal split 3

Young reformers versus older reformers/conservatives — age cannot map neatly to ideology.

## Non-utilitarian culture test

Potential categories:
- rapid-fashion subculture precisely because long life makes novelty attractive;
- short-form competitive art;
- games designed around simultaneous multi-direction body use.

### Verdict
**PASS — DISTINCT THROUGH POLITICAL TIME RATHER THAN SPECIES WISDOM.**

# 7. Cross-species civilization diversity matrix

| Species | Majority-region shape | Diaspora shape | Mixed-institution strength | Main internal political axis | Territorial centrality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1-03 | distributed low-g habitat network | route/family diaspora | high | autonomy vs universal transit standards | medium |
| S1-04 | fluid municipal/city network | mixed dry-habitat diaspora | medium-high | integrated vs concentrated services / ownership | high-medium |
| S1-05 | several cultural/medical centers, no required single state | very high | very high | special-category protection vs universal person rules / privacy | low |
| S1-06 | old regional polity/institution traditions | Imperial/Neutral/veteran diaspora | high | continuity vs entrenchment / memory authority | medium-high |

Result:
- no four copies of the same species-state template;
- no requirement for one sovereign state per species;
- no species is defined entirely by the Empire or by opposition to it.

# 8. Rian-removal test

## S1-03 network

If Rian disappears:
- route/habitat communities still negotiate acceleration and evacuation standards;
- families still move/refuse routes;
- insurers/operators still price service.

PASS.

## S1-04 municipal network

If Rian disappears:
- water/service authorities still budget, repair, ration and dispute ownership;
- integrated/separate district politics continues.

PASS.

## S1-05 diaspora institutions

If Rian disappears:
- medical/legal portability fights, privacy rules and ordinary employment continue.

PASS.

## S1-06 regional institutions

If Rian disappears:
- office-term reform, succession, veteran/casualty politics and local memory disputes continue.

PASS.

No core species is waiting for the protagonist to grant political agency.

# 9. Interaction with Imperial history HOLD

This audit is compatible with the recommended IMP-D layered transformation but does not require canon acceptance of IMP-D yet.

If IMP-D is approved later:
- each species' relationship to Imperial expansion must be different;
- contact/incorporation cannot repeat one conquest/treaty story four times;
- at least one relationship should emphasize trade/service integration;
- at least one should include serious war/coercion;
- at least one should be mostly migration/urban integration;
- at least one should retain strong regional autonomy.

Exact mapping remains HOLD until author history decision.

---

# 10. Failure conditions

Redesign if:
1. every species gets a sovereign ethnostate;
2. every species has one official political spokesperson;
3. every diaspora agrees with a homeland government;
4. species-majority region means species-only region;
5. cultural practices all derive directly from biology;
6. internal political disagreement exists only to prove “not all X are the same” and has no material stake;
7. one species maps cleanly to Imperial loyalist, rebel, merchant or religious faction;
8. Rian becomes the first cross-species mediator who teaches them coexistence.
