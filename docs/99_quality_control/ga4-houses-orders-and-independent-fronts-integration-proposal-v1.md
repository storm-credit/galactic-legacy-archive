## 0. Purpose

GA4 already contains six authentic succession protocols, four primary personal claimants, provincial/covenant alternatives, independent intelligence blocs, local governments and split evidence custody.

It does **not** need a new “noble-house war” pasted over that structure.

This proposal asks:

> How can houses and service orders become visible as independent social/military institutions **without changing any claimant, protocol, outcome, death, custody chain or compact result?**

Answer:

> Derive only the institutional layers already implied by existing canon, and allow them to act on their own clocks before Rian encounters the consequences.

---

# 1. Existing anchors that already justify the layer

Current canon already includes:
- SP-01 registered blood/house succession;
- SP-07 Founding Covenant / signatory-body reversion;
- Old Court / founding-house coalition;
- Elian's dynastic household and Crown Guard families;
- Mara's ministry/civil-service constituency;
- Dor's regular military and military-family constituency;
- Isa's Continuity professional/service constituency;
- provincial/node authorities with their own recognition power;
- four official evidence-retrieval teams;
- Vesper Recovery as a lawful civilian salvage cooperative;
- deputies and succession limits for later offices.

Therefore the missing work is **social embodiment**, not a new constitutional layer.

---

# 2. Three front-stage house/order fronts are enough

Do not introduce 10–16 named houses in GA4.

Recommended front-stage institutional bundles:

### FRONT-H1 — Founding / Covenant House Network

Source:
- existing SP-07 / Old Court / founding-house coalition.

Function:
- historical law, archives, signatory succession, covenant legitimacy.

### FRONT-H2 — Crown-Service / Military-Family Network

Source:
- existing Elian constituency (`Crown Guard families`) plus military-service households around the Protector dispute.

Function:
- separates ceremonial loyalty, household service, serving officers and actual command law.

### FRONT-O1 — Independent Service-Charter / Custody Tradition

Source:
- existing cross-jurisdiction custody, evidence, rescue and node-service needs.

Function:
- gives old service institutions a reason to survive regime changes.

Important:
- FRONT-O1 is a **design slot**, not authorization to insert a new order into E338–345.
- if existing Neutral/Registry/medical/accident-board chains already perform the function, do not duplicate them.

These three are enough to make the social layer visible while keeping the six succession protocols readable.

# 3. FRONT-H1 — Founding / Covenant House Network

## Existing legal basis

SP-07 already assumes:
- original founding houses/institutions;
- descendant or successor signatory bodies;
- incomplete/contested institutional genealogy.

The network therefore already exists conceptually.

## What must be added before naming

Not “which house owns which superweapon.”

Instead define for each future named member:
- which original obligation/privilege it claims;
- what present asset or institution keeps the claim relevant;
- whether the succession chain is biological, adopted, institutional, trust-based or mixed;
- what evidence would weaken its own claim;
- what current constituency it serves besides itself.

## Rian-removal clock

If Rian vanishes during E331–345, the network still must:
- decide whether to invoke SP-07;
- authenticate successor/signatory standing;
- challenge or publish old records;
- negotiate with provinces and courts;
- split over whether emergency reversion protects liberty or restores oligarchy.

## Minimum internal split

At least two positions:

### H1-R — Reversionist
- wants stronger signatory-body authority now;
- believes modern emergency offices exceeded founding limits.

### H1-RF — Reform Covenant
- accepts founding evidence but insists modern provinces, workers, service users and nonhuman citizens must be represented;
- rejects pure hereditary restoration.

This split is stronger than “good house / evil house.”

## Cost before Rian intersection

One or more should occur independently:
- archival release damages own standing;
- branch loses recognition;
- trust assets frozen;
- old ally defects;
- legal challenge invalidates one claimed succession link.

## GA4 story function

The network contributes:
- evidence;
- legal limits;
- historical legitimacy;
not final sovereign truth.

Hard veto:
> No founding house may possess the answer to all six protocols.

# 4. FRONT-H2 — Crown-Service / Military-Family Network

## Why one network, not one faction

Current canon already distinguishes:
- Elian's Crown Guard families / ceremonial loyalists;
- Dor's regular armed-force / military-family constituency;
- actual military command law.

Some families can have relatives in both traditions.

This creates useful internal fracture:
- loyalty to Crown symbol;
- duty to current unit;
- obedience to Protector order;
- household/dependent welfare;
- civil constitutional outcome.

These need not align.

## Rian-removal clock

While Rian is on the evidence mission:
- serving officers receive contradictory lawful orders;
- household foundations must pay dependents;
- guard detachments decide ceremonial protection versus operational deployment;
- reserve families face recall and casualty burden;
- old patrons pressure serving members.

## Required independent choice

A future recurring military-service house should face a choice such as:
- public endorsement of Elian while its serving branch obeys Dor;
- refuse a patron demand to move a unit outside current lawful chain;
- fund families from both sides rather than declare one branch traitors;
- split its public archive rather than falsify a unified family position.

## Result Rian later encounters

Examples:
- a Guard unit is already unavailable because it chose household/civilian protection;
- an officer has resigned rather than accept a disputed transfer;
- a family foundation has publicly contradicted the claimant it historically served;
- one branch has lost promotion/benefits for refusing patron pressure.

None requires Rian POV.

Hard veto:
> Do not create “the legendary pilot bloodline that recognizes Rian.”

# 5. FRONT-O1 — Independent Service-Charter / Custody Tradition

## Need test

GA4 contains many functions that *could* historically generate a service order:
- cross-jurisdiction witness protection;
- evidence custody;
- emergency convoy passage;
- node protection without destructive denial;
- medical evacuation.

But current cards already assign many of these to:
- Neutral medical/evidence staff;
- Registry team;
- Regency team;
- Protector team;
- Continuity team;
- accident board;
- Ardis/Neutral custody;
- Vesper Recovery.

Therefore:

> **Do not add a named knight/service order unless a later card exposes a function these existing actors cannot credibly cover.**

## If later justified

Best candidates:
- K-03 Witness/Custody tradition;
- K-02 Bastion/Node Custodian tradition.

But introduction should be through an existing obligation, not pageantry.

Example structure:

```text
old charter obligation triggers
→ current order must protect person/site it politically dislikes
→ patron/claimant demands exception
→ order refuses or splits
→ cost occurs
→ Rian later encounters changed custody/access situation
```

## Failure mode

Reject:
- cool armor;
- ceremonial sword;
- named ace;
- ancient frame
as sufficient reasons for an order to exist.

# 6. Vesper Recovery classification

Current E338–345 fact:
- Vesper Recovery is a civilian salvage cooperative;
- it reached Baseline first;
- attached emergency power and tow;
- holds a valid rescue/recovery lien;
- the lien covers hull/rescue cost, not people or sovereign evidence.

Proposal classification:
- **closest functional match = G-02 Security / Recovery Cooperative**, not knightly order.

Why this matters:
- it already proves the gray/private economy can create protagonist-independent causality;
- it moves because rescue cost, lien and private-broker offers exist;
- Rian does not create its claim.

Do not rename or recategorize Vesper in canon yet.

Use it as the model for how future private organizations should behave:
> useful, self-interested, legally bounded and able to oppose protagonists without being villains.

# 7. Parallel causality across E331–345

Without adding scenes, the author-side causal map can be read as:

```text
FRONT A — Ardis local authority
six orders → non-accession response → evidence mission

FRONT B — four claimant institutions
lawful competing service/command claims → retrieval deployment → joint hearing

FRONT C — founding/covenant network
SP-07 standing/authentication/reversion dispute continues independently

FRONT D — civilian recovery economy
Vesper reaches Baseline → incurs rescue cost → gains lien → resists exclusive seizure

FRONT E — military/household service networks
conflicting Crown/Protector obligations → unit/family choices continue offscreen
```

Rian is active mainly inside the evidence-mission geometry.

He does not cause:
- the six protocols;
- Ardis's first response;
- the claimant deployments;
- Vesper's lien;
- provincial consultation;
- founding-covenant standing;
- household/service conflicts.

This is exactly the desired structure.

# 8. POV recommendation

Do not add a POV merely to show each house exists.

Preferred:
- most house-state changes appear through changed signatories, court filings, unavailable units, public statements, trust freezes or altered coalition maps.

Use direct house/order POV only when:
1. the internal split produces an irreversible decision;
2. later report would falsely make Rian appear causal;
3. emotional cost belongs to that actor;
4. competing valid duties are the scene's actual conflict.

Reader-facing GA4 remains centered on existing claimants and core cast.

# 9. Multi-species implication

If the multi-species Empire direction is later approved:

- a founding house need not be genetically homogeneous;
- an old signatory body may survive through adoption/institutional succession across species;
- Crown-service families may contain mixed legal kinship;
- service orders may have multi-species membership and environment support.

Do not use species to simplify claimant allegiance.

Required visual/political proof later:
- members of one species appear on more than one succession side;
- one house contains more than one species or legal kinship model where historically justified;
- “bloodline” evidence can matter legally without proving competence or moral legitimacy.
