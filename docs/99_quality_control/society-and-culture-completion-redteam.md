## 1. Verdict

> **PASS FOR WORKING-CANON SOCIETY AND CULTURE FOUNDATION**

S0 blockers:
- 0.

The batch gives the world private life, pleasure, education, family, faith, culture and AI/person ordinary existence. Final named institutions, character-specific relationships and art direction remain open.

## 2. Attack — Faith Traditions Are Political Metaphors

Risk:
- Continuance equals centralism, Passage equals freedom, Witness equals pluralism and Quiet equals anti-Aurel politics.

Controls:
- each tradition has real moral benefit and coercive shadow;
- traditions cross factions and regions;
- secular professional/civic ethics are equally important;
- no supernatural doctrine is proven;
- individuals may combine or reject traditions.

Required character application:
- centralization opponent from Continuance tradition;
- Aurel supporter from Passage/frontier background;
- Maker/Steward expert who rejects technical rule;
- Quiet tradition member who seeks external synchronization.

Verdict:
- PASS WITH CROSS-POLITICAL EXAMPLES REQUIRED.

## 3. Attack — Culture Is Over-Neatly Regional

Risk:
- Core, Orsen, Ardis, frontier and Palimpsest could read as one culture each.

Controls:
- internal class, district, generation and migration differences;
- regional sections describe patterns, not deterministic identities;
- mixed neighborhoods and diaspora are explicit;
- political variation exists inside every region.

Required future practice:
- every front-stage region uses at least two contrasting households/subcultures;
- avoid assigning one accent, food or personality to an entire region.

Verdict:
- PASS.

## 4. Attack — Private Life Is Still Too Functional

Risk:
- food, art, sport and holidays may only illustrate governance, memory or labor.

Controls:
- nonfunctional hobbies and leisure are required;
- at least one celebration/recreation sequence per grand act completes without attack;
- ordinary pleasure can change affection or mood without advancing plot;
- sports and entertainment include banal fandom, status and embarrassment.

Required rule:
- not every personal object, game or song becomes a clue, relic or political symbol.

Verdict:
- PASS.

## 5. Attack — Families Exist Only as Leverage

Risk:
- debt, guardianship, treatment and evacuation make family primarily a hostage system.

Controls:
- several family forms and supportive daily functions;
- guardianship is split, appealable and not ownership;
- distributed and ship/route families have ordinary life;
- individuals may leave family/community expectations;
- partnerships and children have goals beyond political stakes.

Required later cast:
- stable supportive family with no hidden betrayal;
- chosen household that is imperfect but not abusive;
- family disagreement not resolved by death or separation alone.

Verdict:
- PASS WITH SUPPORTING FOCALS REQUIRED.

## 6. Attack — Romance Is Avoided Rather Than Designed

Risk:
- power/consent safeguards may cause the project to omit intimacy and partnership entirely.

Controls:
- multiple legal and cultural partnership forms;
- consent and command conflicts have procedures;
- romance cannot replace independent goals but remains allowed;
- long-distance, ship/route and AI/human relationships are defined.

Open:
- no canonical core romance is selected;
- character-specific chemistry and relationship arcs remain author decisions.

Verdict:
- FOUNDATION PASS / CHARACTER APPLICATION OPEN.

## 7. Attack — Reproduction and Family Technology Are Vague

Risk:
- assisted reproduction, artificial gestation and AI creation can introduce major ethical/technical questions without limits.

Controls:
- only broad methods are established;
- habitat resources, identity, contracts and medical access constrain use;
- no faction’s morality is tied to birth rate;
- institutional ownership and data abuse are explicit risks.

Required later packet if plot uses it centrally:
- exact medical capability, cost, consent and law;
- no surprise reproductive technology used as twist.

Verdict:
- PASS AS BACKGROUND / DETAILED USE GATED.

## 8. Attack — Children, Disabled People and Patients Are Stakes

Risk:
- vulnerable groups may exist only to motivate rescue or policy.

Controls:
- domain-specific rights and preference;
- hobbies, education, work, media and relationships;
- disability shaped by environment and service access, not moral worth;
- care work recognized;
- patients resist inspirational framing;
- youth have ordinary status and conflict.

Required named roster:
- later-act children/youth, disabled workers and patients who act beyond rescue decisions.

Verdict:
- PASS WITH NAMED FOCALS REQUIRED.

## 9. Attack — AI Life Is Abstract or Human Copy

Risk:
- AI/persons become legal categories, or ordinary humans with digital vocabulary.

Controls:
- embodiment, compute, energy, memory, copying and network create distinct material life;
- they also possess ordinary leisure, work and relationships;
- no single political identity;
- copies/derivatives/current persons remain distinct;
- hardware ownership and personhood separated.

Required future examples:
- at least three named AI/composite persons with different embodiment, politics and family form;
- one ordinary dispute unrelated to Archive/Seed;
- one person who prefers limited knowledge or offline rest.

Verdict:
- PASS WITH CHARACTER IMPLEMENTATION OPEN.

## 10. Attack — Holidays Are Exposition Devices or Always Attacked

Controls:
- several holidays have food, sport, family and recreation;
- one completed celebration per grand act required;
- conflict can remain social without physical interruption;
- official and counter-memory versions coexist.

Verdict:
- PASS.

## 11. Attack — Media Can Explain Anything Conveniently

Risk:
- public opinion may change instantly through one broadcast.

Controls:
- route and verification delays;
- source/editing/confidence required;
- different media ecosystems;
- raw evidence does not guarantee interpretation;
- fame and narrative ownership persist.

Required rule:
- one broadcast can trigger action locally, not transform the whole lattice immediately.

Verdict:
- PASS.

## 12. Attack — Sexuality and Partnership Norms Are Too Narrow

Assessment:
- exclusive, multi-adult, ship/route, care and AI/human partnerships are allowed;
- no universal sexual or gender norm has been imposed;
- legal functions are separated from cultural preference.

Open:
- gender/sexuality terminology, region-specific prejudice and character identities remain unspecified.

Required future control:
- avoid treating diversity as exotic setting decoration;
- discrimination, where present, must have institutional/cultural specificity rather than generic modern copy.

Verdict:
- PASS AS OPEN PLURAL BASELINE.

## 13. Attack — War Setting Is Too Joyless

Controls:
- extensive sport, art, food, games, media and festivals;
- ordinary scenes required across grand acts;
- celebration can complete safely;
- institutions are defended partly because they make life enjoyable and predictable.

Required story-architecture application:
- include recovery and leisure in act pacing, not only world-bible entries.

Verdict:
- PASS.

## 14. Attack — Aurel Culture Is Only Dystopian Normality

Controls:
- real improvements to services, education, events and safety;
- ordinary beneficiaries and indifferent citizens;
- cultural narrowing occurs through access/recommendation/status, not constant overt bans;
- some people voluntarily retain synchronization after GA10.

Verdict:
- PASS.
