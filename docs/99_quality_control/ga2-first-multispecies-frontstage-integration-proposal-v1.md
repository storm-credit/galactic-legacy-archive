## 1. Goal

Find the **least disruptive first on-page multi-species exposure** inside GA2 without:
- adding a new episode;
- replacing a named current character;
- changing ship counts;
- changing contract outcomes;
- creating a species subplot;
- using alien biology as a magic solution.

The desired reader experience is:

> “Of course different people already live in this galaxy.”

not:

> “Episode 122: aliens are finally revealed.”

## 2. Why E101–114 should remain almost species-silent

E101–114 already teach:
- five-way ownership/claim stacks;
- Maren's captain boundary;
- engineering signoff;
- 42-person legal crew;
- medical veto;
- multi-signature authority.

Adding biological-world exposition here competes with foundational ship literacy.

Recommendation:
- X0 background only;
- no new named S1 person by default.

Possible X0 only after later approval:
- multi-physiology medical profile option in the crew census;
- adapter category icon on a restraint/medical menu;
- no species noun required.

## 3. Best first window — E122–130

Why:
- first contract already carries 6 patients, 4 caregivers, 2 inspectors and 2 appeal witnesses;
- the ship has 46 assigned personnel;
- E122 explicitly says one patient's condition may not tolerate fastest route;
- E123 turns ship-local life support into real responsibility;
- E126–129 stage multiple groups by medical need and destination choice;
- no new population count is needed.

Key safety rule:
- **do not make the existing medically limiting patient nonhuman merely to explain the veto.**
- the medical veto already works and should not become a species gimmick.

## 4. Four Integration Options

### Option A — S1-03 caregiver first + S1-05 ordinary crew/support second

#### First foreground: S1-03 low-g pressure/vibration-sensitive caregiver

Possible status:
- one of the already-existing four caregivers in the first contract.

Why it fits:
- Parus cruise drum at ~0.35–0.45g is close to S1-03 preferred gravity range;
- therefore the ship is not automatically hostile to them;
- the already-deferred crew-drum bearing vibration becomes an ordinary comfort/sleep problem rather than a new mission blocker.

What the reader learns first:
1. species public name eventually;
2. one practical behavior: uses a vibration-damping rest wrap/mount or relocates sleep position;
3. they are here because they are caring for someone, not because of a special sensory job.

What must NOT happen:
- they detect D4 bearing failure before engineers;
- their body solves navigation;
- their physiology creates the E128 medical stop that already belongs to existing patients/doctor authority.

Independent goal:
- keep their patient/family member on the chosen treatment path and preserve their own appeal/work obligations.

#### Second foreground: S1-05 symbiotic person as ordinary paid support/crew

Possible status:
- one of the already-existing temporary support workers/crew additions, preferably galley/habitation or general service rather than data/medicine.

Why it fits:
- shared ship atmosphere/seating can be close to baseline;
- medical inventory has one routine symbiont-safe note;
- no dramatic “two beings in one body” reveal is needed.

Reader-facing rule:
- first scene is work/pay/space problem;
- biology appears later as one normal medical/food constraint.

Advantages:
- two very different kinds of alienness: environmental sensing vs symbiotic biology;
- both arrive through ordinary care/work;
- neither duplicates hero/pilot/engineer role.

Risk:
- S1-05 eventually intersects identity/provenance law, which could become too neat in the 061 arc.

Control:
- do not use their body to solve 061 evidence; save deeper identity-schema payoff for GA4/8.

**Verdict: RECOMMENDED.**

### Option B — S1-02 service worker + S1-07 technical inspector

Strength:
- immediately visible body differences;
- fits ship/yard material world.

Weakness:
- reproduces “alien = industrial specialist” stereotype;
- S1-07 as inspector/technician activates exactly the engineer-species risk caught by red team.

Verdict:
- useful later, not the first pair.

---

### Option C — S1-04 patient/caregiver + S1-06 claims actor

Strength:
- highly alien visual contrast;
- proves wet-module and long-lifespan law.

Weakness:
- Parus opening configuration does not have mature wet-support capacity;
- making S1-04 one of the current passengers would require real module/water/mass changes and therefore changes cards, not flavor;
- S1-06 longevity can drag GA4-style history questions too early.

Verdict:
- defer.

---

### Option D — no foreground nonhuman until GA3

Strength:
- zero GA2 continuity load.

Weakness:
- GA2 is explicitly the first-ship/frontier/community expansion;
- a mature multi-species civilization would feel retrofitted if nobody matters until GA3.

Verdict:
- reject if the multi-species direction is canonized.

- one routine food/medical requirement later;

Do not add a third core species unless the salvage/community operation genuinely needs it.

# 6. Parus Compatibility Check

## S1-03 caregiver

Parus cruise drum:
- 0.35–0.45g.

S1-03 provisional comfort:
- 0.35–0.65g.

Therefore:
- routine cruise gravity can be comfortable;
- high acceleration remains a concern in other ships/missions but is not forced into this first scene.

Known Parus D4:
- crew-drum bearing vibration.

Use:
- sleep/recovery friction only;
- engineers already know D4 exists;
- nonhuman perception does not discover new failure.

Adapter burden:
- reclining/restraint geometry U2;
- use one portable/temporary support because current passenger count already includes medical/caregiver equipment.

No new module required.

Verdict:
- **physically compatible with minimal IC-1 impact.**

## S1-05 general support worker

Atmosphere/seating:
- designed to be near common standards.

Extra burden:
- food/medical compatibility note;
- quarantine/drug protocol if illness occurs.

No dedicated habitat module.

Verdict:
- **minimal physical impact; character-level IC-2 if recurring.**

# 7. Reader-Memory Budget

When first S1-03 appears, do NOT introduce together:
- homeworld;
- self-name;
- species-majority state;
- religion;
- special technology;
- historical contact date.

First 5-episode memory bundle:

```text
[species public name]
= low-g person
= caregiver on Parus
```

Nothing more is required.

For S1-05:

```text
[species public name]
= ordinary support worker
= one person despite symbiotic biology
= has their own wage/work/family problem
```

Deep biology comes later.

# 10. Red-Team Result

### Risk — alien diversity arrives only as medical inconvenience
Mitigation:
- first S1-03's actual scene goal is caregiving/destination, not physiology;
- second S1-05's goal is work/pay/community.

### Risk — species difference solves a ship problem
Mitigation:
- S1-03 does NOT detect/solve D4;
- D4 is already known and simply affects them differently.

### Risk — token pair disappears after exposition
Mitigation:
- only promote to X2 if their independent goal naturally recurs through GA2.

### Risk — current cards become secretly different
Mitigation:
- this file changes zero scene cards; it defines a future impact option only.
