## 0. Audit question

Two large NONCANON expansions now coexist:

1. a genuinely multi-species 612-system civilization;
2. an Era-1 distributed numbered demonstrator/prototype program.

Because both affect interoperability, there is a strong temptation to make one the hidden explanation for the other.

That would over-connect the setting and create retrospective convenience.

This audit therefore locks the boundary:

> **species diversity may pressure technology, but it does not secretly cause the prototype program; prototype history may affect accessibility standards, but it does not explain the existence or political agency of species.**

---

# 1. Allowed interaction

If both directions are later canonized, the following interactions are reasonable.

### A — body/environment diversity increases interoperability burden

Different populations can require:
- restraint geometry;
- atmosphere/fluid support;
- medical compatibility;
- interface translation;
- accessibility and rescue modules.

This can make Era-1 fragmentation materially harder.

### B — old service systems can include nonhuman accommodation

AUXILIA/P07-descended service philosophy may later support:
- adapter-friendly controls;
- wider service envelopes;
- emergency rescue across multiple body/environment classes.

But this must remain one aspect of broad service interoperability.

### C — later production standards can embed historical bias

Early human-dominant or local standards may persist in:
- cockpit geometry;
- safety assumptions;
- medical baselines;
- certification.

Later multi-species society then adapts or contests those standards.

### D — species institutions can inherit prototype-era technology differently

A species-majority region may use:
- P07-descended service interfaces;
- P18-style low-logistics practices;
- P20-descended production methods;

without those technologies being “their species technology.”

# 2. Forbidden hidden-origin shortcuts

## F1 — “The prototype program existed to solve alien compatibility”

**REJECT.**

The strongest current history is:
- relay/habitat expansion;
- contractor/regional fragmentation;
- later security militarization;
- reconstruction;
- standardization.

Species diversity can increase pressure but cannot become the secret master cause.

## F2 — “07 was designed as the universal alien-compatible frame”

**REJECT.**

07/P07’s strongest identity remains:
- mixed-contractor service;
- rescue/intervention;
- broad diagnostics;
- bounded emergency authority.

Multi-species ergonomics may later be supported through adapters, not define the original whole mission.

## F3 — “Every core species gets a prototype lineage”

**REJECT.**

Species tier and machine lineage are independent.

A species can:
- use ordinary mass frames;
- use cockpit/seat/environment adapters;
- prefer ships/remotes rather than frames;
- have no signature frame family.

## F4 — “P13’s exceptional performance comes from one superior alien pilot species”

**REJECT BY DEFAULT.**

That would turn physiology into a combat caste and erase P13’s intended operator/medical/logistics failure.

If a nonhuman test participant ever exists, species identity must not explain away the non-scalability.

## F5 — “S1-08 engineered people were created by the same program”

**REJECT.**

S1-08 is a people/civilization ontology question.
The maneuver-frame program is an equipment/industrial-history program.

Conflating them would create a single precursor/creator conspiracy.

## F6 — “AXIOM is the alien source of all 01–20 technology”

**REJECT.**

AXIOM remains separate HOLD.

# 3. Chronology dependency

The two historical directions can be approved independently.

### Species history IMP-D

Concerns:
- independent nonhuman civilizations;
- Imperial contact/incorporation;
- migration/treaty/war/integration;
- current multi-species population.

### Prototype H-E history

Concerns:
- Era-1 infrastructure expansion;
- contractor fragmentation;
- demonstrator authorization;
- node/security escalation;
- reconstruction/standardization.

Neither requires the exact chronology of the other to exist.

If both are later canonized, chronology audit must only answer:
- which nonhuman populations were already present in relevant test regions;
- whether any specific accessibility requirement was part of one experiment.

Do not rewrite the whole program after species history is chosen.

# 4. 07 cross-domain safe interpretation

If both P-D2 and multi-species world direction are later approved:

Safe statement:

> 07’s broad service architecture, originally developed to survive fragmented infrastructure and contractor standards, later proved unusually adaptable to mixed-species service and rescue requirements.

Unsafe statement:

> 07 was secretly built for all species from the beginning.

The safe version is stronger because:
- historical design gains unintended longevity;
- later users co-author the lineage;
- open standards evolve rather than being prophetically complete.

---

# 5. P20 cross-domain safe interpretation

P20-style scalable production can historically encode a dominant body/crew assumption.

Later consequence:
- mass standardization lowers costs dramatically;
- nonstandard bodies/environments may pay adapter or exclusion costs;
- later public/open standards attempt to broaden accessibility.

This creates real politics without declaring P20 malicious.

Do not make:
- P20 a species-neutral perfect universal chassis;
- accessibility automatically free once mass production exists.

---

# 6. P17 cross-domain safe interpretation

P17-style bounded authentication can later help mixed institutions recognize:
- different operator authorities;
- rescue roles;
- local medical/service stops.

But identity/personhood must not be reduced to machine authentication.

A species/person’s legal standing comes from current law/personhood institutions, not because P17-compatible hardware recognizes them.

---

# 7. Species-specific chassis gate remains unchanged

Before creating any species-specific independent chassis, prove:

1. common/shared chassis + cockpit insert fails;
2. mission module fails;
3. remote operation fails;
4. body/environment difference changes primary load structure;
5. at least two major systems change;
6. independent certification/parts/cradle/loss path exists;
7. recurring operational demand justifies industrial support.

Species existence alone never passes this gate.

This remains compatible with PF-08 extreme-environment/nonstandard-body demand family.

---

# 8. Political independence

No species should derive political importance from having a special machine.

No prototype lineage should derive legitimacy from being associated with a species.

Forbidden equations:
- species = frame lineage;
- prototype possession = sovereignty;
- old test participation = present territorial title;
- ergonomic compatibility = legal citizenship;
- historical engineer ancestry = exclusive design ownership forever.

These separations directly support GA4/GA8/GA9 themes.
