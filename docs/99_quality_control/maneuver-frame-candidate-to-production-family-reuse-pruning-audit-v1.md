## 0. Audit question

The current lineup index is intentionally a **candidate review surface**, not a promise that every row becomes an independent named chassis.

Earlier role-demand expansion added many rows because real story functions existed.

This audit asks the next question:

> Does each role require a new chassis, or can it be served by a reusable production family, mission package, cockpit/environment insert, permanent refit or local rebuild?

This distinction is essential because:
- role count != chassis count;
- organization count != chassis count;
- faction count != chassis count;
- species count != chassis count.

---

# 1. Status labels used here

### KC — KEEP AS STRONG CHASSIS CANDIDATE

Role and structural burden plausibly justify independent chassis proof.

Not canon approval.

### RF — REUSE-FIRST

The role is real, but an existing/earlier family should be tested before any independent chassis is allowed.

### VF — VARIANT/CONFIGURATION-FIRST

Likely solvable through:
- tool pack;
- cockpit;
- armor;
- sensor/EW package;
- medical module;
- permanent refit on same frame.

### HF — HULL-HISTORY / REBUILD-FIRST

Character or salvage identity may belong to an individual hull/rebuild history rather than a unique production chassis.

### AH — AUTHOR HOLD

Cannot classify until a story/origin decision exists.

# 13. Effect on prototype-program demand

A smaller true chassis set strengthens the prototype architecture.

It means:
- 3–5 O3 historical roots are enough;
- O2 subsystem inheritance explains modern variants;
- modern factions create diversity through manufacturing/service/doctrine, not twenty sacred ancestors;
- 07 remains distinctive;
- failed prototypes remain useful historical causes.
