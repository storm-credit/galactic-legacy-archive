## 2. S1 Major Risks and Required Controls

### S1-01. Six-year rise from correctional cadet to galactic central-key holder can feel too fast

Risk:
- Rian and peers begin at 16–19 and reach civilization-scale authority by age 23–26.

Controls:
- separate expertise, appointment and sovereignty.
- Rian never graduates into ownership of every institution; he borrows emergency authority from mature fleets, offices, operators and local governments.
- older captains, engineers, admirals, administrators and medical professionals remain indispensable.
- every promotion/authority is tied to a specific mandate and expiration.
- show public resistance to “Academy children” receiving authority.
- H-002/M-001 licences require actual calendar time and supervised hours.

Required scene:
- at least once in GA4–5, a competent older professional corrects or overrules the former cadets on a nonmoral technical/professional matter and is proved right.

### S1-02. Core cast all surviving can reduce consequence credibility

Risk:
- Rian, Haren, Nera, Serin, Mia and Ern all survive to GA10.

Controls:
- survival does not preserve status, health, relationships, authority or physical assets.
- Rian loses future index/command and suffers lasting memory/sync limits.
- Haren carries direct legal/political responsibility and relationship loss.
- Nera loses singular authorship/control.
- Serin loses sources and final-history possibility.
- Mia carries unresolved care/service deficits.
- Ern remains politically separate and loses people/assets.
- at least 3–5 recurring secondary figures and several institutions/ships receive irreversible death/destruction/transformation.

Required next packet:
- named-loss ledger including one opening/ship/route/medical/fleet/record-affiliated recurring figure or explicit reason each survives.

### S1-03. The named core may still sound too similar in Korean

Observed clusters:
- Rian / Rivo / Ria.
- Serin / Sei / Sel.
- Oren / Orvan / Or.

Controls:
- do not front-stage same-sound characters in one mini-arc without title/context.
- Ria Mess remains first rename candidate.
- Orvan Sei should be called `오르반` consistently; Serin uses `세린`; Luka uses `루카`.
- side-character naming must avoid `리-/세-/오르-` clusters.

Recommendation:
- rename FC-005 `리아 메스` to **메사 린 / Messa Rin** before manuscript lock.

Canon correction required:
- treat `리아 메스` as provisional despite candidate list.

### S1-04. Aurel’s one reader-facing name across human office/AI/composite can reproduce the very identity confusion the story critiques

Controls:
- every grand act’s first Aurel appearance states current embodiment/office scope.
- dialogue distinguishes `아우렐 집행자`, `아우렐 좌`, `아우렐 인격체` when relevant.
- one independent Aurel-derived person receives a separate chosen personal name in GA10.
- readers must understand ambiguity is institutional continuity, not a cheap “same immortal villain” reveal.

Required next packet:
- Aurel embodiment timeline and final independent-person name.

### S1-05. 파루스 and 07 both survive, creating relic-preservation softness

Controls:
- 파루스 permanently loses strategic propulsion and cannot return as upgraded flagship.
- 07 loses unique combat configuration and original components.
- at least one other beloved recurring combat ship/frame must be destroyed or retired through irreversible damage.
- final use is ordinary training/rescue, not triumphant secret rearmament.

Pass condition:
- epilogue does not tease hidden restoration.

### S1-06. M-001–M-020 payoff density in E834–894 remains dangerous

Highest congestion:
- E834, 841, 846, 858, 861, 863, 868, 872, 889, 894.

Controls:
- no two consecutive payoff episodes without a physical aftermath/mission/relationship episode unless the events are one continuous emergency.
- E851–875 must include at least:
  1. one exterior/physical expedition failure;
  2. one recovery/medical episode;
  3. one character conflict not about lore;
  4. one enemy/faction action changing access;
  5. one ordinary-life or grief sequence.
- no payoff speech exceeds one new central proposition before action resumes.
- GA10 echoes show operational consequences, not origin re-explanation.

### S1-07. Exact payoff episodes may make pacing brittle

Controls:
- ±2 episode movement allowed through change control.
- decisive action, POV and institutional result are harder locks than chapter number.
- if compression occurs, merge only causally inseparable scenes and maintain emotional aftermath.
- exact chapter locks must be revisited if 1100 architecture is contracted.

### S1-08. M-006 “fifth answer” can become thematic self-congratulation

Controls:
- E681 mixed plan must visibly fail people and fronts.
- State-priority and full-Continuity alternatives retain credible lower-death models.
- later regional use in E1067 chooses a different result, proving the fifth answer is a process, not one solution.
- no character calls it the perfect answer.

### S1-09. Haren’s final elected office can feel like inadequate consequence after mass harm

Controls:
- no immediate return to office after GA7.
- exact sentence includes:
  - D3+ authority prohibition for a defined period or permanently without supermajority review;
  - reparations/service obligations;
  - compulsory testimony/data preservation;
  - affected-region oversight;
  - explicit eligibility rules for later bounded office.
- some victims/regions reject his return.
- office is not framed as redemption prize.

Required next packet:
- Haren legal/accountability order.

### S1-10. Nera’s official-death reinterpretation may feel too convenient

Controls:
- earlier future history must never explicitly show a verified corpse.
- personal disappearance and authorship closure must have been genuinely ambiguous.
- other workers/designers suffer real physical deaths/disappearances so the reinterpretation does not erase industrial danger.
- popular histories may continue saying she “died” despite legal correction.

### S1-11. Rian’s final ordinary role can become disguised supreme adviser

Controls:
- no automatic access to fleets/nodes/Seed.
- invitations and licences are local/mission-specific.
- other institutions may reject his advice.
- he has no complete index and cannot outcalculate every local actor.
- final job title should be modest and concrete: route-school instructor, mission safety examiner or travelling mediator—not “guardian of the galaxy.”

### S1-12. Epilogue at CY 751 may be too early to prove civilizational durability

Controls:
- epilogue proves ordinary operation, not final stability.
- mention unresolved shortages, elections, proprietary conflict, central-sync choices and claims.
- avoid claims that the system will last forever.
- use local examples with known limits.
