## 11. Repeated narrative-engine audit

No structural redesign is required, but later drafting must avoid turning the series into one repeated correction loop.

Known repetition risks:
- `Rian knows future answer → current specialist corrects him`;
- `one central rule proposed → plural institution rejects it`;
- `record says X → current evidence complicates X`;
- `fast central solution vs slower plural solution`.

Context differentiation requirement:
- use different current owners, physical carriers, costs and irreversible state;
- allow central solutions to be genuinely useful in some cases;
- allow distributed solutions to cause real delay/harm in some cases;
- preserve episodes where Rian is absent or not decision owner;
- do not turn every institutional conflict into the same moral answer.

The existing detailed design already contains these counterweights (D4 benefit, Seed/Perfect Route benefit, Plan M cost, no-Rian crisis cost, local refusal cost). Context does not flatten them.

**Verdict: PASS / WATCH FOR MANUSCRIPT EXECUTION.**
