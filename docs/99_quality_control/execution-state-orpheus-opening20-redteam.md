## 2. Orpheus S1 Risks

### S1-01. False precision

Risk:
- exact totals and hourly numbers can look scientifically proven when they are design assumptions.

Controls:
- baseline and immediate categories are simulation v1.
- scene narration uses operationally available estimates, not omniscient final totals.
- sources/confidence differ by category.
- later verified ranges remain plural and disputed.

Required:
- every operation card marks whether a number is manifest, sensor estimate, model or later verification.

### S1-02. Plan M may still be favored through assumptions

Risk:
- selected plan receives better human decisions while Plan S/C/G are only modeled abstractly.

Required simulation:
- run all four plans with the same:
  - hostile arrival times;
  - route failures;
  - vessel readiness;
  - destination refusal;
  - medical nontransportability;
  - decision information.

Pass condition:
- at least one plausible S or G run beats M on immediate survival and/or medium-term total deaths.
- M remains selected for combined rights/institutional reasons, not mathematical dominance.

### S1-03. State-function counts may hide class privilege

Control:
- show who counts as “function,” who is excluded and which dependents/operators are genuinely required.
- expose inflated/duplicate official priority entries.
- some informal civilian workers/caregivers are more operationally necessary than officials.
- State packets are dismantled and not preserved by title alone.

### S1-04. The 2.65m theoretical capacity may still allow a hidden better load

Controls:
- capacity is passenger equivalent, not usable seats.
- medical mass, destination capacity, crew, alignment and ship geometry reduce use.
- missed A capacity and C/Grey failures are causally shown.
- no later reveal introduces unused safe mega-capacity.

Required:
- per-window unused-capacity reason ledger.

### S1-05. Rian still resolves the final closure

Current control:
- local/ship commanders close sequentially; Rian only resolves cross-front conflict.

Required scenes:
- at least three final closure/withdrawal decisions occur without Rian contact.
- one decision contradicts his preference and remains valid.

### S1-06. Central Aurel benefit must remain credible after the scene

Control:
- T+24:20 intervention saves 80k–130k from collision.
- later politics must remember this benefit.
- Rian’s refusal of full G cannot be framed as obviously saving more lives.
- beneficiaries may reasonably oppose final distribution.

### S1-07. Ella/Ardo deaths cluster too closely

Risk:
- E680 and E683 plus Vera/ship transformation E684 may overload grief.

Controls:
- deaths occur on separate fronts with independent decisions and constituencies.
- aftermath extends into E687–690 and GA7 opening.
- do not use one combined roll-call montage as sufficient mourning.
- at least one quieter relational/service scene separates emotional peaks in narrative time, even if clock time is short.

### S1-08. Immediate categories must not double count

Control:
- six categories at T+34 are exclusive.
- later `severely endangered` is an analytical cross-category band and must be labeled nonexclusive.
- crew and passengers are reconciled once vessel manifests exist.

## 3. Opening-20 S1 Risks

### S1-09. Mecha/ace-pilot payoff may arrive too slowly

Current:
- E1 physical accident.
- E3 07 stands/moves.
- E5 restricted rescue/manipulation test.
- E16 first full powered operation.

Required control:
- E5 must put Rian physically inside/linked to 07 for a meaningful 07 action, not only team planning.
- E8 or E11 should include a short controlled maneuver/simulation where his future skill is visible but bounded.
- no reader should wait until E16 for the first clear pilot payoff.

Recommended correction:
- E5 final physical step is Rian piloting 07 through one low-output rescue movement while Nera/Haren/Serin/Mia enable it.

### S1-10. Opening is institution-heavy

Risk:
- accident review, record correction, ownership hearing, identity case, contracts and betrayal test can feel like administrative SF rather than mecha adventure.

Controls:
- every institutional beat occurs during repair, test, movement, medical action or route preparation.
- no more than one static hearing/meeting-dominant episode in E1–20.
- E7 hearing includes physical inspection/claim attempt.
- E9 correction happens while access/test clock is running.
- E10 identity case has a treatment/custody action.

### S1-11. Too many core introductions by E4

Current:
- E1 Rian.
- E2 Haren/Serin.
- E3 Nera.
- E4 Mia.

Control:
- only Rian/Haren require full emotional introduction by E5.
- Serin/Nera/Mia receive one clear role/action each, not full backstory.
- E6–15 deepen one at a time.

### S1-12. Rian can look morally polished too early

Controls:
- he makes at least one harmful/controlling wrong move in E1–10 beyond simple mistaken repair.
- recommended:
  - in E6 he tries to assign roles by future hero value and is rejected;
  - in E10 his attempt to “restore” identity would trigger custody until the person refuses.
- apologies/corrections cost status or time.

### S1-13. Fifth answer terminology may become self-conscious brand language

Controls:
- characters initially call it a workaround/process/recorded refusal, not “the fifth answer.”
- project title/thematic label may emerge later through others or narration.
- E5 does not announce a grand philosophy.
- E20 shows the process is incomplete and may fail.

### S1-14. Ern clue before physical presence

Risk:
- sensor footage and warning can make him a mystery delivery device.

Control:
- E12 includes a concrete present action by Ern affecting a person/route, even if direct conversation is minimal.
- White Dock E21+ must quickly pay off the advance presence.

### S1-15. Archive/UI hook repetition

Controls:
- UI appears strongly E1, lightly E4/E6/E11/E13.
- most episode hooks are physical, relational or institutional action.
- do not end E6 and E11 both on label/count screens without immediate human consequence.

### S1-16. Community stakes risk becoming Haren exposition

Controls:
- Dana/remote contact or a community member acts by E9–E12.
- E19 manifests current people but does not make all 36 names reader obligations.
- Haren’s account is leverage, not his only personality.
