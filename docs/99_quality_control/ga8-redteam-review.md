## 2. S1 Major Risks

### S1-01. GA8 can become a lore encyclopedia

Required controls:
- every reveal changes current identity, medicine, pay, command, personhood, route access or activation authority.
- no chapter exists only to explain Seed history.
- one physical clock and faction race operate in every act.
- use 2–3 living focal cases per concept rather than taxonomy lectures.
- project layer names may remain largely behind the scenes.

### S1-02. Seed origin may feel retrofitted or too convenient

Controls:
- every architecture component must connect to GA1–7 systems/events.
- Authority F/G complete the existing civil-domain pattern; they do not grant new powers without physical/institutional prerequisites.
- no hidden master machine or ancient creator reveal.
- local/Institutional/Continuity records disagree even after origin confirmation.
- Seed cannot solve missing energy, people, operators or consent.

### S1-03. P-001 can become a chosen-one central key despite thematic denial

Required controls:
- independent sources prove his connective pattern; he does not declare it.
- selection is an uncertainty-correction attempt, not prophecy or moral worth.
- Authority G activation requires multiple mirrors, operators, rights and institutions.
- P-001 refuses exclusive curator role and cannot operate all layers alone.
- another corrective/representative actor makes a decisive activation choice.
- unique connection remains a danger/temptation for GA9 rather than reward crown.

### S1-04. Regression explanation may overexplain impossible physics

Controls:
- specify operational conditions and limits, not a complete pseudo-scientific derivation.
- no repeatable recipe, arbitrary target selection or body transport.
- contamination, irreversibility and collapse-level conditions remain.
- some mechanism details remain uncertain among sources.

### S1-05. Record-right framework may sound like policy exposition

Controls:
- preserve/correct/forget/refuse/audit rights emerge through specific bodies, claims, medical systems, AI entities and lost evidence.
- no scene introduces all rights simultaneously.
- every right protects and harms a named constituency.
- unresolved conflicts continue into GA9/10.

### S1-06. AI/composite person cases can become abstract philosophy

Controls:
- each case has ongoing relationships, preferences, work, vulnerability and material consequences.
- distinguish long-running agent, composite, reconstruction and bounded tool through action/continuity, not intelligence tests.
- no universal spokesperson or final personhood score.
- one entity/source conflict remains unresolved.

### S1-07. Forced-copy harm may be used as disposable tragedy

Required controls:
- entity/person introduced before crisis with ordinary agency.
- access refusal has real stakes and reasons.
- forced action saves something real but crosses a clear line.
- post-copy identity/status/relationship continues and cannot simply be rolled back.
- responsible actors face legal/political/personal consequences.

### S1-08. Continuity activation may seem obviously evil

Controls:
- plural/current-status systems visibly fail under scale, forgery and latency.
- named patients/crews/families die or suffer from unresolved status/orders.
- central pilot restores real services faster.
- sympathetic Plural, medical, provincial, AI and industrial actors support bounded activation with conditions.
- first GA9 beneficiaries are emotionally and materially clear.

### S1-09. Conversely, plural archive may be portrayed as naive chaos

Controls:
- plural records expose abuse, restore people and prevent singular guilt/ownership.
- Authority F and living mirrors are necessary for any fair activation.
- central consensus cannot reconstruct source context without them.
- operational compression remains bounded by plural evidence and rights, even when violated later.

### S1-10. Record factions can map too neatly onto modern ideological categories

Controls:
- ground conflict in setting-specific identity keys, node routes, AI continuity, medical access, ship command, compensation and technical standards.
- factions contain internal counterexamples and cross-alliances.
- avoid slogan-heavy debates detached from operations.

### S1-11. Too many archive sites and faction races can overload the arc

Controls:
- reader-facing limit five sites, only 2–3 active per act.
- use one main Palimpsest route/cluster rather than many equal ruins.
- Core/Black Ward/Ardis sources can participate remotely/through known actors.
- one source/mirror is lost so completion remains impossible.

### S1-12. P-001 memory contamination may become convenient unreliable narration

Controls:
- distinguish K1 personal memory, Archive phrasing/images and external evidence where possible.
- contamination has persistent neurological/relational cost, not only surprise twists.
- uncertainty disclosed to team/readers through established procedures.
- do not invalidate arbitrary prior scenes.

### S1-13. Authority F/G may create power creep

Controls:
- F translates; it does not settle moral/legal truth.
- G coordinates/compresses under emergency; it cannot repair physical systems or create resources.
- both require multiple custodians/sites/energy/operators and are targetable.
- no full master access to all nodes/records.
- activation immediately creates classification/exclusion costs.

### S1-14. GA9 regime expansion can feel like a surprise betrayal

Controls:
- activation conditions include emergency expansion loopholes/precedents visible before use.
- current crisis and unsynchronized regions genuinely threaten synchronized services.
- Continuity Office/human/AI actors disagree over scope.
- public/institutional demand pushes expansion, not one malicious switch.
- some conditions remain and help resistance/reform in GA9.

### S1-15. 100 episodes may be bloated

Contractible GA8:
- approximately 78–92 episodes.

Likely merges:
- 8A-2 + 8A-3 if rights/access repeat.
- 8B-2 + 8B-3 if personhood and forced access interlock.
- 8C-1 + 8C-2 if P-001 selection/regression can unfold through one operation.

Rule:
- every mini-arc changes identity/service/personhood/site access/origin understanding/activation state.

## 3. S2 Moderate Risks

### S2-01. “Multiple originals” may undermine factual truth

Control:
- physical evidence and provenance still constrain claims.
- situated records can conflict without all interpretations being equally valid.
- intentional fabrication remains distinguishable where evidence permits.

### S2-02. Right-to-Forget can become villainous deletion faction

Control:
- show people freed from prison/predictive identity and intimate memory abuse.
- distinguish personal deletion from shared public evidence.
- radical wing does not represent all advocates.

### S2-03. Truth Assembly can become reckless leak caricature

Control:
- it reveals essential suppressed records and legal limits.
- internal split over privacy/redaction must matter.

### S2-04. State/Helix records alliance may repeat earlier corporate conflict

Distinct function:
- GA8 centers technical/medical/liability provenance and operational identity, not merely ownership of parts.
- Helix may resist Continuity sovereign monopoly even while supporting compression.

### S2-05. Seed activation can erase human politics

Control:
- human institutions authorize, configure, interpret and expand it.
- classifications are implemented by officials, fleets, hospitals, nodes and companies.
- responsibility never shifts solely to “the AI.”

## 5. Collection Audit

Strengths:
- archive access separated into rights and custodians.
- origin/payoffs registered without completion.
- one mirror/record and one entity/person state irreversibly lost.
- bounded activation is both reward and threat.

Reader-facing consolidation:

Cut any target that:
- adds lore without current material consequence.
- duplicates another record right or AI case.
