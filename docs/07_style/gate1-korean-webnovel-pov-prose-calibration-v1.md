## 1. Research Boundary

This calibration uses only public platform metadata, public introductions, serialization length and genre packaging.
It does **not** reproduce or imitate any living author’s sentence-level style.

Public reference set checked on 2026-08-03:

1. `철수를 구하시오` — Munpia official listing.
   - completed 175 episodes.
   - public premise uses a concrete numeric asteroid problem as the immediate hook.
2. `우주천마 3077` — Naver Series official listing.
   - 357 episodes, contest-award labeling, familiar martial-arts protagonist placed in galactic SF.
3. `지구식 구원자 전형` — Naver Series/KakaoPage official listings.
   - completed 368 episodes; public introduction begins with a normal-time marker and an immediate planetary death announcement.
4. `어두운 바다의 등불이 되어` — Munpia official listing.
   - public premise compresses location, job tenure and disaster into two short questions: deep-sea base, fifth day, leak.
5. `우주전함으로 지구를 내 손 안에` — Munpia official listing.
   - ongoing 400+ episode commercial series; public tags combine SF/space with modern-fantasy, gratification and plan-driven genre promises.

Research conclusion permitted by metadata:
- commercially legible SF packaging explains the **human role and immediate problem before the world bible**.
- a familiar genre handle—survival, martial arts, modern fantasy, game-like progression, workplace crisis—can carry unfamiliar SF systems.
- long serialization requires repeated operational promises, not one mystery alone.
- public introductions rely on one concrete contradiction/question rather than broad cosmic history.

Not concluded from metadata:
- exact sentence length, dialogue ratio or an author’s private technique.
- that one platform style is universally correct.

## 2. Project Opening Promise

Reader must understand within the first episode:

1. Rian is living again in a younger correctional-cadet body.
2. he recognizes a future-discarded frame, 07.
3. the Archive says he is not recorded in history.
4. today’s practical danger is immediate and physical, not only cosmic mystery.
5. helping 07/Haren/the cell will cost status, safety or a clean future route.

Recommended first-page promise:

> 미래의 제독이 가장 약했던 시절로 돌아왔는데, 자신도 동료도 아닌 폐기 훈련기 07호가 먼저 그를 알아본다.

Avoid first-page promise:
- a paragraph explaining the Empire, lattice, Seed and thirteen heroes.
- a list of future disasters.
- system-window spam.
- immediate galaxy-saving declaration.

## 3. POV Test Decision

Tested modes:
- Rian first person.
- Rian close third person.

Final recommendation:

> **리안 근접 3인칭을 기본 정본 시점으로 채택한다.**

Allowed secondary POV:
- bounded interludes or arc scenes for Haren, Nera, Serin, Mia, Ern and selected institutional/local actors when Rian cannot witness the decisive action.
- no omniscient hopping inside one scene.

Why close third wins:

1. Rian’s future memory can be shown as subjective and contaminated without every line sounding like a factual confession.
2. the series grows from one cadet to fleets, cities, archives and distributed institutions; other characters must own scenes.
3. tactical perception remains intimate while narration can describe Rian’s blind spots and body from slight distance.
4. first-person “I already know” repetition risks making Haren/Nera/Mia/Ern feel collected through Rian’s judgment.
5. final index removal is clearer when prose style gradually loses ranked future intrusions without changing grammatical person.

First-person use retained for:
- in-world testimony, personal log, letter, inquiry statement or very short Archive/memory fragment.

## 4. Non-Canon Same-Scene Test

Scene function:
- Rian enters the Academy frame hangar during intake.
- 07 is scheduled for stripping.
- Archive returns the unrecorded-person warning.
- a coolant/actuator failure creates immediate physical danger.

This is calibration, not canonical manuscript.

### A. First-Person Test

격납고에 들어서자마자 07호를 알아봤다.

알아봤다는 말은 정확하지 않다. 내가 기억하는 07호는 제국 최후의 항로전에서 오른팔을 잃고, 조종석 절반이 불탄 채 회수된 기체였다. 지금 눈앞의 것은 도색이 벗겨진 훈련기였고, 왼쪽 무릎에는 폐기 예정이라는 붉은 띠가 둘러져 있었다.

그런데도 틀릴 수가 없었다.

목 뒤쪽이 차갑게 저렸다. 오래전에 사라졌어야 할 창이 시야 한복판에 떠올랐다.

[대상 기록을 확인할 수 없습니다.]
[당신은 역사에 기록되지 않았습니다.]

나는 대답하지 않았다. 그 문장을 처음 본 게 아니었기 때문이다. 처음 죽었을 때도, 다시 눈을 떴을 때도 같은 말을 들었다.

철제 발판 아래에서 짧은 파열음이 났다.

07호의 왼쪽 어깨가 내려앉았다. 정비용 고정팔 하나가 비틀리며 옆 통로를 덮쳤고, 붉은 작업복을 입은 학생이 그 아래에서 굳었다.

“숙여!”

내가 먼저 뛰었다.

미래에는 이 사고로 한 명이 손을 잃었다. 그 이름은 기억나지 않았다. 이상하게도 전투함 세 척의 침몰 시각은 떠오르는데, 저 학생의 얼굴은 없었다.

그 사실이 발을 더 빠르게 만들었다.

Assessment:
- strength: immediate voice, regression and guilt are forceful.
- weakness: future-memory explanation occupies the speaker’s voice and makes every fact feel privileged; later ensemble expansion is harder.

### B. Close-Third Test

리안은 격납고에 들어서자마자 07호를 알아봤다.

기억 속의 07호는 오른팔이 없었다. 제국 최후의 항로전에서 조종석 절반까지 태워 먹은 뒤, 승전 기념관이 아니라 부품 야적장으로 끌려간 기체였다. 그러나 지금 눈앞에 선 것은 도색이 벗겨진 훈련기였고, 왼쪽 무릎에는 폐기 예정이라는 붉은 띠가 둘러져 있었다.

같은 기체라고 믿을 근거는 없었다.

그럼에도 리안의 몸은 먼저 알아봤다. 목 뒤쪽이 저리고 손가락이 조종간을 찾듯 말렸다.

시야 한복판에 오래전에 사라졌어야 할 문장이 떠올랐다.

[대상 기록을 확인할 수 없습니다.]
[당신은 역사에 기록되지 않았습니다.]

리안은 문장을 밀어냈다. 지금 확인해야 할 것은 역사가 아니었다.

철제 발판 아래에서 파열음이 났다.

07호의 왼쪽 어깨가 한 뼘 내려앉았다. 정비용 고정팔이 옆 통로를 향해 비틀렸고, 붉은 작업복의 학생이 그 아래에서 굳어 버렸다.

“숙여!”

리안이 먼저 뛰었다.

미래에는 이 사고로 누군가 손을 잃었다. 전투함 세 척의 침몰 시각은 또렷했지만, 그 사람의 이름은 떠오르지 않았다.

기억이 없다는 사실이 이번에는 명령보다 빨랐다.

Assessment:
- strength: intimate body/choice remains, while narration can mark doubt and later rotate to other focal characters.
- strength: Archive display can be treated as experience rather than absolute system narration.
- weakness: must guard against narrator quietly becoming omniscient.

Decision:
- close third passes.

## 5. Sentence and Paragraph Harness

User requirement:
- no blanket short-sentence style.

Default Korean prose target:
- medium-length sentences carry observation, movement and causal relation.
- short sentences are reserved for impact, reversal, decision or sensory interruption.
- paragraph length normally 2–4 sentences.
- one-line paragraphs limited to high-value beats, not every action/dialogue.

Recommended balance per episode draft (floor 5,500 characters, no ceiling — D-20260812-02):
- 45–60% action/interaction.
- 20–30% dialogue.
- 15–25% explanation/reflection embedded in current stakes.
- Archive/UI text usually under 3–5% and absent from many episodes.

These are diagnostic ranges, not quotas.

Avoid:

> 문이 열렸다.
>
> 리안이 걸었다.
>
> 적이 나타났다.
>
> 그는 웃었다.

Preferred:

> 문이 반쯤 열리다 멈췄다. 리안은 어깨를 틈에 밀어 넣은 채, 반대편에서 돌아가는 절단기의 진동을 손바닥으로 먼저 읽었다. 문을 더 열면 빨라진다. 대신 안쪽에 매달린 사람이 먼저 잘린다.

Rule:
- sentence length follows information/action shape, not a fashion for fragments.

## 6. Explanation Harness

Every worldbuilding explanation must answer a current scene question:

- why can’t they leave now?
- what fails if this part is removed?
- who has authority to refuse?
- how long until heat, air, ammunition or route window ends?
- what does this record change for this person today?

Use order:

1. visible problem.
2. character attempt.
3. necessary explanation.
4. changed choice.
5. cost or hook.

Do not explain:
- node hierarchy before a queue/route matters.
- ship class taxonomy before one opponent or repair choice matters.
- succession law before conflicting orders physically block service.
- Seed architecture before current identity/medical/command failure.

---

## 7. Combat Prose Harness

Every combat scene identifies before escalation:

1. immediate objective: escape, capture, repair, escort, delay or destroy.
2. spatial relation: relative position, structures, approach, blind zone or route.
3. constraint: heat, ammunition, body, authorization, civilians or timing.
4. opponent intent.
5. changed state after each exchange.

Mecha detail:
- describe thrust, inertia, sight line, joint/tool contact, heat and structural consequence.
- do not list weapon names without geometry/effect.
- the frame’s body and pilot’s body remain connected through acceleration, sync and injury.

Fleet detail:
- reader-facing formations normally 3–7.
- track purpose and support state, not every hull.
- command decisions show delayed information and subordinate interpretation.

Example principle:

> “적 미사일이 열 발 날아왔다”보다, 왜 파루스가 라디에이터를 접어야 했고 그 선택이 다음 90초 동안 무엇을 못 하게 만드는지 보여준다.

## 8. Webnovel Retention Harness

Episode opening:
- begins with an unresolved action, concrete contradiction or immediate consequence from previous hook.

By first 800–1,200 characters:
- character goal and obstacle visible.

Midpoint:
- information or success changes the problem; avoid pure continuation.

Ending hook types rotate:
- physical threat.
- costly choice.
- identity/record contradiction.
- relationship refusal.
- earned reward with hidden cost.
- new route/mission opportunity.

Do not end every episode with:
- Archive notification.
- villain smile.
- “그때는 몰랐다.”
- unexplained proper noun.

Five-episode opening promise:
- E1: Rian/07/unrecorded warning + immediate physical save/cost.
- E2: correctional institution and Haren leverage through action.
- E3: 07 access requires Nera/maintenance, not pilot destiny.
- E4: future Orpheus memory is incomplete and physically intrusive.
- E5: first cell choice creates a measurable institutional/relationship consequence.

---

## 9. POV Discipline

Close-third information limit:
- only what focal senses, remembers, infers or receives.
- future memory marked by uncertainty/confidence and bodily response.
- no hidden enemy motive in Rian scenes.
- technical explanation comes from Rian’s actual expertise or another character.

POV rotation rule:
- one focal per scene.
- secondary POV enters only for autonomous decisions Rian cannot witness.
- no rotation merely to hide/reveal information cheaply.
- first 20 episodes remain predominantly Rian to stabilize reader attachment.

Suggested full-series POV share, adjustable:
- Rian 55–65%.
- Haren/Nera/Serin/Mia/Ern combined 25–35%.
- captains/local leaders/claimants/institutional actors 10–15%.

## 10. Voice Blind Test

Required before Pre-Writing Gate:

Write the same 800–1,200-character conflict separately from:
- Rian.
- Haren.
- Nera.
- Serin.
- Mia.
- Ern.

Remove names/tags and test attribution.

Pass target:
- ≥70% correct attribution among readers/review agents.

Voice differences rely on:
- primary question.
- selected sensory detail.
- sentence logic.
- what each character refuses to notice.
- humor/anger response.

Do not rely on catchphrases or dialect caricature.
