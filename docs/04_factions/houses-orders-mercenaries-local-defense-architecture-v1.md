## 1. Problem

Current canon has strong top-level institutions but weaker middle-layer organizations.

Existing strengths:
- Imperial state and succession protocols;
- Helix;
- Independence networks;
- Neutral Assembly;
- Ardis/local institutions;
- fleet doctrine;
- local defense references;
- Gray Layer / mercenary seeds.

Current gaps:
- named noble/service houses as durable institutions;
- knight/order-style military-service organizations;
- mercenary companies with real economics and legal limits;
- frontier/local defense forces as a full ecosystem rather than mission issuer;
- clear connection from these organizations to mass frames, specialist frames and prototype-descended equipment.

This document closes the **architecture gap**, not the named-canon gap.

---

## 2. Organizational separation rule

Do not collapse these into one category.

### House

- durable legal/social lineage institution;
- wealth, office, property, patronage and historical obligations;
- may be biological family, adopted legal lineage, corporate-house hybrid or multi-species dynasty.

### Order / “Knight Order”

- oath/charter/service institution;
- membership is earned, appointed or sponsored;
- may be patronized by a house/state/religious body but has its own command, property and traditions.

### Mercenary Company

- contract-based armed service provider;
- loyalty bounded by law, pay, reputation and survival;
- can refuse contracts and change clients.

### Local / Frontier Defense Force

- territorially obligated public or quasi-public defense institution;
- mission is to protect residents, nodes, routes and infrastructure;
- cannot simply leave for a better contract without legal/political consequence.

These four create different equipment and story needs.

# PART I — HOUSES

## 3. Why houses exist in a space empire

A “house” survives because it controls durable relationships, not because aristocratic blood is magical.

Possible material bases:

- node/service concessions;
- old settlement charters;
- shipyards and industrial trusts;
- military patronage;
- land/habitat property;
- court office eligibility;
- universities/academies;
- insurance/banking networks;
- historic fleet/service obligations.

House power can survive leadership death because records, assets, clients and legal standing persist.

---

## 4. House classes

### H-A Dynastic / Crown Houses

Function:
- succession legitimacy, ceremonial offices, old state patronage.

Weakness:
- wealth/recognition may exceed current administrative competence.

### H-B Provincial Service Houses

Function:
- historically maintain routes, garrisons, public works or local offices.

Strength:
- real constituency and regional expertise.

Risk:
- public service treated as inherited property.

### H-C Industrial / Yard Houses

Function:
- founder families or legal lineages tied to shipyards/frame manufacturers/credit networks.

Difference from Helix:
- house may own one industrial bloc while Helix is a multi-company consortium.

### H-D Military Patron Houses

Function:
- fund academies, regiments/orders, veteran networks and ceremonial military assets.

Control:
- do not equate patronage with direct command.

### H-E Archive / Judicial Houses

Function:
- preserve legal, ceremonial or treaty records and old authentication roles.

Risk:
- inherited custody becomes political veto.

### H-F Adoptive / Covenant Houses

Function:
- membership through adoption, oath, marriage, service or institutional succession more than genes.

Value:
- naturally supports multi-species houses and undermines blood-purity simplification.

---

## 5. House depth requirements

Every front-stage house needs:

- founding myth vs actual origin;
- current wealth source;
- debt;
- legal privileges;
- public service obligation;
- internal branches;
- succession rule;
- ordinary employees/dependents;
- one protected community;
- one exploited group;
- military relationship;
- rivals/allies;
- reason it survives a leader death;
- one thing it can lose permanently.

No house is just a surname and heraldry.

---

## 6. Bloodline and genetics rule

House lineage is **not** a genetic power system.

Allowed:
- inherited medical traits;
- engineered disease prevention;
- culturally maintained body modifications;
- historic pilot-interface traditions;
- politically valued ancestry records.

Forbidden by default:
- “royal blood unlocks superframe”;
- intelligence/loyalty/combat skill inherited as destiny;
- pure blood = better person;
- mixed species ancestry = automatic special ability.

A house may *believe* such myths; the setting does not validate them without explicit evidence/change control.

# PART II — ORDERS / KNIGHTLY INSTITUTIONS

## 7. Why a knight order exists in this setting

A space-opera knight order should not be medieval cosplay.

It exists when a durable charter requires:

- highly trained personnel;
- expensive equipment;
- long institutional memory;
- service beyond ordinary unit rotation;
- symbolic legitimacy;
- bounded autonomy from regular military chains.

Potential legal forms:

- Crown charter order;
- provincial defense order;
- rescue/service order;
- judicial/escort order;
- house-sponsored but state-certified order;
- religious/civic service order.

---

## 8. Order types

### O-A Crown/Continuity Guard Order

Mission:
- protect specific offices, records, facilities or succession processes.

Not:
- personal bodyguards who obey one prince absolutely.

Equipment:
- reliable high-readiness standard frames + a small number of institutional heirloom/custom hulls.

### O-B Route / Node Guardian Order

Mission:
- defend and recover specific strategic corridors/nodes.

Equipment:
- patrol/security frames, service frames, limited breach specialists.

### O-C Rescue / Custody Order

Mission:
- protected evacuation, medical custody, disaster entry, ceasefire corridor enforcement.

Equipment:
- rescue/service frames and armored transport rather than duel superframes.

### O-D Provincial/House Patron Order

Mission:
- fulfill an old defense/service charter funded by a house/province.

Risk:
- public duty versus patron interest.

### O-E Frontier Sworn Defense Order

Mission:
- permanent high-risk local defense where regular state rotation is unreliable.

Membership:
- local service oath, mixed citizens/species, veterans and specialists.

Risk:
- becomes hereditary military caste or local oligarchy.

---

## 9. Knight-order equipment rule

An order is not defined by unique superframes.

Recommended composition:

- 60–85% standard/mass or shared specialist frames;
- 10–30% permanent order-specific refits/mission variants;
- 0–10% genuinely custom/heirloom/prototype-descended hulls.

A famous order may have one or two iconic hulls while hundreds of members use ordinary equipment.

This creates the reader contrast the author requested:

> 기사단의 보급기 → 정예 개수형 → 소수 상징기.

---

## 10. Order succession

Orders require:

- commander election/appointment;
- quartermaster/logistics chain;
- legal charter custodian;
- training master;
- medical/safety authority;
- member council or patron oversight where appropriate;
- procedure after patron/commander death.

No legendary knight is the institution.

# PART III — MERCENARY / GRAY-LAYER ARMED ACTORS

## 11. Mercenary market structure

Mercenaries exist because many clients need armed capacity but cannot maintain standing forces.

Clients:
- local governments;
- convoys;
- corporations;
- insurers;
- salvage claims;
- political blocs;
- humanitarian corridors;
- occasionally criminal networks.

Services:
- escort;
- boarding/security;
- salvage denial/recovery;
- training;
- EW;
- convoy reconnaissance;
- temporary garrison;
- prisoner/cargo transfer;
- deniable action.

---

## 12. Mercenary classes

### M-A Licensed Contract Security

- registered;
- insured;
- recognized rules of engagement;
- expensive but legally portable.

### M-B Specialist Technical Company

- niche capability such as EW, salvage, hazardous environment, heavy recovery or mixed-species service.

### M-C Frontier Free Company

- looser regulation;
- locally respected reputation may matter more than central license;
- mixed old equipment.

### M-D Deniable / Gray Company

- shell contracts;
- intelligence/corporate/state clients;
- legal status changes by jurisdiction.

### M-E Predatory Armed Broker

- protection rackets, piracy-adjacent seizure, debt enforcement.

Rule:
- pirates and mercenaries overlap at edges but are not synonyms.

---

## 13. Mercenary economics

A company must pay for:

- salaries;
- death/disability compensation;
- fuel/propellant;
- ammunition;
- frame/ship repair;
- berth;
- insurance/bonds;
- legal defense;
- medical care;
- replacement pilots/crew;
- reputation losses.

Therefore contract choice matters.

A company can win tactically and go bankrupt.

---

## 14. Mercenary equipment ecology

Typical:

- used mass-production frames;
- captured/legalized hulls;
- regional refits;
- commercial modules;
- a few custom machines owned by veteran pilots or company itself.

Rare:
- irreplaceable ancient relics.

Why:
- mercenaries need repairable equipment with predictable operating cost.

Prototype descendant relevance:
- H08 field-repair and H18 low-logistics lineages are more attractive than fragile O4 superprototypes.

---

## 15. Species and mercenaries

Company diversity follows:

- recruitment;
- adapter cost;
- operating environment;
- client law;
- medical support.

Cheap companies may be physiologically homogeneous because they cannot afford multi-species support.
Premium companies may deliberately advertise broad MS2 capacity.

This is logistics/class, not racial loyalty.

# PART IV — LOCAL / FRONTIER DEFENSE

## 16. Why local defense is distinct

Local defense forces protect something they cannot simply abandon:

- family;
- city;
- node;
- habitat;
- route;
- food/medical infrastructure;
- political autonomy.

Unlike mercenaries:
- they have territorial obligations.

Unlike regular Imperial fleet:
- they lack universal supply/rotation and often use mixed equipment.

Unlike revolutionary cells:
- they may support current law and reject rebellion.

---

## 17. Local-defense types

### D-A Provincial Defense Force

- tax-funded;
- formal chain;
- standardized but often older equipment;
- can be absorbed or divided during succession crisis.

### D-B Node/Port Defense Cooperative

- funded by port, insurers, workers and local authority;
- mission narrowly tied to infrastructure.

### D-C Habitat Civil Guard

- local police/security plus emergency mobilization;
- limited heavy weapons.

### D-D Frontier Reserve / Militia

- civilians with periodic training;
- equipment stored locally;
- strong local knowledge, weak sustained readiness.

### D-E Industrial Defense Corps

- yard/mine/foundry protection;
- utility frames with military conversion plus small professional combat cadre.

---

## 18. Local-defense equipment ecology

This is the strongest natural home for **보급기**.

Recommended force mix:

- mass patrol/security frames;
- converted utility frames;
- locally produced open-standard derivatives;
- a few assault/recon specialists;
- tugs/cutters/corvettes;
- fixed node defenses;
- repair infrastructure.

Their signature is not one ace machine but **how many machines can sortie again tomorrow**.

Prototype ancestry relevance:

- H01 common load frame;
- H04 endurance;
- H06 manual fallback;
- H07 service interoperability;
- H08 field repair;
- H18 low logistics;
- H19 mass manufacture.

This connects the 01–20 program to real military ecology without making all prototypes collectibles.

# PART V — PORTFOLIO / STORY INTEGRATION

## 19. Reader-facing organization band

Across 1,100 episodes, proposal only:

- front-stage Houses: 6–10;
- recurring Orders: 4–7;
- recurring Mercenary/Gray companies: 5–8;
- named local-defense forces: 6–10;
- many background organizations remain unnamed or role-labeled.

These counts are memory budgets, not world totals.

---

## 20. GA placement proposal

### GA1

- local defense appears as mission issuer/background institution;
- no named knight-order flood.

### GA2

- mercenary/contract market becomes concrete;
- one local-defense client and one mercenary rival/partner can demonstrate difference.

### GA3

- local defense and industrial defense become central;
- first open-standard mass derivative proves institutional equipment ecology.

### GA4

- Houses and 1–2 Orders become front-stage through succession politics;
- house patronage versus order charter creates conflict.

### GA5

- orders, provincial forces and mercenary contracts split across fleet coalitions;
- common fleet must integrate them without erasing legal identity.

### GA6–7

- local forces defend/evacuate home territory;
- mercenaries choose contracts, desert, honor commitments or exploit collapse;
- orders may split over charter interpretation.

### GA8–10

- reuse established institutions; avoid late proliferation.

---

## 21. Frame-lineup derivation rule

Do not create machines first.

For each organization:

1. define mission;
2. define budget/industry;
3. define environment;
4. define personnel/species/service needs;
5. assign existing frame lineage;
6. test whether mission variant/refit is enough;
7. only if reuse fails, send a new chassis candidate to proof gate.

This reverses the previous gap where missing organizations produced missing frame demand.

---

## 22. Red-team stop conditions

Reject organization design if:

- house exists only to give someone a surname;
- knight order is just ten ace pilots with unique mecha;
- mercenaries fight without payroll/ammunition/repair economics;
- local defense acts like a mobile expeditionary navy;
- hereditary lineage grants pilot superpower;
- one species owns one organization type;
- every organization supports/attacks Rian based only on him;
- organization disappears when leader dies;
- equipment has no support/logistics reason.

## 24. Current verdict

> **PASS AS MISSING-MIDDLE-LAYER ARCHITECTURE.**

The setting needs Houses, Orders, Mercenary Companies and Local Defense Forces as distinct institutional ecosystems. Their equipment should be derived from mission and logistics, which also solves the author’s request for supply frames, knight-order machines, mercenary customs and defense-force lineups without flooding the story with arbitrary unique mecha.
