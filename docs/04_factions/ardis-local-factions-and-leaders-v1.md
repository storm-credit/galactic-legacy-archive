## 1. Design Rule

- no faction represents all residents, workers, refugees or experts.
- each faction controls a different necessary resource and cannot win alone.
- each leader has a constituency, competence, private risk and succession problem.
- protagonists cannot replace local leaders by being more moral or strategically talented.
- at least one sympathetic faction supports centralized protection and at least one local-autonomy faction behaves coercively.

# AR-F01 — Civic Continuity Council

## 2. Constituency

- registered Civic Ring residents.
- municipal employees, teachers, hospitals, small businesses and pensioners.
- families afraid that route collapse or mass inclusion will erase public services.

## 3. Goal

- preserve legal city continuity, services and recognized citizenship.
- obtain Imperial/creditor supplies without surrendering all municipal authority.
- restore node enough to regain tax/traffic revenue.

Independent plan:
1. declare emergency civic government.
2. prioritize hospitals, registered housing and payroll.
3. negotiate temporary Imperial protection and Helix credit.
4. delay full voting/rights for transient residents until capacity recovers.
5. convert local joint trust into a municipal corporation under existing law.

Resources:
- legal recognition, civic courts, records, public service departments, some security.

Weakness:
- little military/industrial control and shrinking revenue.

Internal blocs:
- Continuity Moderates.
- Imperial Restoration.
- Civic Populists.
- Service Technocrats.

## 4. Leader Foundation — AR-CIV-001

Role:
- acting council speaker/mayor after prior governor’s flight, death or incapacity.

Background:
- public hospital or utility administrator, not hereditary politician.

External want:
- prevent service collapse and keep city legally recognized.

Internal need:
- recognize that excluding transient/unregistered workers undermines the services being preserved.

False belief:

> Rights can be expanded after the emergency, but services must first be protected for those already inside the system.

Competence:
- public administration, budgeting, coalition votes and communicating shortages.

Blind spot:
- treats legal registration as contribution/commitment evidence.

Helpful choice:
- keeps hospitals/ration data public despite political cost.

Harmful choice:
- authorizes restricted service zones or delays transit-resident representation.

Voice:
- calm public-service language, names systems and neighborhoods rather than ideology.

# AR-F02 — Foundry Workers’ Compact

## 5. Constituency

- certified and independent ship/frame workers.
- utility crews, recyclers, unions, worker mutuals and foundry families.

Goal:
- keep production and life-support labor under worker/local control.
- obtain wages, safety, housing and ownership/representation.
- prevent Helix/Imperial seizure or radical destruction.

Independent plan:
1. condition repairs on wage/representation guarantees.
2. use strike/slowdown and physical control of yards.
3. support open standard only with worker test/liability authority.
4. form militia/utility-frame defense.
5. demand node trust shares tied to operational labor.

Resources:
- Foundry Cylinder, heavy tools, skilled labor, industrial frames, partial local security.

Weakness:
- internal certification/guild/owner/union divisions and food/medical dependence.

Internal blocs:
- Certified Safety Guild.
- Open Repair Cooperative.
- Worker Mutual/Union.
- Yard Owners and Creditors.
- Militant Defense Committees.

## 6. Leader Foundation — AR-WRK-001

Role:
- elected/temporary Foundry strike and emergency operations chair.

Background:
- senior shift coordinator or safety investigator with family/community in city.

External want:
- prevent wage theft and asset seizure while keeping essential systems running.

Internal need:
- share authority with unregistered workers, users and districts that do not operate machinery.

False belief:

> Those who keep the city physically alive have earned the right to decide its future.

Competence:
- production schedules, labor organization, accident response and bargaining.

Blind spot:
- can turn labor indispensability into technocratic rule.

Helpful choice:
- runs unsafe repairs during crisis and supports bounded open service.

Harmful choice:
- withholds repair/power to force political concessions, harming patients/transit residents.

Voice:
- shift/throughput/safety language, skeptical of symbolic politics.

Relationship with H-002:
- respects skill but rejects outsider celebrity ownership; may demand documentation/training and local labor credit.

# AR-F03 — Node Custodian College

## 7. Constituency

- phase engineers, traffic operators, authentication staff and apprentices.
- families/lineages that maintained the damaged node across generations.

Goal:
- prevent catastrophic restoration and preserve technical integrity.
- retain operator authority and knowledge from military/political capture.
- regain stable L2 status only if city can support it.

Independent plan:
1. limit node output and traffic.
2. demand all parties submit to operator safety rules.
3. keep some service details compartmented.
4. seek specialized Imperial/Helix/Archive assistance without giving total control.
5. choose successor/apprentice system before current experts fail.

Resources:
- physical node access, operator keys, tacit knowledge, traffic data and shutdown capability.

Weakness:
- small population, public distrust, aging workforce, dependence on external parts/energy.

Internal blocs:
- Preservationists.
- Restoration Engineers.
- Hereditary Custodians.
- Open Training Reformers.
- Continuity-aligned operators.

## 8. Leader Foundation — AR-NOD-001

Role:
- senior node operator/college convener.

Background:
- survived original node accident and lost colleagues/family; personally responsible for later shutdown decisions.

External want:
- keep node below irreversible failure and train successors.

Internal need:
- stop treating secrecy and inherited expertise as the only protection from political misuse.

False belief:

> People who do not understand the node cannot meaningfully consent to its risk, so operators must retain the final decision.

Competence:
- phase stability, traffic control, emergency shutdown and failure history.

Blind spot:
- underestimates political/ethical value of local choice and overestimates operator neutrality.

Helpful choice:
- refuses unsafe high-output restoration even under siege.

Harmful choice:
- withholds data/access and prioritizes node hardware over transit habitats.

Voice:
- exact confidence bands, long memory of failures, impatient with simplified promises.

Relationship with P-001/H-002:
- sees 07/open standard as useful but dangerously incomplete.
- can recognize Continuity protocol without knowing Archive origin.

# AR-F04 — Transit and Unregistered Residents’ Assembly

## 9. Constituency

- refugees, stateless workers, stranded crews, informal-market families, identity-closed residents and temporary habitats.

Goal:
- gain service access, representation and protection from forced clearance/recruitment.
- preserve mobility and identity correction.
- prevent being used as labor or bargaining population.

Independent plan:
1. organize district service/defense and message networks.
2. demand provisional residency/voting or negotiated representation.
3. cooperate with Neutral/Independence/local workers selectively.
4. obstruct deportation/clearance and expose records.
5. secure exit routes without abandoning those unable to travel.

Resources:
- population, labor, transit intelligence, informal trade, ships/couriers, public legitimacy if visible.

Weakness:
- internal diversity, weak finance/heavy defense, infiltration and unstable identities.

Internal blocs:
- Long-Term Residents.
- Ship Crews/Merchants.
- Refugee Care Networks.
- Independence Organizers.
- People seeking Imperial/Helix recognition.

## 10. Leader Foundation — AR-TRN-001

Role:
- rotating assembly delegate, not permanent sole leader.

Recommended background:
- former teacher, claims advocate, dock coordinator or caregiver whose own status remains provisional.

External want:
- secure provisional rights and minimum services before external forces arrive.

Internal need:
- accept that assembly cannot claim to speak for every transient person or prevent individual contracts/exit.

False belief:

> Shared exclusion creates enough unity to act as one political community.

Truth:

> People excluded together still have different risks, ambitions and obligations; representation requires dissent and exit.

Competence:
- public organizing, multilingual communication, service distribution and witness collection.

Blind spot:
- can use visible suffering to pressure decisions and silence internal opponents.

Helpful choice:
- organizes evacuation/service networks and exposes identity manipulation.

Harmful choice:
- blocks clearance or movement even for people who choose other routes, fearing fragmentation.

Voice:
- concrete names and district effects; switches registers/languages, avoids abstract “the people” when challenged.

# AR-F05 — Ardis Federalist / Independence Coalition

## 11. Constituency

- local autonomy supporters, Independence cells, some militia/young workers and regions harmed by Lumen/Imperial extraction.

Goal:
- prevent Imperial/Helix capture and declare recognized local/federal autonomy.
- open Silex/Ardis route.
- build defense before provincial task group arrives.

Independent plan:
1. seize or negotiate key defenses and node access.
2. demand immediate local sovereignty.
3. seek E-001/Taris/Lysa support.
4. arm utility frames and organize militia.
5. threaten destruction/denial if takeover is unavoidable.

Resources:
- motivated cells, route contacts, militia, some local political support.

Weakness:
- internal split, weak supplies/heavy fleet and legitimacy among registered/central-stability residents.

Internal blocs:
- Federalist Civic Autonomy.
- Liberation Network.
- Localist Defense.
- Scorched Denial.
- Social/Revolutionary organizers.

## 12. Leader Foundation — AR-FED-001

Role:
- local councilor/defense organizer representing federalist bloc.

Background:
- former Imperial provincial officer, teacher, worker leader or legal advocate; recommended ex-local defense officer who resigned after Lumen abandoned Ardis.

External want:
- secure local command before outside fleets arrive.

Internal need:
- accept power-sharing with groups that may choose continued Imperial, Helix or Neutral ties.

False belief:

> Once external coercion is removed, local interests will align around self-rule.

Competence:
- local defense, political coalition and public legitimacy.

Blind spot:
- can label dissenters/creditor-aligned residents as collaborators.

Helpful choice:
- resists external seizure and shares defense information.

Harmful choice:
- authorizes weapons/denial plan that endangers node/habitats or coerces political unity.

Voice:
- local history and responsibility; more formal than E-001, frustrated with clandestine-cell methods.

# AR-F06 — Helix–Creditor Recovery Authority

## 13. Constituency

- Helix facilities/workers/patients, regional lenders, suppliers, investors and customers needing certified systems.

Goal:
- preserve collateral and critical production/medical assets.
- control/certify node restoration and open standard.
- recover debt through management stake or long-term concessions.

Independent plan:
1. offer emergency medicine/parts/credit.
2. demand management, data, production and authentication rights.
3. divide workers/council through wage and safety guarantees.
4. support local services while preparing security seizure.
5. contain open standard or absorb it into certified stack.

Resources:
- medicine, parts, technical teams, credit, security, contracts and expertise.

Weakness:
- public distrust, cannot govern population alone, internal bloc conflict and Imperial competition.

Internal blocs:
- Manufacturing/Service Compromise.
- Finance/Receivership.
- Medical/Human Performance.
- Security.
- Frontier Operations.

## 14. Leader Foundation — AR-HEL-001

Role:
- regional receivership director or chief recovery engineer, distinct from R-HEL-001 but possibly their superior/rival.

External want:
- make Ardis solvent and technically stable under enforceable management.

Internal need:
- accept loss-sharing and local/user ownership beyond priced contracts.

False belief:

> Those who provide the capital and certified systems needed to prevent collapse have earned management control until the risk is repaid.

Competence:
- restructuring, supply/technical coordination, political negotiation and crisis finance.

Blind spot:
- assumes price can measure consent and local contribution.

Helpful choice:
- delivers medicine/parts and protects facilities from reckless military use.

Harmful choice:
- links essential supply to receivership, excludes unregistered residents and enforces remote locks.

Voice:
- calm, numbers/guarantees, explains who absorbs losses; does not mock locals.

# AR-F07 — Local Defense Command

## 15. Constituency

- regular defense crews, militia, cutter/frame personnel and families across districts.

Goal:
- prevent external occupation and internal civil war.
- maintain ammunition, command and civilian rules of engagement.
- choose lawful authority before fleets arrive.

Independent plan:
1. preserve readiness and key batteries.
2. avoid committing to faction until emergency authority clear.
3. secure weapons/node approaches.
4. negotiate with Imperial command and local council.
5. prepare evacuation/denial options.

Resources:
- local ships, frames, fixed defenses, trained personnel.

Weakness:
- divided loyalty, poor supplies, software locks and no uncontested civilian command.

Internal blocs:
- Imperial regularists.
- Civic loyalists.
- Federalists.
- Professional Neutralists.
- militia/local district units.

## 16. Leader Foundation — AR-DEF-001

Role:
- acting local defense commander.

Background:
- veteran of failed route defense, politically appointed deputy who inherited command.

External want:
- avoid fratricide and preserve credible deterrence.

Internal need:
- accept that waiting for lawful clarity is itself a political choice that can abandon districts.

False belief:

> A professional force can remain neutral until civilians agree on legitimate authority.

Competence:
- local fleet/defense, restraint, logistics and officer trust.

Blind spot:
- delays decisions and protects force integrity over vulnerable zones.

Helpful choice:
- refuses indiscriminate external/internal attack and shares defense sectors.

Harmful choice:
- withholds force from Transit/Garden or considers sacrificing them to protect Anchor Crown.

Voice:
- professional, scenario-based, avoids slogans; under pressure invokes chain of command even when broken.

## 18. Independent Faction Clocks

### Civic Continuity Clock

0 — emergency services and negotiations.
1 — restrict new service enrollment.
2 — seek Imperial/credit receivership.
3 — invoke registered-citizen emergency authority.
4 — accept or resist joint trust depending concessions.
5 — local election/legitimacy crisis.

### Foundry Clock

0 — conditional work and wage demands.
1 — slowdown/strike in noncritical lines.
2 — seize or defend production systems.
3 — form worker militia/standards authority.
4 — split over Helix/open standard.
5 — commit production to local trust or creditor control.

### Custodian Clock

0 — reduce node capacity.
1 — close dangerous sectors/data.
2 — request external expert help.
3 — threaten shutdown if political actors interfere.
4 — apprentices/operators split.
5 — share or centralize node knowledge/authority.

### Transit Assembly Clock

0 — service network and petitions.
1 — public records/identity action.
2 — resist clearance and occupy service facilities.
3 — split over destination/representation.
4 — secure provisional citizenship/rights or align with outside faction.
5 — riot/exodus/organized district defense if excluded.

### Federalist Clock

0 — coalition building.
1 — demand sovereignty/defense seats.
2 — arm militia and seize positions.
3 — split over Scorched denial.
4 — declare autonomy or accept power-share.
5 — destroy/defend node based on takeover threat.

### Helix Clock

0 — supply/receivership offer.
1 — targeted credit and worker deals.
2 — remote locks/claims and security arrival.
3 — take control of key plants/medical systems.
4 — internal split or full receivership attempt.
5 — ally against external Imperial/Seed monopoly or entrench corporate rule.

### Defense Clock

0 — neutrality and readiness.
1 — district security prioritization.
2 — officer/unit defections or divided orders.
3 — emergency commander claims authority.
4 — external fleet arrival forces alignment.
5 — professional force fragments or accepts civilian joint command.

Clocks advance from shortages, attacks and faction actions—not only P-001.

## 19. Joint-Trust Design Targets

A local joint trust cannot be a vague council.

Rights must be split among:

- physical operation and technical stop — Custodian/operators.
- labor/maintenance and production — Foundry workers/owners.
- civic services/tax and registered residents — Civic institutions.
- provisional residents/traffic users — Transit representation.
- defense — civilian-bounded Defense Command.
- route audit/insurance — Neutral/merchant guarantors.
- creditor/technical supply — Helix/others under limited contracts.
- external state relation — Imperial/provincial treaty.

Minimum features:
- time-limited emergency terms and scheduled review.
- multiple signing/authentication keys.
- open budgets and service priorities.
- minimum food/air/medical obligations.
- representation/appeal for unregistered residents.
- local veto/technical stop with emergency override and audit.
- succession/training, not dependence on one operator/hero.
- exit/asset-transfer and liability rules.

Failure modes retained:
- slow crisis response.
- elite/technical capture.
- underfunding.
- local exclusion.
- external sanction.
- fragmented defense.

## 20. Protagonist Relationships

### P-001

- Civic Council sees useful war/charter symbol and dangerous outsider commander.
- Foundry distrusts hero control of production.
- Custodians challenge his future certainty.
- Transit Assembly may support rights precedent but reject strategic sacrifice.
- Federalists court/oppose him.
- Helix offers resources/control.
- Defense Command respects tactics but refuses outsider command.

P-001 cannot become governor.

### H-001

- community subgroup and Transit/Foundry give him standing.
- must not speak for all residents.
- route/logistics expertise can tempt technocratic authority.
- GA3 end should leave him with one elected/contracted role or external network, not total city control.

### H-002

- Foundry/Helix/Node engineers contest her standard.
- gains production/testing institution only through local labor/users.

### S-001/M-001

- identity/medical minimums become city institutions.
- local professionals and residents can reject imported rules.

### CAP-001/ship crew

- first ship is one service/defense actor; local command and yards remain primary.

## 21. Anti-Simplification Controls

- registered residents are not selfish villains; services and pensions are real.
- workers do not automatically deserve city rule because they operate systems.
- node experts are not neutral sages.
- refugees/transit residents are not unified or morally pure.
- federalists can coerce/destroy.
- Helix can save lives and support workers.
- defense neutrality can protect and abandon.
- joint trust is not inevitable consensus; it is negotiated after failures and may remain minority-opposed.
