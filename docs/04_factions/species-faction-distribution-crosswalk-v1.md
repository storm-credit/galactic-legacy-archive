## 1. Core rule

Species is not faction.

For every S1 species eventually promoted, the setting must prove:

- internal class difference;
- political disagreement;
- regional difference;
- at least three institutional contexts;
- at least one mixed-species institution where it is not a token minority;
- at least one member whose profession is unrelated to the species’ most obvious biological advantage.

Reject:
- “species X = Empire”; 
- “species Y = rebels”; 
- “species Z = merchants”; 
- “species Q = warrior race.”

Current major ecosystems used for distribution testing:

- FCT-01 Academy/correctional-education network;

- Common/Ownerless Fleet structures;
- religious/civic/rights movements;
- irregular/mercenary/gray-layer actors;
- AI/composite communities.

Not every species must appear in all, but no S1 species may be politically mono-institutional.

## 3. Distribution test by S1 slot

### S1-01 Humanity / human-derived spectrum

Existing presence:
- all current major institutions.

Design obligation:
- human institutions must show internal regional/cultural difference rather than become “default neutral civilization.”

---

### S1-02 High-gravity compact species

Required multi-context presence:

1. **Imperial provincial/service communities** — ordinary citizens, clerks, public workers and military families.
2. **Helix heavy-industry / yard employment** — some workers and managers, but not species-locked labor caste.
3. **Neutral route/insurance sector** — at least one professional role unrelated to strength.
4. **species-majority local polity or habitat cluster** — demonstrates autonomous politics beyond Imperial/Helix framing.
5. **civic movement** — could address labor/acceleration safety or unrelated cultural issues.

Anti-stereotype focal requirement:
- lawyer, artist, teacher, accountant or clinician before the first famous frontline soldier becomes their defining representative.

---

### S1-03 Low-gravity mechanosensory species

Required presence:

1. Neutral/route ship crews;
2. Imperial infrastructure/safety agencies;
3. Frontier/local communities;
4. cultural/artistic institutions using tactile/vibration media;
5. one faction that **does not** value their structural-sensing stereotype and employs them in ordinary roles.

Potential conflict:
- occupational channeling into maintenance/safety professions.

Anti-stereotype focal:
- politician, cook, historian, entertainer or medic.

---

### S1-04 Fluid-supported amphibious species

Required presence:

1. species-majority aquatic/fluid habitat polity or municipal network;
2. Neutral Assembly route/rescue participation;
3. Helix medical/life-support market participation as both workers and customers;
4. Imperial citizens in mixed dry habitats;
5. local water/service-rights politics that includes allies/opponents of many species.

Anti-stereotype control:
- they are not automatically environmentalists, sailors or water priests.

Potential irregular layer:
- fluid-support logistics makes smuggling/embargo especially consequential, but no species-wide criminal identity.

---

### S1-05 Integrated symbiotic person lineage

Required presence:

1. Imperial civil identity and medical-law institutions;
2. Helix/clinical research environments;
3. right-to-forget / personhood / patient movements;
4. Neutral cross-border medical custody;
5. at least one conservative/traditionalist community that values stable existing law rather than always seeking reform.

Political diversity:
- integrationist citizens;
- privacy/refusal activists;
- medical institutionalists;
- local autonomy supporters;
- people who consider species politics irrelevant to daily life.

Anti-stereotype:
- not collectively obsessed with identity philosophy.

---

### S1-06 Long-lived radial species

Required presence:

1. old Imperial/provincial institutions;
2. Neutral/route historical communities;
3. reform/radical politics represented by individuals who personally remember failures;
4. local polity with different generational law;
5. military/veteran community whose casualty politics differs from outsiders’ expectations.

Avoid:
- all members as archivists, sages or aristocrats.

Anti-stereotype focal:
- impatient young adult, radical organizer, entertainer, ordinary technician.

---

### S1-07 Electrosensory anchored-body species

Required presence:

1. industrial/service sectors;
2. Imperial or local EW/safety institutions;
3. civilian communities with EM-clean architecture;
4. cultural sectors built around tactile/field art or completely unrelated arts;
5. one anti-military civic movement because their physiology makes EW warfare personally dangerous.

Avoid:
- engineer species cliché.

Anti-stereotype focal:
- chef, advocate, cargo broker, teacher, athlete or bureaucrat.

---

### S1-08 Engineered-origin free lineage — category HOLD

If retained as independent nonhuman species, required presence:

1. current citizens across multiple polities;
2. descendants who reject creator-origin identity politics;
3. labor/right movements;
4. people benefiting from Helix/state systems;
5. creator-descendant legal dispute without species-wide victim uniformity.

If reclassified as human-derived clade:
- move to posthuman lineage registry and free one S1 slot.

## 4. Required species-majority institutions

A multi-species galaxy should contain some institutions where humans are not the demographic/cultural majority.

Planning target across the series:

- 2–4 species-majority regional polities or autonomous service networks;
- 2–3 species-majority cities/habitat clusters inside larger multi-species states;
- 3–5 diaspora/community institutions;
- 2–4 mixed-species organizations founded specifically around cross-environment services;
- no requirement for one sovereign state per species.

These are world-depth targets, not canon counts.

---

## 5. Missing faction classes and species interaction

Existing faction QC identified weak coverage for:

- mercenary/pirate/black-market actors;
- AI/nonhuman/alien civilization;
- religious/ideological/civic movements.

Species expansion must not fill these by making one species equal one missing class.

Correct approach:

### Mercenary/irregular

- multi-species companies/crews can exist;
- some specialist environmental companies may skew one species because equipment ecology matters;
- contracts, pay, citizenship and maintenance remain primary identity.

### AI/nonhuman civilization

- biological nonhuman species satisfy the **nonhuman people** side;
- AI/composite civilization remains separate;
- “alien faction” requirement is replaced by several real polities/institutions, not one empire.

### Religious/civic movements

- movements cross species;
- some rituals derive from biology/environment but ideology does not genetically follow.

## 6. Houses, bloodlines and species

Existing GA4 house/succession logic gains a new risk: aristocratic bloodline can be confused with biological species lineage.

Rules:

1. house membership is legal/social, not identical to genetics;
2. adoption, marriage, designation and institutional descent remain possible;
3. multi-species houses can exist where law permits;
4. “pure blood” politics may exist as an ideology but is not narratively validated by superior ability;
5. a house’s frame/ship tradition comes from wealth, doctrine, workshops and service history—not genes.

Potential future design:
- one old founding house may be multi-species through centuries of adoption/coalition, undermining simplistic bloodline nationalism.

Canon status:
- proposal only.

---

## 7. Knight-order implications

A knightly/order institution can become one of the best places to show species/faction separation.

Possible order models:

- Imperial service order open by oath/certification, not blood;
- hereditary patron house funds an order whose pilots come from many species;
- regional defense order built around one environment, causing demographic skew without legal species exclusion;
- rescue/order tradition where service obligation matters more than combat.

Rule:
- no “species X knight order” unless the region/environment/history truly produces it.

This should be solved when the knight-order faction gap is addressed, not by assigning current species a military caste.

---

## 8. Mercenary implications

Multi-species mercenary units create real logistics choices:

- more diverse service needs;
- wider recruitment pool;
- contract premiums for environmental support;
- mixed cockpit/armor inventory;
- specialist medical costs.

A cheap mercenary company may therefore be **less** diverse because it cannot afford adapters, while a premium company may be more diverse because it can.

This creates material class dynamics without racial destiny.

---

## 9. Faction autonomy test

For each eventual named species, answer:

- what does an Imperial member want that a species-majority local government opposes?
- what does a Helix employee gain from certification that a local activist dislikes?
- what does a Neutral captain refuse to do for their own species’ government?
- what does a mercenary from the species sell to both sides?
- what internal class group benefits from current order?
- what group is harmed?

If answers collapse into one collective species interest, redesign.

---

## 10. GA distribution guardrail

Do not introduce species through embassy delegations only.

Preferred first impressions:

- worker;
- patient;
- crew member;
- teacher;
- shop/food/service;
- local official;
- family/household;
- only then large-scale species politics.

This mirrors the project’s existing “ordinary life before abstract system” rule.
