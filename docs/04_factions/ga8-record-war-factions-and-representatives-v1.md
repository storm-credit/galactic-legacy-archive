## 1. Design Rule

GA8 factions disagree over what records are for:

- evidence and accountability;
- identity and citizenship;
- operational command;
- cultural memory;
- technical reconstruction;
- prediction and risk ranking;
- personhood and consent;
- forgetting and new beginnings.

No faction is simply “truth” or “lies.”
Each protects a real constituency and can cause harm through its preferred record regime.

Reader-facing focus should remain 5–6 factions, only 3–5 active per subact.

# REC-F01 — Continuity Authentication Directorate

## 2. Core Position

> Contradictory records are tolerable in history but lethal in command, medicine, identity and route access. A bounded central curator must produce one executable answer.

Constituency:
- hospitals, civil services, military/route operators, registered families, people harmed by forged identities/orders and Continuity staff.

Goal:
- synchronize regional mirrors, activate Authority G and create a trusted current identity/command layer.
- keep plural source evidence beneath one operational result.

Resources:
- Continuity Office, Meridian Anchor, authentication systems, service models, state support and technical expertise.

Benefits:
- stops forged orders, duplicate identity and service denial.
- restores payroll, medicine, traffic and command faster.

Harms:
- ranks/excludes uncertain people and regions.
- hides model assumptions/uncertainty for compliance.
- temporary curation becomes governance.

Representative — REC-L01:
- CLM-04/A-001-stage Continuity Custodian or designated regional curator.

External want:
- prevent another Orpheus/route war by activating a bounded Seed consensus.

Internal need:
- publish uncertainty, split custody and accept refusal even when it reduces service reliability.

Helpful choice:
- exposes forged identity/order and restores services.

Harmful choice:
- freezes contested identities/access or seizes mirrors/keys.

# REC-F02 — Plural Archive Coalition

## 3. Core Position

> No institution may own the final history or identity of people it governs; records must retain provenance, contradiction, correction and affected-person standing.

Constituency:
- Ardis/route federation/local archives, victims, workers, provinces, independent historians, S-001-type verification networks and communities misrecorded by states/companies.

Goal:
- connect mirrors without one canonical compression.
- establish preserve/correct/plural/refuse/audit rights.
- keep operational decisions separate from permanent historical identity.

Resources:
- living local records, witnesses, public trust in some regions, distributed networks and plural legal precedents.

Benefits:
- resists monopolized history and corrects erased people/collective authorship.

Harms:
- slow reconciliation, conflicting identity/claims, propaganda flood and weak emergency execution.

Representative — REC-L02:
- S-001 or an independent plural-archive convener; recommended S-001 remains technical/political core while a separate local/victim representative prevents personal monopoly.

External want:
- secure Authority F and distributed mirror access.

Internal need:
- accept standing operational compression under bounded conditions and responsibility for misinformation/inaction.

Helpful choice:
- restores names/claims and exposes curation.

Harmful choice:
- releases or preserves records that trigger violence, identity theft or operational paralysis.

# REC-F03 — Restoration Truth Assembly

## 4. Core Position

> The crisis exists because records were curated and fragmented. All original evidence should be recovered and published so society can judge the truth.

Constituency:
- historians, victims’ groups, judicial actors, founding-covenant institutions, activists and people denied closure.

Goal:
- recover uncompressed originals, source indices and suppressed histories.
- oppose both Continuity curation and selective local deletion.

Resources:
- archive scholars, courts, old access, public moral legitimacy and some powerful patrons.

Benefits:
- exposes abuse, hidden alternatives and institutional lies.

Harms:
- intimate memory exposure, context collapse, weaponized archives, reidentification and obsession with an impossible total truth.

Representative — REC-L03:
- archive jurist/historian or survivor advocate with personal missing history.

External want:
- open Palimpsest/Founding records and publish source map.

Internal need:
- accept consent, privacy, situated records and that some evidence cannot be made public or complete.

Helpful choice:
- uncovers essential suppressed event/author and blocks false canonical history.

Harmful choice:
- leaks intimate/strategic records or rejects needed redaction/forgetting.

# REC-F04 — Right-to-Forget / Liberation Network

## 5. Core Position

> A record that follows a person forever is a chain. People and communities must be able to delete, sever or refuse identities created by prisons, states, corporations and war.

Constituency:
- administratively dead/duplicated people, escaped workers/cadets, survivors, some AI/composite persons, communities targeted by predictive records and radical Independence actors.

Goal:
- destroy master indices, disable operational use and secure deletion/refusal rights.

Resources:
- covert networks, living witnesses, access through erased identities and support from communities harmed by records.

Benefits:
- frees people from permanent classification and surveillance.

Harms:
- destroys accountability, family/claims/medical continuity and technical safety; can erase victims or shared history without consent.

Representative — REC-L04:
- identity-closed survivor, AI/witness advocate or former record technician.

External want:
- seize/delete sensitive indices and prevent Authority G activation.

Internal need:
- distinguish personal/intimate deletion from destruction of public evidence and others’ rights.

Helpful choice:
- protects vulnerable people and prevents predictive targeting.

Harmful choice:
- deletes/attempts to delete records needed for abuse proof, treatment or dependent claims.

# REC-F05 — AI and Composite Persons Compact

## 6. Core Position

> Stored memory, institutional models, witness interfaces and living AIs are not interchangeable data assets. Each entity must have rights according to continuity, agency, consent and relationship.

Constituency:
- person-claiming AIs, composite/witness systems, AI communities, human families/partners and bounded-tool operators.

Goal:
- prevent Seed/State/Plural actors from copying, merging, deleting or commanding AI/composite entities as records.
- establish personhood/consent and responsibility categories.

Resources:
- technical expertise, old mirror operation, current AI communities and unique access to continuity systems.

Benefits:
- protects persons and clarifies responsibility beyond “evil machine/tool.”

Harms:
- personhood claims can block safety/audit, preserve harmful systems or appropriate human memories as identity.

Representative — REC-L05:
- living AI/community delegate plus one human/composite witness counterpart; avoid one universal spokesperson.

External want:
- participate in Palimpsest/Seed access and prevent forced synchronization.

Internal need:
- accept audit, liability and that not every stored model is a person.

Helpful choice:
- exposes AI/human joint authorship and prevents forced merge/deletion.

Harmful choice:
- withholds critical continuity data or treats source humans as subordinate to composite identity.

# REC-F06 — State/Helix Operational Records Alliance

## 7. Core Position

> Records must support contracts, safety, medicine, fleet readiness and legal responsibility. Unlimited plurality or deletion destroys the systems ordinary people rely on.

Constituency:
- state agencies, Helix manufacturers/medical systems, insurers, employers, pensioners, certified workers and safety regulators.

Goal:
- preserve executable identity, liability, technical provenance and contract continuity.
- license/archive plural records under certified operational layers.

Resources:
- industry, medicine, legal enforcement, shipyards, data and money.

Benefits:
- safety, accountability, services and compensation.

Harms:
- proprietary history, contract lock-in, predictive exclusion and monetized identity.

Representative — REC-L06:
- R-HEL-001/AR-HEL successor or state operational-record official, ideally a known actor to reduce cast.

External want:
- secure technical/medical/contract mirrors and prevent destructive disclosure/deletion.

Internal need:
- accept nonmarket rights, user/worker/community ownership and open audit.

Helpful choice:
- preserves safety provenance/medical records and funds restoration.

Harmful choice:
- asserts ownership, conditions service on data or pushes certified central compression.

## 9. Independent Clocks

0 — audit and limited synchronization.
1 — seize/protect mirrors and Authority G conditions.
2 — issue current identity/command compression.
3 — freeze contested records/people/routes.
4 — activate bounded curator/Seed layer.
5 — emergency scope expands into GA9 preservation regime.

### Plural Coalition Clock

0 — connect living archives.
1 — publish provenance/correction framework.
2 — distribute Authority F/mirror copies.
3 — lose coherence under forgeries/conflicting claims.
4 — accept bounded operational layer or fracture.
5 — build partial plural alternative after Seed activation.

### Restoration Truth Clock

0 — seek originals/source maps.
1 — expose suppressed record.
2 — leak/publish contested material.
3 — trigger political/security backlash.
4 — split over privacy/total disclosure.
5 — contribute limited public archive or radical dump.

### Right-to-Forget Clock

0 — protect identities and demand deletion/refusal.
1 — remove targeted operational records.
2 — attack/seize master indices.
3 — delete or threaten evidence/technical memory.
4 — split over personal vs public deletion.
5 — join rights framework or attempt destructive purge.

### AI/Composite Clock

0 — demand standing/personhood.
1 — block forced copying/synchronization.
2 — reveal Seed authorship/access.
3 — split over activation and responsibility.
4 — one entity/community sides with/against curator.
5 — rights and liability remain contested under GA9 regime.

### State/Helix Clock

0 — secure operational/safety records.
1 — assert contracts/liens/classification.
2 — deploy security/technical teams.
3 — support bounded Seed activation for stability.
4 — resist Continuity monopoly or bargain for role.
5 — become collaborator/opposition within preservation regime.

## 10. Representative Relationship Matrix

### Continuity ↔ Plural

- executable truth vs provenance/contradiction.
- can cooperate through bounded current-status layer and plural history beneath it.

### Truth Assembly ↔ Right-to-Forget

- disclosure/accountability vs privacy/freedom from past.
- neither can claim all victims.

### AI Compact ↔ All

- disputes whether records/interfaces are assets, evidence or persons.

### State/Helix ↔ Continuity

- aligned on operational certainty, conflict over sovereign ownership and proprietary power.

### Plural ↔ AI Compact

- strong on consent/plurality, conflict when composite person claims source records or blocks audit.

### Truth ↔ Continuity

- original evidence vs actionable curated answer.

## 12. GA8 End-State Target

- no faction wins all archives/rights.
- Authority F/plural access is distributed with consent/audit limits.
- Authority G/Seed consensus is partially activated under crisis conditions by a coalition that includes sympathetic actors.
- activation restores/coordinates real services and command.
- it also begins ranking, freezing and assigning current identities/routes/technology by continuity value.
- Plural/AI/Forget/Truth/State actors survive as participants/opposition in GA9.

## 13. Anti-Simplification Controls

- Continuity is not lying about the need for executable records.
- plural records can cause real fraud, violence and paralysis.
- total truth can violate people and destabilize safety.
- forgetting can protect and erase accountability.
- AI personhood is not universal or automatic.
- proprietary operational records save lives and exploit dependence.
- P-001/S-001 do not embody neutral truth.
