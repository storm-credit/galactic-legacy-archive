## 1. Matrix rule

Each slot must prove:

- material base;
- legal/institutional reason to exist;
- independent objective;
- succession/continuity;
- logistics;
- personnel ecology;
- equipment demand;
- failure mode;
- story function beyond “another faction.”

A slot that cannot create distinctive decisions should be merged or removed.

# PART A — HOUSE SLOTS

## HSE-01 — Old Dynastic Service House

Core function:
- one of the old lineages tied to ceremonial succession **and** a real public-service duty such as registry, diplomatic custody or emergency accommodation.

Material base:
- historic trust assets;
- limited properties/habitats;
- ceremonial/legal fees;
- patron networks.

Independent objective:
- preserve house standing by proving it remains useful, not merely ancient.

Internal split:
- bloodline legitimists;
- service reformers;
- commercial pragmatists.

Species structure:
- can be multi-species through adoption/marriage/office succession; this is desirable for breaking “house=blood species” logic.

Military relationship:
- sponsors a small guard/order contingent but does not directly command a fleet.

Equipment demand:
- ceremonial/security standard frames;
- secure transport;
- one possible historic heirloom hull if later justified.

Prototype relevance:
- authentication/service inheritance H17 more important than raw combat lineage.

GA window:
- GA4.

Failure mode:
- tries to convert service custody into political ownership.

Keep if:
- house can matter in law and ordinary services even without an ace pilot.

---

## HSE-02 — Provincial Node/Route House

Core function:
- old regional lineage whose wealth and legitimacy come from maintaining a route/node concession or provincial infrastructure.

Material base:
- traffic fees;
- repair yards;
- local taxation/share agreements;
- land/habitat claims.

Independent objective:
- keep regional route autonomy while avoiding full state collapse.

Internal split:
- Imperial loyalists;
- provincial autonomists;
- younger commercial reformers;
- local worker/client bloc.

Species structure:
- demographics follow region, not house ideology.

Military relationship:
- funds or influences local defense but shares authority with public bodies.

Equipment demand:
- patrol/security mass frames;
- service frames;
- corvettes/cutters;
- limited command systems.

Prototype relevance:
- H01/H04/H07/H18.

GA window:
- GA3–4.

Failure mode:
- confuses infrastructure expertise with hereditary right to rule users.

---

## HSE-03 — Industrial/Yard House

Core function:
- a family/legal lineage that controls a major yard, foundry, design school or parts trust.

Material base:
- industrial shares;
- patents/process rights;
- trained workforce relationships;
- creditor ties.

Independent objective:
- survive Helix consolidation and war requisition while preserving industrial autonomy.

Internal split:
- owners;
- professional managers;
- family engineers;
- worker council allies/opponents;
- creditor branch.

Species structure:
- workforce and family may be mixed; engineering traditions are learned, not genetic.

Military relationship:
- produces/supports frames and ships; may patronize one order/company.

Equipment demand:
- industrial frames;
- prototype testbeds;
- yard defense mass units;
- not an army of custom machines.

Prototype relevance:
- H08/H19/H20 and one historical contractor link possible.

GA window:
- GA2–4.

Failure mode:
- treats safety/data/workforce as family property.

---

## HSE-04 — Covenant/Adoptive House

Core function:
- house defined more by oath/adoption/service succession than direct biological descent.

Material base:
- distributed estates/ships/offices;
- member contributions;
- old treaty rights.

Independent objective:
- prove legal lineage can survive without one bloodline or one homeworld.

Internal split:
- traditional oath branch;
- inclusion reformers;
- property-focused members;
- diaspora members.

Species structure:
- deliberately multi-species; best test case for house/species separation.

Military relationship:
- members serve across different forces; no unified private army by default.

Equipment demand:
- little direct chassis demand;
- archives, transport, mission-authority keys and a small protective cadre.

Prototype relevance:
- service/authentication genealogy H17; perhaps historical relation to multi-contractor program records.

GA window:
- GA4–8.

Failure mode:
- ritual/oath gatekeeping becomes exclusionary despite non-blood rhetoric.

# PART B — ORDER SLOTS

## ORD-01 — Crown Continuity Guard Order

Charter:
- protect succession procedure, specified public records/offices and lawful transition—not one claimant personally.

Material base:
- Crown/state appropriation;
- protected training schools;
- dedicated maintenance network.

Independent objective:
- prevent any claimant from capturing all succession/authentication layers.

Internal split:
- ceremonial legitimists;
- procedure/legalists;
- military-security hardliners.

Species structure:
- oath/certification membership; multi-species.

Force ecology:
- high-readiness standard guard frames;
- command/recon support;
- small number of institutional custom/heirloom hulls.

Prototype relevance:
- H14/H17; maybe O4 exceptional lineage for one historic symbol only.

GA window:
- GA4.

Failure mode:
- “protect continuity” becomes unelected veto.

Reader appeal:
- visually iconic order without making every member an ace.

---

## ORD-02 — Route/Node Guardian Order

Charter:
- defend specific trunk/strategic routes and recover damaged nodes under old multi-party compact.

Material base:
- route toll allocation;
- provincial/state subsidies;
- Neutral/insurer co-funding.

Independent objective:
- keep corridor open even when patrons fight.

Internal split:
- state loyalists;
- charter literalists;
- local-service faction.

Species structure:
- mixed; environmental specialization can affect local chapters.

Force ecology:
- patrol/security mass frames;
- service/rescue frames;
- breach team;
- cutters/frigates;
- repair tenders.

Prototype relevance:
- H02/H07/H10/H18.

GA window:
- hints GA2, stronger GA4–5.

Failure mode:
- corridor neutrality protects trade while ignoring political injustice.

---

## ORD-03 — Rescue/Custody Service Order

Charter:
- enter disasters, carry protected persons/evidence and hold ceasefire/rescue corridors.

Material base:
- Neutral/member contributions;
- medical/service endowments;
- public contract fees.

Independent objective:
- preserve rescue credibility by refusing political capture.

Internal split:
- humanitarian maximalists;
- capacity realists;
- security/custody branch;
- medical independence branch.

Species structure:
- strong MS2 mixed-species infrastructure by necessity.

Force ecology:
- rescue/service frames;
- armored tugs/cutters;
- hospital/evacuation craft;
- a few defensive/security frames.

Prototype relevance:
- H04/H07/H10/H17.

GA window:
- GA2–6.

Failure mode:
- limited capacity becomes moral gatekeeping; protected status can hide unequal access.

---

## ORD-04 — Frontier Sworn Defense Order

Charter:
- defend a dangerous frontier cluster whose regular garrison rotates too slowly or has repeatedly abandoned it.

Material base:
- local taxes;
- charter contributions;
- diaspora donations;
- service land/housing rights.

Independent objective:
- ensure local defense cannot be withdrawn solely by distant political decision.

Internal split:
- local civilian-service faction;
- professional warrior faction;
- hereditary tradition faction;
- reformers opposing military caste formation.

Species structure:
- local demographic mix; oath, not species, defines membership.

Force ecology:
- mostly mass/local frames;
- 10–20% special refits;
- a handful of famous hulls with long repair histories.

Prototype relevance:
- H06/H08/H18/H19.

GA window:
- GA3–7.

Failure mode:
- permanent defenders become local oligarchy and decide civilians exist to sustain the order.

# PART C — MERCENARY COMPANY SLOTS

- merchants, insurers, small polities and relief convoys.

Material base:
- long-term contracts;
- insurance bonds;
- route reputation.

Independent objective:
- maintain loss rate low enough to remain insurable.

Internal split:
- contract managers;
- veteran combat crews;
- shareholder/owner group;

Species structure:
- moderately mixed; supports common MS1, limited MS2.

- sensor drones;
- repair support.

Prototype relevance:

Material base:

Species structure:

Force ecology:
- rebuilt mass frames;

- mixed cutters/corvettes.

Prototype relevance:

GA window:

Material base:

Independent objective:
- keep client identities and legal exposure compartmented while remaining employable.

Species structure:
- selected for mission/cover, not racial ideology.

Force ecology:

# PART D — LOCAL DEFENSE SLOTS

- protect one province/cluster under Imperial legal framework.

Material base:

- state subsidy;

Independent objective:
- keep local systems alive when central orders conflict.

Species structure:

# 17. Cross-slot equipment findings

The 16 organizations do **not** require 16 new frame models.

Demand clusters naturally converge around:

1. patrol/security mass chassis;
2. service/rescue chassis;
3. fleet maneuver standard chassis;
4. breach/assault specialist;
5. recon/EW specialist;
6. command/coordination specialist;
7. rugged low-logistics frontier family;
8. industrial/utility conversion family.

This validates the reuse-first frame portfolio rule.

Unique/custom hull demand is concentrated in:
- ORD-01 symbolic guard;
- ORD-04 historic local order;
- MER-03 veteran free company;
- selected house heirloom cases.

Even there, 1–2 iconic hulls are enough.

## 18. Prototype-program findings

The 01–20 program gains real downstream consequences:

- H07/H08/H18 can feed mercenary/local-defense ecosystems;
- H14/H17 can feed guard/command institutions;
- H10 supports rescue/custody and node guardian orders;
- H19 creates the mass-production root that actually fills provincial/local forces;
- O4 exceptional non-scalable success should remain rare and likely institutional/elite, not mass.

Therefore prototype history becomes industrial ancestry rather than treasure collection.

---

## 19. Species findings

Multi-species support creates organization-level differentiation:

- Neutral/rescue order likely maintains broad MS2 capability;
- cheap frontier militia may support only local biological profiles;
- premium mercenary company can charge for broad compatibility;
- Imperial standard forces support common profiles but may underserve rare ones;
- house/order membership is never species destiny.

This directly connects species design to budgets and logistics.

## 20. Candidate survival verdict

All 16 slots pass as **distinct role-demand concepts** at architecture level.

Potential merge pressure later:
- HSE-02 and DEF-01 can share region but must remain institutionally distinct;
- ORD-02 and DEF-02 can cooperate but differ by charter scope/mobility;
- MER-03 and DEF-04 must differ by ability to leave/change client;
- HSE-03 and DEF-03 differ by ownership versus public/workforce defense mandate.

If later detailed design fails these distinctions, merge instead of adding names.
