## 1. Control Dimensions

Every succession or office crisis tracks six separate dimensions:

1. **Staff** — trained people, routines and internal administration.
2. **Money** — treasury, payroll, credit, claims and procurement.
3. **Keys** — identity, route, technical, data and command authorization.
4. **Force** — ships, frames, security and physical enforcement.
5. **Legitimacy** — constituency, election, oath, law, service and public acceptance.
6. **External recognition** — other states, ports, insurers, courts and factions treating the office as valid.

A successor rarely receives all six.

## 9. GA9 Rian Central-Key Phase

Temporary transfer to Rian:

- Staff: Aurel offices and participating commands follow bounded emergency scope.
- Money: no personal treasury; allocation authority operates through existing budgets/reserves.
- Keys: selected central corrective/command/service keys, time/domain limited.
- Force: participating authenticated forces; captains/regions still physically control units.
- Legitimacy: emergency performance, Archive connection and coalition authorization.
- Recognition: strong in synchronized regions, weak/absent in refusal/deferred regions.

Success:
- lower immediate losses and reduced friendly conflict.

Danger:
- beneficiaries and commanders demand renewal;
- every effective use strengthens personal/central dependency.

No inheritance:
- death/incapacity triggers predefined domain/regional fallback, not a deputy supreme commander.

## 11. GA10 Final Function Distribution

### Identity/legal status

- Staff: regional/current-status offices and cross-region translation.
- Money: regional budgets plus equalization support.
- Keys: no universal final-status key; provisional recognition network.
- Force: courts/security implement locally.
- Legitimacy: local law plus individual appeal.
- Recognition: reciprocal compact and Neutral/cross-region institutions.

### Minimum services

- regional providers and public/Helix/Neutral networks;
- interregional reserve and disaster coordination remain bounded centrally;
- service eligibility cannot be set by one permanent authority.

### Routes/nodes

- local custodians, route federations, state/Neutral partners;
- common safety/translation layer;
- multi-trigger catastrophic coordination.

### Military command

- regional/local defense;
- mission-specific theater commands;
- shared warning/logistics;
- no standing universal commander.

### Technology/AI/industrial authority

- plural certification and providers;
- user/worker/affected-party rights;
- catastrophic-risk alerts and narrow containment with appeal.

### Records/memory

- source/community custody;
- translation/provisional current use;
- correction, consent and forgetting;
- no canonical master history.

### Emergency corrective power

- event-specific multi-party panels;
- automatic expiry;
- public after-action and compensation;
- no permanent holder or inheritable office.

## 14. Succession Audit Checklist

For each future revision:

- who controls staff in the next hour?
- who can pay them?
- which keys physically work?
- who controls armed force?
- who has constituency/legal standing?
- who is recognized by outsiders?
- what expires automatically?
- what cannot be inherited?
- who is excluded or harmed by the transition?
- can Rian/core team influence without appointing the successor?
