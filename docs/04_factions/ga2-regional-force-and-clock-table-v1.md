## 1. Scope

This file covers forces that can materially affect GA2 within days or weeks.
It does not list the entire Empire, Helix or Independence movement.

Force figures distinguish:
- `Present`: physically in or near the corridor.
- `Ready`: deployable within 24–72 hours.
- `Aligned`: may support but requires negotiation and is not centrally controlled.

Reader-facing scenes should track only relevant formations, not the full table.

# FCT-R01 — Imperial Lumen Provincial Authority

## 2. Constituency and Goal

Constituency:
- registered residents, provincial administrators, customs, node staff, regular military families, merchants using Lumen Gate.

GA2 goal:
- maintain legal traffic and prevent K-13 charter/Independence/open standards from destabilizing the corridor.
- secure legacy/service keys without public scandal.
- keep Ardis/Silex alternative route subordinate to Lumen.

Independent plan:
1. impose inspection and licensing on the first ship.
2. collect intelligence through customs and Imperial observer.
3. isolate the team from sensitive routes while offering legal protection.
4. prepare targeted warrants rather than immediate military assault.
5. reinforce Lumen/Brann if Silex restoration or community movement becomes political.

## 3. Local Forces

### Provincial Navy/Security

Present in corridor:
- 1 older frigate at Lumen.
- 3 corvettes: 2 Lumen, 1 Crownline rotation.
- 7 cutters/patrol craft distributed K-13, Vela, Orsen and customs routes.
- 5 armed tugs/utility security craft.
- approximately 34–48 military/security maneuver frames across Lumen/K-13/Orsen, not one deployable unit.

Ready within 72 hours:
- 1 frigate, 2 corvettes, 4–6 cutters, 12–20 frames depending mission and node slots.

Crownline reinforcement in 5–14 days after authorization:
- 1 destroyer or 2 frigates.
- 3–6 corvettes/cutters.
- 20–40 additional frames/boarding/security personnel.

Constraint:
- visible heavy action against students/Neutral traffic creates legal and political cost.
- local forces also patrol food, medical and Coreward traffic.

### Intelligence/Administrative leverage

- identity and weapons permits.
- route priority and customs holds.
- academy sentence/charter authority.
- selected classified warrants.
- provincial media/official narrative.

## 4. Internal Blocs

- `Order Bloc`: enforce law, close K-13 experiment, stop unauthorized standards.
- `Cultivation Bloc`: recruit and protect P-001/H-001/H-002/S-001/M-001.
- `Provincial Autonomy Bloc`: wants leverage against Core and Helix, may support local charter.
- `Continuity Cell`: traces old protocols and favors centralized authentication.
- `Budget/Trade Bloc`: prioritizes open routes and low disruption.

## 5. GA2 Clock

0 — after GA1:
- audits and travel restrictions.

1 — ship moves without clean title/inspection:
- customs hold, weapons limits, observer assignment.

2 — community movement/evidence spreads:
- targeted warrants and identity freezes.

3 — Silex/open standard gains operational value:
- provincial task group forms.

4 — Ardis adopts alternative standard/route:
- governor demands control or invokes node-security emergency.

Clock advances without P-001 if regional incidents occur.

# FCT-R02 — Helix Corridor Consortium

## 6. Constituency and Goal

Constituency:
- Orsen medical staff/patients, certified engineers, Brann/Neris factories and subcontractors, insurers/investors, customers dependent on safe parts.

GA2 goal:
- enforce liens and safety certification.
- capture or contain Open Service lineage.
- recruit H-002 and audit first ship/07.
- prevent unsafe copies from damaging Helix reputation and route safety.
- preserve profitable medical/parts access without submitting to state/Seed monopoly.

Independent plan:
1. use ship lien, parts and insurance instead of immediate violence.
2. offer certified repairs and treatment in exchange for exclusive access.
3. buy ghost-ship salvage/claims before team arrives.
4. embargo uncertified adapters after first failure.
5. split workshop guilds through licenses, warranties and liability.

## 7. Local Forces and Assets

Direct security:
- 2 corporate security corvettes: Orsen and Brann rotation.
- 5 armed patrol/escort craft.
- 12–20 utility/security frames at Orsen/Brann.
- 2 rapid legal/technical seizure teams with shuttles/boarding equipment.

Aligned but not centrally commanded:
- Brann yard security: 1 corvette-equivalent armed tug cluster, 8–14 industrial/security frames.
- certified merchant escorts: 6–12 small craft available by contract.

Industrial leverage:
- controls or strongly influences 35–50% of high-grade frame/ship parts in corridor.
- 60–75% of advanced neural/medical consumables at Orsen/K-13.
- software/certification updates, warranties, maintenance subscriptions.

Constraint:
- security force cannot openly act as navy without Imperial authorization/contract basis.
- manufacturing bloc fears prolonged embargo and worker defection.

## 8. Internal Blocs

- Manufacturing: keep production and skilled labor; willing to license.
- Finance/Claims: maximize lien, damages and control.
- Human Performance: secure neural/medical data and patients.
- AI/Standards: treat open bus as security threat/opportunity.
- Frontier Service: knows local improvisation is necessary, favors bounded openness.

## 9. GA2 Clock

0 — audit and liens.
1 — targeted supply/credit offers.
2 — ghost-parts acquisition and injunction.
3 — first unsafe open-copy incident: public safety campaign/embargo.
4 — standard spreads despite sanctions: corporate security and political intervention.
5 — Ardis adoption threatens lock-in: Helix internal split; some factories defect/license, finance/security escalates.

# FCT-R03 — Frontier Independence Successors

## 10. Constituency and Goal

Constituency:
- legally erased people, worker/community networks, frontier local governments, smugglers, defectors, anti-Imperial organizers.

GA2 goal:
- move people/evidence through Taris/Lysa.
- obtain Open Service technology and Silex route.
- prevent Imperial/Helix control.
- prove a political model beyond rescue cells.

No unified command.

## 11. Regional Blocs and Forces

### Liberation Network

- E-001 and related retrieval/rescue cells.
- 2–4 converted escort/courier craft.
- 4–9 small cutters/tugs across routes.
- 12–25 frames, mostly industrial/converted and dispersed.
- route/document contacts at Lysa, Doran, Neris.

### Federalist Network

- local politicians, councils and militia seeking recognized autonomy.
- 1–2 patrol ships, 3–8 armed local craft, 10–30 militia frames across several settlements.
- stronger legitimacy, weaker covert reach.

### Scorched/Radical Cells

- 1 converted missile/cargo craft or hidden missile caches.
- 2–6 small attack/boarding craft.
- 6–15 militant frames.
- small but willing to destroy Silex/Helix assets and frame others.

### Localists/Smugglers

- dozens of uncentralized couriers/tugs; not automatic combat support.
- information, routes and cargo more valuable than firepower.

Potential regional concentration in 1–3 weeks:
- 6–12 combat-capable ships, 10–25 support/smuggler craft, 30–70 frames if several blocs agree.

Constraint:
- agreement may collapse over community destinations, technology release, civilian risk and who controls Ardis.

## 12. GA2 Clock

0 — E-001 holds partial record/route leverage.
1 — recruit community/crew and seek 07/open standard.
2 — ghost-parts/identity target creates competition with team.
3 — community splits reveal liberation capacity limits.
4 — Helix embargo gives radicals support.
5 — Silex crisis forces choice: restore/capture/destroy.
6 — outcome creates GA3 Independence bloc alignment around Ardis.

# FCT-R04 — Free Route Neutral Assembly, Kael Branches

## 13. Constituency and Goal

Constituency:
- rescue/medical crews, ship cooperatives, insurers, merchants, refugees, ports and archives.

GA2 goal:
- keep Kael and major routes open.
- collect debts/guarantees and avoid being used as free refuge/navy.
- preserve evidence and identity while protecting source/passenger privacy.
- define liability for open standards and the first ship.

## 14. Local Assets

Central branch-controlled/contracted:
- 1 hospital/rescue ship usually in regional rotation.
- 2 rescue cutters.
- 3–5 armed security/customs cutters.
- 6–12 tugs/couriers available through contracts.

Member vessels in region:
- 70–120 merchant/rescue/courier ships of varying size.
- only 10–25 likely to respond to one major call; fewer under combat risk.

Personnel/infrastructure:
- Kael arbitration/claims courts.
- insurance and route-authentication network.
- medical capacity and refugee processing.
- evidence/memory offices.

Constraint:
- member councils and captains retain autonomy.
- moving war contraband or taking a side risks insurance/route retaliation.

## 15. Internal Blocs

- Humanitarian: expand rescue/identity protection.
- Insurance/Claims: preserve solvency and risk rules.
- Merchant: route continuity and low political exposure.
- Security: prevent abuse and forced seizure.
- Memory/Archive: preserve records even when politically costly.

## 16. GA2 Clock

0 — audit first ship and K-13 charter.
1 — require captain, insurance, manifest and medical minimum.
2 — community convoy exceeds capacity/guarantees.
3 — open-standard accident triggers liability freeze.
4 — route conflict threatens Kael neutrality and trade.
5 — choose whether to recognize emergency open certification and support Silex/Ardis corridor.

# FCT-R05 — Brann–Neris Industrial and Worker Network

## 17. Constituency and Goal

Constituency:
- certified and independent workers, family workshops, yard crews, salvage operators, local owners and subcontractors.

GA2 goal:
- maintain jobs, safety and access to parts.
- resist both Helix monopoly and irresponsible free copying.
- gain control/credit for maintenance and modifications.
- decide whether to support Silex/Ardis route investment.

Not initially a unified faction; can become one through GA2.

## 18. Assets

- Brann medium yard capacity.
- Neris custom workshops.
- approximately 6,000–12,000 skilled ship/frame workers across region.
- 30–60 industrial frames, 8–20 security-capable units physically near key yards.
- armed tugs and local security as noted.
- old designs, technician logs and labor claims.

Constraint:
- debts, contracts, guild feuds, uneven quality, reliance on Helix materials and Imperial route permits.

## 19. Internal Blocs

- Certified Safety Guilds.
- Open Repair Cooperatives.
- Yard Owners/Creditors.
- Worker Mutuals/Unions.
- Salvage Houses.
- Smuggling/gray workshops.

## 20. GA2 Clock

0 — H-002/07 draws interest.
1 — workers bid/claim ghost components.
2 — first ship repair creates labor/ownership conflict.
3 — unsafe copy/accident divides network.
4 — Helix embargo threatens layoffs and treatment/parts.
5 — emergency standard council forms or fractures.
6 — Ardis offers production/route stake, launching GA3 politics.

# FCT-R06 — Local Predatory/Opportunist Network

## 21. Purpose

This is not a single “pirate faction.” It covers actors who profit from fragmented ownership and crisis:
- salvage houses falsifying claims.
- route extortion groups.
- corrupt customs/security personnel.
- forged-parts brokers.
- mercenaries hired by legitimate factions.

GA2 goal:
- acquire hulls/components/people/data when owners cannot prove claims.
- prolong conflict and sell access to multiple sides.

Forces:
- 2–5 armed converted craft available in one operation.
- 4–12 small raiders/tugs distributed.
- 8–25 frames/boarding teams through temporary contracts.

Constraint:
- low trust and no strategic unity.
- can be absorbed/turned/witnessed, not final grand enemy.

Clock:
- advances with every unresolved claim, delayed payroll, leaked manifest and route closure.

## 23. Conflict Interaction Matrix

### Imperial vs Helix

Aligned:
- safety, legal control, anti-radical operations.

Conflict:
- data/legacy ownership, local industrial influence, succession/continuity authority.

### Imperial vs Neutral

Aligned:
- authentication, anti-piracy, traffic safety.

Conflict:
- classified relocation, evidence, asylum/medical custody, unpaid debt.

### Helix vs Industrial Network

Aligned:
- safety, parts, work and markets.

Conflict:
- ownership, certification monopoly, labor credit and open standards.

### Independence vs Industrial Network

Aligned:
- local autonomy and access.

Conflict:
- uncontrolled weaponization, destruction, payment and quality.

### Neutral vs Independence

Aligned:
- rescue, stateless identity, evidence.

Conflict:
- contraband, force, capacity and route neutrality.

### All factions at Silex/Ardis

- Imperial: control route/authentication.
- Helix: certify/own service stack.
- Independence: open/capture/destroy.
- Neutral: keep route auditable and nonexclusive.
- Industrial: obtain work/rights and technical governance.
- Local residents: survive and decide, not become strategic object.

P-001’s team matters because it combines:
- 07/service protocol.
- Academy charter and multi-party custody precedent.
- H-001 route/community planning.
- H-002 open-service knowledge.
- S-001 provenance.
- M-001 consent/medical rules.
- first ship as mobile test platform.

They do **not** matter because they outgun the region.

Any GA2 solution requires local faction/resource cooperation.

## 25. GA2 End-State Targets

By episode 210, desired but not guaranteed:

- Imperial provincial authority retains Lumen/Crownline but loses uncontested narrative/control over Silex/Ardis.
- Helix splits: some manufacturing/service actors join bounded open standard; finance/security escalates.
- Independence splits over infrastructure preservation and community consent.
- Neutral Assembly recognizes limited emergency certification/route trusteeship but incurs debt/risk.
- Brann–Neris network forms a provisional standards/worker council.
- Ardis requests/adopts contested standard and becomes GA3 focal point.
- opportunist networks survive and adapt; not magically eliminated.
