## 0. Purpose

This document closes a structural gap identified by the author and prior QC: the setting has fleets, states, corporations and local forces, but the **intermediate organizations that actually buy, inherit, modify, crew and maintain equipment** are under-specified.

Do not begin by inventing named knight orders or mercenary mecha.

Required causal order:

```text
institutional need
→ funding / legal mandate
→ recruitment / succession
→ logistics and maintenance
→ doctrine
→ role demand
→ chassis / ship / support demand
→ named organization and equipment only if story-visible
```

A military organization is not justified by aesthetics alone.

## 1. Taxonomy rule

These are **organization forms**, not species or factions. The same species and culture can appear across several forms; one faction can contain several forms.

### O-01 Provincial / Border Defense Command

Purpose:
- protect one or several inhabited systems, nodes and approaches;
- survive until a larger state fleet arrives;
- escort local shipping and suppress small raids.

Material base:
- provincial taxes, local yards, reserve crews, old state grants.

Typical weakness:
- strong fixed/local knowledge, weak long-range sustainment.

Equipment demand:
- durable patrol/security frames;
- low-logistics fleet maneuver frames;
- corvettes/light escorts;
- tugs, tenders, local sensor craft;
- hardened node-defense tooling.

Prototype hypotheses likely relevant:
- H06 manual fallback;
- H08 field repair;
- H18 low-logistics operation;
- H19 simplified manufacture.

Story value:
- makes the frontier capable of acting before the protagonist arrives.

### O-02 Civic / Habitat Defense Militia

Purpose:
- defend one habitat, station, worker district or convoy community.

Material base:
- municipal budgets, cooperatives, donated/converted industrial assets.

Typical weakness:
- uneven training, political fragmentation, little heavy combat depth.

Equipment demand:
- converted utility frames;
- patrol/security variants;
- fixed defense and evacuation craft;
- standardized emergency tools rather than elite machines.

Rule:
- most militias should **not** possess unique chassis.

### O-03 Imperial / State Household Guard

Purpose:
- protect sovereign office, succession institutions, strategic persons and ceremonial/state infrastructure.

Material base:
- state budget, old institutional endowments, priority certification.

Typical weakness:
- political loyalty disputes and ceremonial obligations.

Equipment demand:
- high-readiness standardized guard frames;
- limited-run command/escort variants;
- secure authentication and non-destructive capture tools.

Control:
- “Guard” does not automatically mean super-unit.

### O-04 Knightly Service Order

Working definition:
- an enduring institution whose members inherit **service obligations, privileges, training traditions and stewardship of equipment or routes**.

It is not automatically hereditary nobility and not necessarily medieval in aesthetics.

Possible legal roots:
- founding-route covenant;
- imperial charter;
- religious/service oath;
- rescue/escort compact;
- house patronage later converted into public duty.

Material base:
- endowment, land/habitat revenue, contract rights, patron houses, public stipend.

Equipment demand:
- common service chassis for most members;
- a few long-lived institutional heirloom hulls;
- specialist escort/breach/command variants;
- strong maintenance genealogy and apprenticeship.

Prototype hypotheses likely relevant:
- H06 fallback;
- H10 rescue/capture;
- H11 breach;
- H14 command;
- H17 emergency authority.

Red-team rule:
- if every knight receives a unique ancient machine, reject.

### O-05 House / Clan Retainer Corps

Purpose:
- protect a political/economic house, its holdings, clients and negotiated obligations.

Material base:
- house wealth, concessions, industrial stakes, historical contracts.

Typical weakness:
- loyalty conflict between house interest and public law.

Equipment demand:
- often licensed state/Helix production models;
- house-standard modifications;
- one or a few ceremonial/heirloom hulls possible;
- transport/security ships.

Control:
- a house may sponsor a knightly order, but house retainer corps and knightly order are not the same institution.

### O-06 Mercenary Contract Company

Purpose:
- sell bounded military/security capability under contract.

Material base:
- client payments, captured/surplus equipment, insurance, salvage rights.

Typical weakness:
- cash flow, spare parts, reputation, contract enforcement, desertion.

Equipment demand:
- maintainable common chassis;
- cross-standard adapters;
- recoverable/repairable weapons;
- transport and mobile workshop capacity more important than mascot ace frames.

Prototype hypotheses likely relevant:
- H07 service interoperability;
- H08 field repair;
- H18 low-logistics operation;
- H20 mission conversion.

Story rule:
- mercenaries must have clients, creditors, families, legal standing and exit conditions; not generic battle junkies.

### O-07 Private Security / Recovery Contractor

Purpose:
- asset protection, boarding, recovery, anti-piracy, facility security.

Material base:
- corporate/service contracts and insurance markets.

Difference from mercenary company:
- narrower legal mandate, higher certification, more emphasis on recovery and liability.

Equipment demand:
- patrol/security frames;
- boarding/capture tools;
- sensor/EW;
- medically safe restraint and evidence systems.

### O-08 Pirate / Raider Flotilla

Purpose:
- survive through predation, smuggling, coercive tolls, capture or political sponsorship.

Material base:
- stolen cargo, hidden yards, corrupt ports, patrons, ransom.

Typical weakness:
- ammunition, medical support, secure repair, internal trust.

Equipment demand:
- modified mass-production frames;
- industrial conversion;
- capture tools;
- stealth/sensor deception;
- disposable small craft.

Rule:
- pirates rarely maintain pristine one-off prototypes without a credible support chain.

### O-09 Smuggler / Gray-Route Cooperative

Purpose:
- move people, medicine, parts, records or contraband outside formal systems.

Material base:
- route knowledge, favors, hidden berths, small contracts.

Equipment demand:
- civilian/utility frames;
- modular cargo and rescue fittings;
- low-signature ships;
- authentication translation, not battlefield supremacy.

### O-10 Rescue / Salvage Service Order

Purpose:
- rescue, wreck stabilization, recovery, hazardous infrastructure service.

Material base:
- mutual insurance, port fees, public contracts, salvage claims.

Equipment demand:
- service/rescue frames;
- tugs;
- medical craft;
- multi-species survival modules;
- open or broad compatibility.

This organization form is a natural institutional home for AUXILIA-descended service philosophy without making 07 its private relic.

### O-11 Religious / Ethical Armed Stewardship Group

Purpose:
- protect sanctuaries, witnesses, pilgrims, archives, patients or ecological sites under an ideological covenant.

Material base:
- donations, endowments, public recognition, mutual-aid networks.

Equipment demand:
- usually standard security/rescue equipment;
- specialized nonlethal/custody tools where doctrine requires.

Rule:
- religion/ideology does **not** automatically justify a unique species-specific mech line.

### O-12 Guild / Worker Defense Compact

Purpose:
- defend yards, mines, transport workers, maintenance corridors and strike infrastructure.

Material base:
- dues, cooperative industry, converted work equipment.

Equipment demand:
- industrial frames;
- service-compatible defense variants;
- tugs, repair ships, sensor/communication craft.

Narrative value:
- makes industrial labor an autonomous military-political actor.

### O-13 Species-majority Regional Defense Body

Purpose:
- defend a region where one nonhuman species is locally dominant while still operating in a mixed polity.

Rule:
- it is a **regional institution**, not “the army of Species X.”

Equipment demand:
- ergonomics/life-support adaptations may be species-specific;
- chassis independence is justified only if body geometry, control architecture or environment forces structural redesign.

This is where the species codex may generate real hardware diversity without species=faction simplification.

### O-14 Multi-species Corridor Protection Service

Purpose:
- protect mixed-species transport, rescue and public-service corridors.

Equipment demand:
- mixed-environment rescue modules;
- adaptable seating/restraint/control interfaces;
- broad medical stabilization;
- service-compatible frames and tenders.

Strategic value:
- a corridor that can physically serve more species has political and economic leverage.

### O-15 Autonomous-System / AI Custodian Force

Purpose:
- defend compute, embodiment, archives, energy and recognized AI-person communities.

Material base:
- service fees, member contributions, host agreements.

Equipment demand:
- remote craft;
- embodied frames where appropriate;
- secure authentication;
- human/nonhuman rescue capability because AI communities interact with biological populations.

Rule:
- AI forces cannot bypass logistics because they are software.

### O-16 Temporary Mission Trust / Coalition Task Group

Purpose:
- combine units from different owners for one objective and time window.

Material base:
- borrowed authority, cost-sharing, repair/pay agreements.

Equipment demand:
- interface bridges;
- common consumables where possible;
- mixed maintenance and command systems.

This is the organizational bridge to GA5 ownerless/common fleet logic.

## 3. Chassis-generation gate

A new organization does **not** automatically create a new frame family.

Create an independent chassis only when at least two of these require structural redesign:

1. body geometry / pilot interface;
2. load path / armor / propulsion;
3. environment / pressure / fluid / gravity envelope;
4. service and maintenance architecture;
5. carrier/cradle interface;
6. legal/authentication system tied to physical safety.

Otherwise use:
- mass-production chassis;
- mission pack;
- cockpit/life-support adaptation;
- software/authentication profile;
- local armor/tool changes.

This prevents faction and species count from inflating the mecha catalog.

## 4. Knight-order blind-spot audit

A convincing knightly order requires answers to all of the following before naming it:

- What public/service problem originally justified its charter?
- Who pays it in peacetime?
- Who can revoke or audit the charter?
- Is membership hereditary, selected, purchased, elected or apprenticed?
- What happens when a member dies?
- Who owns the frame: knight, order, house, state or trust?
- Who performs maintenance?
- Can equipment be sold?
- What civilian duty exists beyond war?
- Why has the regular military not replaced it?
- What privilege makes citizens resent it?
- What service makes citizens defend its continued existence?

Stop condition:
- if the answers reduce to “elite pilots with cool ancestral mecha,” redesign.

---

## 5. Mercenary blind-spot audit

Must track:
- payroll;
- fuel/propellant;
- ammunition;
- medical and casualty replacement;
- spare-part compatibility;
- contract jurisdiction;
- desertion and surrender rights;
- salvage ownership;
- insurance;
- reputation score;
- banned clients;
- family/dependent payments;
- what happens when the employer loses.

A mercenary company that cannot explain two months without a contract is not a durable organization.

---

## 6. Border-defense blind-spot audit

Must separate:
- local civil defense;
- provincial regular force;
- Imperial/state garrison;
- militia/reserve;
- corporate security;
- emergency coalition.

Otherwise every local battle becomes “the local defense force” with no authority distinction.

Key dramatic conflict:
- who may order evacuation;
- who may destroy a node to deny capture;
- who owns ammunition reserves;
- whether a provincial commander may refuse central redeployment.

---

## 7. Species integration rule

Species diversity affects organizations through:
- recruitment standards;
- habitat and barracks environment;
- medical capacity;
- acceleration limits;
- protective equipment;
- cockpit geometry;
- diet and rest cycles;
- casualty evacuation;
- funeral/identity law.

It does **not** define loyalty.

Hard veto:
- `Species A = knight race`
- `Species B = merchant race`
- `Species C = warrior race`

Any such shorthand may exist as propaganda/stereotype, never objective world truth.

---

## 8. Prototype lineage integration

The 01–20 demonstrator program may influence later organizations unevenly.

Examples of valid inheritance:
- one provincial-defense family inherits H18 low-logistics architecture;
- mercenary maintenance practices adopt H07/H08 service ideas without owning the original prototype;
- one knightly order preserves an O2 partial-success subsystem as an heirloom maintenance tradition;
- rescue institutions inherit AUXILIA-compatible service protocols;
- a house guard uses a completely modern Helix chassis with no prototype ancestry.

Rule:
- historical ancestry is distributed knowledge and industry, not a collection of twenty sacred machines.

## 9. Recommended next design sequence

1. design 4–6 **house archetypes** that matter to GA4–GA7 politics without naming every noble family;
2. design 3–4 **knightly-order archetypes** by public function;
3. design 3–5 **mercenary/gray-layer organization archetypes**;
4. split `border defense` into provincial, civic, garrison and reserve models;
5. derive equipment-role demand;
6. only then expand the frame lineup candidate pool.

This keeps organizations upstream of mecha catalog inflation.
